# Patient-anchored effective visibility for the fixed-K documentary-M2 queue

## 1. Child scope and one unresolved defect

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s frozen first-resection population, 24-hour primary operative cutoff, 12/48/72-hour sensitivity cutoffs, 90-day CT/MRI window, 365-day prior-treatment window, possibility-based chronology quarantine, whole-accession radiology units, reversible text lineage, six immutable reader books and nine corpus-wide radiology/pathology pairs, indivisible pathology composites, encounter-documentary M2 endpoint, additive locked pipelines, fixed historical K, strict `>5` documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed positions, exact finite-corpus extrema, whole-patient singleton deletion, source-state and reader uncertainty, and non-causal conclusion limits.

It also preserves the parent’s version-faithful meaning of an exposed accession. A unit is not treated as available merely because an eventual body is in HCC: a later audit must show a unique version crosswalk, pre-cutoff authenticated final status, retrievability by the intended scoring service, a complete version chain, and exact whole-pipeline-input hash identity. HCC cannot establish those properties.

The one repaired scientific/decision-bridge defect is the visibility denominator. The parent’s effective-visibility intervention constrains the fraction of whole accessions exposed globally. The action, however, is a top-`K` patient queue. A global accession quota can be met by concentrating exposed repeated accessions in some patients while leaving every report unit for another patient masked. Thus its `c_F*` is a valid accession-level stress test but is not, by itself, a patient-level availability contract. The child adds an exact patient-anchored frontier using the same whole-accession masks and the same locked scores. It does not claim that the HCC data observe actual workflow allocation.

The child hypothesis is:

> Conditional on every inherited all-eventual-content confirmatory gate passing at the fixed `K_.10`, there is a smallest patient-anchored effective-visibility fraction `c_P* <= 0.80` such that all six comparator-closed undeleted margins and all six whole-patient singleton lower margins remain strictly greater than five in 2020 and separately in 2021, under every admissible source/pathology/reader world and every allowed placement of unavailable whole accessions, provided every eligible patient who has at least one source-clean report unit has at least a fraction `c_P*` of that patient’s clean whole accessions finalized, scoring-service-readable, and pipeline-input-identical before that patient’s inherited cutoff.

The primary fixed-K, >5-per-100-slot estimand is unchanged. The child changes only the sufficient visibility contract used to bridge it to a patient-level queue. The parent’s global accession frontier `c_F*` (renamed `c_A*` below for the accession-level diagnostic) remains a required secondary output and must reproduce the parent numerically; it cannot substitute for `c_P*`.

A value of `c_P*=0.80` is accepted only if every required margin is strictly greater than five at that breakpoint. If the patient frontier does not exist, exceeds .80, or an exact solve is incomplete, the repaired hypothesis is not supported. A negative result is scientifically useful: it identifies that the fixed queue’s apparent robustness depends on a patient-coverage contract that is too demanding for this stored corpus.

## 4. Locked information sets, models, and estimand

The four inherited pipelines are unchanged:

- `B(Z)\): independently optimized acquisition-only comparator;
- `R_clean(Z,S,Q)\): independently optimized report-surface/availability comparator without clinical semantics;
- `G_clean(Z,S,Q,X_clean)\): independently optimized full semantic model;
- `Gclean_mask`: locked `G_clean` with semantic blocks replaced by the training-defined unavailable blocks, without refitting, recalibration, or threshold change.

`Z` includes age, sex, CT/MRI acquisition, eligible-unit count and recency, component/accession ambiguity, and development-grouped `machine model`. `S` contains raw/canonical/markup surface quantities. `Q` contains reason-free whole-block source availability. `X_clean` comes only from the selected complete radiology book. Pathology, outcomes, IDs/hashes, reader identity/agreement, form time, ambiguity width, unrestricted text/tokens, labs, interactions, splines, and test-derived features are forbidden predictors.

Retain the additive logistic models, unpenalized intercept, one patient-balanced outcome-blind preprocessing map over the outer development ledger, byte-identical shared columns, robustly certified zero-slope threshold, elastic-net/ridge grids, one normalized minimax development fit over all nine books and source/terminal states, and independent 2019 worst-state fixed-K tuning. Test outcomes remain sealed until preprocessing, coefficients, hyperparameters, \(N_{ref}\), and K are frozen.

Compute
\[
N_{ref}=\min N_a(\omega,d)
\]
over 2015–2019, all four cutoffs, and every admissible world. Set \(K_\rho=\lfloor\rho N_{ref}\rfloor\) for \(\rho\in\{.05,.10,.20\}\); \(K_{.10}\) is confirmatory. Require \(N_{ref}\ge500\), \(K_{.05}\ge25\), \(K_{.10}\ge50\), and \(K_{.20}\ge100\). K is a retrospective fixed quota, not observed MDT capacity. Confirmatory worlds require \(N\ge K+1\), anchored-grade fraction at least .85, and at least 50 eligible documentary-\(Y=1\) events.

For model M, year \(a\), fixed K, and coherent world \(\omega\),
\[

Ties use the frozen SHA-256 protected patient key. Preserve the parent contrasts:
\[

## 5. Version-faithful effective visibility and the single repair

For each reachable clean whole accession, compute the inherited eventual pipeline-input hash:
\[
H_{HCC}(u)=SHA256(schema\_version\Vert length\text{-}delimited\ ordered\ tuples(
source\_row\_ordinal,field\_name,exact\ raw\ bytes,V/A/E\ lineage)).
\]
Include component cardinality/order, field boundaries, empty fields, raw bytes, parser version, and parser lineage. Keep direct identifiers only in protected internal joins; published artifacts use protected unit/patient hashes.

Define `e_u`:

- `e_u=1`: expose the complete eventual unit exactly as in the parent;
- `e_u=0`: preserve Z and replace every report-body-derived S/Q/X block from u by the development-defined operational-unavailable block in both `R_clean` and `G_clean`; apply the same S/Q mask to `Gclean_mask`; leave B unchanged;
- source-unclean, blank-accession, corrupt, contaminated, nonreversible, or otherwise unreleasable units are fixed unavailable and are not eligible for an e choice;
- the identical e pattern is used in every arm of a contrast, every reader book, every year, every capacity, and paired undeleted/deleted computation.

This e mask is distinct from `Gclean_mask`: e=0 removes all input requiring report-body access, while `Gclean_mask` removes semantic blocks while retaining surface/availability information from an effectively visible report.

In HCC, e is unobserved and is not imputed. An external archive may set \(e_u^{audit}=1\) only if:

1. a stable crosswalk maps the archive to exactly one HCC \((Patient Master Index,Encounter Number,Examination Number)\) unit and all components;
2. before that patient’s inherited cutoff, a version had authenticated final status, was released to and retrievable by the intended scoring service, and was not draft/preliminary;
3. the complete mapped body has \(H_{version}(u)=H_{HCC}(u)\);
4. the chain is complete through the snapshot, including corrections, addenda, retractions, merges, and splits, with timezone and clock provenance;
5. log integrity, duplicate identity, and source-world ambiguity do not prevent determination.

A complete log showing no qualifying version gives e=0. Missing logs, incomplete chains, unresolved crosswalks, or unvalidated mapping are inconclusive, not zero. A preliminary-before/final-after cutoff is zero; a pre-cutoff final followed by a content-changing addendum represented in HCC is zero. The evaluated scoring policy must fail closed and mask nonqualifying content.

### Patient-anchored contract

For a coherent state \(\omega\), year a, and cutoff d, let
\[
A_i(a,\omega,d)=\{u:\ u\text{ is a source-clean, nonblank whole accession attached to patient }i
\text{ and passes the inherited CT/MRI interval}\}.
\]
The set is formed after all inherited state choices and exact joins. It uses the patient key for membership and the whole accession key for units; it never splits a repeated accession. Patients with \(A_i=\varnothing\) stay in the outer eligible population with report blocks unavailable; they are not dropped and impose no artificial report unit.

The parent’s accession-global intervention is retained exactly:
\[
E_A(c)=\{e:\sum_{u\in A(a,\omega,d)}e_u\ge
\lceil c|A(a,\omega,d)|\rceil\}.
\]
Call its frontier \(c_A^*\). It is a secondary regression coordinate and must match the parent’s bounds and witnesses byte-for-byte.

The repaired patient-level intervention is:
\[
E_P(c)=\{e:\ \sum_{u\in A_i(a,\omega,d)}e_u
\ge \lceil c|A_i(a,\omega,d)|\rceil
\quad\text{for every eligible }i\text{ with }|A_i|>0\}.
\]
Thus c=.80 means that each report-bearing patient has at least 80% of their own clean whole accessions effectively visible, with ceiling applied separately per patient. A one-accession patient must have that accession visible at every positive breakpoint. This is an auditable sufficient contract for a patient queue; it does not claim actual clinicians receive that fraction. A patient with no clean unit remains a valid unavailable-information case in every arm.

For each year, contrast, deletion status, world, and c, optimize the same six top-K margins over all e in \(E_P(c)\), with one coherent source/pathology/reader world and the same e in paired arms. The exact breakpoint set is the sorted union of 0, 1, and every \(m/|A(a,\omega,d)|\) and \(m/|A_i(a,\omega,d)|\) that can arise in every world and deletion projection. Feasible sets are nested. Define \(c_P^*\) as the smallest breakpoint for which all six undeleted and six singleton lower endpoints are strictly greater than five. If inherited all-content e=1 fails, neither frontier can rescue it.

The parent-global `c_F*` (reported as `c_A*` in the executable tables) and child-patient frontiers answer different questions:

- \(c_A^*\) asks whether a global accession quota suffices under arbitrary concentration across patients.
- \(c_P^*\) asks whether the fixed patient queue remains robust under a per-patient visibility contract.

The child’s primary bridge criterion is \(c_P^*\le.80\), not a post-hoc choice of the easier frontier. The parent-global result is still reported, and a mismatch between them is an intended scientific result showing why global accession coverage is not a patient decision guarantee.

## 6. Exact finite computation and falsification

Compile finite rows containing protected patient hash, year, cutoff, membership, documentary Y state, Z/S/Q/X blocks, all source/pathology/form/terminal hashes, whole-unit component ordinals, \(H_{HCC}\), and raw backpointers. Preserve full outer rosters, including patients with no eligible clean unit. Build patient-to-unit incidence from exact keys; do not use text similarity or repeated timestamps as accession identity.

For every nine-book pair and every admissible world, solve:

1. locked fits and exact scores with no test outcome exposure;
2. all-content `e=1` gates;
3. parent-global \(E_A(c)\) bounds for regression;
4. patient-anchored \(E_P(c)\) bounds;
5. every whole-patient singleton projection with the same c, K, models, books, and ties.

Use exact MIP or an equivalently certified finite method. Lazy rank-inversion constraints may be used only with a certificate that all integer gaps are zero, all tied optimizers are accounted for, and the final bounds are attained. Require numerical feasibility, transformed-loss residual at most \(10^{-8}\), no rank inversion, raw/quotient/hash replay, and exhaustive agreement with brute force on synthetic fixtures through 12 patients and real-data shards through 20. No state cap, sampled e pattern, beam search, favorable incumbent, omitted book/world, or time-limited bound may decide the hypothesis.

Falsification is prespecified. The patient-anchored hypothesis is falsified locally if any computed coordinate has an attained upper endpoint at most five, with year, contrast, c, world, reader pair, or patient deletion witness reported. It is adverse/brittle if computation completes, all-content gates pass, but \(c_P^*>.80\) or does not exist. It is not a falsification of report timing or reader biology. If the parent-global gate passes but the patient-anchored gate fails, label the result `accession-global-only`: stored-content robustness exists under concentrated global coverage but does not bridge to the stated patient-level visibility contract. If either exact frontier or inherited gate is incomplete, result is inconclusive.

## 7. External patient-coverage bridge

A bridge audit must be a census of every accession in the same frozen 2020 and 2021 HCC outer rosters, not a sample or future cohort. It must provide, read-only:

- protected crosswalks for `Patient Master Index,Encounter Number,Examination Number`, enterprise report-instance IDs, and component IDs;
- every version and predecessor/successor relation;
- authored, preliminary, final, release, correction/addendum/retraction, target-service-readable, and if used clinician-view timestamps, with timezone, source system, and precision;
- final/audience/system-access flags;
- exact versioned findings/impression bytes or a lossless validated export;
- immutable hashes, event-log sequence IDs, extraction time, archive-completeness certificate, and accountable source-owner sign-off.

Apply the inherited state, cutoff, roster, unit, deletion, K, reader, and model rules unchanged. For every year, world, deletion projection, and eligible patient with \(|A_i|>0\), compute
\[
q_P(a,\omega,j)=\min_i
\frac{\sum_{u\in A_i(a,\omega-j)}e_u^{audit}}
{|A_i(a,\omega-j)|}.
\]
The minimum is over patients in that projection with at least one clean unit; patients with no clean unit remain unavailable and are not silently certified. Set \(q_{P,min}\) to the minimum across 2020, 2021, all admissible worlds, undeleted and singleton projections. The external patient bridge clears only when Harbor is patient-anchored supportive, the census is complete, and \(q_{P,min}\ge c_P^*\). Also report global \(q_{A,min}\) and the observed-pattern replay as diagnostics; neither can clear a failed patient contract.

A complete finite census needs no invented confidence interval. A patient-clustered sample, pooled-year estimate, modality-only estimate, one-sided lower confidence limit, or prospective cohort cannot establish the exact same-corpus \(q_{P,min}\). A prospective silent-mode study is a separate transport study with its own sampling frame and uncertainty. Current HCC necessarily emits `external_patient_visibility_status="not_estimable_from_HCC"`.

## 8. One-hot conclusions

Apply conclusions in this order:

1. `inconclusive`: source hash/header, roster/form/book, chronology, leakage, N/K/event/anchoring, fit, parser/hash, patient-deletion, exact optimization, or required replay gate fails.
2. `patient-anchored-visibility-robust-supportive`: all inherited all-content and singleton coordinates are strictly above five; \(c_P^*\) exists and is \(\le.80\); parent-global bounds reproduce.
3. `accession-global-only`: inherited all-content gates and \(c_A^*\le.80\) pass, but the patient-anchored criterion fails or exceeds .80. This is a negative result for the repaired decision bridge, not evidence that HCC reports were late or wrong.
4. `effective-visibility-brittle`: all inherited coordinates pass but \(c_P^*>.80\) or no patient frontier exists, without a qualifying parent-global pass.
5. `semantic-evidence-only`: both years’ \(\Delta_R\) and \(\Delta_{mask}\) pass all-content/singleton gates but at least one \(\Delta_B\) coordinate fails.
6. `policy-only`: both years’ \(\Delta_B\) pass but not all semantic coordinates pass.
7. `falsified-localized`: computation completes and no preceding label applies; report exact witnesses.

Supportive means only that the locked full model’s documentary-M2 top-K margin survives the stored-content intervention when every report-bearing patient satisfies the specified per-patient effective-visibility fraction. It supports obtaining the same-snapshot version/readability census. It does not show that the fraction was historically achieved, that an MDT had K slots, that clinicians acted, or that patients benefited.

Adverse or accession-global-only means the specified patient decision bridge failed. It does not prove reports were late, preliminary text was wrong, semantic features are useless, or another model or capacity would help. Inconclusive means the evidence or computation could not decide. After external data, report `bridge_certified`, `bridge_not_cleared`, or `bridge_inconclusive`; even certification is retrospective and snapshot-specific.
