The primary unresolved claim is a donor-aware, condition-verified context-by-intervention interaction, not a generic mean-expression change.

This is a hypothesis, not an observed result. No dataset-specific effect has been computed in this episode. ## Grounding and claim boundary

The available expert seed and prior candidate board motivate the question: macrophage–stromal interactions and MerTK-associated resolving phenotypes are plausible contexts for rheumatoid arthritis therapy. The strongest evidence currently supports only biological plausibility and the possibility of measuring intervention-associated expression responses. It does not establish that UNC106238 acts directly on MerTK, that an observed response is causal, that a protective state is preserved, or that any in-vitro pattern transfers to patients.

The substantive advance would be to distinguish a context-specific intervention signature from donor/timing/composition artifacts and from a generic transcriptional response. A robust interaction would support prioritizing context-dependent follow-up experiments; a null or reversed interaction would challenge the proposed specificity.
