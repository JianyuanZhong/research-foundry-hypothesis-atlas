# A context-supported, reviewer-calibrated pulmonary-nodule handoff gate: route qualification rather than route prevalence

## Consequential question and substantive repair

The decision available from MIMIC is whether a departure-frozen pulmonary-nodule handoff phenotype is sufficiently *identifiable* to justify a prospective silent-validation study. It is not whether copied text is clinically appropriate, whether clinicians failed to act, or whether one workflow improves outcomes.

The parent correctly preserves the first-eligible admission denominator, reciprocal report/addendum chronology, unique canonical discharge summary (`DS`), recommendation-unit clocks, conservative admission aggregation, and unknown states. Its remaining weakness is that a route can appear attractive mainly because it is prevalent after consensus review. Relative prevalence (`RD_ROUTE`) and a single mixed-case purity margin do not establish that the route survives heterogeneous clinical context or reviewer uncertainty. The parent also asks separate panels for global plan states and content mappings that partly overlap, and its pooled reliability gate can conceal route-specific disagreement or unsupported clinical-context strata.

This child therefore makes **route qualification** primary. It preserves every inherited source, denominator, chronology, state, and noncausal boundary, but replaces prevalence-based lane priority with an auditable, fully crossed evidence-extraction and calibration experiment. Initial reviewers never vote on a workflow. A frozen program derives every route from atomic labels. For each admission it carries the Cartesian product of initial reviewer labels, unit-boundary inventories, and explicit unknown states into a set of compatible routes. A propagation or reconciliation lane is eligible for future silent validation only if it has a nonzero **context-supported robust core**, adequate route-specific events and precision, low consequential disagreement, stable calibration, and no reviewer or supported-context perturbation reversal. Unsupported contexts and any route-changing uncertainty are deferred rather than extrapolated.

The strongest currently supported claim remains only source feasibility. A complete, zero-sampling scan inherited from the parent found 546,028 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 canonical `DS` rows, 17 missing `DS.storetime` values, and 3,764 broad pulmonary retrieval proxies. In this workspace, complete scans independently confirmed 2,295,635 `RR` and 25,720 `AR` radiology rows; all radiology rows had `storetime`; radiology detail contained 25,735 `parent_note_id` and 25,735 `addendum_note_id` rows; and all 331,794 discharge rows had `note_type="DS"`. These are availability checks, not adjudicated opportunities, route prevalence, clinical appropriateness, or benefit.

The unresolved falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the unchanged frozen first-eligible pulmonary-opportunity cohort and its ample departure-available stratum, atomic evidence stored by departure will produce at least one route-specific, context-supported robust admission core (pure omission for clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation and/or incomplete/conflicting plan for clinician reconciliation). The core will remain the same under all initial-reviewer-compatible labels and minimum/maximum unit inventories, will meet route-specific reliability and precision gates within the prespecified supported clinical-context envelope, and will not depend on one reviewer, service/disposition stratum, confidence tier, or consensus adjudication. If so, and only within that supported envelope, MIMIC can justify prospective silent validation of the frozen route; it still cannot establish clinical appropriateness, safety, or benefit.

This hypothesis is adverse or falsified for a proposed route if compatible reviewers cross clinically different route states, route reliability or calibration drifts, the apparent route is confined to unsupported contexts, mixed admissions defeat conservative aggregation, or event/precision gates fail. Sparse data are inconclusive rather than evidence that no handoff problem exists.

## Immutable denominator, reciprocal chronology, canonical artifact, and clocks

Before any discharge text, `DS_store`, downstream trace, comparator outcome, or proposed route is opened, recreate and hash the inherited radiology eligibility manifest. There is zero eligibility sampling. `N_A` remains exactly the first eligible admission per subject:

* adult;
* exact admission-linked radiology report with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]`;
* incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity;
* patient-specific unconditional chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months;
* exclude screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional or optional action, explicit no follow-up, in-hospital death, locked hospice/comfort-only transition, and recommendations first introduced after `D`;
* never infer size, smoking exposure, malignancy risk, life expectancy, preference, recommendation appropriateness, or absent context.

Eligibility reviewers see no discharge artifact, `DS_store`, later trace, or route. Report/addendum chains use only reciprocal, subject-consistent, admission-consistent links in `radiology_detail`. Every operative component must have both `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`. A later material pre-`D` component supersedes an earlier component only inside the reciprocal chain. Missing nodes, nonreciprocal or contradictory links, unresolved operative components, or missing/contradictory times are `U_R`, retained in the cascade and bounds, and never called omissions.

The canonical artifact is the one `note/discharge` row with exact `note_type=="DS"` for `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. A duplicate canonical key stops confirmation. Missing artifact, text, linkage, or `storetime`, and unreadable or contradictory content remain `U_D`. `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.

Preserve the inherited all-`N_A` departure state `Y={F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D}`: faithful; wholly omitted; incomplete/discordant without a departure-frozen text-supported resolution; text-supported alternate resolution; known `DS_store>D`; and unknown. These cells sum exactly to `N_A`; faithful later text never changes `Y=A`.

Preserve `R_store`, `DS_store`, and `G=DS_store-R_store` in hours; `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`; `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`; eventual `Z={F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text}`; and every zero and unknown cell of `J_A=count(V,H,Z)/N_A`. The primary ample, departure-available stratum is unchanged: `E_A={V_D and H in (H_24_72,H_72plus)}`. No `G<0`, `DS_store>D`, unresolved chain, or unknown clock enters `E_A`. Verify row-wise `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G`; only `G`, not the left side, defines recommendation-to-artifact opportunity.

After `N_A` is frozen but before discharge text is opened, two radiology reviewers independently enumerate all eligible recommendation units nested within each index admission. A unit is the smallest operative tuple `{target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, polarity}`. Co-mentioned nodules sharing an action and due interval are one unit; different actions or due windows are separate units; repeated language and reciprocal addendum restatements are deduplicated; a material addendum changes the operative version rather than adding an event. Each deterministic `(subject_id,hadm_id,unit_id)` records component `note_id`, exact source offsets, `R_store,u`, and boundary provenance. Boundary disagreement creates `U_BOUNDARY` and both minimum and maximum compatible inventories; discharge text cannot resolve it.

For every unit calculate `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u` with the same `H` cut points. The index unit preserves inherited `G/E_A`. Additional units cannot add an admission to `E_A`, but every active unresolved unit must have known `G_u>=24h` for a content route. A short or unresolved unit clock makes the admission `A_TIMING` or `A_DEFER`, never a content failure. Recommendation units describe workload; admission is the denominator, decision unit, and resampling unit.

## Auditable blinded adjudication and calibration

### Sampling and assignment

The confirmatory clinical review is the complete frozen `E_A` census. No route-enriched or outcome-enriched sampling is permitted. A 50-admission stratified run-in may refine packet mechanics and definitions before the codebook, software hash, context strata, reviewer roster, and acceptance thresholds are frozen. Run-in labels are destroyed; those admissions return to the census after at least a four-week washout, receive new identifiers, and are randomly reassigned. Run-in outcomes cannot change eligibility, thresholds, or exclusions.

Use disjoint reviewer pools for the three tasks below. Each confirmatory admission is independently reviewed by three radiology-unit reviewers, three context reviewers, and three plan-attribute reviewers selected by a seeded balanced incomplete-block assignment. Assignment is balanced, without exposing outcomes, over admission type, ICU exposure, terminal-service family, discharge-location family, locked length-of-stay bin, age bin, 3/6/12-month interval, and unit count (`1` versus `>1`). Reviewers never see another initial label or consensus. Ten percent of packets, at least 50 per task, are duplicated under unrelated identifiers after a washout to estimate intra-reviewer reliability. Another stratified 10%, at least 50, is assigned to every reviewer in the relevant pool to estimate reviewer effects rather than confounding reviewer with context.

Before confirmatory labels, reviewers must complete a fixed local calibration set of synthetic and source-pattern-derived packets whose expected answers follow the text rules below. These anchors test rule execution, not clinical truth. During review, one blinded drift anchor is interleaved per 20 real packets. Anchor outcomes are stored separately and cannot alter patient labels. No retraining, codebook change, or adjudication is allowed after confirmatory labels begin; drift failure blocks the affected batch and requires a new prospective protocol version rather than silently repairing results.

### Panel U: radiology-only unit boundaries

Panel U sees the reciprocal operative radiology chain and its pre-`D` component order, but no discharge artifact, `DS_store`, admission outcome, later event, context panel label, or workflow language. Reviewers mark source offsets for target, action, interval/date, polarity, and coreference and construct units under the locked rule. Their minimum/maximum compatible inventories are retained. A later adjudicator may explain disagreement but cannot delete it from the primary route set.

### Panel C: observable departure context, never appropriateness

An independent redaction team creates a full canonical `DS` packet stored by `D` in which every pulmonary target, action, timing/date, recipient, target-linked order/referral, target-linked refusal/non-pursuit, and target-linked replacement-plan span is replaced by a typed mask with offsets preserved. Panel C receives that full redacted artifact, the operative radiology chain with the eligible payload masked, and only departure-known structured fields from `admissions`, `patients`, `services`, `transfers`, and `icustays`. It receives no `G/H/V`, plan labels, POE, later note, downstream outcome, or intended route.

Each context reviewer assigns:

* `K_OPEN`: no explicit departure-frozen non-target statement in the available packet closes the possibility of an outpatient pulmonary handoff;
* `K_CLOSED`: explicit global non-target context makes any outpatient follow-up action inapplicable, triggering denominator audit;
* `K_UNKNOWN`: missing, internally conflicting, or ambiguous available context prevents either statement.

`K_OPEN` means only **no observable context-level stop in the available MIMIC packet**. It is never printed as clinically appropriate, necessary, beneficial, or compatible care. Reviewers quote exact evidence, identify the missing fact most likely to change the label, select a confidence tier, and tag one or more prespecified evidence domains: goals/preferences, competing acute illness/prognosis, disposition/access, outside-plan mention, or other. These domains are reviewer evidence tags, not validated diagnoses.

A separate redaction auditor and clinician audit `max(50,20%)` of packets, all `K_CLOSED`, and all packets sampled into the every-reviewer overlap. Target-plan leakage, removal of material non-target context, or inconsistent typed masking is measured with exact one-sided 95% bounds. Redactor identity is randomized across packets and hidden from reviewers. The route analysis is blocked if the upper bound for either target leakage or material context deletion exceeds 2%, or if randomized redactor-specific `K` rates differ by at least 0.05 with a simultaneous interval excluding zero.

### Panel P: atomic plan evidence, not a route vote

Panel P receives the operative radiology unit abstraction and the unredacted canonical `DS` only if `DS_store<=D`. It does not receive structured context fields, `G/H/V`, Panel C labels, eventual notes, POE, later events, comparator results, or intervention language. For every unit it marks exact `DS` spans and independently records target linkage, active versus historical/conditional status, action/modality, due interval/date, accountable recipient or scheduled next step if stated, explicit rationale, and explicit target-linked informed non-pursuit. Reviewers do not answer “superseded,” “safe,” “appropriate,” “copy,” “automate,” or “reconcile.”

A frozen deterministic crosswalk produces:

* `P_F`: same target, active chest/thoracic CT action, and compatible exact interval/date;
* `P_0`: no target-specific active action span, including generic “PCP follow-up” alone;
* `P_PART`: target-linked text exists but action, timing, responsibility, active status, or relation is incomplete;
* `P_CON`: target-linked modality/timing conflicts or is less protective, or target-linked plans conflict, without explicit text establishing a replacement;
* `P_EQ`: explicit executable same-or-earlier compatible future action for the same target, with concrete timing/date and accountable recipient or documented scheduling;
* `P_DEC`: explicit target-linked informed non-pursuit or target-linked goals-of-care decision;
* `P_DEF`: explicit target-linked definitive diagnostic/therapeutic management documented as replacing interval surveillance;
* `P_UN`: linkage, plan meaning, timing, activity, extraction, or unit alignment cannot be determined.

These are text-evidence relations, not judgments of guideline appropriateness or verified execution. A vague specialty mention is `P_PART/P_UN`; an unlinked order is insufficient; scheduled compatible CT with timing and responsibility is `P_EQ`; unexplained incompatible timing is `P_CON`. A plan first stored after `D`, later CT, POE row, later death, or outside-care inference cannot upgrade a state.

### Reviewer uncertainty is retained

For each task preserve all initial labels, spans, confidence, packet version, reviewer identity, elapsed time, and anchor/duplicate status. Consensus is secondary. For admission `i`, construct all logically compatible worlds from the three initial Panel U inventories, three initial Panel C labels, and three initial Panel P labels. Unknown labels map to defer; they are not favorably imputed. A third-party adjudicator may add a label and rationale but cannot narrow the primary set.

The frozen route program is run in every compatible world. Let `Omega_i` be the resulting set of admission routes. A **robust route core** exists only when `Omega_i={r}`. A singleton produced only after consensus is not robust. This makes the clinically consequential uncertainty directly auditable rather than summarized by kappa.

## Conservative admission-level route program

For each unit in an admission whose index lies in `E_A`:

* `U_RESOLVED` for `P_F`, `P_EQ`, `P_DEC`, or `P_DEF`;
* `U_PURE` for `K_OPEN + P_0`, a schema-complete unconditional source payload, known `G_u>=24h`, and no unit-boundary uncertainty;
* `U_RECON` for `K_OPEN + P_PART/P_CON` with known `G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING` for otherwise unresolved `K_OPEN + P_0/P_PART/P_CON` with `G_u<24h`;
* `U_CLOSED` for `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER` for `K_UNKNOWN`, `P_UN`, unresolved clock, `U_BOUNDARY`, unreadable content, or any decision-changing initial-reviewer disagreement.

Aggregate by worst-case admission precedence:

1. `A_DEFER` if any compatible world contains `U_DEFER`, or if `Omega_i` is not singleton. A named subset `A_CONTEXT_UNSUPPORTED` is added below.
2. `A_TIMING` if any unit is `U_TIMING` and none is defer.
3. `A_RECON` if at least one unit is `U_RECON` or active units interact in a way not representable by independent payloads. `A_MIXED` is the subset containing at least one `U_PURE` and at least one reconciliation unit; it is never propagation.
4. `A_AUTO` only if at least one unit is `U_PURE`, every other unit is reproducibly `U_PURE` or `U_RESOLVED/U_CLOSED`, all unit inventories agree, all unresolved-unit clocks are known and at least 24 hours, and no cross-unit target/action/timing conflict exists. This nominates only clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation of exact source attributes; it never authorizes silent insertion.
5. `A_NOACTION` if every unit is reproducibly resolved or closed.

A duplicate recommendation, majority of pure units, favorable consensus, or later faithful artifact cannot move an admission into `A_AUTO`.

## Supported clinical-context envelope

Context heterogeneity is handled by scoped support, not by assuming equal route rates. Before labels are opened, lock these marginal context dimensions from available fields: age (`18–44`, `45–64`, `65–79`, `>=80`), gender as represented, admission type family, ICU exposure, locked length-of-stay quartile based on the outcome-blind `N_A` distribution, terminal-service family, discharge-location family, recommendation interval (`3/6/12` months), and unit multiplicity (`1/>1`). Missing or contradictory structured values form explicit unknown levels.

A context level is **supported** for a route only if it contains at least 30 independently reviewed `E_A` admissions, at least 10 robust events for that route, at least two reviewers from each relevant pool, and estimable route-specific disagreement. These are information-support requirements, not statements that sparse patients are unsuitable. An admission is in route `r`'s supported envelope only if every one of its observed marginal levels is supported for `r`; otherwise its retrospective route is reported but becomes `A_CONTEXT_UNSUPPORTED` for prospective nomination. No cross-product cell is silently pooled to overcome sparsity.

For each adequately sized level, report route burden and consequential-disagreement intervals. Different route burdens across contexts are allowed and may be clinically informative; a route fails robustness only if its definition/reliability changes, compatible assignments cross routes, or its prospective nomination reverses when a supported level or reviewer is omitted. Rare or unsupported contexts remain explicit deferrals and require new data.

## Primary route-level estimands

The all-opportunity denominator remains `N_A`; `E_A` and route denominators are always displayed. Subject is the resampling cluster.

1. **Robust route burdens:** `B_r=100*n(i in E_A, Omega_i={r}, i in supported envelope for r)/N_A` for `r in {A_AUTO,A_RECON,A_NOACTION,A_TIMING}`. Report `A_MIXED`, `A_DEFER`, and `A_CONTEXT_UNSUPPORTED` separately. All `N_A` chronology and route cells, including zero/unknown/non-`E_A`, must sum to `N_A`.
2. **Robust coverage:** `C_ROB=n(i in E_A, |Omega_i|=1)/n(E_A)` and route-specific supported coverage. This is identifiability, not accuracy.
3. **Consequential route uncertainty:** `U_CROSS=n(i in E_A: Omega_i intersects more than one of {AUTO,RECON,NOACTION/TIMING,DEFER})/n(E_A)`. Also report `U_AUTO`, the fraction of admissions with `AUTO in Omega_i` whose set includes any non-auto route, and `U_RECON` analogously. Under the primary conservative output these admissions defer; the estimands reveal what consensus would have hidden.
4. **Mixed-admission diagnostic:** preserve `D_MIX=P(A_RECON or A_DEFER | at least one reviewer-compatible unit-level pure omission)`, with unit-boundary and reviewer bounds. It diagnoses why recommendation-unit-only propagation is unsafe without assigning a patient-harm utility.
5. **Reviewer calibration:** task- and route-specific initial confusion matrices; raw agreement; positive and negative agreement for `AUTO` and `RECON`; kappa and Gwet AC1; intra-reviewer duplicate agreement; drift-anchor rule compliance; reviewer-specific robust-route rates; and crossed subject/reviewer variance sensitivity. Kappa is never treated as truth.
6. **Context robustness:** route burden, `U_CROSS`, unknown/defer rate, and route-specific agreement for every supported marginal level; standardized descriptive rates using the frozen `N_A` context distribution; leave-one-supported-level-out and leave-one-reviewer-out results. Heterogeneous burden is not a failure if route semantics and uncertainty remain stable.
7. **Route reclassification:** full matrices from inherited `Y/Z` to robust route, consensus route, and `Omega_i`; recommendation-unit workload per 100 `N_A`, unit-count distribution, and intraclass correlation. Units are never analyzed as independent patients.

There is no `RD_ROUTE` lane-dominance rule, decision curve, scalar net benefit, or MIMIC-derived utility. `AUTO` and `RECON` qualify independently. A two-lane prospective silent study is nominated when both qualify; one-lane validation when only one qualifies; no route study when neither qualifies. Workload counts are reported transparently for external governance but do not override reliability.

## Inference and predeclared qualification gates

Use a fixed-seed subject-cluster bootstrap with at least 10,000 replicates. Every replicate carries complete `Y/J_A` chronology, all reviewer worlds, min/max unit inventories, supported-context membership, robust routes, route uncertainty, calibration diagnostics, and institutional/comparator controls. Use a crossed subject-and-reviewer bootstrap as sensitivity. One max-statistic simultaneous 95% family covers `B_AUTO`, `B_RECON`, `B_MIXED`, `B_DEFER`, `B_CONTEXT_UNSUPPORTED`, `C_ROB`, `U_CROSS`, `U_AUTO`, `U_RECON`, `D_MIX`, the route-specific supported-level reliability measures, and retained availability contrasts. Pointwise intervals are descriptive only.

Before unblinding route outcomes, clinical governance must ratify the following as measurement/feasibility thresholds rather than utilities. Without ratification, results remain descriptive and cannot nominate a lane.

Global execution gates:

* `N_A>=200`, `U_R<=5%`, `U_D<=5%`, no duplicate canonical `DS`, no denominator/hash drift, and at least 100 admissions in `E_A`;
* redaction error upper one-sided 95% bound `<=2%` for both leakage and material context deletion;
* at least 90% raw agreement and AC1 or kappa `>=0.70` for eligibility, unit boundary, `K`, atomic plan attributes, and final derived route; show both coefficients when prevalence is extreme;
* at least 90% intra-reviewer duplicate agreement, at least 95% drift-anchor rule compliance, and no monotone calibration deterioration across review batches;
* `A_DEFER` from decision-changing disagreement/unknown evidence plus `U_BOUNDARY` no greater than 10% of `E_A`, with its simultaneous upper bound reported; consensus cannot repair a failure.

A particular route qualifies for prospective silent validation only if:

* at least 40 context-supported robust-core admissions have `Omega_i={r}` and at least 10 occur in each context level for which generalization is claimed;
* its all-`N_A` burden has simultaneous interval half-width no greater than 3 admissions per 100 `N_A`, and route-specific consequential-disagreement intervals have half-width no greater than 10 percentage points;
* for `A_AUTO`, the simultaneous upper bound for `U_AUTO` is at most 5%; for `A_RECON`, the analogous upper bound for a non-reconciliation compatible route is at most 10%. These are preapproved route-identification tolerances, not estimates of harm or benefit;
* every initial reviewer, min/max unit inventory, confidence-tier sensitivity, and supported-context leave-one-level-out analysis retains a nonzero robust burden and the same qualify/do-not-qualify conclusion;
* 12-hour and 72-hour `G_u` sensitivities are directionally compatible. The 24-hour definition remains primary; sensitivities cannot add rows to it.

If a supported level has genuinely different route burden but passes semantic reliability, report heterogeneity and scope the prospective study rather than demanding equality. If a level is sparse or unreliable, exclude it only from the *nomination envelope*, never from `N_A` or descriptive results. Event or width failure is inconclusive, not zero burden.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive for prospective silent validation of the two-stage gate** means at least one route has a nonzero context-supported robust core; all source, redaction, chronology, calibration, route-specific reliability, uncertainty, event, and precision gates pass; and qualification survives every initial-reviewer, unit-boundary, reviewer, confidence, and supported-context perturbation. This supports silent testing of the frozen extractor and route only in the explicitly supported envelope.

**Supportive for the clinician-confirmed structured-prepopulation lane** requires `A_AUTO` to qualify independently, including at least 40 robust supported events and `U_AUTO` upper bound `<=5%`. It supports a silent prediction and later mandatory clinician accept/modify/reject evaluation; it does not support insertion, guideline correctness, or safety.

**Supportive for the reconciliation lane** requires `A_RECON` to qualify independently, including robust conflicts/incomplete plans and its route-uncertainty bound. A high reproducible `A_MIXED` burden is supportive of admission-level reconciliation over unit-only copying. If both lanes qualify, nominate a two-lane silent study with lane-specific endpoints; route prevalence does not choose a winner.

**Adverse to propagation-route identifiability** is common reviewer-compatible crossing from auto to reconciliation/resolution/defer, redaction-sensitive context labels, unsupported-context concentration, or mixed-unit aggregation that removes the robust auto core despite adequate events and precision. **Adverse to reconciliation-route identifiability** is failure to reproducibly distinguish incomplete/conflicting text from resolution or unknown despite adequate data. **Adverse to broad generalization** is reliable routing only in a narrow supported envelope; this can still support a scoped study. A comparator showing a generic process is adverse only to pulmonary specificity.

**Inconclusive** includes inadequate `N_A/E_A`, fewer than 40 robust route events, wide simultaneous intervals, poor duplicate/anchor/inter-reviewer reliability, more than 10% decision-changing defer/unknown burden, unit-boundary instability, redaction leakage, reviewer or context leave-out reversal, unsupported context, source/key/clock failure, or 12/72-hour sensitivity reversal. Sparse contexts and routes remain visible and are not interpreted as absent need. Failure of comparator calibration makes specificity inconclusive only.

## What can and cannot be concluded

MIMIC can compute exact source and chronology diagnostics; frozen opportunity, recommendation-unit, and admission counts; departure-frozen text-evidence states; route sets under observed reviewer uncertainty; route-specific robust cores; context support; reliability, workload, mixed-admission, and storage-calibration estimands. An automatic verifier can check source/key/time invariants, packet prohibitions and hashes, deterministic crosswalks, preservation of every reviewer world, route aggregation, resampling, interval logic, and whether reported conclusions match supportive/adverse/inconclusive gates.

Clinical adjudication is still required for spans and text-evidence labels. Neither reviewers nor an automatic verifier can establish guideline appropriateness, complete goals/preferences/prognosis, true report visibility, author awareness, UI exposure, responsibility, outside plans, communication, linked ordering/scheduling/completion, malignancy, harm, utilities, intervention effects, or benefit. The canonical stored `DS` is not a complete contemporaneous chart, and `G` is storage-to-storage rather than a measured editing window.

A supportive result therefore advances the decision bridge in one specific way: it freezes a route whose *retrospective text definition* is reproducible across observed reviewers and supported clinical contexts, making prospective silent validation scientifically warranted. That prospective study must newly collect immutable report-finalization/addendum availability, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission events, route predictions, clinician accept/modify/reject and reasons, accountable team, target-linked order/referral/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing risks, communication receipt, and follow-up. Only a later governed route-stratified trial could test workflow effects or safety.

## Change from the parent and remaining uncertainty

This child preserves the parent's frozen first-eligible all-opportunity admission denominator, reciprocal pre-departure report/addendum chronology, unique canonical `DS`, complete `Y` and `V/H/Z` states, corrected `G=DS_store-R_store`, unit-specific clocks, conservative admission routing, institutional/comparator calibration, source provenance, and strict noncausal limits. It makes a substantive repair by replacing relative route prevalence and a single purity margin with independently qualifying route-level robust cores; separating radiology unit, redacted context, and atomic plan-evidence tasks; using three-reviewer compatible route sets rather than consensus; adding blinded duplicates, rule anchors, drift monitoring, reviewer overlap, randomized redaction audits, and context-supported nomination envelopes; and carrying route-specific uncertainty through simultaneous inference and explicit stop gates.

Uncertainty remains fundamental. Full discharge context can still omit outside plans or undocumented preferences; typed redaction can distort meaning; text-rule anchors measure protocol adherence rather than clinical truth; supported marginal context does not guarantee support for every intersection; and demanding robust cores may defer many clinically important admissions. Those are transparent limitations. This experiment asks whether a carefully scoped route is reproducible enough for the next silent study, not whether the route is clinically correct or ready to deploy.
