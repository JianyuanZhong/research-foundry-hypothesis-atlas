- q: the fraction of the full patient roster whose within-patient requirement is met; and
- p in [0,1): the stakeholder threshold probability for the same K-slot documentary-M2 review action.

The clinical research question is:

> For which patient-balanced stored-content availability levels (α,q), and for which stakeholder preference thresholds p, does locked semantic G universally beat no review in every retained source/reader/deletion state while also retaining the parent's strictly greater-than-5-per-100-slot closure against B, R and same-fit Gmask and enrichment over capacity-matched random K?

## 3. Estimand, hypothesis and exact joint frontier

For year y, source state, reader pair, full-roster state and anchored deletion configuration z, let I_z be the retained patient roster, N_z=|I_z|, E_z the number of documentary-M2 cases, K the frozen quota, h_M(z,r) the number of positive outcomes among policy M's exact top K under whole-accession mask r, and f_M=K-h_M. The mask is applied before scoring exactly as in the parent: exposed units provide their exact stored objects, hidden units receive the development-defined unavailable block; B and Z remain fixed.

For p in [0,1),
NB_G(p;z,r) = {h_G(z,r)-[K-h_G(z,r)]p/(1-p)}/N_z
             = {h_G(z,r)-Kp}/[N_z(1-p)].
Therefore G strictly beats no review exactly when p<h_G/K. Equality is not support. Random K enrichment is
epsilon_rand(z,r)=(N_z h_G-K E_z)/(K N_z),
so it is positive exactly when N_z h_G>K E_z, independent of p. For each equal-K comparator C in {B,R,Gmask}, retain the clinical margin
100[h_G(z,r)-h_C(z,r)]/K>5,
equivalently 20[h_G-h_C]>K as an integer predicate.

### 3.1 Exact patient-balanced mask family

For each full-roster patient i, let E_i be the inherited ordered set of eligible radiology accessions/forced-zero pseudo-units, n_i=|E_i|, and let r_u in {0,1} expose or hide the complete accession object u. Let m_i(r)=sum over u in E_i of r_u.

A nonempty accession is α-adequately exposed when
s_i^α(r)=1{n_i>0 and m_i(r)>=ceil(α n_i)}.
Patients with no eligible unit remain in the denominator and have s_i^α=0, including at α=0. A mask belongs to the exact family R_z(α,q) when
sum over i in I_z of s_i^α(r) >= ceil(q N_z),
with all blank, nonreversible, contaminated or source-unavailable units forced to r_u=0. This is patient-balanced because the patient, not the row or accession, is the unit in the q denominator, and because an accession is indivisible.

The old 80/80 contract is exactly the single descriptive point (α,q)=(4/5,4/5), with the same ceiling conventions. It is not a minimum requirement in this child. The family includes all binary whole-accession masks satisfying the displayed integer constraints; it is not a random-access or report-visibility model.

Only finitely many availability equivalence classes need to be enumerated:
A_z={0} union {k/n_i: n_i>0, 1<=k<=n_i}, and
Q_z={0} union {k/N_z: 0<=k<=N_z}.
For α, these representatives produce every possible value of ceil(α n_i); for q, they produce every possible value of ceil(q N_z). Report the union across retained states, with the exact rational representative and integer ceilings, rather than an arbitrary decimal grid. An interval between adjacent representatives has the same family and is recorded as one equivalence class. If a real external availability target falls in an interval, use its exact ceiling constraints, not interpolation.

The family is nested in the only sense used here: if α' >= α and q' >= q, then R_z(α',q') is a subset of R_z(α,q). This follows directly from nondecreasing ceilings and the patient-count inequality. Consequently, when both families are nonempty, minima over the higher-availability family cannot be lower merely because the feasible mask set grew: the sharp worst-case p frontier, comparator margins and random-enrichment minimum are all minima over a subset. This is a statement about nested universal quantification, not an assumption that G's score or performance is biologically or operationally monotone with visibility. No cross-coordinate tradeoff, smoothness or interpolation is assumed.

### 3.2 Sharp frontier and joint support predicate

For each test year y and availability pair (α,q), minimize over every retained source state, reader pair, full roster, anchored deletion and r in R_z(α,q):
P_y(α,q)=min_z min_r h_G(z,r)/K,
D_C,y(α,q)=min_z min_r 100[h_G(z,r)-h_C(z,r)]/K,
E_y(α,q)=min_z min_r (N_z h_G(z,r)-K E_z)/(K N_z).
These are attained finite-corpus extrema. Store the complete minimizing state/mask witness, integer numerator, denominator and raw backpointers.

A pair is comparator-closed and enrichment-closed exactly when
C_y(α,q) = [D_B,y>5] and [D_R,y>5] and [D_Gmask,y>5] and [E_y>0].
The joint, year-specific support set is
F_y={ (α,q,p): every required R_z(α,q) is nonempty, C_y(α,q), and 0<=p<P_y(α,q) }.
The cross-year experiment reports F_both=F_2020 intersect F_2021, using the same (α,q,p) and requiring closure in both years. Its sharp preference frontier is P_both(α,q)=min(P_2020,P_2021) only at pairs where both year families are nonempty; otherwise the pair is structurally unavailable, not supportive.

For rational p=a/b in lowest terms, the no-review predicate is the exact integer inequality
b h_G(z,r)>a K.
A witness with b h_G<=a K falsifies universal no-review dominance at that (α,q,p). The boundary p=P_y is adverse to strict support when attained, while every rational p below it passes the no-review part if the other closure predicates pass. When P_y=0, no nonnegative p is supported at that availability pair. The former 10/15/20/25/30% points are optional descriptive landmarks only.

The set F_both is the exact sharp frontier for this finite intervention. A conservative frontier may be reported for an external policy that supplies only lower bounds α>=α0 and q>=q0: by the proved family inclusion, support at (α0,q0,p) is sufficient for every higher pair whose families are nonempty. It is conservative if an external policy has additional restrictions not represented in R. It is not valid to infer support at a lower pair from a higher pair, or to infer monotone behavior of an individual reader/model score.

### 3.3 Falsification and attained witnesses

The primary threshold-independent hypothesis, separately in 2020 and 2021, is that the joint frontier is nonempty and that every claimed point on it satisfies all three comparator margins and positive random enrichment. It is falsified by one attained state/mask with any of:

- 20(h_G-h_C)<=K for B, R or Gmask;
- N_z h_G<=K E_z;
- b h_G<=aK for the claimed preference threshold p=a/b;
- an empty required mask family being incorrectly represented as support;
- any violation of fixed K, shared world/roster/deletion, or exact top-K tie rules.

A negative result at all-exposable content (1,1) is maximum-content-signal-adverse; passage at all content but failure at some attainable lower pair is visibility-brittle-signal-adverse. Failure only at a stakeholder p boundary is preference-range-adverse, not a comparator failure. No universal claim is made from an average mask, median patient, favorable reader, or one test year.

A supportive result establishes only universal finite-corpus dominance over the specified equal-K alternatives, positive random-K enrichment, and the exact availability-dependent preference set. Results are disjoint: a lower-pair failure is not hidden by a supportive higher pair, and an empty external range is not relabeled computational support.
