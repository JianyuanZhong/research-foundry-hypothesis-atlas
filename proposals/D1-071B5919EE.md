## Hypothesis to test

When oxygenation is already adequate, the risk associated with sustained hyperoxia exposure differs according to pulmonary status.

## Study population and main data

Patients with complete blood-gas and FiO2 records.

## Suggested approach

Align blood gases, FiO2, and respiratory support in time and stratify by pulmonary disease.

## Scientific significance

Inform stratification in oxygen-therapy trials.

## Main limitations

SpO2 near 100% cannot reliably quantify hyperoxemia.
