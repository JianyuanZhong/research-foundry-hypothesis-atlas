The consequential but bounded question is:

> If exactly K=.10 of the frozen reference cohort were offered for documentary review 24 hours before operation, how large would a lower bound on complete eligible report-bundle exposure have to be for G to retain its capture advantage over B, R, Gmask, and capacity-matched random selection, regardless of which patients and accessions satisfy that bound?

This is useful because it yields an auditable requirement for a later report-archive study without pretending that HCC already contains the archive or workflow evidence. It remains a retrospective documentary allocation experiment and cannot recommend resection margin, transplantation, ablation, systemic treatment, surveillance, or any other clinical action.

The confirmatory falsifiable hypothesis is:

> At rho=.10 and d=24 hours, maximum stored content passes every inherited comparator, random, and anchored whole-patient deletion gate separately in 2020 and 2021, and q_req<1, where q_req is the smallest complete-patient report-bundle coverage lower bound for which those same gates pass in every compatible coupled source, accession-identity, monotone-onset, radiology-reader, pathology-reader, and deletion world.

No clinically important minimum reserve is invented; report q_req and 1-q_req exactly. A requirement close to one may be operationally unattractive, but HCC cannot decide that.

## 6. Monotone whole-accession onset states

Let decision cutoffs be ordered 72, 48, 24, and 12 hours before operation. For patient i, source/operation world omega, cutoff d, and whole accession u, define E_iomega,d,u=1 only if the operation state is valid, u is source-clean and nonblank, CT/MRI membership is valid, and the acquisition interval from examinations.开始时间 lies wholly within [t_iomega,d minus 90 days, t_iomega,d). Otherwise E=0. This enforces acquisition before exposure.

For each eligible complete source version assign one onset category:

- O=0: technically eligible no later than t72;
- O=1: first technically eligible in (t72,t48];
- O=2: first technically eligible in (t48,t24];
- O=3: first technically eligible in (t24,t12];
- O=4: not technically eligible by t12 or only later/never.

The induced visibility vector in 72/48/24/12 order is V(O)=(1{O=0},1{O<=1},1{O<=2},1{O<=3}), and exposure is M_iomega,d,u=E_iomega,d,u V_d(O_iomega,u). One onset belongs to the whole source-matched version, never a phrase or component. The vector is monotone, while cutoff-specific E prevents exposure before acquisition.

HCC contains no observation of O. Therefore the HCC-only state set is {0,1,2,3,4} for every otherwise source-clean accession. These are hypothetical content-withholding states, not imputed release times. An external interval audit retains all contiguous compatible categories; it never substitutes a midpoint. ## 8. Clean HCC estimand: patient-complete bundle coverage requirement

For each primary year and world, define the 24-hour eligible report bundle

A_iomega={u: E_iomega,24,u=1}.

Let I_iomega=1{|A_iomega|>0}. Patients with I=0 remain in N and in every top-K analysis, but are excluded from the report-availability denominator. Report both N and n_A,omega=sum_i I_iomega. This separates observable acquisition eligibility from unobserved report availability.

Define patient-complete exposure

C_iomega(O)=1{I_iomega=1 and sum_{u in A_iomega} M_iomega,24,u=|A_iomega|}.

Thus a patient counts as covered only when every source-clean eligible whole accession in their 24-hour bundle is exposed. Multirow accessions remain indivisible. Define

Q_omega,a(O)=sum_{i in year a} C_iomega(O) / n_A,omega,a.

Patients not counted complete may have any partial bundle, including none; all such identities and subsets remain in the adversarial state set. The contract Q>=q therefore cannot choose favorable patients or ignore harmful partial exposure.

Let R be the finite exact union of rational thresholds k/n_A,omega,a over every retained primary year/source world, including 0 and 1. For q in R, let Z(q) contain every complete coupled state satisfying Q_omega,2020>=q and Q_omega,2021>=q in its own denominator. Define PASS(q)=1 only if every comparator, random, deletion, reader/pathology, operation/source, and onset state in Z(q) passes. Because Z(q2) is a subset of Z(q1) when q2>q1, PASS is monotone even though individual scores need not improve when more reports are exposed.

If the maximum-content state fails, q_req is undefined. Otherwise define

q_req=min{q in R: PASS(q)=1}

and robustness reserve r_bundle=1-q_req.

Compute q_req by exact branch-and-bound, multi-valued decision diagrams, or complete enumeration. Cache only states with identical exact score bits, eligibility, complete-bundle indicators, endpoint terminals, and cutoff outputs, with reversible raw hashes. Emit the attained q_req, the immediately lower failing threshold q_prev when one exists, and raw worst witnesses at both boundaries. No interpolated percentile or favorable attained mask is allowed.

This replaces the parents' alpha-by-q accession-fraction frontier and deletes p, p_safe, and all “preference” labels. HCC does not identify a utility exchange rate. Capture counts, noncapture counts K-H_G, and all comparator margins remain descriptive documentary outputs, not utilities.

The 12/48/72-hour results use the same onset assignments and report patient-complete trajectory coverage at those times. They may reveal temporal fragility but cannot veto or rescue the 24-hour result.

## 9. Exact primary interpretation

Apply this one-hot precedence to the HCC experiment:

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: any source replay, state generation, exact ranking, branch-and-bound certificate, quotient replay, boundary witness, or verification requirement fails.
2. `feasibility_inconclusive`: computation is complete but any locked N, K, or event gate fails.
3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse`: the all-source-clean-content state fails any 24-hour comparator, random, or singleton-deletion gate in either 2020 or 2021. This falsifies the prespecified locked documentary ranking claim; an accidentally favorable withholding state cannot rescue that claim.
4. `complete_bundle_robustness_adverse`: maximum content passes, but q_req=1. The model has no guaranteed positive missing-bundle reserve under adversarial identities; this falsifies the confirmatory robustness hypothesis.
5. `hcc_documentary_robustness_supportive_bridge_pending`: maximum content passes, q_req<1, the immediately lower q_prev fails when it exists, and all exact witnesses verify. This supports only a positive finite-corpus content-withholding reserve and estimates the exact external coverage requirement. It does not support actual availability or clinical action.

These labels are mutually exclusive. A favorable sensitivity cutoff cannot alter them. Report q_req, r_bundle, n_A and N by year/world, H_G, K-H_G, every comparator/random margin, and attained worst witnesses. Variation over source, onset, reader, pathology, and deletion worlds is the uncertainty for the finite frozen corpus; no superpopulation claim is made. Population transport requires another study.

## 10. External bridges and their own disjoint interpretation

A report-archive bridge is required to turn q_req into evidence about technical 24-hour availability. It must be outcome-blind and provide the exact (患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号) crosswalk, source row ordinals and hashes, immutable version and predecessor/amendment identifiers, complete-version hashes, authored/finalized/released/ingested/retrievable/readable/retracted times, timezone/clock provenance, operation-clock validation, and scoring-pipeline input hash. Technical readability means retrievable by the locked pipeline; it does not mean viewed.

A census may identify the exact onset assignment. A probability audit must retain nonresponders and supply a simultaneous design-based lower confidence bound q_L for the same patient-complete denominator in each year/world. Since HCC minima already range over all identities and partial bundles, q_L>=q_req is sufficient for the coverage contract without knowing favorable identities, provided every accession/version/hash and denominator rule matches. An aggregate accession percentage, marginal timestamp histogram, or patient coverage estimate with unmatched identities is insufficient.

Archive-stage labels are separate and one-hot:

1. `archive_inconclusive`: crosswalk, version, clock, denominator, sampling, or simultaneous-bound validation fails.
2. `coverage_contract_not_established`: a valid audit has q_L<q_req; this does not prove actual policy failure.
3. `audited_documentary_adverse`: a census or exact compatible audit state directly fails a primary fixed-K gate.
4. `externally_coverage_bridged_documentary_supportive`: a valid census passes directly, or a valid simultaneous q_L>=q_req places every compatible state inside the passing set.

Even the last label is not action support. A separate prospective silent-mode workflow study must measure when reports are actually available to the intended users, clinician viewing and action, true staffing/capacity and competing workload, delays and failures, downstream decisions, harms, costs, and patient outcomes. Stakeholders must define utilities or preferences using those outcomes. Until those data exist, set `clinical_action_status=not_identified`; do not derive a preference threshold from H_G/K or from the analytic K.

Pathology specimen/slide linkage and adequate sampling, biological MVI validity, procedure/treatment dictionaries, Mandarin semantics, radiologist/pathologist qualifications, and operation clocks require expert adjudication. Transportability, treatment effects, safety, recurrence, survival, and patient benefit require external validation or another study.
