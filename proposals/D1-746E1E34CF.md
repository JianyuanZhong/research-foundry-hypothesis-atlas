# A temporally separated, route-concealed longitudinal falsification layer for departure recommendations

## Decision question and targeted repair

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It does not reopen the clinical question or alter the inherited estimand. The frozen decision remains whether any exact MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity atom may be nominated only for a prospective, within-site **silent** validation of the inherited `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` routes.

The parent materially repairs textual reference validity: two roster-disjoint panels independently identify target-grounded firm-active or firm-incomplete departure text and distinguish it from tentative, completed, superseded, inactive, conflicting, and unknown states. The remaining consequential weakness is longitudinal reference validity. Even a route-concealed, independently labeled firm-active departure sentence can be challenged by a later record that explicitly says that the action had already been completed, cancelled, replaced, or was not the plan at departure. Conversely, a later action record may simply document appropriate evolution after departure. Later MIMIC records therefore cannot be treated as clinical truth, and no post-departure absence can mean completion, failure, adherence, or no need. The addition below makes those two possibilities computable without using future information to construct the opportunity, route, or original label.

The falsifiable claim is deliberately narrow: among parent-challenged units called `R_FIRM_ACTIVE` or `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE`, the rate of **temporally valid, target-linked retrospective contradiction at departure** is sufficiently low for the inherited atom-specific release gates. The experiment does not test whether an action was clinically correct, whether it was viewed, assigned, communicated, ordered, scheduled, performed, beneficial, safe, or needed. A later target-linked trace after departure is a trace of documentation, not a gold-standard outcome.

The strongest claim already supported by the parent is only that, in the supplied pre-departure artifacts, a target-grounded future-directed textual action is not contradicted by the permitted pre-`D` evidence under adverse-compatible independent review. This child tests whether a later, temporally explicit artifact exposes a departure-time contradiction that the pre-departure artifacts did not reveal. It cannot upgrade the parent label; it can only falsify or leave it unresolved.

* reciprocal pre-departure radiology report/addendum reconstruction, one exact canonical `note_type==DS` artifact per admission, and `D=dischtime`;

* enumeration of every recommendation unit, inherited route precedence, all-unit worst-case admission routing, and no top-up, replacement, atom merge, post-label enrichment, or route-triggered verification;

* `POE` and `POE_DETAIL` remain prohibited from population construction, packets, labels, references, routes, and gates;

The longitudinal layer adds no sampled admission. It is generated for every admission in the inherited actionability challenge, normally 300 and never more than 300. If the inherited challenge is smaller, it uses all challenged admissions and reports the limitation. There is no adaptive future-record sampling.

## Presealed longitudinal ledger and strict separation

The parent’s route, atom, recommendation-unit, R1/R2 label, and canonical-DS seals are completed first. Independently, before any route, stratum, panel label, or release gate is joined, a longitudinal program reads the complete source rows and writes a hash-sealed ledger keyed only by `(subject_id, index_hadm_id, unit_id)`. The ledger contains source identifiers, times, deterministic target-link features, censoring flags, and evidence-class codes, never public note text. A second process verifies the ledger against source hashes and only then joins the parent’s identifiers and sampling weights. Future evidence cannot be used to define eligibility, `R_store,u`, `D`, `Y,V,H,Z`, support atoms, route, reference packets, or sample inclusion.

The index episode is the inherited first eligible admission and its `D=dischtime`. For any source row:

* `charttime` describes the documented clinical-time field and `storetime` the repository-time field; both are retained separately.
* A row with `charttime<=D` and `storetime>D` is a **delayed index artifact**, not post-departure care. It is assigned `DELAYED_INDEX_ARTIFACT` and never establishes later completion, failure, or contradiction.
* A row with `charttime>D` and `storetime>D` can enter the future ledger only if its admission and subject linkage pass the rules below. A row with an impossible or unresolved clock ordering is `CLOCK_UNKNOWN`, not favorable evidence.
* The index canonical DS, its detail rows, and all reciprocal pre-`D` radiology rows remain in the parent layer only. A duplicate note identity is not counted twice merely because it appears in both a primary and detail file.

The primary future windows are relative, not calendar claims: `(D,D+30 days]`, `(D+30,D+90 days]`, and `(D+90,D+365 days]`, using the de-identified within-subject timestamps as recorded. The windows are not allowed to redefine the inherited possible-calendar class. A source-wide maximum date is only an administrative extraction boundary, never evidence of complete follow-up. If the source horizon truncates a window, the window is `ADMINISTRATIVELY_CENSORED`; missingness widens the compatible set.

## Recurrent admissions, death, and observation windows

All later admissions of the same subject are retained in chronological order by `admittime`; there is no “first readmission” shortcut. A later admission has a different `(subject_id,hadm_id)` and must satisfy `admittime>D` for ordinary future-care linkage. Its radiology or discharge rows are linked through that later `hadm_id`, then checked for `charttime>D` and `storetime>D`. Same-index rows after `D` are retained only as delayed or post-discharge documentation and are never silently treated as external care. Repeated reports within a later admission are clustered at the index admission for inference, while all qualifying rows remain auditable. A later admission cannot enter the index denominator or be used to manufacture a second opportunity.

Death is handled as censoring, not as an outcome for the recommendation. The conservative administrative censor time is the earliest available of `patients.dod`, a nonempty index `deathtime`, or an explicit death time in a later linked admission, with date-only `dod` represented as an interval rather than a falsely precise timestamp. `hospital_expire_flag==1` means that the post-departure window may be structurally unobservable; it does not mean the recommendation was completed or failed. Future windows ending at death are `DEATH_CENSORED`; windows after death are not denominators for a later trace. If no death or later care is recorded, absence is `NO_OBSERVATION`, not no event. There is no inverse-probability claim that unobserved community care was absent.

The administrative endpoint is therefore an observation-window descriptor, not a “truth” date. The analysis reports window-specific observation opportunity, death censoring, administrative censoring, and unknown target linkage separately. It never imputes a favorable outcome from surviving, a readmission from not being readmitted, or completion from no later note.

## Target linkage and evidence classes

Target linkage is deterministic and presealed. A later text span is linked to an index recommendation unit only when it independently matches the unit’s frozen target fingerprint using the same conservative fields retained by the parent: anatomy and laterality where present, measurement, modality, comparison, or an explicitly linked interval. Exact normalized sentence reuse alone is not sufficient. A same-subject later note with generic “follow-up” language, a mismatched side or measurement, or an unresolved target is `TARGET_UNKNOWN` and cannot be used as a favorable or adverse clinical label. `subject_id` and `hadm_id` joins are necessary but not sufficient.

Every candidate future trace receives exactly one of these mutually exclusive primary classes, with secondary provenance flags retained:

* `RETRO_PRE_D_CONTRADICTION`: a later-stored artifact explicitly and target-specifically states that, before or at `D`, the recommended action was already completed, cancelled, replaced, or not the plan, with a temporal statement that makes the departure-time interpretation testable. This is the only primary adverse endpoint. It challenges the textual departure reference but is not clinical truth.
* `POST_D_EVOLUTION`: a target-linked report, plan, or discharge artifact documents an action, changed interval, or new plan whose stated timing is after `D`, without claiming that the departure text was false. It is an expected longitudinal trace and is never counted as completion failure or adverse clinical outcome.
* `RETRO_SOURCE_CORRECTION_OR_SUPERSESSION`: later text explicitly dates an old plan’s change to pre-`D` or says the discharge plan was superseded before departure. This is included in `RETRO_PRE_D_CONTRADICTION` and separately tabulated for interpretability.
* `DELAYED_INDEX_ARTIFACT`: `charttime<=D<storetime`, or a lineage relation shows the row is an addendum/delayed storage of an index artifact. It cannot count as future care.
* `TARGET_UNKNOWN_OR_AMBIGUOUS`: a later row exists but target, timing, or note lineage is not sufficiently specific.
* `NO_OBSERVATION`: no qualifying source row was observed in the window. It is not completion, failure, adherence, resolution, or evidence that follow-up was unnecessary.
* `DEATH_CENSORED`, `ADMINISTRATIVELY_CENSORED`, or `CLOCK_UNKNOWN`: the window cannot support a definitive trace classification.

The classes are deliberately asymmetric. A later explicit statement can challenge the departure text only when it contains a target-linked temporal assertion about the pre-`D` state. A later performed study cannot prove the original plan was active, appropriate, or completed as intended. A later absence proves nothing.

## Route-concealed challenge and workload closure

The parent’s R1 and R2 labels remain unchanged and receive no future trace before their independently recorded compatible sets are sealed. After both labels are sealed, the route-concealed longitudinal ledger is joined for analysis. If a longitudinal human ambiguity is unavoidable, it is resolved using the parent’s already roster-disjoint R1/R2 packets and cannot trigger an additional admission, reviewer, or route-specific sample; unresolved ambiguity remains `TARGET_UNKNOWN_OR_AMBIGUOUS`. No reviewer sees an inherited route, atom, stratum, sampling weight, expected answer, or another reviewer’s label while making the parent label.

The workload is closed as follows:

* exactly the inherited challenge admissions are processed, at most 300;
* the parent’s two whole-admission readings remain exactly at most 600 total;
* the longitudinal ledger is a deterministic full-source computation for those admissions, not a third adaptive sample or an invitation to inspect only positive future traces;
* all later admissions and all qualifying rows for a challenged subject are scanned by the predeclared program, with no manual post hoc selection;
* no top-up, replacement, route-triggered verification, or late atom creation is allowed;
* if a source member, roster, target parser, ledger seal, or complete all-row scan is unavailable, the affected atom is `DEFER`/inconclusive rather than repaired by sampling a convenient subset.

This closes both human and computational workload. The source scan may be large, but its input members, output ledger, maximum challenged-admission key set, and hash checkpoint are declared before execution; there is no unbounded reviewer workload hidden behind the phrase “longitudinal review.”

## Estimands and adverse-compatible inference

For each inherited atom and route, estimate the conditional, design-weighted mass among parent-challenged units and admissions of:

1. `RETRO_PRE_D_CONTRADICTION` overall and by 30-, 90-, and 365-day temporal window of discovery;
2. target-linked retroactive completion, cancellation, replacement, and source-correction subclasses;
3. post-`D` evolution traces, delayed index artifacts, target-unknown traces, death censoring, administrative censoring, and no-observation;
4. the fraction for which a later trace is actually temporally capable of challenging departure status versus merely describing later evolution;
5. recurrent-admission count and within-subject dependence, without treating recurrence as outcome evidence.

The primary estimator retains the inherited conditional inclusion probabilities and joint admission clusters. Exact finite-population inversion and the parent’s 20,000 Rao-Wu replicates are extended to the new binary endpoint; all units and all longitudinal statuses from an admission stay together. The single inherited simultaneous 95% family includes the added endpoint and its controls. Lower bounds count only fully target-linked, temporally valid observations. Upper adverse bounds assign every ambiguous target, clock anomaly, missing ledger row, failed source join, nonresponse, and unresolved temporal assertion to `RETRO_PRE_D_CONTRADICTION` for release purposes, while reporting a separate compatible-set interval so this conservative assignment is not misdescribed as an observed event rate. `NO_OBSERVATION`, death censoring, and administrative censoring are never assigned to completion or favorable activity.

For an inherited `AUTO` atom, the longitudinal gate is an atom-specific simultaneous upper bound on `RETRO_PRE_D_CONTRADICTION` no greater than 0.05, in addition to every parent gate. Any single adverse-compatible unit with a pre-`D` contradiction blocks `AUTO` under inherited worst-unit admission precedence. For `RECON`, the corresponding upper bound is no greater than 0.10 and every unit must remain compatible with `R_FIRM_ACTIVE` or `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE`; a `RETRO_PRE_D_CONTRADICTION` still blocks that unit and may block the atom under worst-unit routing. `POST_D_EVOLUTION` is not treated as a failure and cannot be converted into completion, benefit, or harm. All sparse, unsupported, unscanned, censored, or wide-bound cases remain `DEFER`; pooled, neighboring-atom, recurrence, favorable absence, consensus, or model rescue is prohibited.

These numerical gates inherit the parent’s operational release framework; they are not clinical safety thresholds. Before compilation, the exact finite-population compatible-world calculation, endpoint denominator, and control multiplicity must be sealed. If the proposed bound cannot be computed with positive inclusion probabilities and complete admission clustering, the result is inconclusive and no atom is nominated.

## Falsification fixtures and leakage controls

The executable implementation must include at least these presealed fixtures:

1. a post-`D` target-linked study with an explicit later action is `POST_D_EVOLUTION`, not completion or failure;
2. a later note explicitly saying a matching study was completed **before discharge** is `RETRO_PRE_D_CONTRADICTION`, even if the note was stored later;
3. a later changed interval whose text dates the change after `D` is evolution, while one that dates the change before `D` is retroactive contradiction;
4. a later report with `charttime<=D<storetime` is a delayed index artifact, not future care;
5. a later record with mismatched laterality, measurement, or modality is target unknown, not a contradiction;
6. an exact copied sentence with preserved target grounding remains copy provenance, not automatic inactivity, while a later explicit pre-`D` cancellation is adverse;
7. two later admissions for one subject are both retained, but neither becomes a new index opportunity and neither is treated as independent of the index admission;
8. no later record, no next admission, and no post-departure DS is always `NO_OBSERVATION`, never completion, failure, or resolution;
9. death before a future window censors it, rather than producing a favorable or adverse label;
10. source-wide date truncation widens administrative-censoring bounds; raw shifted years never create a calendar claim;
11. a target-permutation control, a pre-`D` pseudo-future control, and a route-revelation-before-seal control fail closed if they produce a release-favorable pattern;
12. a late-arriving source row cannot alter the already sealed route or parent label; it can only appear in the future ledger with provenance and an adverse-compatible status.

A target-permutation control replaces an index target fingerprint with a distinct same-subject target while preserving note and time structure; it should not generate a release-favorable retroactive contradiction. A pre-`D` pseudo-future control treats an eligible pre-departure row as if it were future only in a diagnostic run; any apparent pass from this leakage test invalidates the implementation. A route-shuffle audit joins future ledgers to permuted route labels only after all seals; if results change materially when routes are revealed earlier, the run is invalid. Controls are computational checks, not clinical negative controls.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results

A supportive result requires every parent gate plus the new atom-specific temporal gate, complete ledger sealing, route concealment, positive inclusion probabilities, valid recurrent-admission clustering, and at least one fully supported exact atom. It supports only this statement: the available MIMIC artifacts contain a low adverse-compatible rate of explicitly target-linked statements that retrospectively contradict the departure-time textual status, while later evolution and absence remain correctly treated as non-truth. This supports nomination for a prospective within-site **silent** workflow validation only.

An adverse result is an atom-specific upper bound crossing its temporal gate, a single adverse-compatible pre-`D` contradiction under worst-unit precedence, target-link/control failure, clock or delayed-artifact misclassification, source-scan incompleteness, or evidence that route concealment occurred after future labels were known. It excludes that atom from nomination but does not establish clinical harm, negligence, inappropriate care, or actual recommendation failure.

An inconclusive result includes no positive-probability future observation, sparse target-linked traces, high target ambiguity, death or administrative censoring, missing later source members, incomplete recurrent-admission linkage, wide compatible bounds, parser/lineage failures, roster or seal failure, or an infeasible full-source scan within the declared workload. It is not a pass and defaults to `DEFER`. In particular, a high `NO_OBSERVATION` rate is a measurement of observability limitation, not evidence that recommendations were completed or ignored.

## What remains unavailable

Even a perfect longitudinal falsification result cannot establish a clinically live departure obligation. MIMIC does not provide actual viewing or retrieval, edit/sign/finalization semantics, assigned responsibility, conversations, patient preferences, outside records, target-linked orders or referrals, scheduling, execution, external transport, appropriateness, malignancy, benefit, harm, or complete community follow-up. Later documentation is selected by care processes and may be copied or missing. Date shifting and storage-versus-chart clocks support conservative within-subject relative ordering only, not exact calendar transport.

The next study must be a separately approved prospective actual-calendar silent bridge that captures document availability and viewing, assignment and communication, target-linked orders/referrals, scheduling, execution, patient preference, outside care, adjudicated appropriateness, and clinically meaningful outcomes. Only that prospective workflow study, with expert clinical adjudication and an independently governed safety analysis, could support live use, utility, safety, or causal benefit. This retrospective child cannot.
