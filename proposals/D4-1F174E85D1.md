# All-offered intention-to-obtain-UACR-first versus immediate UACR-free cystatin-C allocation at identical capacity

## Decision, evidence boundary, and substantive repair

A service can order cystatin C for exactly 20% of UK Biobank-like adults aged 40–69 whose baseline 2021 race-free creatinine eGFR (eGFRcr) is 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m². The consequential decision is whether to allocate that identical cystatin-C capacity immediately with a UACR-free score or first impose an attempt to obtain UACR for every eligible adult, retain everyone after the attempt by using a frozen fallback when the snapshot UACR is unusable, and then allocate. The primary cross-workflow comparison is therefore all-O AG versus all-O G, not AG_V versus G and not a complete-case analysis.

The strongest evidence currently supported is narrower than the hypothesis. Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796, PMCID PMC10986041; frozen full-text XML source [source checksum] inspected) studied 468,969 UK Biobank participants, internally validated models of creatinine–cystatin eGFR difference, and reported validation AUC about 0.75 for an enriched model. It supports association and internal predictability by demographic, clinical, laboratory and lifestyle characteristics. It does not establish any fixed-capacity policy, incremental grip value after UACR, measured-GFR accuracy, chronicity, temporal availability, treatment benefit, cost-effectiveness, or external transportability. A read-only feasibility audit found O=134,118 and usable censor-aware instance-0 UACR in 130,257 (97.12%); no locked H, D, K18 or policy-yield result was used to choose the design.

The timing-aware assigned parent correctly showed that instance 0 and a computable UACR do not prove that UACR preceded a cystatin-order decision, and it already defined the all-O A/AG workflow with a frozen fallback. Its gap is the absence of a direct AG-versus-G all-offered contrast. The other assigned parent adds a direct contrast and resource ledger, but its UACR-first arm is AG_V: it excludes O\\V and is therefore conditional on snapshot usability. The child combines their orthogonal strengths without treating snapshot-unusable rows as observed protocol failures.

This child adds no new score, fit, population, capacity, temporal boundary, outcome, or materiality threshold. It makes the clinically direct contrast between the existing all-O AG and all-O G selections primary: both select the same n_p from the same O_p, while only the AG strategy imposes |O_p| UACR attempts and uses the frozen median fallback for O_p\V_p. The V analysis remains a per-protocol usable-result estimand; the immediate branch remains UACR-free; actual observed ordering chronology remains nonidentifiable. Because no executable frozen coefficients, numeric ridge grid, fold assignment, or complete inference specification accompanied the lineage, this proposal also freezes those mandatory definitions below rather than requiring a nonexistent parent artifact. That operational specification is necessary for reproducibility and does not use locked outcomes.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

In each untouched test and replication partition, all of the following will hold at the same absolute capacity n_p=floor(0.20×|O_p|):

1. Immediate UACR-free allocation: G will identify at least 2 additional H reclassifications per 1,000 assays versus B and F, and at least 2 versus T; its H-yield ratios will be at least 1.25 versus B and 1.10 versus T. It will exceed the frailty-conditioned grip-placebo distribution and have positive D yield beyond F and T.
2. Protocolized intention to obtain UACR in all O: AG will identify at least 2 additional H reclassifications per 1,000 assays versus A, with ratio at least 1.10, while G will be noninferior to A at a margin of −1 per 1,000.
3. Protocolized usable-result analysis in V: without refitting and at the same O-based n_p, AG_V will identify at least 2 additional H reclassifications per 1,000 versus A_V, with ratio at least 1.10, while G_V will be noninferior to A_V at −1 per 1,000.
4. Direct all-offered workflow contrast: ITT_AG (the all-O AG selection after an imposed attempt-for-all UACR protocol with the frozen fallback) will identify at least 2 additional H reclassifications per 1,000 cystatin assays versus IM_G (the all-O immediate G selection), with H-yield ratio at least 1.10, positive AG-only minus G-only H yield, and positive D yield in both locks with a pooled interval above zero. This direct gate cannot rescue either component strategy.
5. If the prespecified N18 information thresholds pass, the corresponding AG:A and AG_V:A_V H×K18 gains will each be at least 1 per 1,000 with intervals above 0; G:A and G_V:A_V will be noninferior at −1 per 1,000; G will add at least 1 H×K18 case per 1,000 versus B and be noninferior to F and T; and ITT_AG will add at least 1 H×K18 case per 1,000 versus IM_G with a simultaneous pooled lower limit above zero.

All claims must replicate, pass the joint missing-label bounds, simultaneous uncertainty, calibration, placebo, sex-stability, UACR-variant, swap, chronology and leakage gates below. This tests comparative biochemical allocation and N18-coded prognostic enrichment. It does not test measured GFR, persistent CKD, causation, workflow timing in UKB, treatment effects, costs, or clinical benefit.

## Exact predictor construction

All quantiles use NumPy method="linear" (R type 7), all standard deviations are population SD (ddof=0), and all transformation constants come from development O without H. A zero SD, nonunique spline boundary, or nonfinite transform makes the affected policy noncomputable and its branch inconclusive.

For a continuous x with development-O knots ξ1,ξ2,ξ3,ξ4 at the 5th, 35th, 65th and 95th percentiles, define u_j(x)=max(x−ξ_j,0)^3 and, for j=1,2:

r_j(x)=[u_j(x)−((ξ4−ξ_j)/(ξ4−ξ3))u_3(x)+((ξ3−ξ_j)/(ξ4−ξ3))u_4(x)]/(ξ4−ξ1)^2.

For age, BMI and eGFRcr, B contains x,r_1(x),r_2(x), each centered and divided by its development-O mean and population SD. B is exactly: unpenalized intercept; raw sex 0/1; and those nine standardized continuous basis columns. No interaction or future/outcome field enters B.

Raw maximum grip R is the maximum finite positive value among 46-0.0 and 47-0.0. Winsorize it at development-O 0.5th/99.5th percentiles, then Z_R=(winsorized R−μ_R)/σ_R using development-O μ_R and σ_R. G is exactly B plus Z_R once. It has no residualization, spline, interaction, sex-specific transform, handedness, side or missingness column.

Walking W uses B plus one scalar: pace 924-0.0 maps slow code 1→2, steady 2→1, brisk 3→0; blank, −7, −3 and every other value are replaced by the development-O mean of mapped valid values. Overall-health Q uses B plus one scalar retaining valid ordered codes 1–4; blank, −1, −3 and all other values are replaced by the development-O mean of valid values. Each completed scalar is standardized by its own development-O mean/SD, with no indicator. No global negative-code rule is allowed.

For UACR, compute usable values as above. In development O, m_A is the median usable UACR. The primary completed UACR equals observed UACR for V and m_A otherwise. Apply log1p, winsorize at the 1st/99th percentiles of development-O usable log1p(UACR), then standardize by the mean/SD of the completed-and-winsorized development-O values to obtain Z_A. A is exactly B+Z_A. AG is exactly A+Z_R. There is no UACR missing indicator, category, spline, interaction, future value, cystatin value or locked statistic.

Before unlocking, also freeze six UACR stress variants: censored a in {0,3.35,6.7} mg/L crossed with unavailable UACR imputed to the development-O 1st or 99th percentile of usable UACR. Each variant repeats the specified log1p/winsorize/standardize process and refits A/AG only in development. The primary remains a=3.35 with median imputation. Variants cannot rescue primary failure.

Non-rescuing C is A plus: diabetes 2443-0.0 known 0/1; mean of available positive 4080-0.0/4080-0.1; and medication indicator equal to one for code 2 in any 6153 field for females or any 6177 field for males. ## Estimands, missing labels, bootstrap, and simultaneous families

For fixed selected set S and its branch’s n_p:

Y_H(S)=1000Σ_i I(i∈S)H_i/n_p;
Y_D(S)=1000Σ_i I(i∈S)D_i/n_p;
Y_H18(S)=1000Σ_i I(i∈S)H_iK18_i/n_p.

Report exact selected count, observed-label count, each yield, contrasts, ratios, overlap, Jaccard and challenger-only minus comparator-only decomposition. The denominator is always n_p, never observed labels.

For a selected missing H or D, the single-policy lower/upper contribution is 0/1. For contrast S:C let c_i=I(i∈S)−I(i∈C). Its joint lower numerator is the observed numerator plus Σ_missing min(0,c_i), and its upper is the observed numerator plus Σ_missing max(0,c_i); use c_iK18_i for H×K18. Thus a missing H can enter the H×K18 upper bound only when K18=1. One common unknown value is used for a participant shared across policies. Obtain exact ratio extrema over these same binary assignments; never divide marginal bounds. Any feasible zero comparator numerator makes the ratio undefined and the branch inconclusive. More than 1% of O missing valid cystatin makes every allocation-performance branch inconclusive.

Use B=2,000 paired bootstrap draws, with fixed transforms, models, policy membership, n_p and V. For partition name test or replication and zero-based decimal draw b, seed NumPy Generator(PCG64DXSM(seed)) with the unsigned big-endian integer represented by the full digest SHA256(UTF8("ukb-grip-bootstrap-v3|"+partition+"|"+b)). Record NumPy and bit-generator versions. All comparisons in a partition share the draw. Pooled draw b independently resamples each lock with that lock's seed and sums weighted numerators and fixed denominators; never pool participants before resampling. Models and selections are never refit. Missing labels contribute zero to bootstrap point estimates and are handled separately by sharp bounds.

Define three prespecified simultaneous families:

If every s_j is finite and positive and at least 95% of ratio replicates are finite, calculate t_bj=(θ_bj−θ_j)/s_j and q_0.95 as the empirical 95th percentile (NumPy linear quantile) of max_j|t_bj|. ## Exact classification function and result meanings

First run immutable-source, join, equation, lock, leakage, transform, model-column, convergence, tie, budget, date-pairing, V and timing checks. Any failure makes affected performance branches computationally inconclusive; Wobs remains observed_preassay_not_identifiable independently.

A biochemical superiority gate with materiality m is supportive only when its adverse joint difference bound is ≥m, its simultaneous lower confidence bound is >0, and any required ratio has point estimate and adverse feasible lower bound at least its target with simultaneous lower ratio bound >1. It is adverse with adequate precision when its simultaneous upper difference bound is below m, its feasible ratio upper bound or simultaneous ratio upper bound is below the target, or its required directional swap yield is ≤0. Otherwise it is inconclusive. A noninferiority gate is supportive when its adverse joint difference bound is above −1 and its simultaneous one-sided lower bound is >−1; adverse when its favorable joint upper bound or simultaneous upper bound is ≤−1; otherwise inconclusive. D gates follow the same zero or −1 margins. Placebo failure and finite out-of-range calibration are adverse; undefined calibration, variant sign reversal, sex instability, excessive missingness or sparse/invalid computation are inconclusive.

W0 biochemical supportive requires every G:B/G:F/G:T H difference gate, the G:B and G:T ratio gates, positive directional swaps, both-lock placebo, positive both-lock and pooled D versus F/T, calibration and no sex flag. W1_ITT biochemical supportive requires AG:A H difference/ratio/D/swap gates in both locks, G:A H and D noninferiority, all six UACR variants concordant, calibration and no sex flag. W1_PP biochemical supportive requires its preflight plus AG_V:A_V H difference/ratio/D/swap gates in both locks, G_V:A_V H noninferiority, all variants concordant, calibration and no sex flag. The primary direct gate is supportive only when ITT_AG:IM_G meets its H difference, ratio, directional-swap and D criteria in both locks, every W0 and W1_ITT component gate supports, variants are concordant, and all computational checks pass. The compound biochemical claim requires all three branches and the primary direct gate supportive; one branch cannot rescue another.

If biochemical gates pass and N18 is sufficient, full N18 support requires adverse-bound Δ_H18(G:B)≥1 and simultaneous pooled interval >0; G:F and G:T lower bounds >−1; adverse-bound Δ_H18(AG:A), Δ_H18(AG_V:A_V) and Δ_H18(ITT_AG:IM_G) each ≥1 with pooled intervals >0; and G:A and G_V:A_V lower bounds >−1. A sufficiently powered superiority result is adverse if its upper interval is below +1 or wholly ≤0; noninferiority is adverse if its upper interval is ≤−1. Boundary-crossing intervals are inconclusive.

Emit exactly one global performance label:

- full_supportive: all three biochemical branches and the primary direct all-O gate support, every branch-specific and direct N18 sufficiency threshold passes, and every N18 materiality/noninferiority gate supports.
- biochemical_allocation_supportive_chronic_consequence_unresolved: all three biochemical branches and the primary direct all-O gate support, but any N18 sufficiency condition fails or a non-adverse N18 interval crosses its boundary.

For the direct claim, supportive means AG has reproducibly greater prespecified target yield than G under this exact snapshot-anchored fallback regime and equal cystatin count, while total-resource and implementation consequences remain unresolved. Adverse means adequately precise results refute at least one prespecified direct superiority/materiality requirement; it argues against this exact AG allocation rule in this emulation but cannot prove that prospective UACR collection is useless. Inconclusive means the direct hypothesis remains unresolved because precision, replication, missing labels, robustness, calibration, information thresholds or computation did not clear the frozen gates. ## Verification contract and limits

The result file must contain source/hash/header audits; O/L/V/U counts and exclusions; timing audit; preprocessing/fold/grid/fit hashes; coefficients and F choice; selected eid hashes and counts; all yields, bounds, swaps, bootstrap seeds/fallbacks/families; calibration, placebo, sex and N18 sufficiency outputs; four workflow blocks; the two-arm resource ledger; a direct_all_O_equal_cystatin result block; overall_workflow_decision.winner_identifiable=false; and machine-generated reasons for the global label.

Mandatory adversarial fixtures include correct supportive, adverse and inconclusive direct outputs and reject: wrong creatinine/UACR units; confusing 30505 with 30515; treating <6.7 as measured 6.7; changing O, V or n; using 20% of V; admitting imputed UACR into V; refitting in V or locks; any B-column mismatch; G other than B+Z_R; AG other than A+Z_R; locked preprocessing; altered folds/grid/ties/seeds; rounded-eGFR T ordering; outcome leakage; marginal rather than joint bounds; independently bootstrapped policies; mispaired diagnosis/date arrays; N17 censoring N18; same-day death after N18; V evidence labeled immediate/all-offered/observed-preassay; all-O median-fallback evidence labeled usable-result; numeric Wobs output; assignable_prior_count=0 interpreted as post-decision urine; all-O AG restricted to V; O\V dropped rather than assigned the fallback; imposed attempts reported as observed actions; snapshot usability reported as future collection success; equal cystatin counts reported as equal cost; a supportive direct result reported as workflow superiority or benefit; an adverse direct result reported as proof that prospective UACR can never help; and correct supportive, adverse or inconclusive arithmetic attached to the wrong workflow or an unsupported clinical conclusion.

The automatic verifier can establish source identity and field absence; joins; O/L/V membership; equations; partitions; deterministic fits and rankings; exact capacity; labels and chronology; bounds; resampling; multiplicity; gates; and whether conclusions follow computed outputs. It cannot establish true pre-order UACR availability, assay turnaround, persistent albuminuria or reduced eGFR, measured GFR, adjudicated CKD, complete event capture, muscle mechanism, fairness, clinical action, benefit, harm, cost-effectiveness or external transport. Those require participant-level urine collection/result and allocation/order timestamps, repeat urine and filtration measurements, measured-GFR/body-composition data, outpatient/chart/prescription/action data, nephrology and equity review, an external cohort, and ultimately a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study.
