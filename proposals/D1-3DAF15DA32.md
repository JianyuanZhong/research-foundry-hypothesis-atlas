# Event-anchored biochemical perturbation and early recovery around first subsequent strict TACE

## Substantive advance over the parent

This proposal preserves the parent candidate's frozen common-TACE2 cohort, 2018–2024 era, day-14 institutional-observation landmark, medication ontology, grace-period pathway rules, ambiguity exclusions, exact assay policy, and baseline pathway membership. It changes the clinically ambiguous follow-up question into an explicitly event-anchored experiment: among patients whose first subsequent strict TACE occurs on TACE2 calendar days 15–90, compare the biochemical perturbation and early recovery observed around that procedure between the frozen systemic-record and TACE2-only pathways.

The change is interpretive and estimand-defining, not a reclassification of the baseline pathways. A day-30–90 laboratory value is not treated as stable hepatic reserve. The later strict TACE is selected before examining laboratory values, and only the first eligible subsequent patient-day episode is used. The selected population is a prognostic/observational care population. Conditioning on receipt of a repeat TACE can induce selection/collider bias because repeat treatment may depend on disease burden, response, hepatic reserve, imaging, scheduling, clinician preference, and baseline pathway. Therefore no contrast below is a causal effect of repeat TACE, systemic treatment, or a sustained strategy.

## Clinical question and hypothesis

Among adults with recorded HCC who reach the same strictly defined TACE2 and subsequently undergo their first strict TACE on TACE2 days 15–90, is the frozen days-1–14 systemic-record pathway associated with a different within-person biochemical response to, and early recovery after, that observed repeat TACE than the ambiguity-excluded TACE2-only pathway?

The prespecified principal biochemical profile is:

1. acute albumin perturbation, defined as acute albumin minus the pre-event albumin (a lower value is an adverse direction);
2. acute total-bilirubin perturbation, defined as acute total bilirubin minus the pre-event total bilirubin (a higher value is an adverse direction);
3. seven-day residual from baseline for each assay, recovery value minus pre-event value; and
4. recovery from the acute value, recovery minus acute value, interpreted jointly with the residual because a numerically favorable rebound can still leave a patient worse than baseline.

Albumin and total bilirubin remain separate outcomes; they are not pooled, converted to ALBI, or combined across unknown units. A supportive biochemical pattern requires less albumin decline or greater albumin increase and no larger bilirubin rise, with compatible recovery direction. Discordant assays are mixed/adverse rather than being averaged away.

The strongest claim supported by the feasibility evidence is only that this design is computable and that the selected event-centered assays are sparse but present. The unresolved claim is the stated within-person association in the repeat-TACE-selected population. It does not test medication delivery, adherence, regimen intent, imaging response, progression, survival, comparative effectiveness, or clinical benefit.

## Event-selected population and clocks

Among the 319 and 1,491 frozen patients, identify all strict TACE patient-day episodes after TACE2 day 14. Select the first subsequent strict TACE per patient, ordered by normalized calendar day then procedure start timestamp. Do not replace it with a later event because of laboratory availability. The principal event population is those whose selected first event is on days 15–90 inclusive. Retain a flow table containing the full frozen arms, all patients with an event on day 15 or later, the day-15–90 event-selected subset, assay-observed subsets, and complete-pair subsets.

The complete-source audit found 526 TACE2-only and 159 systemic patients in the principal event-selected population. Event timing was similar descriptively: median day 55 in both arms; TACE2-only Q1/Q3 44/69, systemic Q1/Q3 43/69, with ranges 21–90 and 23–89, respectively. These counts establish feasibility, not comparability or causal identification.

Anchor all laboratory times to the exact `procedures.Start Time` of the selected repeat TACE, not to calendar midnight and not merely to the event encounter. Restrict principal outcomes to exact `labs.Test` labels `Albumin` and `Total bilirubin`; retain `Specimen Type`. After trimming and removing commas, accept only an entire `Quantitative Result` string consisting of an optional leading `<`, `>`, `≤`, or `≥` and a signed decimal/scientific numeric literal. Reject unrelated prefixes/suffixes and never extract embedded numeric substrings. Preserve the inequality marker for audit; the primary numeric analysis uses the parsed bound as recorded and is sensitivity-limited by this convention.

For each assay and patient, select deterministically:

- pre-event: latest valid assay from -336 hours through strictly before 0 hours;
- acute target-nearest: valid assay after 0 through 72 hours minimizing absolute distance to +48 hours, then earlier relative time, then latest timestamp for exact ties;
- acute adverse-extreme sensitivity: minimum albumin or maximum total bilirubin in >0 through 72 hours, with deterministic timestamp tie-breaking;
- early recovery: valid assay in >72 through 336 hours minimizing absolute distance to +168 hours, then earlier relative time, then latest timestamp for exact ties.

The target-nearest acute selector is principal because it represents a prespecified common early post-event clock; the adverse-extreme selector is a locked sensitivity and not selected using arm-specific results. The audit shows acute assay availability is materially better than complete recovery: for albumin, pre/acute/recovery counts are 75/120/46 systemic and 290/384/135 TACE2-only; for bilirubin they are 75/121/46 and 290/385/135. Complete three-slot albumin-bilirubin pairs are 22 systemic and 54 TACE2-only under either acute selector. The expected sparse recovery means the paired biochemical profile is precision-limited and may be inconclusive.

The audit enumerated exact event-window labels without pooling them. In addition to the principal albumin and total bilirubin labels, exact `International Normalized Ratio` and `Alanine Aminotransferase` have support; `Aspartate Aminotransferase` had no accepted slots under the audited exact label. These assays may be reported as exploratory context only if the executable run reproduces valid support and does not alter the principal endpoint hierarchy.

## Estimands and analysis

The primary estimand is a descriptive, selected-population contrast among patients whose first subsequent strict TACE occurs on days 15–90 and who have the required assay slots:

`E_w[acute - pre | baseline systemic-record, selected event and observed slots] - E_w[acute - pre | baseline TACE2-only, selected event and observed slots]`,

with analogous contrasts for recovery minus pre and recovery minus acute, separately for albumin and bilirubin. This is a fixed-baseline pathway association conditional on a future observed-care event and assay observation. It is not a repeat-TACE effect, treatment effect, per-protocol effect, as-treated effect, direct effect, or sustained-strategy effect.

Use overlap weights from a propensity model for the frozen baseline arm, fit using only information available by TACE2: age, sex, TACE1–TACE2 interval, TACE2 year/month, department, prior encounter and procedure counts, prior text flags for resection/ablation/transplant, encounter-timed diagnosis proxies for cirrhosis/portal hypertension/ascites/encephalopathy/variceal bleeding/portal-vein invasion/metastasis, and closest pre-TACE2 exact albumin, bilirubin, INR, platelet, creatinine, and sodium with missingness flags. Do not include the repeat-TACE event, post-TACE2 medications/procedures, event context, or outcome in this model. Report raw and weighted denominators, standardized mean differences, propensity support, weight distribution, arm-specific ESS, and robust patient-level 95% confidence intervals. Fit weighted change regressions with arm, baseline assay, and only prespecified covariates that remain materially imbalanced; use heteroskedasticity-robust patient-level uncertainty.

Because event receipt and assay availability are selected, additionally model, in all 1,810 frozen-arm patients, indicators for subsequent day-15–90 strict TACE, pre-slot, acute-slot, recovery-slot, and complete paired observation using the same pre-TACE2 predictors. Report arm-specific selection/observation fractions, five-fold cross-validated AUC and calibration, selected versus nonselected covariate balance, and predicted-probability ranges. Inverse probability of selection or observation is a sensitivity for transport across measured predictors only. It cannot repair unmeasured clinical selection or collider bias. Report untruncated and locked 1st/99th percentile-truncated combined weights and ESS; if weights collapse, use complete-case descriptive results and label them inconclusive.

## Required context decomposition and selected-pathway sensitivities

Before interpreting biochemical coefficients, report by baseline arm in the full frozen cohort and in the event/assay subsets:

- later systemic record, later strict TACE, either, and mutually exclusive later-event categories through day 90;
- exact encounter matching, admission linkage using earliest admission through latest discharge plus one day, duplicate encounter-row counts, and department multiplicity;
- selected assay same encounter as strict TACE, strict TACE within ±3 and ±7 calendar days, any-procedure same encounter and within ±7 days, nearest signed TACE distance, and whether the assay precedes or follows the procedure;
- pre, acute, and recovery timing, assay density by rows/days/encounters, and all weighted versions.

Admission linkage is a timing proxy and does not prove acute illness. Procedure proximity is symmetric and does not prove causation. A later systemic record is a record/order, not verified administration; a later TACE may be planned, response-guided, progression-guided, or preference-driven.

Using the same pre-TACE2 overlap model and no arm reclassification, run locked sensitivities restricted to: no later systemic/TACE before the selected assay; no later systemic; no later strict TACE; assay more than 7 days from every strict TACE; assay more than 3 days from every strict TACE; and mutually exclusive neither/systemic-only/TACE-only/both categories when each arm has at least 20 unweighted patients and ESS at least 10. These are selected-pathway descriptive sensitivities and may induce collider bias. Never promote one because it is favorable.

Retain the parent’s outcome-blind temporal and ontology checks: baseline windows -14 to -1 and -60 to -1; earliest/latest follow-up within the event-relative windows; target-nearest acute versus adverse-extreme acute; strict versus already-prespecified broad TACE ontology; encounter-wide versus exact-day bevacizumab partnering; and 2020–2024 era. Use a same-assay pre-exposure negative control, comparing a value in days -60 to -31 before TACE2 with the frozen days -30 to -1 baseline. A material pre-exposure contrast capable of explaining the event contrast falsifies a newly emergent pathway interpretation; a null negative control does not establish exchangeability.

## Falsification, interpretation, and fallback branches

- **Supportive, context-bounded:** reconstruction, overlap, observation, and ESS gates pass; albumin shows less adverse acute perturbation and/or better recovery in systemic-record patients without a larger bilirubin rise; acute selectors agree; no-event and procedure-distant sensitivities are directionally compatible; and the negative control does not explain the result. Conclude only a favorable association in the repeat-TACE-selected observed-care population, not liver preservation or treatment benefit.
- **Adverse:** systemic-record patients show greater albumin decline and/or bilirubin rise with coherent recovery and adequate support. This argues against a favorable association but does not establish medication toxicity or causation.
- **Precise null:** clinically meaningful intervals are narrow around no difference, assays are coherent, and computational gates pass. Report no detectable recorded-pathway contrast, not equivalence, equal safety, or absence of individual harm.
- **Selection/context falsification:** the primary contrast disappears or reverses in adequately supported no-event or procedure-distant subsets, is concentrated around the repeat TACE, or differs with measured selection/observation adjustment. Report context dependence and selection uncertainty; do not identify a causal mediator.
- **Timing/measurement falsification:** results reverse between target-nearest and adverse-extreme selectors, depend on a boundary convention, show major assay-context imbalance, or are driven by sparse/extreme observation weights. Treat the biochemical claim as unstable.
- **Methodological falsification:** any frozen cohort count, arm count, event-order rule, exact-label rule, or join/time boundary fails; or the pre-exposure negative control is materially explanatory. Report the failed premise rather than choosing a favorable reconstruction.
- **Inconclusive:** fewer than 30 systemic complete profiles, systemic ESS below 20 (and for the principal joint profile the audit already indicates severe precision limitation), total ESS below 300, nonpositivity, wide intervals spanning plausible benefit and harm, poor observation calibration, combined-weight collapse, discordant albumin/bilirubin, or unavailable adjudication prevents interpretation. If the event-centered complete profile is inadequate, retain the full 319/1,491 frozen cohort and report only event-selection and assay-observation feasibility/context; do not substitute non-event-centered day-30–90 values as stable reserve.

## Limits and required additional evidence

The dataset lacks administration/adherence, indication, dose, duration, treatment intent, time-varying severity and decision covariates, imaging, validated Child-Pugh components, assay units, outside-care capture, adjudicated reason for testing or repeat TACE, toxicity attribution, and complete vital-status linkage. Exact dates and identifiers remain internal. Stronger claims about causal pathway effects, repeat-TACE benefit/harm, stable hepatic reserve, imaging response, progression, survival, or clinical decision utility require longitudinal administration data, adjudicated imaging and clinical context, validated assay units and thresholds, time-varying causal methods under explicit assumptions, and external or prospective validation. An automatic verifier can check reconstruction, event ordering, slot selection, joins, context flags, weights, balance, ESS, intervals, and whether the stated branch follows computed outputs; it cannot establish clinical intent, causation, administration, or clinical importance.
