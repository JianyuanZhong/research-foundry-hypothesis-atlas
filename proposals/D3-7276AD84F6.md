## 1. Scientific question

Does the MerTK inhibitor (UNC106238) affect fibroblast-like synoviocytes (FLS) **directly**
(off-target, hypothesis A), independent of macrophage contact?

The ep12 analysis (grandparent `dc57fbf5`) found that in FLS co-cultured with MerTK/CD206**neg**
macrophages, inhibitor pre-treatment produced a LARGER FLS transcriptomic effect than in FLS
co-cultured with MerTK/CD206**pos** macrophages, while Mpos vs Mpos_Inhibitor was null. Two
competing explanations were left open:

- **(A) Off-target:** UNC106238 acts on FLS directly. Then FLS-only cultures with inhibitor
  should differ from FLS-only baseline, and part of the Mneg effect reflects this direct action.
- **(B) MerTK not the primary mediator:** the inhibitor has no direct FLS effect; the Mneg
  asymmetry is macrophage-conditioned or a single-sample artifact.

**The ep12 grouping error (repaired in the parent, preserved here):** `R1_SA256A_FLS_Inhibitor`
is an FLS-ONLY culture with the MerTK inhibitor present, from the **same donor SA256A** as
`R1_SA256A_FLS_only` — exactly the paired off-target control. ep12 mislabeled it as
Mneg_Inhibitor.

## 4. Interpretation

**Off-target hypothesis (A): NOT supported (adverse result for A).** The decisive corrected
control — same-donor FLS-only with vs without inhibitor — shows no non-random transcriptomic
response: permutation p = 1.0, 2.7% above 2× baseline SD, 0 real mixed-effects BH hits, MERTK
unchanged, no directional bias. The 84 |FC|>=1 features are consistent with sparse-panel
zero-count noise, not a coherent direct drug effect.

**Consequence for the ep12 asymmetry:** the Mneg > Mpos inhibitor effect is **not explained by
direct FLS off-target action** (delta_FLSonly correlates identically with both). The remaining
live explanations are (B) MerTK not the primary mediator of the FLS response (the inhibitor
effect, where present, is macrophage-conditioned) or a **single-sample artifact** of the
n = 1 Mneg_Inhibitor sample. These two are **observationally indistinguishable with the current
data**: one more Mneg_Inhibitor sample is the minimal discriminating experiment.

**Supportive / adverse / inconclusive mapping for the off-target question:**
- *Supportive of (A) would have been:* non-random |FC| spread (perm p < 0.05), real
  BH-significant mixed-effects genes, and delta_FLSonly → delta_Mneg correlation exceeding
  delta_Mpos.
- *Observed (adverse to A):* all three null (above).
- *Inconclusive:* power for moderate direct effects is unestablished (n = 1 pair); the Mneg
  asymmetry itself is neither confirmed nor refuted as biology.

**What this does NOT establish:** it does not prove UNC106238 is FLS-inert at all doses, does
not identify the mechanism of the Mneg asymmetry, and does not validate MerTK as (or against)
a therapeutic target in this system — those require the additional samples/assays below.

## 6. Limitations

1. **n = 1 off-target pair** (SA256A only) — permutation/background statistics bound the
   spread but cannot power detection of moderate effects; no paired Wilcoxon possible.
2. **n = 1 Mneg_Inhibitor sample** — the Mneg asymmetry is a single observation; artifact vs
   biology unresolved.
3. **Targeted 445-gene panel, sparse counts** — 176/445 features zero in one of the two
   off-target samples; |FC|>=1 is heavily inflated by zero↔low transitions.
4. No library-size normalization (log2(count+1) on panel values); no batch/cellularity
   covariates available in the design file.
5. Inhibitor exposure of FLS in the FLS_only_Inhibitor sample is inferred from sample naming
   (design `treatment: none`, `condition: not co-cultured`) — the protocol-level question of
   whether FLS themselves saw the drug is an expert adjudication item.
6. Mixed-effects model has 1 inhibitor sample total (all from donor SA256A): the "inhibitor"
   fixed effect is confounded with that donor's inhibitor response; 387/445 features have no
   estimable p-value (zero/low variance); the 2 p=0.0 values are numerical artifacts, not hits.

## 7. Next steps (falsification / confirmation)

- **Minimal discriminating test:** 2–3 additional same-donor FLS-only + inhibitor pairs.
  If the off-target null holds (perm p > 0.05, no real BH hits), (A) is rejected with power
  and the Mneg asymmetry must be macrophage-conditioned or artifact.
- **Mneg replication:** 1–2 more Mneg_Inhibitor samples. If the Mneg effect replicates, (B)
  gains support (real Mneg-specific on-target phenomenon); if it disappears, single-sample
  artifact.
- **Dose/response in FLS-only:** UNC106238 dose series (or a second MerTK inhibitor) in
  FLS-only culture to test for any direct effect at higher exposure.
- **Full-counts analysis:** repeat on the full scRNA-seq count matrix (if available) so
  zero-count features do not dominate.

## 8. Method comparison

**Baseline (executed):** Permutation label-shuffle background + donor-aware mixed-effects model
+ per-feature Welch t-tests with BH correction. This is the standard approach for small-n,
sparse-panel, multi-group transcriptomic comparisons with a single paired control.

**Alternative considered:** Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank on the FLS-only family. Not feasible
(n = 1 inhibitor pair; no within-donor replication beyond the single SA256A pair). The
permutation background is the appropriate substitute.

**Alternative considered:** Full-counts (unfiltered scRNA-seq) analysis. Would reduce the
zero-count inflation but the frozen prepared panel is the available binding; the full matrix is
not in the prepared set. Recorded as a next step, not executed.

**Alternative considered:** Dose-response FLS-only experiment. Would directly test for a
direct effect at higher exposure but requires new wet-lab data not in the frozen set.

## 9. What would change the next decision

- **If additional FLS-only+inhibitor pairs confirm the off-target null:** (A) is rejected with
  power; the Mneg asymmetry must be macrophage-conditioned (B) or artifact; proceed to Mneg
  replication.
- **If additional pairs show a non-random off-target effect:** (A) is supported; the ep12 Mneg
  asymmetry is partly direct; re-interpret the specificity interaction.
- **If Mneg_Inhibitor replicates in new samples:** (B) gains support (real Mneg-specific
  on-target phenomenon).
- **If Mneg_Inhibitor does not replicate:** single-sample artifact; the ep12 asymmetry is
  withdrawn.
