## Decision-relevant question and falsifiable hypothesis

Cardio-oncology services cannot review every cancer survivor at the same intensity. The unresolved question is whether a prespecified, small pre-cancer physiological panel can rank incident-cancer survivors so that a fixed-capacity review list contains materially more *first recorded heart-failure events* than an age/sex/site/timing list, while accounting for death as a competing event. This is a more consequential successor to the parent’s low-versus-high reserve contrast: it asks whether the signal changes prioritisation when review capacity is limited, not merely whether two extremes differ.

Primary hypothesis:

> Among adults without recorded heart failure at an incident-cancer landmark, a 10%-capacity review list ranked by pre-cancer grip, creatinine-derived eGFR, resting pulse and systolic pressure will concentrate five-year cumulative incidence of a first recorded `I50.*` diagnosis more than a transparent demographic/cancer-timing baseline. A matched nonlinear model will add scientifically meaningful ranking information only if prespecified threshold or cross-system interaction structure improves event concentration and held-out calibration at the same capacity.

The estimand is predictive, not causal. In the late calendar-time test set, for each model and fixed capacity `K` in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20} (primary `K=0.10`), select exactly the highest-risk K proportion of eligible cancer survivors using training-derived score rules. Report (i) the five-year competing-risk heart-failure CIF in selected versus non-selected people, (ii) the selected-list event capture fraction, and (iii) the paired difference in capture fraction and CIF concentration between the reserve model and the demographic/cancer-timing baseline. The primary claim requires the 10% result; the other capacities are prespecified sensitivity analyses, not a search over thresholds.

This could inform the design of a survivorship review queue, but the data cannot establish that referral, prevention, medication, or treatment would improve outcomes. It is a clinically interpretable triage-performance hypothesis, not a treatment policy recommendation.

## Supported evidence versus unresolved claim

The strongest available evidence is structural. The local UKB snapshot contains one-row-per-`eid` structured tables, assessment dates and measurements, biomarker fields, cancer diagnosis/date arrays, 259 diagnosis/date pairs, and two death-date fields. The UKB-08 card and the parent are hypotheses/designs, not evidence that low reserve predicts heart failure. The local catalog does not certify most field labels, units, missing-value meanings, cancer treatment, stage, outpatient ascertainment, or heart-failure adjudication.

The experiment tests an association and a held-out fixed-capacity ranking contrast for a *recorded* post-cancer `I50.*` endpoint. It does not test cancer stage, recurrence, chemotherapy or radiotherapy cardiotoxicity, treatment benefit, infection susceptibility, biological frailty, measured GFR, ejection fraction, natriuretic peptides, symptom onset, or causal reserve mechanisms. A supportive result would therefore establish only a transport-tested prognostic concentration in this export, not a validated clinical triage policy.

## Population and time

Let `T0 = 53-0.0`, the first assessment-centre date. Require `T0` between 2006-01-01 and 2010-12-31, valid recruitment sex and age, positive valid `46-0.0` and `47-0.0`, valid `102-0.0`, at least one valid `4080-0.0`/`4080-0.1`, and valid `30700-0.0`. Bilateral grip is the mean of fields 46 and 47; systolic pressure is the mean of available valid readings. The solver must certify field labels, units, coding and instance meaning before interpreting measurements; if that certificate fails, it may produce structural audits but not a physiological claim.

Define the first valid malignant-neoplasm code in `C00–C96/C97` from the matched `40006/40005` pairs after `T0`, requiring `TC >= T0 + 365 days`. Exclude any valid cancer pair at or before `T0`; absent records do not prove cancer-free status. Restrict the primary cancer landmark to 2010-01-01 through 2018-12-31. Site is the first three-character ICD-10 group, with prespecified reporting groups C18–C20, C33–C34, C50 and C61 only when support gates pass.

Exclude death on or before `TC`, any recorded `I50.*` code/date on or before `TC`, malformed code/date pairs, invalid reserve inputs, duplicate keys and failed joins. The primary endpoint is the first valid same-position `I50.*` diagnosis/date in `(TC, TC + 5 calendar years]`; death from `40000-0.0` or `40000-1.0` is a competing event, with same-day death assigned to death. Five-year censoring is allowed only after an observation/ascertainment certificate through 2023-12-31. Without that certificate, report only “recorded I50 in available structured records”; the five-year CIF and capacity claim are inconclusive.

## Inputs and fitted alternatives

Use participant split `SHA256(UTF8(decimal(eid))) mod 5`. Fit on landmarks 2010–2015 and apply with no refit to 2016–2018. All standardization, missingness handling, knots, thresholds and model fitting are training-fold operations.

The common control set is age at `T0`, sex, first cancer site group, cancer landmark year, and `TC-T0`. The reserve inputs are training-standardized bilateral grip, race-free creatinine eGFR using a prespecified 2021 CKD-EPI creatinine equation after unit certification, resting pulse, and systolic pressure. Cystatin C and `30720-0.0` are deliberately excluded; this is not a kidney-assay proposal.

- `Mbase` is a transparent control-only discrete-time complementary-log-log model with separate annual heart-failure and death hazards. It defines the existing information available to a basic cancer-survivorship queue.
- `Mlinear` adds the four reserve inputs linearly to `Mbase`. Its coefficients and partial effects are the transparent reserve test and preserve continuity with candidate-72.
- `Mnonlinear` uses the identical cohort, rows, split, missingness policy, hazards and controls, replacing only the four linear reserve terms with four separate degree-3 restricted cubic spline bases and the two prespecified interactions grip×eGFRcr and pulse×systolic pressure. It can reveal threshold, saturation and cross-system coupling that Mlinear averages away.

For each model, compute competing-risk five-year HF CIF, rank the late test set, and evaluate exactly the top 5%, 10% and 20%. The primary scientific comparison is `Mlinear` versus `Mbase` at 10%; the substantive learned alternative is `Mnonlinear` versus `Mlinear`, with `Mbase` retained to separate reserve information from routine demographic/site composition. Report held-out integrated Brier score, five-year calibration intercept/slope, CIF, selected-list capture, selected-versus-unselected CIF, and paired changes. A small discrimination or Brier change without stable capacity concentration or interpretable surface structure is not a substantive advance.

## Uncertainty, falsification and interpretation gates

Use participant-level bootstrap intervals (prespecified 1,000 replicates, stratified resampling only if required by a declared support rule) for CIF, capture, selected-list contrasts, and model differences. Also report fold-to-fold and early/late transport variation. Do not use individual-year rows as independent bootstrap units.

Support for the fixed-capacity hypothesis requires: source/header/key and field-semantics gates pass; observation completeness passes; at least 100 people in the primary selected list and 50 qualifying post-cancer I50 events overall, with at least 10 events in selected and unselected groups; Mlinear beats Mbase in 10% event capture or selected-versus-unselected CIF with a 95% interval excluding zero; the direction is present in the early evaluation or prespecified cross-fold check; and it is not explained by one cancer site. A nonlinear claim additionally requires a reproducible late-test capacity improvement over Mlinear, a nontrivial paired uncertainty interval, and a stable prespecified threshold or interaction pattern.

Adverse evidence is a null or reversed reserve concentration, worse calibration or capture from adding reserve, no transport to 2016–2018, or a signal that disappears when site/year/timing are controlled. If Mnonlinear changes the list but not held-out concentration, its extra complexity is not scientifically supported. A result driven by a single unsupported field, a single site, or a code/date permutation is adverse for the stated hypothesis.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed units/labels, malformed pairings, inadequate events, absent observation completeness, unstable intervals, non-overlap in training-derived top-K thresholds, or contradictory prospective and recorded-event modes. Inconclusive does not support or refute reserve.

Required falsification audits are: permute I50 dates within site/year/fold strata; permute each reserve input within age-band/sex/site/year strata; randomize capacity labels; shift one position between each cancer code/date pair and each diagnosis/date pair and require failure; omit site from Mbase; compare complete-case with fold-local imputation; permute landmark year and early/late labels; and repeat after excluding the first 90 days after cancer. Survival of the reserve claim after breaking reserve-outcome linkage falsifies attribution.

Supportive results establish only a recorded-event ranking association in this UKB export. Clinical adjudication, treatment effects, referral benefit, cost/harm weights, stage, recurrence, oncology treatment, echocardiography, biomarkers, complete outpatient capture and external validation require additional data and expert review. No automatic verifier can establish those claims.

## Alternatives, deferred branches and compute

The fixed-capacity estimand was selected over the parent’s sole low/high contrast because it directly addresses a review bottleneck. The linear model remains the transparent scientific baseline; the spline/interactions model is a matched learned alternative that adds shape and coupling information on exactly the same inputs. A boosted-tree model is deferred because it would add flexibility without improving clinical interpretability unless the spline alternative fails a predeclared implementation check. A disease-sequence transformer is deferred because the local arrays are sparse code/date positions rather than a certified longitudinal sequence, and it would test representation rather than capacity-constrained reserve. A latent organ-aging/GP model is deferred because organ-age labels, specimen timing and validated genetics are unavailable. Cystatin-C/eGFRcys, N17/N18, imaging, treatment and stage remain out of scope.

The full solver planning envelope is 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB RAM, up to 28,800 seconds, with no mandatory GPU. This is a small tabular fit: CPU is the planned device. Estimated workload is 5–20 minutes for selected-column extraction and joins, under 10 minutes for each model family, and approximately 1–4 hours for 1,000 bootstrap refits on 16 CPUs; these are planning estimates, not measured full-study results. A short discovery header/schema audit was measured in under one minute, but it does not estimate fit time. An A100 is unnecessary unless a bounded benchmark shows bootstrap parallelism exceeds the CPU envelope; GPU use would not change the estimand.

## Independent UKB direction considered

The adjacent UKB-06 seed and imported candidate `[prior hypothesis]` ask whether pre-illness endothelial/coagulation proteins modify cardiovascular risk after infection. The available `health_outcomes` table can bind the same `41270-0.j`/`41280-0.j` diagnosis/date pairs, and `biological_samples` contains assay-shaped fields, but the local catalog does not certify protein identities/units/timing or infection setting, severity and onset. It also does not provide an adjudicated post-infection cardiovascular endpoint or complete observation guarantee. The `genomics` table is explicitly derived metadata, not a sequence/variant bundle, so UKB-07 CHIP is also dependency-limited. A recorded-code adaptation could be proposed, but its infection landmark and exposure semantics would be weaker and its causal/mediator interpretation less defensible than this cancer-landmark design. It was therefore not selected as a parent or hidden substitute. Revisiting it requires verified field metadata, infection ascertainment and a prespecified cardiovascular codebook.
