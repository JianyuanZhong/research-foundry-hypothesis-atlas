# eICU ventilation care-plan labels fail a target-independent, hospital-held-out fitness-for-use test

## Clinically important unresolved question

The two literal eICU care-plan values `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` are tempting cohort, quality-measure, or decision-support variables. Prior work in this lineage established that, among a frozen target-labelled cohort, their distribution and downstream structured chart associations are highly hospital-dependent, that a setting result was peak-pressure dominated, and that durable liberation could not be validly ascertained. A separate target-independent audit established sparse target receipt and material denominator sensitivity to recency of explicit airway documentation. Those findings do not answer whether the labels nevertheless add reproducible patient-state information after selection without the labels.

**Strongest claim already supported before this experiment:** exact target receipt is sparse and concentrated by hospital; target-conditioned label contrasts do not transport as a common multicenter phenotype; and available eICU fields do not establish durable liberation.

**Falsifiable hypothesis tested here:** within target-independent, strictly eligible adult ventilation person-landmarks, the three-state feature (daily-evaluation target, no-trial target, or missing exact target) adds reproducible prediction of a prespecified post-classification reduction in non-peak ventilator-setting chart proxies beyond landmark and pre-landmark respiratory documentation/state, both within common-support hospitals and when each hospital is held out. Support additionally requires that exact-label choice itself be estimable in multiple hospitals and that the result persist under bounded explicit-airway recency definitions.

This matters because failure means these fields should not be used without site-specific validation as multicenter cohort inclusion, quality indicators, or decision-support features. Success would only establish transportable structured-documentation information, not care delivery or benefit.

## Variables, temporal boundaries, outcomes, and baselines

Pre-landmark documentation opportunity is measured in `respiratoryCharting` during [L-360,L): row count, distinct labels, robust-component row count, and latest valid FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, and ventilator rate. The exposure feature is the post-freeze three-state exact-label feature. The outcome window begins only after classification, (L+360,L+1080], and is considered complete only when `unitdischargeoffset > L+1080`.

The primary structured proxy is any reduction from the latest pre-L value to a post-window value by at least 0.05 FiO2, 2 cm H2O PEEP, 2 cm H2O pressure support, or 2 breaths/min set ventilator rate. Peak-pressure labels are deliberately excluded. Component availability is retained, and rows without any paired component are missing rather than non-events. A proposed RT-off proxy was also checked, but yielded zero events in the four exact-airway primary variants and is therefore non-estimable rather than negative clinical evidence.

Two comparisons are prespecified:

1. **Receipt:** all eligible rows, comparing a landmark/documentation/state baseline with the same model plus the three-state exact-label feature. Common-support hospitals require at least 3 target and 10 non-target rows and outcome variation.
2. **Choice:** target recipients only, comparing daily-evaluation versus no-trial. Common support requires at least 3 rows under each target and outcome variation.

Within-hospital fits include hospital and landmark fixed effects and are descriptive. Transport is tested by leave-one-hospital-out regularized logistic prediction; hospital identity is necessarily unavailable in the held-out model. Reported metrics are log loss, Brier score, AUROC, mean prediction, and observed-minus-predicted calibration difference. Hospital is the transport unit. Repeated stay-landmarks are not interpreted as independent causal observations.

Calibration was heterogeneous across held-out hospitals. This pattern is adverse to reproducible multicenter fitness, not a claim that labels have no local informational content.

## Prespecified falsification and interpretation

A supportive result required the unbounded and at least two of three bounded variants each to have: at least 500 complete rows; at least 30% paired robust-outcome coverage; at least three receipt-support and two choice-support hospitals; and both receipt and choice improving pooled held-out log loss by at least 0.005 with a majority of hospital folds improved. The outcome-coverage requirement passed, but every variant failed choice common support and choice held-out estimation. Bounded receipt validation worsened rather than improved prediction. The overall executed gate is therefore **adverse fitness for use**.

* **Supportive:** reproducible incremental held-out information under the rule above would justify further prospective validation of the labels as structured-documentation features.
* **Adverse (observed):** sparse receipt, single-hospital choice overlap, and failure under bounded-airway transport falsify the proposed multicenter fitness hypothesis for this snapshot and design. The labels should not be used unqualified for multicenter cohorting, quality measurement, or decision support.
* **Inconclusive:** adequate overlap with unstable estimates near threshold, or insufficient outcome coverage alone, would require more data rather than an adverse conclusion. Here the adverse conclusion is specifically about measurable fitness because both overlap and bounded held-out performance failed.

## Evidence limits and required additional data

All outcomes are structured chart proxies. `no_target_label` and other target-missing states do not mean no extubation evaluation or SBT occurred. The experiment cannot establish actual invasive ventilation at every landmark, delivered ventilator changes, clinician intent, spontaneous breathing trial delivery, extubation readiness, extubation, durable liberation, quality, benefit, or causality. Those stronger claims require independently time-resolved device/airway data, direct SBT and extubation records, chart review by clinicians blinded to label, and preferably prospective multisite validation. Clinical adjudication is also needed to decide whether these literal values encode local workflow semantics. An automatic verifier can check population construction, windows, counts, models, gates, and whether conclusions match computed outputs; it cannot establish bedside truth.
