# Creatinine–cystatin C discordance, low muscle reserve, and prospectively recorded kidney events

## Unresolved question, supported claim, and hypothesis

The unresolved clinical question is whether a cystatin-C estimate that is materially lower than a creatinine estimate identifies a subgroup whose creatinine-only assessment misses prospective renal vulnerability, and whether that signal is stronger among people with lower measured grip strength.

The inspected local readable article, `references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt`, is Haines et al. (CJASN 2023). It studied 38 mechanically ventilated ICU patients with serial creatinine, cystatin C, measured GFR and muscle ultrasound. Its results support a physiological concern that creatinine-based eGFR may overestimate filtration when muscle is lost, particularly during prolonged critical illness. They do not establish a UK Biobank community association, an AKI interaction, an eGFR accuracy claim in this cohort, or benefit from ordering cystatin C.

The primary hypothesis is:

> Among eligible UK Biobank participants without a previously recorded hospital N17 or N18 diagnosis, a lower standardized difference between cystatin-C eGFR and creatinine eGFR is associated with a larger hazard of a first subsequently recorded hospital N17 diagnosis at lower baseline grip strength, after adjustment for creatinine eGFR, age, sex and BMI.

The estimand is the prespecified discordance-by-grip interaction in a cause-specific time-to-first-recorded-hospital-AKI model. The primary outcome is not “true AKI,” AKI stage, or measured loss of filtration: it is a first post-index hospital diagnosis code in the normalized ICD-10 N17 category with its paired diagnosis date. N18 is a secondary outcome labelled “first subsequently recorded hospital CKD diagnosis,” not incident CKD in the biological sense. A secondary clinical contrast is the risk difference between eGFRcr >=60 and eGFRcys <60 versus concordant estimates, evaluated separately at lower and higher grip; this is descriptive and does not replace the continuous interaction estimand.

A supportive result could motivate external validation of cystatin-C testing or monitoring strategies. It cannot justify a policy, prove that creatinine is inaccurate in an individual, establish causal effect modification, or show treatment benefit.

## Frozen renal exposure definitions

Convert creatinine from umol/L to mg/dL by division by 88.4 and retain cystatin C in mg/L. Use age in years from `21022-0.0` and sex coding from the approved coding-9 metadata (0 female, 1 male); do not infer sex from another field.

The primary equations are frozen before outcome fitting:

- Race-free CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine eGFR:
  `eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^a * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^Age * 1.012^(Female)`, where `k=0.7, a=-0.241` for female and `k=0.9, a=-0.302` for male.
- Standalone CKD-EPI cystatin-C eGFR (2012 cystatin-C equation, which has no race term):
  `eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.996^Age * 0.932^(Female)`.

Both are in ml/min/1.73 m2. The choice is deliberately explicit: the design compares two standalone estimates and does not label either one measured GFR. It does not use a race coefficient. The primary discordance is `D = eGFRcys - eGFRcr`; lower D is the direction predicted to be compatible with creatinine overestimation. Standardize eGFRcr, D and grip using training data, with the same transformations frozen for validation and test sets.

Reject nonfinite or physiologically impossible assay values according to the verified field metadata and report the count. A sensitivity model uses the raw-unit log biomarker terms (with age and sex) rather than relying on the eGFR equations; this tests whether the interaction is an equation artifact. A second sensitivity uses the repeat biomarker instance only if its timing can be aligned, and labels the resulting selected population separately.

## Analysis and the two comparable methods

### Transparent primary baseline

Fit a cause-specific Cox model for first post-index recorded N17 event with:

`eGFRcr_z + D_z + grip_z + D_z*grip_z + age_z + sex + BMI_z`.

The primary scientific result is the interaction coefficient and its 95% interval, supplemented by model-based marginal five-year risks and risk differences at prespecified low/median/high grip and D values. Report proportional-hazard diagnostics, event counts, risk-set support/positivity, influence diagnostics and robust (eid-clustered) uncertainty. Restricted cubic splines for D and grip are a sensitivity model, not an opportunity to replace the prespecified interaction after seeing results. Repeat for recorded N18 as a secondary outcome with the same cohort and design.

The descriptive eGFRcr >=60/eGFRcys <60 contrast is estimated only if each cell has adequate support; otherwise report the cell as unsupported rather than merge categories. The analysis is complete-case for the required baseline fields. Report a flow of source rows, joins, missingness, exclusions, and included-versus-excluded baseline distributions. Multiple imputation or inverse-probability weighting is deferred unless field-level missingness and predictors of inclusion support a defensible missing-at-random model; any such model must be fit inside training data and cannot repair absent outcome linkage.

### Same-input nonlinear survival alternative

Fit a small discrete-time gradient-boosted hazard model on exactly the same complete-case participants and the same transformed scientific inputs (eGFRcr_z, D_z, grip_z, age_z, sex, BMI_z), with no added diagnoses, labs, imaging or treatment variables. Expand follow-up into one-year intervals through five years, with one row per person-interval while event-free and uncensored. The target is the conditional binary indicator of first recorded N17 in that interval; death removes subsequent intervals. Use shallow boosted trees (maximum depth 2–3, fixed regularization, minimum leaf size prespecified) and choose the small number of boosting iterations only on the validation split. This model is a nonlinear survival surface, not a causal model.

Randomly assign participants by `eid` to 60% train, 20% validation and 20% test with a frozen seed and report the seed. All equation-derived transformations, standardization, tuning and any imputation are learned in training only. Fit the transparent Cox model on the same splits for held-out comparison. Evaluate five-year IPCW Brier score, time-dependent AUC, calibration slope/intercept and calibration plots on the untouched test set with participant-level bootstrap intervals. Estimate the joint D-by-grip surface on a prespecified clinically relevant grid; summarize whether a threshold or non-additive region is stable across bootstrap resamples and whether it changes the clinically interpretable risk contrast. Do not call a small AUC/Brier change a scientific advance.

The alternative is retained because it can reveal thresholding or curved/non-additive regions that one linear interaction coefficient loses while preserving the same biomarker/functional question. It is deferred if there are too few paired events, inadequate interval support, failed calibration, or unstable surfaces. It is not selected because it is complex, neural, GPU-based, or marginally more predictive; this bounded model is CPU-only within 4 CPUs, 8 GiB RAM and a two-hour fitting/uncertainty budget, with streaming extraction and a bounded bootstrap.

## Missingness, selection, reverse causation and falsification

The complete-case cohort is a selected biomarker/assessment subgroup. Report selection explicitly and do not generalize to all UKB adults. Compare included and excluded participants using fields available before outcome fitting. One-side-grip and repeat-biomarker analyses are sensitivity cohorts, not pooled repairs. A two-year landmark analysis excludes anyone with a recorded N17/N18 event on or before two years after baseline and begins follow-up at two years; it tests sensitivity to occult illness and early reverse causation, at the cost of survivor selection. A one-year version may be added only as a labelled sensitivity, not as a new primary analysis.

Before outcome fitting, prespecify a nonrenal hospital negative control using the same paired arrays, such as normalized K35 (appendicitis category), only after its code semantics and event support are verified. A comparable D-by-grip association for the negative control weakens renal specificity but does not prove confounding. Permute D within sex-by-age strata using a frozen seed; the interaction and learned surface should disappear. Failure is a pipeline/leakage diagnostic, not evidence for the hypothesis. Also test whether the signal is eliminated by baseline N18 exclusion choices, the strict date rule, or raw-biomarker sensitivity.

Supportive results require adequate paired-date event support, a directionally prespecified interaction with uncertainty excluding a prespecified minimally important interaction, persistence under strict code/date and two-year-landmark sensitivities, no comparable negative-control signal, and a stable learned surface if the alternative is interpretable. They support a prognostic association in selected UKB participants.

Adverse results include a null or opposite interaction with sufficiently narrow uncertainty, no incremental signal beyond eGFRcr, dependence on early or same-day records, a comparable negative-control association, or a learned surface that is unstable or merely improves a metric trivially. These are informative negative findings. Inconclusive results include inability to verify units, baseline instance timing, 41270 code semantics, death-date meaning or administrative end date; too few paired events; inadequate risk-set support; substantial date/code discordance; or failed model diagnostics. Inconclusive results permit no clinical conclusion.

## Required deliverable and limits

The experiment must newly fit/estimate: the analytic cohort and missingness flow; paired code/date and censoring audit; eGFRcr, eGFRcys and discordance distributions; the primary N17 interaction with uncertainty and marginal risks; the N18 secondary analysis; the competing-death and strict timing sensitivities; the negative-control and within-stratum permutation diagnostics; and, if prerequisites pass, the held-out nonlinear hazard metrics and D-by-grip surface with uncertainty. Conclusions must cite these computed outputs.

The data cannot establish measured filtration accuracy, AKI severity or KDIGO stage, outpatient disease absence, causal mediation by muscle, effects of treatment, clinical utility, or transportability. Resolving those claims requires adjudicated longitudinal laboratory/urine-output AKI, measured GFR or a valid external reference, richer inflammation and muscle measures, treatment data, clinical review, an independent cohort, and ultimately a prospective decision-impact study. Revisit deferred weighting, repeat-measure trajectories, or a more complex longitudinal model only if a verified field/date manifest, adequate repeated measurements, and event support become available; revisit the nonlinear model only if the required paired-event and censoring audit passes and a stable clinically interpretable surface is observed.
