## Hypothesis to test

At the same sleep duration, irregular sleep-wake schedules increase diabetes risk among people with metabolic abnormalities.

## Study population and main data

Accelerometry, shift-work status, and baseline metabolic measures.

## Suggested approach

Start follow-up at the accelerometer assessment and test the interaction between schedule regularity and metabolic status.

## Scientific significance

Determine whether sleep-wake regularity provides additional risk information.

## Main limitations

Short-term monitoring may not represent long-term habits, and reverse causation is possible.
