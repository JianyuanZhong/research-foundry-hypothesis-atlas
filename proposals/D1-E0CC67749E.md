## Unresolved question and clinical importance

Among adults recorded as invasively ventilated at ICU hour 48 after recent vasopressor support, substantial positive recorded fluid balance, adequate recorded MAP, urine and potassium, no active RRT/ECMO, and no recent loop exposure, is starting or restarting a qualifying furosemide or bumetanide delivery record during the next 12 hours associated with a clinically important increase in being alive and recorded outside an ICU at day 7, without prespecified recorded renal, RRT, terminal-status, or death harms?

This could identify a stabilized, fluid-positive ICU population for prospective testing of de-resuscitative diuresis. An observational association cannot recommend treatment: it may reflect clinician selection, recorded rather than actual delivery, or discharge and transfer processes.

The strongest available evidence supports only uncertainty and feasibility. I inspected the full XML for Bircher et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC13154636; DOI 10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100075), which describes uncertainty about when and how fluid de-escalation should be targeted; the full XML for Kuriyama et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC13476641; DOI 10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120), which reports heterogeneous mostly low/very-low-certainty diuretic evidence; and Yehya et al. 2019 (PMCID PMC6812447; DOI 10.1164/rccm.201810-2050CP), which supports caution with composite failure-free endpoints. These sources do not validate this exposure, endpoint, or causal analysis. The research-ambition README and availability restrictions were inspected; the cancer demonstration main article and STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not claimed as read.

Hypothesis: in the repaired retrospective population, the equal-clone A12-minus-B12 interval for the lower-bound destination-neutral outcome D7-RINO (alive and recorded outside ICU at day 7, with death forced unfavorable) has a 95% bootstrap lower confidence limit above +0.05, while recorded-harm, endpoint-component, temporal-validity, exposure-semantics, positivity, balance and falsification gates pass. Null and adverse findings are informative.

The substantive advance is endpoint validity. The prior AIFD7 bundled HOME, rehabilitation, skilled nursing, psychiatry, generic facility, open non-ICU location, death and ICU states in one favorable/unfavorable construct. This successor makes the primary endpoint independent of discharge destination: D7-RINO is favorable only for a closed admission with no exact death and no terminal or acute-hospital disposition, or an open admission with exactly one active non-ICU transfer at D7 and no ICU interval covering D7; exact death and terminal/acute-hospital outcomes are unfavorable; unresolved topology is bounded [0,1]. Discharge destination is a reporting overlay, never the primary favorable assignment. The experiment concerns recorded ICU topology, not recovery, survival after discharge, function, or treatment benefit.
