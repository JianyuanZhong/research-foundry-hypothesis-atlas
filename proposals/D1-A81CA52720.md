# HCC observation-process child — substantive revision

Parent: [prior hypothesis]. The remaining ambiguity is whether an apparent response–recurrence association is response-driven prognostic information or a product of differential surveillance, treatment selection, and outcome ascertainment. This revision makes that rival testable as a two-part observation-process estimand, adds a negative-control outcome, and specifies a clinically interpretable adequacy cohort. No model or clinical outcome has been computed.

## Scientific opening and unresolved claim

The strongest evidence currently supports that baseline tumor burden, liver reserve, AFP, treatment heterogeneity, and imaging features are prognostic in retrospective HCC cohorts [K1], that imaging features require complementary expert interpretation [K2], and that detection/immortal-time and ascertainment bias are material threats in hospital follow-up [K3]. It does not establish that a local EHR response trajectory adds information after the observation opportunity is measured, nor that an EHR label is a validated radiologic response or recurrence.

The unresolved claim is narrower and falsifiable: among patients with an index locoregional HCC treatment and adequate post-treatment observation opportunity, does an explicitly documented response trajectory add held-out information about subsequently documented recurrence/progression after conditioning on baseline disease/treatment and measured surveillance? The primary estimand is a patient-level, chronological held-out comparison of (i) baseline + measured observation process versus (ii) that model plus response trajectory. It is not a causal treatment effect, a mechanism, or a claim about residual viable tumor.

The key rival is explicit: more intensive follow-up and treatment selection may create both more response labels and more recurrence labels. A second rival is that treatment intensity and disease severity jointly determine response, follow-up, and recurrence. The design therefore separates response ascertainment, observation opportunity, and recurrence ascertainment. It does not identify unobserved outside-hospital surveillance or unmeasured label error.

## Cohort, timing, and labels

T0 is the earliest qualifying first locoregional procedure in procedures (手术, 开始时间): prespecified TACE/embolization, thermal ablation, hepatic resection, or other locoregional treatment; ambiguous terms remain “other.” Require HCC diagnosis in diagnoses (诊断名称, 诊断类型) or an adjudicated imaging/diagnosis report. Exclude prior qualifying treatment in the available record. Baseline is 90 days before through T0 and contains age, sex, treatment category, pre-T0 tumor-burden proxy, cirrhosis/liver-disease proxy, AFP, liver-function labs, and prior treatment count. No post-T0 field enters baseline.

The response window is T0+30 through T0+90 days. A liver imaging report in examinations (检查, 检查所见, 检查诊断, 开始时间) must explicitly support complete response, partial response, or indeterminate; missing is not complete response. Follow-up begins at the last response-window imaging date (or T0+90 if none), ends at first explicit post-response recurrence/progression, loss to follow-up, documented death, or administrative end 2026-01-01. Recurrence requires dated post-response imaging or a diagnosis linked to a valid post-response encounter; history, postoperative status, and orders alone are not outcomes. Primary analysis requires 180 days observable follow-up; all-follow-up inverse-probability-of-censoring is secondary.

## Observation-process estimand and negative control

For every patient derive, without using post-response outcome text:
1. Response ascertainment: whether at least one liver imaging report in T0+30–T0+90 explicitly supports complete, partial, or indeterminate response; report date, modality, number of reports, and category.
2. Observation opportunity: all dated encounters and imaging reports in T0–T0+365, number and timing of liver imaging reports in T0–T0+90 and T0+90–T0+365, and interval from T0 to first post-response imaging.
3. Surveillance orders: dated orders in orders (医嘱(非药品), 开立时间, 开始时间, 结束时间, 医嘱状态, 频次) requesting liver/upper-abdominal imaging or related surveillance, used only as an observation-process descriptor and never as an outcome.
4. Treatment-selection proxies: treatment category/intensity at T0, subsequent qualifying procedures, and temporally valid orders/medication exposure when available. These are prognostic/selection descriptors, not causal treatment effects.

The observation audit reports, by response category and surveillance stratum, number with explicit response, number with post-response imaging, number with any encounter, follow-up duration, and number with documented recurrence. It compares recurrence ascertainment across response categories after accounting for measured observation opportunity. This can distinguish a signal concentrated in intensively observed patients from one that persists across measured strata, but cannot identify unmeasured outside-hospital surveillance or label validity.

A negative-control outcome is a non-HCC, non-treatment-related event that should not be caused by response but may be more frequently documented in intensively observed patients, such as a dated benign or unrelated diagnosis in diagnoses or a non-liver examination event in examinations, with the exact term list frozen before model fitting. The response model is evaluated for this negative control using the same chronological split and observation variables. A response signal for the negative control, or a large difference in negative-control ascertainment across response groups after observation adjustment, is adverse evidence for a surveillance/ascertainment explanation. A null negative control is not proof of no bias.

The primary analysis is restricted to patients with adequate observation opportunity, prespecified as at least one dated liver imaging report in T0+30–T0+90 and at least one dated encounter or imaging report in T0+90–T0+365, with 180 days observable follow-up. Patients without this opportunity remain in the observation audit and are not silently treated as recurrence-free. A secondary all-follow-up analysis uses inverse-probability-of-censoring weights based only on pre-outcome observation variables. This makes the clinical estimand explicit: prediction among patients who can be observed enough to contribute a recurrence label, not prediction for an unobserved population.

## Models, uncertainty, and falsification

Baseline: penalized Cox or pooled logistic model using only pre-T0 variables (age, sex, treatment, tumor-burden proxy, liver disease, AFP, liver function, time since diagnosis), with missingness indicators. Fit on earlier index years, tune on a middle validation year, and evaluate on held-out later years; never split by row. Report integrated Brier score, time-dependent AUC, calibration intercept/slope, risk differences, and bootstrap intervals.

Alternative: patient-level gradient-boosted survival model adding response category and longitudinal summaries (AFP slope/level, liver-function change, imaging count/timing, residual-disease indicators) from T0 through the response window, plus the measured observation-process variables. This is scientifically useful because it tests whether temporal response information adds what baseline and measured observation intensity lose; it is not selected for complexity. A latent residual-versus-de-novo mechanistic model is deferred because local data cannot distinguish etiologies without validated imaging adjudication, pathology/genomics/ctDNA. CPU is appropriate; GPU is not needed. Planned solver envelope: 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB, 2 GPUs, 28800 seconds; no model has been fitted.

Supportive: response remains associated after observation adjustment and the response-augmented model improves held-out calibration/Brier score with stable direction across treatment strata, calendar periods, and adequate-surveillance strata; recurrence ascertainment is not materially different across response groups after accounting for measured observation opportunity; and the negative-control outcome does not show a comparable response signal.

Adverse: association disappears after observation adjustment, is confined to one modality or one observation stratum, reverses across periods, or the alternative is worse than baseline; this favors burden, selection, or ascertainment explanations. A response signal for the negative control is also adverse.

Inconclusive: sparse adjudicated response/recurrence, wide uncertainty, poor calibration, or excessive missingness; this does not refute biology. A null in poorly observed patients is not evidence that response has no biological meaning.

Computation can check joins, dates, leakage, surveillance definitions, model fitting, and conclusion/output consistency. It cannot establish mechanism, treatment benefit, residual viable tumor, or an action threshold. Expert radiology/clinical adjudication of response, recurrence, tumor burden, and treatment intent is essential; prospective validation and ideally imaging/pathology or molecular residual-disease data are needed for stronger claims.

## Deliverable and alternatives

The solver must construct the frozen cohort, audit response/recurrence labels, derive observation-process variables, fit both models on a chronological patient split, estimate uncertainty and calibration, evaluate the negative-control outcome, and report whether response adds information after measured observation adjustment. Completion is established by cohort flow, label-audit results, observation-stratum tables, negative-control results, held-out metrics, confidence intervals, and conclusions tied to those outputs—not by readiness or a favorable hypothesis. No computed results are claimed here.

Not chosen: a latent mechanistic residual-versus-de-novo model is deferred because local data cannot distinguish etiologies without validated imaging adjudication, pathology/genomics/ctDNA. A causal treatment-effect analysis is not identified by this observational design. A simple baseline remains necessary to prevent a learned model from being credited for information already present in baseline severity or observation intensity. Revisit the mechanistic alternative only if expert-validated imaging labels, pathology/genomics, or ctDNA become available.
