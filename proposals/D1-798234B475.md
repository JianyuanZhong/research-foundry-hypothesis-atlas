# Auditable fixed-capacity enrichment of observed transfusion-risk heterogeneity

## Deliverable and unresolved claim

This MIMIC-IV 3.1 adaptation tests an observed-risk claim, not a treatment effect. Before fitting, the strongest supported claim is only that the snapshot contains temporally indexed hemoglobin, physiologic, PRBC input, ICU procedure, admission and patient fields sufficient to attempt this analysis. The dictionary audit found the prespecified item labels, but did not establish event counts, unit/status quality, or estimability.

Among adult ICU episodes reaching a first hemoglobin landmark of 6.5-8.0 g/dL with no observed recent PRBC, does a prespecified record-defined physiologic-stress interaction identify, at a fixed 10% capacity, a held-out subgroup with a reproducibly different 24-hour observed deterioration contrast between recorded PRBC and no recorded PRBC, beyond hemoglobin-only and stress-only selection, with calibration and locked later-era transport? This matters because a positive result would identify a compact phenotype for prospective adjudicated transfusion-trial enrichment or review prioritization; a negative result would prevent premature use of a plausible stress phenotype.

## Modifier, outcome and estimand

Use `icu/chartevents.csv.gz` (`subject_id,hadm_id,stay_id,charttime,storetime,itemid,valuenum,valueuom,warning`) and `icu/d_items.csv.gz` for labels/units. Within [t0-12h,t0], with clinical and recording time no later than t0, high stress requires one supply plus one demand/support component, or two independent supply components. Supply: lactate 50813/52442/53154 >=2, MAP 220052/220181 <65, or active pressor inputs 221289,229617,221653,221749,229630-229632,221906,221986,222315. Demand/support: HR 220045 >=110, RR 220210 >=24, or active ventilation procedures 225792/225794. Low stress requires no positive component plus valid supply and demand coverage; otherwise stress is indeterminate. This is a record phenotype, not ischemia, tissue hypoxia, oxygen delivery/consumption, or transfusion indication.

From tau through tau+24h, assign first mutually exclusive state: D if admissions.deathtime is in the window; E if a new ventilation procedure (224385 or 225792), new listed pressor start when none active at tau, or new renal-replacement procedure (225441,225802,225803,225805,225809,225955); X if ICU exit before D/E; N if no event; U if irreconcilable/missing times. Primary Y=D or E, with D/E-specific cumulative incidence and X competing. Fit label-specific standardized observed risks and define H=Delta_high-Delta_low, where Delta_s=Risk(Y|A=1,S=s)-Risk(Y|A=0,S=s), and subject score R_i=risk_i(A=1)-risk_i(A=0). R is not an individual causal effect.

## Baseline, alternative and evaluation

Fit B0/Bhet/Bmask cause-specific penalized hazards for D,E,X. B0 uses A, S, spline hemoglobin, prespecified pre-t0 physiology/support/context, missingness and observation intensity without A-by-S; Bhet adds only A-by-S and is the primary heterogeneity model; Bmask keeps masks/counts/context but masks physiologic values to test process explanation. Hemoglobin-only and stress-only selectors are simple non-model baselines. A substantive alternative J1 is a masked chronological GRU over twelve one-hour pre-t0 bins of the same streams, masks and time-since-observation channels, static context and A, predicting identical hazards. It could reveal recent trajectories/asynchrony lost by aggregate baseline summaries; it is not justified by a small predictive gain.

Use deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 development split. Fit only in anchor-year development eras 2008-2016; use 2017-2019 locked calibration and 2020-2022 untouched transport, without refitting or changing q/cutoffs. On held-out episodes evaluate label-specific calibration, H and D/E components, R distribution, overlap/ESS/balance and event capture/PPV at exactly q=5%, 10% primary, 20%. Select by |R| for model selectors; retain signed R. Compare all methods on identical episodes, outcomes, splits and bootstrap procedure. Report 1,000 subject bootstrap intervals and simultaneous intervals for H/capacity comparisons. Completion is a valid gated estimate or complete not-estimable report.

## Gates, falsification and interpretation

Report flow, support, U, cell counts, event counts, missingness, units/status ambiguity, care-unit mix and observation intensity. If any A-by-stress cell has <50 subjects or <20 Y events, or common support/ESS/calibration fails, declare that estimand not estimable rather than pool or retune. Falsifications locked before test outcomes: permute A within hemoglobin/stress/care-unit/year strata; permute stress within hemoglobin/A/care-unit/year; compare Bmask to Bhet; test future A against pre-t0 escalation; item/landmark and stress-component sensitivities; non-PRBC process controls; q results by care unit, hospital day, era and observation intensity; and scan for post-t0 clinical/store times, terminal/death fields and post-exposure physiology.

Support requires all gates, simultaneous H interval outside [-0.05,0.05], compatible Bhet/J1 sign, permutation collapse, no comparable Bmask result, and at q=0.10 improved capture/PPV over both simple selectors with calibration retained and the same direction in locked later eras within +/-0.05 risk points. This supports only reproducible observed-risk enrichment for prospective adjudicated validation. Adverse results include reversal, simple-selector parity, Bmask parity, permutation persistence, miscalibration, support loss or transport reversal; they argue against use and diagnose process/era explanations. Inconclusive results include not-estimable cells, wide intervals, unresolved units/status, unstable J1 or adequate prediction without stable label-specific contrast; they support no clinical action.

MIMIC lacks adjudicated ischemia, oxygen delivery/consumption, complete active bleeding, transfusion intent/rationale, compatibility, bedside appropriateness, rescue completeness, randomized assignment and external validation. Notes, diagnoses and procedures cannot supply those claims without validated adjudication. Stronger causal, benefit/harm, utility, bedside-threshold and generalizability conclusions require expert review plus prospective/randomized or external studies.
