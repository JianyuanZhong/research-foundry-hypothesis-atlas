# Episode 26: respiratory benefit with landmark creatinine noninferiority after post-shock recorded-loop initiation

**Parent:** `[prior hypothesis]`

## Substantive repair and clinical question

The parent is the highest-ranked valid MIMIC design. It correctly freezes each subject's earliest broad hour-48 ventilated ICU opportunity, uses a source-symmetric ICU `inputevents` recorded-delivery contrast, masks the retrospectively reconstructed baseline-ventilation Boolean, keeps all other eligibility and covariates decision-available, treats death and ICU exit as common competing closures, and makes definite recorded respiratory recovery by day 28 (DRR28) an explicitly partially identified primary benefit endpoint.

Its remaining clinically consequential weakness is the renal side of the decision. The supportive branch excludes a large increase in observed RRT7, but dialysis is a late and uncommon event. A strategy could appear to improve respiratory recovery while increasing substantial subdialysis creatinine worsening and still pass the parent's renal gate. Conversely, selecting only people with preserved "renal reserve," as the Episode-25 child proposed, fragments an already bounded endpoint into coarse, sparse strata and does not answer the broad decision.

This child retains the broad parent population and DRR28 benefit estimand and adds a prespecified, separately reported **48-hour landmark recorded creatinine worsening (LCW48)** harm estimand. It tests a bivariate clinical hypothesis rather than hiding benefit and harm in an arbitrary utility score:

1. qualifying ICU-input-system recorded furosemide/bumetanide delivery before the 24-hour decision closure increases the overlap-population DRR28 risk by more than 5 percentage points; and
2. it does not increase LCW48 by 5 percentage points or more, while retaining the parent's stricter recorded-terminal and RRT7 gates.

LCW48 is a record-defined renal-stress signal, not acute tubular injury, true KDIGO AKI, or toxicity. Diuresis and hemoconcentration can raise creatinine without structural injury, and sparse or treatment-dependent testing can miss changes. Those limits motivate a fixed landmark, missing-outcome bounds, and a no-safety-language rule.

## Falsification and interpretation

A **supportive** result requires all of the following: the one-sided 95% lower limit for `Delta_DRR_L` exceeds +0.05; the one-sided 95% upper limit for `Delta_Cr_U` is below +0.05; the parent's conservative upper recorded death/hospice/acute-discharge effect is below +0.03 and observed-RRT7 effect below +0.05; no adverse ALDL7 or destination reversal; each arm ESS at least 75; eligible n at least 200; at least 100 qualifying A deliveries and 100 B completions/compatible closures; acceptable calibration/balance/weights; and all partition, source, key, unit, clock, clone, bound, negative-control, and sensitivity checks pass. The LCW48 individual-bound width and weighted unknown fraction must each be <=0.20 in each arm with an absolute arm difference <=0.05; otherwise renal noninferiority cannot be declared. Support means only that the record-defined strategy shows a hypothesis-generating respiratory benefit signal while excluding the prespecified excess in landmark recorded creatinine worsening. It does not mean causal benefit or renal safety.

An **adverse** result is emitted if the one-sided 95% lower limit for `Delta_Cr_L` is at least +0.05, or under the parent's rules if recorded terminal harm is at least +0.03, observed RRT7 harm is at least +0.05 under both date brackets, or the DRR28/ALDL7 upper bound excludes no harm. This is an adverse record signal. It cannot be called loop-induced AKI or toxicity.

The respiratory margin is **falsified** if the one-sided 95% upper limit for `Delta_DRR_U` is <=+0.05. A precise LCW48 contrast below +0.05 cannot rescue a margin-falsified benefit hypothesis. Conversely, respiratory support with a wide renal bound is not supportive overall.

**Inconclusive** includes all other estimable results: discordant benefit and renal evidence; wide DRR28 or LCW48 bounds; precise DRR effects between 0 and +0.05; missing landmark creatinine beyond the frozen limits; differential testing; low n/ESS/positivity; >25% E-first closures; >1% ambiguity, contradiction, or late exposure confirmation; >5% B eMAR loop contamination; sensitivity reversal; failed negative controls; or unreconciled state/bound arithmetic. Inconclusive renal evidence must not be restated as no renal harm. A narrow null or adverse result is scientifically valuable and must not trigger threshold, window, item, population, or endpoint changes.

Prespecified sensitivities are planned-extubation item 227194 alone; creatinine baseline windows 6h and 24h; follow-up windows 42-54h and 24-72h; continuous change; peak-through-72h explicitly labeled surveillance-sensitive; Paused exposure; treatment-first ties; 6/12-hour treatment grace; contemporaneous store-time; date-bracket extremes; and the parent's observed-odds bias grid. Item 52546 remains an exclusion/missing-key audit, not a sensitivity, because no source rows are admission-linked. No sensitivity may replace the frozen primary.
