# Does creatinine–cystatin-C discordance distinguish acute-coded from chronic-coded renal risk when primary-diagnosis confirmation is unavailable?

## Episode 8 successor and unresolved claim

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The strongest directly relevant evidence inspected is Haines et al., *Comparison of Cystatin C and Creatinine in the Assessment of Measured Kidney Function during Critical Illness*, locally available at `references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt`. In 38 mechanically ventilated adults, serial creatinine, cystatin C, iohexol clearance and rectus-femoris ultrasound showed that creatinine-based eGFR could overestimate measured dysfunction during muscle loss. That establishes biological plausibility in a selected ICU cohort with unavailable-to-UKB measured-GFR and muscle anchors; it does not establish prognostic value or AKI specificity in community UK Biobank participants.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is: among UK Biobank adults free of a dated N17/N18/N19 code at biomarker assessment, positive creatinine-based minus cystatin-C-based eGFR discordance (D), particularly with lower grip, is more strongly associated with a first subsequent **all-diagnosis hospital-coded N17-only phenotype** than with an N18-only phenotype, after adjustment for pre-index diagnosis-date contact burden and broad renal history. This can inform whether discordance is worth investigating as an acute-coded vulnerability signal versus a chronic-coded kidney signal. It cannot establish that a patient has adjudicated AKI or CKD, or that cystatin-C-triggered surveillance improves outcomes.

The episode-8 source audit found a crucial availability constraint: 41270/41280 contain ICD-10-like N17/N18/N19 codes and paired dates, but nonempty 41271 values inspected at scale include strings such as `V252`, `6262`, `78909`, and `7890`, rather than N17/N18/N19. Thus 41271/41281 cannot be treated as a verified ICD-10 primary-inpatient renal endpoint in this snapshot. The primary-inpatient confirmation proposed by the parent is removed as an estimand, not substituted with an easier outcome. Its code-system crosswalk is an explicit missing dependency. The primary question remains testable using the verified all-diagnosis arrays, with a stronger ascertainment limitation.

## Exposure and outcome definitions

- Female k=0.7, a=-0.241; male k=0.9, a=-0.302.
- eGFRcr = 142 x min(Scr/k,1)^a x max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) x 0.9938^Age x 1.012 if female.
- eGFRcys = 133 x min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) x max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) x 0.9961^Age x 0.932 if female.
- D=eGFRcr-eGFRcys; positive D is the hypothesized creatinine-reassuring direction.

Primary grip is the maximum valid left/right value; weaker-side and sex-standardized grip are sensitivity analyses. BMI and weight are adjustment variables, not measured muscle-mass proxies. Standardize D using training data only.

## Estimands and analyses

The primary estimand is the cause-specific hazard and five-year Aalen–Johansen cumulative-incidence contrast for N17-only versus N18-only across the 10th-to-90th percentile D profile and low-versus-median grip profile. Report hazard ratios, absolute risks, risk differences and participant-bootstrap intervals. Prespecified materiality is at least a 0.5 percentage-point N17-minus-N18 five-year risk differential in the low-grip/high-D profile with uncertainty not compatible with reversal. This is an information criterion, not surveillance utility.

Fit separate cause-specific models for N17-only, N18-only, mixed/other and death. Death is censored for cause-specific hazards and retained in Aalen–Johansen; Fine–Gray is sensitivity. Nested models:

- M0: age, sex, BMI, weight, grip and eGFRcr.
- M1: M0 plus eGFRcys (or D; never all three together).
- M2: M1 plus continuous D-by-grip interaction and restricted cubic splines for age and eGFRcr.
- M3: M2 plus log1p(pre_contact_days) and pre_any_contact.

The acute-versus-chronic claim requires a larger N17-than-N18 contrast in M3, persistence under broad-history exclusion, and consistency under the documented all-diagnosis ascertainment definition. It must not be described as “primary inpatient confirmed.” If all-diagnosis coding is too sparse or date pairing is unstable, the result is inconclusive.

The substantive learned alternative uses exactly M0–M3 inputs in a CPU gradient-boosted multi-task discrete-time competing-risk model with separate N17-only, N18-only, mixed/other and death outputs. Use participant-level SHA-256(eid) buckets: 0–59 train, 60–79 tune, 80–99 test; eid is never a predictor. Use shallow trees, bounded tuning and early stopping. Compare held-out event-specific Brier scores, calibration intercept/slope, absolute event-type contrasts and accumulated-local-effect curves with bootstrap intervals. Its scientific purpose is to reveal nonlinear D/grip thresholds and endpoint-specific heterogeneity that one interaction can miss; a small predictive gain alone is not success. ## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence requires unit/date/join gates to pass; adequate N17-only and N18-only events; a prespecified D-by-low-grip association materially larger for N17-only than N18-only; persistence after contact adjustment and broad-history exclusion; a negative-control contact effect materially smaller than the renal contrast; and a concordant learned-model endpoint contrast with held-out calibration. It supports an acute-coded versus chronic-coded prognostic distinction only.

Adverse evidence is a larger N18 association, a null/reversed contrast, attenuation after contact adjustment or broad-history exclusion, a similarly large nonrenal-contact association, or learned-model calibration failure. It argues against an acute-specific coded interpretation but does not prove cystatin C has no value.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed units, duplicate/non-one-to-one joins, impossible dates, sparse N17/N18 events, severe missingness, unpaired code/date burden, leakage, unstable bootstrap or nonconvergence. The unavailable primary-code-system crosswalk is not repaired by relabeling 41271 values. Do not replace a failed N17/N18 test by an all-renal endpoint.

QA can verify source hashes, joins, formulas, paired-date rules, contact proxies, risk sets, splits, calibration and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It cannot establish AKI severity, CKD stage, measured GFR, urine output, inflammation, muscle mass, causality, treatment effect, surveillance utility, clinical appropriateness or transportability. Those require adjudicated laboratory trajectories/urine output, measured GFR, body-composition anchors, treatment/encounter context, expert adjudication and independent prospective validation.
