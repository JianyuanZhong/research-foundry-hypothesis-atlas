# Skeptical methodologist audit: retain the record-density-standardized kidney-specificity design

## Decision

I recommend retaining the scientific design embodied by the assigned parents, with no new population, timing, outcome, estimand, analysis, or falsification evolution. The record-density layer is the one defensible ascertainment-validity advance available in this UKB snapshot. A further extension would either duplicate the same broad-record proxy or require evidence that the catalog does not contain: encounter identifiers, validated admission/utilization measures, narrative clinical context, outpatient capture, adjudicated CKD, measured GFR, or cause-specific censoring.

This is a scientific retention decision, not a packaging recommendation. The current design should proceed only with its existing integrity and nonrescue gates.

## Claim separation

The strongest available evidence supports computational feasibility only: the frozen UKB data contain the required visit-1 predictors, biomarkers, death dates, and paired inpatient diagnosis/date arrays; the inherited artifacts establish O1_hold=2,334, capacity n=466, AFG1/AF1 overlap 435 with 31 directional swaps, and aggregate event feasibility (40 all-holdout first inpatient N18-before-death events). These facts do not support AFG1 superiority, kidney-specificity, incident or persistent CKD, measured-GFR accuracy, causality, or patient benefit.

The unresolved claim is narrower: in the exact frozen AFG1-versus-AF1 allocation, does the N18 contrast exceed the broad nonrenal diagnosis-record contrast after standardizing both to the same pre-index record-density distribution? A supportive result remains coded-record enrichment in this internal target population, not a clinical policy recommendation.

## Audit of outcome-blindness

The record-density variable is outcome-blind if, and only if, the implementation enforces the parent’s order:

1. freeze O1_hold, the two immutable 466-person rosters, overlap/swap hashes, and RD definitions;
2. for every O1_hold participant, compute R_i from only the lookback interval [t1 minus five calendar years, t1), using the frozen nonrenal prefix set A–M/S–T/Z and same-position valid dates;
3. freeze RD0 (R=0), RD1 (R=1), RD2+ (R>=2), and q_s=N_s/2334;
4. only then open policy-specific post-t1 E18, G, death, yield, or bootstrap results.

The shared code set does not itself create leakage: the lookback records precede t1, and the code set is fixed before policy-specific outcomes are read. The design must continue to treat a qualifying code with a blank or unparseable paired date as an integrity failure, never as a nonevent. Repeated array positions count as records, not admissions or encounters.

## Control audit

G is an appropriate conservative ascertainment proxy for the stated question, provided it is described exactly as a first post-t1 nonrenal inpatient diagnosis-record endpoint. It excludes N, R, O, P, Q, and U prefixes and uses the same position-paired arrays as E18. It is not a validated negative control for hospital contact, frailty, disease burden, or causal inference. M, S, and T codes may be related to grip, falls, injury, or genuine illness; therefore a positive or adverse G contrast can reflect biology or morbidity as well as recording intensity.

No stronger control is computable from the configured UKB fields. Narrowing the code set would require external clinical adjudication of which diagnoses are unrelated to grip and kidney disease; replacing record counts with encounters is impossible without encounter IDs; and using narrative or outpatient utilization is unavailable. The existing design handles this limitation correctly by allowing supportive, adverse, mixed, and inconclusive outcomes and by refusing to call G an admissions measure. An adverse or mixed result is clinically useful because it blocks a kidney-specific deployment interpretation; it does not prove AF1 superiority or equivalence.

## Estimands, denominators, and transportability

The fixed-denominator parent estimands must remain primary and nonrescuing:

- Delta18 = fixed 466-slot N18 yield contrast;
- DeltaG = fixed 466-slot broad nonrenal yield contrast;
- Psi = Delta18 - DeltaG.

The record-density estimand is a qualification, not a replacement:

- r18_s(p) and rG_s(p) use all policy members in stratum s;
- q_s=N_s/2334 is frozen from the entire O1_hold;
- Y18_RD(p) and YG_RD(p) standardize to that O1_hold distribution;
- Psi_RD=Delta18_RD-DeltaG_RD.

These denominators are transportable only to the frozen O1_hold target, not to UK Biobank participants generally, another health system, or a clinical population. The existing requirements to report every stratum count, policy count, numerator, rate, q_s, weighted contribution, symmetric-difference composition, and fixed-denominator identity are necessary to expose extrapolation. The existing minimum-count, overlap, finite-bootstrap, variance, and integrity gates should be retained. No survivor, complete-case, event, swap, or outcome-dependent denominator is acceptable.

## Interpretation and evidence limits

Supportive: inherited hierarchy/N18 support, all RD gates pass, the paired lower limit for Psi_RD is above zero, and generic enrichment remains below the frozen one-event materiality margin. This supports only standardized kidney-coded enrichment over the broad nonrenal record proxy in O1_hold.

Adverse: Psi_RD is below zero with uncertainty and/or generic enrichment is materially positive. This falsifies the narrow kidney-specificity claim for this snapshot/allocation, not equivalence, AF1 superiority, absence of kidney disease, or lack of grip value.

Mixed: Psi_RD is positive but the generic contrast is materially enriched, or standardized and unstandardized specificity disagree. The result requires an explicit generic-ascertainment qualification and cannot support kidney-specific deployment.

Inconclusive: sparse strata/swaps, failed integrity or chronology, boundary equality, unstable variance/bootstraps, or inherited upstream/N18 failure. Inconclusive means unresolved, never no difference.

Automatic verification can establish source identity, field presence, joins, frozen memberships, chronology, outcome-blind RD construction, fixed denominators, standardization arithmetic, bootstrap arithmetic, gates, and whether conclusion sentences follow computed outputs. It cannot establish complete capture, admissions, incident/persistent/adjudicated CKD, measured GFR, outpatient disease, censoring/emigration, mechanism, actionability, workflow burden, cost, fairness, transportability, safety, or patient benefit. Those require clinical adjudication, missing linkage, external validation, expert review, and a prospective testing-strategy study.

## Recommendation

Retain the assigned parents’ record-density-standardized experiment as the scientific successor. Do not add a new subgroup, mortality endpoint, timing extension, alternate control, or second density estimand: each is either sparse/nonidentifying in the 2,334-person/466-slot design or depends on unavailable clinical evidence. The scientifically important advance is the pre-index ascertainment qualification already present; the remaining uncertainty is clinical validity, not an unmet computable design feature.
