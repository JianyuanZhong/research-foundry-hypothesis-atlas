# Measurement-robust six-hour landmark analysis of the hypotension–creatinine signal

## Scientific deliverable

The future solver must newly construct an auditable six-hour eICU landmark cohort and interval ledger, fit the prespecified matched transparent models, and fit a measurement-aware state-space alternative on the same stays and outcomes. It must emit:

1. the parent estimand: the standardized 48-hour observed-creatinine-rise burden gradient contrast between documented prompt-after-low and delayed/no-record timing;
2. that contrast within fixed development-derived MAP-observation-density strata and with explicit observation-process covariates;
3. a parallel, independently defined non-invasive-MAP estimate using `vitalAperiodic.noninvasivemean`, never pooled with periodic MAP;
4. a paired-channel latent-MAP estimate, only if paired-channel support and model diagnostics pass the predeclared gate;
5. hospital-specific effects, between-hospital heterogeneity, hospital-held-out transport, calibration and cluster-bootstrap uncertainty;
6. a conclusion labelled supportive, adverse or inconclusive that identifies whether the observed signal is robust to measurement channel and density.

Completion requires the cohort flow, stay-level and interval ledgers, fitted model objects or coefficient tables, paired-channel diagnostics, standardized contrasts, uncertainty, held-out transport and every falsification output. Readiness, source availability and a successful fit do not establish the hypothesis. Discovery has fit no scientific model and claims no direction or effect size.

## Unresolved question, evidence boundary and hypothesis

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is only that the frozen eICU 2.0 snapshot contains ICU-relative periodic MAP, non-invasive MAP, ICU-keyed creatinine results with result-revision offsets, infusion records, ICU disposition, admission descriptors, Apache descriptors and hospital identifiers that can be joined by the specified keys. Direct managed header inspection on 2026-09-14 verified those fields. A full-stream availability audit is retained in `support/diagnostic_eicu_availability.txt`; its row and label counts are availability evidence, not evidence that a pressor was administered, that hypotension caused kidney injury, or that any contrast transports.

The parent’s unresolved claim is whether documented rescue timing modifies the association between early low-MAP burden and a later observed creatinine-rise signal within hospitals and across hospitals, versus the association being produced by testing opportunity and competing ICU exit. A residual uncertainty remains after that design: the primary burden is built from recorded periodic MAP, so sampling density, channel choice and temporal interpolation may make “low-MAP burden” partly a measurement process. A patient monitored more densely can have both more opportunities to record a low value and more opportunities to have creatinine tested.

The experiment tests:

> Among adult ICU stays that have a pre-low creatinine baseline, a first raw periodic MAP below 65 during the first six ICU hours, no qualifying early creatinine rise, and remain observable beyond six hours, is the prompt-after-low versus delayed/no documented-pressor difference in the 6–48-hour observed creatinine-rise burden gradient preserved when low-MAP burden is evaluated across observation-density strata and with an independent non-invasive-MAP channel?

Primary directional hypothesis: the prompt-after-low timing context attenuates the low-MAP burden gradient by at least 2 absolute percentage points at 48 hours, and the sign and clinically material magnitude remain after explicit test-opportunity/exit modeling, fixed observation-density stratification, and the independent non-invasive-MAP sensitivity. The latent paired-channel model is confirmatory of measurement robustness only if its diagnostics pass; it is not allowed to rescue an otherwise failed primary analysis.

This is a noncausal, recorded-process hypothesis. “Prompt,” “pressor,” and “timing” refer to an infusionDrug record, not verified administration, start, dose, indication or intent. The endpoint is an observed creatinine-rise signal, not adjudicated KDIGO AKI. A stable association across channels would advance knowledge by separating a transportable hemodynamic-recording signal from one that depends on how MAP is observed. It would not justify changing vasopressors or MAP targets.

The hypothesis is falsified by a reproducible null or reversal of the timing interaction, by loss of the direction in supported held-out hospitals, by failure of the contrast in most observation-density strata, or by an attenuation to below the 2-point margin after the independent channel and test/exit decomposition. If periodic-MAP results are strong but non-invasive or latent results are null, the appropriate conclusion is measurement-channel dependence or inconclusive evidence, not pressor harm or a biological renal null.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Use the frozen eICU 2.0 snapshot `[source checksum]`. The unit is an ICU stay keyed by `patientunitstayid`; repeated stays are retained. All stays sharing `uniquepid` remain in one split and a `uniquepid`-clustered sensitivity is required. Time zero is ICU admission and all offsets are integer minutes relative to ICU admission.

Primary cohort:

- age >=18 from `patient.age`; parse `> 89` as 90 and retain an age-censored flag;
- at least two numeric plausible `vitalPeriodic.systemicmean` observations in [20,200] mmHg, with raw observation offsets in [0,360];
- at least one numeric creatinine in [0,60] minutes with case-folded `labname=creatinine`, and both `labmeasurenamesystem` and `labmeasurenameinterface` verified as mg/dL;
- nonmissing, nonnegative `patient.unitdischargeoffset`, and `unitdischargeoffset>360`;
- a first raw periodic low-MAP time in [0,360], defined as the earliest valid `systemicmean<65`;
- the first qualifying creatinine result at or before that first low-MAP time;
- no qualifying result >= baseline + 0.3 mg/dL after baseline through minute 360.

The first qualifying creatinine is the smallest `labresultoffset` in [0,60] satisfying the frozen label/unit rule, not the minimum value. Deduplicate exact `labid` rows; if multiple valid rows remain at the selected offset, use their median and report the count. Exclude urinary creatinine, invalid numeric values and unknown/incompatible units. Do not convert unverified units. The broader cohort without a pre-low baseline is a mandatory sensitivity labelled post-exposure-baseline selection. Stays without a periodic low MAP are a descriptive companion cohort and do not enter the timing interaction.

The primary periodic MAP ledger uses raw `vitalPeriodic` observations. Collapse duplicate stay/time rows by prespecified median. Linearly bridge gaps <=30 minutes only; retain observed minutes, bridged minutes, all unbridged gaps and coverage indicators. The primary burden is trapezoidal area below 65 in mmHg-min. Secondary exposure summaries are observed minutes below 65, longest low-MAP run, low-episode count, observed-coverage fraction, recovery slope and minimum observed MAP. Early MAP level, slope, persistence and recovery are included so burden is not a disguised nadir comparison. The first-low time and timing category always use raw observations and are unchanged by interpolation.

Follow-up starts strictly after minute 360 and ends at `min(2880, unitdischargeoffset)`. A post-landmark creatinine is eligible only when `labresultoffset>360`, `labresultoffset<=end_i`, and it occurs before unit exit. A result exactly at 360 belongs to the baseline window. Unit exits at or before 360 are pre-landmark exclusions, not post-landmark competing outcomes. Terminal outcomes in 30-minute intervals are: first observed qualifying creatinine rise; unit death before that rise, identified by `unitdischargestatus="Expired"` or `unitdischargelocation="Death"`; alive discharge/transfer before that rise; or no terminal event by 2,880 minutes while still within the unit boundary. A qualifying test without a rise is not terminal.

The process decomposition is mandatory and is not called mediation:

- first qualifying post-landmark creatinine-test opportunity, regardless of rise;
- recorded rise conditional on a qualifying test opportunity;
- death CIF and alive-exit CIF before a rise/test;
- observed-rise CIF as the primary endpoint.

Each interval uses only prior information: test history, all-numeric-lab activity, time since prior qualifying creatinine and any lab, MAP state, observation density and exit boundary. No future result may enter a predictor.

## Recorded timing exposure

Define the first documented target-pressor record as the smallest `infusionDrug.infusionoffset` in [0,360] whose case-insensitive `drugname` matches the frozen map for norepinephrine, noradrenaline, vasopressin, phenylephrine, dopamine, epinephrine or adrenaline. Categories are mutually exclusive:

1. pre-low/concurrent: record <= first raw periodic low-MAP time;
2. prompt-after-low: record strictly after low MAP and <=30 minutes later;
3. delayed-after-low: >30 minutes after low MAP and <=360 minutes;
4. no documented target-pressor by 360.

A same-minute MAP and infusion record is pre-low/concurrent. Report agent-family indicators and distinct families. Do not infer intensity from `drugrate`, `infusionrate`, `drugamount`, `volumeoffluid` or `patientweight`; they are audit fields only, with a positive-rate-record sensitivity explicitly labelled as such.

## New measurement-robustness variables

All density cutpoints and any channel-noise parameters are learned in grouped development data only and frozen before held-out evaluation.

For each stay, record for the primary periodic channel:

- number of valid `systemicmean` observations in [0,360];
- observed fraction of the 360-minute window;
- median and 90th-percentile gap, longest gap and number of gaps >30 minutes;
- number of observations below 65 and the fraction of valid observations below 65;
- whether the first low MAP is supported by at least two observations within 30 minutes;
- the burden computed with no interpolation and with the parent’s <=30-minute interpolation.

Define low/medium/high observation-density strata using development tertiles of the pair (valid observation count, observed fraction), with a deterministic tie rule and a minimum support gate. The density variables are adjustment/stratification variables, not substitutes for the exposure. The primary D must be reported separately in every supported stratum, with no post hoc merging.

Construct the independent non-invasive channel from `vitalAperiodic.noninvasivemean`, using the same valid range, duplicate-time median, 0–360 window and <=30-minute interpolation rules, but without pooling rows with `vitalPeriodic`. In the channel-replicated analysis, re-identify the first low MAP, burden and timing category from non-invasive MAP and require at least two valid non-invasive observations and a pre-low creatinine baseline. Report channel-specific cohort flow and the overlap with the primary cohort. A non-invasive analysis that lacks the predeclared support is not silently replaced by pooled data; it is reported as unevaluable.

The paired-channel alternative uses periodic and non-invasive observations within +/-5 minutes as paired measurements. It fits, separately by hospital when supported and otherwise with a development-fitted channel effect, a robust local-level state-space model over [0,360]: latent MAP follows a piecewise-random-walk process, each channel has an estimated observation error and channel-specific offset, and missing measurements contribute no likelihood term. A robust Student-t observation model is allowed only as a fixed, predeclared option selected before held-out evaluation. Estimate latent low-MAP area, latent minimum, recovery and low-episode descriptors, then use only these pre-landmark summaries in the same post-landmark process models. Do not call the latent state true physiology. The latent branch is gated by paired support (at least 1,000 paired observations and at least 10 supported hospitals), positive-definite covariance, effective sample size, posterior predictive checks and held-out calibration. If the gate fails, the paired-channel branch is inconclusive and the periodic/non-invasive analyses remain the evidence.

## Estimand and transport

For timing category c and hospital h, let G_h,c be the standardized 48-hour observed-rise CIF difference between the development P75 and P25 of the primary periodic burden, standardizing the same distribution of pre-low creatinine, minimum MAP, trajectory, coverage, density stratum, static covariates and admission-risk descriptors. The primary timing-context estimand is:

`D_h = G_h,prompt-after-low - G_h,delayed-after-low/no-record`.

Delayed and no-record are reported separately and jointly only with adequate overlap. The primary population estimand is the hospital-level-standardized development average of D_h. The new robustness estimands are D_density,q in each supported observation-density stratum, D_noninvasive from the channel-replicated cohort, and D_latent from the gated state-space branch. The central measurement claim is agreement of sign and clinically material magnitude, not numerical identity.

Hospital splits are whole-hospital held-out, with all stays from a hospital in one split. The held-out patient-standardized contrast uses development-fitted coefficients, density cutpoints, maps, channel parameters, regularization and thresholds. Held-out hospitals are never refit, tuned or recalibrated. Report patient-standardized and equal-hospital held-out D, hospital-specific D_h, between-hospital variance and a prediction interval for a new hospital. At least five supported held-out hospitals and at least 70% sign consistency among supported hospitals are required for a transport claim. Support requires >=50 landmark stays per hospital, both compared timing categories, overlap of burden distributions and adequate events/tests.

Transport is supportive only when:

- held-out primary periodic D has the development sign, absolute point estimate >=2 percentage points and a 95% hospital-cluster bootstrap interval excluding zero;
- equal-hospital held-out D agrees in sign with a 95% hospital-cluster interval excluding zero;
- the supported-hospital sign criterion is met;
- density-stratum estimates do not show a reproducible reversal, and the non-invasive D agrees in sign with the primary D when evaluable;
- calibration intercept/slope and Brier score are within fixed tolerances; AUROC/AUPRC are secondary.

Pooled/equal-hospital disagreement, supported-channel disagreement, or adequate primary transport with inadequate channel support is inconclusive for the measurement-robust claim.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternative

The transparent matched baseline uses the identical ledger and nested cause-specific pooled-logistic models for observed rise, death and alive exit, plus the first-test process:

- B0: burden and timing category only;
- B1: add minimum MAP and fixed burden-shape summaries;
- B2: add early MAP level, slope, persistence, recovery and density/coverage descriptors;
- B3: add baseline creatinine, demographics and admission-source/unit descriptors;
- B4: add development-frozen Apache admission-risk descriptors;
- B5: add prior-history test opportunity, all-lab activity and explicit exit-boundary terms;
- B6: add the prespecified burden-by-timing interaction and hospital random intercepts/slopes where supported.

Use regularization fixed in grouped development folds, splines only at prespecified knots, and no future lab information. Report standardized CIFs, interaction contrasts, calibration, condition numbers and convergence. The scientific comparison is D and its robustness, not a small discrimination change.

The substantive alternative is the paired-channel local-level state-space plus piecewise-exponential multi-state model described above. It represents the ordered pre-landmark MAP history and separate post-landmark transitions to first test, recorded rise given test, death and alive exit, with hospital effects where estimable. The baseline collapses measurements into recorded burden and marginal interval hazards; it loses the distinction between sparse sampling, channel disagreement, latent trajectory and recovery ordering. The alternative can reveal whether the timing interaction is carried by a measurement-robust low-MAP trajectory or by channel-specific recording artifacts while retaining the same outcome, landmark, censoring, hospital split and process decomposition. It does not identify causal treatment effects or latent biological injury.

A generic neural sequence model is deferred. Revisit it only if both transparent branches pass all support checks but leave reproducible shape-specific residual miscalibration across held-out hospitals; any revisit must preserve the same estimands, density/channel audits, calibration and uncertainty. A causal pressor study is deferred because infusionDrug lacks validated administration semantics, universal dose units, indication, intent and complete capture, and treatment is confounded by evolving illness. A clinical AKI study requires adjudicated KDIGO, urine output and renal reserve not available here.

## Falsification and interpretation

Support for the primary directional claim requires the B6 interaction and the ordered alternative to agree in direction, development and held-out primary D to meet the 2-point/interval rule, equal-hospital transport to agree, and the effect not to reverse across supported density strata after test-opportunity and competing-exit modeling. Support for the measurement-robust extension additionally requires an evaluable non-invasive analysis with concordant direction and magnitude within a prespecified 2-point tolerance, or a gated latent model with concordant direction and calibrated held-out behavior. Channel disagreement is an adverse result for robustness even if the primary periodic result is positive.

Adverse results include a null/reversed interaction, amplification rather than attenuation, density-gradient reversal, non-invasive or latent disagreement, failure of held-out transport, or disappearance after test/exit representation. These imply that the recorded signal is not robust in the tested boundary; they do not prove pressor harm, equivalence, or absence of biological kidney injury.

Inconclusive results include fewer than 10 held-out hospitals or five supported held-out hospitals, sparse timing/burden/event support, no overlap, near-universal or absent pressor documentation, inadequate non-invasive support, fewer than 1,000 paired observations or 10 latent-model hospitals, nonconvergence, boundary random effects, severe collinearity, invalid units/labels/disposition, extreme weights, failed posterior predictive checks, or disagreement among pooled and equal-hospital estimates. Inconclusive is a scientific result about data support, not a positive finding.

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes and headers, exact joins, filters, offsets, maps, deduplication, split integrity, density strata, paired matching, state-space diagnostics, model fits, standardization, bootstrap intervals, calibration, transport and sensitivity reproduction. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for actual pressor administration/start/dose/indication/intent, infusion completeness, intended MAP target, pre-ICU renal reserve, urine output, KDIGO AKI, assay validity and whether an observed rise reflects true kidney injury. No result from this retrospective design establishes that prompt pressor use helps or harms, that hypotension causes AKI, or that clinicians should change treatment.
