# Early invasive-MAP burden versus nadir for subsequent creatinine-defined AKI in eICU

## Status and scientific deliverable

This is a fully specified observational experiment, not an executed result. The solver must newly construct the cohort and 5-minute exposure sequences, estimate the prespecified burden-versus-nadir association, fit and validate both the transparent and temporal models on hospital-held-out data, quantify uncertainty and heterogeneity, execute the artifact/severity/treatment checks, and emit the tables/figures listed below. Completion means those computed outputs exist and every reported conclusion is traceable to them; confirmation of the hypothesis is not required.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is that depth-duration summaries of ICU hypotension are associated with AKI in selected septic or shock cohorts [K1, K2]. A trial of higher versus lower MAP targets in septic shock did not improve overall mortality and increased atrial fibrillation, while a renal-replacement subgroup signal occurred among patients with chronic hypertension [K3]. The unresolved claim tested here is narrower: **among adult eICU stays without creatinine-defined AKI by hour 24 and with adequate first-day invasive MAP and later creatinine observation, greater first-24-hour MAP-deficit burden predicts new creatinine-defined AKI during hours 24–72 beyond MAP nadir, baseline severity, recorded vasopressor exposure, monitoring density, and timing pattern, and this increment transports to hospitals excluded from fitting.**

This is clinically important because nadirs are artifact-prone and discard persistence. If burden adds stable information, monitoring and risk-stratification systems should summarize exposure history rather than alert on the single lowest value. It would not establish that raising MAP, shortening hypotension, or targeting 65 mmHg prevents AKI.

## Scientific opening, advance, and rival explanations

Maheshwari et al. associated time-weighted and cumulative MAP deficits with AKI in 8,782 septic adults across 110 hospitals, but exposure extended until outcome/discharge/seven days and the inspected work did not test incremental value over nadir [K1]. Panwar et al. prospectively associated relative mean-perfusion-pressure deficit with kidney outcomes in 302 vasopressor-treated patients, suggesting baseline pressure and venous pressure may matter; eICU lacks reliable pre-illness MAP [K2]. These observations motivate, but do not prove, the duration claim. The proposed advance is temporal separation at a 24-hour landmark, an explicit within-question comparison against nadir, whole-hospital transport evaluation, and direct stress tests of monitoring and treatment artifacts in a broad multicenter ICU population.

Leading explanation: sustained pressure deficit represents a renal hypoperfusion dose missed by an isolated nadir. At the same nadir and measured baseline risk, patients with larger area below MAP 65 should have greater subsequent AKI risk; genuinely persistent/late exposure may retain signal after removal of singleton lows.

Strong rivals and differing predictions:

1. **Severity or evolving AKI:** shock severity causes both prolonged hypotension and delayed creatinine rise. The burden coefficient should attenuate materially after admission APACHE physiology, baseline creatinine, diagnosis, ventilation, lactate (where present), and early creatinine trajectory; events concentrated immediately after hour 24 weaken temporal interpretation. Adjustment cannot remove unmeasured severity.
2. **Vasopressor/treatment process:** pressor records mark severe shock, alter MAP, and may have independent effects. A burden signal confined to pressor-treated patients or lost after recorded pressor timing/intensity adjustment is prognostic but not evidence for direct pressure injury. Because eICU infusion rows do not reliably encode stop times or universally standardized doses, residual treatment confounding remains.
3. **Measurement density and artifact:** sicker patients receive arterial lines and denser recording; isolated transducer artifacts create extreme nadirs. A true persistence signal should survive coverage/gap adjustment, removal of singleton excursions, stricter physiologic/run rules, and restriction to near-complete monitoring. Failure under these checks favors measurement artifact.
4. **Timing/shape:** equal total burden may differ if fragmented, concentrated late, or contemporaneous with pressors. If only late burden predicts, latent injury/severity or creatinine timing is more plausible than a simple cumulative dose. If ordering adds reproducible held-out information at fixed burden and nadir, the scalar metric is incomplete.
5. **Individual pressure requirement:** chronic hypertension and venous congestion may make absolute MAP 65 an imperfect exposure [K2, K3]. Past-history hypertension and MAP-minus-CVP sensitivity can bound this issue, but pre-illness pressure is unavailable; no personalized threshold can be claimed.
6. **Outcome observation:** post-landmark creatinine testing, survival, and ICU retention are informative. Results apply to patients with an observed 24–72-hour creatinine. Inverse-observation weighting is a sensitivity, not a cure for unmeasured clinician intent or post-discharge AKI.

The smallest decisive check is the prespecified adjusted, hospital-held-out burden contrast after fixing nadir. Continue toward prospective validation if positive and stable; revise toward timing/phenotype-specific work if the scalar burden fails but the temporal pattern transports; abandon burden-as-added-marker if a clinically consequential increment is excluded; defer if observation/coverage leaves uncertainty too wide.

## Population and temporal boundaries

1. Adult (`age >=18`; map `'> 89'` to 90 only for modeling) first eligible ICU stay per `uniquepid`, ordered by `unitvisitnumber` then `patientunitstayid`. This prevents patient leakage.
2. ICU admission is minute 0. Require `unitdischargeoffset >=1440` and alive/in ICU at the hour-24 landmark.
3. Require an admission creatinine (C0): the value closest to minute 0 within [-360,+360], preferring pre-zero on ties, with 0.1–20 mg/dL. Duplicate same-minute values are median-combined.
4. Require at least one creatinine during (360,1440] to screen early AKI and at least one during (1440,4320] for outcome ascertainment.
5. Exclude chronic dialysis/ESKD indicated by `apacheApsVar.dialysis`, pre-landmark dialysis entries, or frozen diagnosis/history rules. Exclude any creatinine by minute 1440 that is >=0.3 mg/dL above C0 or >=1.5×C0. This creates an AKI-free landmark by the available operational definition, not proof of normal pre-illness renal function.
6. Primary MAP coverage: within [0,1440), retain `systemicmean` 20–160 mmHg, median duplicate timestamps, require >=230 of 288 expected five-minute bins (>=80%) and no gap >60 minutes. Preprocessing is frozen before outcomes are inspected.

Primary outcome: new creatinine-defined AKI in (1440,4320]. Apply the >=0.3 mg/dL criterion only to a rise occurring within 48 hours of C0 (therefore through minute 2880 for the chosen admission baseline); apply >=1.5×C0 through minute 4320. Report stage 2/3 threshold sensitivity (>=2×/3× C0; creatinine >=4 mg/dL with >=0.3 rise) and 24–48 versus 48–72 timing. This is an operational KDIGO-creatinine phenotype; eICU cannot enforce the full seven-day KDIGO baseline window, and clinical adjudication is required for chronic baseline, dialysis indications, and alternative causes.

## Exposure construction and covariates

Map valid measurements to exact five-minute bins, median-combine collisions, and leave missing bins missing. Primary MAP deficit burden is
`AUC65 = sum(max(65 - MAP_t, 0) * 5/60)` in mmHg-hours across observed bins; also report monitored-time-normalized deficit `AUC65 / observed_hours`, percent observed time below 65, longest consecutive run below 65, number of episodes, and nadir. The prespecified inferential exposure is `log1p(AUC65)` modeled with a restricted cubic spline; the reported standardized contrast is the eligible-cohort 75th versus 25th percentile at fixed covariates and nadir. Nadir is modeled flexibly, not dichotomized.

Baseline covariates available by hour 6: age, sex, ethnicity, C0, admission diagnosis/unittype/source, hospital characteristics, admission MAP/heart rate, ventilation, hypertension/CKD/diabetes history, and first-six-hour lactate and creatinine slope. Missingness indicators plus fold-specific median/mode imputation are fit only in training hospitals. APACHE score/APS or component fallback is included in the rival-adjusted model; because APACHE may summarize the first ICU day and overlap the exposure window, label it a severity control rather than a pre-exposure confounder and report a model excluding APACHE alongside the primary adjusted model.

Prespecified probes add first-day pressor indicators/timing and parsable norepinephrine-equivalent exposure, first-day lactate trajectory, fluid balance and measurement-density features. These are treatment/severity-process adjustments that change interpretation and may block mediation; compare estimates rather than calling attenuation mechanistic proof. Freeze exact pressor regex and unit conversion from observed `drugname`/unit combinations, report unconvertible rows, and never treat absent infusion rows as certain nonuse.

## Transparent baseline and primary analysis

The same hospital folds and outcomes are used throughout.

- **B0 (nadir baseline):** penalized logistic regression with baseline covariates, hospital descriptors, and spline of first-day MAP nadir.
- **B1 (prespecified burden model):** B0 plus spline of `log1p(AUC65)`. This nested contrast directly tests whether accumulated exposure contributes beyond nadir.
- **B2 (primary rival-adjusted model):** B1 plus first-day MAP observation count, observed fraction, maximum gap, number of same-minute collisions, recorded pressor class/timing and parsable norepinephrine-equivalent exposure, APACHE severity, and first-day lactate/creatinine trajectories. The headline burden contrast and conclusion come from B2. Because these variables overlap exposure and may be treatment mediators or consequences of severity, report B1 beside B2; attenuation separates a robust prognostic marker from one explained by recorded care/severity but cannot identify mechanism.
- Fit preprocessing and ridge penalty within development hospitals. For inference, obtain the standardized 75th-vs-25th percentile risk difference and odds ratio from B1 with a hospital-cluster bootstrap (at least 1,000 resamples) and report spline plots and effective support. A secondary mixed-effects model estimates hospital random intercept and burden-slope heterogeneity; it is not used to leak test-hospital outcomes into prediction.

Whole hospitals—not rows—are assigned once using a fixed hash seed and greedy balancing of eligible counts/events into five outer folds. No `uniquepid` crosses folds. Each fold is held out in turn; inner hospital-grouped cross-validation selects penalties. Pool out-of-fold predictions. Primary transport metrics are log loss and Brier score (95% hospital-cluster bootstrap CI); report calibration intercept/slope, calibration plots, AUROC, AUPRC, and decision curves only as secondary descriptive metrics. Compare B2–B0 paired out-of-fold log loss and Brier score; B1–B0 is the minimally adjusted decomposition. Report fold effects rather than only an average; report individual hospital effects only for hospitals with >=50 eligible stays and >=10 events, retaining smaller hospitals in their outer fold.

## Scientifically substantive temporal alternative

**T1: masked temporal convolutional network (TCN).** Input is the 288-bin first-day sequence with channels: clipped standardized MAP, deficit below 65, observation mask, time since last observation, heart rate, and recorded pressor state/intensity where reconstructable, plus the same static baseline covariates as B1. Two to four dilated 1-D convolution blocks (kernel 3–5, <=128 channels, dropout), global masked pooling, and a logistic head are fit to 24–72-hour AKI by weighted binary cross-entropy; tune only depth/width/dropout/learning rate in inner hospital folds and use early stopping on log loss. Calibration is fit in validation hospitals. Use 5 seeds and report between-seed variability.

T1 can retain timing, contiguity, recovery, fragmentation, interaction with pressor support, and missingness patterns that B1 collapses. Its scientific comparison is not “can a neural net gain a tiny AUC,” but whether temporal organization contains transportable kidney-risk information beyond the same total burden and nadir. Evaluate T1 versus B2 on paired out-of-fold log loss/Brier/calibration, giving T1 the same static rival-adjustment inputs. Perform locked perturbations in held-out hospitals: (a) permute episode order within a stay while preserving its multiset of MAP bins, AUC65 and nadir; (b) merge hypotensive bins into one contiguous episode while preserving depth histogram; and (c) move the same burden from early to late day when valid coverage permits. Report prediction changes with cluster CIs. These are model probes, not patient counterfactual causal effects.

Select T1 for scientific interpretation only if it improves held-out log loss over B2 with a 95% CI excluding no improvement, remains calibrated, and perturbation effects are consistent across at least four of five outer folds and seeds. Otherwise B2 remains primary and T1 is reported as a negative comparison. A shape-constrained distributed-lag GAM using six four-hour bins is the retained interpretable fallback if TCN training is unstable; it would be revisited if T1 is unstable or clearly overfits.

## Artifact, timing, treatment, and selection checks

1. Recompute after deleting isolated low bins whose immediate valid neighbors are both >=65; then require two consecutive low bins; then apply stricter 30–140 mmHg limits.
2. Repeat at >=95% coverage/no gap >20 minutes and stratify by coverage quartile, bin count, maximum gap, and hospital.
3. Recompute AUC by trapezoidal interpolation only across gaps <=10 minutes; compare with observed-bin sum. Analyze `vitalAperiodic.noninvasivemean` as a separate intermittent modality check, never as equivalent continuous exposure.
4. Replace 65 with prespecified 55, 60, and 70 mmHg; these are robustness analyses, not threshold discovery. Report all.
5. Decompose burden into 0–6, 6–12, and 12–24 hours; shift landmark/outcome to 12–60 and 36–84 hours where support permits. Persistent association confined to late exposure is flagged as timing-sensitive.
6. Add pressor process and contemporaneous lactate/creatinine trajectory; stratify by recorded pressor use. Report treatment-record completeness and unconvertible rates.
7. Where CVP coverage is adequate, repeat with mean perfusion pressure `systemicmean-cvp`; stratify by recorded hypertension. Absence of pre-illness MAP prevents a direct relative-deficit analysis [K2].
8. Model probability of having post-landmark creatinine among all landmark survivors with adequate MAP, using baseline variables, early lab frequency and hospital; use stabilized inverse-observation weights with truncation at 1st/99th percentiles. Compare weighted estimates and describe nonobserved/dead/discharged groups. No imputed AKI label is allowed.
9. Negative-control measurement check: test whether first-day MAP burden predicts a clearly pre-exposure characteristic (sex) after age/hospital; a signal indicates hospital/selection structure, not biological causality. This does not validate absence of confounding.

## Falsification and interpretation

Before inspecting outcomes, define a clinically consequential positive increment as an adjusted absolute AKI-risk increase of at least 1 percentage point for the 75th-versus-25th percentile AUC65 contrast at fixed nadir and covariates. This is a decision margin for this study, not a treatment effect.

- **Supportive:** B2’s standardized burden risk difference has a lower 95% cluster-bootstrap confidence bound of at least +1 percentage point; B2 improves hospital-held-out log loss over B0 with its paired 95% CI excluding 0 in the favorable direction; the effect direction holds in at least four of five folds and survives singleton-low and >=95%-coverage checks. This supports transportable incremental prognostic information, not causality or a therapeutic target.
- **Adverse/falsifying for clinically consequential added value:** the upper 95% CI for the B2 adjusted risk difference is <+1 percentage point and B2 does not improve held-out log loss; or the association reverses. Conclude that first-day AUC65 has no demonstrated clinically consequential increment beyond nadir in this selected population. A temporal T1 signal could motivate a revised shape/timing hypothesis, not rescue the scalar claim.
- **Artifact-adverse:** the primary signal disappears or reverses under singleton-low/coverage checks, is driven by one fold/hospital, or is confined to unparseable treatment/measurement strata. Conclude the result is not robust and favors measurement/selection explanations.
- **Inconclusive:** CIs include both 0 and +1 percentage point, fewer than four outer folds have adequate events, model calibration fails, observation weights are unstable, or checks materially disagree. Do not call an imprecise null refutation; report the exact limitation and seek external or prospectively standardized data.

Multiplicity: the B2 burden contrast and B2-vs-B0 log-loss difference are co-primary and require both for the supportive label. All thresholds, subgroups, T1 perturbations and interactions are explicitly secondary; report estimates/CIs and control false-discovery rate within each family without redefining the primary claim.

## Compute and feasibility

A bounded 8-CPU/64-GiB diagnostic completed in 91 seconds. Under an earlier approximate cohort phenotype it found 33,068 adult first stays with any valid first-day invasive MAP, 14,483 with >=230 bins/no gap >60 minutes, and 8,786 provisionally eligible stays with 1,413 provisional outcome events across 165 hospitals. Median invasive-MAP nadir was 56 mmHg and median approximate AUC65 was 5.58 mmHg-hours among adequately monitored stays. These are support counts, not study results: the probe selected the first stay before full eligibility, did not require minute-1440 survival exactly, and applied the 0.3 mg/dL creatinine rise through hour 72 rather than its correct 48-hour window. The solver must recompute the frozen cohort/outcome; no association was estimated. The probe also verified 1,277,760 exact `creatinine` rows across 190,455 stays, heterogeneous pressor names/units, source headers, and pandas 2.2.3, NumPy 1.26.4, DuckDB 1.5.5, PyArrow 25.0.1, scikit-learn 1.6.1 and statsmodels 0.15.0. The full input sizes include 1.78 GB `vitalPeriodic` and 517 MB `lab`.

Planned solver envelope: preprocessing and B0/B1 on 8–16 CPUs, 64–128 GiB RAM, approximately 1–3 hours; 1 A100 GPU for T1, approximately 1–3 hours across folds/seeds, with CPU fallback likely 4–8 hours; bootstrap/aggregation another 1–3 hours. Total remains within the configured planning envelope (16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB, up to 8 GPUs, 28,800 seconds). These are unverified estimates, not measured full-run timings. One GPU is scientifically adequate; additional GPUs are unnecessary unless fold-level parallelism is chosen.

## Alternatives not selected and revisit criteria

- Relative MAP/MPP deficit was not primary because pre-illness MAP is unavailable and CVP is incomplete; revisit with linked outpatient BP or standardized baseline pressure [K2].
- A causal marginal structural model or target-trial emulation was deferred because treatment assignment, fluid/pressor indications, and infusion intervals are not sufficiently reliable. Revisit with audited medication administration and time-varying clinical intent.
- Raw-waveform morphology was excluded because eICU provides five-minute summaries, not waveforms.
- Urine-output KDIGO was excluded because reliable hourly body-weight-normalized urine output and censoring cannot be guaranteed; revisit after clinical phenotype validation.
- T1 was chosen over a larger transformer because 288 time steps and a small number of physiologic channels do not justify excess capacity. Revisit only if TCN underfits while event support and calibration are adequate.
