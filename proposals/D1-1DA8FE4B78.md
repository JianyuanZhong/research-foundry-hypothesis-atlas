# Discharge-boundary availability versus faithful interval concordance in incidental pulmonary-nodule handoff

## Decision question and substantive advance

When an incidental pulmonary-nodule CT recommendation is already observable by the time of hospital discharge, is the larger directly observable transition failure that the canonical discharge artifact was not yet stored at discharge, or that an artifact stored by discharge failed to carry a faithful, interval-specific plan? This distinction determines whether a prospective intervention should first target finalization/availability (including a true departure-time visibility check) or structured content reconciliation.

The prior consolidated design established the clinically meaningful process question and an observation-aware same-system surveillance appendix. The latest repair correctly moved the primary boundary to actual `dischtime` and separated delayed artifacts from content failure. This child makes the hierarchy of estimands explicit so that neither strict discharge availability nor content fidelity is estimated in a post-index-selected denominator:

1. **Primary all-opportunity decomposition:** every first eligible, adjudicated recommendation opportunity with a resolvable pre-discharge report state is retained, and mutually exclusive states at the actual discharge boundary are assigned over that same denominator. `A` (artifact not observed stored by discharge) and `C` (content failure among artifacts observed stored by discharge) are not compared as two selected cohorts.
2. **Conditional observable-content estimand:** among the subset whose sole canonical artifact is observed stored by discharge, estimate the four-way content distribution and faithful fraction. This is a descriptive conditional estimand about the text that is observable at the boundary; it is not the primary mechanism comparison and is never used to infer the size of the availability gap.
3. **Post-discharge transition description:** delayed artifacts are followed to +24 hours and +7 days to distinguish a strict boundary state from later text content, without retroactively changing the primary state.
4. **Downstream observation-aware appendix:** same-system CT is a joint observable state, not adherence, and no restriction to patients with a later MIMIC trace is used for the primary surveillance contrast.

This is a substantive advance over the earlier +24-hour-gated fidelity target: it preserves opportunities whose discharge artifact is delayed, distinguishes “not observed stored by discharge” from missing/unclassifiable data, and reports faithful content only where a text artifact was actually observable. It also prevents interval mismatch, reciprocal addendum ambiguity, stale-summary chronology, and post-index trace conditioning from being converted into clinical failure claims.

## Evidence boundary and falsifiable hypotheses

The strongest available evidence supports a measurable communication/documentation problem, not causal benefit from discharge wording. Kwan et al. reported absent interval imaging in 35/65 patients with explicit follow-up suggestions and an association between discharge-summary mention and timely imaging (J Hosp Med 2019; PMID 30794133; PMCID PMC6625441). A 2023 tracking-program report observed 34/67 follow-up scans after implementation versus 16/52 before implementation (PMCID PMC10287591), while a 2025 systematic review found promising but heterogeneous tracking interventions and serious or critical risk of bias in most nonrandomized studies (PMID 40081655; PMCID PMC12264345). These observations motivate a rigorous process experiment; they do not establish that recommendations were appropriate, that stored text was communicated, that outside imaging did not occur, or that either workflow intervention improves patient benefit.

The frozen parent audits are feasibility evidence only. The lexical audit scanned all 2,321,355 radiology rows and found 1,194 first-subject lexical candidates; proxy counts were not clinical labels. A complete discharge timestamp audit found 331,794 `DS` rows: 86,609 stored by discharge, 147,800 stored after discharge through 24 hours, 76,977 from 24 hours through 7 days, 20,329 after 7 days, and 17 with missing `storetime`; every classifiable delayed row had `charttime <= dischtime`. This supports a possible signing/export or timestamp artifact, but `charttime` is not visibility, signature, transmission, or physical departure. The parent chain audit found 25,735 reciprocal edges, 25,600 fully resolved chronological same-admission `RR -> AR` pairs, and 135 edges involving absent nodes. The complete discharge-detail audit found only masked `author` rows and no section structure.

### Primary hypothesis

Among adults with a first, adjudicated, incidental pulmonary-nodule opportunity whose recommendation-bearing report state is resolvable and available by actual discharge, the strict discharge-boundary artifact-availability proportion exceeds the strict discharge-boundary content-failure proportion by at least 0.05:

`Delta = p_A - p_C > 0.05`,

where both proportions use the same all-opportunity denominator `N`. The 0.05 margin is an operational intervention-prioritization margin, not a clinical quality threshold; report sensitivity at 0 and 0.10. The availability intervention is nominated first only if the simultaneous two-sided 95% lower limit for `Delta` exceeds +0.05 and all annotation, precision, and unresolved-state gates pass. The content intervention is nominated first only if the simultaneous upper limit is below -0.05. Otherwise the finding is inconclusive for single-mechanism prioritization and supports a bundled or 2-by-2 factorial prospective test, or additional timestamp/mechanism data.

The hypothesis is falsified for the availability-first decision when the lower limit does not exceed +0.05, including when partial-identification bounds over unresolved data cross the margin. A statistically large `p_A` does not establish a workflow availability failure without prospective validation of visibility/finalization at departure.

### Secondary conditional content question

Among `N_obs`, the subset of `N` with a sole canonical `DS` artifact having known `storetime <= dischtime`, what proportions are faithful, omission, incomplete/discordant, or explicit alternate resolution? This is not a new denominator for comparing `A` to `C`; it answers the narrower question “when an artifact is observed at discharge, what does its text contain?” Report `N_obs/N` first and the conditional multinomial distribution second. A report stored after the artifact is not excluded from `N_obs`; it is adjudicated as a chronology subtype, because excluding it would condition on relative note timing and could conceal stale-artifact failure. The recommendation still must be available by discharge for eligibility.

## Population, denominator, and temporal construction

Scan all 2,321,355 radiology rows; no row sampling is permitted. Automated lexical retrieval is high-sensitivity candidate screening only. Two radiology-trained reviewers independently adjudicate every rule-screened candidate while blinded to discharge text and later outcomes.

Include adults age >=18 with a base CT whose `(subject_id, hadm_id)` matches an admission and whose base `charttime` satisfies `admittime - 12 hours <= charttime <= dischtime`; CT coverage must be chest CT, CTPA/CTA, or abdominal CT including lung bases, using all detail fields plus text review for ambiguity; the report must describe an incidental pulmonary/lung nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity and an affirmed patient-specific unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months, first present in a resolvable report state with `storetime <= dischtime`.

Exclude scheduled lung-cancer screening, an index examination explicitly for nodule surveillance, lung-cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, an actively managed primary pulmonary malignancy, nonpulmonary nodules, conditional/optional recommendations dependent on unavailable risk, explicit no-follow-up recommendations, hospice/comfort-only transitions, in-hospital death, and a pre-discharge CT explicitly satisfying or superseding the recommendation. Do not require “new” nodule status, Fleischner eligibility, smoking pack-years, or guideline concordance; those are incompletely represented. Abstract size, type, multiplicity, stability/newness, and prior same-system imaging only when stated.

Select the first eligible admission per subject by base `charttime`, then base `storetime`, then `note_id`, without using discharge state or future outcomes. Repeat the entire eligibility cascade descriptively with a zero-hour admission boundary and with the available `edregtime/edouttime` window. Repeat key counts among subjects with at least two prior years of same-system radiology trace; this is a sensitivity, not an eligibility requirement.

Define `N` before reading or using the discharge content outcome as the set of first eligible opportunities with a resolvable report/addendum state and recommendation available by `dischtime`. Do not require a DS row, known DS `storetime`, artifact storage by discharge, or recommendation-before-DS ordering for membership in `N`; those are downstream discharge-boundary states. This is the essential protection against post-index conditioning. A missing admission link or unresolved chain remains outside `N` and is reported in the unconditional screening cascade with eligibility bounds, not recoded into a failure.

## Strict discharge state decomposition

For each `i in N`, determine the sole DS row and its `storetime` state at `dischtime`. Assign exactly one of the following mutually exclusive states:

- **`A` observed artifact-availability gap:** the sole DS row exists and has known `storetime > dischtime`. This means only that storage was not observed by the strict boundary. It is not proof that the artifact was invisible, unsigned, unavailable in the UI, not transmitted, or absent at physical departure.
- **`F` faithful observable artifact:** the sole DS row has known `storetime <= dischtime`, and blinded transition adjudication finds an active patient-specific pulmonary-nodule mention, chest/thoracic CT action, and the same exact 3/6/12-month interval or an unambiguously equivalent calendar due date.
- **`C_O` omission content gap:** DS stored by discharge, but no active patient-specific actionable nodule follow-up content is present.
- **`C_I` incomplete/discordant content gap:** DS stored by discharge, but it contains a nodule without action, CT without usable timing, or a different action/interval without explicit alternate resolution. A mismatched interval is not faithful merely because it is approximately close; calendar interval concordance is exact. A clinically documented alternate plan is separated into `R`, not forced into `C_I`.
- **`R` explicit alternate resolution:** DS stored by discharge and documents refusal/preference, goals-of-care choice, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, an already scheduled outside-system follow-up, or a documented clinical decision superseding CT. This records documentation only and is neither success nor failure of faithful propagation.
- **`U_D` unresolved discharge data:** no DS row linked to the admission, missing DS `storetime`, contradictory admission linkage, unreadable text, or another prespecified inability to classify. A missing or unknown artifact is not `A`, and unresolved text is not content failure.

The strict all-opportunity risks are `p_A=sum(A)/N`, `p_C=sum(C_O+C_I)/N`, and `p_F=sum(F)/N`; the primary contrast is paired `Delta=mean(I_A-I_C)`. Report `C_O` and `C_I` separately, as well as their union. The observable-content denominator is `N_obs=sum(A? no: F+C_O+C_I+R)`, restricted to artifacts stored by discharge with known time and classifiable text; its conditional faithful fraction is `F/N_obs`, and its conditional multinomial vector is `(F,C_O,C_I,R)/N_obs`. The same-opportunity all-state vector `(A,F,C_O,C_I,R,U_D)/N` is primary. No ratio of `p_A` to `p_C`, no conditional content risk replacing `p_C`, and no exclusion of stale chronology is allowed.

If the sole DS row was stored before discharge but before the first qualifying report/addendum recommendation, the artifact is still in `N_obs`; reviewers classify its content normally and tag it as `artifact-before-recommendation`. If the artifact predates the recommendation and omits it, this is a prespecified stale-artifact/update subtype of `C_O` or `C_I`, not evidence that a writer ignored information already available at artifact creation. If the recommendation was available by discharge but the artifact was stored after discharge, it remains `A` regardless of whether the later text is faithful, omitted, or discordant.

At +24 hours and +7 days, construct transition matrices from the strict state. A delayed `A` can later become `F`, `C_O`, `C_I`, or `R`; it remains `A` in the primary strict analysis. `U_D` can become classifiable later but is never retroactively reclassified as a strict discharge failure. Post-discharge addenda cannot change eligibility or strict content. Report `charttime-dischtime` and `storetime-dischtime` distributions. The audit finding that delayed DS rows had `charttime <= dischtime` is compatible with signing/export latency but cannot establish user visibility.

## Blinded interval-concordance adjudication

After report-state fields are locked, two transition reviewers independently inspect the sole DS text while blinded to the radiology text, exact recommendation, report/DS chronology, and all later care. They abstract whether the text contains a patient-specific nodule, an action, the interval or due date, responsible service if stated, and a documented alternate resolution. Only after both abstractions are locked does the algorithm match report and discharge fields.

Faithful means active patient-specific pulmonary nodule plus chest/thoracic CT plus the same exact 3/6/12-month interval or an unambiguous equivalent calendar due date. “Follow up with PCP,” generic guideline boilerplate, negated/history/family-history text, or copied report prose without an active patient-specific plan is not faithful. A different interval or modality is incomplete/discordant unless an explicit alternate resolution is documented. Do not silently treat “three to six months,” “in several months,” or a non-calendar day approximation as exact concordance; retain these as interval-uncertain/incomplete unless the adjudication codebook explicitly establishes an unambiguous due date. Reviewers do not assess guideline appropriateness or infer undocumented communication, order placement, scheduling, patient receipt, or outside care.

Report raw agreement, Gwet AC1 with subject-bootstrap 95% intervals, and kappa for eligibility, exact interval, and the four content states. Confirmatory inference requires complete consensus, raw agreement >=0.80, and AC1 >=0.70 for each domain. If a gate fails, revise the codebook and independently re-adjudicate affected records once; persistent failure permits only reviewer-specific bounds. Automated screening must have sensitivity >=0.95 with lower 95% Wilson bound >0.90 against consensus. Automated state labels cannot replace adjudication unless each relevant PPV is >=0.90 with lower bound >0.80.

## Primary analysis and precision gates

Report the unconditional cascade: all lexical candidates, CT metadata-confirmed candidates, adjudication dispositions, `U_R`, `N`, recommendation availability by discharge, one-final-DS linkage and timing states, and the full strict state vector. The strict primary denominator is `N`, not `N_obs`, not the +24-hour artifact-gated subset, and not a later observable-trace subset.

Use 9,999 fixed-seed subject-level nonparametric bootstrap replicates. Because only the first opportunity per subject is retained, subject is the resampling unit. In each replicate recompute `p_A`, `p_C`, `p_F`, the four conditional content proportions in `N_obs`, and `Delta`. Construct a two-sided family-wise 95% max-|t| simultaneous interval for the three primary all-opportunity quantities (`p_A`, `p_C`, `Delta`) using multinomial influence-function standard errors. If a component is zero or studentization fails, use a prespecified non-studentized max-deviation bootstrap and report exact multinomial score intervals as a check. Provide simultaneous intervals for the full strict state vector and exact intervals for the conditional content vector.

A dominance conclusion requires at least 200 `N` opportunities, at least 20 events in each `A` and `C`, no `U_D` greater than 5% of `N`, all annotation/screen gates, and simultaneous `Delta` interval width <=0.10. If `U_D` exceeds 5%, assign unresolved states first entirely to `A` and then entirely to `C` to provide partial-identification bounds; intervention selection is inconclusive unless the same margin conclusion holds throughout. The `N_obs` content fraction is reported even when its event or agreement gates fail, but it cannot rescue an invalid all-opportunity dominance analysis.

Descriptive strata are interval (3/6/12 months), CT coverage, artifact-before/after recommendation chronology, final medical/surgical service, ICU exposure, transition count, and discharge destination. Interaction contrasts are exploratory and do not select the intervention. Repeat the strict state analysis at the zero-hour and ED timing sensitivities. A reversal indicates boundary dependence, not a stable mechanism.

## Observation-aware same-system surveillance appendix

Downstream imaging is not used to choose between availability and content interventions. If retained, define time zero as `max(dischtime, DS.storetime)` for classifiable artifacts and `dischtime` for the strict all-opportunity state, with no event at or before time zero. The due date is index `charttime` plus 3/6/12 calendar months. The primary observed event is the first same-system chest CT/CTA with `charttime` in `[due date - 42 days, due date + 42 days]` and after time zero; report +/-30- and +/-90-day sensitivities. Blinded outcome reviewers classify nodule-focused surveillance from indication/comparison/impression language. Also report any post-time-zero chest CT, and separate CTs occurring only during a later acute admission; acute imaging is not automatically surveillance.

Assign every `N` opportunity a joint observation state with this fixed precedence:

1. MIMIC-observed recommendation-timed CT before recorded death.
2. Recorded death before the window end, with no earlier timed CT and `deathtime` or `dod <= due+42`.
3. No timed CT or recorded death, but a later MIMIC radiology event or admission after `due+42` (continued same-system trace).
4. No subsequent trace/unknown capture.

The primary secondary estimand is the all-`N` risk of state 1, reported by strict discharge state and by `F` versus `C_O` only when cell sizes support descriptive comparison. Never condition the main contrast on continued trace or on `N_obs`, since both can be post-index colliders. Continued trace is itself an outcome. An inverse-probability-of-observation analysis may be a sensitivity under an explicitly unverifiable missing-at-random assumption, never a replacement for the all-eligible estimand. MIMIC absence is not nonadherence, failed follow-up, or proof of absent outside care.

Preserve the complete parent feasibility findings as nonconfirmatory context: among proxy-faithful versus proxy-omitted cases, continued trace was 253/403 (62.8%) versus 99/188 (52.7%); among trace-positive cases timed CT was 44/253 (17.4%) versus 16/99 (16.2%); any chest CT was 77/253 (30.4%) versus 30/99 (30.3%); in all proxy-gated cases timed CT was 49/403 (12.2%) versus 17/188 (9.0%). These proxy results do not establish a downstream effect and reinforce why an observed-only analysis is unsafe.

Falsification checks include a temporal placebo window centered on `index charttime - interval`, generic non-chest radiology in the recommendation window, differential continued-trace rates, and mechanism specificity requiring nodule-focused CT to be at least as directionally supportive as any chest CT. A same-direction placebo or generic-utilization association as large as the surveillance association, an association driven by acute/non-nodule CT, materially different observation states, or failed outcome annotation gates makes the secondary association inconclusive. No causal or patient-benefit claim is permitted.

## Falsification and interpretation matrix

- **Availability-first support:** simultaneous lower bound for `Delta` > +0.05, agreement/precision gates pass, unresolved bounds do not reverse, and either a local timestamp crosswalk confirms the delayed artifact was not visible/final at departure or the result is explicitly labeled a storage-timestamp signal awaiting validation. This supports prioritizing a discharge-time canonical-artifact finalization/availability check, not a claim of failed communication.
- **Availability interpretation falsified:** most strict-delayed artifacts become faithful rapidly and a prospective crosswalk shows they were visible/final/transmitted at departure. The MIMIC signal then supports timestamp/pipeline validation, not an availability workflow intervention.
- **Content-first support:** simultaneous upper bound for `Delta` < -0.05, with omission/incomplete states not predominantly alternate resolutions, all gates pass, and interval-concordance adjudication is reliable. This supports a structured radiology-to-discharge insertion/reconciliation trigger, including stale-artifact update handling.
- **Neither dominates:** interval crosses the margin, width/event/agreement gates fail, or unresolved bounds cross a decision. Test a bundle/factorial workflow or obtain local timing data rather than choosing from point estimates.
- **Adverse process result:** `p_A` and `p_C` are both low and precise, or most nonfaithful available artifacts are explicit alternate resolutions. The discharge artifact is unlikely to be the principal observable bottleneck; active tracking, scheduling, patient communication, or cross-system exchange deserves a new prospective study.
- **Coexisting timing and content vulnerability:** many `A` opportunities later become incomplete/discordant. Preserve strict primary attribution to `A` and do not double-count, while a bundled intervention becomes preferable if no margin dominates.
- **Surveillance support:** only if all-`N` MIMIC-observed timed CT association is positive and precise, negative controls are smaller, observation-state imbalance does not plausibly explain it, and all event/annotation gates pass. This remains a same-system association, not adherence or benefit.
- **Surveillance adverse/inconclusive:** an adequately precise risk difference is nonpositive, only acute CT is associated, a placebo/generic control is comparable, or observation/positivity gates fail. Do not convert the near-null feasibility audit into a positive conclusion.

## Computation, adjudication, and missing-evidence boundary

An automatic verifier can check source hashes and archive members; catalog schemas; joins on `(subject_id, hadm_id)` and `(subject_id, note_id)`; reciprocal graph reconstruction; one-final-DS logic; strict `storetime <= dischtime` states; first-subject selection; no post-index denominator restriction; exact calendar-month arithmetic; mutually exclusive state assignment; conditional versus all-opportunity denominators; transition matrices; bootstrap intervals and margin gates; observation-state precedence; and whether conclusions match computed outputs.

Clinical/radiology experts are required to adjudicate incidental status, CT coverage, unconditionality, exact interval, addendum meaning, faithful text, discordance, and alternate resolution. MIMIC cannot establish guideline appropriateness, morphology sufficient for clinical decision-making, smoking pack-years, what clinicians or patients saw, signature or UI visibility, actual departure, patient discussion or preference beyond documentation, orders/referrals/appointments, outside imaging, adherence, cancer outcome, radiation harm, survival, or patient benefit. A prospective local audit must link `storetime`, signature/finalization, UI visibility, patient release/transmission, and physical departure before calling `A` a workflow availability failure. A randomized, stepped-wedge, or factorial prospective study with outpatient/outside-system capture and clinical adjudication is required for causal or patient-benefit claims.

## What changed from the two assessed parents and remaining uncertainty

This child retains the higher-mu consolidated parent's exact source bindings, reciprocal report/addendum graph, blinded adjudication, strict cutoff, complete audits, observation-aware outcome framing, and negative feasibility findings. It retains the lower-mu repair's all-opportunity denominator, five-way discharge decomposition, partial-identification bounds, and explicit warning that `charttime` cannot prove availability.

The substantive additional repair is an explicit two-level estimand architecture: `N` is frozen before any discharge artifact timing/content or later trace is used; `A` and `C` share `N`; and `F/(F+C_O+C_I+R)` is reported only as a conditional description among observable artifacts, never as the primary content-versus-availability comparison. The child also makes the one-final-summary rule non-negotiable (the sole DS row, not a latest-version substitution), retains recommendation-before-artifact chronology as a stale-artifact subtype rather than an exclusion, requires exact interval concordance rather than approximate timing, and extends the observation-aware no-post-index-conditioning rule to every surveillance contrast.

Uncertainty remains about the semantics of note `storetime`, user visibility, finalization, transmission, departure, undocumented alternate plans, outside care, recommendation appropriateness, and the sparse number of confirmatory eligible opportunities. If adjudication, precision, or timestamp validation fails, the appropriate result is a bounded or inconclusive process finding, not an inferred clinical intervention effect.
