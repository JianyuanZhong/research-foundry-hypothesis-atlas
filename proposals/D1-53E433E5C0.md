# Deterministic unit matching and availability-typed bridge (successor of [prior hypothesis])

## Status, parent, and substantive repair

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`, which is the highest-mu recent MIMIC proposal but was assessed **repairable**. It preserves that candidate's scientific schema and all inherited design choices: the first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject pulmonary-opportunity population; reciprocal pre-departure radiology chronology; one canonical exact discharge-summary (DS) artifact; unit-specific clocks and exhaustive `Y,V,H,Z`; atom `g=(possible-calendar class, terminal curr_service, workflow class)`; all-unit adverse routing with `AUTO > RECON > TIMING > DEFER > NOACTION`; support-atlas construction; stratified positive-probability sampling and hash-rank challenge selection; compatible-set inference with shared reviewer error; the no-rescue rules; the ceilings `A<=900`, `C<=300`, 2,370 worst-case physician hours and 84,000 packet minutes; and the prospective-silent-only conclusion ceiling.

No population, admission denominator, route, atom, chronology, time boundary, sample, allocation, primary inherited estimand, or causal claim is changed. The advance is an executable repair of the bridge layer's remaining ambiguity. The parent says that `D_REPRO` requires H1 and H2 inventories to agree, but does not define a deterministic unit matcher/ordering; it does not fully separate H3 from the reproducibility functional; and its handling of zero-unit `NOACTION` and `DEFER` cases is underspecified. This child freezes those rules before any real label and makes the evidence-availability vector auditable rather than treating unavailable workflow variables as generic unknowns.

The clinical question remains important but deliberately bounded: can a supported discharge-routing signature be shown to be reproducible in stored documentation while also revealing whether its text contains the explicit fields needed to state a next action? This matters because a documentation-consistent retrospective route could still be unsafe to bridge prospectively if its target, action, interval, responsibility, replacement, or conflict resolution is not explicit. MIMIC can test the documentation construct and identify missing evidence; it cannot establish delivery of care, appropriateness, safety, completion, or benefit.

## Evidence-supported claim and untested claim

The strongest claim available from the configured sources is that admission-linked structured fields, pre-discharge radiology reports/details, and a canonical DS text/detail artifact can form a reproducible stored-text packet. The source catalog explicitly describes deidentified subject-specific shifted timestamps with preserved within-subject intervals, and the supplied metadata identifies radiology and discharge text but no event stream proving viewing, signature/finalization, communication, order execution, responsibility acceptance, scheduling, patient preference/capacity, follow-up, or clinical outcome.

The untested hypothesis is:

> For at least some exact inherited atom-route cells, independent route-blinded panels will reproduce a deterministic set of target recommendation units, yet a nonzero and estimable fraction of those units will lack one or more explicit fields required to state a next action without inference; the missing-field pattern will differ from mere route disagreement and will specify what a prospective silent study must capture.

`D_REPRO` is a documentation reproducibility construct. `A_TEXT_COMPLETE` is explicit textual completeness. Neither is clinical actionability, appropriateness, workflow performance, or a latent gold standard. A high documentation-to-text gap is a reason to require prospective reconciliation/data capture, not evidence that a clinician failed or that a patient was harmed.

## Canonical unit identity, matching, and serialization

The following algorithm is protocol-hashed and frozen before challenge selection. It is used identically by H1 and H2 and is implemented by code after labels; reviewers do not see the other panel's inventory.

### 1. Source-span identity

Every extracted unit must point to one or more source spans. A span identifier is a tuple

`S=(artifact_role, subject_id, hadm_id, note_id, field_ordinal_or_0, section_token_or_0, start_offset_or_-1, end_offset_or_-1)`.

`artifact_role` is one of `RADIOLOGY_REPORT`, `RADIOLOGY_DETAIL`, `DS_REPORT`, or `DS_DETAIL`. `section_token` is the deterministic ordinal of the section heading under the frozen tokenizer. Offsets are UTF-8 character offsets in the exact stored text after no whitespace-changing normalization. For structured detail rows, `field_ordinal` is used and offsets are `-1`. A missing or unreadable span identifier is `UNKNOWN`, not a synthetic match.

### 2. Frozen field normalization

For matching only, normalize Unicode to NFC, lowercase, collapse runs of whitespace, trim, and map only the preregistered synonyms in the codebook for target cluster, modality, laterality, action, polarity, interval, recipient, replacement, and conflict. Do not use a learned model, unconstrained stemming, or semantic similarity. Values outside the dictionary are `OTHER_UNMAPPED` and never match a named value. The raw span and raw reviewer code are retained.

### 3. Panel inventory key and order

Each panel emits one row per candidate unit with

`K=(primary_span_id, sorted_secondary_span_ids, target_cluster, modality, laterality, action, due_interval, polarity, recipient, replacement, conflict)`.

Unknown fields are represented by a literal `UNKNOWN`, not an empty string. The panel's canonical order is the lexicographic order of `(primary_span_id, min secondary span ID, target cluster, modality, laterality, action, due interval, polarity, reviewer-local ordinal)`, where the final reviewer-local ordinal is only a stable tie-breaker for exact duplicate rows. Duplicate rows with identical span IDs and fields are collapsed; conflicting rows sharing a primary span remain separate and force unresolved matching. No ordering is learned from route, atom, or outcome.

### 4. Deterministic cross-panel matching

Let `L1` and `L2` be the ordered H1 and H2 inventories. First match rows having identical nonmissing `primary_span_id` and identical target-cluster/modality/laterality/action identity fields. For each unmatched row, permit a second-stage match only when the sets of nonmissing span IDs overlap and all nonmissing identity fields agree. Construct the bipartite graph of these eligible edges. Select the unique maximum-cardinality matching; if multiple maximum matchings remain, select the lexicographically smallest sorted edge list. If the tie cannot be resolved by that rule because duplicate identical spans remain, mark the connected component `UNRESOLVED_DUPLICATE` and do not silently match it. Unmatched rows on either side are retained as adverse-compatible additions/deletions.

This matcher is not a consensus procedure and does not use H3. A unit is reproducible only if the required identity and field comparisons below pass after this fixed matching. The final unit list is the lexicographic serialization of the matched pairs followed by H1-only and H2-only rows, with an immutable `unit_key` equal to the SHA-256 of the serialized source-span and frozen-field tuple. The serialized inventory, edge list, matching, tie status, and hash are stored as proof traces.

## Explicit bridge algebra and coding table

For admission `i`, `U_i` is the inherited canonical unit universe after the parent's reciprocal-restatement handling. The inherited route remains computed first from its locked atomic labels and is never changed by bridge fields.

For each admission, define `valid_p` for each H1/H2 inventory as true only if packet integrity, source-span syntax, codebook vocabulary, and required fields are valid. Define `MATCH_i` as true only when all units are matched by the deterministic algorithm, no duplicate component is unresolved, and every nonmissing identity/field comparison is equal. Then:

`D_REPRO_i=1` iff `valid_1=valid_2=1`, `MATCH_i=1`, and every required route-defining field has identical non-unknown values in the matched H1/H2 rows.

`D_REPRO_i=0` iff the inventories are valid but a deterministic mismatch, unmatched unit, contradiction, or unresolved duplicate is present. `D_REPRO_i=UNKNOWN` iff packet corruption, nonresponse, unreadable span, or invalid required syntax prevents the state from being decided. Unknown is retained in the parent compatible-state construction; it is never converted to favorable agreement.

`A_TEXT_COMPLETE_i=1` iff the admission has at least one retained actionable unit, H2 is valid, and every required field for every retained unit is explicit, internally nonconflicting, and not inferred from a proxy. Required fields are target/cluster, action/modality, due interval or explicitly `NO_DUE_INTERVAL_REQUIRED`, polarity, recipient/responsibility or explicitly `NO_RECIPIENT_REQUIRED`, replacement/conflict rule, and supporting source span. A field explicitly stating that no action is required is an explicit negative action, not missingness.

`A_TEXT_COMPLETE_i=0` iff a valid actionable inventory has one or more required fields absent, contradictory, unreadable, or proxy-inferred. It is `UNKNOWN` if H2 validity or packet integrity prevents classification. `A_TEXT_GAP_i=1` iff `D_REPRO_i=1` and `A_TEXT_COMPLETE_i=0`; otherwise it is not assigned a favorable/adverse value outside the declared ratio domain.

### Pre-label eligibility table

| inherited state | retained actionable units | `D_REPRO` | `A_TEXT_COMPLETE` | inclusion in `G_text` |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `NOACTION`, valid empty H1 and H2 inventories | 0 | 1 | `NA` | denominator excluded; report separately as valid empty-inventory rate |
| `NOACTION`, either panel nonempty or unresolved | 0 after no safe collapse | 0 or `UNKNOWN` under rules above | `NA` only if both valid empty; else `UNKNOWN` | excluded; adverse-compatible state retained |
| `DEFER` with one or more explicit actionable units | >0 | computed normally | computed normally from H2 fields; unresolved fields are 0/`UNKNOWN`, never automatically complete | included only if `D_REPRO=1` and H2 state is decidable |
| `DEFER` with no actionable units and valid empty inventories | 0 | 1 | `NA` | excluded; report as `DEFER` empty-inventory state |
| any route with packet corruption/nonresponse | unknown | `UNKNOWN` | `UNKNOWN` or `NA` only when valid empty inventories are proven | excluded from point ratio and retained in compatible bounds |

`G_text` is therefore the jointly estimated finite-population ratio

`G_text = P_w(A_TEXT_COMPLETE=0 | D_REPRO=1, actionable-domain, inherited atom, inherited route)`,

where the denominator is explicitly the `D_REPRO=1` admissions with at least one retained actionable unit and decidable H2 state. The zero-unit domain is never made to look favorable by assigning `A_TEXT_COMPLETE=1`. A separate table reports the fraction of valid zero-unit `NOACTION`/`DEFER` records and all reasons for exclusion. Numerator and denominator contributions are optimized jointly in every compatible state; marginal extrema are prohibited.

## H3 evidence-availability audit

The following eight bridge facts are frozen before labels: `VIEWED_BEFORE_DEPARTURE`, `FINALIZED_SIGNED`, `COMMUNICATED`, `ORDER_OR_REFERRAL_EXECUTED`, `RESPONSIBLE_TEAM_ACCEPTED`, `SCHEDULED_OR_HANDOFF_CONFIRMED`, `PATIENT_OR_CAREGIVER_CAPACITY_PREFERENCE`, and `POST_DISCHARGE_FOLLOWUP_OR_OUTCOME`.

Before H3 labels, generate a machine-readable availability manifest from the exact source catalog and schemas. The manifest has, for each fact, the permitted source/table/column candidates, whether a direct event field exists, and the rule distinguishing `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` from `UNKNOWN`. For this snapshot, there is no direct event field for any of the eight facts. `storetime`, `charttime`, `transfertime`, `intime`, `outtime`, `dischtime`, `deathtime`, `discharge_location`, service, ICU exposure, note wording, or death is not a substitute for any listed workflow event. Consequently, an H3 reviewer must code each fact `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` unless a future explicitly permitted source column is added before protocol sealing; it is never coded `OBSERVED` merely because a proxy exists. `UNKNOWN` is reserved for a source/packet that is nominally available but unreadable, corrupted, or blocked from inspection under the packet rules. `CONTRADICTED` is used only if an actual permitted direct event field exists and explicitly contradicts the fact; no such field is present in this MIMIC snapshot.

This yields `E_WORKFLOW_GAP_i=1` when any of the eight is `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` or `UNKNOWN`, and `E_OUTCOME_GAP_i=1` when the follow-up/outcome fact is either. These are availability findings, not negative clinical outcomes. H3 is not included in `D_REPRO`; it is reported as a separate evidence vector, preventing an unavoidable source limitation from masquerading as reviewer disagreement.

Supportive results are a narrow, bounded atom-level pattern with non-adverse-compatible `D_REPRO`, a finite and stable `G_text`, reproducible missing-field reasons, and a predeclared H3 vector. They justify only a prospective silent data-collection specification and perhaps a governed silent bridge design. Adverse results include a large/wide actionability gap, unmatched or unstable units, route-invariant control failures, or atom-specific unresolved fields; the affected atom remains `DEFER` and is not nominated. A high H3 gap caused solely by unavailable MIMIC workflow/outcome fields is an evidence limitation, not clinical failure. Inconclusive results include sparse support, wide compatible bounds, nonresponse, packet/seal/chronology failure, or unresolved source availability; inconclusive is not safety.

Falsification occurs if a reviewer sees route/atom/weight information before sealing; H3 changes `D_REPRO`, `A_TEXT_COMPLETE`, route, atom, sample, or chronology; the same inventory yields different matching under repeated execution; ties are resolved by analyst choice; empty `NOACTION` is counted as complete; `DEFER` is automatically treated as incomplete or complete; missing workflow evidence is relabeled observed; compatible ratio extrema are optimized separately; or any conclusion calls textual completeness clinical actionability, calls absent workflow data a negative outcome, or claims causality/benefit.

A successful retrospective bridge still requires a separately governed prospective silent study with direct timestamps/provenance for viewing, finalization, communication, responsible-team acceptance, order/referral execution, scheduling/handoff, patient/caregiver capacity and preference, outside-plan visibility, and follow-up/outcomes. Expert adjudication is required for appropriateness and safety, and an outcome study is required for harm, utility, or benefit. No live deployment, care delivery, follow-up completion, temporal transport, or clinical benefit is inferable from this MIMIC experiment.
