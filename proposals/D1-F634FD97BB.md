# Preoperative HCC MVI: transparent trajectories before learned order

Status: substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. Population, outcome, day −90:−1 window, temporal split, bindings, and clinical question are unchanged. A new aggregate audit was executed; no hypothesis model was fitted.

## Question, evidence boundary, and advance

The clinical question remains whether longitudinal preoperative routine-care measurements add reproducible information about pathology-confirmed microvascular invasion (MVI) before first recorded HCC resection. A reliable increment could inform surgical approach/margin discussions, neoadjuvant-study eligibility, and counseling; it cannot show treatment benefit.

Inspected evidence supports that a static AFP/tumor-size/margin score attained C-statistics near 0.81 in two Chinese validation cohorts and that MVI is pathologic [K1]. Three-tier M0/M1/M2 grading is defined histopathologically and M2 was associated with recurrence/mortality in one selected retrospective series [K2]. Routine-care observation times can encode care behavior and severity and must not be treated automatically as biology [K3].

The parent's T-value versus latest-snapshot contrast was insufficient: gain could arise from simple slopes, extrema, variability, or cross-marker co-change rather than learned chronology. The repaired hypothesis is:

> In the unchanged strict cohort, transparent day −90:−1 trajectory summaries will improve temporally held-out M1/M2-versus-M0 prediction over the latest snapshot; any stronger claim that learned chronology adds value requires T-value to beat matched transparent value summaries and permutation-invariant learning, and requires order destruction to attenuate the gain. Improvement confined to real-time/intensity features supports an observation-process explanation, not tumor biology.

This separates whether longitudinal values help, whether interpretable summaries suffice, and whether residual chronology predicts. Tumor burden, reserve, prior treatment, workflow, assay/template era, and informative measurement remain rivals.

## Matched model ladder

All dictionaries, thresholds, transformations, penalties, hyperparameters, and selection rules are learned in train and frozen through validation before test. Learned models use five fixed seeds.

### S-value and S-full: latest snapshots

S-value is penalized logistic regression on age, sex, year, service, latest strict value and missing indicator by exact label observed in ≥100 training patients, and prespecified AFP, PIVKA-II, platelets, albumin, bilirubin, ALT, AST, INR/PT, GGT, ALP, WBC, hemoglobin, creatinine, plus latest report-derived size, multiplicity, irregular margin/capsule, and modality. S-full preserves the parent's operational B by adding days since each latest value and total distinct measurement days. Prespecified continuous terms use splines; other labels use elastic net. Report the reduced AFP/size/margin benchmark.

S-value is the matched no-timing comparator for V and T-value. S-full is the matched clinical snapshot for F and T-full. Discard any report containing MVI-grade, generic MVI/microvascular-invasion, pathology, or postoperative wording before allowed extraction. Repeat without any report inputs.

### V: transparent value trajectories

Use S-value's static/report inputs and exact-label vocabulary. Per label add scaled-value first/latest/minimum/maximum/median/range/IQR or MAD/SD (≥3 values), latest-minus-first, three equal-observation-rank block medians, Theil–Sen slope versus normalized observation rank, ordinal position of min/max, and direction-change count. Add availability indicators; zero-fill undefined summaries after training scaling. These capture trend, extrema, variability, and coarse shape without absolute event times, gaps, panel size, or visit intensity. Fit elastic-net logistic regression with splines for core labels and prespecified interactions: AFP–PIVKA-II, ALT–AST, albumin–bilirubin, INR–platelets, GGT–ALP, and WBC–platelets.

### F: transparent full trajectory/timing

Start from S-full, add every V summary, then add per label count, distinct days, span, recency, median/max gap, day of first/latest/min/max, Theil–Sen slope per day, and medians in −90:−31, −30:−8, −7:−1 plus adjacent changes. For the six pairs add shared-day availability, Spearman co-change (≥3 shared days), concordant-change fraction, mean product of consecutive standardized changes, and maximum joint excursion. Add total event/day/panel counts and early/middle/late distribution. F captures interpretable timing, synchrony, and observation intensity without learned order. Report V and F separately because F includes care-process information.

### D-bag, T-value, T-shuffle, T-full

Same-day CSV row order is never chronology. Each model first pools all exact-label/scaled-value tokens on a calendar day with a permutation-invariant set encoder; only the resulting day embeddings may be ordered.

- D-bag: permutation-invariant Deep Sets over all label/value tokens, with S-value static/report inputs and without position, timestamp, gap, panel, or count tokens. It learns nonlinear cross-marker interactions but not chronology.
- T-value: ≤4-layer, width ≤128, ≤8-head transformer over ordinally ordered day embeddings, with S-value static/report inputs. It sees day rank but no date, day-to-index, gap, panel size, or observation-count feature.
- T-shuffle: identical T-value architecture trained/evaluated with deterministic permutations of each patient's day embeddings, independently seeded and averaged over 20 locked evaluation permutations. It preserves each day's contemporaneous marker set, the patient label/value multiset, and number of observed days while destroying between-day chronology.
- T-full: T-value plus calendar day-to-index, inter-day gap, within-day panel composition/size, and observation intensity, with S-full static/report inputs. A timing-only T-full control masks all values. A value–day reassignment control permutes day embeddings across that patient's observed timing grid, preserving observation days/panel sizes while breaking value–time alignment.

Cap at 512 raw tokens before within-day pooling using training-frozen deterministic day-stratified retention that preserves latest and earlier days; report truncation by split/outcome. Use weighted binary cross-entropy, dropout, validation-log-loss early stopping, and five seeds. Also evaluate frozen T-value on 20 permuted day orders as an OOD falsification, never alone as proof.

V tests simple value trajectories without real-time information. F is the complete transparent trajectory comparator capturing trend, extrema, variability, synchronization, timing, and intensity. D separates nonlinear value sets from order. T-value tests residual ordinal patterns. T-full tests learned timing/process patterns beyond F, never automatically biology.

## Estimands, metrics, and interpretation rules

Report paired AUROC, AUPRC, log loss, Brier, calibration intercept/slope/in-the-large, and decision net benefit at risks 0.20/0.30/0.40/0.50 on identical test patients with 2,000 paired patient bootstraps. Report sensitivity/specificity/PPV/NPV at validation-selected 80% sensitivity and calibration by sex, age <65/≥65, tumor size ≤5/>5 cm, service, and year when ≥20 events/cell.

Fixed comparisons:

1. V − S-value: transparent value trajectory versus a matched no-timing snapshot; V must also be noninferior to S-full.
2. F − S-full: complete transparent trajectory/timing versus the parent's operational snapshot.
3. T-value − V and T-value − D-bag: residual learned ordinal structure beyond hand summaries and nonlinear unordered interactions.
4. T-value − T-shuffle plus frozen-model day-order destruction: chronology dependence.
5. T-full − F and T-full − T-value, plus timing-only and value–day reassignment controls: learned timing/process beyond transparent timing and ordinal values.

Transparent value-trajectory support requires locked-test V versus S-value AUROC improvement ≥0.02 and Brier reduction ≥0.01, both paired intervals excluding zero, and no material inferiority to S-full (lower 95% bound >−0.01 AUROC and Brier increase <0.005). Complete transparent longitudinal support instead requires F versus S-full to meet the same ≥0.02 AUROC and ≥0.01 Brier improvement gates. Either claim also requires validation-frozen calibration slope 0.8–1.2, net-benefit gain at ≥2 adjacent thresholds without material harm elsewhere, and no direction reversal in 2019 versus 2020–2021.

A separate learned order-specific claim requires all: T-value has ≥0.02 AUROC and ≥0.01 Brier improvement versus V with intervals excluding zero; improves both metrics versus D-bag with intervals excluding zero; beats T-shuffle; order destruction removes ≥50% of T-value's Brier gain over V and materially attenuates AUROC; calibration/net-benefit gates pass; and no era reversal. This supports order-sensitive prediction, not mechanism.

If V beats S-value or F beats S-full but T-value fails versus V/D, interpretable trajectories help and added learned-order value is rejected. If D matches T-value, nonlinear value interactions suffice. If only F/T-full improves, timing/synchrony/care process predicts; it is not tumor biology. T-full alone cannot satisfy value/order claims.

Adverse evidence: precise exclusion of material V−S-value and F−S-full gains; V or D/T-shuffle matching T-value; order destruction not attenuating gain; gain confined to F/T-full; stable S-value/S-full/V/F with learned-model miscalibration; or era/subgroup reversal. Inconclusive: broad intervals, failed adjudication, seed/dependency instability, marked era reversal, poor calibration, severe truncation, or gain confined to day −1/flagged-report patients. Imprecise null is not biological refutation.

Mandatory sensitivities/falsifications:

- day −7 cutoff, latest-day-only, and exclusion of 13 encounter-clock anomalies;
- report-free run and conservative rejection of flagged reports;
- exclude >1-day qualifying-start-span encounters and clinically review ambiguous procedures;
- any-prior-recorded-treatment exclusion and treatment/service strata;
- labs-only, report-only, demographics/observation-only controls;
- same-day panel collapse, exact-label restriction, leave-one-year-out fits, outcome permutation, automated feature-time leakage tests;
- blinded hepatopathologist review of 200 encounters (50 M0/M1/M2 plus 50 conflict/era/ambiguous-index cases), verifying grade, HCC, specimen correspondence, and template/sampling concerns. Require ≥95% overall and ≥90% per era/grade stratum;
- blinded radiology/hepatobiliary review of 200 report extractions, including all MVI-flagged reports, confirming no pathology/postoperative content consumption.

Support establishes only internal temporally held-out prediction. Causality, tumor biology, external transport, sampling adequacy, and treatment benefit require external prospective data, standardized assays/imaging, expert pathology review, and decision-impact study.
