## 0. What changed vs the parent (the scientific defect repaired)

The parent tests whether the stress-hyperglycemia ratio (SHR) is separable from absolute
admission glucose and from selective/invalid HbA1c measurement. Two defects remain:

1. **Collinearity / non-identifiability.** SHR = G_adm / (28.7·HbA1c − 46.7) shares its
   numerator with absolute admission glucose G_adm. Entering "SHR" and "absolute glucose"
   together in one logistic model is ill-conditioned: the "independent SHR" coefficient is a
   near-degenerate contrast, so a null *or* a positive result is hard to interpret. This child
   replaces it with a *prespecified additive decomposition* in the two measured quantities and
   an exactly separable log-ratio parameterisation.
2. **No negative-control exposure.** The parent's rivals (R-absolute, R-select, R-validity)
   are discriminated mainly by measurement-propensity IPW and strata. Adding a
   pre-registered negative-control **exposure** (HbA1c-implied chronic glycemia alone) gives a
   direct directional test that separates acute-on-chronic signalling from baseline metabolic
   risk and from selection.

Method exploration is on the SAME question (see §7): an interpretable penalized-logistic
baseline **B0** vs a substantive joint **latent measurement-selection + outcome** model **A1**.

## 1. Unresolved question and its clinical importance

SHR is being promoted as a "readily available, cost-free tool for clinical risk stratification"
and trial enrichment [K1], and is linked to downstream outcomes such as delirium and mortality
[K2]. The unresolved question is **what the SHR association is**: does *relative*
(acute-on-chronic) hyperglycemia carry prognostic information **beyond absolute admission
glucose, known diabetes and severity** — or is any apparent independent SHR effect produced by
(a) **selective HbA1c measurement** (only a severity/dysglycemia-enriched subset of ICU
patients get HbA1c), or (b) **invalid/biased HbA1c** in acute illness (anemia, transfusion,
renal failure)?

Consequence of resolving: if relative glycemia is separable, SHR supports a
metabolic-reserve interpretation and could stratify/enrich trials. If it is **not** separable
once absolute glucose, severity and measurement propensity are accounted for — or if the
apparent effect tracks measurement selection rather than glycemia — then SHR should **not** be
deployed as a stratification or enrichment marker, preventing a redundant biomarker from being
adopted. External evidence already hints the relative metric can add little discrimination
beyond absolute glucose [K3]. Either outcome changes a decision.

## 2. Leading claim vs strongest rivals (opposing predictions)

- **H-relative (leading).** At the same absolute admission glucose, higher *relative* elevation
  (higher G_adm relative to chronic Gc) identifies higher risk. Prediction: in a model already
  containing G_adm, the chronic-glycemia term Gc (or the log-ratio log G_adm − log Gc) is
  non-null with a sign implying higher risk at high acute / low chronic glucose; the increment
  survives measurement-propensity IPW; the negative-control exposure (Gc alone) has a smaller /
  differently-shaped effect; the negative-control outcome is null.
- **R-absolute (rival).** Only absolute glucose and severity matter; relative elevation adds
  nothing. Prediction: Gc adds ~0 discrimination beyond M1 (G_adm), CI includes 0.
- **R-select (rival, informative measurement).** HbA1c is measured in a severity/dysglycemia-
  selected subset; apparent effects are cohort composition. Prediction: the Gc/log-ratio effect
  attenuates markedly under measurement-propensity IPW; the negative-control outcome shows a
  parallel signal; A1 attributes a large share of the effect to the selection factor.
- **R-validity (rival, measurement error).** HbA1c is unreliable in acute illness. Prediction:
  the effect is unstable across anemia/transfusion/renal strata and shrinks under
  measurement-error / error-in-variable correction; A1's measurement submodel absorbs it.

These are separable on the same fitted outputs: H-relative survives glucose adjustment AND IPW
AND a null negative control; R-absolute dies at M1→M2; R-select produces strong IPW attenuation
and a non-null negative control; R-validity produces stratum instability.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries, variables, outcomes (frozen design)

- **Source.** Frozen archive `mimic-iv-3.1.zip`; members `mimic-iv-3.1/hosp/labevents.csv.gz`,
  `hosp/d_labitems.csv.gz`, `hosp/admissions.csv.gz`, `hosp/patients.csv.gz`,
  `hosp/diagnoses_icd.csv.gz`, `hosp/d_icd_diagnoses.csv.gz`, `icu/icustays.csv.gz`,
  `icu/chartevents.csv.gz`.
- **Population.** Adults (≥18, `patients.anchor_age`) with a **first** ICU stay (`icustays`)
  and both a measurable admission glucose and a measurable HbA1c. Index time `t0` = first
  `icustays.intime`.
- **Admission glucose.** First `labevents` value with `itemid=50931` (Chemistry; sensitivity
  `50809`/`52027` Blood-gas, `52569`) in `[t0−6 h, t0+24 h]`. Valid range 20–600 mg/dL.
- **HbA1c.** `labevents` `itemid=50852` ("% Hemoglobin A1c"); sensitivity `50854` (absolute).
  Taken in `[t0−24 h, t0+24 h]`. Valid range 3–20 %.
- **Derived quantities.** `Gc = 28.7·HbA1c(%) − 46.7` (chronic-glycemia-implied average glucose,
  mg/dL); `SHR = G_adm / Gc`. Both `G_adm` and `Gc` standardised (per-SD) from the training fold.
- **Keys / joins.** `subject_id` (patient), `hadm_id` (admission), `stay_id` (ICU stay);
  `labevents` joins on `subject_id`(+`hadm_id`); `icustays` links `hadm_id ↔ stay_id` via
  `intime`/`outtime`; `admissions` gives `admittime`/`dischtime`/`deathtime`;
  `diagnoses_icd`+`d_icd_diagnoses` give diabetes/anemia/renal flags.
- **Exposure (primary, decomposed).** Two standardised terms entered **separately**:
  `G_adm` and `Gc`, plus the product `G_adm×Gc`; and the exactly separable log-ratio
  `lr = log G_adm − log Gc`. The estimand for H-relative is the coefficient on `Gc` (and on
  `lr`) **conditional on `G_adm`**.
- **Negative-control exposure.** `Gc` alone (chronic glycemia) — see §5.
- **Primary outcome.** 28-day all-cause mortality from `admissions.deathtime`; censored at
  `dischtime` when `deathtime` is absent. (Linkage to a national death index is **not** in the
  configured tables → mortality is hospital-recorded only; stated limitation.)
- **Negative-control outcome.** Incident ICU-acquired venous thromboembolism (ICD codes in
  `diagnoses_icd`) — a deterioration not plausibly caused by acute glycemia. Declared
  secondary and imperfect (coding/ascertainment bias).
- **Covariates.** age, sex, admission type, a severity score built from
  `chartevents`/`labevents` (SOFA-style components), sepsis flag, known-diabetes flag, baseline
  creatinine, hemoglobin, transfusion indicator.

## 4. Statistical analysis (pre-specified)

- **Split.** Locked 70/30, ordered by `icustays.intime` (leakage-safe ordering; MIMIC-IV dates
  are per-subject shifted, so this is an ordering proxy, not a calendar-era split). Model
  selection on train; reported effects/CIs on held-out test.
- **Adjustment ladder.** M0 = severity + age/sex; **M1 = M0 + G_adm**; **M2 = M1 + Gc**;
  **M3 = M2 + G_adm×Gc**. The decisive contrast is **M1 → M2**: does the chronic-glycemia term
  add beyond absolute glucose?
- **Discrimination.** ΔAUROC(M1→M2) with 1000-sample bootstrap CI; calibration slope.
- **Shape.** Restricted cubic splines for `G_adm`, `Gc`, and the log-ratio.
- **Selection arm.** Propensity model for HbA1c measurement (demographics, severity, diabetes
  dx, admission type) → IPW stabilised weights; recompute M1→M2 in the weighted cohort.
- **Validity arm.** Effect across anemia (low hemoglobin), transfusion, and renal-function
  strata; quantitative bias / measurement-error sensitivity.
- **Unmeasured confounding.** E-value for the M1→M2 contrast.
- **Alternatives A1.** Joint model: measurement-selection submodel (selected-into-HbA1c) +
  outcome submodel sharing a latent chronic-glycemia/severity factor, fitted by EM/MLE (CPU);
  reports the fraction of the apparent `Gc` effect attributable to selection vs glycemia.

## 5. Falsification / outcome rules (pre-specified)

- **Supportive (H-relative).** In M2, the `Gc` (or log-ratio) coefficient has 95% CI excluding
  0 **and** ΔAUROC(M1→M2) ≥ 0.01 (prespecified) with CI excluding 0, **and** the effect persists
  under HbA1c-measurement IPW, **and** the negative-control exposure (Gc alone) is distinctly
  weaker/differently shaped, **and** the negative-control outcome is null.
- **Adverse (R-absolute).** ΔAUROC(M1→M2) ≈ 0 (CI includes 0) and the `Gc` coefficient CI
  includes 0 → SHR adds nothing beyond absolute admission glucose.
- **Adverse (R-select/R-validity).** Large attenuation of the `Gc` effect under IPW, a non-null
  negative-control outcome, or strong stratum instability → SHR is a measurement/selection
  artifact. A1 attributing most of the effect to the selection factor supports this.
- **Inconclusive.** Power-gate failure (see §6), wide CIs spanning both the supportive and
  adverse thresholds, or results dominated by M3/threshold choices. Reported as inconclusive,
  **not** as evidence for either claim.
- **Negative controls count as adversarial tests:** a positive negative-control outcome or
  exposure weakens any H-relative conclusion and is reported as such.

## 6. Power gate (bounded, executable)

A prespecified probe reads `labevents` (itemids 50852/50854/50931/50809/52027/52569) +
`icustays` + `admissions` and reports: N first-ICU stays with both measurements, and the
28-day mortality event count. Continue only if events ≥ the prespecified minimum (EPV ≥ 10 for
M3); otherwise the result is **inconclusive** and the question is deferred. This probe is
executable in the readiness/compile stage.

## 7. Method comparison on the SAME question (required)

- **B0 — interpretable baseline.** L2-penalized logistic regression, outcome ~ M0 + `G_adm` +
  `Gc` (+ interaction). Inputs: the frozen cohort table above. Fit target: 28-day mortality.
  Split: locked 70/30. Evaluation: ΔAUROC(M1→M2), calibrated coefficients, bootstrap CIs.
  Budget: CPU, seconds–minutes. **What B0 assumes:** HbA1c is measured-at-random given measured
  covariates.
- **A1 — substantive alternative.** Joint measurement-selection + outcome latent-factor model
  fitted by EM/MLE. Inputs: same table plus the HbA1c-measurement indicator. Fit target: the
  selection and outcome equations jointly. Evaluation: share of the M1→M2 effect attributable
  to selection vs glycemia, with bootstrap CIs. Budget: CPU, minutes (<1 h).
  **What A1 reveals that B0 cannot:** the R-select rival is a *latent-selection* problem — if
  selection into HbA1c measurement depends on an unmeasured factor also affecting outcome, B0's
  adjusted coefficient is biased and cannot say by how much; A1 identifies and quantifies that
  channel. If A1 shows negligible selection contribution, that strengthens H-relative; if large,
  it supports R-select. Neither B0 nor A1 can establish mechanism.

No GPU is required; both fit on CPU. GPU is not requested.

## 9. Honest limits

Single-centre retrospective MIMIC-IV; date-shifted timestamps; out-of-hospital death
unobservable (hospital-recorded mortality only); HbA1c measured selectively so prevalence and
power are unobserved until the §6 probe runs; HbA1c unreliable in acute illness (bias/validity
rival mitigated but not removed); no cystatin C or continuous glucose data; negative-control
outcome imperfect. Association only — no causal/mechanistic claim; clinical adjudication and
external validation would be required for any causal conclusion.
