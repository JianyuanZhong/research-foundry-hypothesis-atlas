# Episode 32 — Is within-person first-diagnosis ORDER of a co-present cardiometabolic pair (E11/I10) separable from ANTECEDENT care-intensity/ascertainment?

Operation: **evolve** of the top valid, delivery-complete parent `[prior hypothesis]` (mu 32.49, sigma 6.66, comparisons 1, `valid`, `reference_delivery=complete`); grandparent chain `[prior hypothesis]` (mu 33.03) → `[prior hypothesis]` (mu 33.90). The scientific question (does first-diagnosis ORDER add information beyond co-presence?) is preserved and the verified bindings are unchanged. **No Δ_order or scientific effect is asserted in this document — this is a pre-registered design.**

## The single scientific change, and why it matters
The parent operationalised the care-intensity/ascertainment rival (B1) with a documentation-breadth proxy measured **contemporaneously** with the two index diagnoses (distinct ICD-10 blocks *present anywhere* in the record). That proxy lies **on the causal path**: because a patient who is jointly investigated and coded for E11 and I10 will accrue those very codes, breadth-total is partly a *consequence* of the exposure and cannot separate "order reflects joint/care coding" (B1) from "order reflects biology" (A). Its own diagnostic showed the target group is far higher-breadth (median 28 vs 8 blocks), i.e. the confound is large and the parent's proxy cannot exclude it.

**This child makes B1 separable** by re-specifying the discriminator as a **temporally ANTECEDENT documentation-breadth proxy**: the breadth accrued **strictly before** the earlier of the two index diagnoses. Because it is dated before the pair exists, it cannot be mechanically produced by the index co-coding event, so conditioning on it (and stratifying by it) removes the most obvious ascertainment pathway. A generic-record-volume **negative-control outcome** completes the test: if ORDER predicts the *generic* post-index volume as strongly as the *disease-specific* accrual, the ORDER signal is care coupling, not disease sequence.

## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Multimorbidity tools (counts, CKM stage, multimorbidity indices) represent burden as a **set/count**. Clinicians reason about **sequence** — diabetes-then-hypertension vs hypertension-then-diabetes. [K1] reports the *direction* of a cardiometabolic↔internalising trajectory predicts 10-year cardiovascular/renal risk, so order is not obviously inert; but [K1] does not test whether pairwise order beats a co-presence/count baseline and does not adjudicate the coding artifact. The concrete unresolved question: **among people with a fixed co-present pair, does first-diagnosis ORDER carry out-of-sample information about downstream ICD-10 accrual that survives an antecedent care-intensity proxy?** Either answer is decision-relevant — if no, the set/count representation is adequate and sequence should not be promoted in stratification (a useful negative); if yes, sequence warrants a temporally-resolved cohort follow-up.

## Leading claim vs rivals; discriminating observations
- **Leading claim (A):** holding the pair fixed, ORDER adds out-of-sample information about downstream ICD-10 accrual beyond co-presence/count, age, sex, interval, co-coding intensity **and antecedent breadth** (Δ_order ≠ 0).
- **Rival B1 (strongest; care-coupling/ascertainment) — now separable:** "order" is a marker of joint coding/testing and accumulated care contact, not biology.
- **Rival B2 (shared continuum):** order is a projection of one graded burden process; the useful signal is stage/count [K3].
- **Rival B3 (network/hub):** apparent pairwise structure is hub centrality, not ordering [K2].

**Discriminating observations (pre-registered):**
1. **Antecedent-breadth conditioning + stratification (NEW).** Compute `antecedent_breadth = # distinct 3-char ICD-10 blocks whose first-occurrence date is strictly before min(first-E11, first-I10)`. B1 predicts Δ_order shrinks toward 0 after adding it and/or is concentrated in high-antecedent-breadth strata; A predicts Δ_order persists with the proxy in the model and in low/mid strata.
2. **Negative-control outcome (NEW, primary falsifier).** Generic post-index volume = total distinct blocks first-dated strictly after the later pair member (any category). Disease-specific outcome = post-index accrual restricted to cardiometabolic blocks (I00–I99, E00–E90). B1 predicts the ORDER feature predicts generic volume ≈ as strongly as disease-specific accrual; A predicts it is specific to the disease-relevant outcome.
3. **Long-interval subgroup (≥365 days),** where same-encounter co-coding is weakest; combined here with antecedent conditioning.
4. **Co-coding-intensity gradient:** B1 predicts Δ_order concentrated in the top same-encounter co-coding bin.
5. **E11/E78 screening-coupling control pair** (mechanically co-tested lipid disorder) vs the biological E11/I10 pair.

## Pre-registered estimand (frozen for compilation)
Δ_order = held-out difference in discrimination (ΔAUC) **and** in a proper scoring rule (log-loss/Brier) between:
- **M_base:** set/count + age + sex + interval + co-coding (+ antecedent_breadth),
- **M_order:** M_base **+** the binary ORDER feature,
by participant-level cross-validation with a seed-fixed split (repeated K-fold, e.g. 5×5). Secondary: **within-stratum permutation null** — permute ORDER within strata of (age-decile × sex × antecedent-breadth bin × co-coding bin) to preserve the ascertainment structure; report the distribution and p-value. Ties excluded from the ORDER feature.

## Baseline vs substantive alternative (method exploration, same question)
- **Baseline (simple, interpretable):** M_order with ORDER as a single term — exactly the set/count-plus-flag comparison the multimorbidity literature uses; its Δ_order is the primary estimand.
- **Substantive alternative (learned):** a **diagnosis-event sequence model** over the ordered list of dated ICD-10 blocks before the later pair member (position of the pair inside the coding sequence). Information the baseline loses: whether ORDER's value is really *the pair's position within a longer within-person sequence* rather than which of two members came first. If the learned model's held-out gain ≤ the single ORDER term's, the simple term suffices and complexity is unwarranted; if larger **and it survives the antecedent proxy + negative control**, that is a genuine separable sequence signal.
- **Inputs/target/split/eval:** same table/columns, same target, same seed-fixed split, same held-out ΔAUC + proper score; both compared under identical folds.
- **Device/resource:** small tabular fit on ≈2×10⁴ discordant participants → CPU adequate; bounded GPU optional only for the sequence model. Solver envelope per inputs.json: 16 CPU / up to 8 GPU / 28800 s. The antecedent diagnostic adds no GPU need.
- **Selection reason:** the alternatives test whether the information is in the *pair relation* (baseline) or *sequence position* (learned) — a real uncertainty, not a small metric gain. See `work/gen-ep32/diag_antecedent.py`.

## Falsification criteria (pre-registered)
- **Supportive (A):** Δ_order > 0 with 95% CI excluding 0 on held-out data, ADDITION of `antecedent_breadth` does **not** collapse it, it persists in the ≥365-day subgroup and in low/mid antecedent-breadth strata, and it is **larger for the disease-specific outcome than for the generic-volume negative control**.
- **Adverse (B1):** Δ_order ≈ 0 in the long-interval subgroup; OR concentrated in the top co-coding bin; OR **collapses after adding antecedent_breadth**; OR **predicts the generic-volume negative control as strongly as the disease-specific outcome**; OR the E11/E78 control shows an equal-or-larger "order" effect → set/count representation is adequate; do not promote sequence.
- **Inconclusive:** point estimate > 0 but CI spans 0, or the empty-antecedent-window fraction / tie structure destabilises the estimator → report inconclusive, explicitly not positive.
- **Unidentifiable here:** a true biological ordering mechanism; any causal/incidence statement (no draw/death dates, prevalent records).

## Contribution beyond [K1]–[K3]
Beyond [K1] (order predicts risk but untested against a count baseline and without an ascertainment control), this proposal (i) holds the pair fixed, (ii) operationalises care coupling with a **temporally antecedent** breadth proxy that cannot be produced by the index event, and (iii) adds a generic-volume negative control. [K2] and [K3] bound the hub and shared-burden rivals but do not test order. The advance is a **falsifiable, ascertainment-separable test**, not a new topic.

## Honest limits
[K1]–[K3] are **abstract-only** (labeled in key-references.json; snapshots self-hashed). No draw/death/censoring dates → prevalent records design, no causal claim. 3-char blocks are unadjudicated (need a clinical code list). Same-day ties are ~40% of dated pairs and are excluded, limiting power. **Antecedent breadth is still an imperfect care-intensity proxy** (prior care contact correlates with future accrual), and the pre-index window is empty in 28.7% of discordant pairs, so it can under- or over-adjust; the generic-volume negative control is the safeguard, not proof. Findings concern coding/accrual patterns, not biological mechanism, and imply no treatment recommendation.
