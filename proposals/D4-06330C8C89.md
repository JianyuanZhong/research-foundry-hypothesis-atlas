## Question, evidence boundary, and claims

Existing evidence supports creatinine and cystatin C as filtration biomarkers and supports use of combined equations in appropriate assessment; it does not establish that baseline discordance improves a testing decision in this export. The primary unresolved question remains: among adults with certified baseline creatinine-equation eGFR at least 60 and a complete qualifying first available UK Biobank repeat assessment, does baseline cystatin-C/creatinine discordance improve baseline-only ranking of repeat creatinine-equation status beyond age, sex, and baseline creatinine eGFR?

The new, prespecified secondary question is: within the same selected repeat-attender cohort, does the baseline-only incremental ranking identify participants with higher risk of **all-cause death from the repeat assessment date through five years**, compared with the baseline model, and does any apparent enrichment persist after accounting descriptively for the observed interval band? This is a prognostic association/transport-of-ranking question, not an effect of ordering a test. It cannot establish that cystatin C causes mortality, that testing changes mortality, CKD, chronicity, measured GFR, treatment, cost-effectiveness, or patient utility.

The strongest claim licensed by supportive results is limited to conditional prediction/ranking in observed complete first-repeat attenders, plus (only if the additional endpoint certificate passes) prognostic enrichment for subsequent all-cause death in that selected cohort. A positive result cannot justify a clinical threshold or a recommendation to order cystatin C. A negative mortality extension does not invalidate biomarker utility for other outcomes; a positive mortality extension does not prove benefit from testing.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

Retain all parent falsifiers: exact source/schema/hash and join checks; certificate digest and contradiction checks; independent equation tests for both sexes and all min/max branches; static baseline-only leakage audit; 200 fold-aware label permutations (seed 520242); permutation of Z within baseline eGFRcr decile and sex; reversed-label negative control; interval-band sensitivity; selection-weighted descriptive sensitivity; and ten-repeat stability. Add:

1. **Mortality label-shift sentinel:** shift all certified death dates by a fixed prespecified offset of +365 days in a private test copy and verify that the five-year label changes only according to the declared boundary; failure indicates date arithmetic or inclusive-boundary error. This sentinel cannot be reported as biology.
2. **Administrative-censoring sentinel:** replace `C` with `C-1 day` in a private test and verify only the expected boundary labels change; unresolved deaths after `C` force neutral stop.
3. **Negative endpoint:** repeat the entire mortality pipeline for the artificial reversed label `1-Ydeath5`; an equally large discordance gain is evidence of leakage or coding failure, not support.
4. **Competing-event honesty:** because all-cause death is the event, no competing-risk interpretation is made. Hospital diagnoses, cancer, CKD codes, treatment, and cause-specific death are not added without separate certified fields and adjudication.
5. **Attendance dependence:** mortality contrasts are explicitly conditional on complete repeat attendance and follow-up opportunity. Weighted descriptive attendance analyses cannot make the result population-representative or causal.

Interpretation is gated as follows. **Supportive primary** requires all original technical/null gates, favorable paired Brier and capacity contrasts over the contiguous 5–15% capacity region with simultaneous 98% uncertainty, repeat stability, and no materially adverse interval or equation sensitivity. **Supportive mortality extension** additionally requires the mortality certificate, complete endpoint labeling/censoring checks, sufficient events, and the same prespecified favorable region; it supports only incremental prognostic ranking for five-year all-cause death in `S_M`. **Adverse** means technically valid, reproducibly worse M1 performance; it argues against incremental ranking for that estimand, not against cystatin C generally. **Inconclusive** means uncertainty crosses the null, heterogeneity is material, event scarcity occurs, or gains appear at isolated capacities/endpoints; no threshold or clinical recommendation follows. **Neutral technical stop** means missing/contradictory certificate, source/schema mismatch, date/censor ambiguity, equation or null failure, or incomplete mortality ascertainment; it is neither adverse nor evidence against the biology.

Even a supportive mortality extension does not establish testing benefit, treatment benefit, CKD, chronicity, measured GFR, causality, utility, cost-effectiveness, or transportability. Those require clinical adjudication, repeated clinical records, measured GFR, observed testing/treatment decisions, harms/costs/preferences, and a prospective or target-trial-compatible design.
