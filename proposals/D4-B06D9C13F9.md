# Fail-closed field certification for baseline-only repeat kidney-status prioritization

## Lineage and substantive repair

This proposal is a single-parent successor of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's scientific question, estimand, source fields, equations, model dimensions, deterministic cross-fitting, uncertainty, nulls, and noncausal claim boundaries. The only scientific-execution repair is to make field certification, evidence states, and output permissions machine-checkable and fail-closed. It also corrects the parent's stale attempt-specific schema and guide paths and distinguishes the SHA-256 of each schema artifact from the schema's internal declared fingerprint.

The question remains: conditional on certified field and assay semantics, does adding baseline cystatin-C/creatinine discordance improve out-of-fold prioritization of an equation-derived low creatinine-eGFR state at the next qualifying observed exported repeat, compared with sex, age, and baseline creatinine-eGFR alone? The repeat is an observed exported instance 1 occurring 2 through 8 years after baseline, inclusive. This is schedule-marginal among participants who have a qualifying observed repeat; it is not fixed-horizon risk, not a claim that instance 1 is the first clinical test, and not a causal or clinical-utility study.

## Evidence already available and the current honest state

The frozen local evidence establishes the UKB snapshot, exact source files, table schemas, required column presence, one-to-one `eid` relationships, and field 31 coding (0 female, 1 male). It also supplies metadata for field 21022 age at recruitment in years, but the active model field is 21003. The accessible metadata does not establish the required semantic certificate for active field 21003, dates 53, creatinine 30700, or cystatin C 30720. In particular, it does not establish their analyte identity, units, matrix, assay/platform, field-specific missing codes, date calendar/timezone and exact collection-date meaning, or baseline/instance-1 comparability. The supplied field-ID lists are not coding dictionaries; negative numeric values must not be globally interpreted as missing.

Accordingly, this proposal does **not** claim that the scientific lane is currently certified. With the evidence presently frozen, the deterministic expected outcome is a neutral certification stop and no biological analysis. The protocol remains useful because it produces an auditable deficiency report and validates all non-biological execution machinery without silently converting assumptions into results. A missing local certificate is not evidence against the kidney hypothesis.

The latest-board assessment of the parent calls it valid but explicitly retains the semantic-certification stop. This successor therefore changes execution governance, not the estimand: the current workspace schema artifacts and source headers are authoritative for structural bindings, while the local metadata remains insufficient for active field semantics. No inherited diagnostic counts, prior candidate text, or field-number convention can upgrade that status.

The CKD-EPI formula source, when cited, supports the published equation, not UKB field identity, assay traceability, measured-GFR equivalence, CKD diagnosis, or chronicity.

## Machine-checkable certificate contract

The executor must receive a separate frozen JSON artifact, for example `field_certificate.json`, not infer certification from the CSV values or from a top-level self-declared status. Its canonical UTF-8 representation uses sorted object keys, UTF-8 JSON without insignificant whitespace, and deterministic number/string serialization. The certificate digest is SHA-256 over that canonical representation with the `certificate_sha256` member omitted; the executor recomputes it and rejects a mismatch.

Required top-level members are:

```json
{
  "certificate_schema_version": "ukb-field-certificate-v1",
  "certificate_id": "...",
  "snapshot_id": "...",
  "catalog_sha256": "...",
  "created_at": "RFC3339",
  "canonicalization": "RFC8785-compatible-json-with-certificate_sha256-omitted",
  "required_fields": {"31-0.0": {}, "21003-0.0": {}, "21003-1.0": {}, "53-0.0": {}, "53-1.0": {}, "30700-0.0": {}, "30700-1.0": {}, "30720-0.0": {}, "30720-1.0": {}},
  "cross_instance_claims": [],
  "evidence": [],
  "deficiencies": [],
  "certificate_sha256": "..."
}
```

Each `evidence` record must contain exactly one required field ID and these explicit objects:

* `field_id`, `table_id`, `source_id`, `source_sha256`, `schema_artifact_sha256`, and `declared_schema_sha256`;
* `semantic_label`, `instance`, and `array_meaning`;
* `value_type`, `valid_domain_or_range`, and `missing_tokens` (an exhaustive field-specific list, including an explicit empty list when documented);
* applicable concepts: `sex_coding`, `age_unit_and_meaning`, `date_format`, `calendar`, `timezone`, `exact_date_meaning`, `analyte_identity`, `matrix`, `unit`, `assay_or_platform`, and `measurement_method`;
* `evidence_class`, `directness`, `source_url`, `source_version`, `retrieval_date`, `artifact_hash`, and `claim_text`.

Non-applicable concepts must be represented explicitly as `null` with an explanation, not silently omitted. `evidence_class` is one of `DIRECT_FROZEN`, `VALIDATED_HARMONIZATION`, `ASSUMED`, `MISSING`, or `CONFLICT`; `directness` is one of `DIRECT`, `INFERRED`, or `ASSUMED`. `DIRECT_FROZEN` requires a frozen source artifact that explicitly makes the field-level claim. `VALIDATED_HARMONIZATION` requires a versioned, independently validated mapping with its own artifact hash and a documented equivalence rule. `ASSUMED` can be recorded for audit diagnostics only. `MISSING` means the claim is not established. `CONFLICT` means two applicable sources disagree. Numeric magnitude checks, operator declarations, a familiar field number, a formula citation, or a Showcase error page are never sufficient evidence.

The certificate must explicitly establish, separately for each field and instance, all of the following:

1. Field identity and semantic label, including that 21003 is the intended age field rather than substituting 21022.
2. Instance and array meaning; the protocol must call instance 1 an exported repeat unless a separate source proves a stronger first-test statement.
3. Sex coding for field 31, including valid domain and missing tokens.
4. Age unit and meaning for 21003 at instances 0 and 1.
5. Exact date representation, calendar, timezone, missing codes, and whether 53 is an assessment/collection date suitable for elapsed-time calculation.
6. Creatinine and cystatin-C analyte identities, serum/plasma matrix, units, assay/platform or measurement method, valid ranges, and field-specific missing codes for both instances.
7. Evidence-backed comparability or a precisely specified validated harmonization between baseline and repeat measurements for each renal analyte.
8. Source URL, version, retrieval date, artifact hash, and whether each assertion is direct or harmonized.

`cross_instance_claims` must include explicit claims for 21003, 53, 30700, and 30720, naming both instances and the evidence supporting comparability. A claim that only the instance number matches is insufficient. A certificate evaluator derives its state from records and evidence; it ignores a claimed top-level status and rejects unknown evidence classes, duplicate field records, missing required concepts, nonmatching snapshot/catalog/source/schema hashes, unsupported conversions, or contradictory claims.

The evaluator writes a private deficiency artifact containing stable deficiency codes, affected fields, missing concepts, conflicting artifacts, and the certificate digest. In the current workspace it must report missing or unsupported evidence for active age 21003, dates 53, creatinine 30700, and cystatin C 30720 rather than fabricating values. The partial local metadata for sex 31 and unused age 21022 may be recorded as supporting evidence only for those fields and cannot satisfy the active 21003 requirement.

## Two-lane execution and fail-closed output contract

Every run has a structural lane and exactly one downstream semantic lane. Lane S performs only read-only source hashing, complete-header/schema comparison, key integrity, and horizontal-join checks. Lane C (certified) may derive participant variables only after a certificate evaluator returns `CERTIFIED_READY`. Lane N (neutral/assumption-conditioned) may execute parser, equation, model-shape, fold, quota, bootstrap, and permutation code paths on synthetic fixtures or metadata-only structural checks; it must never read participant values into an output-bearing object. “Assumption-conditioned” means an assumption is recorded as an unmet input to the certificate, not that the assumption is applied to participant rows.

The executor must enforce an output capability token: `scientific_output=true` is constructible only from `CERTIFIED_READY`; all other states carry `scientific_output=false` and `scientific_output_code=NO_SCIENTIFIC_RESULT`. Before writing any artifact, recursively reject forbidden participant-derived keys or values, including nested aliases and arrays. A neutral artifact is valid only if its top-level `lane` is `NEUTRAL`, `participant_values_used` is false, `participant_rows_emitted` is zero, and every synthetic value is tagged `synthetic=true`. Any attempted participant read by Lane N, any missing provenance tag, or any forbidden key causes `NEUTRAL_QA_FAIL` and suppresses the artifact.

The machine-checkable permission table is: (i) `SOURCE_INTEGRITY_FAIL`, `FIELD_CERTIFICATE_MISSING`, `FIELD_CERTIFICATE_INVALID`, `FIELD_CERTIFICATE_INCOMPLETE`, `FIELD_CERTIFICATE_CONFLICT`: only status, deficiency codes, certificate/source/schema digests, and metadata-only or synthetic QA; (ii) `STRUCTURAL_READY`: the same, plus aggregate table/header/key/join diagnostics, never participant-derived clinical summaries; (iii) `NEUTRAL_QA_PASS/FAIL`: the preceding allowlist plus synthetic test outcomes; (iv) `CERTIFIED_READY`: no participant output yet, but certified analysis may start; (v) `CERTIFIED_ANALYSIS_COMPLETE/FAIL`: certified aggregate scientific outputs only, with all private row-level artifacts withheld. No state before `CERTIFIED_READY` permits cohort masks, dates, biomarker values, eGFR, outcome counts, predictions, weights, folds, or scientific verdicts.

## Monotone status machine and output policy

The status is derived, mutually exclusive, and monotone within one execution. A later stage cannot downgrade a prior integrity fact or upgrade on an assumption. Use these states in order:

1. `SOURCE_INTEGRITY_FAIL`: catalog, snapshot, source hash, schema artifact hash, declared schema fingerprint, exact header, or source-read-only check failed. No participant-derived output.
2. `STRUCTURAL_READY`: all source and schema checks pass; this state says only that required columns and join structure are available.
3. `FIELD_CERTIFICATE_MISSING`: no certificate was supplied.
4. `FIELD_CERTIFICATE_INVALID`: certificate parse, canonical digest, schema, source, snapshot, evidence-class, or required-record validation failed.
5. `FIELD_CERTIFICATE_INCOMPLETE`: certificate is well formed but one or more required semantic claims are missing or only assumed.
6. `FIELD_CERTIFICATE_CONFLICT`: applicable evidence conflicts or cross-instance comparability is unresolved.
7. `NEUTRAL_QA_PASS` or `NEUTRAL_QA_FAIL`: only the certificate-gated neutral QA suite ran; these are technical audit outcomes, never scientific outcomes. `NEUTRAL_QA_PASS` does not mean certified.
8. `CERTIFIED_READY`: all required claims are direct frozen or validated harmonization evidence, with no conflicts, and the exact integrity checks pass. Only this state can enter scientific derivation.
9. `CERTIFIED_ANALYSIS_COMPLETE` or `CERTIFIED_ANALYSIS_FAIL`: the certified scientific lane completed or encountered a technical failure.

The implementation must reject attempts to jump from any state before `CERTIFIED_READY` to scientific analysis. If certificate evidence is incomplete, neutral QA may still run, but its result remains a neutral technical label. Violated assumptions, malformed parsers, or schema problems are technical failures, not adverse biological evidence.

The neutral QA artifact has an allowlist of keys such as `status`, `certificate_id`, `certificate_sha256`, `catalog_sha256`, source/schema artifact hashes, expected-header hashes, table-level row counts, duplicate-header counts, key uniqueness/nullness counts, join cardinality checks, raw-token parser test-vector results, missing-token configuration completeness, synthetic formula-vector pass/fail, synthetic model matrix dimensions, synthetic fold/tie/quota arithmetic, baseline-only static-lineage checks, and synthetic bootstrap/permutation code-path checks. It may include aggregate technical counts needed to audit parsing and joins.

The neutral artifact must be rejected if it contains any forbidden key or value class: participant identifiers; participant-derived biomarker distributions, quantiles, or missingness summaries; biological names attached to raw values; converted creatinine or cystatin-C values; eGFR values; B/R/S cohort masks or inherited cohort anchors; elapsed-time or date distributions from participant rows; event or outcome counts; predictions; calibration, capacity, PPV, capture, event-yield, net-benefit, threshold, permutation-statistic, or bootstrap-result metrics; weights or fold assignments; or supportive, adverse, or biological-inconclusive verdicts. Synthetic test vectors must remain visibly synthetic and cannot be mixed with participant-derived output. A forbidden-key scan runs before serialization and fails closed.

## Equations and locked model specification

After certification only, if creatinine is certified in micromoles/L, use `Scr=raw_30700/88.4`; any alternative conversion requires explicit certificate support. Cystatin C must be certified in mg/L or have a certificate-supported conversion. With `F=1` female and `F=0` male:

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^a * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^Age * 1.012^F`, with `(k,a)=(0.7,-0.241)` for female and `(0.9,-0.302)` for male.

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.9961^Age * 0.932^F`.

`Z = log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)` and `Ycr = I(eGFRcr_1 < 60)`. No equation output is allowed before certification.

M0 uses baseline `[F, age, eGFRcr]`; M1 appends baseline `Z`. No repeat field, repeat age, repeat date, elapsed time, attendance, outcome, or future-derived field may enter either score. For each continuous variable, use bounds age `[40,80]` and baseline eGFRcr `[60,120]`, knots at lower, one-third, two-thirds, and upper, `d_j=max(x-k_j,0)^3/(k4-k_j)`, and basis `[u,d1-d3,d2-d3,d3]`. Center each basis on the training fold and scale by the population training-fold standard deviation; standardize F and Z with training moments, using one for a zero standard deviation. The hard dimensions are exactly M0 `1 + 4 + 4 = 9` non-intercept columns and M1 `9 + 1 = 10`; every other dimension is `FAIL_MODEL_SPEC`, never an automatic repair.

Fit float64 `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(penalty='l2', C=1.0, solver='lbfgs', fit_intercept=True, class_weight=None, max_iter=1000, tol=1e-10, random_state=0)`.

## Deterministic evaluation, capacity, and uncertainty

Use ten outcome-stratified five-fold repeats with seeds `104729, 104759, 104773, 104779, 104789, 104801, 104827, 104831, 104849, 104851`. Stable-sort by `(Ycr,eid)`, with ascending numeric `eid` ties, then round-robin within each outcome label. Reconstruct folds identically after each outcome permutation. Every prediction is held out and M0/M1 predictions are paired.

For q from `.01` through `.20`, select `ceil(q*n)` by descending paired OOF risk and ascending `eid`; report capture, event fraction, PPV, and incremental capture/event yield, emphasizing contiguous 5–15% capacity. For thresholds `.001,.0025,.005,.0075,.010,.015,.020,.030,.040,.050,.075,.100,.150,.200`, report the mathematical contrast `NB=TP/n-FP/n*p_t/(1-p_t)`, DeltaNB, test-all, and test-none. These are assumption-indexed policy contrasts, not observed clinical utility. At 5% and 10%, allocate fold quotas by floor plus largest remainder, with fold-index ties; report overlap/Jaccard/entrants/leavers and identities privately. Timing-band contributions decompose globally selected sets and sum to the pooled result; they never rerank within bands.

Use 4,000 paired participant bootstrap draws with seed `520243` and frozen OOF predictions. Report 98% intervals for DeltaNB at 2% and delta event yield at 10%, with `ddof=1` studentization and failure on zero/nonfinite standard deviation. Use the exact simultaneous eight-member family: DeltaNB at 1% and 5%; DeltaE at 5%; borderline and far contributions to global 10% selection; and DeltaNB at 2% in `[2,4)`, `[4,5)`, and `[5,8]`. Use 200 fold-aware outcome permutations with seed `520242`, permuting within baseline eGFR strata `[60,75)` and `>=75`, reconstructing folds and refitting; use randomized-plus-one tails statistic by statistic. Also permute Z within baseline-eGFRcr deciles and sex, reverse labels, and run a private elapsed-time leakage sentinel. Reproduction of a gain under predictor-null, reversed labels, or leakage control cannot support the hypothesis.

## Interpretation and falsification

Support requires certified evidence, exact integrity, complete computation, corrected 9/10 dimensions, calibration without material M1 harm, simultaneous positive evidence across contiguous 5–15%, positive practical floors on both axes, repeat stability, and no null/sentinel replication. The prespecified practical gates are DeltaNB(2%) point `>= .001` with lower 98% bound above zero, Delta event yield at 10% point `>= 5` with lower 98% bound above zero, and at least 8 of 10 favorable repeat contrasts on each axis. Support is still only incremental prioritization for the observed exported repeat.

Adverse requires certified, reproducible negative incremental evidence with an upper interval below zero and at least 8 of 10 adverse repeats, or certified material calibration harm. Failure to meet practical floors with intervals crossing zero is scientific inconclusive. Certification failure, technical failure, incomplete nulls, poor positivity, nonfinite computation, or unexplained anchor mismatch is operational/technical inconclusive and must not be relabeled adverse biology. Supportive, adverse, and scientific-inconclusive verdicts can be emitted only by a completed certified scientific lane; a pre-certification stop is `NO_SCIENTIFIC_RESULT`.

The computation cannot establish measured-GFR accuracy, persistent CKD/chronicity, kidney failure, that instance 1 is the first clinical test, outcomes among non-attenders, assay validity beyond the supplied certificate, causal testing benefit or harm, clinical threshold appropriateness, patient preference, cost-effectiveness, treatment impact, or transportability. Those require clinical adjudication, additional linked evidence, or another study. Rows, eids, predictions, folds, weights, selections, and resampling arrays remain private; only aggregate certified outputs and neutral technical diagnostics may be released.

## Verification obligations

A verifier must independently check source and schema hashes, dual hash semantics, certificate digest and required evidence, state transitions, forbidden-key scanning, exact equations (including `0.9961`), 9/10 dimensions, baseline-only lineage, deterministic fold/tie/quota rules, arithmetic identities, bootstrap/null completion, and interpretation-gate consistency. It must test that correct neutral QA cannot be mistaken for a scientific result and that an unsupported biological conclusion is rejected even when computation is otherwise correct. It may verify computable claims only; assay validity, clinical meaning, and causal or utility claims remain outside automatic verification.
