# eICU exact ventilation care-plan targets fail a site-balanced broad-deployment fitness test

## Question, clinical importance, and substantive advance

The assessed parent established that the literal eICU care-plan targets `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` are sparse in a target-independent denominator, lack multisite overlap for target choice, and do not reproducibly improve prediction of a post-classification non-peak respiratory-setting proxy under bounded-airway leave-one-hospital-out validation. One ambiguity remained: the parent's receipt validation was restricted to only three or four hospitals with local exact-target support and its pooled result could be dominated by target-rich hospital 420. Repeated landmarks within a stay also lacked cluster-resampled uncertainty.

**Strongest claim already supported before this child:** in this eICU snapshot and target-independent design, exact-target receipt is sparse, label choice cannot be transported beyond one supporting hospital, and bounded-airway receipt models do not show reproducible incremental structured-documentation information. This is evidence against unqualified multicenter fitness, not evidence about whether bedside evaluation or an SBT occurred.

**Unresolved hypothesis tested by this child:** if the exact three-state care-plan feature is fit for broad multicenter deployment, adding it to the frozen pre-landmark baseline should lower both row-weighted and equal-hospital-weighted held-out log loss across hospitals with adequate outcome observations, including hospitals that never record an exact target; improvement should persist under explicit-airway recency restrictions and after resampling stays rather than treating repeated landmarks as independent.

The substantive advance is a deployment-relevant estimand. It retains the parent's common-support analysis for comparability, but adds leave-one-hospital-out evaluation in every hospital with at least 20 outcome-observed rows and outcome variation, regardless of local target receipt; gives each hospital equal weight in a macro estimand; quantifies exact-target site concentration; and adds stay-cluster bootstrap uncertainty conditional on the fitted models. This directly tests whether a feature whose vocabulary is absent at many sites can safely be treated as a generic multicenter predictor rather than allowing hospital 420 or target-support restrictions to determine the conclusion.

## Computable analysis and falsification

For each airway definition, the executable fits the parent's regularized logistic baseline and the same model plus the three-state label. Categorical landmark and label values use one-hot encoding with unknown levels ignored. Numerical variables use median imputation with missingness indicators and standardization. Logistic regression uses `C=1.0`, `max_iter=2000`, and no class weights.

Two estimands are reported:

1. **Common support:** eligible held-out hospitals have at least 20 outcome-observed rows, outcome variation, at least 3 exact targets, and at least 10 non-targets. Training for each fold is restricted to the other common-support hospitals. This exactly reproduces the parent's receipt estimand.
2. **Broad deployment (primary child estimand):** eligible held-out hospitals need at least 20 outcome-observed rows and outcome variation, but need not contain any exact target. Each fold trains on all other outcome-observed rows in the frozen variant and tests only the held-out hospital. Hospitals lacking enough test outcomes are not claimed to be validated.

For each estimand, the analysis reports held-out-hospital and pooled row-weighted log loss, Brier score, AUROC, mean prediction, and observed-minus-predicted calibration. The primary deployment interpretation requires both pooled micro delta log loss and the unweighted mean of hospital-specific delta log loss (equal-hospital macro) so a large hospital cannot determine both estimands. Target concentration is summarized by the largest hospital's share and the Herfindahl-Hirschman index (sum of squared hospital target shares).

Repeated person-landmarks are addressed by 300 bootstrap replicates resampling `sid` stays with replacement within every held-out hospital; all landmarks from a sampled stay move together. Loss contributions are aggregated exactly from stay-level sufficient statistics. This bootstrap is conditional on already fitted leave-one-hospital-out models: it measures held-out test-set/stay-cluster uncertainty, not training-model uncertainty. Hospital remains the transport unit.

The prespecified supportive rule requires the unbounded variant and at least two of the three bounded variants to improve both micro and equal-hospital macro log loss, with the 97.5th percentile of both stay-cluster bootstrap delta distributions below zero. The adverse rule requires no bounded variant to meet support and at least two bounded variants to have at least 0.75 bootstrap probability that equal-hospital macro log loss is worse than baseline. Otherwise the result is inconclusive.

## Meaning of supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes

* **Supportive (not observed):** reproducible micro and equal-hospital improvement under the specified robustness rule would support further prospective evaluation of the exact values as structured-documentation features. It would not validate actual care or benefit.
* **Adverse (observed):** extreme target concentration, absence of the vocabulary in most evaluable deployment hospitals, and consistent bounded-variant macro degradation falsify broad multicenter predictive fitness under this population, baseline, proxy outcome, and snapshot. The exact feature should not be used unqualified for multicenter cohort inclusion, benchmarking, quality measurement, or decision support.
* **Inconclusive (not observed):** mixed signs, bootstrap intervals spanning zero without high probability of worsening, too few outcome-observed hospitals, or failure of model execution would warrant more data rather than an adverse conclusion.

The negative result is clinically useful because it prevents a sparse, site-specific documentation field from being promoted as a generic indicator of ventilator-weaning care. It does not show that a site-specific implementation is never useful, nor that labels are merely local conventions.

## Evidence limits, adjudication, and verification boundary

The experiment establishes only predictive fitness for a structured-chart proxy. It cannot establish actual invasive ventilation at every landmark, device-delivered setting changes, daily extubation evaluation, SBT performance, extubation readiness, extubation, durable liberation, clinician intent, care quality, benefit, harm, or causality. No-target, other-value, and conflict states are not clinical non-events. The small adverse prediction deltas are not estimates of clinical harm.

Stronger clinical claims require independently time-resolved airway/device data, direct SBT and extubation records, clinician chart review blinded to the care-plan target, local data-dictionary/workflow adjudication, and preferably prospective external multisite validation. A second dataset is required to establish transport beyond this eICU snapshot. The stay bootstrap does not propagate model-fitting uncertainty; a nested refit bootstrap or repeated multisite study would be required for that stronger uncertainty claim.

An automatic verifier can check the input hash; exact filters; row, stay, hospital, target, and zero-target-hospital counts; leave-one-hospital-out separation; model features; hospital-specific predictions; micro and macro deltas; concentration calculations; stay-level bootstrap grouping and seed; gate logic; and whether the adverse wording follows from computed output. It cannot adjudicate bedside care, label semantics, clinical quality, benefit, harm, or causal interpretation.
