# Episode 26: sex-stratified robustness and equity audit of the frozen low-UACR cystatin allocation experiment

## Successor and scientific change

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s frozen O/O1/O1_hold populations, recruitment and visit chronology, 2021 race-free equations, development/test/replication locks, score transforms, fitted coefficients and penalties, rank/tie rules, exact AG1/G1 rosters, endpoints, nine-year competing-death chronology, fixed-denominator parent estimands, bootstrap, parent gates, multiplicity convention, and noncausal evidence limits.

The only scientific addition is a prespecified recorded-sex robustness/equity audit of the same two already-frozen lists. It does not refit, rerank, refill, reweight, or change either list, and adds no clinical diagnosis or outcome.

The unresolved decision question is:

> If a health system uses the frozen all-offered AG1 rule rather than the UACR-free G1 rule under the same 20% testing capacity, is its observed low-UACR/subsequent inpatient-N18 enrichment present in both recorded-sex strata, or could the overall contrast be concentrated in one stratum?

This is clinically consequential because a policy that appears useful only in one sex could have different implementation and clinical-utility implications across patients. This study can falsify only the narrower claim of sex-robust recorded-code yield and can describe allocation-composition differences; it cannot establish normative fairness, benefit, or harm.

## Evidence boundary

The inspected public evidence supports the following, and no more.

- Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796, PMCID PMC10986041; inspected PMCID source id `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) studied nearly 469,000 UK Biobank participants and reported predictors of large eGFRcys–eGFRcr differences, including sex and muscle-related/metabolic factors. This supports association and internal prediction of discordance, not this capacity-constrained AG1-versus-G1 policy, low-UACR N18 yield, sex robustness, or benefit from testing.
- Lees et al. (JAMA Network Open 2022, DOI 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.38300, PMCID PMC9597396; inspected PMCID source id `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) supports prognostic relevance of cystatin-C reclassification in a low-albuminuria UK Biobank setting. Its inspected limitations include single baseline measurements, no measured GFR, volunteer-cohort selection, and limited generalizability; it does not test AG1 versus G1, a nine-year inpatient N18 endpoint, a 20% quota, or sex-stratified allocation.

Thus the strongest supported claim is that cystatin–creatinine discordance can be predicted and may carry prognostic information, including with low albuminuria. The parent tests a reproducible fixed-capacity recorded low-UACR/N18 yield contrast. This child tests whether that contrast is present in both recorded-sex strata. Neither the papers nor this experiment supplies measured GFR, repeated clinically timed eGFR/UACR, complete outpatient/primary-care capture, kidney-replacement data, downstream actions, costs, harms, or a prospective intervention. Unavailable paper text or supplementary methods are not treated as inspected.

## Hypotheses

The parent hypothesis remains exactly:

> Among the exact O1_hold population and the two frozen 466-person lists, the baseline-trained all-offered UACR-plus-grip rule AG1 yields at least one additional participant per fixed cystatin-assay list with observed visit-1 UACR <3.0 mg/mmol and a subsequent recorded inpatient N18 event before death within nine calendar years than the UACR-free grip rule G1, with a point difference at least +2/1,000 and a one-sided 95% lower limit strictly above zero.

The new secondary child hypothesis is:

> In each valid recorded-sex stratum s in {0,1} (0=female, 1=male under frozen UKB coding), the frozen AG1 list has a larger yield of J_A1,9 events per 1,000 offered members than the frozen G1 list, with a point contrast at least +2/1,000 and a multiplicity-protected one-sided 97.5% lower confidence limit strictly above zero.

This is a fixed-list policy-yield hypothesis, not a causal effect, fairness proof, CKD-truth hypothesis, measured-GFR hypothesis, or claim that anyone benefited from being tested. Failure in one stratum falsifies the joint child claim but does not by itself falsify or rescue the unchanged parent claim.

## New sex-stratified quantities

Let `S_i` be recorded sex from `population.31-0.0`; only values 0=female and 1=male are valid under frozen metadata. Let `N_s=sum_{i in O1_hold}1(S_i=s)` and `n_{p,s}=sum_{i in S_p}1(S_i=s)`. These are pre-outcome roster counts, not weights.

Primary child quantity:
`Z_s(p)=1000*sum_{i in S_p}1(S_i=s)J_A1,9(i)/n_{p,s}`,
`Delta_s=Z_s(AG1)-Z_s(G1)`.

If either `n_{p,s}=0`, `Delta_s` is undefined and that stratum is inconclusive. Its denominator is the number offered by the fixed policy in that sex; never use UACR-observed, event, survivor, or complete-case denominators.

Also report, without making a fairness claim, `Q_s(p)=1000*n_{p,s}/466` (share of fixed offers), `Qdiff_s=Q_s(AG1)-Q_s(G1)`, and `P_s(p)=1000*sum_{i in S_p}1(S_i=s)J_A1,9(i)/N_s` (recorded events captured per 1,000 O1_hold members of sex s). The robustness claim is based on `Delta_s`, not `P_s` or `Qdiff_s`. Do not pool strata, externally standardize, adjust after selection, or use sex in fitting AG/G.
