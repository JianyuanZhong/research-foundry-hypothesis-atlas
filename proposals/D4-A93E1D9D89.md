# Preregistered continuous cystatin-C rescue after the adverse binary Direction-A result

## Lineage, unresolved question, and evidentiary boundary

This is a post-result child of `[prior hypothesis]`, not a reinterpretation of it. The parent established an adverse result for the prespecified binary feature `D = I(eGFRcys_0 < 60)`: in the exact selected Direction-A cohort, adding `D` to the flexible creatinine comparator produced small, repeat-stable held-out loss reductions that failed its multiplicity gate and significantly worsened concentration of outcomes at the inherited 10% capacity. That result remains frozen regardless of this child's result.

The unresolved hypothesis is narrower and different: **among the same qualifying selected first-repeat attenders with baseline creatinine eGFR at least 60, does one prespecified continuous measure of baseline cystatin-versus-creatinine discordance add reproducible held-out information beyond the unchanged flexible creatinine comparator for repeat creatinine-derived eGFR below 60, while preserving calibration and improving outcome concentration at the same 10% capacity?** A threshold can discard ordering within both sides of 60, so failure of `D` does not logically settle this question. Conversely, a successful continuous result would not rehabilitate the failed binary rule.

The strongest existing claim is the parent's adverse predictive/10%-capacity result for `D` under its protocol. The new claim is untested. This child freezes one continuous encoding, one model extension, one information primary, and one capacity before inspecting any continuous-rescue result. Because the encoding and hierarchy were chosen after seeing the binary parent's result and use the same UKB snapshot, even a passing result will be described only as internally held-out post-parent rescue evidence requiring external replication, not pristine confirmation.

The clinical motivation is triage: if baseline cystatin C continuously reorders risk beyond a strong creatinine model, it could concentrate more future low-eGFR observations into a fixed number of follow-up positions. The outcome is nevertheless a repeat creatinine equation threshold, not measured GFR, adjudicated CKD, symptoms, kidney failure, or treatment benefit. This experiment cannot establish that ordering people this way improves health or justifies cystatin-C testing.

## Frozen equations, feature, and target

Convert creatinine from micromoles/L to mg/dL by division by 88.4. Use the parent's unrounded race-free 2021 CKD-EPI creatinine equation:

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^a * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^age * sex_factor`,

where `(k,a,sex_factor)=(0.7,-0.241,1.012)` for females and `(0.9,-0.302,1)` for males. Define the unchanged outcome at the observed first repeat as `Y = I(eGFRcr_1 < 60)` using repeat creatinine, repeat age, and baseline sex.

Use the parent's unrounded 2012 cystatin-C-only equation:

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.996^age * sex_factor`,

where the sex factor is 0.932 for females and 1 for males.

Freeze the only added cystatin-derived feature as

`Z = log(eGFRcys_0 / eGFRcr_0)`.

Lower `Z` means lower cystatin-derived relative to creatinine-derived filtration. This dimensionless relative-discordance encoding uses the full positive baseline cystatin measurement, has no chosen clinical cutpoint, and is fixed before its outcome association or predictive performance is inspected. Within each training fold, center `Z` by its training mean and divide by its training population standard deviation; apply those values to the held-out fold. Do not winsorize, categorize, spline, interact, residualize, tune, or substitute raw cystatin C, `eGFRcys_0`, an absolute difference, another ratio, or any threshold. Invalid/nonpositive inputs have already been excluded by the frozen population. A zero/nonfinite training standard deviation is computational failure.

### Primary incremental-information estimand

For participant `i` and repeat `r`, calculate Bernoulli log loss for each held-out prediction. The primary estimand is

`Delta_LL = mean_{i,r}[LL(Mc) - LL(M0)]`.

Negative values favor continuous cystatin information. Log loss is the sole inferential information score because it is a strictly proper score directly sensitive to probabilistic information, and selecting Brier alone after observing the parent's nominally favorable Brier result would be vulnerable to metric cherry-picking. Brier difference and AUC difference may be reported as descriptive points only; they cannot rescue the hierarchy and receive no threshold search.

### Sole fixed-capacity estimand

Allocate integer slots by floor plus largest fractional remainder across the five folds, yielding exactly 1,637 selected participants per repeat (9.9988%). `Delta_events_10 = mean_r[sum(Y selected by Mc) - sum(Y selected by M0)]`.

The exact selected-risk translation is `Delta_events_10 / 1637`; the exact event-capture translation is `Delta_events_10 / 309`. ### Calibration

Using each participant's mean held-out prediction across the ten repeats, estimate joint calibration intercept and slope by logistic recalibration, plus observed-minus-mean-predicted risk (risk-scale calibration-in-the-large). Define Mc-minus-M0 degradation as:

- `d_int = |intercept_Mc| - |intercept_M0|`;
- `d_slope = |slope_Mc - 1| - |slope_M0 - 1|`;

Retain the parent's operational noninferiority margins: 0.10, 0.10, and 0.002 respectively. These are computational tolerances, not clinically adjudicated safety limits.
