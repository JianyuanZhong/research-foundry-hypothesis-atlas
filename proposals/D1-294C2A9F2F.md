# Does interhospital-transfer status retain incremental association with deterioration after a fixed 24-hour receiving-ICU assessment?

## Status and scientific opening

This is a complete repair of expert seed `[starting question]` / parent `[prior hypothesis]`. It is a planned, falsifiable eICU experiment; no adjusted association has been estimated. The mature hyperoxia branch is parked because its all-hour exposure failed outcome-blind identifiability and needs external device/clinician evidence.

A single-center tertiary medical-ICU cohort reported 25% mortality among transfers versus 21% among direct admissions and found both patient- and system-level prognostic factors [K1]. A transferred-only cohort found that transfer-day organ dysfunction, very high oxygen requirement, and pre-transfer arrest identified early mortality risk [K2]. These support the strongest current claim: transferred ICU patients can be a high-risk selected group, and receiving-ICU organ state contains important prognostic information. They do **not** establish that transfer itself causes harm, or whether transfer status remains useful at a fixed receiving-ICU landmark after transparent organ-injury and trajectory adjustment.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among adult first ICU-unit admissions from another hospital who remain in the receiving ICU at hour 24 and have not yet received invasive ventilation, vasoactive infusion, or acute renal-replacement therapy there, `unitadmitsource='Other Hospital'` retains a clinically meaningful (prespecified as at least 2 percentage points) within-overlap-hospital incremental association with newly documented organ support or ICU death during hours 24–72, after adjustment for first-day organ injury. The rival is that early multivariate trajectory, hospital selection, and observation intensity account for the apparent penalty.

This is a prognostic/descriptive association, not a causal transfer effect. Resolution matters because transfer status is often treated as an intrinsic penalty in receiving-ICU triage and benchmarking. If its residual signal disappears after observable first-day course, status-based escalation or risk adjustment should yield to measured trajectory. If it persists robustly, transfer status is a useful flag for missing pre-arrival history or unmeasured selection—but still not evidence that transport caused deterioration.

## Actual scientific deliverable

The solver must newly fit:

1. a transparent hospital-adjusted static landmark model (B0), and
2. an ordered masked trajectory model (M1) on identical person-safe, held-hospital folds,

then estimate the transfer-minus-ED standardized risk difference (RD), odds ratio, uncertainty, held-out incremental prediction from adding transfer status, heterogeneity, and all prespecified gates. Completion requires machine-readable cohort flow, support/overlap audit, feature dictionary, fold assignment, model artifacts, calibration/discrimination tables, primary/sensitivity estimates, bootstrap draws, falsification report, and a claim-to-output table. A planned analysis or readiness probe is not completion.

## Frozen population, exposure, landmark, and outcome

### Population

Include rows satisfying all of:

- age at least 18, with `age='> 89'` coded as 90 only for modeling;
- `unitvisitnumber=1` (first ICU unit visit in that hospitalization);
- `unitadmitsource` exactly `Other Hospital` or `Emergency Department`;
- `unitdischargeoffset>1440` and not known dead/discharged before minute 1440;
- no receiving-ICU invasive mechanical ventilation, qualifying vasoactive infusion, or acute RRT documented in `[0,1440]`;
- hospital has at least 10 otherwise eligible `Other Hospital` and 20 `Emergency Department` stays, determined without inspecting outcomes.

All qualifying stays remain; repeated `uniquepid` and hospitalizations are not silently discarded. They are kept together in folds and clustered in inference. Report exact exclusions. The bounded pre-study audit (before final support exclusions) found 48 overlap hospitals, 2,512 transfers, and 22,913 ED controls. This is feasibility evidence only.

### Exposure and comparators

Primary exposure (A=1): `unitadmitsource='Other Hospital'`. Primary comparator (A=0): exactly `Emergency Department`. This is “outside-hospital source versus direct receiving-hospital ED presentation,” not proof that every control bypassed all prior care.

Required sensitivities:

1. replace controls with exact `Direct Admit`;
2. pool `Emergency Department` and `Direct Admit`;
3. restrict to records where `hospitaladmitsource` is nonmissing and concordant with the unit-source classification;
4. exclude `unitstaytype!='admit'`;
5. exclude `hospitaladmitoffset>0` as a clock-inconsistency check.

Do not recode `Other ICU`, `ICU`, `Floor`, `ICU to SDU`, nulls, or `Other` as interhospital transfer.

### Landmark and outcome

Landmark (L=1440) minutes after receiving-ICU admission. Covariates use offsets `[0,1440]`; outcome begins strictly after 1440, preventing same-timestamp leakage. Primary horizon ends at minute 4320 (72 hours).

Primary binary outcome: first newly documented occurrence in `(1440,4320]` of any:

- invasive mechanical ventilation: frozen training-derived normalized `treatmentstring` dictionary rooted at `pulmonary|ventilation and oxygenation|mechanical ventilation`, corroborated by `respiratoryCare.airwaytype` in `Oral ETT, Nasal ETT, Tracheostomy, Double-Lumen Tube, Cricothyrotomy` or valid `ventstartoffset`;
- vasoactive infusion: a positive-rate `infusionDrug` record whose normalized `drugname` maps to norepinephrine/Levophed, epinephrine, phenylephrine, vasopressin, or dopamine; dobutamine and milrinone are excluded;
- acute RRT: normalized `treatmentstring` rooted at `renal|dialysis|`, excluding strings containing `chronic renal failure`, corroborated by positive `dialysistotal`/RRT-labelled `intakeOutput`;
- `unitdischargestatus='Expired'` with `unitdischargeoffset` in the window.

The dictionary must be frozen using training folds only, versioned, and list every accepted raw string; no outcome-informed expansion. Primary endpoint is **newly documented support/death**, not clinically adjudicated onset. Components and time to first event are mandatory. Live ICU discharge before 72 hours is a competing event, not a non-event assumed observable to 72 hours: report the cause-specific landmark analysis plus cumulative incidence with live ICU discharge as competing risk. A sensitivity counts subsequent hospital death after live ICU discharge only when `hospitaldischargestatus='Expired'` and `hospitaldischargeoffset<=4320`.

## Variables and rival explanations

### B0: transparent static arrival-window adjustment

Use no post-landmark values. Prespecified covariates:

- demographics: age spline, gender, ethnicity with missing/other explicit;
- case mix: `unittype`, frozen broad group from `apacheadmissiondx` (grouping learned from strings in training only, with “other”), `hospitaladmitoffset`;
- hospital: bed category, teaching status, region;
- six transparent organ domains from first-day raw data, each retaining continuous value, clinically interpretable bins, and missing indicator:
  - cardiovascular: minimum MAP (periodic systemic mean or aperiodic non-invasive mean), minimum systolic BP, heart-rate max/min, first-day vasoactive/support indicator;
  - respiratory: minimum SpO2, maximum respiratory rate, PaO2 and pH nearest to hour 24, `apacheApsVar.pao2/fio2` when both valid, and first-day invasive ventilation/intubation;
  - renal: maximum creatinine and BUN, `apacheApsVar.urine`, observed urine total, first-day dialysis;
  - hepatic: maximum bilirubin and minimum albumin;
  - coagulation/inflammation: minimum platelets and WBC max/min;
  - neurologic: last and minimum valid GCS total, or summed valid components, plus `apacheApsVar.eyes/motor/verbal/meds`.
- timing/measurement: count of values by domain, fraction of 24 hourly bins observed, and time since last observation at landmark.

Physiologic range rules are frozen before outcome modeling and out-of-range values become missing plus a range-error flag. For duplicated timestamps use median (continuous) or maximum support state. Static summaries are median, min, max, first, last, and last-minus-first as appropriate. This is transparent “organ-injury adjustment,” not a claim to reconstruct SOFA or pre-transfer disease.

Fit a hospital-stratified logistic generalized linear model with restricted splines and elastic-net shrinkage selected inside training folds. Report a no-shrinkage reduced model as a coefficient-stability sensitivity. B0-minus excludes transfer; B0-plus adds transfer. The estimands are the held-out standardized RD (mean[p(A=1,X)-p(A=0,X)]), corresponding odds ratio, and paired change in log loss/Brier score when transfer is added. “Setting” A here is a standardization operation, not a causal intervention.

### M1: ordered learned alternative

M1 uses the same patients, raw pre-landmark variables, outcome, and outer folds. Convert `[0,1440]` to 24 one-hour bins. Each bin contains robust summaries of the B0 physiology/labs/support state plus per-channel observed mask, count, and time-since-last-measurement; no forward fill crosses more than six hours, and the model receives the mask and elapsed time. Static demographics/case mix/hospital attributes enter after temporal encoding.

Fit a compact monotone-time masked GRU-D (one or two layers, hidden width 32–64, dropout) or equivalent predeclared masked temporal convolution if GRU-D dependencies fail readiness. Fit M1-minus and M1-plus (transfer added only after the temporal encoder) with weighted binary cross-entropy and early stopping. Calibration uses held-out training components only. Output hourly hidden-state summaries, permutation/occlusion effects by organ domain and observation-mask channel, and transfer-stratified calibration.

This alternative can retain order, persistence, cross-organ synchrony, and informative observation gaps that B0 collapses into extrema/deltas. It tests the substantive rival that a deteriorating multivariate trajectory or denser observation—not unexplained transfer status—drives the apparent penalty. It does not reveal a biological mechanism merely because it predicts well.

### Identical split and fitting protocol

Construct five outer folds from connected components of the bipartite `uniquepid`–`hospitalid` graph, so neither a person nor hospital crosses folds. Balance folds on exposure counts without using outcomes; save `folds.parquet`. If a connected component contains over 20% of hospitals or any test fold has fewer than 20 transfer outcomes, use leave-hospital-cluster-out repeated three-fold cross-fitting and report the deviation; do not leak persons to preserve five folds. All preprocessing, imputation constants, diagnosis grouping, dictionary discovery, tuning, and calibration are training-fold only. B0 and M1 use exactly the same outer predictions.

Use inverse-frequency loss weighting only for fitting, never to redefine prevalence. Obtain 95% intervals from 1,000 cluster bootstraps resampling hospitals and then persons within hospitals; rerun the complete standardization using saved out-of-fold predictions. Also fit a hierarchical hospital random-intercept/random-transfer-slope analysis for heterogeneity. Report IQR/range of hospital-specific contrasts only where support permits; do not rank named hospitals.

## Gates and falsification criteria

These are frozen before fitting the outcome models.

1. **Support gate:** after final exclusions, at least 40 hospitals, 2,000 transfers, 15,000 ED controls, 75 transfer events, and 750 ED events; each endpoint component must have at least 20 transfer events or be reported descriptively only.
2. **Exposure gate:** `unitadmitsource` is nonmissing by construction; at least 70% agreement with nonmissing `hospitaladmitsource` within each exposure arm. Failure narrows the claim to a unit-source label analysis.
3. **Outcome-source gate:** training-derived dual-source audit must show at least 80% positive agreement for ventilation and RRT where both contributing tables are available. Vasopressor requires a positive-rate infusion record. Report single-source sensitivities. Failure downgrades the endpoint to “documentation signal” and prevents a clinical-deterioration claim.
4. **Overlap gate:** within every retained hospital both arms remain after exclusions; cross-fitted propensity from B0 covariates must place at least 90% of transfers within control common support, overlap-weight effective transfer sample size at least 60% of nominal, and 99th/median weight ratio below 20. Trim only in a declared sensitivity.
5. **Observation gate:** each arm must have at least 90% coverage in vital, lab, and nursing domains, already suggested by the audit. M1 must report performance within observation-density quartiles. If the transfer RD changes sign or by over 50% between the middle two quartiles, observation artifact remains unresolved.
6. **Hospital influence gate:** deleting any one hospital must not shift the primary M1 RD by more than 1 percentage point or change its sign; no hospital contributes over 20% of absolute standardized contrast weight.
7. **Model gate:** on held-out hospitals, calibration slope 0.8–1.2 and intercept within ±0.05 for both arms, with Brier skill no worse than an intercept-plus-hospital-characteristic model. M1 must beat B0-minus in paired log loss with a cluster-bootstrap 95% interval excluding zero; otherwise ordered complexity is deferred and B0 remains the adequate model.
8. **Temporal leakage gate:** no source offset over 1440, revised lab availability over 1440, discharge field other than the outcome, or APACHE predicted/result field may enter features. An automated maximum-offset and column-denylist test must pass.
9. **Robustness gate:** conclusions must agree in direction under exact Direct Admit control, pooled control, concordant hospital source, `unitstaytype='admit'`, hard death/dual-source support endpoint, and competing-risk analysis. A reversal is adverse to transportable “transfer penalty” language.
10. **Negative-control gate:** transfer status must not improve prediction of a deliberately permuted outcome within hospital/fold over 200 permutations; empirical p below 0.01 indicates leakage or pipeline error and invalidates results.

No gate may be relaxed after inspecting the transfer estimate.

## Interpretation rules

- **Supportive:** all gates pass; B0 adjusted RD and M1 adjusted RD are both at least +2 percentage points, both lower 95% bounds exceed 0, M1-plus improves held-out prediction over M1-minus, and required sensitivities retain direction. This supports transfer status as incremental prognostic information after observable receiving-ICU state. It does not show transfer caused harm.
- **Adverse—static explanation:** B0 RD is below 1 point with upper 95% bound below +2, regardless of crude difference. Transparent first-day organ injury/hospital mix adequately accounts for a clinically meaningful residual penalty.
- **Adverse—trajectory/observation rival:** B0 is positive, but M1 RD is below 1 point with upper bound below +2, transfer adds no held-out predictive value, and M1 passes its model gate. Ordered trajectory/observation information accounts for the apparent incremental signal.
- **Adverse—heterogeneous/nontransportable:** pooled association is driven by one hospital, reverses across specified source/outcome sensitivities, or has marked random-slope heterogeneity. Report heterogeneity; do not claim a general transfer penalty.
- **Inconclusive:** intervals include both 0 and +2 points; support/overlap/measurement/model gates fail; B0 and M1 disagree while M1 does not clearly outperform; or event support is inadequate. An imprecise null does not falsify a clinically meaningful association.

A crude positive result alone is not supportive. A negative result is a complete, clinically useful outcome if gates and uncertainty are reported.

## Clinical-adjudication and causal boundary

Automatically checkable claims are cohort construction, source columns/offsets, exposure counts, overlap, dictionary membership, event counts, folds, leakage, fitted predictions, calibration, RDs/ORs/intervals, sensitivity direction, and whether interpretation follows the rules.

Not automatically establishable are true onset of ventilation/vasopressor/RRT, transfer indication, physiologic state at the referring hospital, transport duration/adverse events, treatment limitation, appropriateness of escalation, preventability, or causality. An intensivist review of a stratified, transfer-blinded sample of at least 100 source timelines per endpoint is required before calling the computed endpoint clinical deterioration rather than newly documented support. External referring-hospital records and transport logs, plus a prospective or quasi-experimental design, are required to infer harm from transfer or delay. Hospital identifiers must remain private; no provider ranking.

## Alternatives considered and revisit triggers

- **Unadjusted/matched mortality comparison:** rejected as scientifically inadequate because it conflates case severity, landmark survival, hospital selection, and observation.
- **Propensity matching alone:** retained only as a sensitivity; matching can discard transfers and cannot encode evolving first-day physiology.
- **B0 only:** retained as the transparent baseline, but insufficient if order/gaps contain substantive severity information.
- **M1 masked ordered model:** selected because it directly tests the leading rival on the same question and splits.
- **Transformer or large latent state-space model:** deferred. The 24-step sequence is short; revisit only if M1 underfits while support/calibration pass and residual patterns show longer-range interactions.
- **Causal transfer-effect study:** deferred until referring-hospital records, transfer timing/indication, transport events, and treatment-limitation data exist.
- **Hyperoxia branch:** parked pending external device and clinician validation after adverse all-hour exposure identifiability.

The smallest decision-changing check is the frozen support/overlap/outcome-source audit before model fitting. Failure stops association modeling or narrows the endpoint claim; passage triggers B0 and then M1. M1 is continued only if its held-out model gate passes.
