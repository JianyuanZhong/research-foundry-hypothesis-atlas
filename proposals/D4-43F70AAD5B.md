## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens nominate single circulating proteins (GDF15, CHI3L1, FABP1, IL-6/CRP family) as early-detection biomarkers of many diseases. This branch tests whether a robust cross-disease protein signal marks a **disease-specific process (A)** or a **shared systemic correlate (B)**. Episodes 15/17 eliminated chronological age/sex and disease co-occurrence via case-case specificity. The strongest uncovered rival is **treatment/intervention (operative) state**: many circulating proteins change with surgery/operative management, drugs and care intensity, so an apparently disease-preferential protein could be a *treatment/procedure correlate* rather than disease biology. This is consequential: it decides whether a nominated protein is a disease-biology target or only a marker of being under active operative treatment.

## Evidence base (closest inspected work)
- **[K1]** Lewandowska et al. 2026 (abstract_only) — circulating inflammatory proteins are frequently severity/pharmacodynamic markers and their dynamics can dissociate from clinical improvement; their roles "should not be interpreted interchangeably". This bounds the branch: an apparent disease-specific signal may be a treatment/severity correlate. No cross-disease estimand.
- **[K2]** Guan et al. 2026 (abstract_only) — ProAL50, a 50-protein UKB score, indexes a trans-disease allostatic-load state predicting cardiometabolic/malignant/pulmonary/hepatic/renal disease and mortality. Direct support for a shared axis (claim B); does not test per-protein disease-preferential residual.
- **[K3]** Luo et al. 2026 (abstract_only) — cross-system "protein aging modules" (IL-6, CRP, CXCL9/10, GDF15, IGF-1, VCAM-1, NT-proBNP, NfL, GFAP) track multisystem decline. Supports shared modules but not per-protein disease-specificity, and does not control treatment state.

**Advance over [K1-K3]:** rather than asserting either shared (B) or disease-specific (A) biology, this study makes the distinction **falsifiable per protein** with a held-out cross-disease transfer-specificity gap that must survive co-occurrence and operative-treatment contrasts, plus a permutation null. [K2]/[K3] establish a shared axis but never isolate per-protein disease-preferential residuals in a diseased-only design; [K1] warns markers mix severity/treatment roles but gives no quantitative separation.

## Design
- **Population:** UKB participants in the Olink subset present in the dta table (n = 53,014). Analysis unit: per-protein (2,923) × disease-block. Inclusion rule: a protein/disease cell is analysed only if ≥200 cases and ≥50 provide unrelated-procedure exposure (pre-specified case-count/power rule).
- **Temporal boundary:** prevalent/current ICD-10 and OPCS state (single frozen snapshot); no incident/washout contrast is claimed.
- **Variables:** exposure = per-protein NPX (standardised); covariates = `n_31_0_0` (sex), `n_21003_0_0` (age), age-decile, comorbidity count/multimorbidity strata; disease labels from `s_41270_0_*` mapped to ICD-10 chapter/blocks; operative-treatment exposure from OPCS `s_41200_0_*`/`s_41210_0_*`.
- **Primary estimand (collider-safe):** the per-protein cross-disease **transfer-specificity gap** Δ_D = (discrimination of protein for disease D) − (transfer to unrelated disease D′), computed **within diseased-only case-case comparisons**, and stratified/adjusted on **UNRELATED** OPCS procedures only (pre-specified OPCS-block × disease-block exclusions to avoid adjusting for procedures plausibly caused by D). Plain OPCS-adjusted Δ_D is demoted to a **non-interpretable-as-null diagnostic**.
- **Baseline vs alternative (same estimand):** baseline = 1-D shared systemic axis (leave-one-protein-out / elastic-net shared-loading score). Substantive alternative = **elastic-net / jointly-estimated latent shared-axis model** on the held-out half, which can reveal disease-preferential residuals that a single shared axis loses (information the 1-D baseline discards) and can separate per-protein disease-specific signal from a generic shared/treatment correlate.
- **Negative controls:** no-ICD participants; V01–Y98 external-cause codes; procedure-only contrast; permutation null.
- **Split:** disease-orthogonal split / held-out half with a permutation null × (age-decile × sex × comorbidity) strata.
- **Analysis & falsification:** 1,000-permutation band within strata; **supportive** = Δ_D^treat band lower bound > 0; **adverse** = raw/case-case contrast positive but collapses under the unrelated-procedure contrast; **inconclusive** = band straddles 0 or cells under the case-count rule.
- **Required solver outputs:** `results.json` (per-protein Δ_D and bands), `split_manifest.json`, `code_list.json` (ICD-10 × OPCS blocks with adjudication flags).
