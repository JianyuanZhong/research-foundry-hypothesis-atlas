## Question and bounded advance

This successor retains the Lead-frozen design from `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`, rather than changing its cohort, estimand, endpoint, or causal boundary. The unresolved, falsifiable question is whether the exact care-plan label adds reproducible information about a later independently constructed respiratory-chart trajectory after adjustment for pre-decision respiratory state and documentation opportunity, and whether that increment transports to hospitals not used for fitting. The principal advance is an executable source audit that separates the valid primary experiment from an optional first-day Apache residual-state sensitivity.
