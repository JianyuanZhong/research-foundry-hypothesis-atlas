# Episode 18 successor: does the late acidemic SpO2 phenotype remain materially prognostic after pre-index APACHE severity adjustment?

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and substantive advance

Among invasively ventilated adults who have an eligible acidemic SpO2/PaO2 pair strictly after the first 24 ICU hours and by 72 hours, does a released monitor SpO2 of 94–95%, rather than 96–97%, identify an absolute hospital-mortality risk at least 10 percentage points higher after standardization for first-day APACHE-IVa predicted hospital mortality, recorded hospital, charted FiO2, PaCO2, pH, and index time?

This is a **noncausal prognostic comparison of recorded phenotypes**. It is not an effect of assigning an oxygen target, changing FiO2, drawing an ABG, treating acidemia, or prolonging either saturation band. The revised question matters because the Episode-17 parent left its strongest validity threat unresolved: the lower band could simply mark globally sicker patients. If the association survives a severity measure built from demographics, diagnosis, and physiology in the first ICU day, it would provide a stronger rationale for prospective validation of post-day-1 risk recognition. If it does not, the clinically important conclusion is that the striking concurrent SpO2/PaO2 mapping should not be promoted into a mortality-risk rationale.

The child makes a scientific, not mechanical, change. It replaces the parent's 0–72-hour earliest-stay measured-overlap target with a **strict post-24-hour landmark target**, requires a valid APACHE-IVa prediction and complete PaCO2, retains only hospitals with both SpO2 bands, and replaces sparse exact cells with outcome-blind logistic overlap weighting. These changes ensure the APACHE first-day information precedes the index and avoid extrapolating across hospitals that recorded only one band.

## Evidence-supported claim versus hypothesis tested

**Strongest claim supported before this experiment:** the parent’s exact extraction is reproducible. It yields 556 eligible observations, 440 earliest-stay indices with complete future hospital outcome, and 156 exact-cell overlap stays. Within the earlier measurement study, lower versus higher SpO2 mapped differently to concurrent PaO2 categories. These are measurement and feasibility facts, not adjusted outcome evidence. The parent’s weighted mortality point diagnostic was −2.97 points and had no bootstrap interval; it supports no inferential mortality conclusion.

**Unresolved hypothesis tested here:**

[
Delta_{APACHE}
= P_w(D=1mid L=1)-P_w(D=1mid L=0) ge 0.10,
]

where (L=1) is recorded SpO2 94–95%, (L=0) is 96–97%, (D) is hospital death after the index, and (w) defines the overlap population in post-day-1, APACHE-covered hospitals with empirical within-site band overlap. The +10-point absolute margin is a predeclared material prognostic threshold, not a validated MCID or treatment-effect margin.

The full-source audit found an outcome-blind primary cohort of 126 people in nine hospitals, with 45 lower-band stays (13 deaths, 32 survivors) and 81 higher-band stays (37 deaths, 44 survivors). The primary overlap fit had ESS 43.49 and 75.34, exact numerical balance, no propensity outside 0.05–0.95, and maximum site weight 39.19%. Its weighted point diagnostic was 29.96% versus 46.72%, (Delta_{APACHE}=-16.75) points. No bootstrap interval or inferential classification was computed. This adverse-looking diagnostic is disclosed and cannot change the hypothesis, margin, or acceptance rules.

## Primary standardization and estimand

Fit the exposure model without outcome or PaO2:

[
operatorname{logit}P(L=1mid X)=alpha+gamma_h+
f_1(operatorname{logit}(r_{APACHE}))+
f_2(FiO2)+f_3(PaCO2)+f_4(pH)+f_5(draw),
]

implemented as an unpenalized logistic regression with intercept, (H-1) hospital indicators, and continuous terms standardized by the primary-cohort mean and population SD. Use (operatorname{logit}(mathrm{clip}(r_{APACHE},0.005,0.995))). The primary terms are linear; nonlinearity is tested in a fixed sensitivity. Do not select variables or tune the model with death.

For fitted propensity (e_i), assign (w_i=1-e_i) to lower-band stays and (w_i=e_i) to higher-band stays, normalize weights separately by band, and estimate the lower-minus-higher weighted hospital-death risk difference. Logistic maximum-likelihood score equations provide exact mean balance for included terms when the fit is finite; this is a measured-overlap prognostic target, not a causal propensity-score estimand.

Use 5,000 deterministic hierarchical, exposure-stratified bootstrap replicates with seed 20260918:

1. sample the nine original hospitals with replacement, creating nine distinct hospital-copy IDs;
2. within each copied hospital and each SpO2 band, sample `uniquepid` clusters with replacement, preserving that original hospital-band’s cluster count and carrying all indexed stays;
3. assign new person-copy IDs, re-standardize continuous variables, refit the exposure model using hospital-copy indicators, recompute overlap weights, and estimate the risk difference.

All 126 audited people are unique, but clustering remains required if reconstruction differs. Report percentile two-sided 95% intervals, defined/failed replicates, raw and weighted risks, RD, RR when both risks are nonzero, propensity ranges, weight sums, ESS, maximum normalized row weight, site weight fractions, and weighted standardized mean differences (SMDs).

## Gates, baselines, sensitivities, and falsification

Support requires all of:

- <=10% failed bootstrap replicates and finite exposure-model convergence;
- at least 10 deaths and 30 survivors in the lower band and 30 deaths and 40 survivors in the higher band;
- ESS >=40 lower and >=60 higher;
- all nine audited hospitals retained, no propensity <0.05 or >0.95, and no hospital >40% of total overlap weight;
- maximum absolute weighted SMD <=0.05 across APACHE logit, FiO2, PaCO2, pH, draw, and hospital indicators;
- no required sensitivity or deletion of a hospital with >=10 primary rows changes the point estimate to <=0.

A gate failure cannot create support. Fixed analyses are:

- raw mortality RD in the 164 post-24-hour indices before APACHE exclusion, and in the 158 APACHE-covered indices before comparable-hospital restriction;
- replace APACHE predicted risk with standardized `apachescore`, then with `acutephysiologyscore` plus age, each as a complete-case sensitivity with attrition reported;
- nonlinear continuous terms using fixed three-knot restricted cubic splines at 10th/50th/90th cohort percentiles;
- ridge logistic overlap fits in all 158 APACHE-covered stays with fixed C={0.1,1,10}, hospital indicators included, explicitly a broader model-supported transport target;
- exclude stays having any eligible pair at 0–1440 minutes;
- 24-hour time bins replaced by 12-hour bins through adjustment terms; five-point FiO2 and ten-mm Hg PaCO2 coarsened exact strata as diagnostics;
- earliest qualifying index per `uniquepid`;
- exact `unitdischargestatus` with future `unitdischargeoffset`, a different, less patient-centered endpoint;
- leave each original hospital out and refit; highlight the four hospitals with >=10 rows;
- report hospital-specific raw RDs only where both bands have >=5 rows and at least three deaths total; do not infer homogeneity from sparse sites;
- descriptive PaO2 categories <70, 70–<90, >=90 by band and death, without category-specific tests.

For context only, reproduce the parent’s 440 all-index and 156 exact-cell results. They are different populations/estimands and cannot be pooled with this child.

**Supportive:** if the 95% interval lower bound is >=+0.10 and every gate passes, conclude only that the lower late acidemic recorded phenotype is associated with at least 10 points greater observed hospital-mortality risk after measured first-day severity, site, and index-physiology standardization in the nine-hospital overlap target. The next step is independent prospective synchronized validation of risk recognition.

**Adverse:** if the interval upper bound is <+0.10, the material prognostic hypothesis is falsified. A positive estimate below +10 may indicate a smaller association; an interval crossing zero leaves direction unresolved. If the entire interval is below zero, report an opposite observed association—not protection from lower oxygenation, not benefit of a lower target, and not evidence that APACHE adjustment caused the reversal.

**Inconclusive:** if the interval includes +0.10, or a support gate fails without a robust adverse result, EICU cannot resolve the material claim. Sensitivity conflict precludes support. Negative and inconclusive outcomes are valid scientific results.

## Unavailable evidence, adjudication, and verifier limits

APACHE fields have no result timestamp; the literature supports first-day inputs, but the table cannot prove prospective bedside availability. APACHE coverage is selected, its risk may be miscalibrated, and restricting to survivors/observed patients after 24 hours creates landmark selection. ABG ordering remains a clinician-selected mechanism. One-band hospitals are outside the primary target. No adjustment eliminates residual confounding or proves transportability to current practice.
