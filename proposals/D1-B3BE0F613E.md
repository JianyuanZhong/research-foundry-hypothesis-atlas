# Coverage-gated lactate-by-perfusion heterogeneity at the first observed live ICU exit

## Status and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive MIMIC-IV successor to the selected parent [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the expert seed's lactate-by-perfusion question and the parent's strict coverage idea, but makes the index, first-transition endpoint, untimed-death handling, selection audit, and clinical robustness estimand deterministic.

The future Harbor solver must newly produce either (a) a gate-passing, leakage-controlled competing-risk study of whether the prognostic association of persistent hyperlactatemia differs between improving and worsening measured perfusion immediately before a live ICU exit, or (b) a complete prespecified gate-failure audit. No fitted result or clinical conclusion is claimed here.

Completion requires:

1. an all-candidate flow with one deterministic first eligible live exit per admission, exclusion reasons, and a row-level coverage manifest;
2. strict dual clinical-time/recording-time features over the 12 hours ending one hour before exit;
3. mutually exclusive first-transition labels through 48 hours: ICU readmission (R), timed in-hospital death (D), alive hospital discharge (S), event-free ascertainment (N), and unresolved ascertainment (U);
4. the primary persistent-lactate-by-perfusion interaction in 48-hour R/D cumulative incidence with S competing, plus the prespecified R-specific and D-specific alternative estimands;
5. matched held-out transparent B0/B1/Bmask/Bdisc analyses and a training-only learned M2 trajectory sensitivity, all on the same population, split, outcomes and uncertainty procedure;
6. outcome-independent coverage diagnostics, a separate fixed endpoint-count support gate, an outcome-free exit-selection overlap audit, timing/coverage/label/process falsifications, and a conclusion ledger.

A coverage or event-support failure makes the strict interaction inconclusive; it does not authorize selecting another exit, loosening timing, coding absent pressors as zero, merging phenotype cells, or choosing a favorable subset.

## Unresolved question, evidence boundary and clinical importance

The strongest available evidence is limited to: the configured snapshot contains MIMIC-IV 3.1 ICU stays, admission endpoints, repeated lactate and organ laboratory values, MAP observations, vasoactive intervals, urine output, and observation timestamps; the source guide documents subject-specific shifted time with preserved within-subject intervals; and the verified dictionary rows map the proposed item IDs to the stated tables and units. The expert seed is an untested hypothesis. Neither proxy validity, phenotype prevalence, event support, nor the association has been established.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among adults at their first eligible live ICU exit, with repeated lactate and adequately ascertained MAP/vasoactive measurements, is the excess 48-hour risk of ICU readmission or timed in-hospital death associated with persistent hyperlactatemia smaller when measured perfusion is improving than when it is worsening?

The primary estimand is the standardized difference-in-differences of 48-hour cumulative incidence, with the first transition among R, D and S competing:
theta_RD = [CIF_RD(P,I) - CIF_RD(NL,I)] - [CIF_RD(P,W) - CIF_RD(NL,W)],
where P means persistent lactate, NL means nonpersistent lactate, I improving perfusion, and W worsening perfusion. S is included as a competing transition, not silently coded as no event. The directional hypothesis is theta_RD < 0; the prespecified clinically meaningful null region is [-0.02,+0.02] absolute risk difference. A negative point estimate inside that region is not support for a clinically meaningful interaction.

The substantive robustness estimand is two cause-specific clinical contrasts using the same four cells and competing S:
theta_R = [CIF_R(P,I)-CIF_R(NL,I)]-[CIF_R(P,W)-CIF_R(NL,W)]
and
theta_D = [CIF_D(P,I)-CIF_D(NL,I)]-[CIF_D(P,W)-CIF_D(NL,W)].
These distinguish an excess ICU-return transition from an excess timed-death transition. They are not causal treatment effects and do not establish that an ICU return was preventable. A pattern confined to D has different clinical implications from a pattern confined to R; component claims are made only when their component event-support rule passes. Report both 24-hour and 48-hour CIFs as fixed horizon summaries; 48 hours is primary.

This advances the parent by separating a clinically meaningful transition question from a composite association that could hide whether the signal is return-to-ICU or impending death, while making the administrative discharge transition and unknown death timing explicit. A robust interaction could justify prospective evaluation of context-aware risk communication. A null, reversed, process-only, or unstable result would argue against adding this retrospective distinction to risk stratification. Neither result diagnoses microcirculatory dysfunction, identifies lactate etiology, establishes fluid responsiveness, estimates treatment benefit, evaluates discharge appropriateness, or supports a bedside policy.

## Population and exact time-zero rule

Use only the MIMIC dataset for this experiment; HCC, eICU and UKB remain directly available in their read-only source areas and are not mixed into this MIMIC population.

Join icu/icustays to hosp/admissions on (subject_id, hadm_id). An adult candidate must have anchor_age >= 18 in hosp/patients, joined by subject_id. MIMIC's value 91 is retained as its documented >89 representation. An absent admission or patient join is a linkage/eligibility failure recorded in the flow, never silently discarded.

For each hadm_id, sort ICU rows by (outtime, intime, stay_id) and select the first row satisfying all of the following:

- finite intime and outtime, with intime < outtime;
- joined adult patient and admission;
- a known live ICU exit at t0 = outtime: deathtime is null with hospital_expire_flag = 0, or deathtime > t0;
- t0 - intime >= 13 hours, so that tL = t0 - 1 hour has a full 12-hour feature window.

A row with hospital_expire_flag=1 and missing deathtime is not called a known live exit: it is reported as an index-death-timing-unknown exclusion. A row with missing hospital_expire_flag and missing deathtime is a separate index-life-unknown exclusion; missingness is not treated as flag 0. A known deathtime <= t0, reversed interval, missing time, or insufficient 13-hour history is an exclusion with its own reason. A later ICU exit is never substituted after coverage or endpoint outcomes are seen. The first row is chosen before any coverage flag, phenotype, endpoint, model, or gate is computed. This is the selection-overlap audit's index population.

The feature landmark is exactly tL=t0-1 hour; the primary window is [t0-13h,t0-1h] = [tL-12h,tL], with early [tL-12h,tL-6h] and late (tL-6h,tL]. Closed/open boundaries are applied exactly as written. Index outtime is used only to define time zero and the window boundary, never as a feature. Do not use los, last_careunit, future transfers, discharge fields, notes, dod, or any post-tL measurement as a feature.

## Exact first-transition endpoint and ascertainment

For each selected index, define the horizon H = t0 + 48 hours. Construct known timed candidates only in the open-left, closed-right interval (t0,H]:

- R: the minimum intime of a different stay_id in the same (subject_id,hadm_id) with t0 < intime <= H;
- D: deathtime when nonmissing and t0 < deathtime <= H;
- S: dischtime when nonmissing, hospital_expire_flag = 0, and t0 < dischtime <= H.

R is not created from the index stay, an ICU row with intime <= t0, or a new admission with a different hadm_id. The exact known-event tie order is D before R before S. Thus, at the same timestamp, timed death is D; otherwise ICU re-entry is R; otherwise alive hospital discharge is S. If a timed death is known after an earlier R or S, the earlier transition remains the label. Record all tied candidates and the tie decision in the endpoint manifest.

N is not a fourth clinical event: it means no R, D or S candidate through H and known ascertainment of being event-free for the horizon. If deathtime > H, that timed death establishes no D by H; if dischtime > H with hospital_expire_flag = 0, that establishes live observation beyond H. If deathtime and dischtime are both absent and there is no timed death, the absence of follow-up is not N.

U is a first-transition-unknown label, not N. It is assigned when hospital_expire_flag=1 and deathtime is missing, because the death time and its ordering relative to R/S cannot be recovered. It is also assigned when no timed event is present by H but the available admission fields cannot establish follow-up through H (for example, missing dischtime and no timed deathtime > H). A missing hospital_expire_flag is recorded as an endpoint-quality warning; it is not evidence of survival. In the primary label, flag-missing/no-deathtime cases are U whenever the missing flag prevents establishing S or N; a fixed sensitivity treats every such case as U. If U could precede a known R/S, retain U rather than claiming a known first transition.

Primary CIF and model analyses use known R/D/S/N labels; U remains in the flow and U-rate gate and is never imputed into N. Prespecified U bounds are: favorable-for-null, assign every U to N at H; adverse, assign every U to D at t0 + 1 second; report both without selecting between them. A known D/R/S label is not overwritten by a later event after H. Report counts and timing for R, D, S, N and U, including known timed-death versus flag disagreement.

## Feature construction, dual availability and measurement gate

All primary feature rows must satisfy both clinical and recording availability: nonmissing clinical time <= tL and nonmissing recording time <= tL. A row with a valid charttime but late storetime is excluded from primary features and counted as delayed storage. The chart-time-only matrix (charttime <= tL, with storetime ignored only for this fixed sensitivity) is an explicitly non-operational temporal sensitivity, not primary evidence. Both matrices report pre/post timing counts, delayed-storage counts, unit rejection, invalid-value rejection, distinct clinical timestamps, and median chart-to-store lag.

The endpoint-independent coverage gate G_cov is frozen before any R/D/S/N/U label, event count, model fit, estimate, calibration, or held-out prediction is inspected. It has one row per selected eligible exit with flags, reason codes, and the full counts below. It requires in each early and late half:

1. at least one valid lactate row;
2. at least two valid MAP observations at two distinct clinical timestamps; and
3. at least one positive-duration unioned target-pressor interval.

G_cov=1 only if all six half-level requirements pass. Failure does not remove the row from the all-eligible flow. It remains eligible for descriptive, MAP-only and coverage sensitivities but cannot enter the strict four-cell interaction.

Lactate is hosp/labevents, item 50813, with numeric valuenum, canonical valueuom=mmol/L after case/whitespace normalization, valid charttime and storetime, and both times <= tL. Count distinct labevent_id rows, distinct clinical timestamps, accepted/rejected units and invalid values. Early/late lactate is the median of accepted values in the half; persistent means both medians >=2.0 mmol/L, and 4.0 mmol/L is a fixed sensitivity. Nonpersistent means every other case with both medians defined; no outcome-driven threshold or cell pooling is allowed.

MAP is icu/chartevents, items 220052, 220181 and 225312, numeric valuenum, canonical valueuom=mmHg, valid charttime/storetime, both <= tL. Within each stay_id,charttime, collapse duplicate selected items by the median; then use distinct clinical timestamps for counts and the half median. Record a bit vector of item presence, item-level counts, invalid values/units, and delayed rows.

Target vasoactive intervals are icu/inputevents, item IDs 221906, 222315, 221289, 229617, 221749, 229630, 229631, 229632, 221662, 221653 and 221986. Keep only finite starttime < endtime, nonmissing storetime <= tL, and intervals overlapping a half; clip to the half and union overlaps after collapsing exact repeats on (stay_id,itemid,starttime,endtime,orderid,linkorderid). The target interval count requires positive union duration. Record item-presence bits and rejected/invalid/delayed counts. Do not pool rate, amount, concentration or units, and never interpret missing target-agent rows as zero pressor. input_any_valid_n is a separate all-input process-intensity count and is never evidence of absent pressor.

Secondary corroboration uses icu/outputevents, item 226559 (Foley), numeric value, canonical valueuom=mL, valid charttime/storetime <=tL; record presence, volume and timing, but Foley cannot repair a failed primary MAP/pressor requirement. Organ-audit fields are hosp/labevents items 50861 ALT, 50878 AST, 50885 total bilirubin, 50912 creatinine, 50882 bicarbonate, 51222 hemoglobin, 51265 platelets and 51300 WBC, using labevent_id,subject_id,hadm_id,itemid,charttime,storetime,value,valuenum,valueuom and the same dual-time/unit/value rules. organ_test_n is a process/test indicator separate from values.

For every domain and half, retain before/after timing and value filters, distinct-time counts, median lag, availability fractions, rejected-unit counts, invalid-value counts, and duplicate/overlap diagnostics. Join ICU measurements by stay_id; join labs and admissions by (subject_id,hadm_id); join demographics by subject_id. The feature whitelist contains no endpoint field.

## Perfusion phenotype

Use MAP half medians and unioned target-pressor minutes. Improving is MAP late minus early >=5 mmHg and late target-pressor minutes <= early minutes. Worsening is MAP late minus early <=-5 mmHg and late target-pressor minutes >= early minutes. All other cases are indeterminate, retained in the flow and descriptive sensitivity, and never forced into I or W. The primary four cells are P/NL × I/W. Cell membership is frozen before endpoint labels and models.

## Separate event-support gate and selection-overlap audit

The endpoint-count support gate G_sup is distinct from G_cov. It uses frozen R/D/S/N/U labels after endpoint construction but before any fit, effect estimate, calibration result, or held-out prediction is inspected. It is endpoint-count-based but endpoint-result-independent: no threshold depends on association direction, size, or model performance.

For each primary P/NL × I/W cell require:

- n >= 40;
- at least 10 known R/D events in the complete gated cohort;
- at least 8 subjects and at least 3 known R/D events in the deterministic held-out test cell;
- U <=10% overall and <=20% within the cell.

The split is subject-level 70/15/15, stratified only by frozen primary cell, frozen endpoint label and anchor-year group; no subject crosses partitions. If integer allocation prevents test support, G_sup fails. Fewer than five R or five D events in a cell disallows a component-specific conclusion. No cell is dropped, merged with indeterminate, or redefined after labels. If G_cov or G_sup fails, report descriptive flows and fixed sensitivities but call the strict primary interaction inconclusive.

To audit selection overlap without using endpoints, fit an outcome-free q on subject-held-out/cross-fitted ICU-hour records. A positive record is a selected eligible index exit at its t0; controls are one-hour records h from any valid ICU stay satisfying intime+13h <= h < outtime and h not equal to a selected index exit. q uses only information available by h under the same dual clinical/store-time rule: first/current care-unit from icu/icustays and hosp/transfers, baseline demographics/admission context, 12-hour measurement counts/masks, MAP/lactate/pressor summaries and input/process intensity. It never uses R/D/S/U, discharge fields, outtime, future data or endpoint labels as features. Estimate q with subject-blocked cross-fitting; apply it only as an overlap stress test, not as causal inverse-selection correction.

In each primary cell, report the fraction of exits with q in [0.05,0.95], q decile support among controls (>=20 controls in every occupied exit-q decile), and stabilized inverse-odds ESS (sum w)^2/sum(w^2) >=50. Report unweighted estimates and the fixed q-weighted descriptive audit. q failure does not repair the cohort; the unweighted analysis remains descriptive and the selection-robust interpretation is inconclusive.

## Matched baselines, learned alternative and actual deliverable

All comparisons use the same strict gated population, landmark, labels, subject split, held-out test set, and subject bootstrap. Newly fitted outputs, rather than readiness or model complexity, establish completion.

- B0 is the transparent latest-state/context baseline: penalized discrete-time cause-specific complementary-log-log hazards for R, D and S, using prespecified admission/demographic context (gender, anchor_age, anchor_year_group, admission_type, admission_location, insurance, first_careunit), last-half physiology, last-half missingness, and process variables. It excludes discharge_location, dischtime, deathtime, hospital_expire_flag, last_careunit, outtime, los, dod and all future rows.
- B1 adds order-blind early/late and 12 one-hour-bin summaries of the same physiology, organ labs, Foley, pressor and observation-process fields. It tests generic history/measurement intensity without trajectory order.
- Bmask uses context, missingness, counts, testing, Foley-charting and pressor-presence history while removing physiologic values. It tests whether a measurement process rather than physiology explains the signal.
- Bdisc is the selected transparent scientific alternative: B1 plus fixed P/NL status, I/W/indeterminate status, early/late values, MAP/pressor changes, their interaction, organ values and separate availability indicators. It can reveal auditable cross-domain effect modification that B0/latest state and B1/order-blind summaries lose.
- M2 is a training-only learned alternative: a diagonal-covariance two-component Gaussian mixture fit on standardized, imputed training-only early-to-late change vectors for lactate, MAP, target-pressor minutes, Foley volume and organ summaries. A component receives I/W language only if its training means satisfy the fixed direction inequalities; otherwise it remains unlabeled. Its posterior enters the same cause-specific hazard model. It may reveal nonlinear coupled trajectories, but it is sensitivity evidence and cannot validate the biological proxy.

B0, B1, Bmask and Bdisc are fit with training-only preprocessing, clipping, imputation, scaling and calibration. M2 is fit only on training data and evaluated on held-out data. The learned alternative is not required to confirm the hypothesis: a Bdisc gain over B1 tests incremental prognostic information, while theta_RD/theta_R/theta_D test the clinical interaction. All predictions include calibration intercept/slope, transition-aware Brier score, AUROC/AUPRC for R and D, 48-hour CIFs, fixed 5/10/20% operating tables, paired model differences and subject-bootstrap 95% intervals. A model gain alone is not evidence of physiology, and an interaction without a model gain is still interpreted as non-operational retrospective association.

Before narrowing to Bdisc/M2, the alternatives were matched on exact inputs, target, split, uncertainty and resource accounting. A chronology-aware GRU/transformer is deferred because this child's scientific advance is endpoint/ascertainment and component estimands, while B1/Bdisc/M2 test trajectory information with more auditability. It is not excluded for being neural or GPU-capable. A fluid-response model is deferred because the available inputevents lack clinician intent, fluid responsiveness, valid treatment counterfactuals and adequate time-varying confounding control; it would answer a different causal question. A fixed-landmark asynchronous kidney/respiratory recovery study is a scientifically credible alternative (candidate context [prior hypothesis]), but it changes the clinical question and does not repair the selected lactate ascertainment boundary, so it is deferred rather than substituted.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted B0/B1/Bmask/Bdisc held-out predictions and calibrated R/D/S CIF estimates plus theta_RD, theta_R and theta_D with uncertainty, or a complete gate-failure audit; the M2 representation and its held-out sensitivity; the measurement/selection/endpoint audit manifests; falsification outputs; and the claim ledger. No fitted result is supplied in this proposal.

## Analysis, falsification and interpretation

Estimate cause-specific hazards and Aalen-Johansen 24/48-hour CIFs for R, D and S. Compute standardized four-cell CIF contrasts and theta values on the held-out set, with subject bootstrap preserving the split and admission clustering. For the model comparison, primary performance is transition-aware Brier/calibration for R and D; discrimination is secondary. Report unweighted estimates first, with q-weighted descriptive sensitivity separately.

Falsifications are fixed before fitting: source dictionary label/link/unit checks; duplicate and overlap handling; no-future clinical/recording timestamps; first-exit and join audit; early/late permutation preserving availability and counts; outcome permutation within partitions; MAP-only, pressor-only, arterial-MAP-only, Foley and lactate-4.0 sensitivities; chart-time-only versus dual-time matrix; 24-hour versus 48-hour horizon; complete-ascertainment and U favorable/adverse bounds; 4/6/8-bin aggregation; process-only/Bmask and outcome-free q negative controls; care-unit, anchor-year and measurement-intensity strata; and q common-support/ESS diagnostics. The q negative control uses no endpoint labels and cannot be used to claim a discharge policy effect.

Supportive, adverse and inconclusive interpretations are frozen as follows.

- Interaction support requires G_cov and G_sup to pass, theta_RD < -0.02 with a 95% interval wholly below -0.02, and lower R/D CIF for P than NL in I with the prespecified direction pattern. Component-specific support additionally requires its component event rule and consistent theta_R or theta_D. Bdisc calibration improvement is required only for the stronger claim that the phenotype adds operational prognostic information.
- Adverse evidence is theta_RD >=0 or reversed, a component pattern inconsistent with the proposed context, Bdisc no better calibrated than B1, Bdisc no better than Bmask, a process-only explanation, outcome-permutation resistance, or instability across fixed timing/coverage/U/endpoint sensitivities. A Bdisc gain confined to S is not evidence for the R/D hypothesis.
- Inconclusive evidence includes any coverage/support gate failure, U above its fixed limits, too few component events, q overlap/ESS failure for selection-robust interpretation, high indeterminate fraction, incompatible units, unresolved joins, calibration failure, or an interaction interval wider than six percentage points. These criteria are not permission to redefine the study.

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes and member names, schema/column and item support, joins, exact time-zero and label logic, coverage and support flags, split integrity, fitted predictions, CIFs, contrasts, uncertainty, q overlap/ESS and falsification outputs. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for microcirculatory dysfunction, lactate cause, hemodynamic physiology, clinician intent, fluid response, discharge appropriateness/preventability, causal treatment or discharge effects, monitoring benefit, utility, ward surveillance and transportability. The result remains prognostic association in retrospective EHR data.
