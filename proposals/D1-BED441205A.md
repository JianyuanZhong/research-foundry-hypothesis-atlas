# Cross-table audit of ventilator care-plan labels against literal RT state sequences: workflow non-overlap prevents convergent validation

## Status, parents, and substantive advance

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the former parent's exact 540 person-landmarks, exact care-plan labels, first-label decision time D, five landmarks, eligibility/exclusions, repeated-person structure, and hospital-transportability cautions. It preserves the latter parent's complete literal `RT Vent On/Off` extraction, exact-record deduplication followed by deterministic earliest-anchored 30-minute clustering, semantic separation of `Start`, `Off`, and `Suspended`, exclusion of mixed-direction conflicts and Off+Suspended ambiguity from clean-state analyses, fixed respiratory-setting families, episode-order warning, and prohibition against assigning bedside clinical meaning to chart values.

The new experiment directly links those two previously separate measurement systems. It asks whether the literal care-plan distinction

- `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation`, versus
- `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`

at the frozen decision time D is followed by reproducibly different literal RT state sequences or structured respiratory-setting trajectories at 6, 24, and 48 hours. This is clinically consequential as a measurement-validity question: using these care-plan labels for multicenter quality measurement, cohorting, or decision support would require evidence that an independently charted respiratory workflow supplies convergent, temporally aligned information. A pooled association alone would be insufficient if the two fields do not coexist at the same hospitals or if differences reflect generic charting availability.

The executed audit yields a negative but useful measurement result. Literal state-sequence concordance is not estimable in this frozen cohort: only eight landmarks had any later clean literal RT state before ICU discharge, all fixed-horizon event analyses met the prespecified sparse-event stop, and the inherited principal care-plan overlap hospitals 248, 252, and 420 had zero clean literal RT clusters anywhere in the full source. Pooled setting-family availability was lower under the evaluation label, but hospital directions were mixed, leave-one-hospital-out behavior was unstable at 6 and 24 hours, and generic-documentation support attenuated or reversed the contrast. Thus the evidence is adverse to using literal `RT Vent On/Off` as a convergent validator of these care-plan labels in this cohort, while remaining inconclusive about every patient-level clinical interpretation.

## Evidence-supported claim versus hypothesis tested

Before this child, the strongest supported claims were:

1. The exact care-plan labels are strongly hospital-concentrated and do not behave as an established common multicenter phenotype.
2. Durable liberation was not ascertainable under the locked parent algorithms: independent signals occurred in only 13/309 evaluation rows and 4/231 no-trial rows overall, only 1/130 versus 0/173 in hospitals 248, 252, and 420, and approximately half of union signals had strict 48-hour observation. This endpoint is not retried here.
3. The inherited 12-hour setting/action proxies were inverse, peak-pressure dominated, sparse after exclusions, and hospital-sensitive. They are preserved as adverse/unstable findings and are not re-estimated here.
4. In the separate RT-event population, literal `Off` and `Suspended` were followed by different structured charting regimes, but RT episodes were highly hospital-concentrated and order-dependent. Neither value had validated bedside semantics, and the values must not be pooled.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis tested by this child was narrower than a clinical-effect hypothesis: in the exact frozen care-plan population, the evaluation label would show a positive evaluation-minus-no-trial difference in post-D literal Off occurrence together with lower later structured-setting positivity, and these directions would reproduce across adequately supported hospitals, leave-one-hospital-out analyses, first-landmark restriction, and generic respiratory-charting support restriction. Support would establish only cross-table documentation concordance.

The strongest conclusion supported after execution is instead that the proposed literal field and the care-plan comparison have inadequate hospital and event overlap for convergent validation. This is a documentation-system incompatibility finding. It is not evidence that a respiratory transition did not occur, and it does not validate or refute actual spontaneous-breathing-trial delivery, current ventilation, ventilator discontinuation, extubation, liberation, reintubation, quality, benefit, appropriateness, safety, or causality.

## Population, temporal boundaries, and variables

The analysis unit remains the frozen person-landmark, not an RT episode. Seventy-six persons contribute multiple eligible landmarks; 173 rows belong to repeated persons, and the maximum is five landmarks per person. Each person's eligible landmarks are ordered deterministically by `(landmark, patientunitstayid)` for first-landmark sensitivity analysis. Primary uncertainty therefore resamples persons, not rows; hospital-bootstrap uncertainty separately assesses site dependence.

For every landmark:

- D is the exact inherited care-plan decision time.
- Prior clean state is the latest clean literal RT cluster strictly before D.
- Prior history includes the number of clean clusters before D and whether the latest prior state was within 24 hours.
- A clean state exactly at D is recorded separately as concurrent and is not silently assigned to prior or post-D time.
- Post-D literal records satisfy `D < respchartoffset <= D+h` and must be on or before `unitdischargeoffset`.
- Complete ICU follow-up at horizon h requires `D+h <= unitdischargeoffset`.
- Settings use fixed partitions `[D-360,D)`, `[D,D+360)`, `[D+360,D+1440)`, and `[D+1440,D+2880)` minutes; values are not carried forward.
- Generic support requires at least one respiratory-charting timestamp in every consecutive six-hour interval through the horizon, and is a sensitivity restriction rather than proof of clinical observation.

Literal outcomes at 6, 24, and 48 hours are the occurrence and time of the first clean Off, Suspended, and Start after D, the first off-direction state classified as Off, Suspended, or none, and ordered Start→Off, Off→Start, and Suspended→Start sequences. An `Off by h` indicator means that an Off occurrence exists by h; it does not mean that Off was the first post-D state. Mixed clusters do not count.

The setting outcome for each horizon is positivity in the corresponding post-D fixed bin: 0–6, 6–24, or 24–48 hours. This is a charting-trajectory marker, not proof of ventilation or support.

## Analysis and prespecified falsification

For each label and horizon, the audit reports complete-observation denominators, events, rates, Wilson intervals, and evaluation-minus-no-trial risk differences. Risk-difference uncertainty uses 3,000-replicate percentile bootstraps clustered separately by `uniquepid` and `hospitalid`. Sensitivities restrict to the first eligible landmark per person and to complete rows with generic respiratory-chart support in every six-hour interval. Prior-state strata remain explicit because a pre-D Start or other literal state could alter subsequent documentation opportunity.

A common-support hospital requires at least five complete rows under each care-plan label at the relevant horizon. Within common support, results include site-specific risk differences, equal-hospital and row-weighted summaries, and equal-hospital leave-one-hospital-out estimates. The inherited hospitals 248, 252, and 420 are also reported exactly, even when they lack literal events. Hospital-wide RT workflow coverage is measured from the full literal extraction, not only linked rows.

The prespecified literal-event modeling and transportability stop is triggered if either care-plan label has fewer than 10 events or fewer than three hospitals have at least five complete rows under each label. A descriptive linear probability setting model was planned within common support with evaluation-label indicator, prior-state category, log prior clean-state count, landmark fixed effects, hospital fixed effects, and person-clustered sandwich covariance. `prior_any` was correctly omitted because it is determined by the non-none prior-state indicators. Each final model matrix nevertheless had rank 9 with 10 columns, so all adjusted models stopped as rank deficient. No non-unique coefficient is reported or interpreted.

The frozen supportive documentation-concordance gate required all of the following: a positive evaluation-minus-no-trial Off difference; negative 24- and 48-hour setting differences; matching direction in at least 75% of common-support hospitals; direction preserved under every leave-one-hospital-out estimate, first-landmark restriction, and generic-support restriction; and no literal sparse-event stop. Null, reversed, or site-specific evidence was adverse to concordance. Sparse, truncated, unstable, or inadequately supported evidence was inconclusive for the patient-level relationship.

## Executed results

### Workflow overlap and prior state

The audit retained exactly 540 rows, 443 persons/stays, and 74 hospitals. Only 18/540 landmarks had any prior clean literal RT state: 17 had latest prior Start, one had prior Suspended, and none had prior Off; 522 had none. No landmark had a clean literal state exactly at D. Only 8/540 had any later clean literal RT state before ICU discharge: their first later states were Off for seven and Suspended for one. The other 532 had none.

Only 18 frozen rows were located in hospitals having any clean literal RT cluster anywhere in the full source. Only eight of the 74 cohort hospitals used a clean literal RT workflow at all. Hospital 73 alone contained nine frozen rows but 2,751 clean RT clusters source-wide. Most importantly, hospitals 248, 252, and 420—the inherited principal care-plan overlap hospitals—had zero clean RT clusters source-wide. The main hospitals permitting the care-plan label comparison therefore did not use the literal RT state workflow proposed as its validator.

### Literal state events

At 6 hours, 309 evaluation and 230 no-trial rows had complete ICU observation. Neither label had any Off, Suspended, Start, Start→Off, Off→Start, or Suspended→Start event. Every literal analysis stopped for sparsity.

At 24 hours, complete denominators were 293 evaluation and 219 no-trial rows. Off occurred in 2/293 evaluation rows (0.68%) and 0/219 no-trial rows. Suspended occurred in 1/293 (0.34%) and 0/219. Start and every ordered sequence remained zero in both groups. Events occurred outside the care-plan common-support hospitals, whose event counts were all zero. Every literal analysis stopped.

At 48 hours, complete denominators were 252 evaluation and 194 no-trial rows. Off occurred in 2/252 evaluation rows (0.79%) and 0/194 no-trial rows; Suspended occurred in 1/252 (0.40%) and 0/194. Start and every ordered sequence remained zero. Every literal analysis stopped.

These zero and near-zero counts do not show that clinical transitions were absent. They show that this literal field cannot measure them in the frozen care-plan population.

### Fixed-bin structured-setting trajectories

For 0–6 hours, setting positivity was 249/309 (80.58%) under evaluation and 214/230 (93.04%) under no-trial, pooled risk difference −12.46 percentage points. The person-bootstrap 95% interval was −18.28 to −7.08 points and the hospital-bootstrap interval was −23.57 to −4.60. The first-landmark sensitivity was −11.86 points, but generic-support restriction attenuated the contrast to −3.73. Common-support site differences were +0.21 at hospital 248, +1.24 at 252, −11.43 at 417, and −0.92 at 420; one equal-hospital leave-one-out estimate crossed to +0.18. Direction was not stable.

For 6–24 hours, positivity was 234/293 (79.86%) versus 199/219 (90.87%), pooled difference −11.00 points. Person- and hospital-bootstrap intervals were −17.06 to −4.97 and −21.26 to −2.76 points. The first-landmark sensitivity was −11.46, while generic-support restriction attenuated it to −1.05. Common-support site differences split direction: −8.82 at hospital 248, −2.16 at 252, +12.86 at 417, and +2.98 at 420. Equal-hospital and row-weighted common-support differences were +1.21 and −1.48 points, and leave-one-hospital-out estimates changed sign.

For 24–48 hours, positivity was 188/252 (74.60%) versus 166/194 (85.57%), pooled difference −10.96 points. Person- and hospital-bootstrap intervals were −18.19 to −3.26 and −21.10 to −2.27 points. The first-landmark sensitivity was −12.31, but generic-support restriction reversed the contrast to +1.20. Common-support site differences were −13.62 at hospital 248, +4.73 at 252, 0 at 417, and −8.56 at 420. Although equal-hospital leave-one-out summaries remained negative, site directions were mixed and the support-restricted reversal defeats a robust concordance claim.

The pooled inverse setting contrast is therefore a real description of these rows but does not establish a transportable care-plan distinction. Its attenuation/reversal under generic respiratory-chart support is compatible with differential charting availability. The data cannot determine whether local interfaces, workflow, patient mix, or another mechanism produced it.

## Gate result and interpretation

The supportive gate failed. Literal events were far below threshold, the principal overlap hospitals had no literal RT workflow, site-specific setting directions were mixed, 6- and 24-hour leave-one-hospital-out summaries were unstable, and the 48-hour generic-support contrast reversed. A strong adverse patient-level or clinical conclusion is also prohibited because the independent literal outcome was unavailable rather than demonstrably discordant in adequately supported observations.

The appropriate interpretation has two levels:

1. **Adverse for measurement compatibility:** literal `RT Vent On/Off` cannot serve as a cross-table convergent validator of the two frozen care-plan labels in this population and snapshot. The source-wide hospital non-overlap and prespecified event stops are directly computable falsification evidence.
2. **Inconclusive for clinical meaning:** the analysis cannot determine whether either label corresponds to SBT delivery or intent, current invasive ventilation, ventilator discontinuation, extubation, liberation, reintubation, benefit, quality, appropriateness, safety, or causal effects. It also cannot determine whether absence of literal RT charting means absence of a bedside event.

The structured-setting results do not rescue the literal validation. They document a pooled inverse association that is site-specific and sensitive to generic respiratory documentation support. They are not an adverse clinical effect.

## Exact additional evidence required

Resolving the clinical hypothesis requires independently timestamped evidence not available in these structured fields: ventilator connection/disconnection; invasive versus noninvasive mode; endotracheal/tracheostomy airway insertion and removal; adjudicated SBT delivery and result; adjudicated extubation and reintubation; post-event oxygen device; post-ICU respiratory support; and local care-plan/RT interface and workflow dictionaries. A follow-up study should use blinded clinician adjudication sampled by hospital, care-plan label, literal state, episode order, and discordant chart sequence, with sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, inter-rater reliability, and hospital-level transportability reported. Expert review is needed to determine whether local interface configuration explains zero source-wide overlap.

## Computational verification boundary

An automated verifier can check source/artifact hashes; full row counts; exact cohort identity; all 540 D/label checks; literal normalization; exact and anchored deduplication; conflict/ambiguity handling; joins; prior/concurrent/post-D boundaries; ICU completeness; fixed bins; lack of carry-forward; setting-family classification; repeated-person and hospital clustering; complete denominators; Wilson intervals; bootstrap contrasts; generic-support restrictions; common-support membership; site and leave-one-out estimates; sparse-event stops; source-wide RT workflow coverage; and model-rank stops. Submitted conclusions must be linked to these outputs and must accept supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes without favoring confirmation.

No automatic computation can establish care intent, actual SBT delivery, bedside ventilation/device status, airway removal, extubation, liberation, reintubation, documentation correctness, causal effects, quality, appropriateness, benefit, safety, or transportability beyond observed workflows. Those claims require missing independent evidence, clinical adjudication, local workflow review, or another study.
