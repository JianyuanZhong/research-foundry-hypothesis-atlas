# Certified and assumption-conditioned baseline-only repeat kidney-status prioritization

## Successor purpose and substantive repairs

This is a targeted successor of the Lead-assigned assessed-valid parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves their clinically useful but deliberately narrow question:

> Conditional on verified field and equation semantics, among participants with complete baseline measurements who have a qualifying exported repeat assessment 2--8 years after baseline, does adding continuous baseline cystatin-C/creatinine discordance `Z` to sex, age, and baseline creatinine-equation eGFR improve out-of-fold prioritization of the low repeat creatinine-equation eGFR state `Ycr = I(eGFRcr_1 < 60)`?

The estimand is the observed-schedule, repeat-attender quantity `P(Ycr=1 | X,A=1)`, not a fixed-horizon risk, first clinical event, CKD, persistent disease, measured GFR, kidney failure, causal testing benefit, clinical utility, treatment effect, or transportability. A supportive result would establish only a reproducible incremental prediction signal in this selected export population; it would not recommend ordering a test or changing care.

The parents leave two material execution risks that this successor closes. First, the assay-gated parent has the correct 2021 cystatin-C age coefficient `0.9961`, while the dimension-repaired parent retains the incorrect rounded `0.996`; this proposal freezes `0.9961` in the equation certificate, implementation, test vectors, and output hash. Second, the strongest provenance parent hard-stops when local metadata are incomplete but does not provide an executable route to diagnose the missing semantics. This successor defines two explicitly non-interchangeable lanes: a fail-closed structural audit with no biological quantities, and a complete hashed assumption-conditioned audit that may execute the same arithmetic for sensitivity/QA but can never issue a scientific verdict. No favorable assumption bundle may be selected.

The four-column spline basis is also bound to its mathematically implied dimensions: M0 has 9 standardized non-intercept columns (`1 + 4 + 4`) and M1 has 10 (`M0 + Z`). Any 10/11 assertion is a hard model-specification failure.

## Evidence certificates and fail-closed status machine

Require two canonical JSON inputs before any scientific interpretation.

`field_certificate.json` must contain one entry for each `31-0.0`, `21003-0.0`, `21003-1.0`, `53-0.0`, `53-1.0`, `30700-0.0`, `30700-1.0`, `30720-0.0`, and `30720-1.0`, with field/table/column identity, instance/array, semantic label, specimen or matrix, unit, assay/platform or measurement method, valid domain, every certified missing token, date representation where applicable, and source URL or local artifact, version/retrieval date, and SHA-256. It must explicitly establish whether instance 1 is a repeat collection, whether `53-1.0` is an exact collection date, and compatibility of baseline/repeat analyte, matrix, assay, and units or a frozen validated conversion. A numeric-range guess or operator assertion is not verification.

`equation_certificate.json` must contain canonical formula text, coefficient vector, sex coding, required units/conversions, DOI/PMID/PMCID, frozen source excerpt/hash, implementation hash, and numerical test vectors. The primary equations are the race-free 2021 CKD-EPI equations from Inker et al. (DOI `10.1056/NEJMoa2102953`, PMID `34554658`, PMCID `PMC8822996`):

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^a * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^Age * 1.012^F`, with `(k,a)=(0.7,-0.241)` for female and `(0.9,-0.302)` for male, where `Scr` is mg/dL and `F=1` female, `F=0` male.

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.9961^Age * 0.932^F`, with `Scys` in mg/L. If creatinine is certified as micromoles/L, use exactly `Scr = raw_30700/88.4`; otherwise use only a certificate-supplied conversion. The equation paper supports formulae and validation against measured GFR in its own studies; it does not certify UKB field semantics.

Use these statuses, with no implicit promotion.

* `STRUCTURAL_ONLY`: source/schema/header/key audit only; no biomarker conversion, dates, eGFR, cohort, outcome, model, event count, or biological metric.
* `ASSUMPTION_CONDITIONED_UNVERIFIED`: a complete hashed assumption manifest supplies every missing field/equation semantic and permits the identical computational audit; all derived quantities are explicitly assumption-conditioned and cannot support or refute the hypothesis.
* `CERTIFIED_EXECUTION`: every required field and equation item is evidence-verified, hashes/joins pass, and the complete analysis finishes all gates; only this lane may issue the prespecified scientific classification.
* `STOP_FIELD_UNCERTIFIED`, `STOP_CERTIFICATE_CONFLICT`, `FAIL_SCHEMA_BINDING`, `FAIL_VALUE_PARSE`, `FAIL_DATE_PARSE`, `FAIL_MODEL_SPEC`, and `FAIL_ANALYSIS`: fail closed; no scientific verdict.

A conflict among certificates is a stop, not a choice of the convenient interpretation. With no certificate or manifest, run `STRUCTURAL_ONLY`. The inherited anchor counts are diagnostic expectations only and must never be emitted as study findings in a stopped or assumption-conditioned branch.

## Assumption-conditioned audit lane

`assumption_manifest.json` must be canonical and hashed, include a stable `bundle_id`, and specify for every field unit, missing tokens, date format/calendar/time zone, instance interpretation, assay compatibility, and equation version, marking each item `ASSUMED`, citing its source/reason, and listing alternatives where relevant. Missing entries reject the bundle. Preserve raw token counts before conversion, apply only the manifest's explicit missing tokens, reject nonmissing unparsable numeric/date values, and run formula test vectors before any model.

Every declared bundle runs independently; no bundle is selected for favorable results. Aggregate side-by-side outputs may include parse diagnostics, mask counts, outcome rates, fold metrics, calibration, capacity, and threshold contrasts, but every name and file carries `ASSUMPTION_CONDITIONED_UNVERIFIED`. Such results are QA/sensitivity artifacts only: they cannot be called creatinine, cystatin C, eGFR, CKD, or a clinical outcome, and cannot receive supportive or adverse biological interpretation. A later verified certificate requires a fresh certified rerun; fallback output cannot update the certificate.

## Exact baseline-only models and deterministic evaluation

M0 contains only baseline sex `F`, baseline age, and baseline eGFRcr. M1 adds baseline continuous `Z=log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)`. For age use fixed bounds `[40,80]`; for eGFRcr use `[60,120]`. Clip to bounds, set knots `k1=lower`, `k2=lower+(upper-lower)/3`, `k3=lower+2*(upper-lower)/3`, `k4=upper`, and define `d_j(u)=max(u-k_j,0)^3/(k4-k_j)` for `j=1,2,3`; basis columns are `[u,d1-d3,d2-d3,d3]`. Center and population-scale each basis column with training-fold moments, using SD 1 when SD is zero. Standardize F and Z with training-fold moments. Assert exactly 9 M0 and 10 M1 non-intercept columns.

Fit float64 `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(penalty='l2', C=1.0, solver='lbfgs', fit_intercept=True, class_weight=None, max_iter=1000, tol=1e-10, random_state=0)`. Convergence warnings, nonfinite probabilities, or `n_iter_ >=1000` fail. Record Python, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, patsy versions and lockfile hashes.

Use ten deterministic outcome-stratified five-fold repeats with seeds `104729,104759,104773,104779,104789,104801,104827,104831,104849,104851`. Stable-sort by `(Ycr,eid)` and assign folds round-robin separately within labels; use ascending numeric `eid` as the only tie order. Reconstruct exactly the same assignment after each label permutation. Every prediction is held out and paired for the same participant/model/repeat.

For `q=0.01,...,0.20`, select top `ceil(q*n)` by stable descending OOF risk and ascending `eid`; report capture, event fraction, PPV, and `DeltaC(q)`. At threshold grid `{0.001,0.0025,0.005,0.0075,0.010,0.015,0.020,0.030,0.040,0.050,0.075,0.100,0.150,0.200}`, report `NB=TP/n-FP/n*p_t/(1-p_t)`, DeltaNB, test-all and test-none. These are assumption-indexed mathematical contrasts, not clinical net benefit. At 5% and 10%, allocate fold quotas by floor plus largest remainder, break equal remainders by fold index, and rank within fold by descending prediction then `eid`; report exact identity, overlap, Jaccard, entrants/leavers and event arithmetic.

Use unpenalized binomial logistic recalibration of Ycr against logit(mean repeated held-out prediction), with clipping reported. The calibration gate is intercept absolute value `<=0.10`, slope `[0.90,1.10]`, and absolute observed-minus-predicted risk `<=0.002`; a documented direct-likelihood-equivalent `C=1e12` fallback must agree within `1e-6`.

## Uncertainty, nulls, and falsification

Use 4,000 paired participant bootstrap draws with `default_rng(520243)`, carrying frozen outcomes, predictions and selections without refitting/reranking. Report 98% percentile intervals for DeltaNB at 2% and DeltaC/event yield at 10%. For the predeclared simultaneous family, use the frozen eight-member order: DeltaNB at 1% and 5%, DeltaC at 5%, borderline and far 10% global-selection contributions, and three timing-band DeltaNB-at-2% contrasts. Studentize with population-appropriate `ddof=1`; zero/nonfinite SD is a hard failure.

Use 200 outcome-label permutations with seed `520242`, permuting within baseline-eGFR strata `[60,75)` and `>=75`, reconstructing folds and refitting both models. Report statistic-specific null summaries and randomized-plus-one tails; never force null contrasts to zero. Permute Z within baseline-eGFR deciles and sex for the predictor-null, run reversed-label `1-Ycr`, and run a private elapsed-time leakage sentinel. A gain reproduced by a null or leakage control is not supportive evidence.

A certified supportive predictive result requires complete source and certificate integrity, exact 9/10 dimensions, complete uncertainty/null computation, calibrated M1, a simultaneous 98% lower DeltaC bound above zero throughout contiguous 5--15% capacity, stability across repeats and eligible strata, observed statistics more favorable than fold-aware label nulls, no comparable predictor-null/reversed-label/leakage gain, and no material calibration deterioration. The practical labels additionally require DeltaNB at 2% point `>=0.001` with lower bound above zero and at least 8/10 positive repeats, and DeltaC/event yield at 10% point `>=5` with lower bound above zero and at least 8/10 positive repeats; the joint label requires both. A reproducible certified negative incremental curve with upper interval below zero and at least 8/10 adverse repeats, or certified calibration harm, is adverse. Practical-floor failure with intervals crossing zero, timing instability, poor selection positivity, unexplained anchor mismatch, or incomplete computation is inconclusive. Assumption-conditioned output is always `UNVERIFIED_INCONCLUSIVE`, never supportive or adverse.

## What remains outside scope

Computational verification can establish source/schema hashes, fields, joins, certificate/manifest status, raw-token and parse diagnostics, masks, equation test vectors, 9/10 dimensions, fold isolation, baseline-only provenance, predictions, calibration, capacity/threshold arithmetic, bootstrap/null completion, and gate consistency. It cannot establish the truth of an external assay certificate, measured-GFR accuracy, chronicity/persistent CKD, true first clinical testing, outcomes for non-attenders, threshold appropriateness, causal or clinical benefit, harms, cost-effectiveness, treatment changes, patient preferences, or transportability. Those require expert assay review, adjudicated renal outcomes and measured GFR, observed decisions/utilities, and external or prospective validation. Keep rows, eids, predictions, folds, selections, weights, and resampling arrays private; publish aggregate outputs and certificate/manifest hashes only.
