# Within-macrophage inflammatory state, not composition, drives the macrophage-associated signal

## Question and clinical importance

In rheumatoid arthritis, macrophage-associated inflammatory signals are often interpreted as evidence of macrophage recruitment or abundance. However, the same signal could instead reflect a shift in within-macrophage inflammatory state. Resolving this distinction is clinically consequential: if the signal is driven by composition, interventions should target macrophage recruitment or tissue infiltration; if it is driven by within-cell state, interventions should target intracellular signaling or macrophage reprogramming.

## Hypothesis

The macrophage-associated inflammatory signal in E-MTAB-8322/8873 is better explained by within-macrophage inflammatory state than by macrophage abundance/composition. A donor-aware model trained on macrophage-only pseudobulk expression (E-MTAB-8322) will predict the inflammatory signal in the targeted macrophage–FLS coculture data (E-MTAB-8873) better than a composition-only baseline.

## Evidence and closest inspected work

- [K1] Bao et al. 2026, Front Immunol, doi:10.3389/fimmu.2026.1873778 — supports the relevance of macrophage–fibroblast communication in RA synovium.
- [K2] IGFBP5-related fibroblast state work — supports the biological plausibility of reciprocal stromal–myeloid signaling.
- [K3] Cross-tissue transcriptomic analysis — supports shared myeloid-inflammatory programs but does not establish directionality in an RA coculture.

## Design

- Population: matched-donor macrophage and FLS coculture samples from E-MTAB-8322 and E-MTAB-8873.
- Variables: macrophage inflammatory state (within-cell expression), macrophage abundance/composition (cell counts/proportions), FLS inflammatory response.
- Outcome: FLS inflammatory signal in coculture.
- Baseline: composition-only model using macrophage abundance/proportions.
- Alternative: donor-aware model using macrophage-only pseudobulk expression.
- Analysis: donor-aware pseudobulk/dependence handling, donor-stratified analysis, and comparison of baseline versus learned alternative.
- Falsification: if the composition-only baseline performs as well as or better than the within-state model, the hypothesis is not supported.

## Limitations

- The targeted panel does not measure all genes.
- The `cells` field is a proxy for composition.
- SA149/SA149P relatedness is unresolved.
- The hypothesis is a directional association, not a causal claim.
