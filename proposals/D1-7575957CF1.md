## Unresolved decision, supported evidence, and substantive advance

The bedside decision is whether an adult who remains invasively ventilated and markedly fluid-positive after a *recent recorded* vasopressor course and six recorded pressor-free hours should start or restart an ICU-recorded furosemide or bumetanide delivery during the next 12 hours while recorded ventilation remains open, or avoid one. Earlier airway liberation would matter, but creatinine rise, renal replacement therapy (RRT), death, hospice discharge, or acute transfer could outweigh it.

The strongest claim supported now is narrower than clinical benefit. Relevant passages were inspected in the frozen full XML of Bircher et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13154636`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100075`, source `[source checksum]`, file [source checksum]). It states that optimal timing, volume and duration of fluid removal remain uncertain and that current strategies rely heavily on clinical judgment without standardized criteria. The frozen full XML of Kuriyama et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13476641`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120`, source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports 26 randomized trials with 1,652 participants; evidence was clinically heterogeneous and mostly low or very-low certainty, largely from heart-failure and post-cardiovascular-surgery populations. It does not resolve this recent-post-pressor timing policy. The frozen full XML of Orieux et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13218125`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083`, source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) calls renal congestion hypothesis-generating, says interventional evidence and validated targets are lacking, and describes hemodynamic-guided decongestion as supported only by feasibility data. These sources support importance and uncertainty, not efficacy, renal safety, a 24-hour biological threshold, or exchangeability.

Exact MIMIC streams can compute imperfect recorded pressor, loop, airway, renal and terminal proxies. A treatment- and outcome-blind discovery audit found 7,663 broad earliest adult hour-48 ventilated opportunities. Of 1,607 passing the inherited any-prior-pressor plus six-hour stability rule, 843 (52.5%) had no valid pressor delivery in the prior 24 hours, including 381 with last delivery more than 36 hours before `t0`; the repaired 24-hour rule retained 764 before MAP, balance, urine, potassium, loop-washout, ECMO and RRT gates. This demonstrates a material population-label problem and feasibility risk, not shock truth, selection-bias direction, treatment effect, or final cohort size.

The valid leader independently showed that the old baseline `e0(X0)` response used eventual initiation before a post-`t0` shared closure, so 865 broad eventual-noninitiator labels had truncated opportunity. It repaired that defect by targeting every eligible subject with equal A/B clones and numerator-free sequential compatibility weights. The recent-pressor sibling repaired the population label but retained the contaminated measured-overlap estimand. This child combines the two nonredundant repairs and gives them one unambiguous precedence contract.

The unresolved falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among every namespaced-discovery subject passing the frozen earliest hour-48 opportunity and recent-pressor/stable pre-`t0` eligibility, assignment-compatible A12 follow-up increases the lower bound of five-day recorded 48-hour airway-event-free extubation by more than 0.05 versus B12, while one-sided upper limits remain below +0.03 for recorded creatinine rise and creatinine-or-death, +0.05 for multisource RRT and RRT-or-death, and +0.03 for recorded terminal/hospice/acute-transfer harm.

This is a hypothesis about a record-defined observational policy association. It is not yet a conclusion, a pharmacologic effect, proof of durable extubation or renal safety, or a recommendation.

## Strategies, endpoints, estimand, and analysis

Loop items remain furosemide 221794/228340 and bumetanide 229639. After exact-duplicate collapse and `linkorderid` course grouping, a qualifying row needs consistent keys, positive duration, accepted status, and positive amount in mg or rate in mg/hour; onset is earliest qualifying `starttime`. Unknown units, nonpositive or contradictory rows, Paused, literal Bolus, cancelled/order-only rows and exact ties are ambiguous.

For the earliest accepted extubation task time `X`, deterministically select the linked same-stay item-225792 ventilation interval `V` and set `C=min(X,V.endtime)`. A12 requires first qualifying loop start strictly in `(t0,min(t0+12h,C))`; B12 avoids through that boundary. A shared closure before divergence retains both compatible clones and their common endpoint state; it never creates a baseline B label. Precedence remains resolved death, exact loop/airway ambiguity, positive-gap ICU exit, strictly ordered loop/C, then `G12=t0+12h`.

The target is every subject passing repaired frozen pre-`t0` eligibility. Initialize constant equal A12 and B12 clones. No post-`t0` event or factual eventual-initiation label may determine inclusion or baseline weight. There is no baseline propensity, `e0` restriction, overlap tilt or X0-dependent primary numerator.

For every inherited non-deterministic clone-hour decision, cross-fit the denominator probability of the observed compatible action from X0, hour and history available strictly at interval start, using the inherited separate arm-specific L2 logistic models and five subject folds. Save raw probabilities; clip modeled probabilities to [0.01,0.99] only for constructing `W=product(1/p_den)`, then apply pooled 1st/99th truncation. Deterministic compatibility multiplies by exactly 1.0. Primary estimation uses arm-normalized Hájek means. For every bounded state `[L,U]`, compute `DeltaL=pA_L-pB_U` and `DeltaU=pA_U-pB_L`; never complete-case delete or midpoint-impute.

Add pre-`t0` pressor X0 summaries: hours from last valid pressor end to `t0`; union duration clipped to the recent window; course count; last item; multi-pressor overlap; and missing/late confirmation. X0 uses only rows with `storetime<=t0`. Exact-deduplicate intervals, take their union across IDs rather than summing overlaps, and count unique `(itemid,linkorderid)`, falling back to `(itemid,orderid)` and then exact-deduplicated intervals.

Primary RHA-E48-D5 is the inherited bounded record proxy: an accepted extubation by day 5 followed by 48 hours alive with the index admission open and without same-admission recorded recurrent invasive ventilation or tracheostomy. C48/W48/D48/M48 observation classes and chart-augmented, continuous-ICU and ward-gap-as-unknown variants remain mandatory. W48 is absence of an ICU airway record during off-ICU time, not proof of continuous airway independence.

## Falsification, result meaning, and verification

Execution is **infeasible** if a required source/hash/state/model/strategy-minimum/endpoint/bootstrap requirement fails. A completed run is **validity-inconclusive** if practical positivity, calibration, X0/history balance, negative controls, ambiguity, eMAR contamination, timing, observation, renal ascertainment, process controls, population-window sensitivity or weight sensitivity fails.

If computation and validity pass:

- **Supportive-recorded** requires no Holm-rejected adverse family, airway lower limit above +0.05, chart and continuous-ICU airway lower limits above zero, all renal/death upper limits below their margins, nonadverse disposition and every diagnostic passing. It would support prioritizing external validation or randomization, not treatment adoption.
- **Adverse-FWER** requires a positive adverse-oriented statistic rejected after Holm. It is an observational concern that may justify critical-care/nephrology review or de-prioritization, not proof of toxicity.
- **Margin-falsified** requires no adjusted adverse family and airway upper limit at or below +0.05. It rejects only the specified five-point recorded-proxy improvement.
- Every other feasible validity-passing result is **statistically inconclusive**. Inconclusive does not mean no effect, equivalence or safety. Safety alerts are always reported, including when validity fails.

The verifier can recompute source hashes/headers, partition, earliest opportunity, pressor interval boundaries, remote exclusions, no later-stay reopening, equal clones, absence of `e0`/tilt/primary numerator, state transitions, weights, bounds, complete sensitivity rebuilds, bootstrap/Holm accounting, and whether conclusions match computed outputs. Fixtures must include correct computations paired with unsupported causal/safety/recommendation text, as well as correct supportive, adverse, margin-falsified, validity-inconclusive, statistical-inconclusive and infeasible wording.

Automation cannot establish actual pressor/loop/RRT receipt or nonreceipt; true shock recovery, congestion or perfusion adequacy; extubation readiness or durable airway independence; true KDIGO AKI or renal safety; treatment intent or goals of care; exchangeability, causality, transportability or clinical actionability. Those stronger conclusions require blinded critical-care/nephrology adjudication using fuller notes, imaging/hemodynamics or linked outcomes, external replication and preferably randomization.
