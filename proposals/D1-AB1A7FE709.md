# Documentary-M2 patient-retention frontier under incomplete preoperative report bundles

## 1. Successor, unresolved question, and substantive advance

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's adult first-eligible-resection population, point/interval operative-clock states, 24-hour confirmatory cutoff, 90-day CT/MRI window, 365-day recorded-prior-treatment lookback, 2015–2018 development/2019 lock/separate 2020 and 2021 tests, whole-accession report units, complete radiology/pathology reader books, encounter-documentary M2 endpoint, locked B/R/G/same-fit-Gmask pipelines, fixed K, strict comparator/random capture gates, coupled uncertainty, exact attained finite-corpus extrema, anchored whole-patient deletion, and all non-causal limits.

The parent's advance was to require exact equality between the incomplete-report G queue and the same-world maximum-stored-content G queue. Its unresolved clinical-interpretation defect is that this binary endpoint treats every substitution alike. It does not distinguish loss of a patient whose eventual same-encounter pathology is read as documentary M2 from replacement of one documentary-negative patient by another. Conversely, aggregate capture can remain unchanged when one oracle-selected documentary-M2 patient is dropped and a different documentary-M2 patient is gained. Thus aggregate capture is too coarse for patient protection, while exact whole-queue equality is not specific to the queue's stated high-risk-capture objective.

This child adds no model, feature, threshold, clinical action, or external dataset. It inserts one nested, outcome-aware evaluation frontier between the inherited capture frontier q_req and exact-set frontier q_dec: q_M2, the smallest complete-patient bundle-coverage threshold at which every documentary-M2 patient in the same-world maximum-content queue is retained in the incomplete-report queue, while all inherited capture gates also pass. The mandatory logical order is q_req <= q_M2 <= q_dec. This separates:

1. preservation of aggregate documentary-M2 count;
2. preservation of the identities of maximum-content-queued documentary-M2 patients; and
3. preservation of every queued patient.

The distinction is clinically consequential because a scarce-review policy acts on individual patients. However, replacing a documentary-negative patient is not called harmless: HCC lacks downstream actions, harms, benefit, and patient utilities. The experiment only identifies whether the stronger documentary-M2 patient-retention claim survives incomplete stored-report bundles.

## 2. Evidence-supported claim versus hypothesis to be tested

**Strongest claim supported before execution.** Direct catalog, schema, header, and full core-table scans establish that HCC snapshot `[source checksum]` contains patient/encounter-linked demographics, source-labelled procedures with start/end fields, CT/MRI-like examination accessions with acquisition-like `start time` and eventual stored narrative, same-encounter untimed pathology narrative, and dated medication/order rows. The Episode-79 no-sampling audit read all headers and every row of encounters, procedures, examinations, and pathology: 105,044, 338,040, 419,996, and 46,395 data rows respectively; every required column was present. This verifies source feasibility, not cohort eligibility, semantic correctness, model performance, report availability, or M2 biology.

Current literature motivates the question but does not answer it. Li et al. reported in a retrospective 243-patient resection cohort that M2 was associated with higher recurrence and mortality, especially in the first two years (DOI `10.1186/s12957-026-04229-2`; inspected frozen full-text XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]). That observational study does not establish that an M2 triage queue improves outcomes. A 2026 systematic review of 36 AI models for early recurrence after HCC resection found 35 retrospective studies, 27 single-centre studies, external validation in 6, inconsistent calibration/decision-curve reporting, and mostly high risk of bias (DOI `10.3390/cancers18132028`; inspected frozen full-text XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]). That review concerns recurrence models, not this report-bundle frontier, and supplies context rather than validation.

**Unresolved computable claim.** At 24 hours before the source-documented first eligible resection, can incomplete exposure of eligible stored CT/MRI report bundles preserve every maximum-content-queued documentary-M2 patient, across every compatible source, onset, reader, pathology, year, and anchored-deletion state, at a complete-patient conditional coverage threshold below one?

**Confirmatory hypothesis.** At rho=0.10 and d=24 hours, maximum stored content passes every inherited G-versus-B, G-versus-R, G-versus-Gmask, and G-versus-capacity-matched-random strict margin gate in both 2020 and 2021; q_req<1; and q_M2<1. Exact all-patient equality q_dec<1 is a stronger secondary result, not required for the primary patient-retention hypothesis.

A supportive result establishes only a finite-corpus, retrospective documentary-M2 patient-retention reserve for a frozen score under hypothetical stored-content masks. It does not establish actual preoperative report release/view, biological MVI, treatment choice, recurrence reduction, survival benefit, safety, fairness, utility, or deployment readiness.

## 3. Population and temporal boundaries

For each coupled source/operation world omega:

- Include adults (age >=18 from encounters `age`) at their earliest source-documented eligible hepatobiliary resection according to a preregistered procedure dictionary reviewed by a hepatobiliary surgeon.
- Require same-`(Patient master index, Encounter number)` pathology narrative sufficient to place the encounter in the documentary target frame under at least one complete pathology book.
- Exclude an earlier qualifying resection and any recorded transplant, TACE/embolization, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `[t_op-365 days, t_op)`, using procedures, medications, and non-medication orders. Ambiguous membership remains an outer source state; do not choose the favorable state.
- Treat a clinically supported anaesthesia-system procedure timestamp as a point. Treat midnight/date-like case-record values as `[date,date+24h)`. Missing, contradictory, non-reversible, or clinically ambiguous times remain unavailable/alternative states. Never substitute encounter admission or discharge time for operation time.
- Set the confirmatory decision deadline to `t_dec=t_op-24h`. Candidate CT/MRI acquisition intervals must lie wholly within `[t_op-90d,t_dec)`. Locked 12-, 48-, and 72-hour analyses are non-rescuing sensitivities.
- Use patient-disjoint calendar roles: 2015–2018 development, 2019 preprocessing/hyperparameter/K lock, separate 2020 and 2021 tests, and 2022 onward audit only. Any patient possibly belonging to a later role is quarantined from every earlier role.

The unit of analysis is one patient at the first eligible episode. The policy unit is one fixed queue position. The target endpoint is encounter-documentary M2, not specimen-linked or biological MVI.

## 5. Reader books, endpoint, predictors, and baselines

Two qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists and an adjudicator independently create three complete immutable corpus-wide radiology books. Two qualified Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists and an adjudicator create three complete immutable corpus-wide pathology books. Readers see only their permitted source fields. The nine global radiology-by-pathology book pairs are fixed across all patients, years, cutoffs, source/onset worlds, models, thresholds, and deletions; no row-level favorable mixing is allowed.

Pathology terminals are `OUT`, `IN0-A`, `IN1-A`, `IN0-U`, and `IN1-U`. Every same-encounter pathology row remains indivisible in a book. Each complete analysis state maps the documentary endpoint to binary `Y_i(z)`; unresolved alternatives remain separate complete states. No pathology value enters a predictor.

Retain the parent's already specified patient-balanced, additive, outcome-blind pipelines:

- `B(Z)`: acquisition-only structured information;
- `R(Z,S,Q)`: the same structured block plus report surface/availability;
- `G(Z,S,Q,X)`: full structured, surface, and blinded-reader semantic content;
- `Gmask`: the same fitted G object with every semantic block replaced by its training-defined unavailable block, without refitting;
- capacity-matched random K: exact expected documentary-M2 capture `K*Y/N`, represented by integer cross-products.

Z contains age, sex, eligible acquisition count/recency, acquisition modality/ambiguity, and development-grouped machine. S/Q contain only report length, component count, field-presence and availability/surface features. X contains only frozen radiologist-book clinical-content fields. IDs, test labels, reader identity, pathology, labs, untimed notes, future rows, and test-derived dictionaries are forbidden. Freeze preprocessing, feature order, coefficients, regularization, score bytes, tie keys, and `K_rho=floor(rho*N_ref)` from 2019. Require `N_ref>=500`, `K_.05>=25`, `K_.10>=50`, and `K_.20>=100`; each test state requires `N>=K_.10` and at least 50 documentary-positive events.

## 6. Coupled whole-accession exposure states

For patient i, source world omega, cutoff d, and whole accession u, `E_iomega,d,u=1` only if operation membership/time, accession identity, CT/MRI modality, and acquisition-like `start time` are valid and the acquisition interval lies wholly in the prespecified window.

Each source-clean eligible accession receives one coupled monotone onset state: O=0 eligible by 72h; O=1 first eligible in (72,48]h; O=2 in (48,24]h; O=3 in (24,12]h; O=4 later/never. In 72/48/24/12 order, `V(O)=(1[O=0],1[O<=1],1[O<=2],1[O<=3])` and `M=E*V`. M means hypothetical exposure of eventual stored content. It is not a report authored/final/release/retrieval/view event.

For d=24 retain, in every omega and reader book, the full target N, patients with at least one eligible accession n_A, structural entry fraction `e=n_A/N`, and counts of zero-acquisition, incomplete-bundle, and complete-bundle patients. ## 7. Locked inherited capture and exact-queue analyses

For every complete state `z=(omega,O,year,radiology/pathology book pair,deletion)`, score the already-fitted pipelines and construct exact fixed-K queues with frozen binary64 score/tie serialization. Anchored deletion removes every record of one patient without refitting, reranking, backfill, or requota; an originally offered deleted patient leaves `EMPTY`.

Let `S_G(z)` be the non-EMPTY patient set in the incomplete-content G queue and `S_max(z)` the non-EMPTY set from the same fitted G pipeline with every source-clean eligible accession exposed in the identical source, reader, pathology, year, and deletion state. The all-content object is an oracle for eventual stored content, not truth or deployable information.

Retain the strict inherited gates `100*(H_G-H_C)/K>5` for `C in {B,R,Gmask}` and the exact random-K cross-product equivalent to `100*H_G/K-100*Y/N>5`, separately in both years and every allowed state/deletion. Retain the exact rational threshold set R, monotone PASS(q), q_req, q_dec, immediately lower threshold witnesses, queue hashes, set differences, and ordered-slot disagreement. q_req is the smallest q for which every inherited aggregate gate passes in all states with Q>=q. q_dec is the smallest q additionally requiring `S_G(z)=S_max(z)`.

## 8. New documentary-M2 patient-retention estimand

Within each complete same-world pathology state define:

`P_max(z)={i: i in S_max(z) and Y_i(z)=1}`.

Define the retained-oracle-positive predicate

`J_M2(z)=1[P_max(z) subseteq S_G(z)]`.

This asks whether every maximum-content-queued documentary-M2 patient is still offered under incomplete bundles. It does not reward a different M2 patient as a substitute for a lost oracle-selected M2 patient. It also does not call non-M2 replacements harmless.

For every `q in R`, define `M2_PASS(q)=1` only when:

1. inherited `PASS(q)=1` in every eligible 2020 and 2021 state with Q>=q; and
2. `J_M2(z)=1` in every one of those identical coupled states.

If maximum content and feasibility gates pass, define:

`q_M2=min{q in R: M2_PASS(q)=1}`.

Emit exact `q_req, q_M2, q_dec`, the invariant `q_req<=q_M2<=q_dec`, and the gaps `q_M2-q_req` and `q_dec-q_M2`. At every boundary state emit protected hashes for:

- `S_max \ S_G` split into documentary-positive and documentary-negative counts;
- `S_G \ S_max` split into documentary-positive and documentary-negative counts;
- `P_max`, lost oracle-positive patients, EMPTY positions, and ordered-slot disagreements;
- all corresponding H_G, H_max, comparator captures, N, Y, K, n_A, e, and Q.

No arbitrary replacement tolerance is introduced. q_M2 is the exact zero-lost-oracle-positive endpoint. Full replacement-count and positive/negative set-difference curves over every q are descriptive and cannot rescue failure.

## 9. Falsification and one-hot interpretation

Apply this precedence-ordered, mutually exclusive primary label:

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: any source replay, raw-ordinal retention, report grouping, clock interval, coupled-state generation, reader-book completeness, locked score/tie replay, fixed-K construction, deletion, threshold enumeration, boundary witness, or invariant check fails.
2. `feasibility_inconclusive`: any N/K/event/n_A/structural-denominator requirement fails.
3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse`: maximum stored content fails any inherited comparator, random, year, reader, source-world, or deletion gate.
4. `aggregate_capture_reserve_adverse`: maximum content passes but q_req is undefined or q_req=1.
5. `patient_retention_adverse`: q_req<1 but q_M2 is undefined or q_M2=1. Aggregate capture can survive missing bundles, but at least one allowed nontrivial-coverage state drops a maximum-content-queued documentary-M2 patient. This falsifies the confirmatory hypothesis.
6. `documentary_M2_retention_supportive_queue_fragile`: q_M2<1 but q_dec is undefined or q_dec=1. The primary documentary-M2 patient-retention hypothesis is supported, while exact whole-queue identity is not; substitutions involving patients classified documentary-negative remain and cannot be assumed clinically harmless.
7. `full_documentary_queue_supportive_bridge_pending`: q_dec<1, all minimality/boundary witnesses verify, and the required invariant holds. This supports the stronger exact-queue reserve.
8. `external_bridge_pending`: HCC computation supports category 6 or 7 but no complete frozen-frame archive proves actual final/release/retrieval/readability before the deadline. This is the mandatory deployment suffix, not adverse evidence about stored-content performance.

Categories 6 and 7 are supportive only for finite-corpus documentary ranking under hypothetical stored-content exposure. A 12/48/72-hour sensitivity, 5%/20% capacity, pooled year, average reader, average source state, or favorable deletion cannot rescue a failed confirmatory label. Equality and boundary cases are failures because thresholds are strict.

Supportive means the exact stored-content experiment met its prespecified conditions. Adverse is scientifically informative and means the stronger patient-retention hypothesis was falsified under at least one allowed state. Inconclusive means feasibility, provenance, or uncertainty prevented the test; it is never recoded as adverse or supportive.

## 10. Exact data still required for stronger conclusions

An operational report-availability bridge requires, for every frozen HCC patient and whole accession: one-to-one row/component crosswalk; immutable report/version IDs and predecessor/amendment/retraction lineage; authored, final, release, ingestion, retrieval, parse-readability and clinician-view events with timezone and clock provenance; exact as-of-deadline payload hashes; all nonresponders; and year-specific patient-level simultaneous lower bounds on the unchanged N/n_A frame. HCC supplies none of these timing/version fields, so HCC alone cannot convert q_M2 or q_dec into decision-time availability.

Qualified Chinese-reading radiologists and hepatobiliary pathologists must adjudicate dictionaries and complete reader books. Biological MVI requires specimen/accession/time linkage, sampling protocol, blocks/slides or structured pathology; HCC lacks them. Whether substitutions are clinically acceptable requires patient/stakeholder utilities, review capacity, clinician action and harms. Treatment benefit, recurrence, survival, quality of life, cost, fairness, and external transport require prospective silent-mode validation, linked outcomes, and ultimately an interventional or otherwise decision-appropriate study. The current experiment cannot establish any of those claims.
