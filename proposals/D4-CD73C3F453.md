## Unresolved clinical question

The strongest available evidence supports only that baseline metabolic indices can be associated with later coded cardiometabolic disease and that structured survival or multistate models can describe coded progression. It does not establish that a dated within-person change between two local UKB assessment-linked panels adds information beyond current levels, nor that a first administrative code represents persistent clinical disease.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among UK Biobank participants aged 40–69 with exactly one established coded domain (diabetes, cardiovascular disease, or chronic kidney disease) at both valid assessment-linked panels, does annualized change in HbA1c, triglycerides, and HDL add route-specific prognostic information beyond d1 levels, domain identity, disease duration, and prespecified covariates for the first subsequent additional domain; and, where participant-level observation opportunity is directly supported, does the signal persist for a later same-domain confirmation code within 30–365 days?

This is an internal, conditional prognostic hypothesis in the observed repeat-assessment/assay-complete population. It is not a causal effect of changing a biomarker, a biological slope, adjudicated onset, proof of independent encounters, a monitoring benefit, or a policy threshold.

E1 is the primary recorded-code estimand: the earliest valid paired code/date for an additional target domain after d1. E2 is a confirmation-qualified robustness estimand, but its interpretation is gated by observed opportunity. A result supporting E1 but not E2 is evidence of an ascertainment-fragile coded transition, not robust progression. If E2 lacks enough directly supported opportunities, the robust-progression claim is inconclusive rather than converted into a claim from unconfirmed episodes.

- diabetes: ICD-10 E10–E14 or ICD-9 250;
- cardiovascular disease: ICD-10 I20–I25, I50, I60–I69 or ICD-9 410–414, 428, 430–438;
- chronic kidney disease: ICD-10 N18/N19 or ICD-9 585;
- negative control: ICD-10 K35 or ICD-9 540.

A diagnosis is valid only when code and date share the same suffix. Scan every listed suffix, because a bounded read-only sample showed date arrays are not necessarily chronological. Preserve code-only, date-only, invalid-date, duplicate-date, and pre-landmark cells in the audit; none creates an event without a valid same-suffix pair.

Eligibility is age 40–69 inclusive, valid sex, five-source `eid` overlap, valid `d0=assessment.53-0.0` and `d1=assessment.53-1.0` with d0<d1 and 365–3,650 days between them, normalized agreement of main/assessment dates, exactly one target domain at both d0 and d1 with its first paired date no later than d0, no death on/before d1, and finite canonical HbA1c/HDL/triglycerides at assay instances 0 and 1. Primary time zero is d1. Existing-domain duration is (d1 minus first valid domain date)/365.25.

Use d1 HbA1c, triglycerides, HDL and annualized change (x1−x0)/gap-years, reversing HDL so larger values mean worsening. These are assessment-linked changes, not biological slopes or specimen-time trajectories. Delayed entry at d1+365, alternate lags, gaps, and stable-domain definitions are labeled sensitivities.

## E1 and opportunity-aware E2

For E1, identify each absent domain’s earliest valid post-d1 paired date. A unique earliest new domain is `SECOND_DOMAIN_UNIQUE`; simultaneous new domains are `SECOND_DOMAIN_MULTI` with the member set. A same-day death takes precedence, with reverse precedence as sensitivity. Death is absorbing and competing.

For E2, t1k is the first post-d1 paired code/date for absent domain k. A confirmation t2k is the earliest later distinct date for k satisfying 30≤t2k−t1k≤365. Distinct slots on the same date are not independent confirmations. A participant’s E2 event is the earliest confirmation across absent domains; ties are `CONFIRMED_MULTI`; same-day death takes precedence.

The critical observation rule is:

- Compute `C_source` as the descriptive maximum finite date in the diagnosis/date/death arrays. It is not an individual follow-up date and not administrative closure.
- Do not use a participant’s last diagnosis date as a surrogate last observation date.
- A death date is direct evidence of observation at that death date. If death occurs before t2, death is a competing transition; no later confirmation is imputed.
- For an alive participant, classify U(k) (“observed through t1k+365 with no qualifying confirmation”) only if a solver audit identifies a direct, pre-specified observation/closure basis covering t1k+365. The configured metadata currently provides no per-person last-contact field, temporal-column declaration, source-release closure manifest, or validated administrative cutoff, so this criterion may fail for many or all alive episodes.
- If t2k is observed before the relevant observed boundary, classify confirmation. If death occurs before t2k, classify death. If the participant-level opportunity cannot be established through t1k+365, classify `B_UNKNOWN(k)`/right-censored and never U. If a documented source-level closure is later verified, `B_UNKNOWN` can be deterministically split into U versus supported confirmation opportunity.
- Never condition d1 cohort entry on eventual t1/t2. Pending first codes remain part of the d1-origin risk set; boundary-unknown episodes are not negatives.
- A participant with U(k) for one domain remains eligible to be followed for another absent domain’s confirmation; state simplification must preserve this.

The primary E2 output is therefore d1-origin cumulative incidence of an observed confirmation, with death and opportunity-unknown censoring explicitly displayed. A complete-opportunity E2 analysis is secondary and is admissible only for episodes with directly supported opportunity; it cannot be the primary estimand when closure is unverified. If the opportunity audit yields too few complete episodes, E1 can still be reported while robust confirmation-qualified progression is inconclusive.

Both E1 and E2 must report source-boundary-supported horizons only. A five-year estimate or calibration/alert claim requires documented administrative closure; otherwise it is conditional on an explicitly unverified closure assumption.

## Models and substantive alternative

Use the same participants, state definitions, death treatment, time zero, censoring, weights, split, uncertainty, transformations, and locked test for every comparison.

B0 is a simple route/state-specific cause-specific hazard model using d1 HbA1c, triglycerides, HDL, established domain, duration, age, sex, gap, BMI, SBP, DBP, smoking, and center. Fit-frozen restricted cubic splines and fit-defined missingness rules are allowed. E2 B0 generates d1-origin confirmation predictions without selecting future t1/t2.

M_common adds a fit-standardized coordinated deterioration score C from HbA1c, triglycerides, and reverse-HDL change. M_change adds orthogonal discordance contrasts D1 and D2 to B0+C. The primary contrast is M_change−B0; C−B0 asks whether coherent deterioration matters; M_change−M_common asks whether discordance adds information current levels and common burden lose. Any orthogonalization is fit-only and frozen. Route/state interaction claims require support; unsupported discordance is inconclusive.

This is a substantive alternative because the baseline answers whether current levels are sufficient, while C/D can reveal coherent movement or marker-specific discordance that a current-level-only representation loses. A recurrent/attention model is deferred: only two verified assay panels exist, so extra sequence capacity cannot identify a turning point or repair date-proxy and opportunity selection. Reconsider after at least three verified dated panels and evidence of incremental information beyond C/D under the same endpoint checks. A latent error model is deferred because main and biological-samples copies are export duplicates, not independent replicates; it requires assay QC or an external reliability model.

Emit code/date, same-date duplicate, simultaneous-domain, death-tie, confirmation-pair, opportunity, and source-agreement audits.

Report supported transition coefficients and intervals; E1 and E2 state occupancy/cumulative incidence at supported horizons; integrated/time-dependent Brier, calibration, discrimination, restricted mean event-free survival; paired 200-resample participant bootstrap intervals for C−B0, M_change−B0, and M_change−M_common; and 5/10/20% risk operating-point summaries. Thresholds are computational summaries, not approved actions. E2 predictions are d1-origin predictions, never evaluated only among t1/t2 participants.

Mandatory falsification includes d1+365 delayed entry, 0/2/5-year lags, alternate gaps/domain definitions, within-stratum change permutation, K35/540 negative control, source-concordant and main-only assay sensitivities, E1 versus E2, reversed death precedence, source-closure audit, opportunity-stratified tables, and a check that B_UNKNOWN is never U and death while pending is not ordinary censoring.

Supportive evidence requires at least 20 events overall and 5 locked-test events, route/state support (at least 10 fit/tuning and 3 locked-test events where route claims are made), stable joins and ties, adequate weighting positivity/ESS, a worsening-direction interval excluding the null, and a locked paired performance improvement in Brier/calibration or at least 10% relative net benefit at a frozen threshold. It must survive completeness/source-concordance and lag sensitivities and show no comparable negative-control/permutation signal. E2 support additionally requires enough directly supported opportunities and an explicit opportunity-distribution audit; E1-only support is not robust-progression support.

Adverse means no incremental information on a supported endpoint, worse locked performance, disappearance after duration/selection/source adjustment, a negative-control/permutation signal, or E1 improvement that disappears under opportunity-supported E2. Inconclusive means sparse events/routes, failed positivity, unstable joins/ties, unverified opportunity, substantial B_UNKNOWN episodes, poor convergence/calibration, intervals spanning meaningful benefit and no benefit, or materially divergent timing/selection/phenotype sensitivities. This is not a claim that metabolic biology is absent.

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes/headers, canonical roles, joins, flow, code/date pairing, event/tie logic, opportunity classification, model fits, locked predictions, uncertainty and falsification. Clinical onset, independent encounters, code validity, treatment/adherence, causality, monitoring benefit, transportability, nonreturner generalization and policy utility require adjudication, expert review, unavailable evidence, or another study.
