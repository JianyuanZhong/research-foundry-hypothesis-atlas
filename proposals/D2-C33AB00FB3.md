# Fibroblast presence modulates JAK inhibitor transcript response in synovial macrophage cocultures: context-dependent drug efficacy in rheumatoid arthritis

## Seed
D2-T1 (Context-specific intervention response)

## Domain
Therapeutic Target Prioritization

## Parent
[prior hypothesis] (D2-T1 seed: Does the intervention-associated transcript response depend on macrophage-fibroblast context?)

## Question
Does the transcript response to the JAK inhibitor A77 (and other anti-inflammatory drugs) in synovial tissue cocultures depend on the presence of fibroblast-like synoviocytes (FLS), as measured by the difference in drug response between macrophage+T cell (MT) and macrophage+T cell+FLS (MTF) contexts? Specifically, do JAK/STAT pathway genes show attenuated drug response in the presence of fibroblasts, suggesting fibroblast-mediated compensation for JAK inhibition?

## Background
Kuo et al. 2019 [K1] defined HBEGF+ inflammatory macrophages in RA synovial tissue using single-cell RNA sequencing. They showed that "most medications used to treat RA patients targeted HBEGF+ inflammatory macrophages; however, in some cases, medication redirected them into a state that is not expected to resolve inflammation." The GSE95588 dataset from this study contains bulk RNA-seq of synovial cocultures with systematically varied cellular composition (M, MT, MTF) and drug treatments.

Wei & Xu 2026 [K2] proposed that "failed resolution" in difficult-to-treat RA is driven by a self-sustaining stromal-myeloid circuit where fibroblasts and macrophages reciprocally reinforce each other's pathogenic states. This framework predicts that drugs targeting one arm of the circuit (e.g., JAK signaling in macrophages) may be buffered by the other arm (fibroblast-derived signals).

Pascual-García et al. 2026 [K3] reviewed evidence that JAK inhibitors exert broad effects on RA-FLS function, "likely reflecting their ability to block multiple cytokine-dependent signaling pathways." This suggests that fibroblasts are not passive bystanders but active participants in JAK inhibitor response.

## Hypothesis
The transcript response to the JAK inhibitor A77 in synovial cocultures is significantly different in the presence of fibroblasts (MTF) compared to macrophages+T cells alone (MT), with JAK/STAT pathway genes showing attenuated drug response in MTF. This would indicate that fibroblast-derived signals compensate for JAK inhibition at the transcriptional level, potentially explaining why JAK inhibitors may be less effective in fibroblast-rich joint environments.

## Discovery-Level Evidence (This Episode)
A feasibility analysis of GSE95588_d1d2d3d4 (70 samples, 23,710 genes, 4 donors) revealed:

1. **Structural finding**: No M-context drug treatments exist. The contrast is MTF vs MT (fibroblast effect), not M vs MTF.
2. **A77 (JAK inhibitor, 3 donors)**: 1,573 genes with context-dependent response (p<0.05). JAK1 (p=0.007), TYK2 (p=0.014), and STAT5A (p=0.014) show significantly attenuated drug response in MTF vs MT.
3. **IL1B and PTGS2 context reversal**: A77 *induces* IL1B in MT (resp=+1.97) but *suppresses* it in MTF (resp=−0.21), a dramatic qualitative reversal (contrast=−2.19, p=0.08, n=3).
4. **aIL6 (anti-IL-6, 2 donors)**: Fewer context-dependent genes (549 vs 1,573), no pathway genes significant - consistent with more specific target engagement.
5. **Tofacitinib**: Underpowered (only 1 donor with paired data).

## Analysis Plan (Full Study)
1. **CPM normalization** → log2(CPM+1)
2. **Per-sample drug response**: log2CPM_drug − log2CPM_vehicle (same donor, same context)
3. **Context contrast**: response_MTF − response_MT (per donor, per gene)
4. **Per-gene one-sample t-test** on contrast vs 0 across donors
5. **FDR correction** (Benjamini-Hochberg) across all genes
6. **Pathway enrichment**: JAK/STAT, IL-6, NF-κB pathways
7. **Cross-dataset validation**: Check if top context-dependent genes from GSE95588 show similar patterns in E-MTAB-8316
8. **Drug comparison**: A77 vs aIL6 vs Dex vs other drugs (specificity of context dependence)

## Falsification Criteria
- **Supportive**: ≥5 JAK/STAT or IL-6 pathway genes show FDR<0.1 context-dependent response in the predicted direction (attenuated in MTF); effect consistent across ≥2 donors; A77 shows more context-dependent genes than aIL6
- **Adverse**: No pathway genes reach FDR<0.1; or context-dependent genes are not enriched for JAK/STAT or IL-6 pathways; or the effect is inconsistent across donors; or aIL6 shows equal or greater context dependence than A77
- **Inconclusive**: Fewer than 2 donors have paired MT/MTF data for a given drug; or effect size is small (|contrast| < 0.5) despite statistical significance; or FDR correction eliminates all significant genes

## Limitations
1. **Donor mapping**: donor_id=null in design.json; P1-P4 prefixes used as donor identifiers based on column naming convention
2. **Small sample size**: 2-3 donors per drug; paired t-test with n=3 has low power
3. **Bulk RNA-seq**: MT and MTF are bulk measurements; cannot distinguish macrophage vs fibroblast transcriptional changes
4. **No M-context drug data**: The contrast is MTF vs MT, not M vs MTF
5. **Tofacitinib underpowered**: Only 1 donor with paired data
6. **Vehicle control is unstimulated**: Drug response includes both drug effect and stimulation effect
7. **No protein-level validation**: Transcript changes do not necessarily reflect functional protein changes
8. **Ex vivo system**: May not fully recapitulate in vivo synovial microenvironment
