# A separable all-randomized pathway estimand for vasopressor implementation, rescue, death, exit, and measurement error

## Decision question and substantive advance over the selected parent

The future decision is whether norepinephrine-first (NE-first) and vasopressin-first (VP-first) discontinuation strategies can be compared with a locked EHR endpoint without clinically consequential endpoint distortion. `[prior hypothesis]` correctly retained every randomized patient and correctly defined measurement error as paired EHR-versus-reference disagreement rather than as nonimplementation. Its remaining weakness is that its decision-facing `strategy-failure` risk difference still adds unlike clinical events: failure to execute the assigned strategy, rescue after successful cessation, death, and ICU exit. Even with perfect measurement, that scalar can be clinically ambiguous. A strategy with less rescue but more death, or less implementation but more early exit, could have the same composite rate as its comparator. Measurement agreement cannot make such cancellation clinically interpretable.

This child therefore makes the primary clinical estimand a **separable all-randomized pathway vector**, not a scalar strategy-failure outcome. Every randomized first patient remains in every denominator. The components are:

1. assigned-strategy implementation by 120 minutes;
2. intent-qualified post-cessation rescue per randomized patient;
3. death before completion of the assigned pathway, with pre- and post-cessation timing retained;
4. alive ICU exit before completion, with pre- and post-cessation timing retained; and
5. stable six-hour completion off the assigned drug.

EHR-versus-reference disagreement is a different layer and is never itself a clinical disposition. It is estimated for each frozen component and for the parent's unchanged binary composite guardrail. The parent’s exact paired risk-difference (RD) distortion identity, strict simultaneous 95% bound below 0.025, fixed 1,200-patient pilot, fixed 5,920-patient confirmation, immutable prospective rules, frozen adverse MIMIC evidence, and noncausal scope are retained. No component may be collapsed into a favorable treatment claim, and no weighting of rescue against death, exit, or implementation is estimated from the data.

The confirmatory hypotheses are deliberately narrow and falsifiable:

* **unchanged guardrail:** the simultaneous 95% upper bound on absolute EHR-versus-reference distortion of the locked legacy all-randomized composite RD is strictly below 0.025; and
* **separable measurement claim:** under one simultaneous 95% family, the upper bound on absolute distortion is strictly below 0.025 for each prespecified pathway component RD.

The first hypothesis preserves the selected parent’s fixed operating characteristic and cannot be rescued by the new decomposition. The second prevents aggregate agreement or cancellation from validating an EHR system that mismeasures one clinically distinct part of the pathway. The study does not test whether either stopping strategy improves survival, ICU discharge, rescue, or any overall patient-centered outcome.

## Strongest evidence currently supported and the unresolved claim

All inherited MIMIC results are frozen as adverse feasibility evidence. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, observed actions were 211 NE-first (3.49%) and 167 VP-first (2.76%), both below the frozen 5% positivity gate; 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort contains 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate failures. These groups are observational, sparse, post-baseline selected, and nonexchangeable.

Among 73 failures in 136 determinate episodes, 59 include a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 are restart-only. MCS status is unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models gave negligible Brier improvement, approximately 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1, both below the frozen 0.01 gate, with adverse later transport; no model or subgroup advances.

The complete transaction audit used all 137 inherited episodes and all rows remaining after declared stay, item, and time filters. It reproduced all endpoint boundaries and all 59 raw restarts. Five symmetric reconstructions invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices. The inherited shared-chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices, 10 NE-first and 3 VP-first, all at `Paused` boundaries. These are EHR transaction-instability findings only. They do not establish false physical pump delivery, clinician intent, or rescue.

Thus the strongest supported claim is that the EHR-side endpoint is computable, restart-dominated, sparse in observed stopping groups, and materially sensitive to transaction rules. MIMIC does not contain randomization, protocol permissibility or assignment, pump-native delivered-rate truth, an escrowed cross-system clock map, adjudicated rescue intent, or reliable evidence that MCS is absent. The unresolved claim requires a new prospective randomized and fully verified cohort: whether the locked EHR system measures each clinically distinct all-randomized pathway component, and the unchanged legacy composite, with less than 0.025 arm-RD distortion.

## Population, randomization, and immutable clocks

All operating rules below are inherited unchanged.

The program includes at least 12 hospitals, at least two adult ICUs per hospital where available, and at least two pump–EHR interface families. Contracted expansion to at most 24 hospitals may occur before confirmation. A configuration is the immutable tuple `(site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major version, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version, clock-map method/version)`. A site or configuration added or changed after confirmatory first-patient enrollment is separately versioned and cannot silently pool with the locked mixture.

Each configuration first completes a 50-episode nonrandomized technical run-in. At least 95% must have unique patient–pump–channel–drug linkage, complete pump export from two hours before candidate assignment through pathway closure, and an escrowed clock map; at least 90% must have contemporaneous material suitable for blinded intent adjudication. One repeat run-in with 50 wholly new episodes is allowed after repair. A second failure excludes the configuration. Run-in episodes enter no trial estimand.

Eligibility is frozen before assignment and cannot depend on implementation, actual first-stopped drug, later rescue, death, exit, capture, adherence, or EHR/reference agreement:

1. age at least 18 and alive in an adult ICU;
2. linked pump-delivered NE and VP concurrently for at least six continuous hours;
3. at the start of the final 30 minutes before randomization, NE greater than 0 and no more than 0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP greater than 0 and no more than 0.04 U/min, with neither increased during those 30 minutes;
4. no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the preceding two hours;
5. at least three valid MAPs spanning at least three of the four preceding 30-minute bins, including the final bin, with median MAP at least 65 mmHg and minimum at least 55 mmHg; and
6. before assignment, the treating team records that either protocolized first-stop strategy is clinically permissible.

The first eligible randomization per person is the sole confirmatory unit. Randomization time `tr` is immutable. Assignment is 1:1 in configuration-stratified permuted blocks: `Z=0` NE-first and `Z=1` VP-first. Actual first-stopped drug is post-randomization and never replaces `Z`.

The assigned drug should cease within 120 minutes. Reference qualifying cessation `t0_R` is the first linked pump-native delivered-rate time in `[tr,tr+120 min]` at which the assigned drug falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and remains below threshold continuously for five minutes while the comparator remains above its threshold. A programmed rate without linked delivery is insufficient. Nonassigned-drug cessation first is crossover. Both threshold crossings within five minutes are simultaneous. A later assigned cessation cannot erase crossover or simultaneous implementation deviation.

Implementation is observed until `min(tr+120 min, death, ICU exit)`. After reference assigned cessation, the pathway is observed over `(t0_R,min[t0_R+6 h,death,ICU exit]]`. Pump export, vital status, and ICU location are also retained through `t0_R+6 h` after an earlier rescue so a subsequent death or exit is not erased, although the mutually exclusive first-disposition state remains rescue. Each source uses a prospectively escrowed affine clock map with offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving rule, fitting rows, residuals, and version. Its ambiguity band is the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual, capped at 60 seconds. A configuration needing a wider band fails. An uncertainty interval crossing an ordering, five-minute, 120-minute, or six-hour boundary is indeterminate and is never rounded toward concordance.

## Reference and EHR pathway states for every randomized patient

Each patient receives one immutable reference implementation state `I_R` and one EHR implementation state `I_E`:

* `AQ`: assigned-drug qualifying cessation by 120 minutes;
* `XO`: nonassigned drug qualifies first while assigned drug remains on;
* `SIM`: both qualify within five minutes;
* `NI_OVERRIDE`: timestamped clinician override or aborted protocol with no qualifying cessation;
* `NI_NO_STOP`: no qualifying cessation and no override while alive and in ICU at 120 minutes;
* `EXIT_PRE`: alive ICU exit before qualifying cessation;
* `DEATH_PRE`: death before qualifying cessation or the implementation boundary; or
* `IND_I`: linkage, export, clock, boundary, or other uncertainty can alter the state.

Reference states use linked delivered pump rates, trial implementation records, vital status, ICU location, and mapped source clocks. EHR states use only the locked EHR intervals, EHR-visible assignment/override codes, and EHR death/ICU feeds. `NI_OVERRIDE` is allowed only from a prospectively required timestamped reason code, never retrospectively inferred from narrative.

For corresponding-source `AQ`, post-cessation state `O_R` or `O_E` is exactly one of:

* `RESCUE`: the assigned drug returns to threshold for at least five continuous minutes before closure; reference rescue additionally requires two blinded adjudicators to classify resumption as deliberate treatment or prevention of worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion;
* `DEATH_POST`: death before rescue and before six-hour completion;
* `EXIT_POST_OFF`: alive ICU exit before rescue and six hours, with complete capture through exit and assigned drug still below threshold;
* `NO_EVENT_6H`: six complete hours with no rescue or death and no earlier ICU exit; or
* `IND_O`: linkage, export, clock, intent, death, exit, or boundary uncertainty can alter the state.

If the corresponding implementation state is not `AQ`, `O` is exactly `SNA`, structural non-applicability rather than missingness. Rescue followed by death keeps first disposition `RESCUE` and separately sets the subsequent-death flag. Death before rescue is `DEATH_POST`. Equivalent rules apply to alive exit. No patient is deleted and no later event overwrites the implementation state.

A mutually exclusive pathway disposition `C` is derived separately in the reference (`C_R`) and EHR (`C_E`) systems:

* `IMPLEMENTATION_DEVIATION` for `I in {XO,SIM,NI_OVERRIDE,NI_NO_STOP}`;
* `DEATH_PRE` for `I=DEATH_PRE`;
* `EXIT_PRE` for `I=EXIT_PRE`;
* `RESCUE_AFTER_AQ` for `I=AQ,O=RESCUE`;
* `DEATH_POST_AQ` for `I=AQ,O=DEATH_POST`;
* `EXIT_POST_AQ_OFF` for `I=AQ,O=EXIT_POST_OFF`;
* `STABLE_OFF_6H` for `I=AQ,O=NO_EVENT_6H`; or
* `INDETERMINATE` for `I=IND_I` or `I=AQ,O=IND_O`.

This partition is not an ordinal outcome. In particular, `EXIT_POST_AQ_OFF` is not declared equivalent to six-hour success, `EXIT_PRE` is not declared a clinical failure, and death is not exchanged against rescue. Those value judgments require clinical governance and a later trial protocol.

## Separable all-randomized clinical estimand

For each determinate reference component `k` and arm `z`, define `p_zk=P(H_Rk=1|Z=z)`, where every `H_Rk` has all randomized patients in its denominator:

* `H_impl=1{I_R=AQ}`: assigned strategy implemented by 120 minutes;
* `H_rescue=1{C_R=RESCUE_AFTER_AQ}`: intent-qualified rescue after assigned cessation per randomized patient;
* `H_death_pre=1{C_R=DEATH_PRE}`;
* `H_death_post=1{C_R=DEATH_POST_AQ}`;
* `H_exit_pre=1{C_R=EXIT_PRE}`;
* `H_exit_post=1{C_R=EXIT_POST_AQ_OFF}`; and
* `H_stable=1{C_R=STABLE_OFF_6H}`.

The clinical estimand is the vector `Delta_R,k=p_1k-p_0k`, reported with exact/randomization-compatible confidence intervals and the full arm-specific pathway distribution. `H_rescue` is deliberately all-randomized; the conditional rescue proportion among `I_R=AQ` is a transparent post-randomization diagnostic only. The `I_R=AQ` rate must always be shown next to rescue so a low rescue burden produced by nonimplementation cannot be called favorable. Pre- and post-cessation death and exit are never merged in the primary table. Also report any death and any alive exit through the six-hour pathway horizon, including subsequent death after rescue, as safety/disposition flags rather than as mutually exclusive pathway states.

No scalar treatment ranking is primary. The legacy composite is retained only as the selected parent’s immutable **measurement guardrail**:

`Y_R=1` for `I_R in {XO,SIM,NI_OVERRIDE,NI_NO_STOP,EXIT_PRE,DEATH_PRE}` or `I_R=AQ,O_R in {RESCUE,DEATH_POST}`;

`Y_R=0` for `I_R=AQ,O_R in {NO_EVENT_6H,EXIT_POST_OFF}`;

and indeterminate otherwise, with identical EHR mapping. Its clinical coding remains subject to pretrial panel ratification, but a pass cannot be interpreted as evidence that its unlike components are clinically exchangeable. It exists so this child does not relax the already selected 0.025 distortion falsification gate or its fixed-N design.

## EHR-versus-reference disagreement and exact RD-distortion inference

Measurement is a paired relation between EHR and reference labels, not a patient disposition. For the legacy composite and for each component `k`, define in arm `z`, over all randomized patients,

`f_zk=P(H_Ek=1,H_Rk=0|Z=z)` and `g_zk=P(H_Ek=0,H_Rk=1|Z=z)`.

For the legacy composite substitute `Y_E,Y_R`. These are false-positive and false-negative probabilities per randomized patient, not one minus specificity or sensitivity. The exact arm-rate bias and randomized RD distortion are

`d_zk=q_zk-p_zk=f_zk-g_zk`,

`b_k=(Delta_E,k-Delta_R,k)=f_1k-g_1k-f_0k+g_0k`.

### Unchanged legacy guardrail

For `Y`, retain the parent’s four two-sided Clopper–Pearson intervals, each with `alpha=0.0125`. Their Cartesian product has at least 95% simultaneous Bonferroni coverage. Exact conservative limits are

`b_L=L(f_1)-U(g_1)-U(f_0)+L(g_0)` and

`b_U=U(f_1)-L(g_1)-L(f_0)+U(g_0)`.

`B_U=max(|b_L|,|b_U|)` must be strictly below 0.025; equality fails. Nothing in the component analysis can override failure.

### Simultaneous separable-component family

Let `K=7` for implementation, rescue, pre-death, post-death, pre-exit, post-exit, and stable completion. For each of the `4K=28` all-denominator error probabilities, construct a two-sided Clopper–Pearson interval with `alpha*=0.05/(4K)`. By Bonferroni, all 28 intervals cover simultaneously with probability at least 0.95. For each `k`, use the same linear endpoints above and define `B_Uk=max(|b_Lk|,|b_Uk|)`. A separable measurement claim requires `B_Uk<0.025` for every component. This is stricter than the parent and deliberately prevents a well-measured high-frequency component from hiding poor rescue, death, or exit measurement.

The 5,920-person sample size was designed for the unchanged guardrail, not to promise that all seven stricter familywise component bounds will pass. The sample size, alpha rules, endpoints, and gate cannot be adapted after pilot or confirmation data. If component precision is insufficient, the separable claim fails under the fixed rules; no post-lock enrollment extension is allowed.

### Indeterminate labels and exact finite optimization

Every patient is retained in a paired `C_E x C_R` table with the seven determinate states plus `INDETERMINATE`. The frozen binary mappings derive the legacy and component-specific `3 x 3` tables. Unknown labels are allocated jointly as nonnegative integer counts compatible with the observed aggregate cells. For every feasible completion, compute all relevant `f`, `g`, exact intervals, and distortion bounds. Report the maximum legacy `B_U` and each maximum `B_Uk`, with an integer completion witness. Separate favorable completion for one metric and unfavorable completion for another cannot be combined into a claimed pass. Aggregate sparse convolution/branch-and-bound is used; person-level enumeration is permitted only as an oracle when at most eight labels are unknown. A timeout, missing witness, disagreement with exhaustive tiny-table tests, or use of non-integer/marginally incompatible imputations is inconclusive and cannot validate the endpoint.

Indeterminacy above 2% overall, above 3% in an arm-by-configuration cell with at least 50 patients, or differing by more than two percentage points between arms is operationally adverse even if exact bounds pass. Missing EHR-negative verification or coding loss of capture as no event invalidates the analysis.

## Clinical implementation diagnostics and cancellation safeguards

Reference implementation is strategy performance, not endpoint accuracy. Report by `Z`: every `I_R` category, time to first attempt, actual first-stopped drug, deviation reason, `t0_R`, every `C_R` category, subsequent death after rescue, and each all-randomized component RD. Conditional rescue among immutable `I_R=AQ` is clearly labeled selected post-randomization description and never called ITT.

The inherited implementation gates remain unchanged: assigned cessation below 60% in either arm, crossover/simultaneous cessation above 15%, arm difference in assigned cessation above 10 percentage points, or any independent safety-board concern is adverse for strategy feasibility or interpretability but is not EHR measurement error unless `C_E` disagrees with `C_R`. Excellent implementation cannot rescue a distortion failure.

Report the complete `I_E x I_R` and `C_E x C_R` tables by assignment and configuration, death/exit timestamp agreement, rescue timing, one-to-one pump/EHR matching, and each state-pair contribution to every `b_k` and the legacy `b`. The parent’s cancellation rules remain: even if the legacy bound passes, a common component with at least 100 reference events per arm and sensitivity below 0.80, a state contribution with absolute arm-differential error above 0.010, or sign reversal of estimated legacy `b` after removing a component with more than 20 discordances is adverse. The new familywise component gate is additional and cannot be replaced by pooled agreement, overall accuracy, PPV, NPV, or conditional sensitivity/specificity.

## Immutable two-stage operation

### Independent pilot

After technical run-in, enroll exactly 1,200 consecutive first randomized patients, 600 per arm, over at most 12 months. Every patient receives full pump/reference and EHR verification. Pilot patients are excluded from confirmatory accuracy, clinical, and transport estimands and cannot be pooled later.

Before pilot error tables are released, require: at least 100 randomized patients/month over the final three pilot months with a one-sided 95% Poisson lower bound at least 85/month; at least 12 active hospitals and two interface families; no hospital above 15%; at least 98% complete linkage/export/clock/vital-status records in each arm; at least 95% determinate reference/EHR state pairs in each arm; adjudicator raw agreement at least 85% and at least one of Gwet AC1 or kappa at least 0.70; and no safety/equipoise stop.

After those operational results are frozen, an independent pilot statistician may release paired errors and recommend only `stop` or `proceed unchanged`. Confirmation does not launch if the unchanged conservative legacy completion bound is at least 0.060, if either arm’s one-sided 95% upper bound exceeds 0.025 for either all-denominator legacy false-positive or false-negative probability, if component cancellation is adverse, if any newly separated death/exit/rescue component shows an observed absolute arm-differential measurement error at least 0.025, or if strategy implementation makes the pathway clinically uninterpretable. These are pilot futility/safety findings, not endpoint validation. The pilot cannot change sample size, alphas, thresholds, state mappings, windows, eligibility, configuration pooling, or analysis. Any change requires a new protocol, run-in, and pilot.

### Fixed confirmation

Enroll exactly 5,920 new first randomized patients, 2,960 per arm, under one common start and database lock, or close unsuccessfully at 48 months. No outcome-, denominator-, adherence-, event-, component-, arm-, or accuracy-dependent stopping; no sample-size re-estimation; no post-lock extension.

The inherited exact no-simulation design calculation remains the justification for the unchanged legacy guardrail. Under planning false-positive and false-negative probabilities of 0.005 per arm, exact binomial count-tail envelopes at tail 0.0125 and an eight-event Bonferroni assurance lower bound of 0.90 yield error counts 7–24 at `n=2,960`; the worst legacy confidence envelope is `[-0.0249929186,0.0249929186]`. At `n=2,959`, the bound is 0.0250013417 and fails. This planning alternative is not evidence from MIMIC, not an acceptance threshold, and not assurance for the seven-component family.

Confirmation requires 123.3 patients/month over 48 months. Before launch, signed contracts must project at least 130/month and the final three observed pilot months must average at least 100/month. The pilot lower accrual bound of 85/month is not by itself sufficient. Failure to complete 5,920 under the fixed lock is inconclusive.

## EHR algorithm, adjudication, and matching

The EHR algorithm is frozen before pilot enrollment. Local NE mappings corresponding to MIMIC item 221906 retain mcg/kg/min or divide mcg/min by a contemporaneous valid weight of 30–300 kg; rates above 5 mcg/kg/min are rejected. VP mappings corresponding to item 222315 retain U/min or divide U/hour by 60; rates above 0.2 U/min are rejected. Positive intervals require `endtime>starttime`. Primary intervals cannot be repaired using `statusdescription`, transaction chains, `storetime`, caregiver, open-bag, transfer, programmed-rate, or order fields; these are diagnostics only.

Two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators review pump and monitor traces, workflow events, cointerventions, and timestamped intent evidence, but not `C_E`, EHR/reference error tables, assignment-specific accuracy, or each other’s labels. A third blinded adjudicator resolves disagreement. Technical/procedural pause, bag or syringe replacement, channel exchange, transfer continuation, mistaken-stop correction, reconciliation, and documentation fragmentation without interrupted delivery are not rescue. Unresolved intent produces `IND_O`.

Secondary event matching is one-to-one maximum-cardinality/minimum-total-absolute-lag matching within person, randomization, drug, and window at locked ±10 minutes. Ties use earlier pump time, then earlier mapped EHR time, then immutable row ID. Matching never changes an immutable state.

## Recurrence and transport

Later eligible episodes are collected under identical rules but never enlarge the first-patient denominator. A recurrence requires a new six-hour dual-drug baseline and starts at least 24 hours after prior `t0`, or after prior randomization if no `t0` exists. It retains original assignment unless separately approved re-randomization occurs before a new eligibility lock. Recurrent pathway vectors and measurement errors use assignment/configuration-stratified patient-cluster bootstrap and diagnostic marginal GEE and cannot rescue primary failure.

No hospital, configuration, or interface family may provide more than 20% of either arm. At least eight hospitals and both interface families must each contribute at least 100 patients per arm. Report configuration-specific pathway transitions, hierarchical multinomial summaries, prediction intervals, and leave-one-hospital/family-out exact legacy and component completion bounds. Every leave-out mixture must retain legacy `B_U<0.025` and all component `B_Uk<0.025`. A configuration with at least 500 patients per arm must have legacy `B_U<0.05`; sparse configurations are inconclusive. Material assignment-by-configuration interaction or adjusted state-error range above 0.10 is adverse. Support is restricted to the locked enumerated configurations and pathway rules.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive:** all 5,920 new confirmatory patients are retained; the worst feasible completion has unchanged legacy `B_U<0.025`; every simultaneously covered component has `B_Uk<0.025`; implementation, cancellation, indeterminacy, adjudication, clock, configuration concentration, recurrence, and all leave-out gates pass. This supports only that the locked EHR system measures each specified all-randomized pathway-component RD and the legacy composite RD within the stated distortion in the enrolled configuration mixture. It does not show that either strategy is favorable. A later identical trial must report the full vector and widen each EHR-based RD interval by its validated distortion bound.

**Adverse:** completed confirmation has legacy `B_U>=0.025`, any component `B_Uk>=0.025`, arm-differential death/exit/rescue misclassification, cancellation, or harmful configuration heterogeneity. These falsify the corresponding endpoint-measurement claim even if the EHR treatment contrast appears favorable. Separately, assigned implementation below 60%, excessive crossover/simultaneous cessation, arm imbalance, more rescue, more death, delayed exit, or a safety-board concern is adverse clinical/strategy evidence and must be reported in its own component; it is not relabeled as measurement error.

**Inconclusive:** governance does not ratify the pathway definitions or legacy guardrail; randomization is not permissible; technical run-in, pump linkage, clock, intent adjudication, MCS ascertainment, determinacy, or exact optimization fails; contracted accrual cannot plausibly complete the immutable sample; 5,920 patients are not completed by 48 months; or a later trial changes population, endpoint, strategy, death/exit handling, configuration mixture, or clocks. Under the fixed operating rules, completed confirmation whose required strict bound does not pass is adverse rather than converted post hoc to a precision-only success. Inconclusive never supports validity, safety, benefit, or noninferiority.

The key advance is that no clinical conclusion rests on a scalar that trades nonimplementation, rescue, death, and ICU exit against one another. The all-randomized pathway vector keeps randomization intact and exposes each mechanism, while separate paired EHR/reference discordance tables identify measurement failure. The exact legacy distortion gate and all fixed prospective operating rules remain immutable; the added simultaneous component gates make the successor stricter, not easier to pass.

## Verifier requirements and noncomputable claims

The verifier must reproduce eligibility before `tr`, one primary unit per person, retention under `Z`, every immutable `I/O/C` state, structural `SNA`, the all-randomized component indicators and denominators, complete EHR-positive/negative/indeterminate verification, legacy and component paired tables, integer-compatible joint completions and witnesses, exact Clopper–Pearson intervals, both simultaneous alpha allocations, all distortion bounds, pilot exclusion, `n=2,960` versus `2,959` arithmetic for the unchanged guardrail, common confirmation lock, recurrence separation, and transport gates.

Required adversarial tests include: low rescue caused by nonimplementation cannot be called favorable; less rescue with more death cannot be collapsed to benefit; early alive exit cannot be called success or failure outside its own state; rescue followed by death preserves both first disposition and subsequent death; crossover followed by assigned cessation stays crossover; excellent pooled composite agreement with poor rescue or death measurement fails; high nonimplementation with perfect EHR/reference agreement may pass measurement but remains adverse implementation; selecting `AQ` patients may be shown only as conditional diagnosis; different best-case imputations cannot create a joint pass; exact boundary/duplicate/spanning-interval/clock-ambiguity cases remain indeterminate when appropriate; pilot reuse, adaptive `N`, arm-specific lock, or endpoint-dependent extension invalidates confirmation; and no supportive measurement output can be described as treatment benefit, safety, efficacy, or noninferiority.

Computation can establish row accounting, deterministic state derivation from frozen inputs, exact bounds, sample-size arithmetic, and whether a written conclusion follows from outputs. It cannot ratify clinical permissibility or the lack of scalar weighting, infer physical delivery from EHR, resolve rescue intent without expert adjudication, establish absence of MCS, judge equipoise or safety, transport to changed configurations, or determine which discontinuation strategy patients should receive. Those stronger claims require clinical governance, independent safety review, the missing prospective modalities, and a separate appropriately powered randomized clinical study.
