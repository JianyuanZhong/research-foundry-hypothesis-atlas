# Actionability- and supersession-audited route selection for pulmonary discharge handoff

## Decision question and independent repair

The frozen question is whether a pulmonary recommendation opportunity can be assigned to `AUTO`, clinician `RECON`ciliation, or `DEFER` without allowing a documentation-only route to bypass an unresolved actionability or a later discharge plan. The proposed experiment does not ask whether the recommendation is guideline-concordant, whether the patient benefits, or whether anyone actually receives or follows it. It asks a narrower decision question that is both clinically consequential and computable from the available record:

> **Among the frozen first-eligible pulmonary opportunities, how often does the emitted route belong to the conservative set of routes independently adjudicated as operationally permissible, after accounting for whether the recommendation is sufficiently specified to act on and whether an explicit discharge plan supersedes or conflicts with it?**

The targeted hypothesis is:

> **H1 (route-selection hypothesis):** With the inherited exact atom, chronology, route rules, source-bound canonical discharge artifact, outcome-blind controls, and positive-probability finite-population sample held fixed, a route selector that emits `AUTO` only for an explicitly actionable, non-superseded, unconflicted unit, `RECON` for actionable but competing or superseded content, and `DEFER` for unavailable/unknown/unsafe content will achieve high conservative route admissibility without increasing missed unsafe or unresolved admissions relative to the inherited selector.

This hypothesis is falsifiable by independent sealed panel labels. It is not a claim that a panel has established clinical appropriateness or that any route was delivered.

## Supported evidence, unresolved claim, and substantive advance

The local MIMIC snapshot supports admission and departure times, subject linkage, pre-departure service chronology, radiology reports and addenda with chart and storage times, a structured radiology-detail reconstruction, discharge summaries with storage times, and discharge-detail fields. The strongest supported claim is therefore a retrospective, stored-documentation experiment with a well-defined information boundary. The data do not show notification, viewing, responsible clinician, interface rendering, transmission, order/referral/scheduling, outside care, patient preference, completion, safety, benefit, or causal effects.

Existing inherited work can test atom extraction, route reproducibility, minority-unit precedence, timing, pooled/domain heterogeneity, and prospective-silent packet readiness. It does not independently answer whether an otherwise reproducible `AUTO` output is sufficiently specified for action or whether an explicit plan in the discharge artifact supersedes the radiology recommendation. The substantive advance is to make those two latent failure modes pre-label adjudication panels and to turn the decision into a three-way route-selection estimand rather than treating every extracted recommendation as equally actionable or treating disagreement as a generic engineering failure.

## Independent actionability panel

This panel tests documentation sufficiency, not clinical appropriateness. Two independently assigned reviewers, followed by a locked adjudicator for disagreements, inspect the reciprocal radiology chain and its structured detail reconstruction at `D`, plus permitted admission/death/disposition fields under the inherited role policy. They do not see the discharge plan text, route, `B`, terminal-service domain, or other panel labels. They label every frozen recommendation unit using a fixed rubric:

* `A_ACTIONABLE`: exact target/lesion identity, modality/exam, unconditional action, and due interval/date are explicit and internally consistent; the recommendation is present in a source component available by `D`; no unresolved negation, hypothetical, history-only, optional/conditional language, or unit-boundary ambiguity remains.
* `A_NONACTIONABLE`: the text explicitly negates follow-up, is conditional/optional, historical or hypothetical, or lacks a required atom component under the frozen dictionary. This is a documentation state, not evidence that no clinical action was indicated.
* `A_UNKNOWN`: missing/late/conflicting source, unreadable text, unresolved reciprocal chain, unresolved coreference/negation, or reviewer disagreement that cannot be resolved from the permitted view.

Reviewers record only span offsets/field names, atom components present/missing, availability status, and a reason code. They may not infer size, malignancy risk, smoking, life expectancy, guideline appropriateness, patient preference, or clinician responsibility. Panel disagreements remain `U_A` until adjudication; an unresolvable `U_A` is adverse-compatible with `A_UNKNOWN` and cannot support `AUTO`.

## Independent superseding-plan panel

A second pair of reviewers, with a separate adjudicator and separate random assignment, inspects the same frozen radiology atom view and the canonical DS plus ordered discharge-detail view. The panel is blinded to actionability labels, route, domain, sampling stratum, and outcomes. It assesses whether the discharge artifact explicitly carries forward, changes, cancels, or conflicts with the radiology plan. It uses only evidence available by `D`; a DS stored after `D` is `S_UNKNOWN` for the primary estimand.

For each unit, label one mutually exclusive state:

* `S_CONFIRM`: DS explicitly repeats the same target, modality, and due interval/date, or explicitly links its plan to the radiology recommendation without changing it.
* `S_NONE`: no explicit competing or superseding plan is present in the DS. This is not proof of absence of an outside plan or proof of delivery.
* `S_SUPERSEDES`: DS explicitly cancels/replaces the radiology action, changes its modality or due interval/date, or documents an explicit no-follow-up/alternative plan for the same target.
* `S_CONFLICT`: DS contains a contemporaneous, incompatible actionable plan without a determinate precedence, or the two artifacts disagree in a way the rubric cannot resolve.
* `S_UNKNOWN`: unavailable/late/missing/contradictory DS, missing linkage, unreadable text, unresolved coreference, or panel disagreement.

A supersession is recorded only with explicit target linkage and an extractable changed action; absence, generic “follow up with doctor,” or a non-specific problem-list mention cannot be called supersession. `S_SUPERSEDES` does not mean that the new plan is clinically better or appropriate. The panel records source spans, note IDs, storetimes, target-link evidence, old/new modality and interval, and precedence reason. If the DS is unavailable, the panel cannot use a later `D+` note to convert the primary state.

## Route-selection estimand and locked decision rule

Let `r_i` be the inherited emitted route for admission `i` after all units and `G` are evaluated (`AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`). For primary reporting collapse `TIMING` into the `DEFER` family while retaining its separate subtype. Define the independent, conservative admissible-route set `K_i` from all units after panel release but without changing the selector:

* `AUTO ∈ K_i` only if every decision-relevant unit is `A_ACTIONABLE`, has `S_CONFIRM` or `S_NONE`, has no `S_SUPERSEDES`/`S_CONFLICT`, has finite inherited clock `H>=24h`, and all required source components are `AVAILABLE` by `D`.
* `RECON ∈ K_i` if at least one unit is actionable but there is a superseding/conflicting plan, a mixed plan requiring human reconciliation, or an actionability/supersession disagreement that is resolvable only by clinician review; `RECON` is not admissible for an unavailable or fundamentally uninterpretable atom unless the inherited route explicitly classifies it as reconciliation rather than defer.
* `DEFER ∈ K_i` if source availability, target/unit identity, timing, or panel evidence is unknown/unresolved, or if any active unit is unsafe to auto-forward. `TIMING` is a reported `DEFER` subtype when the only established barrier is insufficient `H`.
* An admission with multiple units uses worst-unit precedence: any `DEFER`-compatible unresolved unit excludes `AUTO`; any actionable competing/superseded unit excludes `AUTO` and requires `RECON` or `DEFER` according to the above rule. No favorable unit can rescue it.

The primary finite-population estimand is the design-weighted route-admissibility proportion

`Theta_R = sum_i w_i I(r_i ∈ K_i) / sum_i w_i`,

with `K_i` treated as an adverse-compatible set when a panel state is unknown. Report the three-way route-specific components `Theta_AUTO = P_w(AUTO∈K_i and r_i=AUTO)`, `Theta_RECON = P_w(r_i=RECON and RECON∈K_i)`, and `Theta_DEFER = P_w(r_i∈{DEFER,TIMING} and DEFER∈K_i)`, plus excess-auto `P_w(r_i=AUTO and AUTO∉K_i)`, missed-reconciliation `P_w(r_i=DEFER/TIMING and RECON∈K_i)`, and unresolved-route mass. These are documentation/operational route estimands, not treatment effects, utility, or clinical outcomes.

The primary contrast is against the inherited selector under identical sample, weights, packets, and labels. A secondary, pre-specified comparator is a deliberately naive selector that maps every exact actionable atom with a canonical DS to `AUTO`, while a missing/late DS maps to `DEFER`; it cannot be used for tuning. Also report the parent's original pooled/domain gates and the new panel-stratified results. No model is fitted, and no panel consensus replaces the pre-specified adjudication rule.

## Controls and falsification criteria

In addition to every inherited control, freeze the following tests before labels:

1. **Panel-route permutation**: permute sealed panel labels relative to route outputs. Any route-admissibility signal that persists beyond the predeclared null simulation indicates linkage or leakage.
2. **Actionability redaction control**: remove the action/interval/target spans from the radiology view. The panel must become `A_UNKNOWN` or `A_NONACTIONABLE`, never gain `A_ACTIONABLE`.
3. **Supersession-target swap**: replace a DS plan's target span with an unrelated target in a synthetic fixture. `S_SUPERSEDES` must become `S_NONE`/`S_UNKNOWN`, not remain linked.
4. **DS chronology control**: shift DS `storetime` after `D` while leaving text unchanged. Primary `S_CONFIRM`/`S_SUPERSEDES` must become `S_UNKNOWN`; `D+` packets cannot repair the `D` label.
5. **Contradiction control**: inject explicit changed intervals or modality conflicts into a synthetic DS. The panel must produce `S_CONFLICT` or `S_SUPERSEDES`, and the route-admissibility bound must exclude `AUTO`.
6. **No-actionability shortcut**: inject a precise atom with no canonical DS. The route cannot become `AUTO` merely because the radiology text is precise; it must follow the inherited DS/clock rule.
7. **Minority-unit control**: make one unit `A_UNKNOWN` or `S_CONFLICT` in an otherwise clean admission. Whole-admission `AUTO` must be excluded.
8. **Late-arrival monotonicity, storetime isolation, field-order, cutoff permutation, domain-label permutation, and all parent domain-masking/workload/reviewer-balance traps** must pass unchanged.
9. **Falsification simulation**: plant superseding plans only in one supported service or late possible-calendar domain at a rate that leaves pooled `Theta_R` above its margin while that domain fails. The domain gate, not pooled performance, must reject unrestricted route selection.

Computational verification must recompute hashes, joins, canonical artifact, eligibility, domains, sample probabilities, packets, panel seals, route outputs, `K_i`, HT/Hájek results, exact/replicate bounds, and gates. The verifier can detect route leakage, wrong time rules, unsupported domains, and conclusions inconsistent with outputs. It cannot determine whether a panel's interpretation is clinically correct, whether an explicit plan was actually transmitted or viewed, or whether any route improved care.

## Inference and preregistered gates

Use inherited HT totals and Hájek ratios with phase-1/phase-2 design variance and nested Rao–Wu resampling; retain all-unit adverse precedence and partial identification over unknown panel states. Use the same simultaneous family for pooled, temporal, service, route, and panel endpoints. Zero observed adverse events never implies zero finite-population risk; exact hypergeometric inversion supplies conservative bounds for rare events. Report sampled and estimated finite-population denominators, weights, ESS, unknown and disagreement mass, panel-specific nonresponse, adjudication rate, and physician-hours.

Before labels, governance must approve the following nomination margins, which are operational rather than clinical safety thresholds:

* lower simultaneous 95% bound for `Theta_R` at least `0.90` overall and in every supported temporal/service domain;
* upper simultaneous 95% bound for excess `AUTO` at most `0.05` overall and `0.05` in every supported domain;
* upper bound for `AUTO` among `A_ACTIONABLE` units with `S_NONE/S_CONFIRM` at most `0.05` adverse incompatibility;
* lower bound for capture of `S_SUPERSEDES`, `S_CONFLICT`, and `A_UNKNOWN` admissions by `RECON/DEFER` at least `0.95`, separately where route support permits;
* actionability and supersession unknown/disagreement mass at most `0.05` for each panel in every claimable domain; otherwise the corresponding route claim is inconclusive;
* no supported-domain route-admissibility estimate more than `0.10` below pooled, no absolute excess-auto drift over `0.02`, and no workload ratio over the inherited `1.50` margin or approved absolute budget;
* all inherited route purity, minority safety, timing, serialization, specificity, support, and deterministic control gates pass simultaneously.

A route/domain is claimable only with the inherited event, denominator, ESS, and positive-probability requirements; sparse domains, no panel-positive support, census-only representation, or wide exact bounds are `INCONCLUSIVE`, never pooled away. Routes pass independently: a `RECON`/`DEFER`-only silent validation may be nominated if `AUTO` fails, but no retrospective result authorizes automation.

## Interpretation and noncausal ceiling

**Supportive** means the inherited selector and independent panels yield high route admissibility, low adverse-compatible excess `AUTO`, high capture of superseding/conflicting/unknown cases, bounded panel disagreement, and all temporal/service, serialization, leakage, and workload gates pass. This supports only a site-local prospective silent validation of the frozen route process, with actual packet receipt, transmission, viewing, responsibility, UI rendering, patient preference, orders/referrals/scheduling, completion, outside-care capture, and independent clinical adjudication measured prospectively.

**Adverse** means excess `AUTO`, missed superseding/conflicting/unknown cases, low route admissibility, domain-specific failure, minority-unit failure, leakage, or unacceptable workload. This falsifies the proposed route-selection rule or its unrestricted domain claim, not clinical benefit or clinician performance.

**Inconclusive** means sparse/unsupported strata or domains, missing times or canonical DS, unresolved panel disagreement, nonresponse, wide adverse bounds, broken seals, or failure of a clinical governance prerequisite. Do not convert unknowns to favorable `S_NONE`, `A_ACTIONABLE`, or complete-case success.

The experiment cannot establish appropriateness, responsibility, delivery, viewing, safety, follow-up, completion, benefit, transport to another hospital, or causality. Even a pass cannot establish that a superseding discharge plan was the right plan. Those claims require independent clinical adjudication with richer context and a prospective silent implementation; benefit or safety requires a governed comparative study. Its checkable outputs are route admissibility under the frozen rubrics, actionability/supersession documentation rates, chronology and serialization, finite-population domain contrasts, adverse bounds, and workload.

## Required additional evidence and advance

A prospective study should log final report/addendum finalization, DS drafting/editing/signing/transmission, recipient and responsibility, packet receipt/view/rendering, action or reconciliation, patient preference, competing risks, downstream orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan capture, and follow-up completion. It should independently adjudicate clinical appropriateness and whether the discharge plan actually superseded the radiology plan. Another site is required for external transport. The present experiment advances evidence from pooled reproducibility and domain-stressed packet readiness to an independently adjudicated, route-selection-relevant documentation estimand while remaining explicit about its finite-population and noncausal ceiling.
