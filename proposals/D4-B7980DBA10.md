# Evidence-gated, selection-sensitive baseline-only kidney-status prioritization

The **uncertified audit lane** always runs the data-integrity, parser, equation, feature-provenance, fold, negative-control, and arithmetic tests that do not require asserting what a renal field means. The hypothesis is unchanged: among participants with a complete first available qualifying exported repeat, does adding baseline cystatin-C/creatinine discordance improve prioritization of the observed repeat's creatinine-equation low-status label beyond sex, age, and baseline creatinine-equation eGFR? This is a schedule-marginal discrimination question, not a claim about CKD, measured GFR, chronicity, treatment benefit, or the benefit of ordering a test.

The strongest evidence currently available is limited to computational feasibility and a provisional pre-repair aggregate audit: the audit reported 502,370 joined rows, B=468,887, R=16,546, S_A=16,372, 309 primary labels, a near-zero 2% net-benefit contrast, and an uncertain 10% event-yield contrast. The present proposal tests the repaired protocol only after certification; reproducing the provisional pattern remains inconclusive, not adverse by itself.

A public source is evidence only when the frozen artifact itself is retained and its hash and retrieval metadata are recorded; a search snippet, failed retrieval, or analyst assertion is not certification. A declared implementation artifact may specify parsing, but cannot by itself establish a biological label, unit, assay, or missing-code meaning.

The local `[internal dataset path]` and `metadata.json` do not provide this certificate. Showcase retrievals that returned short error pages are retained as failed retrieval diagnostics, not evidence. The audit lane runs regardless and may perform only operations invariant to the missing semantics: source/header/schema/hash/key checks; raw parse and nonmissing malformed-value counts; field availability; duplicate/overlap checks; date parser diagnostics without treating parsed dates as scientifically valid; dimensional and equation unit-test vectors; feature-allowlist and leakage checks; deterministic fold and tie checks on synthetic or clearly labelled raw-input tests; arithmetic identities; and negative-control harness tests. ## Certified equations, outcome, models, and exact dimensions

After certification, convert creatinine using the certified unit; if and only if the certificate establishes micromoles/L, `Scr_mg_dL=raw_30700/88.4`. For Female `F=1`, Male `F=0`, use the prespecified race-free equations:

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^a * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^Age * 1.012^F`, with `(k,a)=(0.7,-0.241)` for Female and `(0.9,-0.302)` for Male.

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.996^Age * 0.932^F`.

Define `Z=log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)` and primary `Y=I(eGFRcr_1<60)`. `Ycys=I(eGFRcys_1<60)` is secondary only. These are equation-derived status labels, not measured GFR, CKD, chronicity, kidney failure, or patient-important outcomes. Equation references are Inker et al. 2021, DOI `10.1056/NEJMoa2102953`, and Inker et al. 2012, DOI `10.1056/NEJMoa1114248`; citation does not replace the field certificate.

M0 uses exactly `[F, rcs4(age), rcs4(eGFRcr)]`; M1 appends standardized baseline Z. The explicit four-column `rcs4` basis has 1+4+4=9 non-intercept columns in M0 and 10 in M1. Use fixed age bounds `[40,80]` and eGFRcr bounds `[60,120]`, clipped values, knots at lower, one-third, two-thirds, upper, and the defined truncated-cubic basis. Center and scale using training-fold moments, replacing zero SD only with 1. Fit float64 `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(penalty='l2',C=1.0,solver='lbfgs',fit_intercept=True,class_weight=None,max_iter=1000,tol=1e-10,random_state=0)`. Any dimension other than 9/10, convergence warning, nonfinite prediction, or `n_iter_ >= 1000` is `FAIL_MODEL_SPEC`.

## Outcomes, negative controls, and falsification

At q=`0.01,...,0.20`, report paired OOF top-`ceil(q*n)` capture, event fraction, PPV, and `DeltaC(q)`. At thresholds `{0.001,0.0025,0.005,0.0075,0.010,0.015,0.020,0.030,0.040,0.050,0.075,0.100,0.150,0.200}`, report NB, DeltaNB, treat-all, and treat-none. Use `NB=TP/n-FP/n*p_t/(1-p_t)`. Prespecify the 2% threshold contrast and 10% capacity event-yield contrast as co-primary; practical floors are DeltaNB(2%) >= 0.001 and DeltaE(10%) >= 5.

Run all controls in both model pipelines and preserve identical folds and resampling mechanics:

1. **Outcome null:** permute Y within baseline-eGFR strata `[60,75)` and `>=75`, refit both models 200 times, and report statistic-specific randomized-plus-one tail fractions. It tests whether the observed contrast exceeds a fold-aware outcome-null distribution.
2. **Predictor null:** permute Z within baseline-eGFR deciles crossed with sex, refit M1, and compare against M0. A gain comparable to the observed gain blocks support.
3. **Sham marker control:** replace Z by a deterministic within-stratum permutation generated from `(seed,eid)` and verify that its full capacity/threshold curve is null-compatible; a sham gain indicates an implementation or multiplicity problem.
4. **Reversed-label sentinel:** repeat the pipeline on `1-Y` privately. A gain of comparable sign and magnitude after reversal is a coding/fold failure, not evidence.
5. **Timing-leakage sentinel:** privately add elapsed time to a diagnostic score. It must not be reported as a policy model; if it is accidentally present in M0/M1, fail feature provenance. A strong sentinel gain is an audit warning, not a positive result.
6. **Outcome-definition concordance:** repeat only as a secondary check with `Ycys`; disagreement between primary and secondary equation-derived labels is descriptive assay/equation sensitivity and cannot be interpreted as clinical validation.

Negative controls are falsification checks, not proof of absence of bias. Any predictor-null, sham, reversed-label, or leakage failure blocks a supportive label. Null-compatible primary gains are adverse to the claim of incremental evidence, but are not proof that cystatin C lacks clinical value outside this estimand.

## Uncertainty, selection branches, and decision rules

Use 4,000 paired participant bootstrap draws, seed `520241`, recomputing capacity and threshold summaries from frozen OOF predictions. Give separate two-sided simultaneous 98% max-|T| bands for capacity, DeltaNB, calibration, weighted-versus-unweighted differences, and prespecified schedule strata. A zero/nonfinite studentizing SD is a declared failure. Report repeat-to-repeat signs, exact selected quotas, overlap/Jaccard, entrants/leavers, and arithmetic identities; timing bands `[2,4)`, `[4,6)`, `[6,8]` are descriptive global-selection decompositions only, require N>=1,000 and 20 events for directional statements, and never rerank within band.

Calibration is checked separately for M0 and M1 by unpenalized binomial logistic recalibration of logit(mean repeated OOF risk), with a documented high-C fallback only if it agrees within `1e-6` with direct likelihood. Require absolute intercept <=0.10, slope in `[0.90,1.10]`, and absolute observed-minus-predicted risk <=0.002 for the support gate.

Decision rules are mutually exclusive and applied after all hard checks.

* **Supportive schedule-marginal evidence** requires certified fields and hashes; valid 9/10 dimensions and complete predictions; all controls pass; calibration passes; selection sensitivity does not block; at least 8/10 repeat-specific contrasts are positive on each axis; the simultaneous 98% lower bound is above zero and point estimate meets the practical floor for both DeltaNB(2%) and DeltaE(10%); and the observed statistics are more favorable than their corresponding fold-aware null summaries. This supports only incremental prioritization evidence among observed complete repeat attenders.
* **Adverse incremental evidence** requires a completed certified run and either a reproducibly negative incremental curve with a simultaneous upper bound below zero and >=8/10 adverse repeats on an axis, or a null-compatible/predictor-null/sham/reversal/leakage failure that invalidates the claimed incremental signal. It does not establish that testing is useless or that the biomarker has no other clinical role.
* **Operational stop** is used for hash/join/schema failure, malformed nonmissing values, certificate failure, model/convergence failure, missing predictions, failed resampling arithmetic, or failed control execution. `STOP_FIELD_UNCERTIFIED` is explicitly inconclusive about biology.
* **Inconclusive** applies when bands cross zero, one practical floor is not resolved, signs are unstable, selection weighting changes the decision-relevant sign, positivity is poor, schedule strata are underpowered, or the primary gain is isolated without control separation. A threshold-only or capacity-only resolution is reported as mixed, not collapsed into support.

The provisional prior audit's near-zero 2% result with unresolved capacity would therefore remain inconclusive even if reproduced; it is not silently upgraded by the new selection or negative-control machinery.

## What this can and cannot establish

Automatic verification can establish source and schema hashes, required headers and key uniqueness, certificate presence and provenance, raw parsing diagnostics, field-specific masks, equations, exact feature dimensions, baseline-only provenance, folds, controls, calibration, selection sensitivity, resampling, arithmetic identities, and consistency between gates and labels. It cannot establish the truth of a supplied assay certificate without expert review, measured GFR accuracy, CKD chronicity, causal benefit or harm from ordering tests, clinical utility, cost-effectiveness, attendance mechanisms, or transportability. Those require assay documentation and expert adjudication, persistent CKD and measured GFR data, observed decisions and consequences, utility/harm information, and prospective external validation. Keep rows, eids, predictions, folds, weights, selections, and resampling arrays private; publish only aggregate diagnostics and hashes.
