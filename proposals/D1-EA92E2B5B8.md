# Proposal: A pre-action landmark trial of norepinephrine-first versus vasopressin-first de-escalation

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and advance

When an ICU patient has received norepinephrine and vasopressin concurrently for at least 6 hours and is now stable on low doses, should the bedside team attempt to stop norepinephrine first or vasopressin first?

The directional hypothesis is that a strategy of **initiating norepinephrine discontinuation first within 60 minutes of a genuinely eligible pre-action landmark** reduces 6-hour hemodynamic failure by at least 5 absolute percentage points relative to initiating vasopressin discontinuation first.

The parent’s strongest evidence-supported claim remains limited: prior observational studies and meta-analyses suggest discontinuation order may be associated with short-term hypotension, but results are heterogeneous, a recent weighted cohort was null, and no mortality or universal superiority claim is established. The unresolved claim tested here is the proximal effect of assigning the first discontinuation strategy at a stable decision time. MIMIC can support a falsifiable observational target-trial emulation; it cannot recover clinician intent, guarantee exchangeability, or establish a practice-changing causal recommendation.

This child’s substantive advance is to remove future discontinuation from eligibility and time zero. Every patient enters at a landmark while both agents are still active. Two strategy clones are created at that instant, the same 60-minute grace period is applied to both, outcomes accrue from the landmark, and observed actions only determine strategy adherence. Restart, early rescue, death, and comparator cessation are never eligibility exclusions. This repairs the parent’s immortal-time/collider problem and makes the clinical estimand “what if we chose which agent to stop next now?” rather than “what happened among eventual successful stoppers?”

## Target trial

### Population and temporal boundaries

Use the first ICU stay per `subject_id` and the first qualifying landmark in that stay. At 15-minute candidate landmarks, using only records with clinical times at or before the landmark, require:

1. Age at hospital admission at least 18 years, calculated as `patients.anchor_age + year(admissions.admittime) - patients.anchor_year` (ages over 89 represented as 91 under MIMIC conventions).
2. At least 6 cumulative continuous hours in the current reconstructed episode during which positive-dose norepinephrine and vasopressin are simultaneously active. Neither a future stop nor future ICU residence is required.
3. Both agents active at the landmark and 30 minutes earlier, with normalized doses at both times of norepinephrine >0 and <=0.20 mcg/kg/min and vasopressin >0 and <=0.04 U/min; neither dose may have risen between those two times.
4. No epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine active during the preceding 2 hours. Active mechanical circulatory support at the landmark is a primary exclusion because it defines a materially different de-escalation decision; active inotrope or RRT is retained but flagged for phenotype stratification and sensitivity exclusion.
5. In the 2 hours before the landmark, at least three valid MAP times, at least one within 30 minutes, median MAP >=65 mmHg, no MAP <55 mmHg, and no more than one 30-minute bin without a MAP. These requirements concern only pre-landmark stability.
6. Patient alive and in the ICU at the landmark. Do not require survival, ICU residence, MAP observation, or continued drug coverage after it.

The primary design is a sequence of nested trials: open a new trial every 60 minutes while eligibility persists and before either drug’s first true stop. Each trial has the same strategies, grace period, and outcome definition; pool trials with a trial-landmark indicator, patient-clustered bootstrap, and equal total patient-level weighting so long episodes do not dominate. A first-qualifying-landmark-only analysis is required as a simpler sensitivity. This change is clinically important: a completed reconstruction audit found that only 145 of 2,170 first structural landmarks were followed by either stop within 60 minutes, so making the first landmark primary would impose severe artificial-censoring positivity loss. Repeated trials preserve genuinely pre-action eligibility while allowing a patient to re-enter when the same bedside decision remains open. If the pooled design still fails the prespecified action-probability or effective-sample-size gates, comparative inference is infeasible rather than rescued by indexing backward from the observed stop.

### Pre-index shock phenotype without leakage

Do not label the primary cohort “septic shock” from discharge diagnoses. Construct mutually exclusive, decision-time proxy strata from data timestamped before the landmark:

* **Cardiac/mechanical-support proxy:** active IABP, Impella, ECMO/VAD or other dictionary-confirmed mechanical circulatory support procedure, active dobutamine/milrinone, or cardiac critical-care location. Active mechanical support is excluded from the primary cohort; the remaining cardiac-support proxy is reported separately.
* **Infection-compatible vasodilatory proxy:** a blood/urine/respiratory culture collected before the landmark plus systemic antibacterial administration before the landmark, with no active mechanical support. Culture positivity is not required and later culture results are not used at baseline.
* **Postoperative vasoplegia proxy:** surgery service/procedure context before the landmark, no infection-compatible evidence, and no cardiac-support proxy.
* **Mixed/unknown:** all other or conflicting patterns.

These are measured proxies, not adjudicated etiologies. Admission ICD codes may adjust for chronic comorbidity and describe subgroups but cannot establish time-zero shock cause. The primary analysis spans non-mechanical-support phenotypes and includes phenotype indicators; estimates by phenotype are exploratory unless each arm passes event and overlap gates. Blinded clinician review of sampled timelines is required before describing any stratum as clinically septic, cardiogenic, or vasoplegic.

### Strategies, grace period, and assignment

At each eligible landmark create two clones:

* **NE-first strategy:** initiate true norepinephrine discontinuation within 60 minutes while vasopressin is active at the discontinuation instant.
* **VP-first strategy:** initiate true vasopressin discontinuation within 60 minutes while norepinephrine is active at the discontinuation instant.

Follow-up starts at the landmark, not at the later stop. Before either qualifying action, both clones remain compatible and outcomes accrue to both. At the first observed qualifying action, retain the compatible clone and artificially censor the incompatible clone. If neither action occurs by minute 60, censor both at minute 60. If both stops are within 5 minutes, censor both at the earlier boundary as simultaneous/nonadherent. Death or rescue before an action is an outcome in both clones, not an exclusion. Use inverse-probability-of-artificial-censoring weights in 15-minute bins based on history available before each bin. This symmetric cloning/grace procedure is the primary assignment analysis and avoids giving one observed arm guaranteed survival or stability time.

The intervention concerns only which drug is stopped first. Subsequent stopping of the other drug is unrestricted. Restart of the stopped agent, escalation, and addition of another vasopressor are outcome components rather than adherence exclusions. A secondary, more intensive sustained-strategy estimand may require the other agent to remain active for 60 minutes; it is per-protocol and must be analyzed with separate clone-censor weights, never by retrospectively selecting successful adherers.

A descriptive initiator analysis compares patients who initiate either first-stop action within the common grace period. It is explicitly secondary because conditioning on observed initiation can select patients, even when all baseline variables are landmarked.

## Administration reconstruction and true-stop semantics

Use positive-rate `icu/inputevents` rows with valid `starttime < endtime`. Normalize only recognized units:

* Norepinephrine: `mcg/kg/min` directly; if `mcg/min` occurs, divide by a contemporaneous valid `patientweight` (30-300 kg). Reject rather than reinterpret any other unit.
* Vasopressin: `units/hour / 60`; `units/min` directly. Reject unsupported units and implausible normalized values; retain raw and converted values in an audit table.

Within `stay_id,itemid`, resolve overlapping rows at each instant using the latest `starttime`, then latest `storetime` only as a tie-breaker, never as clinical chronology. Union overlapping or exactly abutting positive-rate rows even across `orderid` when no zero-dose interval exists; label cross-order abutments as probable bag changes. Bridge a positive gap of at most 5 minutes only when `orderid` or `linkorderid` continues and the earlier status is not `Paused`, `Stopped`, or cancelled. Never bridge a positive gap solely because it is short. Primary true discontinuation is the end of the reconstructed episode with no positive-rate row beginning within 10 minutes; exact/overlapping rows therefore cannot create a false stop. Repeat with 0-, 10-, and 15-minute bridge rules and 5-/15-minute restart windows.

Use `statusdescription`, `isopenbag`, `continueinnextdept`, `orderid`, and `linkorderid` as boundary-quality evidence, not as stand-alone truth. Optionally validate sampled boundaries against timestamp-near `hosp/emar`/`emar_detail` fields `event_txt`, `administration_type`, `prior_infusion_rate`, `infusion_rate`, `infusion_rate_unit`, `infusion_complete`, `new_iv_bag_hung`, `continued_infusion_in_other_location`, and `restart_interval`; eMAR has no direct ICU input-event key, so temporal concordance is validation only. A blinded pharmacist/critical-care review of at least 100 balanced candidate boundaries must classify true stop, bag change, pause/restart, or indeterminate. Positive predictive value below 0.80 for either arm makes effect estimation inconclusive.

## Outcomes under death, discharge, and intermittent MAP

The primary outcome is an **unfavorable 6-hour status from the landmark**, defined by any of:

1. death;
2. restart of the first-stopped drug after its boundary;
3. initiation of epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine;
4. remaining norepinephrine increase >=0.05 mcg/kg/min above its landmark dose, or re-initiation of norepinephrine after an NE-first stop;
5. two valid MAP values <60 mmHg separated by 5-120 minutes and occurring in distinct 30-minute bins; or
6. ICU exit while any vasopressor remains active or `continueinnextdept` indicates continuing infusion.

Alive ICU discharge before 6 hours with both study pressors off and no preceding failure is a success, not censoring. Alive and still in ICU at 6 hours without any failure is also a success if MAP ascertainment is adequate. Death is always the worst component and cannot be hidden by missing MAP. Report every component and an ordinal status (death; nonfatal hemodynamic failure/rescue or exit on pressor; alive ICU at 6 hours without failure; alive ICU discharge off pressors without failure).

For patients alive and still in ICU without rescue, MAP-based status is adequately observed only if at least one valid MAP is present in four of the twelve post-landmark 30-minute bins, including one in the final 2 hours. Otherwise label the binary endpoint indeterminate rather than assuming stability. Estimate the primary risk using inverse-probability-of-observation weights and report extreme bounds treating all indeterminate cases as failures versus successes. The rescue/death-only composite is a required monitoring-independent sensitivity outcome.

MAP ascertainment uses `chartevents.charttime`, never `storetime`; item 220052 is arterial MAP and 220181 non-invasive MAP. Accept numeric values 20-200 mmHg with `warning` blank or 0 and compatible units. Deduplicate identical `stay_id,charttime`, preferring arterial; do not count concurrent modalities twice. No last-observation carry-forward is used for failure. For secondary area below 65, linearly interpolate only across gaps <=15 minutes. Report arterial-only, dense-monitoring, and no-interpolation analyses, plus arm-specific measurements per hour and observed bins.

Secondary outcomes are 6-hour rescue/death, each primary component, MAP area below 65, alive and off all vasopressors at 24 hours, vasopressor-free hours through 24 hours, 24-hour lactate change, RRT initiation by 48 hours, stage 2/3 AKI by 48 hours where baseline creatinine and urine coverage permit, invasive ventilation at 24 hours, ICU/hospital discharge, and in-hospital mortality. These contextual outcomes do not support a mortality-benefit claim. `patients.dod` is reported only where its relation to the indexed admission is unambiguous; complete outpatient death ascertainment is unavailable.

## Estimands and analysis

The primary estimand is the 6-hour risk difference and risk ratio for unfavorable status under assignment at the eligible landmark to NE-first versus VP-first initiation within 60 minutes. Estimate weighted standardized cumulative risks from the cloned cohort, with artificial-censor weights and baseline outcome standardization. The transparent baseline uses pooled logistic models with prespecified linear/spline terms; a cross-fitted boosted nuisance-model analysis is sensitivity, not a substitute for diagnostics.

Baseline and pre-bin variables include age, sex, race, care unit, admission type, elapsed hospital/ICU time, anchor-year group and hour/day; phenotype proxy; current and 30-minute doses and slopes; dual-agent duration and dose AUC; 0-2 and 2-6 hour MAP level, slope, variability, modality and frequency; heart rate; lactate, creatinine, pH and oxygenation; urine output and fluid balance; crystalloid/albumin exposure; ventilation, RRT, inotrope and steroid exposure; and missingness/measurement intensity. Baseline models use only values before the landmark. Artificial-censor models in each 15-minute bin may use evolving history measured before that bin. Multiple imputation is allowed for covariates, never for actions, infusion boundaries, death, rescue, or MAP failures.

Cluster bootstrap all uncertainty by `subject_id` and preserve both clones and repeated sensitivity landmarks within each resample. Report unadjusted landmark risks, parsimonious standardized estimates, weighted estimates, arm-specific action and event counts, weight percentiles, effective sample size, standardized mean differences, missingness, and phenotype distributions.

Required positivity gates are: at least 200 unique eligible stays; at least 40 primary failures associated with each strategy; estimated probability of each action by 60 minutes >=0.05 in every retained major propensity stratum; absolute weighted SMD <0.10 for all core covariates; strategy-specific ESS >=30% of observed initiators; and no more than 1% of stabilized artificial-censor weights above the prespecified 99th-percentile truncation point. Failure of a gate permits cohort/feasibility reporting but makes a comparative causal interpretation inconclusive. Do not broaden dose/stability criteria after seeing outcomes to rescue sample size.

## Falsification and diagnostics

* **Pseudo-index:** assign the eventual recorded first-stop label at a landmark 6 hours earlier while both drugs remain active. After baseline adjustment, an absolute standardized difference >0.10 in pre-decision MAP/lactate slope or an apparent >=3-mmHg MAP effect before treatment indicates residual selection and invalidates causal wording.
* **Monitoring control:** adjusted post-landmark MAP measurement-rate ratio outside 0.80-1.25, or >20 percentage-point arm difference in adequate MAP coverage, requires the rescue/death-only endpoint and bounds to agree directionally; otherwise the result is inconclusive.
* **Boundary robustness:** direction reversal or >5-point risk-difference shift across prespecified gap/restart/simultaneous-stop rules is inconclusive. Pharmacist-review PPV must be >=0.80 in both arms and differ by <0.10.
* **Preference/positivity:** report action by care unit, hour, and anchor-year group; repeat leave-one-care-unit and leave-one-anchor-year-group out. A strategy absent or nearly deterministic within clinically important strata fails positivity.
* **Phenotype robustness:** primary direction must not be created solely by the mixed/unknown or cardiac-support proxy stratum. Discharge ICD-based “sepsis” analyses are descriptive only.

A result is **supportive** only if the adjusted NE-first risk difference is <=-5 percentage points, its 95% CI excludes 0, rescue/death and MAP components do not materially conflict, all positivity/monitoring/boundary gates pass, and there is no >=3-point adverse signal in death or RRT. It supports prospective protocol testing or randomization, not causal superiority in practice.

A result is **adverse/contrary** if VP-first lowers unfavorable status by at least 5 points with compatible uncertainty, or NE-first reduces recorded hypotension but increases rescue, death, or RRT. This falsifies the directional hypothesis.

A result is **inconclusive** if its CI includes both >=5-point benefit and harm; fewer than 200 episodes or 40 failures per strategy remain; overlap, balance, weights, adjudication, or monitoring gates fail; endpoint bounds cross clinically important benefit and harm; or reasonable boundary definitions reverse the direction. A precise null within the -5 to +5 point clinical margin, with all diagnostics passing, supports absence of a clinically important proximal difference in this MIMIC population but still does not prove equivalence elsewhere.

## Full-table feasibility evidence and remaining uncertainty

A completed DuckDB scan of the full extracted `inputevents.csv.gz` member (no row sampling) found 459,798/459,800 norepinephrine rows in the supported `mcg/kg/min` unit and two unsupported `mg/kg/min` rows; vasopressin had 37,160 `units/hour` rows and three `units/min` rows. The dominant status transitions occur at exactly zero-minute boundaries, including many cross-order `FinishedRunning` transitions, empirically supporting explicit bag-change handling rather than treating every row end as cessation. A deliberately conservative row-pair audit, which required one NE row and one VP row to overlap continuously for 6 hours rather than reconstructing multi-row episodes, produced 928 pre-action low-dose landmarks. Within 30 minutes, 110 were classified NE-first, 18 VP-first, and 800 had neither strategy; 565 were adult first-ICU episodes, with 11 deaths and two alive ICU exits within 6 hours.

A second completed full-inputevents audit reconstructed multi-row episodes and computed dose at the exact landmark. Before adult/first-ICU, MAP, mechanical-support, and phenotype criteria, it found 2,170 first structural landmarks requiring six overlapping hours, low/non-rising doses, and no competing pressor in the prior 2 hours. Within the symmetric 60-minute grace period, 68 initiated NE-first, 77 VP-first, 13 stopped simultaneously within 5 minutes, and 2,012 initiated neither. Counts were identical under 0-, 5-, 10-, and 15-minute positive-gap sensitivities because bridging additionally required order/link-order continuity and no preceding stop/pause. The audit also exposed implausible maximum normalized rates (NE 359.6 mcg/kg/min; VP 40 U/min), so final code must prespecify plausible-rate exclusions and report them rather than relying only on positive/supported units. These findings improve arm balance relative to the conservative row-pair query but reveal a more important limitation: first-landmark action positivity is poor. They justify the prespecified sequence of hourly nested trials while eligibility persists, not backward indexing from a future stop. If repeated trials do not restore the declared probability/ESS gates, the comparative study is infeasible/inconclusive.

These numbers prove source-field computability and expose possible action-positivity limitations, but they are not final analytic counts: neither audit applies definitive MAP/phenotype criteria, and the first audit does not accumulate adjacent segments. Future row ends are used only to tabulate post-landmark actions, never to decide landmark eligibility. The definitive implementation must publish all attrition and action probabilities before effect estimation.
