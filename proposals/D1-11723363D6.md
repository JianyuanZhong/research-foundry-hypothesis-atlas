# Discharge-boundary fidelity of interval-specific pulmonary-nodule recommendations

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and unresolved claim

An incidental pulmonary-nodule recommendation can be lost for at least two different reasons: the discharge artifact may not be stored by the inpatient discharge boundary, or an artifact stored by that boundary may fail to carry the patient-specific action and interval. A later same-system CT is a separate, capture-limited consequence and must not be used to decide which documentation bottleneck occurred.

Published observational studies support a communication and tracking problem and the feasibility of structured follow-up, but they do not establish that discharge wording causes surveillance, that the index recommendation is guideline-appropriate, or that documentation improves cancer outcomes. The strongest claim available here is narrower: MIMIC can measure a report-to-discharge documentation process and can separately describe subsequent same-system imaging. The unresolved claim tested by this experiment is whether the discharge-boundary process states are sufficiently frequent and precise to justify prioritizing artifact finalization/availability or recommendation reconciliation in a prospective workflow.

The primary estimand is an all-opportunity, mutually exclusive disposition distribution. It is not a comparison restricted to summaries that happened to be stored on time. The secondary estimand is descriptive and observation-aware: the all-eligible probability of a recommendation-timed MIMIC chest CT by disposition, with continued local trace and death reported as separate states. No patient without a local trace is called nonadherent.

## Population and frozen temporal rules

The unit is the first eligible recommendation admission per subject. Select it before inspecting discharge text, discharge storage timing, or any post-discharge trace.

1. Link a radiology row to an admission on `(subject_id,hadm_id)` with `admittime - 12 hours <= charttime <= dischtime`. Repeat with the exact zero-hour lower boundary and, as a timing sensitivity, the available ED registration/out-time window.
2. Require age at admission >=18.
3. Use `radiology_detail` exam fields when available, with a predeclared text fallback, to identify chest CT/CTA or an abdominal CT with adjudicated lung-base coverage. Exclude lung-screening examinations and known/suspected lung-cancer staging or surveillance.
4. The high-sensitivity lexical screen must find a pulmonary/lung nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity and an unconditional, patient-specific recommendation for chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 **calendar months**. Conditional/optional recommendations, explicit no-follow-up recommendations, and nonpulmonary nodules are excluded. Lexical labels are candidate retrieval only.
5. Reconstruct reciprocal base-report/addendum chains. For eligibility, the recommendation-bearing component must have `charttime <= dischtime` or be explicitly adjudicated as event-time uncertain. A component with `charttime > dischtime` but early `storetime` is timestamp-discordant and cannot silently become pre-discharge knowledge. A recommendation first introduced only after dischtime is retained in a late-addendum cascade but excluded from the primary opportunity denominator.
6. Two radiology-trained reviewers, blinded to discharge text and future events, adjudicate incidental status, true nodule versus infection/atelectasis/granuloma, index coverage, unconditionality, exact interval, cancer-work-up status, prior stability, and addendum effect. A third reviewer resolves disagreement. Patients dying before exposure ascertainment and active hospice/comfort-only transitions are excluded; mortality is not used to select the opportunity after the boundary.
7. The primary opportunity denominator `N` is frozen after radiology adjudication and chain resolution, before reading discharge content, discharge timing, or later outcomes. Report the complete cascade: lexical candidates, metadata-confirmed candidates, clinical exclusions, timestamp-uncertain chains, late-addendum candidates, and `N`.

## Canonical discharge artifact and exhaustive process states

First count `DS` rows per `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. In the audited snapshot there is one `DS` row per 331,794 admission keys and no within-admission version ties; therefore the sole `DS` row is canonical. Do not substitute an earlier note or choose a latest note by a rule that was not frozen after this audit. If a future refresh has multiple rows or ties, stop and freeze a new version rule before analysis.

Use `dischtime` as the primary boundary `D`. `storetime` is an observed storage timestamp only; it is not evidence of signing, user visibility, transmission, departure, notification, or patient receipt. `charttime` describes note chronology but cannot replace `storetime` for artifact availability.

For every member of `N`, assign exactly one of the following states at `D`:

* `A` delayed storage signal: a canonical DS exists with known `storetime > D`. This is not called invisibility or communication failure.
* `F` faithful content: canonical DS has known `storetime <= D`, is classifiable, and blinded transition adjudication finds an active patient-specific pulmonary-nodule mention, chest/thoracic CT action, and the same 3/6/12-month interval or an unambiguous equivalent calendar due date.
* `C_O` content omission: canonical DS is stored by `D` and classifiable, but contains no patient-specific actionable nodule plan.
* `C_I` incomplete/discordant content: canonical DS is stored by `D` and classifiable, but nodule/action/timing is incomplete or interval/modality-discordant without an explicit alternate clinical resolution.
* `R` documented alternate resolution: canonical DS is stored by `D` and explicitly records a concrete alternate plan such as a documented goals-of-care decision, refusal/preference, specialist takeover, or already scheduled outside follow-up. This is neither faithful propagation nor content failure, and is not inferred from silence.
* `U_D` unresolved artifact: no linked DS row, missing `storetime`, unreadable/contradictory linkage, or unclassifiable text. Unknown is not omission.

These six states are mutually exclusive and exhaustive over `N`. Report the full vector and the unconditional proportions. The conditional content distribution among classifiable summaries is descriptive only and never replaces the all-opportunity estimand. Report `C_O` and `C_I` separately even when a secondary aggregate `C=C_O+C_I` is used.

Record chronology subtypes: report recommendation `charttime/storetime`, first recommendation-introducing component `charttime/storetime`, DS `charttime`, DS `storetime`, stale artifact where DS was stored before the recommendation became available, and timestamp-discordant chain. A delayed `A` can later be described as faithful, incomplete, omitted, or alternate at +24 hours and +7 days, but those later states do not retroactively change the primary state at `D`. Missing summaries and summaries stored after `D` are never silently coded as omission.

## Primary estimand, uncertainty, and decision rule

The primary report is the multinomial state vector. The operational process contrast is

`Delta = P(A) - P(C_O + C_I)`.

This is a one-sample multinomial contrast, not a paired treatment comparison and not a causal effect. Use a fixed-seed subject-level bootstrap with 9,999 replicates to obtain simultaneous intervals for all state proportions and `Delta`; use exact multinomial intervals when a component is zero. Report sensitivity margins of 0 and 0.10 around an explicitly operational prioritization margin of 0.05. The 0.05 is not a clinical-quality threshold.

A workflow-prioritization result requires at least 200 adjudicated opportunities, at least 20 `A` and 20 content-failure events, unresolved `U_D <=5%`, raw independent agreement >=0.80 and Gwet AC1 >=0.70 for eligibility, interval, and state, and simultaneous interval width for `Delta <0.10`. If `U_D >5%`, give partial-identification bounds by assigning all unresolved states to `A` and then to `C`; no intervention priority is declared unless the conclusion holds across bounds. If gates fail, report the cascade and exact descriptive state proportions only.

Interpretation is deliberately limited. Supportive evidence is a precise, gate-passing dominance of one process state across exact and conservative timestamp rules. It prioritizes prospective validation/testing of that workflow. It does not prove that delayed storage prevented visibility or that faithful text caused imaging. An adverse result is low/precise process failure or predominance of documented alternate resolutions, redirecting attention to tracking, scheduling, communication, or exchange. Inconclusive evidence is sparse `N`, unresolved-chain burden, agreement failure, a timing reversal, or an interval crossing the operational margin.

## Secondary observation-aware imaging analysis

For every member of `N`, calculate due date as index `charttime` plus the adjudicated 3, 6, or 12 calendar months using calendar arithmetic with end-of-month clamping; never use a 30.4375-day approximation. For descriptive artifact-timing analyses report both `D` and `max(D, DS.storetime)` where the latter is defined; the all-opportunity denominator is unchanged.

The event is the first same-system chest CT/CTA report with `charttime` after the chosen time zero and within due date +/-42 days. Blinded outcome reviewers classify nodule-focused surveillance, acute/symptom-driven imaging, or indeterminate. Report +/-30 and +/-90-day sensitivities and exclude CTs occurring only during later acute admissions as a specificity sensitivity, not as a claim about clinical intent.

Assign one all-`N` observation state with fixed precedence:

1. timely CT before a recorded or interval-certain death;
2. death before the window end without an earlier timely CT;
3. no timely CT/death but a later MIMIC radiology report or admission after due+42 days, indicating continued local trace;
4. no later local trace, unknown/right-censored capture.

Date-only `dod` and overlapping CT/death times are interval-censored; retain an ambiguity state and bounds rather than asserting temporal precedence. Death is a competing state, not failed surveillance. MIMIC absence is not outside-care absence or nonadherence.

Report all-`N` state risks by primary process state. Do not condition the primary secondary description on later trace or on a discharge subset. An inverse-probability-of-observation analysis is optional sensitivity analysis under an explicitly unverifiable missing-at-random assumption. The process-state analysis remains primary even if no downstream association is estimable.

## Adjudication, falsification, and analysis boundaries

Two independent transition reviewers, blinded to the index report and later imaging, classify discharge content. They distinguish active patient-specific plans from negation, history, family history, hypothetical language, copied prose, and generic primary-care advice. Outcome reviewers are blinded to process state. Preserve raw labels, consensus, disagreement, and reason.

Mandatory falsification checks are a pre-index temporal placebo for CT, non-chest imaging in the due window, continued-trace rates by process state, nodule-focused versus generic/acute CT specificity, and exact versus zero-hour/ED linkage. A placebo or generic-imaging association as large as the descriptive process-state association indicates utilization confounding, leakage, or extraction error; an association driven by acute CT is not evidence of recommendation handoff. No heading-effect model is planned because the audited `discharge_detail` has no usable sections and the dedicated-heading proxy is sparse.

If an association analysis is attempted, report crude exact/Wilson intervals first and use overlap-weighted logistic regression only if each contrasted state has at least 30 adjudicated subjects, at least 15 timed events where relevant, effective sample size >40, common support, and weighted absolute standardized mean differences <=0.10. Covariates must be known by `D`: age category, sex, race/language/insurance, admission type/location, length of stay, ICU and transfer measures, final service, discharge location, interval, index coverage, abstracted nodule features, prior local radiology/admission counts, COPD/emphysema, tobacco-code availability, malignancy history excluding active lung cancer, and severity. No later trace or outcome enters adjustment. With poor overlap or sparse events, stop at stratified description.

These labels are not clinical truth. The audit also found 25,735 reciprocal report/addendum edges, 25,600 present chronological same-admission RR-to-AR pairs, and 135 edges involving absent nodes; these missing nodes remain an unresolved-chain category rather than a silently favorable or unfavorable state. Seventeen discharge rows had missing `storetime` in the audit.

The same proxy audit found continued local trace in 253/403 lexical-faithful and 99/188 lexical-omitted subjects. Among those with trace, recommendation-timed CT was 44/253 (17.4%) versus 16/99 (16.2%); any chest CT was 77/253 (30.4%) versus 30/99 (30.3%). These are feasibility and negative downstream signals, not causal evidence. They motivate making downstream imaging secondary and all-eligible rather than conditioning the primary question on later observability.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

This child repairs the parent by making the directly computable discharge-boundary process the primary estimand and relegating same-system CT to an all-eligible, capture-limited secondary description. It freezes the denominator before discharge and follow-up inspection; separates delayed storage, faithful content, omission, incomplete/discordant content, explicit alternate resolution, and unknown artifact states; treats `storetime` as storage evidence rather than visibility; reconstructs addenda without allowing post-discharge event-time content into eligibility; uses exact calendar-month dates; and handles death and coarsened timestamps with interval-censored bounds. It retires the infeasible heading mechanism and avoids calling later local observation proof of adherence.

Remaining uncertainties require evidence unavailable in MIMIC: whether a stored artifact was visible, signed, transmitted, or received; whether an undocumented alternate plan existed; actual outside imaging and orders/referrals; patient preference and notification; image pixels and guideline appropriateness; pathology, cancer stage, radiation harm, survival, and complete mortality. A prospective timestamp crosswalk and expert adjudication are required to interpret `A` clinically, and a randomized, stepped-wedge, or factorial workflow study with outside-system capture is required for causal benefit claims.
