# Value-blind opportunity, landmark-holdout, and competing-exit transport audit for the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Purpose and evidence boundary

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the Lead-frozen scientific schema without changing the population, labels, landmarks, decision time, exclusions, windows, thresholds, outcome classes, or estimand.

The available evidence supports only that the two exact care-plan strings are sparse structured documentation whose distribution may differ by hospital and charting opportunity. It does not establish that either string denotes an SBT, an extubation evaluation, readiness, clinician intent, delivered ventilator settings, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or a causal intervention. The unresolved, falsifiable claim remains narrow: among the frozen labelled person-landmarks, does the exact label add reproducible information about an independently chart-derived later respiratory-support trajectory beyond pre-`D` state and opportunity, and does that increment persist when measurement opportunity is defined without looking at values and when both hospitals and landmarks are held out?

The substantive advance over the assigned parents is a stricter measurement/opportunity audit. Their opportunity masks can be affected by whether a value parses successfully; that is not value-blind ascertainment. This child separates **structural opportunity** (a source row of a relevant charting type/label at a distinct clinical time) from **value availability/validity** (whether the recorded value passes the frozen parser). Structural masks are frozen before reading arm-stratified outcomes and never inspect `respchartvalue`. It also adds a stay-isolated leave-one-landmark-out transport analysis, so a result cannot be called landmark-transportable merely because the same stay contributes another landmark to training. A competing-exit-aware three-valued opportunity representation prevents early discharge from being mislabeled as a documentation failure. These additions are diagnostics and transport restrictions, not a new endpoint or cohort.

All findings remain descriptive and noncausal. A supportive result can establish only incremental prediction of the specified structured eICU chart construct under the tested transport envelope. No result can establish bedside semantics or external validity.

## Value-blind ascertainment decomposition

The compiler must produce an ascertainment row for every frozen landmark before any arm-stratified endpoint summary. It must use the frozen parser's **label/type mapping**, but structural masks must never read or branch on `respchartvalue`.

### Structural opportunity masks

For each component `j` and window `w ∈ {B,E,T}`, define a structural record as a row in `respiratoryCharting` whose normalized (`respcharttypecat`, `respchartvaluelabel`) pair is in the pre-frozen component mapping, whose `respchartoffset` is finite and falls in the locked window, and whose row identity is available. Do not require a nonmissing or numerically valid value. Define:

`R_{j,w} = 1` iff at least two structural records for component `j` have distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes; otherwise 0. Record raw row count, distinct offsets, first/last offset, duplicate count, and reasons (`no_row`, `one_offset`, `spacing_lt_30`, `nonfinite_offset`, `unmatched_label`). The structural state-opportunity mask is:

`O_w^struct = 1{Σ_j R_{j,w} >= 3}`.

This is a charting opportunity descriptor, not an endpoint and not evidence that values are usable. For the E airway check, define `A_E^struct` from the presence of a structurally recognized respiratory-care record in E/T sufficient to evaluate the already frozen lexical/interval predicate, without using numeric settings; separately retain `E_no_rows` and `E_truncated`.

Structural opportunity is explicitly three-valued when a competing exit truncates the relevant window. For each window, encode `window_status ∈ {complete, truncated_by_exit, invalid_exit_time}` using the exit offset and the window endpoint. `O_w^struct` is `NA_truncated` rather than 0 for a window whose observation horizon is truncated by an exit; for an untruncated window it is 1 or 0 as above. This prevents an exit from being interpreted as a no-charting opportunity. Report the joint pattern `(C, window_status_B, window_status_E, window_status_T, O_B^struct, O_E^struct, O_T^struct, A_E^struct)` and exact counts by arm, hospital, and landmark, including no-record and sparse-spacing reasons.

### Value availability is separate

After the structural manifest and masks are frozen, parse values with the unchanged parser. Define `V_{j,w}` as at least one finite, in-bounds parsed value for component `j` in window `w`; retain `V_w^any`, component-level valid counts, malformed/ambiguous/out-of-bound counts, and same-time conflicts. `V` is a measurement-validity descriptor and is never called value-blind. The endpoint still requires the unchanged two-observation/30-minute rule and three-of-four state rule, so `V` cannot create a trajectory class.

The primary ascertainment table therefore contains both `(O^struct, V, K, C)` but never substitutes `V` for `O^struct`. A mask-only diagnostic uses only structural masks, window status, row counts, timestamps, and parser-independent reasons; it must not use parsed values or the endpoint class. A value-availability diagnostic may use `V` but is labelled measurement validity, not opportunity.

### Outcome-blind diagnostic models

Fit three prespecified, outcome-blind diagnostic targets with the same held-out separation as the main models:

* `Q0`/`Q1`: predict the joint structural opportunity pattern from pre-D features, with and without the exact label;
* `V0`/`V1`: predict value availability/parser-reason patterns, with and without the label, while never using endpoint values or `K`;
* `K0`/`K1`: predict the primary five-state `K`, with `K0` using pre-D state plus pre-D opportunity and `K1` adding only the exact label.

All preprocessing, imputation, calibration, regularization, and weighting are fitted on training units only. The structural mask model is diagnostic and cannot classify `K`. A label increment in `Q` without a corresponding value-based trajectory increment is adverse to a patient-state interpretation; a gain reproduced by structural masks or confined to `non_exit_unobservable` is documentation/ascertainment information only. Do not treat a difference in post-D opportunity as confounding adjustment or erase it by reweighting.

## Pre-D feature and leakage barrier

Construct `X` before endpoint or post-D mask calculation. `X` contains only B summaries and missingness, baseline airway indicator, available baseline `sao2` and `respiration`, age category and inherited pre-D covariates, landmark, and pre-D structural opportunity. Pre-D opportunity is computed analogously from source rows with clinical time `<=D`, retaining entry-minus-clinical lag only as a diagnostic. It contains no `respchartvalue` from after D, post-D counts or masks, endpoint flags, future exit, care-plan field, or treatment string.

Emit separate machine-readable manifests `pre_D_baseline`, `post_D_endpoint`, and `post_D_diagnostic`, each with column lineage and clinical-time inequality. Treat any post-D leakage, care-plan contamination, endpoint-guided mapping, or use of `treatmentstring` in the primary endpoint/baseline as a hard invalidation. The opportunity-only negative control contains no respiratory values and no label strings. M0 uses identical `X` without the exact label; M1 adds only the exact two-level label.

## Hospital and landmark transport procedure

### Primary leave-one-hospital-out

Retain leave-one-hospital-out prediction as the primary transport procedure. For each of the 74 hospital folds, fit M0 and M1 on all other hospitals and predict the held-out rows' five K states. Hospital ID is a fold boundary, never a patient predictor; no held-out target-rate calibration, hospital intercept, or held-out opportunity distribution may enter training. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss and Brier score, finite-support calibration, and separate losses for all-row K, classifiable trajectory rows, competing exits, and non-exit unobservability. Keep repeated landmarks together in all resampling and uncertainty analyses.

A held-out fold may provide an arm contrast only when both exact labels and the relevant target support are present. Otherwise retain transparent all-row predictions and mark the contrast unavailable. A gain confined to exit or unobservability is not a respiratory-state gain.

### Stay-isolated leave-one-landmark-out

Add a prespecified landmark transport analysis over the five frozen landmarks. For each `L` in `{1440,2880,4320,5760,7200}`, hold out all rows at `L`. To prevent the same ICU stay from appearing in training at another landmark, remove **all landmarks of every stay represented in the held-out `L` test rows** from the training set for that fold. This is a stay-isolated leave-one-landmark-out fold; it does not alter the 540-row endpoint accounting and does not discard test rows. Fit all preprocessing and probability estimation on the remaining rows only, then predict the held-out `L` rows. Report per-landmark micro loss, equal-landmark macro loss, classifiable and all-row support, both arms, and the paired M1-M0 increment. Do not use landmark-specific target rates from the held-out rows.

If a landmark fold has fewer than 20 training stay clusters, lacks a label, has no finite support for a target, or cannot fit the prespecified model, mark that fold unavailable rather than pooling it into a favorable summary. Require at least four of five valid landmark folds for a landmark-transport statement. Because the five landmarks are repeated observations within stays, uncertainty for the aggregate uses stay-cluster resampling and reports effective stay clusters; no row bootstrap is allowed.

The primary hospital and secondary landmark analyses are complementary. A label increment is transport-robust only if the hospital envelope passes and the landmark analysis has at least four valid folds with no more than one prespecified unfavorable-direction fold. This is a prediction/transport criterion, not external validity.

### Site-balanced envelope and support selection

Before inspecting effect directions, define supported hospitals using frozen counts: at least 10 classifiable, non-competing rows under each exact label, and at least 50% of all classifiable, non-competing rows represented. Retain all 74 hospitals in support tables, including zero/one-arm sites. If fewer than five hospitals qualify, the site-balanced classifiable envelope is unavailable/inconclusive.

For each supported hospital, report within-site all-row `Δ_k`, classifiable contrasts where denominators are positive, held-out M1-M0 loss increments, structural opportunity distributions, value-availability distributions, exit timing, and unobservability. Summarize row-weighted and equal-site estimates, median, IQR, sign concordance, and maximum leave-one-site-out change. Do not drop a site for an unfavorable direction. No single supported site may contribute more than 25% of supported classifiable/non-competing rows for a cross-hospital patient-state interpretation. Disagreement between row-weighted and equal-site summaries is heterogeneity/inconclusive, not a reason to choose a preferred weighting.

For landmark summaries, report equal-landmark mean and all five per-landmark estimates, but do not use a landmark as a predictor in the held-out test. A site-balanced pattern that appears at one landmark only is not transport-robust.

## Opportunity-preserving null and temporal falsifications

All nulls are generated from machine-readable row and cluster artifacts before interpreting observed increments. Use the broad parent null and the stricter structural-opportunity null.

### Cluster-constrained opportunity-preserving permutation

For each hospital `h` and landmark `L`, form a pre-D structural signature from the scalar opportunity score and its components: pre-D structural row count, distinct clinical timestamps, number of mapped component labels, minimum-spacing indicators, and pre-D missingness indicators. Quantile bins and sparse-bin collapsing are fit once in each training partition using only pre-D information, with deterministic boundaries and no endpoint or post-D values. The exact signature includes `h`, `L`, and the collapsed opportunity stratum.

Permute the exact label assignments by **whole stay blocks** within each `(h,L,opportunity-signature)` cell. A stay block includes all its frozen landmark rows; an assignment is valid only if the participating stays have identical signature vectors at every landmark at which they occur, so no stay is split and all repeated-landmark structure is preserved. Use a fixed seeded constrained permutation algorithm: independently shuffle eligible stay blocks within cells, reject assignments that do not preserve the observed exact arm count at every `(h,L)` cell, and record attempted, accepted, rejected, and empty cells. Do not relax the constraints to obtain a null. If fewer than 20 nonempty valid cells or fewer than 500 valid permutations are obtained, the opportunity-preserving null is unavailable/inconclusive.

For each valid permutation rerun the predeclared label-added contrast/prediction calculation without refitting any outcome-guided parser or selecting sites. Compare the observed M1-M0 increment and all-row `Δ` to the empirical null distribution. If the observed increment is typical under this opportunity-preserving null, it is adverse to a value-based patient-state interpretation even if the broad hospital-by-landmark null is unfavorable. If the exact whole-stay constraints are infeasible, report the reason and do not substitute row-level shuffling as a purported cluster-preserving null.

### Additional falsifications

Retain the parents' prespecified placebo `(D-1440,D-720]` versus B, shifted exposure time `D-720` with the locked outcome window, component/source ablations, airway-corroborated and non-corroborated analyses, late-horizon and non-peak safety sensitivities, and pre-D observation-weighted sensitivity. A comparable/larger placebo increment, early/late reversal, or signal surviving only one component/source/site is adverse or inconclusive under the relevant support failure; it is not evidence for bedside meaning.

The as-entered clock sensitivity remains secondary. Keep only observations with finite `respchartentryoffset` no later than the end of their assigned clinical window (`<=D` for B, `<=D+720` for E, `<=D+2160` for T), report late-entry attrition as missingness, and use it only if source semantics pass audit. Do not replace clinical time with entry time.

## Uncertainty and fail-closed interpretation gates

Use a stay-cluster bootstrap, fixed seed `61061`, target 1,000 and minimum 500 valid replicates, refitting the complete held-out pipeline. Report attempted, valid, failed, effective stay clusters, and for site-balanced summaries the number of replicates losing site support. Never use a row bootstrap to repair lost support. Intervals are descriptive uncertainty, not clinical-effect proof.

The following mechanical gates are required:

1. **Integrity:** parent artifacts, source/schema hashes, exact labels, joins, boundaries, parser manifest, column isolation, and endpoint precedence all pass. Failure is invalid.
2. **Total-state accounting:** every row has exactly one `K`; exits precede measurement rules; no exit or non-exit-unobservable row is stable/mixed. Failure is invalid.
3. **Feasibility:** at least 40% of all 540 rows classifiable and at least 10 hospitals with both labels among classifiable rows is supportive feasibility; below 20% or below five such hospitals is adverse feasibility; intermediate is inconclusive. This does not change the endpoint.
4. **Structural opportunity audit:** the complete three-valued structural-mask table and separate value-validity table are emitted before endpoint interpretation. If masks cannot be computed without reading values, the value-blind audit fails closed and is inconclusive.
5. **Hospital transport:** at least five supported hospitals, at least 10 classifiable non-competing rows per label in each, at least 50% supported-row coverage, row-weighted/equal-site directional concordance, no supported-site dominance above 25%, and at least five estimable held-out hospital folds are required for a cross-hospital patient-state interpretation.
6. **Landmark transport:** at least four of five stay-isolated leave-one-landmark-out folds are valid, both label support and finite target scoring support exist, and no more than one valid landmark fold reverses the prespecified favorable direction. Otherwise landmark transport is inconclusive, not pooled away.
7. **Incremental information:** M1 must improve both micro and equal-hospital macro held-out log loss for all-row K and the classifiable trajectory in the unbounded primary plus at least two frozen airway/observation sensitivities; the stay-cluster interval must exclude zero favorably; no more than 25% of estimable held-out hospitals may reverse direction. The same claim must not be explained by structural-mask gains, value-availability gains alone, exits, unobservability, source density, entry lag, or the as-entered clock.
8. **Opportunity-preserving null:** the exact cluster-constrained null must have at least 20 valid cells and 500 valid permutations. An observed increment typical under that null is adverse to patient-state interpretation. Unsupported null inference is inconclusive.
9. **Trajectory coherence:** evaluation-minus-no-trial de-escalation direction and the stringent airway/safety sensitivity must agree in at least two prespecified supported overlap hospitals and must not reverse across early/late or landmark analyses.

Supportive output establishes only reproducible incremental prediction of the defined eICU chart construct within this snapshot and tested transport envelope. Adverse output indicates that the label increment is not robust for this endpoint or is explainable by opportunity, measurement validity, exits, or site/landmark composition; it does not establish absence of bedside evaluation, harm, or lack of treatment. Inconclusive output identifies artifact failure, sparse classifiability, competing exits, unsupported structural masks, parser instability, failed positivity, unavailable clocks, or infeasible constrained permutations. Bedside semantics and stronger conclusions require adjudicated SBT/extubation/reintubation outcomes, synchronized device logs, local interface dictionaries, complete workflow data, expert review, and external/prospective replication.
