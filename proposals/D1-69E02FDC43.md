## 4. Frozen source objects and annotation rosters

A radiology unit remains `u=(patient master index,encounter number,examination number)`, with every exact-deduplicated component in raw order and every field boundary retained. Blank `examination number` cannot provide primary semantics. The inherited parser creates disjoint canonical text nodes `V`, markup/attribute nodes `A`, and parse-error lineage `E`. Semantics may originate only in reversible source-byte ranges represented in `V`. Parse-disagreeing, nonreversible, treatment/pathology/specimen/MVI-grade-contaminated, or unresolved-context units receive the inherited joint unavailable block; no phrase is selectively removed and no patient is dropped.

A pathology object is `v=(Patient Master Index,Visit Number,composite_hash)`: exact-deduplicate same-encounter pathology rows across all six fields, retain every raw ordinal, and serialize every `Pathology,Examination findings,Examination diagnosis` field in raw row/field order with immutable boundaries and source-byte offsets. Because the source has no pathology item/accession/time field, all such rows in the selected encounter are one indivisible documentary composite. No row may be assigned to a particular resection specimen. A genuinely absent/blank composite is a deterministic documentary nonmembership source state, not a missing human form.

Before any radiology semantics, pathology grade, model score, event count, top-K set, or result is exposed to readers or modeling analysts, an independent data steward freezes:

1. every usable radiology unit reachable as latest CT or MRI from any structural `S+` state; and
2. every nonempty pathology composite reachable from any candidate procedure encounter, without filtering on HCC or grade semantics.

The same two qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists independently review every usable radiology roster item; a third qualified radiologist then submits an adjudication form for every item. Separately, the same two qualified Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists independently review every nonempty pathology item; a third pathologist then submits an adjudication form for every item. These six people define six corpus-wide reader books.

Radiologists see only deidentified whole-unit `V`, modality, and immutable component/field boundaries. They do not see pathology, diagnoses, the other modality, calendar role, model outputs, top-K status, or other independent forms. Pathologists necessarily see the pathology words that define the endpoint, so they are not falsely described as blinded to outcome content; they do not see radiology text/forms, model outputs, top-K status, test results, or other independent forms. Adjudicators see the two forms in their own specialty only after both are signed. Source-embedded dates remain intact because selective text redaction is forbidden; interfaces hide system-added identifiers and split metadata, and possible split inference from source text is reported as an unautomatable limitation.

All radiology forms are frozen before pathology forms are released to radiologists or the modeling team. A steward may generate the any-world chronology quarantine and seal test labels, but 2020/2021 pathology terminals and radiology semantic forms remain inaccessible to model developers until preprocessing, hyperparameters, coefficients, `N_ref`, and K are frozen.

## 5. Indivisible forms and complete reader books

### 5.1 Radiology forms

For each usable `u`, each radiologist `j_R in {R1,R2,RA}` submits one complete concept-and-span vector `f^R_{j_R}(u)` under the inherited schema: modality-specific maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites, with the frozen categorical levels including explicit `uncertain`, `not-mentioned`, and `source-text-unavailable` where appropriate.

A form is indivisible. No concept, numeric value, span, or absence attestation may be borrowed from another form. For a deterministic unavailable unit, every book points to the same inherited unavailable block with source lineage; this is source absence, not human noncompletion.

### 5.2 Pathology forms as finite joint relations

For each nonempty `v`, each pathologist `j_P in {P1,P2,PA}` submits one indivisible form containing:

- HCC-frame judgment: `supported`, `not-supported`, or `indeterminate`;
- grade judgment conditional on HCC membership: anchored `M2`, anchored `M0/M1-excluding-M2`, or `indeterminate/inadequate/contradictory`;
- required text spans and attribution/context judgments; and
- a deterministically derived finite terminal set under the frozen rubric.

The only terminal atoms are `OUT`, `IN0-A`, `IN1-A`, `IN0-U`, and `IN1-U`. `OUT` means documentary HCC-frame nonmembership and has no Y. `INy-A` means membership with anchored documentary `Y=y`; `INy-U` is one completion of an unanchored/indeterminate documentary grade and is marked unanchored for ascertainment gates. The rubric mapping is relational: supported+anchored M2 maps to `{IN1-A}`; supported+anchored M0/M1 excluding M2 to `{IN0-A}`; supported+grade-indeterminate to `{IN0-U,IN1-U}`; not-supported to `{OUT}`; and HCC-frame-indeterminate adds `OUT` to the applicable conditional-in-frame set. Thus `{OUT,IN1-A}` is possible when the HCC attribution is uncertain but M2 is explicit if attributed, while fully indeterminate text maps to `{OUT,IN0-U,IN1-U}`.

The verifier regenerates this terminal set from the signed judgments; a reader or adjudicator cannot invent an unlisted combination. The form's HCC judgment, grade judgment, terminal set, spans, and reasons stay together. No HCC-positive call may be paired with another reader's M2 call; no `OUT` is converted to `Y=0); and no uncertain grade is imputed negative. Empty source composites deterministically yield `{OUT}` in all books and are distinguished from missing forms.

### 5.3 No cross-item synthetic readers

Define complete immutable books

`B^R_j = {f^R_j(u): every radiology roster item u}` and
`B^P_k = {f^P_k(v): every pathology roster item v}`.

The primary panel index is the predeclared set
`G = {R1,R2,RA} × {P1,P2,PA}`, exactly nine book pairs. For one world, `g=(j_R,j_P)` is chosen once and applies to the entire corpus, every cutoff sensitivity, and every stage of analysis. Source state `s_i` selects which already-read objects are relevant for patient `i`; it never selects a reader. A pathology terminal `tau_i` may be chosen only from the selected pathology form's terminal set. This is same-form set-valued uncertainty, not cross-reader mixing.

The old per-accession product `∏_u unique{f_1(u),f_2(u),f_A(u)}` is explicitly retired. The optimizer may not create a roster-wide mosaic, swap reader identity by patient/year/model arm, or combine concepts within forms. Identical whole books or analysis-equivalent worlds may be quotiented only with all original book and form backpointers retained. `(RA,PA)` is the adjudicated-only secondary diagnostic; it cannot rescue the primary nine-pair envelope.

After every gate passes, a coordinate is supportive iff `delta_e^->5`, heterogeneous iff `delta_e^-<=5<delta_e^+`, and all-world adverse iff `delta_e^+<=5`. Equality to 5 fails the strict hypothesis. Global labels are exhaustive and mutually exclusive:

1. `whole-panel-universal-supportive` iff all four lower endpoints exceed 5;
2. `universal-adverse-without-positive-coordinate` iff every upper endpoint is at most 5;
3. `decisive-heterogeneity` otherwise, annotated with radiology-, pathology-, or joint-panel sensitivity only when its paired-witness rule is met;
4. `inconclusive` with precedence if any mandatory gate fails.

Any lower endpoint at or below 5 falsifies the universal conjunction. A supportive adjudicated-only cell cannot rescue the primary. Support permits only: “Within this frozen eventual-report corpus, clean canonical report-text concepts added more than 5 encounter-documentary M2 captures per 100 fixed positions under every declared source, pathology-completion, and whole reader-book pair in both test years.” It does not establish correct labels, unseen-reader robustness, preoperative visibility, image features, specimen-linked biological M2, benefit, or deployment readiness.

Heterogeneity falsifies universal robustness and identifies an attained dependency, not a correct reader. Inconclusive means annotation, source, event, fit, or exact-computation requirements failed and is not negative evidence.
