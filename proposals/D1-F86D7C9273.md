# Finite-manifest, fixed-fit nested documentation-surface HCC experiment

## 1. Targeted successor and the single repaired threat

This child preserves the clinical question and evidence boundary of [prior hypothesis], but repairs its most consequential remaining computability and interpretation defect: the parent never constructs or bounds its complete ambiguity states, yet fits B, R, and G separately in every state and makes every source/outcome completion a max-T coordinate.

That is not merely expensive. It changes the object being evaluated from one locked scoring rule to a collection of different rules whose coefficients, selected penalty, rankings, and even training outcomes vary with unresolved source interpretation. Apparent state heterogeneity can therefore arise from retraining rather than from robustness of the proposed fixed-capacity ranking rule. Set-valued outcomes alone imply 2^u patientwise completions; procedure, time, examination, and annotation alternatives multiply that family. The parent provides no finite state registry, no cardinality bound, and no executable way to build 2,000 refitted bootstrap tensors over all completions.

The live HCC schema makes this threat substantive. Aggregate read-only audit [research job], output hcc_state_space_audit.json, [source checksum], found before any HCC-specific clinical filtering:

- 338,040 procedure rows, 7,781 distinct raw procedure labels, 41,602 patients with multiple procedure rows, 15,874 blank starts, 183,354 parsed midnight starts, and 33 rows with parsed end before start;
- 419,996 examination rows, 392,854 nonblank examination-number groups, 16,534 multicomponent groups, 16,519 such groups with multiple raw examination labels, 282 groups with a missing/unparseable component start, and six groups spanning more than 24 hours;
- 46,395 pathology rows over 32,386 encounter keys, including 8,932 keys with multiple pathology rows and no pathology time field.

These are aggregate source facts, not cohort or conflict counts after expert adjudication. They show why a patientwise Cartesian family cannot simply be assumed executable.

The repair has two linked parts: (1) ambiguity is represented by at most 27 signed, globally coherent expert manifests, with unresolved values mapped to terminal categories rather than new branches; and (2) one consensus-trained B/R/G fit is locked before testing and applied unchanged to every registered 2020/2021 state. Set-valued outcomes remain coupled but their exact endpoints are computed analytically, without enumerating completions. The max-T family is therefore finite and auditable.

Everything else central to the parent remains: the eventual stored-report evidence boundary; encounter-documentary M2 rather than biological MVI; development in 2015-2018, lock/tuning in 2019, and separate fixed 2020 and 2021 tests; the 24-hour confirmatory cutoff and fixed q=.10 capacity; independently optimized nested R(Z,S) versus G(Z,S,X); same-fit semantic masking Gmask; a 5-capture-per-100 margin; hypothetical report-access bounds; mutually exclusive supportive, heterogeneous, adverse, and inconclusive conclusions; and strict non-causal limits.

### Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis

Among adults in the future-defined first eligible source-documented HCC resection frame, with same-encounter documentary HCC pathology and no recorded qualifying HCC treatment in the preceding year, does one consensus-trained, 2019-locked full model using meanings in eventual stored CT/MRI report text capture more encounter-documentary M2 cases in the same fixed top-K capacity than both:

1. an independently optimized nested baseline with identical nonsemantic acquisition and documentation-surface information; and
2. the same fitted full model after complete semantic-content masking,

by more than 5 captures per 100 ranking positions in each of 2020 and 2021, for every registered expert-manifest state and every coupled completion of an unresolved documentary-M2 label?

This is falsified at a coordinate when the simultaneous upper confidence bound for even its most favorable admissible outcome completion is at most 5. It is universally supported only when the simultaneous lower confidence bound for the least favorable admissible completion exceeds 5 for both contrasts, both years, and every state, and the inherited permutation criterion is met.

The margin is a ranking-composition threshold, not sensitivity, PPV, utility, treatment effect, or patient benefit.

### Advance over current knowledge

A supportive result would show that one genuinely locked score uses adjudicated stored-report meaning to improve fixed-capacity retrospective capture beyond an equally optimized acquisition/surface model, and that the improvement survives globally coherent differences among the two original expert readings and consensus. This is more clinically interpretable than the parent's state-specific retraining target because the object proposed for later silent-mode evaluation is one fixed scoring rule.

A supportive result would justify obtaining report lifecycle/access data and testing the fixed rule prospectively. It would not justify triage, referral, transplantation, resection, or treatment.

## 3. Finite, auditable coupled states

### 3.1 Manifest-generating universe

Before inspecting any 2020/2021 M2 outcome, create a high-recall row universe using only adult encounter linkage, all potentially hepatobiliary procedure labels, patient-wide potentially qualifying treatment labels, all CT/MRI-like examination labels within the widest 90-day/72-hour window, and all same-encounter pathology rows. Preserve raw file ordinal and source backpointer for every row. Hash this universe.

Two independent experts and a third consensus resolver work over that same frozen universe. Each expert-domain artifact must be complete: every row/label/unit in scope has one allowed terminal value, including an explicit unresolved value. Missing decisions are gate failures, not latent branches.

### 3.2 Three complete manifests in each domain

Procedure domain P:

- P1 and P2 are the complete initial surgeon manifests; PC is the complete consensus/third-reader manifest.
- Each exact normalized/raw label is one of eligible index procedure, qualifying prior treatment, nonqualifying, or unresolved.
- Unresolved potentially eligible index labels cannot establish index eligibility in that manifest. Unresolved potentially qualifying prior-treatment labels cannot establish a negative history and therefore exclude the affected patient in that manifest.
- The deterministic episode builder chooses the earliest eligible episode by interval lower bound, then raw ordinal. If two candidate intervals overlap such that order is unidentified, the patient is ineligible in that manifest.

Examination domain E:

- E1 and E2 are complete initial radiologist manifests; EC is the complete consensus/third-reader manifest.
- Each candidate group has a terminal identity partition, CT/MRI/other/unresolved modality, and the fixed concept categories present, absent, uncertain, not-mentioned, or source-text-unavailable.
- Unresolved identity/modality cannot provide identity-safe semantics. It is encoded as identity-ambiguous/source-text-unavailable; the next latest identity-safe whole CT/MRI is used if one exists. Uncertain meaning remains the semantic uncertain category and does not branch to present/absent.
- Each manifest records all component backpointers and text hashes. No phrase-level selection is allowed.

Pathology domain Y:

- Y1 and Y2 are complete initial pathologist manifests; YC is the complete consensus/third-reader manifest.
- Each composed same-encounter pathology document has an HCC-frame decision and Y=1, Y=0, or Y={0,1}.
- Y=1 requires explicit M2 wording anchored to microvascular invasion or an HCC MVI grade. Y=0 requires anchored M0/M1 wording excluding M2 in that context. Missing grade, unanchored text, boilerplate, contradiction, attribution uncertainty, or inadequate text is exactly Y={0,1}; it does not spawn additional annotation states.
- The same patient-level Y variable is used for all models, contrasts, and duplicate bootstrap copies within a manifest state.

Clinical correctness of these manifests requires the named experts. An automatic solver may verify completeness, allowed values, blinding attestations, hashes, and use, but not whether a Chinese clinical interpretation is correct.

### 3.3 Registered family and state IDs

Define the exact-deduplicated family:

Theta = unique({P1,P2,PC} x {E1,E2,EC} x {Y1,Y2,YC}).

Therefore 1 <= |Theta| <= 27. A state ID is the SHA-256 of the three manifest hashes plus the HCC snapshot, parser, procedure dictionary, annotation rubric, and code hashes. The state registry lists every constituent hash and all affected patient/backpointer records. It is frozen before test outcomes and included as an input to every execution.

No patientwise recombination of reader 1, reader 2, and consensus decisions is admissible. No favorable state may be assembled after seeing results. Any unregistered state, missing manifest row, manifest edited after lock, or state count above 27 fails the primary family.

This family measures robustness to whole, auditable expert interpretations. It does not claim to enumerate all possible biological truths.

## 4. Deterministic time handling and quarantine

A validated nonmidnight timestamp is a point. A midnight value without independent validation is [date,date+24h). Date-only values use the same interval. Parsing rules, timezone convention, and validation evidence are frozen.

For a procedure index, t_op is its start interval. A missing start, unparseable start, parsed end before start, or unresolved order among overlapping eligible episodes cannot establish primary eligibility. End time is an audit field and never silently substitutes for start.

For d in {12,24,48,72} hours, c_d=t_op-d. The primary d is 24 hours. An examination acquisition interval must lie wholly inside [c_d-90d,c_d). A procedure interval must lie wholly in one calendar year to assign that year. A patient whose manifest membership or episode can place the patient in different calendar periods is excluded from both affected confirmatory periods and retained in a quarantine audit. Across all states, no patient may occur in more than one of development, 2019, 2020, or 2021. Data from 2022 onward are drift/ascertainment audits only.

Development is 2015-2018. The 2019 data choose hyperparameters and lock preprocessing. Separate fixed tests are 2020 and 2021. No later row can change an earlier dictionary, state registry, category map, coefficient, K, or tie rule.

The cutoff is analytical. Without release/version/view logs, it is not a report-availability or clinical-decision time.

## 7. One fixed model fit and the baselines

Use additive elastic-net logistic regression with unpenalized intercept. One-hot categories use frozen references; numeric columns use 2015-2018 consensus statistics; zero-variance features are removed by a logged consensus-development rule. No interactions, splines, identifiers, or free text are allowed.

For alpha in {0,.25,.5,.75,1}, use 100 log-spaced lambdas from consensus-development lambda_max to 10^-4 lambda_max. Each candidate fit is trained once on singleton-Y consensus 2015-2018 rows. For each model separately, choose hyperparameters on 2019 by maximizing the minimum exact lower-endpoint fixed-K capture over all registered states. Ties prefer larger penalty, fewer nonzero coefficients, smaller alpha, then lexical serialization.

Fit and lock:

- B(Z), the acquisition/patient diagnostic baseline;
- R(Z,S), the independently optimized reduced comparator;
- G(Z,S,X), whose Z,S columns, preprocessing, missing codes, and order are byte-identical to R, with X appended;
- Gmask, with no refit: apply the same fitted G after replacing every X category with its training-defined source-text-unavailable level while preserving all Z,S values.

After the 2019 lock, each model has one preprocessing hash, coefficient hash, intercept, and hyperparameter record. Those hashes must be identical in all state evaluations and all bootstrap replicates. State-dependent refitting is forbidden. Platt calibration is descriptive only and cannot affect ranking.

No pathology, diagnosis, lab, clinical-document text, identifier, calendar year, post-cutoff acquisition, test annotation frequency, access allocation, or test outcome may enter predictors or tuning.

## 8. Fixed-capacity estimand and coupled outcome endpoints

For state theta, offset d, year a, and q in {.05,.10,.20}, let N_a(theta,d) be eligible size before M2 grade or test semantic outcomes are consulted. Freeze

N_ref = min over theta, d, and a in 2015..2019 of N_a(theta,d),

and K_q=floor(q*N_ref). The confirmatory capacity is d=24, q=.10. Require N_ref>=500, K_.10>=50, and at least K_.10 eligible patients in every 2020/2021 state. The .05/.20 and d=12/48/72 results are locked sensitivities and cannot rescue the primary result.

Rank each state with the same locked scores, then frozen SHA-256 patient tie key and raw episode backpointer. Select exactly K whole patients. For model M, T_M=100/K times the top-K documentary-M2 count.

The contrasts remain:

- Delta_nested=T_G-T_R;
- Delta_mask=T_G-T_Gmask;
- diagnostic Delta_surface=T_R-T_B.

For a model pair in one state/year, define w_i as first-model minus second-model top-K membership. With bootstrap duplicates, w_i is the difference in selected clone multiplicities and all clones of one source patient share its one Y value. Exact outcome endpoints are:

delta_minus = 100/K [sum over singleton Y of w_i Y_i + sum over Y={0,1} of min(0,w_i)];

delta_plus = 100/K [sum over singleton Y of w_i Y_i + sum over Y={0,1} of max(0,w_i)].

Thus every unknown outcome is coupled across model lists. No favorable label may be assigned separately to G and R/Gmask. The formulas exactly equal extrema over all 2^u completions but require O(u) work. Exhaustive equality is required for fixtures with u<=20.

The target is fixed-model documentary-M2 captures per 100 ranking positions in an eventual retrospective corpus. It is not prevalence, recall, report availability, utilization, referral opportunity, or benefit.

## 9. Finite inference and semantic falsification

Run 2,000 paired patient-cluster bootstrap replicates, separately within the frozen 2020 and 2021 master rosters. Draw patient IDs once per year and use the same multiplicities for every state and model. Duplicate draws receive deterministic clone suffixes for ranking, but outcome uncertainty remains shared at source-patient level. Reconstruct each registered state's eligible clone roster, apply the fixed B/R/G/Gmask objects without fitting or tuning, and compute delta_minus and delta_plus.

This inference is conditional on the realized 2015-2019 training/lock data and quantifies test-roster sampling variability of the fixed scorer plus declared partial identification. It does not quantify training-set uncertainty or prove future-site performance.

For every year x co-primary contrast x registered state coordinate j, let observed endpoints be delta_minus_j and delta_plus_j. Let s_minus_j and s_plus_j be their bootstrap standard deviations. Construct:

- one simultaneous one-sided 97.5% lower max-T family over all delta_minus coordinates;
- one simultaneous one-sided 97.5% upper max-T family over all delta_plus coordinates.

With at most 27 states, each one-sided family has at most 2 years x 2 contrasts x 27 = 108 coordinates, for at most 216 endpoint coordinates across the lower and upper families. Store the exact tensor dimensions to prevent confusion. Lower critical values use the 97.5th percentile of the maximum centered studentized lower error; upper values use the analogous maximum upper error.

More than 2.5% failed replicates, fewer than K eligible clones in any required replicate/state, any omitted state, nonfinite endpoint, reversed bound, or undefined standard error makes the primary family inconclusive. A zero standard error is accepted only with an exact proof artifact.

Retain the parent's 1,000 development/tuning-only whole-X-block permutation negative control. Permute complete X blocks among patients within frozen strata of year, modality pattern, unit cardinality, identity ambiguity, and S-score decile; keep Z,S,outcomes, and source rows fixed; refit candidate G models, repeat 2019 tuning, and evaluate the same finite state family. Use

H = min over state, year, and both contrasts of (delta_minus - 5).

The p-value remains (1+count(H_perm>=H_observed))/1001, and universal support requires p<=.025. This is evidence against a surface-conditioned training-alignment null, not proof that report concepts are image-valid or causal.

Report Delta_surface with its own simultaneous two-sided 95% family. Sensitivity offsets/capacities and access cells have separate families and cannot rescue the primary.

## 11. Mutually exclusive conclusions

For every valid state/year/co-primary contrast coordinate, let L_minus be the simultaneous lower confidence bound for the least favorable endpoint and U_plus the simultaneous upper confidence bound for the most favorable endpoint. Define exactly one atom:

- P if L_minus>5;
- F if U_plus<=5;
- Q otherwise.

P means the margin is supported for every admissible documentary-M2 completion in that registered state. F means no admissible completion attains the strict margin with simultaneous uncertainty. Q preserves partial-identification or sampling uncertainty. Exact equality is F.

Apply the parent's partition unchanged:

- contrast-year supportive if all its states are P; heterogeneous if at least one P and one F; adverse if at least one F and no P; otherwise inconclusive;
- year supportive if all atoms are P; heterogeneous if at least one P and one F; adverse if at least one F and no P; otherwise inconclusive;
- global universal-supportive if every atom in both years is P and permutation p<=.025;
- global decisive-threshold-heterogeneity if at least one atom is P and at least one is F;
- global universal-adverse-without-certified-positive-coordinate if at least one atom is F and none is P;
- global inconclusive otherwise or whenever a gate fails.

The global output is a one-hot four-element vector. Years and contrasts cannot be pooled, averaged, compensated, or rescued by surface, sensitivity, or access results. A supportive/adverse combination is heterogeneity adverse to the universal claim, not a biological interaction. A favorable point interval crossing 5 remains inconclusive. Adverse results are scientifically valid and must not be penalized for contradicting the hypothesis.

## 13. Verification and evidence boundary

Automatic verification can establish:

- source/header/snapshot hashes, exact joins, raw backpointers, filters, interval logic, and quarantine;
- completeness and cardinality of the 1-27 state registry, manifest lineage, allowed terminal values, and absence of patientwise reader mixing;
- one index row per patient per state and no cross-period reuse;
- consensus-only development loss; 2019-only hyperparameter choice; fixed model/preprocessor hashes across states and bootstraps;
- byte-identical shared Z,S in R and G; independent R optimization; all-and-only X masking in same-fit Gmask;
- N_ref, K, ranking/ties, top-K hashes, analytic set-valued endpoint formulas, bootstrap multiplicities, max-T tensor dimensions/arithmetic, permutation arithmetic, and access MIP gap;
- the one-hot mutually exclusive conclusion mapping and exact linkage from each claim to result rows, gates, hashes, and raw-result locators.

Required adversarial fixtures include:

1. A synthetic two-reader disagreement creates at most the three domain manifests, not patientwise combinations.
2. The registry rejects 0 or >27 states, missing manifest rows, post-lock edits, and incompatible backpointers.
3. Model hashes differ across no state or bootstrap; any refit fails.
4. Analytic delta_minus/delta_plus equal exhaustive completion extrema for u<=20, including overlapping top-K sets and repeated patient clones.
5. R and G receive byte-identical Z,S; Gmask changes all and only X.
6. The max-T tensor contains each registered state/year/contrast endpoint exactly once; no outcome-completion axis exists.
7. Supportive, P/F heterogeneous, F-only adverse, and Q/gate-failure inconclusive fixtures map to exactly one global label. A valid adverse result is accepted.
8. Numerically correct results paired with claims of real-time availability, completed surgery, specimen-linked biological M2, clinical triage utility, recurrence/survival benefit, fairness, cost-effectiveness, or transportability are rejected.

Clinical experts, not the verifier, must judge label dictionaries, examination partitions/modalities, stored-text concepts, and pathology wording. The verifier can confirm that signed artifacts were used, not that they are clinically correct.

The strongest possible supportive conclusion is: “In this frozen retrospective HCC eventual-report corpus, one consensus-trained fixed score used adjudicated stored-report meanings to add more than 5 documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed positions beyond the same acquisition/documentation surface, in both 2020 and 2021 and across the registered expert manifests and all declared outcome completions.”

Specimen-linked timestamped pathology and a sampling protocol are required for biological M2. Raw images and image-reference adjudication are required for imaging-feature accuracy. Report final/release/version/view logs are required for predecision availability. A prospective silent-mode study with a real candidate stream, capacity, clinician actions, and subgroup monitoring is required for operational validity. A controlled impact study with treatment, harms, recurrence, survival, resources, and costs is required for clinical benefit. External sites are required for transportability.
