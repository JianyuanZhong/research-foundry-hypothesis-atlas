# Provisional E-MTAB-8873 repair: donor-aware macrophage-condition signature

## Proposed relationship

In treatment-none E-MTAB-8873 samples, exposure of RA FLS to MerTK/CD206-positive macrophages is associated with a reproducible, condition-specific transcriptional change relative to both FLS-only and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage co-culture. The claim is intentionally observational: the available data can test a donor-aware association, not direct ligand-receptor action or a clinical mechanism.

## Evidence and exact opening

The parent proposal, supported by inspected literature, establishes that reciprocal macrophage-FLS communication is a plausible unresolved explanation for sustained RA synovial inflammation, but does not establish this targeted assay's response. The local prepared source contains a 445-feature matrix and a 33-record design with sample_id, donor_id, donor_status, condition, treatment, age, sex, tissue, cell_type, cells, and source_metadata. The matrix has a feature column plus 33 sample columns that join to design sample_id. The feature identifiers retain source format (gene|transcript|Reference_end). The design explicitly marks composite contributing-donor records (for example SA266A, SA277A) and includes one donor/metadata conflict that must not be silently resolved.

The unresolved issue is whether “donor-aware” can be executed when donor_id is sometimes a composite pair and other_donor_id is empty. The repair is to define the estimand at the level of observed contributing-donor groups, not to pretend each sample is an independent donor or to split a composite pair without source evidence.

## Executable population and contrasts

Use all 33 records with a verified sample_id join. Restrict the primary analysis to treatment=none. Define the primary contrast as MerTK/CD206-positive macrophage co-culture versus FLS-only, with a secondary contrast versus MerTK/CD206-negative co-culture. Mixed macrophage conditions are a composition check; LPS and inhibitor conditions are mechanistic context and are not independent causal replicates.

For each feature, use log2(count + 1) or a prespecified small count offset. The baseline is a donor-aware stratified contrast: compute condition differences within each observed contributing-donor group that has both arms, then combine group-level differences with a fixed or random-effects summary that treats groups as the dependence unit. If a group has only one arm, it contributes to descriptive arm summaries but not to that paired contrast. Report the number of contributing groups, arm counts, and which groups support each contrast.

The hierarchical alternative uses the same inputs and contrasts, with a feature-level random intercept/slope or partial-pooling model across the 445 measured features and a contributing-donor-group random effect. It can reveal coordinated condition-specific structure while borrowing information across features, but it cannot recover donor identity that is absent from the source. It is scientifically meaningful only if the group-level baseline is estimable; otherwise it is deferred and the result is inconclusive.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive requires a prespecified effect-size threshold (for example absolute log2 fold change >=0.25), donor-group-aware uncertainty, separation from both controls, and no comparable signal in the negative-control or composition checks. Adverse means no separation, reversal, or loss of signal after donor-group adjustment, or a signal confined to one contributing group. Inconclusive means the design cannot support the paired contrast, donor mapping remains unresolved, uncertainty is too wide, or the hierarchical model is not identifiable.

The exact deliverable is a fitted contrast table with feature effects, group support, uncertainty, permutation/clustered uncertainty, negative-control behavior, and an explicit supportive/adverse/inconclusive label. No computed result is claimed here.

## Missing evidence and limits

The source does not provide independent biological replicates, complete donor-level covariates, or a verified crosswalk that resolves composite donor strings into separate biological units. Cell-level rows are not independent donors. Targeted expression cannot establish effects on unmeasured genes. Clinical phenotype, treatment history, direct receptor occupancy, ligand-receptor specificity, and external validation are unavailable. Causal or clinical claims require perturbation, independent replication, or expert adjudication.
