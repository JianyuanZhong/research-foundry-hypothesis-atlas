## Revised renal and competing-event outcomes

## Falsification, branch priority, and interpretation

Priority is now executable and safety-preserving:

1. Infeasible if a source, population, overlap, model, or bootstrap execution gate fails.
2. Inconclusive-diagnostic if balance, calibration, negative-control, missingness, discordance, or required sensitivity gates fail.
3. Adverse if any parent adverse criterion holds, or the renal/death lower confidence limit crosses +0.03 for RCRD48 or +0.05 for RRT7-M/RRTD7.
4. Supportive-recorded only if every parent supportive condition holds and all three added upper confidence limits stay below their margins.
5. Margin-falsified if the airway upper limit is at most +0.05 without an adverse result.
6. Inconclusive otherwise.

Thus simultaneous respiratory support and renal/terminal adversity is adverse, never supportive. Supportive results would justify only an overlap-population association with the recorded airway proxy while all prespecified recorded harm bounds remain below margins. Adverse results identify a record-defined safety signal, not loop toxicity. Margin-falsified rejects the five-point respiratory margin, not every possible physiologic benefit. Inconclusive means overlap, capture, competing events, diagnostics, precision, or sensitivities did not resolve the decision. Negative findings remain valuable.
