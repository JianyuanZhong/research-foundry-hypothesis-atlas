# Baseline-deployable cystatin-C discordance prioritization at the next observed repeat

## Lineage, unresolved claim, and clinical importance

This is a targeted successor of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`, retaining its exact UK Biobank snapshot, three-file construction, field/equation certificate, selected complete-repeat-attender evidence boundary, and aggregate-only reporting. The substantive repair is that the deployable score contains **no future-known repeat interval, repeat date, repeat biomarker, or any other post-baseline variable**. Timing is used only to define the observed outcome and in prespecified secondary diagnostics; it is never a feature, tuning variable, ranking variable, or selection criterion.

The strongest existing claim is narrow: among people who have a qualifying repeat biomarker observation, a baseline cystatin-C-derived low-eGFR state is associated with the repeat creatinine-derived low-eGFR state after adjustment. That association does not establish incremental out-of-sample information, a fixed-horizon risk, CKD, or a benefit from ordering a test. The unresolved falsifiable claim is:

> Conditional on a certified interpretation of the exported fields, among UK Biobank participants with baseline creatinine-equation eGFR >=60 and complete qualifying instance-1 repeat biomarkers, does baseline cystatin-C threshold discordance improve baseline-only out-of-sample prediction of the observed repeat creatinine-equation eGFR <60 state beyond a prespecified creatinine/age/sex comparator?

This matters because clinicians may consider whether a second kidney marker could identify which patients merit closer repeat assessment. The experiment can establish only incremental predictive information in an observed-repeat protocol. It cannot establish that testing should be ordered, that a patient benefits, that CKD is present, or that risk is the same in people who do not return.

## Baseline-only prediction experiment

Compare two fixed algorithms, evaluated on the identical frozen `S_A` rows with ten repeated outer five-fold cross-fitting (seeds `260911` through `260920`), stratified by `E x Y` only:

* `M0`: ridge logistic regression with baseline eGFRcr, baseline age, and sex;
* `M1`: the same model plus `E` and the prespecified interaction `E*(eGFRcr_0-60)/10`.

Use training-only restricted cubic splines (five eGFR knots at quantiles .05,.275,.50,.725,.95; four age knots at .05,.35,.65,.95), sex-by-eGFR interactions as prespecified, training-only centering/scaling, unpenalized intercept, and the fixed lambda grid `10^(-4),10^(-3.5),...,10^4`; select lambda by nested five-fold log loss one-standard-error rule using only training data. The exact feature specification must be dimension-audited; no `t`, date, repeat value, missingness learned from repeat fields, or post-baseline field may enter. Inner seeds are `outer_seed+1000+outer_fold`. Enforce zero eid overlap between each test participant and every training/tuning row used for that prediction. Average the ten out-of-fold probabilities per participant; clip only log loss to `[1e-6,1-1e-6]` and report clipping counts.

Primary estimand: `Delta_Brier=1000*[mean((Y-p0)^2)-mean((Y-p1)^2)]` over `S_A`. Secondary, descriptive capacity contrast: for `K=ceil(.10*N)`, rank by averaged out-of-fold probabilities with tie-break `SHA-256("direction-A-capacity-v1|"+eid)` and report `Delta_rank10=1000*(events in top-K M1-events in top-K M0)/K`. This is a fixed-capacity, schedule-marginal protocol-ranking contrast among people already observed to have a qualifying complete repeat; it is not event yield from ordering a test, net benefit, utility, or a testing policy.

Use paired participant bootstrap of the frozen ten-column prediction matrices, 2,000 successful replicates (maximum 2,500 attempts), seed `260921`, percentile 95% interval and plus-one symmetric null-centered p-value. Test Brier first; only if its prespecified gate passes, evaluate the capacity secondary. Report aggregate predictions/metrics only; never export eid-level rows or clinical notes.

Adverse means the added baseline marker reproducibly worsens the primary score, or a prespecified artifact/null/fold/calibration gate fails in a direction undermining the claim. Inconclusive means the interval includes zero, support is insufficient, signs are unstable, bootstrap fails, or timing bands are too sparse; non-rejection is not equivalence.
