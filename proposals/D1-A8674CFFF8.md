# Ascertainment-stratified partial identification for frozen HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B validation

## Successor, unresolved claim, and scope

This is a narrow two-parent successor of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen adult HCC repeat-TACE prognostic schema, exact event and assay clocks, annual forward blocks, locked nested ridge models, and noncausal estimand. The only substantive change is to make the missing-`Y` partial-identification layer explicitly stratified by outcome-blind ascertainment opportunity, so that the analysis can show whether any unidentified contrast is concentrated in episodes with weaker recording opportunity rather than hiding that uncertainty in one aggregate interval.

The hypothesis remains:

> Among adults selected for a recorded repeat TACE, does contemporaneous pre-repeat assay `P` add reproducible short-horizon prognostic information for the subsequent same-encounter assay `Y` beyond remote baseline `B` and the identical compact decision-time core, when the analysis is evaluated forward in calendar time and does not assume that missing `Y` is ignorable?

The strongest existing evidence is narrower: the retained full-source outcome-blind audit supports the 2022 block for the selected complete-triplet mixture, while 2023 and 2024 are not transport-interpretable because of arm sparsity and severe, measured outcome-observation loss. The new stratified bounds below are a prespecified executable analysis, not executed results. No new numeric bound, robustness conclusion, or clinical finding is claimed here.

This is prognostic association only. It does not estimate treatment effects, response, toxicity, safety, mortality, hepatic failure, clinical benefit, utility, actionability, transport to another institution, or an intervention rule. A recorded medication order is not administration, an examination row is not completed follow-up, and a laboratory value without unit/reference metadata is not a validated clinical severity measure.

## Frozen cohort, clocks, models, and estimand

Reconstruct and assert exactly 319 systemic-record and 1,491 comparator patients; 159 and 526 selected repeat events; and complete assay triplets albumin 40/118 and total bilirubin 41/119 (systemic/comparator). Event selection is outcome-blind and may not inspect labs, examinations, documents, or future outcomes.

For each assay and selected event, `B` is the latest finite uncensored value in the TACE2 calendar-day window [day -30, day -1]. `P` is the latest finite value in the selected repeat encounter in [event time -72 hours, event time). `Y` is the finite value in that encounter in (event time, event time +72 hours], selected nearest +24 hours using the inherited deterministic tie rule. Require `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time`, positive P lead, and one patient-assay row after duplicate/tie handling. Construct `BP_eligible` before looking at Y; absent Y is missing, never a negative result.

Use origins 2021-01-01, 2022-01-01, 2023-01-01, and 2024-01-01. Training events have event date < origin; future blocks are disjoint `[origin, origin+365 days)`. Fit the locked models using only observed complete-triplet training rows:

```text
M0: Y ~ B + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
M1: M0 + P
```

Within each origin, use training-only age median imputation plus missingness indicator, sex coding, variance filtering, centering/scaling, inner leave-one-patient-out alpha selection over [0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100], and ridge fitting. Freeze finite pre-origin predictions `p0_i,p1_i` for every BP-eligible future row. The primary estimand remains observed complete-triplet standardized held-out RMSE gain `G=(RMSE_0-RMSE_1)/SD_test(Y)`, with the parent fixed-prediction paired arm-stratified patient-frequency bootstrap and its original point-estimate SD denominator. The partial-identification layer cannot change the primary fit, test rows, denominator, or CI.

## Outcome-blind ascertainment strata

For every assay, origin, and block, create the funnel `selected event -> has_B -> has_P -> BP_eligible -> Y_observed -> complete`. For every BP-eligible row, before inspecting or selecting Y, compute three binary recording-opportunity indicators:

- `exam_opportunity=1` iff an `examinations` row linked by the composite key has valid `开始时间` in `(event_time,event_time+72h]`.
- `followup_encounter=1` iff an additional distinct `encounters` row has valid `就诊时间` in `(event_time,event_time+72h]`.
- `document_presence=1` iff a nonempty `clinical_documents` row is linked to the selected encounter by the composite key. It is untimed and is not evidence of a Y-window follow-up.

Assign exactly one mutually exclusive stratum by precedence: `exam+followup`, `exam_only`, `followup_only`, `document_only` (document present with neither timed indicator), and `none`. Retain a separate document cross-tab for the cases where a timed indicator co-occurs with a document. These strata are ascertainment proxies, not completed care or clinical adjudication. Report N, observed-Y count, observed fraction and Wilson 95% interval by stratum, arm, assay, origin, and future block; suppress or mark cells with fewer than five BP-eligible rows. No stratum is silently excluded.

Fit the existing training-only outcome-observation logistic diagnostic from pre-Y B/P, age, sex, P-lead, TACE2-to-repeat days, arm, calendar block, and the three indicators. Report predicted probability range, Brier score, observed fraction, inverse-observation-probability ESS among observed rows, and pre-Y observed-versus-missing SMDs. The model is a diagnostic only: do not call it MAR evidence, impute Y, or reweight the primary estimand. Preserve the parent executed support/selection artifact and its findings as historical support only; do not represent them as execution of this successor.

## Ascertainment-stratified missing-Y partial identification

Run the following deterministic calculation for every assay, origin, future block, arm, and ascertainment stratum with at least one BP-eligible row. Fit M0/M1 only on exact observed training triplets and freeze predictions for all BP rows. Let `N_s` be BP-eligible rows in stratum s, `n_s,mis` the rows lacking Y, and `N=sum_s N_s`. Define the training-derived range `L=min(Y_train)` and `U=max(Y_train)` over finite parser-valid training outcomes. If the range is empty, degenerate, any observed future Y lies outside `[L,U]`, or a required prediction is nonfinite, mark the affected stratum and aggregate temporal bound `unsupported` and do not widen the range or drop rows. A full-source range may be emitted only as separately labeled descriptive information.

For observed row i, set `e0_i=(Y_i-p0_i)^2`, `e1_i=(Y_i-p1_i)^2`, and `d_i=e0_i-e1_i`. For missing row i and model j, use the range completion extrema

```text
e_j,min=(clip(p_j,L,U)-p_j)^2
 e_j,max=max((L-p_j)^2,(U-p_j)^2).
```

For the M0-minus-M1 contrast use the direct affine identity

```text
d_i(y)=2*y*(p1_i-p0_i)+p0_i^2-p1_i^2.
```

Within each stratum, add observed errors and the exact missing-row endpoint extrema `min(d_i(L),d_i(U))` and `max(d_i(L),d_i(U))`, divide by fixed `N_s`, and report sharp conditional `Delta_s,lower` and `Delta_s,upper` MSE-contrast bounds. Aggregate the strata by summing endpoint numerators and dividing by fixed `N`; do not average stratum estimates with ad hoc weights. Also report arm-specific aggregates using the arm-specific BP denominator. A positive Delta means lower M1 squared error, not clinical improvement.

Report, separately for every stratum and aggregate, the three deterministic endpoint scenarios: all missing at L; all at U; and each missing row at the least-favorable endpoint for the lower M0-minus-M1 contrast (L when its affine slope `2*(p1-p0) >= 0`, otherwise U). The identification region is conditional on observed rows and frozen predictions, not a confidence interval and not a clinically plausible imputation. If uncertainty around bounds is requested, optionally run only the fixed-prediction patient-cluster bootstrap specified by the parent, within arm and retaining stratum membership; never combine its percentile interval with the deterministic region.

For each model and stratum, divide its SSE extrema by N_s for MSE extrema. The conservative non-primary RMSE-difference enclosure is `[sqrt(MSE0_lower)-sqrt(MSE1_upper), sqrt(MSE0_upper)-sqrt(MSE1_lower)]`; it is not sharp and is never standardized by an unidentified test SD. If test-Y SD is nonfinite or zero, primary G and its CI are non-estimable while valid unstandardized bounds remain reportable.

Add a deterministic ascertainment concentration table: `width_s = Delta_s,upper-Delta_s,lower`, `missing_fraction_s`, and `width_s*N_s/(width*N)` when total width is positive. This is a decomposition of mathematical uncertainty, not evidence that an ascertainment stratum caused missingness. If every missing row is bounded identically, report zero contribution rather than inventing a ranking.

## Common-displacement tipping analysis

For each stratum and aggregate, let `m_ref` be the observed future-Y mean if available, otherwise the training observed-Y mean marked training-derived. Set every missing row to `clip(m_ref+delta,L,U)` and evaluate the affine contrast. The function is continuous piecewise linear with shared breakpoints `L-m_ref` and `U-m_ref`; on each segment solve using slope `sum_missing 2*(p1-p0)/N_s`, retain roots only within the segment, and verify by direct endpoint evaluation. Report all valid roots and select the smallest absolute root, ties by smallest delta. If no root exists, report sign-robust only for this common-displacement scenario. Report stratum roots separately and do not interpret a small delta as clinical plausibility.

## Falsification, interpretation, and verification contract

Supportive interpretation requires reconstruction and clocks to pass, the outcome-blind BP and complete-triplet arm gates to pass, an estimable primary result, no material ascertainment warning, and Delta strictly positive in every declared aggregate and non-small stratum. It supports only selected short-horizon prognostic association in that calendar mixture. A positive bound is deterministic conditional evidence, not a sampling-confidence statement and not transport evidence.

Adverse interpretation is a gated primary contrast at or below zero, repeated M1 degradation, or a bounded Delta that is nonpositive in a non-small stratum. Selection-sensitive interpretation takes precedence when observed-case performance is favorable but any aggregate or clinically relevant stratum bound crosses zero, missingness is concentrated in low-opportunity strata, observation ESS is low, or observed-versus-missing SMDs are large. Failed gates, unsupported training range, nonfinite predictions, zero/undefined SD, fewer than two observed patient clusters, or dependence on a full-source range are inconclusive. A favorable sparse point estimate never overrides these statuses. Existing 2022 support and 2023/2024 transport-inconclusive findings remain parent findings only; this proposal makes no new finding.

A verifier must check that (1) strata are built before Y is read; (2) untimed documents never create a Y-window timestamp; (3) examination or encounter presence is not called completed follow-up; (4) all BP rows remain in the bound denominator; (5) bounds use direct affine contrasts rather than subtracting separately optimized model bounds; (6) common-displacement tipping is piecewise linear; (7) bound strata are aggregated by endpoint numerators and fixed denominators; (8) the primary fit and fixed-denominator bootstrap remain unchanged; (9) deterministic bounds are not labeled confidence intervals; (10) small-cell and failed-range branches remain inconclusive; and (11) no output is upgraded to causal, treatment, safety, utility, transport, benefit, or actionability evidence.

Computationally checkable outputs are source hashes and headers, duplicate-safe joins, frozen denominators and clocks, outcome-blind funnel and strata, finite training-range checks, all endpoint arithmetic, concentration decomposition, tipping roots, prediction finiteness, and bootstrap bookkeeping. Units/reference ranges, severity, imaging response, rationale, administration, external care, mortality, completed follow-up, and clinical benefit require expert adjudication and an independent temporal/external/prospective dataset.
