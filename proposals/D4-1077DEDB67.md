## Unresolved clinical question and substantive advance

The mature parent asks whether direction-free movement between a prior and current eight-field panel improves fixed-capacity prioritization of a pooled later recorded circulatory event after controlling for current and prior values, repeat spacing, calendar timing, and measured repeat-panel selection. That is a useful ranking question, but a pooled circulatory rank can conceal the clinically different transition being prioritized: a person whose first new recorded domain is cardiovascular may require a different review pathway from one whose first new domain is renal or diabetes-family. UKB-09 motivates this distinction, but is only an untested seed and supplies no local field semantics or result.

The strongest available evidence is structural: all sixteen cells of the eight-field pane are exposed at instances 0 and 1; field 53 has instances 0 and 1 in the assessment table; health outcomes exposes 259 same-position code/date pairs for 41270/41280 plus death dates; and the catalog specifies one-to-one horizontal eid joins. This supports a bounded recorded-endpoint experiment. It does not establish that the eight fields are clinically “metabolic,” that instance 1 was collected on the assessment date, that a numeric change is deterioration, that a code is incident disease, or that a negative record means disease-free follow-up.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among UKB participants with a valid comparable paired panel and no prior recorded cardiovascular, renal-family, or diabetes-family endpoint at the current assessment, adding fold-local direction-free cross-field movement to current/prior levels and observed repeat-selection information improves calibrated discrimination and fixed-capacity capture of the first subsequent recorded domain, and changes domain assignment (cardiovascular versus renal-family versus diabetes-family) rather than merely reproducing the parent’s generic circulatory ranking.

A null or domain-neutral result is scientifically informative: it would favor the simpler current/prior model for this recorded-transition task and weaken the case that repeat movement identifies an organ-specific review pathway. A positive result supports only conditional prognostic association in repeat-observed UKB participants and prospective evaluation of domain-aware review; it does not establish causal disease progression, treatment benefit, or a clinical threshold.

## Population and time boundaries

Require all sixteen biomarker cells at both instances under prespecified numeric/range/missingness rules; without certified units and comparable field identity, use only an explicitly recorded numeric movement representation and report the gate as unresolved.

Construct the repeat-selection population from every T0-complete person with valid T0 and the eight T0 fields, whether or not T1 is observed. Define S=1 only after valid T1, all eight T1 fields, timing and quality rules; never use future outcomes to define S. Fit the observed selection model using T0 fields, recruitment age, sex and T0 calendar year only. Report selection fraction, overlap, standardized differences, extreme propensities and weighted ESS. Weighting addresses measured repeat availability only.

The analytic cohort is S=1, with no qualifying C/R/M endpoint and no death on or before T1, and with endpoint-ascertainment eligibility. Split by deterministic participant-level hash of eid: early fitting/evaluation has T1 through 2012-12-31; late application has T1 from 2013-01-01 through 2013-12-31 and no refit. Follow each T1 for five calendar years with explicit leap-day handling. No late outcomes, cutpoints, hyperparameters, or transforms may enter early fitting. If five-year completion cannot be certified, report recorded follow-up only and mark clinical five-year inference inconclusive.

## Recorded first-domain endpoint

For every j=0,...,258, pair 41270-0.j only with 41280-0.j; never sort arrays independently or nearest-date match. Normalize nonempty codes by trimming, uppercasing and taking the ICD-10 category before the decimal. After dictionary review, prespecify and freeze:

- C: cardiovascular category I00-I99;
- R: renal/urinary category N00-N29;
- M: diabetes-family category E08-E13.

These are labels for recorded code families, not proof that a participant has true organ disease or that the biomarker panel is metabolic. A nonempty code without a valid same-position date, or a nonempty date without its same-position code, is an endpoint-integrity failure. Any pre-T1 C/R/M code with an invalid date is not silently treated as absent. An accepted participant with a malformed required endpoint position fails closed.

Parse 40000-0.0 and 40000-1.0 as death dates; if both are present they must agree, otherwise fail closed. For each eligible participant, find the earliest valid post-T1 date through H among C/R/M code-family records. If exactly one family is present on that date, label C, R or M; if two or three families share the date, label X (multi-domain tie). Death at an earlier or equal date is D; otherwise no C/R/M or death through H is N. The outcome is therefore C/R/M/X/D/N, a first recorded transition from an endpoint-free landmark—not disease onset or a biological transition. Require at least 100 total C/R/M events and at least 30 events in each single-domain class, with support/ESS criteria fixed before outcome inspection; otherwise the organ-specific analysis is inconclusive and only the prespecified pooled continuity output may be reported.

## Movement and nested models

Within each early training fold, fit an empirical percentile map for each field using eligible training-fold T0 values only; apply it unchanged to T0/T1 in held-out and late data, clip and report out-of-range values. For field j, u_ij=|F_j(x_i,T1)-F_j(x_i,T0)|; B_i=mean_j(u_ij), H_i=SD_j(u_ij). Do not name analytes, assign worsening directions, or call B/H deterioration. Fit stabilized observed-selection weights w=Pr(S=1)/p(S=1|T0 panel, age, sex, T0 year), with early-fold propensity and early 1st/99th percentile truncation; report unweighted and weighted results.

Use a single early participant-level five-fold split. All imputation, maps, ranges, propensity, penalties, calibration and cutpoints are fold-local. Models are:

- M0: current eight-field values + recruitment age + sex + T1 calendar year;
- Ms: M0 + L + T0 calendar year + fold-local logit selection propensity;
- Mp: Ms + eight prior T0 values;
- Mm: Mp + B + H (primary movement increment);
- Mu: Mp + all eight u_ij (secondary movement pattern).

Use regularized multinomial additive models for C/R/M/X/D/N. The primary scientific contrast is Mm versus Mp; Ms and Mp isolate spacing/observation metadata and prior absolute levels. The parent’s pooled circulatory target and fixed-capacity Mm-versus-Mp analysis are continuity outputs, not the new organ-specific claim.

## Fixed-capacity and domain-specific evaluation

Freeze the parent-style central current-risk band A from early out-of-fold Ms predictions for the parent pooled circulatory target, using q20 <= r < q80, and apply q20/q80 unchanged in 2013. Set K=floor(0.10*n_A,early) before inspecting outcomes; require K>=40 and n_A,late>=K. For every model, select exactly K highest held-out max(p_C,p_R,p_M) within A, with deterministic eid-hash ties. This is one fixed total review capacity, not three separate domain quotas. Assign a reviewed participant the argmax domain, retaining X/D probabilities.

Report total target capture/yield and domain-specific C/R/M capture, yield and assignment confusion in the K list, plus weighted and unweighted multiclass Brier score, log loss, calibration intercept/slope, and conditional domain log loss among observed C/R/M events. The primary organ-specific estimand is the paired change in conditional domain log loss and macro one-vs-rest Brier for Mm-Mp; the fixed-capacity estimand is the change in total capture and per-domain capture at K. Also report whether the selected domain composition changes relative to Mp and whether gains remain after the parent pooled circulatory score is included in Mp. No metric is interpreted as clinical utility without a prospective policy study.

For the learned alternative, fit matched shallow gradient-boosted multinomial models Gs/Gp/Gm/Gu with exactly the same inputs, participants, split, endpoint, A, K, weights, calibration and bootstrap. The learned models are retained because thresholds, saturation and interactions can distinguish domain-specific patterns that an additive model averages away. A learned-vs-additive difference is a functional-form finding, not mechanism evidence.

Use paired participant bootstrap with 2,000 replicates within fixed era-by-A strata, preserving frozen predictions, lists, weights, A and K; report support failures and simultaneous/multiplicity-adjusted intervals. Report early and late and the transport contrast (late minus early). No late refit is permitted.

## Falsification and interpretation

Required nulls are: within-fold/era permutation of C/R/M/X/D/N labels; within-A permutation of B/H preserving current levels, propensity, missingness and K; participant permutation of prior panels before movement construction; replacement of prior with current values (u=0); early/late label permutation; random-K lists; and deliberately broken same-position code/date pairing, which must trigger the integrity gate. Compare complete-case with fold-local missingness only as a prespecified sensitivity. All nulls and gate outcomes are retained.

Support requires certified fields/timing/ranges, valid joins and endpoint pairing, complete five-year ascertainment, event/domain support, overlap/ESS, calibrated held-out predictions, a positive movement-vs-prior domain improvement with a simultaneous interval excluding zero, concordant fixed-capacity and per-domain results, no early/late sign reversal, stable folds, and nulls that remove the gain. This supports only a conditional association with recorded first-domain prioritization in the supported repeat-observed population.

Adverse evidence includes no domain improvement, gain explained by prior levels or repeat selection, movement-null survival, calibration failure, random-list superiority, domain instability, temporal reversal, or a pooled gain with no domain separation. It weakens movement-aware organ-specific review for this endpoint; it does not prove no trajectory is useful elsewhere.

Inconclusive evidence includes missing dictionary/units, unverified specimen timing, malformed pairs, death disagreement, incomplete follow-up, sparse domain events, poor overlap/ESS, broad intervals, unstable missingness/clipping, or inability to separate analytical variation, regression to the mean, treatment, assay drift and selection. A clinical adjudication study is required to establish actual disease onset, outpatient disease, symptom/severity context, treatment effects, specimen comparability and actionable thresholds. External validation and prospective benefit require another study. An automatic verifier can check the data construction, fits, predictions, metrics, uncertainty, nulls and conclusion-to-output linkage, but cannot establish these clinical claims.

The actual deliverable is a reproducible package containing the fail-closed/certified audit; exact joins and cohort flow; fold manifests, propensities, weights, percentile maps and clipping; frozen A and K; held-out early/no-refit late predictions for M0, Ms, Mp, Mm, Mu and matched learned models; pooled continuity and organ-specific metrics; domain composition; 2,000-replicate uncertainty; falsification outputs; and a conclusion explicitly linked to those files. A row count, readiness probe or isolated fit is not completion.

CPU is appropriate for the small tabular experiment. The future planning envelope is <=16 CPUs, 262144 MiB and 28,800 seconds; unverified estimate is 2–8 CPU-hours and 64–128 GiB peak memory, with no GPU required. Discovery used no model fit. A GPU is not excluded categorically, but should be reconsidered only if a measured solver diagnostic shows the matched learned model cannot fit within the CPU envelope.

Not chosen: signed analyte worsening (no certified local directions/semantics); raw-unit differences (units/scales absent); latent GP/sequence/mechanistic models (no richer dated trajectories or adjudicated latent target); images and raw sequence variants (unavailable; genomics is derived metadata); separate per-domain quotas (violates fixed total capacity); and treatment/cause claims (no treatment exposure or causal design). Revisit these only with the stated missing evidence or a new study.
