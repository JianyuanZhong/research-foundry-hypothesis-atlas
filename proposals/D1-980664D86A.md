# MIMIC AKI discordance: all-eligible landmark flow and component-specific outcomes

Status: design-only substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. No row scan, cohort count, fitted model, Harbor execution, or clinical result was run.

## Scientific question and advance

The component-specific question is retained:

> After a creatinine-defined AKI episode, does lagged discordance between creatinine recovery and direct urine/recorded-input recovery identify different subsequent risks of hospital death and ICU-recorded RRT?

The parent’s unresolved selection problem is made primary rather than secondary. A conventional landmark risk set that keeps only patients alive in the ICU at L with adequate observations conditions simultaneously on survival, ICU retention, support, and the opportunity to be measured. A discordance contrast could therefore be a survivor/ICU-exit/measurement process signal rather than a renal-recovery signal. The clinically useful advance is an all-eligible flow that makes those transitions visible and reports the renal contrast only where its component is actually observed.

The strongest evidence inspected supports three bounded premises: creatinine-only kidney assessment can be less reliable in critical illness or changing muscle mass [K1]; AKI is clinically consequential as a systemic syndrome with nonrenal complications [K2]; and dynamic landmark and sequence models should be compared with patient-level separation and calibration, not only discrimination [K3]. None establishes this MIMIC phenotype, an all-eligible estimand, true GFR, true fluid balance, complete RRT capture, mechanism, or causality.

The falsifiable hypothesis is therefore observational and MIMIC-specific:

> At L=48 hours after t0, among all first-eligible ICU AKI episodes, the all-eligible transition/outcome profile will show a component-specific difference between observed discordant and concordant recovery that is not reproduced by the pre-L survival/ICU-exit/observation flow or by a renal-value-blinded measurement-opportunity pseudo-state; the direction will have support at L=24 or 72. If the difference disappears after the flow/observation test, the renal-discordance interpretation is falsified for this dataset.

This is not a causal treatment claim or a claim that discordance measures intrinsic kidney injury.

## Population, time boundaries and all-eligible estimand

Use the parent’s exact cohort and t0 construction in `binding-audit.md`:

- adults with `hosp/patients.anchor_age >= 18`;
- join `icu/icustays` to `hosp/admissions` on `(subject_id,hadm_id)`, require the parent’s finite and temporal validity checks, clip follow-up at valid `dischtime`, and retain an `outtime > dischtime` exception flag;
- within each admission sort by `intime`, then `stay_id`, and retain exactly the first eligible ICU stay; do not replace it with a later stay;
- define t0 from primary `hosp/labevents.itemid=50912` using the parent’s clinical-`charttime`, comparator, duplicate-median, blank-`hadm_id`, pre-t0 RRT exclusion, and fail-closed rules. Item 52546 remains sensitivity-only and `storetime` never advances t0;
- for each L in `{24,48,72}` hours, `Ltime=t0+L`, `A_L=[max(t0,Ltime-24h),Ltime-6h)`, `W_L=[Ltime-6h,Ltime)`, with the parent’s six-hour bins, urine opportunity, input precedence, order categories, exact item sets and half-open interval rules unchanged.

Every t0-eligible episode is retained in the L flow. It is never dropped merely because it dies, exits the ICU, receives recorded ICU RRT, or lacks an exposure-quality observation before L. Define a mutually exclusive state using the first valid transition in `[t0,Ltime)`, with the parent’s exact tie precedence preserved and all ties recorded:

1. `D0`: hospital death before L, using valid `hosp/admissions.deathtime` and the parent’s death-time precedence.
2. `X0`: alive ICU exit before L, using clipped `icu/icustays.outtime` (and `hosp/transfers` only as a descriptive movement check).
3. `R0`: exact parent ICU-recorded RRT before L while alive and in the ICU, from `icu/procedureevents`.
4. `U0`: lost/unknown follow-up before L when no valid transition can be ordered because the observable interval or required timestamp is invalid/truncated. This is a data-ascertainment state, not assumed survival.
5. If none of D0/X0/R0/U0 occurs, the episode reaches L alive in the index ICU stay without incident ICU RRT. It is then classified, separately for each parent component c, as `C_c` (concordant), `D_c` (discordant), or `U_c` (insufficient/ambiguous observation). `U_c` is retained; it is not silently excluded. A combined state is the vector of component states, not a replacement composite.

The primary all-eligible descriptive estimand is the joint state/outcome profile:
`J_{Y,g}(L)=P(S_L=g, Y_L=1)` and `p_g(L)=P(S_L=g)`, for every pre-L transition/unknown state and every observed component state, where Y is hospital death or ICU-recorded RRT in the post-L clock below. Report the prevalence-weighted component contributions `J_{Y,D_c}-J_{Y,C_c}` only as a descriptive all-eligible quantity; it is not a potential-outcome contrast because D and C are not defined for D0/X0/R0/U0 episodes.

The component-specific clinical contrasts that remain estimable are explicitly narrower:

- `RD_H^obs(L,c)=F_H(7d | S_L=D_c, reached L observed)-F_H(7d | S_L=C_c, reached L observed)`;
- `RD_R^obs(L,c)=F_R(7d | S_L=D_c, reached L observed)-F_R(7d | S_L=C_c, reached L observed)`;
- the same hospital-death and ICU-RRT risks for `U_c`, and the pre-L flow probabilities `p_{D0},p_{X0},p_{R0},p_{U0}`, are reported beside them.

These are observed landmark prognostic contrasts conditional on reaching and being measured at L. There is no identifiable all-eligible causal or counterfactual “discordant versus concordant” contrast for episodes whose component state is not observed. A solver must not relabel pre-L transitions as concordant or discordant and must not call `RD_R^obs` hospital-wide RRT.

Post-L clocks are unchanged from the parent. Hospital death is the first valid `hosp/admissions.deathtime` in `[Ltime,Ltime+7d)`, no later than valid `dischtime`, with `hospital_expire_flag` a consistency check and alive discharge a competing event. ICU-recorded RRT is the first exact parent RRT `icu/procedureevents` row with finite `starttime` in that window, strictly before clipped ICU exit and while alive. ICU exit and hospital death before RRT compete; exact ties use the parent’s frozen precedence. Ward/post-ICU RRT is not imputed. For D0 no post-L death is assigned; for X0/R0/U0 any subsequent hospital-death information is reported as a state-specific descriptive outcome, while ICU-RRT after ICU exit is explicitly unascertainable rather than treated as zero.

## Selection and measurement falsification

The decisive check is a flow comparison, not an additional adjustment covariate.

First, publish the all-eligible L flow: denominators at t0, first pre-L transition counts and times, reach-L counts, component observation/unknown counts, and seven-day component-specific outcomes for every state. Compare the pre-L transition and observation probabilities associated with eventual D_c and C_c among subjects who reach L. A large D/C difference in pre-L survival, ICU retention, or unknown-state probability is direct evidence that the usual landmark contrast is selected.

Second, estimate a pre-L selection-weighted sensitivity. Using only t0 and the strictly pre-exposure context `B_L=[a_L-6h,a_L)`, where `a_L=max(t0,Ltime-24h)`, fit a training-frozen multinomial flow model for D0/X0/R0/U0 versus reaching L and being observed. Stabilized weights are used only to standardize the observed D_c/C_c outcome contrast to the all-eligible pre-L context distribution; no weight is interpreted causally. Report the effective sample size, truncation rule fixed before test evaluation, and weighted/unweighted `RD_H^obs`/`RD_R^obs`. A material collapse after this flow standardization supports landmark selection; persistence with adequate support weakens that explanation but does not prove a renal mechanism.

Third, create a renal-value-blinded measurement-opportunity pseudo-state. Withhold creatinine and urine values, use only B/A observation counts, unique observed hours, input-row presence, procedure/chart density and masks, and construct a matched pseudo-D/C contrast with the same all-eligible flow and component outcomes. If the pseudo-state reproduces the direction and magnitude of the renal D/C hospital-death or ICU-RRT contrast, measurement opportunity is a clinically meaningful falsification. This is not a negative-control outcome and does not claim that documentation causes death.

Required B context is strictly before A: the parent’s fixed six-vital panel (220045, 220210, 220277, 220052, 220181, 223762) and fixed laboratory tuples (primary creatinine 50912 plus the parent’s verified lactate, bicarbonate, sodium, bilirubin, platelet, WBC and urea tuples), accepted administered input, exact support/procedure bindings, and nonrenal observation density. Use finite values, exact dictionary matches, compatible units, clinical `charttime`/`starttime`, duplicate rules, masks and truncated-duration flags. Missing is not normal. If B has fewer than two valid observation hours, mark it incomplete; do not impute or silently stratify it away. The parent’s A-window process measures remain separate and cannot replace B.

Supportive results require: a coherent all-eligible flow; a L=48 D/C component contrast with uncertainty excluding only clinically prespecified triviality, directional support at L=24 or L=72; persistence after pre-L flow standardization in adequately supported cells; no comparable renal-value-blinded opportunity contrast; and no explanation by ICU-exit composition or boundary/storetime/item sensitivity. This supports a reproducible conditional prognostic association plus an all-eligible state profile, not mechanism, true GFR, or causality.

Adverse results are a collapse after flow standardization, concentration in D0/X0/R0/U0-prone strata, a comparable opportunity pseudo-state, a large D/C difference in reach-L/observation probability, or an ICU-exit gradient that accounts for ICU-RRT. These falsify the claim that the observed gap is specifically renal discordance, while not proving normal kidneys. Inconclusive results include sparse transitions, fewer than 10 component events, high unknown fractions, unstable weights/effective sample size, invalid timestamps, or a learned improvement without calibrated component-flow replication. An imprecise null is not refutation.

## Matched baseline and substantive learned alternative

The actual scientific deliverable is newly estimated all-eligible flow probabilities and state-specific component outcomes, plus the two matched analyses below. Both use the same cohort, t0, L values, B/A/W boundaries, exact item rules, outcomes, transition precedence and deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 split seeded `20261002`; all admissions of a subject remain in one partition.

Baseline: a fixed-summary competing-risk model with six-hour discrete hazards. At each time bin it uses prespecified renal summaries (creatinine level/peak/slope, direct-urine values and masks), accepted input/composition, support/order, B context and observation masks available up to that bin. It has absorbing pre-L heads for D0/X0/R0/U0 and a post-L head for hospital death/alive discharge and ICU-RRT/ICU exit/death. It estimates AJ-compatible state probabilities and standardized observed D/C contrasts. This is the auditable primary because its information use and transition precedence are inspectable.

Learned alternative: a small two-stream masked GRU (CPU-first; one A100 optional) on the same six-hour sequence. The renal stream contains creatinine/direct urine, item composition and masks; the process stream contains accepted input, support/procedure indicators, chart/lab density, observation opportunity, missingness and ordering. Static age/sex/first-unit and B summaries are concatenated. The model has absorbing competing-transition heads before L and separate component-specific post-L cause heads. It receives no post-transition rows, future ICU movement, post-L value, or outcome-derived feature.

The learned model adds order and persistence that fixed summaries lose: whether creatinine improves before urine, whether input accumulation persists across bins, whether support precedes or follows the renal change, and whether missingness/observation intensity changes before the transition. The alternative cannot reveal latent GFR, true volume, treatment intent or mechanism. It is retained only if held-out state-flow calibration and component CIF/Brier performance are stable, the result is not driven by the U0/X0/R0 heads, and it changes a component/order/selection interpretation at L=48 with directional support at another landmark. A small AUROC gain or opaque embedding is not a scientific success.

Outputs: exact flow and cell counts; D0/X0/R0/U0 timing table; C/D/U component denominators; AJ and model-based seven-day hospital-death and ICU-RRT CIFs; unweighted and flow-standardized contrasts; opportunity pseudo-state contrast; held-out multinomial calibration, component CIF calibration, Brier scores, observed/predicted event counts, effective sample size and 1,000 subject-cluster bootstrap intervals. Conclusions must link each claim to saved computed outputs and label supportive/adverse/inconclusive.

Future solver resources are an estimate, not a measured runtime: 16 CPU, 262,144 MiB RAM, at most 28,800 seconds. CPU is appropriate for archive scans, fixed summaries, flow/AJ estimation and bootstrap. The GRU may use CPU if sparse sequence size permits; an allocated A100 job may use `cuda:0` for repeated fits, but ordinary shell CUDA visibility is not a feasibility test. Unverified quantities are eligible t0 episodes/events, transition sparsity, feature materialization size, effective sample size and convergence.

## Alternatives not chosen and revisit criteria

- A latent GFR/volume state-space model is deferred because the configured data lack measured GFR, cystatin C, muscle mass, tubular injury markers and adjudicated volume; revisit only with those markers and expert validation.
- A causal treatment model is deferred because input/support intent, complete treatment exposure and post-ICU RRT are unavailable; revisit with treatment indication and complete follow-up.
- A larger transformer is deferred because the scientific uncertainty is flow selection and component ascertainment, not a small prediction gain; revisit if a bounded GRU shows reproducible order/persistence information and the solver budget supports a preregistered scaling check.
- A paired observed-only landmark analysis is retained only as a sensitivity, never the primary estimand, because it conditions on survival and observation opportunity.

The minimal continuation decision is: continue to full fitting only if the readiness flow can resolve all state precedence and exact item manifests; revise/stop the renal contrast if the flow is dominated by unknown/transition states or the opportunity pseudo-state reproduces it; seek adjudication/another cohort if the result remains clinically important but non-identifiable from these tables.
