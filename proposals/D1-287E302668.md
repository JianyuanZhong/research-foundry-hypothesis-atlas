# Biological reserve decline versus ascertainment intensity after first TACE

## Scientific deliverable

This is a substantive HCC successor to [prior hypothesis]. It preserves exactly the parent's first high-specificity TACE index, recorded-compensated cohort, baseline [index-14,index] and early [index+3,index+14] six-assay windows, and outcome [index+15,index+90] boundary. The successor sharpens the unresolved claim by decomposing information into:

1. a reserve-value trajectory (the measured change in albumin, bilirubin, INR/PT, platelets, creatinine and sodium); and
2. an ascertainment/opportunity process (how often the patient was present, examined and measured before the outcome window, plus whether the six assays were observed).

The new fitted deliverable is a paired, uncertainty-quantified analysis of the same patient-index records: (a) a prespecified multinomial reserve-versus-process model for no recorded event, diagnosis without corroboration, and diagnosis plus same-encounter examination corroboration; (b) a static regularized baseline and a temporal learned model evaluated on the same endpoints and locked patients; (c) process-channel ablations and record-censoring weights; and (d) all prespecified falsification and sensitivity outputs. A schema or availability audit alone is not completion.

## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the available evidence is only that this snapshot contains encounter-linked procedure and diagnosis records, timestamped laboratory results, and timestamped examination text from which an observed-care prognostic association can be computed. The prior bounded scan found 17,270 recorded-compensated first-index patients and sparse later diagnosis/corroboration events, but did not establish biological decompensation, complete TACE capture, reliable text interpretation, or complete follow-up.

The unresolved clinical claim is:

> In the exact recorded-compensated first high-specificity TACE cohort, early deterioration in the six-assay reserve trajectory is associated with the strict corroborated proxy (a new qualifying decompensation diagnosis plus a positive, same-encounter examination record) after adjustment for pre-day-15 ascertainment opportunity, whereas diagnosis-only events are more strongly explained by ascertainment intensity. A temporal model using ordered assay values should add information beyond the static baseline; any additional gain that disappears when only the process channel is removed is evidence of measurement opportunity rather than reserve biology.

This is a prognostic and measurement-process hypothesis, not a causal effect of TACE. The primary scientific contrast is the reserve association for corroborated events versus diagnosis-only events and its attenuation after process adjustment. The model comparison tests whether trajectory shape and timing carry information lost by a static summary, not whether a more complex model is clinically superior by virtue of a small prediction gain.

Support for the hypothesis requires, jointly, a non-negligible reserve-trajectory association with corroborated events, a smaller or more process-sensitive association for diagnosis-only events, paired uncertainty that is compatible with the contrast, and temporal value information that remains when measurement-process features are ablated. A diagnosis signal alone is not evidence of biological reserve decline.

## Outcomes and ascertainment process

Let D be the first new qualifying diagnosis during [index+15,index+90] for ascites, hepatic encephalopathy, hepatic failure or upper gastrointestinal bleeding. Diagnosis time is derived only from its linked encounter's 就诊时间, with the parent's 入院时间 fallback. Exclude uncertain, historical, hypothetical and clearly negated labels using the frozen parent lexical rules. D is an observed diagnosis record, not adjudicated clinical decompensation.

Let C be D plus a positive examination in the same (患者主索引,就诊号) for the first qualifying D encounter, with parseable examinations.开始时间 in [index+15,index+90]. The positive domain terms are the parent's frozen vocabulary in 检查所见 or 检查诊断 (ascites, encephalopathy, gastrointestinal bleeding or hepatic failure). Apply the conservative local context parser; retain rejected/ambiguous rows in an audit. Same-patient or different-encounter examination linkage is not corroboration. C is an unvalidated lexical corroboration proxy, not independent clinical adjudication.

Each patient-index is assigned one mutually exclusive category: no D, D without C, or C. Report D's four components, the first event time, C domain, text exclusions and treatment/opportunity counts separately. Medications are never used to replace C.

Ascertainment variables are defined before day 15 unless explicitly labeled outcome-window descriptive:

- pre-index opportunity: counts of linked encounters, lab rows, examination rows and orders in [index-90,index), plus days since the last such record;
- index/early opportunity: encounter count, examination count, order count, lab count, per-assay observation indicators, observation delays and assay counts in [index-14,index+14], using only information available by index+14;
- encounter context: age, sex, index 就诊科室, index 就诊时间, 入院时间, 出院时间, and whether discharge is parseable. These are opportunity/context variables, not hospital identity;
- outcome-window counts of encounters, labs and examinations are descriptive by category only and are not primary predictors, because the event can generate its own opportunity.

The reserve channel uses numeric, valid, unit-compatible values for albumin (白蛋白), total bilirubin (总胆红素), INR (国际标准化比值; PT replacement is a sensitivity), platelets (血小板计数 or a separately validated exact primary label), creatinine (肌酐) and sodium (钠). Acute-labelled variants require an explicit assay dictionary. Reject ambiguous, nonnumeric, impossible and unit-unsupported results; never pool unverified assay units. Orient all six so higher is better, standardize within training data, and retain assay identity, time-from-index and value. Missingness, delays and counts are process features, not biological values.

## Estimands and analysis

### Reserve/process decomposition

On the complete-window analysis population, fit a prespecified multinomial elastic-net model with reference category no D:

logit P(Y=k)/P(Y=no D) = alpha_k + theta_k R + phi_k B + psi_k(R x B) + gamma_k A + covariates, for k in {D-without-C,C}.

Here R is the continuous six-assay early-worsening score (higher means worse change), B is baseline reserve, A is the pre-day-15 ascertainment opportunity block, and covariates are age, sex, index department, prior HCC/decompensation history and baseline assay availability/recency. Fit process features as a separate block with shrinkage; do not substitute them for missing reserve values. The primary contrasts are:

- theta_C - theta_Donly, the paired difference in reserve association;
- the change in theta_C and theta_Donly after adding A;
- adjusted absolute-risk contrasts for one training-set SD worse R at prespecified training-derived B quartiles; and
- the corresponding C-versus-D incidence/concordance and opportunity-stratified estimates.

The diagnosis-only and C estimates are correlated because C is a subset of D. Use a patient-index bootstrap that refits both outcome equations in each replicate; report paired two-sided 95% intervals and a prespecified clinically negligible interaction margin, with no p-value-only decision. If fewer than 20 complete-window C events or fewer than 10 in either event category remain, do not fit an unstable C multinomial equation: report exact/binomial or bootstrap incidence and paired descriptive contrasts only.

### Static versus temporal learned alternative

The prediction comparison uses the same complete-window patient-index rows, split once at the patient level into 70% train, 15% validation and 15% locked test, stratified as far as event counts permit. A later-index holdout is added only if it has adequate event support.

- M_static: regularized logistic models for D and, if supported, C, using baseline six-assay reserve values, demographics, prior diagnoses, index context and pre-index opportunity A. It has no early assay values and is a transparent estimate of what a static baseline can establish.
- M_temporal: gradient-boosted trees for the same endpoint, using ordered assay values from exactly [index-14,index+14], assay identity, time-from-index, baseline/early indicator, age, sex, prior history, index context, and process features (missingness, delays, counts, encounter/examination/order opportunity). Tune only on the validation split, cap at 500 trees and 10 configurations, then lock test predictions.
- M_value-only is a prespecified ablation of the learned alternative retaining ordered assay values, assay identity and time but excluding missingness/count/opportunity variables. It is needed to distinguish temporal reserve information from process information; it is not an additional claim of clinical superiority.

Evaluate all models on the identical locked patients and endpoint with Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, AUROC, AUPRC and decision-curve net benefit where event support makes those quantities meaningful. Report paired bootstrap intervals. A temporal gain is scientifically supportive only if M_value-only adds information beyond M_static and is not eliminated by the full/process ablation; a gain confined to process variables is ascertainment information. Feature importance is exploratory and cannot replace the prespecified coefficient contrasts.

### Record follow-up and competing opportunity

The primary binary negative requires an encounter at or beyond day 90. As a sensitivity, construct a time-to-recorded-D analysis from day 15, with event time from linked diagnosis and censoring at the last linked encounter observed before day 90 (or day 14 for patients with no post-day-14 encounter). Fit stabilized inverse-probability-of-record-follow-up weights from index/pre-index and day-14 covariates, truncate at the 1st/99th percentiles, and report weighted versus complete-window estimates. This is censoring for loss of observable record opportunity, not mortality or transplant. The snapshot has no reliable death, transplant or complete outside-care capture, so a biological competing-risk analysis is unavailable. Outcome-window encounter/examination counts are reported descriptively, never used as primary predictors.

## Falsification and sensitivity criteria

Prespecify and report:

- strict C versus the broader examination vocabulary, with all vocabulary changes frozen before model fitting and an ambiguity audit;
- same-encounter C versus a deliberately over-broad any-encounter linkage as an ascertainment stress test; the latter is not a clinical endpoint;
- process-adjusted versus unadjusted reserve coefficients and opportunity-stratified estimates;
- complete-window versus weighted record-censoring analysis;
- INR versus PT replacement and exact assay-label sensitivities;
- permuted within-patient lab timestamps in the early window, refitting the temporal model; loss of a timing-specific gain is required for a temporal-ordering claim;
- if a diagnosis vocabulary can be frozen from the full diagnosis label inventory before outcome analysis, a non-hepatic diagnosis negative-control endpoint in the same window. If no clinically coherent frozen negative control is available, omit it and state why rather than selecting one post hoc.

A falsification signal is present if an association is similar for any-encounter rather than same-encounter corroboration, disappears or reverses after pre-day-15 opportunity adjustment, is driven by missingness/counts while M_value-only is null, or survives timestamp permutation unchanged while the claim depends on timing. These results do not prove absence of biological decline; they weaken the proposed separation.

## Interpretation and evidence limits

Supportive results are a positive reserve association for C with a paired interval excluding the negligible margin, materially weaker/process-sensitive D-only association, stable strict-vocabulary and opportunity-stratified results, and a calibrated temporal value-only signal beyond the static baseline. This supports incremental prediction of a stricter observed-care proxy and justifies blinded chart review and external validation. It does not establish true decompensation, TACE toxicity, treatment effect, causality, or clinical utility.

Adverse results are a null/reverse C association with a positive D-only association, strong attenuation after A, dominance of D-without-C, vocabulary instability, or learned gains confined to process features. This supports ascertainment intensity/coding as a plausible explanation for the parent signal. It does not show that biological reserve decline is irrelevant.

Inconclusive results include fewer than the support threshold of C events, wide paired intervals, unreliable assay mapping, insufficient complete-window negatives, severe follow-up imbalance, unstable lexical context, failed later-index holdout, or temporal models that cannot be evaluated with meaningful uncertainty. Do not convert these into a negative biological conclusion.

Automatic verification can check snapshot/source hashes, read-only access, exact TACE/HCC/compensation filters, one index per patient, all relative boundaries, assay dictionary and rejection counts, two-key joins, C's same-encounter/time requirement, mutually exclusive categories, patient-level split, locked predictions, bootstrap refits and conclusion-to-output links. It cannot adjudicate Chinese clinical text, determine whether an examination finding is clinically true, infer treatment indication, recover deaths/transplants/outside care, establish causal mechanisms, or show that prospective monitoring improves outcomes. Those claims require blinded expert chart review, better longitudinal clinical evidence, external validation and a prospective/interventional study.

The HCC source does not provide tumor stage, radiographic response, treatment dose or intent, validated decompensation grades, reliable hospital identity, complete outpatient capture, death/transplant endpoints, images or waveforms. These are explicit clinical evidence limits. The other configured datasets (MIMIC, eICU and UKB) remain accessible but have no justified participant-level key to this HCC snapshot and are not joined.

## Method alternatives and revisit rule

The static regularized model is retained because it provides transparent reserve/process coefficient contrasts and stable calibration under sparse C events. The temporal boosted alternative is retained because ordered values can reveal nonlinear deterioration, timing asymmetry and recovery patterns lost by a single early-worsening composite. Both use exact available inputs, the same patient split, the same D/C endpoints, and patient-bootstrap uncertainty. The bounded fitting budget is 4 CPUs, 16 GiB RAM, at most 500 trees and 10 validation configurations, within the 7,200-second science limit; streamed extraction is expected to take 20–45 minutes after cohort construction. GPU use is unnecessary for this tabular experiment, but no method is excluded on hardware grounds.

A latent Gaussian-process/mixed-effects model is deferred because it would add missing-data and trajectory assumptions before corroborated event validity is established; revisit only with adequate repeated, clinically adjudicated events and a question about patient-specific latent slopes. A transformer/generative sequence model is deferred because extra sequence capacity cannot solve unvalidated outcome text and may amplify surveillance shortcuts; revisit only after evidence shows that clinically meaningful long-range history, rather than measurement opportunity, is the unresolved uncertainty. The earlier demonstrations are method context, not reproduction targets: the HCC snapshot lacks the external validation, complete multimodal imaging and genetic inputs needed for their stronger claims.
