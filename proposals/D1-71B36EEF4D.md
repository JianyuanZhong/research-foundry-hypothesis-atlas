# Episode 45 (MIMIC-IV island) — RESTART from UNUSED expert seed `[starting question]`

Working proposal (Lead-owned). Status: design + bindings only; NO cohort rows, models or estimands computed this episode.

## Question and clinical importance
When culture/susceptibility results return in a critically ill patient on empiric broad-spectrum
antimicrobials, is **early de-escalation** (narrowing coverage) on average safe, **and** is any
apparent safety an artifact of confounding by indication that hides harm in patients who are
**not yet improving**? This is a consequential, guideline-relevant ICU decision: continuing
broad coverage drives resistance/toxicity, but premature narrowing in a still-deteriorating
patient could delay adequate therapy.

## Unresolved question vs. existing evidence
Closest inspected work [K1] (Zhang 2026, MIMIC-IV v3.1) emulated target trials at a **fixed
day-4** reassessment and found antipseudomonal de-escalation was associated with *lower*, not
higher, 30-day mortality (RD -4.91 pp, 95% CI -7.27 to -2.23), and anti-MRSA de-escalation
non-inferior (RD -1.44 pp, 95% CI -3.75 to 1.11). [K2] shows the de-escalation evidence base is
fragmented. [K3] establishes target-trial emulation as the methodological precedent for antibiotic
strategy questions in MIMIC-IV. **Remaining gap:** no inspected work tests *effect modification by
pre-landmark organ-function trajectory* at a culture-result-driven landmark — exactly the subgroup
where a safety claim is most in doubt, because clinicians de-escalate the patients they judge to be
recovering (confounding by indication).

## Hypotheses (leading claim vs. strongest rival)
- **Leading claim (H1):** Early de-escalation after results return is not harmful overall and
  remains non-harmful even in patients whose organ function is *not* improving.
- **Strongest rival (H2, confounding by indication / care-trajectory):** any average "benefit" is
  produced by selection of recovering patients into de-escalation; in *non-improving* patients
  de-escalation may be harmful (delayed adequate therapy). H1 and H2 make **opposite predictions
  for the interaction** `Delta = RD(non-improving) - RD(improving)`.
- Also test a **negative-control exposure** (discontinuation of a non-antimicrobial chronic
  medication at the same landmark) and a **negative-control outcome** (a non-infection 28-day
  event). H2 predicts the negative-control exposure will also show an apparent protective
  association; H1 does not.

## Population (MIMIC-IV v3.1; first ICU stay per subject)
Adults >=18, first `icustays.stay_id`, non-null `hadm_id`, suspected/confirmed infection with a
microbiology result, and empiric broad-spectrum antibiotic exposure during the ICU stay.
Population is defined ONLY from MIMIC-IV tables; no cross-dataset patient joins.

## Landmark and exposure
- **Landmark t_R** = earliest `microbiologyevents.storetime` for the `hadm_id` with an organism
  identified (`org_name` non-null) OR a susceptibility result (`interpretation` in {S,I,R}),
  with `storetime` within `[icustays.intime, icustays.intime + 72h]`. Iterate all eligible
  micro rows per stay and take the minimum qualifying `storetime`.
- **Exposure (de-escalation)**: within `(t_R, t_R + 48h]`, the total **antimicrobial spectrum
  breadth score** of ongoing empiric agents decreases by >=1 and no new broad-spectrum agent is
  added. Spectrum score uses an *explicit* drug -> breadth dictionary (0-4 across
  gram-negative/pseudomonal, gram-positive/MRSA, anaerobe, atypical) applied to
  `prescriptions.drug` (and cross-checked in `emar.medication`/`pharmacy.medication`).
  Continuation = score unchanged or increased. Reconstruct ongoing coverage from
  `prescriptions.starttime`/`stoptime` (and `pharmacy.starttime/stoptime`).
- **Effect modifier M** = pre-landmark organ-function trajectory, dichotomized at a prespecified
  cutoff: improving vs non-improving, from the change in a partial SOFA between
  `(t_R-48h, t_R-24h]` and `(t_R-24h, t_R]`.

## Outcome and time fields
- Primary: **28-day all-cause mortality** from `admissions.deathtime` (else `patients.dod`,
  `patients` anchor-year offset), measured from `t_R`.
- Secondary: 14-day mortality; in-hospital death (`admissions.hospital_expire_flag`); death-adjusted
  antibiotic-free days.

## Methods — baseline vs. substantive alternative
- **B0 (primary, interpretable):** propensity-adjusted logistic/Cox over the M0->M1->M2 ladder,
  reporting the adjusted 28-day risk difference `RD` with bootstrap 95% CI, overall and by M.
- **A1 (alternative, substantive):** target-trial emulation with time-varying IPTW/AIPW
  (g-computation) that censors on post-landmark treatment switching and captures time-varying
  confounding; reports the marginal `RD` and the interaction `Delta`. Chosen because the baseline
  cannot represent post-landmark confounding/attainment dynamics that H2 turns on.
- **A2 (deferred):** latent/sequence representation of the organ trajectory. Deferred because the
  cohort, landmark and split must be fixed first; not required to answer the modification question.
  Revisit if A1 attributes the interaction to measurement/missingness.

## Split
Locked **temporal 70/30 split by `icustays.intime`** (earliest 70% development, latest 30% held-out
confirmation), identical convention to prior MIMIC episodes. No peeking for a favorable result.

## Falsification criteria (prespecified bands, 28-day mortality risk-difference scale)
- **Supportive (H1):** overall adjusted `RD <= +0.02` with upper 95% CI `<= +0.05`; interaction
  `Delta <= +0.02`; negative-control exposure `RD <= +0.01`.
- **Adverse (H2):** overall adjusted `RD >= +0.03` with lower 95% CI `> 0` (harm), **or**
  `Delta >= +0.03` (harm concentrated in non-improvers).
- **Inconclusive:** CIs span the bands / power insufficient (a genuine possible outcome).
- Consistency required across B0 and A1 and on the held-out 30%.
