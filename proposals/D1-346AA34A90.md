# HCC post-TACE trajectory: measurement-validity successor

Status: proposed Harbor experiment; no full study or model fit has been executed.

## Question and advance

Among adults with HCC and a first observed TACE, does an assay-indexed laboratory trajectory during days 0–7 add held-out prognostic information beyond baseline for a new post-discharge decompensation-coded admission during days 8–90? The parent established the clinically important opening: early injury may be transient, while coordinated synthetic/renal deterioration could mark later loss of reserve. This successor preserves that question but makes its interpretation falsifiable against the strongest rivals: baseline severity, observation intensity, assay/unit heterogeneity, transient AST/ALT injury, and coding/documentation artifacts.

A supportive result is only an incremental prognostic regularity. It cannot establish TACE causality, a biological latent mechanism, treatment benefit, or a safe action threshold.

## Population, time zero, estimand, and outcomes

Use only HCC. Identify each patient's first procedure row whose Surgery contains TACE, Transcatheter Arterial Chemoembolization, Transcatheter Hepatic Arterial Embolization, or Arterial Chemoembolization, with parseable Start Time and a matching encounter. Require age >=18 and an HCC diagnosis (at minimum Hepatocellular Carcinoma or Malignant Tumor of the Liver) before/on the index encounter. Exclude prior/index high-specificity decompensation terms: Ascites, Hepatic Encephalopathy, Gastrointestinal Bleeding, Esophageal and Gastric Varices, Hepatorenal Syndrome, Acute Liver Failure, Hepatic Failure, Spontaneous Peritonitis, or Jaundice; report broader cirrhosis-decompensation flags separately. Missing labs do not exclude patients.

Time zero is earliest qualifying Surgery.Start time. Baseline is the closest assay-specific quantitative result in [-14,0] days; [-30,0] is a sensitivity window. Dynamic features use only [0,7] days. The primary event is the first distinct encounter after the index Discharge time whose admission time is in [8,90] days and whose diagnosis contains a prespecified high-specificity component. Missing discharge is reported, not silently converted to a new admission. Report composite plus ascites, encephalopathy, bleeding, renal/hepatic failure, and jaundice components; also report any admission and repeat TACE as non-equivalent secondary outcomes.

The primary estimand is the paired chronological-test-set difference between a baseline-only model and a dynamic model in Brier score, calibration, discrimination, and decision-curve net benefit. This is not a treatment-effect estimand.

## Measurement and endpoint validity

Labs have no unit column. Freeze an assay dictionary from test values before outcome analysis. Parse only numeric quantitative results; preserve qualitative results, specimen type, raw-name variants, duplicate rate, timing and missingness. Use within-assay training-set standardization and within-patient changes/ranks; do not pool raw thresholds across assays or claim valid ALBI/MELD/Child-Pugh without verified units. Candidate assays include bilirubin, albumin, platelets, PT/INR, creatinine, sodium, AST/ALT and AFP, but presence and clinical comparability must be demonstrated, not assumed. Aggregate nearest observations to fixed [0,1–2,3–4,5–7] day bins with a deterministic tie rule; retain all timestamps for sensitivity/state-space analysis.

Build pre-index and early observation controls: counts of encounters, admissions, departments, procedures, medications, orders, examinations and labs; number of lab timestamps, assays, encounters with labs, first-lab lag, last observed day, and bin-specific counts. Compare dynamic models without and with these controls and restrict to common support (at least one baseline and one day 3–7 lab). A signal disappearing after controls is utilization-compatible.

The primary endpoint uses transparent diagnosis-name lexical rules. A sensitivity endpoint requires the same component in clinical_documents fields listed above. Report diagnosis-only, document-confirmed, discordant and missing-document strata. Do not infer planned/unplanned status. Produce a blinded clinician adjudication packet to determine true new/worsening decompensation, planned status, competing illness/HCC progression, TACE injury, and text validity.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

B0 is L2-regularized logistic regression using time-zero age, sex, department, HCC/cirrhosis/portal-vein-thrombus/prior-decompensation flags, procedure context, pre-index utilization, baseline assay-specific values and missingness. A no-lab B0 is a sensitivity comparator.

B1 adds prespecified [0,7] assay summaries: baseline-to-last delta, robust slope, minimum/maximum, last value, observation count, first-lab lag, missingness, and separate AST/ALT-flare blocks. Fit B0/B1 on the same one-row-per-index-patient data.

The substantive alternative is a two-factor measurement-error state-space model on the same assays and time window: a slowly varying reserve state and a transient post-procedure injury state, with assay-specific loadings/noise, irregular timestamps, missingness and duplicate handling. Predict from the day-7 posterior plus the same baseline covariates; report posterior uncertainty, loadings, reserve-versus-injury contributions, and held-out predictions. It can reveal shared temporal structure and attenuation from measurement error that B1 slopes/extrema lose, but latent states are not mechanisms. A neural sequence model is deferred because it does not directly resolve the assay and utilization rivals; revisit only after stable state-space results and an independent temporal test set.

Use train 2010-07-27–2018-12-31, validation 2019-01-01–2021-12-31, test 2022-01-01–2025-10-03 or an equivalent complete-follow-up cutoff after recomputing source maxima. Keep patients in one split and tune only train/validation. Request CPU (4–8 cores, <32 GB for B0/B1; approximately 16 CPUs and 64–128 GB for state-space/bootstrap, estimated not measured); GPU is not warranted for this low-dimensional tabular likelihood.

## Falsification, uncertainty, and interpretation

Required negative controls are timestamp permutation, a matched pre-index pseudo-window, measurement-only prediction, any-admission and repeat-TACE outcomes, and AST/ALT-only versus reserve-panel-only blocks. Support requires a calibrated held-out incremental signal with paired uncertainty excluding a practically negligible gain, persistence after utilization/common-support controls, weak pre-index performance, no timestamp-permutation reproduction, and component/document concordance. Adverse results are disappearance after controls, timestamp permutation or pre-index reproduction, enzyme-only/measurement-density dominance, or specificity only for any admission/repeat TACE. Inconclusive results are sparse coverage, unstable convergence, too few events, endpoint discordance or wide intervals; they defer interpretation rather than refute the hypothesis.

Computationally checkable outputs are cohort flow, joins, timestamp ordering, assay audit, held-out predictions/metrics, calibration, decision curves, uncertainty, component tables and negative-control behavior. Clinical truth, planned status, mechanism, causality, mortality benefit, action thresholds and external validity require adjudication or another study. Missing essentials include verified assay units, imaging/tumor burden, embolized territory/drug/dose, ECOG, portal pressure, death linkage and external validation.

All are abstract-only inspected evidence; no inaccessible full text is claimed.
