# When creatinine recovery is discordant with observed Foley output after an early ICU renal insult

## Scientific deliverable

Fit and report one prespecified, noncausal MIMIC-IV v3.1 landmark analysis. Completion requires:

1. a read-only provenance manifest and cohort-flow table;
2. an independently reproducible item-level support audit, including dictionary labels/units, raw and valid rows, duplicate resolution, storage-time exclusions, hourly Foley coverage, weight availability, all four phenotype cells, and post-landmark outcome counts;
3. the primary standardized post-landmark in-hospital mortality contrast, with subject-clustered 95% uncertainty and a competing-risk/time-to-event sensitivity;
4. two newly fitted models on the same subject-held-out split: a transparent ridge-logistic baseline without urine/fluid channels and a nonlinear six-hour temporal model with a urine/fluid ablation;
5. held-out AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and bootstrap uncertainty, plus grouped feature-family summaries;
6. threshold, coverage, creatinine-source, weight, early-RRT and within-stay time-permutation checks; and
7. a predeclared supportive, adverse, or inconclusive conclusion tied to computed outputs.

The primary estimate and model outputs are new work. The parent’s 26,970-stay landmark diagnostic is a feasibility check only, not a result; no phenotype prevalence or effect estimate is assumed here.

## Unresolved question and evidence boundary

The strongest relevant evidence inspected is Haines et al., “Comparison of Cystatin C and Creatinine in the Assessment of Measured Kidney Function during Critical Illness” (CJASN 2023, DOI 10.2215/CJN.0000000000000203). In a selected 38-patient prolonged-critical-illness cohort with cystatin C, muscle ultrasound and iohexol clearance, creatinine-based eGFR overestimated measured kidney function and the discrepancy increased with muscle loss. This supports concern that a falling creatinine can be an incomplete recovery marker; it does not establish that charted Foley output captures the missing signal, that discordance is prevalent or prognostic in MIMIC, or that changing treatment would improve outcomes.

The falsifiable claim is:

> Among adults with an observed early creatinine-AKI-like rise who remain alive in their first ICU stay at 72 hours and have adequate paired measurements, creatinine recovery without adequate late Foley-recorded output recovery is associated with higher subsequent in-hospital mortality than recovery of both observed measures.

This is explicitly an observed-measurement association, not KDIGO AKI, true GFR, Foley patency, volume status, muscle loss, mechanism, or a treatment effect. A reproducible signal could justify confirmatory renal-function measurement and closer monitoring when creatinine appears to recover. It would advance knowledge by testing a fixed trajectory-defined discordance against a concordant recovery reference while quantifying whether the signal survives selective measurement and timing falsifications.

## Population, time zero and phenotype

Use one row per earliest valid ICU stay within each admission, selected from `icu/icustays` by (`subject_id`, `hadm_id`) and joined to `hosp/admissions` on those keys and to `hosp/patients` on `subject_id`. Include adults with `anchor_age >= 18), valid `intime < outtime`, and `t0 = intime + 72 hours < outtime); retain and report MIMIC’s age-91 representation. Exclude `admissions.deathtime <= t0`. Repeated admissions may contribute, but all splitting and bootstrap resampling is by `subject_id`.

All feature timestamps must satisfy `intime <= clinical_time < t0`; rows with `storetime` are retained only when `storetime < t0). No post-t0 field, later ICU stay, treatment, or outcome may enter prediction. Use half-open windows [0,24), [24,48), and [48,72) hours.

Creatinine is the union of `icu/chartevents` itemid 220615 and `hosp/labevents` itemid 50912, linked to `icu/d_items` and `hosp/d_labitems` for labels and units. Hospital labs are joined to the index stay by `hadm_id) and timestamp containment. Retain finite 0.1–30 mg/dL values with compatible units; report invalid, nonnumeric, out-of-range and incompatible-unit rows. At identical clinical timestamps, prefer ICU 220615, then collapse same-source duplicates by a deterministic median. Report ICU-only, hospital-only and combined-source sensitivity.

Require at least one valid creatinine in each window. Let early be the minimum [0,24) value, rise the maximum [24,48) value, peak the maximum [0,48) value, and late the minimum [48,72) value. Eligibility for an observed early creatinine-AKI-like rise is rise >= early + 0.3 mg/dL OR rise/early >= 1.5. Observed creatinine recovery is late <= peak - 0.3 mg/dL OR late/peak <= 0.75. These labels are measurement rules, not KDIGO diagnoses.

Foley output uses only `icu/outputevents` itemid 226559, verified in `icu/d_items` as `linksto=outputevents` and unit mL. Use `stay_id, charttime, storetime, value, valueuom`; retain finite nonnegative mL rows under the same time rules and aggregate into integer hours from `intime`. Missing hours are missing, never zero. Require at least 12 observed hourly bins in both [0,24) and [48,72) for the primary paired analysis; report an 18-hour dense-coverage sensitivity and all coverage strata.

Weights use `icu/chartevents` itemids 226512 and 224639, requiring finite 20–300 kg values with compatible kg units. Use the latest qualifying pre-48-hour weight. Define late output recovery as late Foley mL divided by observed late hours and weight >=0.5 mL/kg/observed hour. The primary contrast is within observed-AKI-like and creatinine-recovered stays with paired coverage and weight: Foley-output recovery versus Foley-output non-recovery. Preserve all four creatinine/output cells descriptively; report unweighted mL/hour separately rather than mixing missing weights into the primary scale.

## Outcomes and estimand

The primary outcome is first in-hospital death after t0 and by `admissions.dischtime`, using `deathtime` with `hospital_expire_flag` only as a consistency check. Estimate:

RD_obs = P(death by discharge | output non-recovery, observed cohort) -
         P(death by discharge | output recovery, observed cohort),

standardized over the empirical pre-t0 covariate distribution using a prespecified logistic model, with at least 500 subject-cluster bootstrap resamples. Report risk ratio as secondary and use robust Poisson only as a convergence sensitivity.

Secondary, non-primary descriptions are first new charted RRT after t0 using `icu/procedureevents` itemids 225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809 and 225955; first later ICU readmission in the same admission among index-stay survivors with death as a competing event; and death/new-RRT composite. These are charted events, not proof of renal indication. Report a 14-day Aalen–Johansen/cause-specific sensitivity with alive discharge as a competing state.

Adjustment uses only pre-t0 context and severity: age, gender, admission type/location/insurance and first care unit; creatinine levels/change/count/density; MAP 220181, HR 220045, respiratory rate 220210 and SpO2 220277 from `icu/chartevents`; lactate 50813/52442/53154 from `hosp/labevents`; vasoactive itemids 221906, 221662, 221749, 229630, 229631, 229632, 221289 and 229617 from `icu/inputevents`; ventilation 225792/225794 and pre-t0 RRT from `icu/procedureevents`; and CKD/ESRD diagnosis proxies from `hosp/diagnoses_icd` joined on `icd_code, icd_version` to `hosp/d_icd_diagnoses`. Report missingness and measurement density by phenotype; do not treat missing output as zero.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The baseline is newly fitted ridge-penalized logistic regression for post-t0 death using demographics/context, creatinine history, vital/lactate summaries, support indicators, CKD proxy and missingness, but excluding Foley output, weight-standardized output, input-minus-output and fluid trajectories. This asks whether the discordance question adds information beyond a transparent creatinine-centered severity model.

The alternative is newly fitted CPU `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (or fixed equivalent) on the identical eligibility, target, t0 boundary and 80/20 subject-held-out split, with inner grouped validation only in the training subjects. In six-hour bins across [0,72), summarize last/mean/min/max/slope/variability/count/missingness for creatinine, Foley output, weight-standardized output, input-minus-output, MAP, HR, respiratory rate, SpO2, lactate, vasoactive exposure, ventilation and RRT. Fit a full temporal model and an a priori ablation removing urine/output/weight/input-minus-output channels. Calibrate only in training folds, evaluate once on held-out subjects, and report AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, calibration intercept/slope, calibration plots and subject-cluster bootstrap intervals.

The baseline preserves interpretability and prevents a generic severity score from being mistaken for the renal claim. The temporal alternative can reveal nonlinear timing and interactions—particularly falling creatinine with persistently low output—that a snapshot/additive baseline loses. This is scientifically useful only if paired support is adequate; it is bounded at about 2–4 CPU cores, <=8 GB RAM and a few hours including streaming extraction and fitting. A neural sequence or latent-state model is deferred unless paired temporal support and the temporal signal justify it; complexity and GPU use are not evidence of clinical value.

## Falsification and interpretation

Run the support audit before fitting. If either primary phenotype cell is not estimable, mortality events are sparse, or observation is strongly phenotype-dependent, report an inconclusive/no-go result and do not replace the question with an easier endpoint.

Prespecify absolute-only and relative-only creatinine thresholds; 12 versus 18 Foley hours; ICU-only, hospital-only and combined creatinine; weighted versus unweighted output; pre-t0 RRT exclusion/stratification; coverage adjustment; and a within-stay permutation that shuffles Foley records across the 72 hourly bins while preserving each stay’s total volume and observed-bin count. Persistence after destroying late-versus-early timing falsifies the timing-specific discordance interpretation or indicates domination by total-volume/measurement-process effects.

Supportive evidence requires a positive primary adjusted RD with a 95% interval excluding zero, direction retained under dense coverage and at least two source/threshold checks, and reproducible held-out calibration/risk information from urine/fluid features beyond both baseline and ablation. Adverse evidence is a precise near-null/reversed primary contrast, reversal under dense/source/threshold checks, or persistence after time permutation; temporal model improvement alone cannot rescue the primary claim. Inconclusive evidence includes sparse cells/events, unstable estimates, strong differential observation, failed calibration or failed support audit. Every conclusion must cite computed output fields.

The verifier can check joins, item support, filtering, time boundaries, estimates, predictions, uncertainty, leakage and interpretation of supportive/adverse/inconclusive outputs. It cannot establish KDIGO AKI, true GFR, Foley patency, volume status, mechanism, causal treatment effects, clinical utility or external validity. Those require clinician adjudication, cystatin C/measured GFR, muscle and volume assessment, treatment indications, and prospective/external validation. A positive result cannot justify starting, stopping, delaying or withholding dialysis, diuresis or fluids.

## Demonstration and seed dispositions

Delphi’s inspected paper supports adapting dated structured trajectories, but its UK Biobank cohort and Danish external validation are unavailable; this is not a reproduction. ALADYNOULLI’s genetics and exact full inputs are unavailable, so an EHR-only latent model is a different deferred question. Oncoformer’s images and complete main paper/STAR Methods are unavailable; no multimodal claim is made, though its accessible supplement motivates explicit missingness/ablation checks.

The ten MIMIC seeds were assessed repairable. Seeds 01 and 03 informed this branch. Seeds 02 (diuretic timing), 08 (creatinine during diuresis), 09 (transfusion heterogeneity), 06 (sedation) and 07 (antimicrobial de-escalation) remain deferred because treatment indication, ischemia/volume/source-control evidence or time-varying confounding is insufficient. Seeds 04 (hyperlactatemia), 05 (stress hyperglycemia) and 10 (residual ICU instability) remain viable future directions but are not more data-bound in this episode. Their cards and provenance remain available for revisiting.

Actual completion is the audit, primary RD_obs with uncertainty, and held-out baseline/full/ablation predictions with uncertainty; until those are computed, no clinical result is claimed.
