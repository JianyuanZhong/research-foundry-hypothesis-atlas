## Decision and substantive repair

The frozen clinical question, population, hour-48 boundary, A12/B12 equal-clone estimand, bounded outcomes, uncertainty, multiplicity, and noncausal boundary are retained. The repair targets one remaining clinically material threat: the primary A12 clock is an ICU `inputevents.starttime`, but `icu/inputevents` has no `emar_id` or `pharmacy_id`. Thus the primary record can be temporally precise without proving that the loop dose was documented as administered in the hospital medication-administration record. A prescription or pharmacy order would not solve this because it represents an order/intention.

Add a prespecified, outcome-blind medication-documentation concordance gate and sensitivity audit. It uses exact eMAR-to-pharmacy/prescription joins where available, eMAR administration status and dose-detail fields, and an explicitly approximate eMAR-to-inputevents time comparison. It never moves the primary onset, changes an E_R/M/G subject, turns an eMAR absence into B12, or calls any record clinician-known, bedside, received, causal, safe, or actionable. If the audit cannot establish adequate concordance, treatment-direction results are validity-inconclusive rather than adverse or supportive.

No treatment effect or contrast was estimated while proposing this child.

## Evidence and unresolved claim

The strongest available evidence is a source fact: MIMIC-IV 3.1 contains timestamped ICU loop records in `icu/inputevents` and separate hospital eMAR records with `charttime`, `event_txt`, `pharmacy_id`, and `storetime`; eMAR detail contains dose and completeness fields. The catalog also contains `hosp/pharmacy` and `hosp/prescriptions`, which join to eMAR by `pharmacy_id`. The ICU input table has no deterministic key into those hospital tables. Public MIMIC documentation describes eMAR as bedside medication-administration documentation, but eMAR coverage is not universal and the two systems can disagree. This establishes a repairable documentation-validity concern, not actual delivery.

The unresolved experimental claim remains: in the frozen post-shock phenotype, is compatibility with initiating an ICU-recorded furosemide/bumetanide course in the next 12 hours associated with better bounded recorded airway outcome without crossing renal, RRT, mortality, or disposition margins than compatibility with avoiding initiation? The repair adds the narrower falsifiable claim that the inputevents-based A12/B12 labels are sufficiently concordant with an independently documented administration record to support interpreting a directional result as a recorded-strategy association.

## Falsification and interpretation

A clinically feasible branch is materially reversed if its category changes, an airway bound moves >=0.05, or midpoint signs reverse with both absolute magnitudes >=0.02. A strict eMAR audit fails if its fixed arm minima/coverage are not met, A concordance <0.80, B conflict >0.05, or exact joins/detail/dose/status rules are not reproducible.

- **Supportive-recorded** requires all inherited E_R validity, uncertainty, outcome, temporal and sensitivity requirements, the medication-validity gate, airway lower bound >+0.05, no Holm-rejected adverse family, inherited renal/RRT/terminal margins, nonadverse disposition, and no material reversal. It supports only a reproducible recorded-strategy association suitable for external validation or randomization.
- **Adverse-FWER** requires an adjusted adverse E_R family with all validity axes, including medication concordance, passing. It is an observational recorded-proxy concern, not toxicity, renal safety, causality or a recommendation.
- **Margin-falsified** requires validity passing, no adjusted adverse family, and airway upper bound <=+0.05. It rejects only the prespecified recorded five-point improvement.
- **Medication-ascertainment-inconclusive** applies to any population where the new eMAR gate fails. A directional primary estimate may be printed descriptively with its bounds, but no treatment-direction classification follows.
- **Temporal-validity-inconclusive**, **recording-time-inferential-infeasible**, **all-row measurement-limit**, **validity-inconclusive**, **statistically inconclusive**, and **primary-infeasible** retain the parent definitions. Any eMAR/inputevents mismatch is a measurement-validity finding, never adverse treatment evidence.
- If eMAR supports an A record but its timing is >2 hours from the ICU onset, report timing discordance; do not move onset or infer a second dose. If eMAR is positive in B, report B documentation conflict; do not infer exposure receipt. If eMAR has no row, no linked pharmacy record, a nonpositive/unparseable dose, or a non-Administered status, report unknown/ambiguous.
