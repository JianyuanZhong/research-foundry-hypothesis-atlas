# Calibrating discharge-artifact delay before prioritizing a pulmonary-nodule handoff intervention

## Decision question, evidence boundary, and targeted repair

The decision is whether a prospective workflow should first target (i) pre-departure finalization/availability, (ii) reconciliation of an interval-specific nodule plan into the discharge artifact, or (iii) downstream tracking and cross-system capture. The inherited decomposition is clinically useful because it keeps these transitions separate. Its unresolved decision-validity flaw is that `note/discharge.storetime` is only an observed database-storage time. It is not departure-time visibility, signature, transmission, bedside communication, patient receipt, or completion of a follow-up order. Consequently, a large discharge-boundary `A` state could be ordinary institutional discharge-documentation latency and could not, by itself, justify an intervention aimed at nodule handoff.

This child preserves the parent's frozen all-opportunity denominator, reciprocal report/addendum rules, report-versus-addendum chronology, discharge text/addendum handling, downstream observation states, outside-care unknownness, and noncausal boundaries. It adds a locked **institutional-latency calibration and falsification layer**. `A` remains a descriptive storage-delay state, but mechanism prioritization is prohibited unless the nodule opportunity's excess delay is distinguishable from ordinary discharge workflow and remains stable over multiple locked boundaries and controls. Controls calibrate a storage process; they do not prove physical visibility or communication.

The strongest claim available after a successful experiment is narrow: in this MIMIC snapshot, among adjudicated pre-discharge nodule opportunities, canonical discharge-summary storage delay and/or content discordance is greater than an explicitly calibrated ordinary-discharge workflow comparator under prespecified timestamp rules. The untested stronger claim is that clinicians or patients could not see, receive, understand, or act on the recommendation at departure, or that changing the workflow improves surveillance, cancer outcomes, or safety. Those require a prospective timestamp crosswalk, clinical adjudication of communication, and linked intervention/outcome data.

## Population and frozen all-opportunity denominator

The unit is the first eligible admission per subject. Include adults with an imaging report linked to an admission and charted from `admittime-12h` through `D = dischtime`; exclude in-hospital death, hospice/comfort-only transition, screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, explicit no-follow-up, conditional/optional plans, and recommendations first introduced after `D`. Require a patient-specific unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months after an incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity. Do not invent unavailable appropriateness inputs such as pack-years or complete imaging measurements; mark them unknown.

Reconstruct radiology report/addendum chains only from reciprocal subject- and admission-consistent detail links. A recommendation component is pre-departure knowledge only when both `charttime <= D` and `storetime <= D`, unless a blinded reviewer marks timestamp semantics uncertain. A post-`D` first introduction or material change is a late candidate, not an eligible opportunity. Missing nodes, contradictory links, missing timestamps, and chart/store conflicts form `U_R`, never an omission or an availability failure. Two independent radiology-trained reviewers adjudicate CT coverage, incidental status, recommendation, interval, addendum meaning, eligibility, and timestamp uncertainty while blinded to discharge text, discharge storetime, later imaging, and calibration-control labels. Freeze `N` and the complete cascade before examining any discharge artifact, discharge storetime, later trace, death, or control outcome: lexical candidates, metadata-linked candidates, exclusions, late addenda, `U_R`, and final `N` all remain reported.

This preserves the parent's primary mutually exclusive all-`N` states:

* `F`: the canonical DS is stored by `D`, classifiable, and faithfully carries a patient-specific nodule, chest/thoracic CT action, and exact compatible interval/date.
* `C_O`: stored by `D` and classifiable, but no patient-specific actionable plan.
* `C_I`: stored by `D`, but nodule/action/timing is incomplete or discordant without a documented alternate resolution.
* `R`: stored by `D` with a documented alternate resolution such as refusal/preferences, goals-of-care decision, specialist takeover with concrete alternative, or scheduled outside follow-up. This is neither faithful success nor content failure.
* `A`: a canonical DS exists with known storetime but is not observed stored by `D`; `A` is a storage-delay signal only.
* `U_D`: missing storetime, absent/unreadable/contradictory artifact linkage, or unclassifiable text. It remains unknown.

A stale DS stored before the recommendation component became available is a chronology marker and does not get blamed for content omission. `A` remains `A` at the primary boundary even when later stored text becomes `F`, `C`, or `R`; transitions by +24h and +7d are descriptive matrices. No post-storage subset replaces the all-opportunity denominator. Report `p_A`, `p_{C_O}`, `p_{C_I}`, `p_R`, `p_F`, `p_{U_D}`, the inherited `p_A-p_C` (where `C=C_O+C_I`), and joint intervals/partial-identification bounds.

## Locked calibration and falsification design

### Control definitions

Before reading discharge text or late outcomes, create three control layers. Their labels are fixed before viewing `F/C/R/A` outcomes.

1. **Ordinary-discharge artifact controls (`O`):** all adult admission keys with a unique DS row and nonmissing `dischtime`/`storetime`, excluding every frozen eligible nodule opportunity and its subject's index admission. This is the institution-wide DS-storage workflow. It is not a no-risk group and is not used to estimate a causal effect.
2. **Chest-imaging workflow controls (`I`):** all linked inpatient chest/CT radiology reports in the same `admittime-12h` to `D` window that fail the nodule-recommendation screen, with exact report `charttime/storetime`, note type, and admission linkage retained. This calibrates whether any delay is a broad radiology reporting phenomenon rather than a nodule-recommendation phenomenon. It is a negative control for nodule-specific report timing, not proof that a recommendation was visible.
3. **Admission-structure matched controls (`M`):** for each adjudicated `N` admission, select up to four controls from `O` using deterministic exact strata formed only from pre-index data: admission type, discharge location, ICU-stay indicator, length-of-stay bin based on `D-admittime` (0-1, >1-3, >3-7, >7 days), and terminal service family from `services`; if a stratum lacks four controls, use all available controls and record shortfall. Matching is for calibration precision and workflow comparability, not exchangeability or causal adjustment. The exact strata and any fallback must be locked before delay values are inspected. A full-`O` standardized analysis is primary; matched `M` is a sensitivity analysis that avoids dependence on arbitrary sampled controls.

A second, clinically meaningful control is a **non-nodule actionable discharge-plan subset** only if reviewers can adjudicate it from the same text sources without using nodule cases; otherwise it is not fabricated. `discharge_detail` field names are never assumed to be sections. Control adjudication is blinded to exposure state where practical and never converts lexical absence into clinical absence.

### Timing variables and three locked boundaries

For each `N`, `O`, and `M` admission define:

* `L_DS = storetime_DS - D`, with missing storetime retained as unknown;
* `B_D = I(L_DS <= 0)`, `B_24 = I(L_DS <= 24h)`, and `B_7 = I(L_DS <= 168h)`;
* `L_RAD = storetime_recommendation_component - charttime_recommendation_component` and `K_D = I(storetime_recommendation_component <= D)`;
* `S = I(storetime_DS < storetime_recommendation_component)` as a stale-artifact chronology marker, never an inferred content failure.

`D`, `D+24h`, and `D+7d` are locked descriptive boundaries. `D+24h` and `D+7d` do not claim a grace period of clinical safety or visibility. If storetime is missing or ordering is contradictory, use `U_D`/unknown rather than imputation. A future refresh with more than one DS row per admission stops the run and requires a new version rule; this snapshot has exactly one DS row per key.

### Primary calibration estimand

Let `q_g(b)` be the probability of late DS storage, `1-B_b`, in group `g` at boundary `b`, estimated over all eligible `N` and all ordinary controls `O` using inverse-stratum standardization only for descriptive comparability. The primary nodule-specific excess-storage contrast is

`Delta_b = q_N(b) - q_O^std(b)`,

with `q_N(b)` calculated over all frozen `N`, not only admissions whose DS is observed by `b`. Report Wilson/Newcombe or cluster bootstrap 95% intervals, with bootstrap resampling by subject and one index admission per subject. Also report the matched-control contrast `q_N(b)-q_M(b)` and the unstandardized `q_O(b)`; disagreement is a calibration warning, not an opportunity to choose a favorable comparator. No adjustment variable may be selected after looking at `A`, `C`, or surveillance.

The decision-facing calibration gate is deliberately conjunctive:

* **Support for a nodule-specific storage problem:** at every primary boundary `b in {D,D+24h,D+7d}`, the simultaneous 95% lower bound for `Delta_b` is greater than 0 and the point estimate is at least 0.05 at `D` or at least 0.03 at both later boundaries; the same directional result must hold against `M` or the full-`O` standardization, with no material (<0.02 absolute) comparator reversal.
* **Radiology-process falsification:** the nodule recommendation component must not be slower than chest imaging controls by an amount that fully explains `Delta_b`; report `L_RAD` and `K_D` contrasts. If nodule and chest controls have comparable or slower radiology lag and the DS excess disappears after conditioning descriptively on `K_D`, the result is not a DS-specific handoff signal and mechanism prioritization is blocked.
* **Boundary stability:** the sign of `Delta_b` must not reverse under exact, zero-hour, and 12-hour report-to-admission linkage windows, and no more than one of the three boundaries may have a simultaneous interval crossing zero. The primary `D` result remains the only departure-boundary descriptive estimand; sensitivity windows do not create additional opportunities.
* **Adequate information:** at least 200 adjudicated `N`, at least 20 observed `A` and 20 `C` events when those comparisons are invoked, `U_R <=5%`, `U_D <=5%`, reviewer AC1/kappa and raw agreement prespecified as acceptable by the adjudication protocol, and simultaneous interval width <0.10 for the relevant contrasts. Failure is inconclusive, not evidence for the opposite mechanism.

These thresholds are operational decision rules, not validated quality standards. Even passing them supports only an excess storage-latency phenotype relative to ordinary workflow. It does not establish physical departure visibility, signature, communication, receipt, or intervention benefit. If the calibration gate fails, report the inherited all-`N` decomposition but do not prioritize `A` over `C`, and do not label the result a visibility failure.

### Content calibration and reciprocal falsification

For artifacts observed by each boundary, retain the parent content states and compare the nodule-specific conditional content distribution with a predeclared control content task only if expert adjudication defines a valid control target. Never compare `F/(F+C)` across different denominators as the primary result. A high `A` rate plus ordinary-control delay is documentation latency; high `C` among timely DS artifacts with calibrated delay near zero is a content-reconciliation signal; both high is a bundled process signal; both low redirects attention to downstream observation or unknown outside care.

Use the following negative controls and perturbations, each locked before final inference:

* chest radiology reports that fail the nodule recommendation screen;
* non-chest imaging in the due window for the secondary observation analysis;
* acute-admission versus nodule-focused CT as a surveillance specificity control;
* exact versus 0-hour versus 12-hour report/admission linkage;
* permutation of control labels within exact pre-index strata, which must remove any apparent calibrated difference except Monte Carlo error;
* a report-charttime-only analysis, which must not be substituted for storetime and should not be interpreted as visibility;
* all-`O` versus matched-`M` calibration, where a large reversal blocks prioritization.

A control signal as large as the nodule signal, a signal confined to acute/non-nodule CT, a reversal under timestamp bounds, or disappearance after radiology-lag calibration makes the mechanism claim inconclusive.

## Secondary observation outcome, retained unchanged

For every frozen `N`, define due date as the index recommendation's exact calendar-month date plus a fixed 42-day window. Start at `D`; use `max(D,storetime_DS)` only as an artifact-timed sensitivity, without changing `N`. The mutually exclusive all-`N` states are same-system timely chest CT/CTA before interval-certain death; death before the window with no prior timely CT; no timely CT/death but a later MIMIC radiology/admission trace; no later trace (outside-care/loss-of-capture unknown); and indeterminate CT/death ordering. `dod` is interval-censored to `[dod 00:00,dod+1 day)` unless a more precise death time exists. State 3 is not adherence, state 4 is not nonadherence, and in-hospital death is a competing event. Report this vector jointly by primary discharge state, but do not condition either primary process estimand or follow-up estimand on later trace. Orders, referrals, communication, appointments, outside imaging, malignancy, treatment, and patient benefit are unavailable.

## Analysis, supportive/adverse/inconclusive outcomes, and falsification

Primary analysis is an all-opportunity multinomial description plus calibrated `Delta_b` contrasts, with simultaneous intervals across the three boundaries and predeclared control comparisons. Use subject-cluster bootstrap or exact/binomial methods appropriate to the fixed index unit. Covariates from patients, admissions, services, transfers, ICU, diagnoses, and dictionary tables are descriptive and locked before outcome inspection; no propensity or causal model is required. Any adjusted model is exploratory and cannot rescue a failed calibration gate.

* **Supportive:** adjudication is reliable; `N` and unknownness/event gates pass; `A` exceeds ordinary and chest controls under the locked boundaries; the excess is not explained by report-lag or matching; and the sign is stable. This justifies a prospective timestamp-crosswalk study that measures author finalization, signature, transmission, EHR availability, and patient communication. It does not justify a causal workflow recommendation.
* **Adverse for availability-first prioritization:** `A` is similar to ordinary controls, the excess disappears after radiology-lag calibration, or content/alternate resolution dominates among timely artifacts. Redirects the prospective design toward content reconciliation, tracking, scheduling, communication, or exchange.
* **Inconclusive:** sparse `N`, excess unknownness, disagreement, missing/contradictory times, duplicate DS rows, control-support failure, interval crossing/reversal, comparator instability, or incompatible lexical-audit implementations. Report bounds and do not select a mechanism.

Automatic verification can check the computed source hashes, row counts, archive members, exact joins, one-DS-per-key rule, frozen-`N` ordering, blinded-control lock, boundary arithmetic, reciprocal links, state exhaustiveness, standardized/matched contrasts, simultaneous intervals, negative-control outputs, and conclusion-to-output linkage. It cannot determine whether storage represented physical visibility, signature, transmission, communication, receipt, recommendation appropriateness, outside-care completion, malignancy, or patient benefit. Those claims require expert review and prospective/linked data. No causal, adherence, or patient-benefit conclusion is permitted.
