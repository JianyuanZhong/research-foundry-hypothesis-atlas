# Proposal: ascertainment-safe AFP/hepatic trajectories and specificity-graded post-TACE pathways

## Episode 17 repair and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of assessed valid [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the parent’s population, index ontology, day-45 unconditioned primary transition, temporal boundaries, joins, and full-index estimand. The repair is an explicit outcome-ascertainment layer: every primary therapeutic transition is retained, but the solver must decompose it by encounter-constrained episode identity, procedural specificity, source concordance, and encounter-proxy diagnosis support. This tests whether an apparent AFP/hepatic trajectory signal survives increasingly specific recorded-care definitions without conditioning the primary target on a future reassessment.

The actual deliverable is newly fitted leakage-safe B0, Bobs and B2 competing-transition models on the locked primary target and its specificity overlays, plus the same-target irregular-time M3 if feasible. Completion requires reproducible index/episode/diagnosis-tier/landmark/split manifests; locked predictions; cumulative-incidence, calibration, alert-curve and patient-clustered uncertainty outputs; episode-source and diagnosis-ascertainment contrasts; negative controls; and an interpretation map linking each claim to an output field, estimate, interval and rule. Discovery has fitted no model and claims no clinical result.

## Strongest supported evidence and unresolved claim

The available evidence supports a linkable local recorded-care substrate, not a clinical treatment label. The inspected HCC sources contain dated encounters, dated procedure starts/ends, dated labs, dated examinations, and diagnoses joined to encounters but without a native diagnosis timestamp. Direct audits found 338,040 procedure rows in 95,674 encounters: 15,874 rows have no start time; 1,538 contain the exact name `TACE`; 11,389 contain `TACE`; 76,231 contain `Chemoembolization`; 58,046 contain `Transarterial chemoembolization`; 68,874 contain both `Hepatic artery` and `embolization`; and 78,916 contain `Hepatic arteriography`. Rows come from both `Medical Record Surgery` (230,916) and `Anesthesia System` (107,124). These counts show why a literal label, a component bundle, and a broad lexical ontology are not interchangeable; they do not establish treatment completion, intent, or benefit.

The diagnosis table contains 344,420 rows with `hepatocellular carcinoma` or `HCC`, covering 105,044 encounters and 43,815 patients. Diagnosis types include admission diagnosis, discharge diagnosis, attending physician diagnosis, pathological diagnosis, unknown and blank; 24,134 of those patients have only one HCC-labeled encounter and 19,681 have two or more. Thus encounter-time anchoring can support an ascertainment sensitivity, but it cannot recover the true diagnosis onset or clinical adjudication.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among adults with a first recorded TACE-like episode and no encounter-constrained therapeutic liver-directed episode through day 45, does early AFP/hepatic trajectory add reproducible information about a later recorded therapeutic pathway beyond measurement, contact and local-record retention, and does that information persist when the later event is restricted to a more specific procedure bundle with encounter-linked HCC support?

The primary hypothesis remains the parent’s full-index hypothesis: on the unconditioned eligible day-45 risk set, adding early AFP/hepatic values and changes to an observation/pathway baseline reduces the locked-test alert fraction at 90% sensitivity for the first primary-ontology therapeutic transition by at least 10 percentage points, with uncertainty excluding zero, while the gain is not reproduced for a non-liver capture control or pre-index placebo. The repair adds a specificity hypothesis: the direction of the incremental gain remains present for the high-specificity therapeutic tier and for the encounter-proxy HCC-supported subset, and does not appear only after selecting patients with a future reassessment opportunity. A failure of this overlay does not erase the primary result; it changes its interpretation to possible coding/episode/capture association.

This is a prognostic/recorded-pathway question. Even a fully supportive computation cannot establish viable tumour, recurrence, radiologic response, treatment need, treatment intent, treatment benefit, survival, utility, appropriateness, or external validity.

## Population and fixed temporal boundaries

All child tables are aggregated to `(Patient Master Index, Encounter Number)` before any join to `encounters`; no many-to-many child join is allowed.

1. Parse `procedures.Start time`. The primary episode groups rows only within the same patient and same encounter, ordered by start time, with a 24-hour gap rule within that encounter. Preserve raw `Surgery`, both procedure times, `Surgery source`, row count, lexical component flags, composite status and missing-time flags. Untimed rows cannot define an event and are audited separately.
2. Freeze the procedure vocabulary audit before modeling. The index is the earliest timed encounter-constrained episode containing literal `TACE`, a name containing `transarterial chemoembolization`, or both `hepatic artery` and `embolization`. Exact ties remain ties. The locked lexical sensitivity and the parent’s patient-time 24-hour grouping are reported; cross-encounter rows are never silently merged.
3. Require age >=18 and nonmissing `sex` from the index encounter.
4. Require a diagnosis name containing `hepatocellular carcinoma` on an encounter from 180 days before through 7 days after the index encounter. Because `diagnoses` has no native timestamp, `encounters.Encounter Time` is a cohort-window proxy only. A sensitivity excludes cases whose same-encounter diagnosis ordering cannot be established.
5. Restrict index `encounters.Encounter Time` to <= `2025-01-01 00:00:00`, with a nominal 365-day observation window. Show maximum locally observed follow-up for every index.
6. The day-45 risk set excludes any primary-ontology therapeutic liver-directed episode after the index through day 45. Diagnostic-only contacts remain eligible. Early therapeutic paths are reported, not silently discarded.
7. Follow days 46–365. The parent’s first primary-ontology therapeutic episode remains the primary recorded outcome, unconditioned on any later lab, examination, diagnosis, or reassessment opportunity. Alternate therapeutic liver-directed, diagnostic-only, mixed/unclassified, non-liver and end-of-local-observation states remain competing recorded states.
8. Keep the parent’s 14/30-day reassessment-opportunity descriptions secondary. An opportunity is a dated `labs.Test Time` or `examinations.Start Time` strictly before the therapeutic episode and outside its encounter. It is never required for the primary outcome.
9. Keep the parent’s dynamic first diagnostic-only liver-contact landmark, with predictors frozen immediately before contact. Add an encounter-proxy HCC-contact landmark only as a sensitivity: first post-day-45 encounter whose joined diagnosis contains `hepatocellular carcinoma`, with no qualifying therapeutic episode in that encounter. Call it a diagnosis contact, not diagnosis onset.
10. Use patient-level temporal splits: 2010–2022 fit, 2023 tuning, and 2024 through the cutoff locked test when support permits; otherwise deterministic patient hashing. No patient crosses splits. All thresholds, imputations, weights, episode vocabulary decisions and M3 tuning are fit-data-only.

## Primary estimand and specificity overlay

The primary estimand is incremental predictive information for the first broad recorded therapeutic transition from day 45 in the full eligible risk set, with competing recorded states and local-observation loss explicit. Report cause-specific discrete hazards, cumulative incidence through 30, 90 and 320 days after day 45, calibration and alert curves. The estimate is about local recorded pathways.

The primary label is not replaced. For each primary-ontology therapeutic episode, create a hierarchical ascertainment label using only rows in that same encounter-constrained episode:

- `P_broad`: the parent’s locked primary ontology: literal `TACE`, `transarterial chemoembolization`, or both `hepatic artery` and `embolization`.
- `S_explicit`: exact `TACE` or an explicit `chemoembolization`/`transarterial chemoembolization` component. This is a higher lexical-specificity recorded label, not proof of delivery or intent.
- `S_bundle`: `S_explicit` plus a same-episode hepatic arterial corroborator such as `hepatic arteriography`, `hepatic artery cannulation`, `local hepatic perfusion`, or `intra-arterial injection of chemotherapy drugs`, with the exact matched strings archived. This tests whether a multi-component episode is more specific than a single procedure row.
- `A_ambiguous`: `P_broad` without `S_explicit`, including hepatic arterial embolization/angiography combinations that do not explicitly contain chemoembolization.
- `U_mixed`: rows with competing liver-directed or non-liver components for which the locked ontology cannot uniquely assign a therapeutic class.

The future solver must report the original `P_broad` transition as the primary result and `S_explicit`, `S_bundle`, `A_ambiguous` and `U_mixed` as specificity strata/secondary transitions. It must not make `S_bundle` a new eligibility requirement or substitute it for the primary. Source (`medical record surgery` versus `anesthesia system`) and row-level agreement are contrasts, not evidence that either source is clinically authoritative.

Add diagnosis ascertainment labels without pretending to recover diagnosis time:

- `D_event`: an HCC diagnosis row joined to the outcome encounter, using encounter time as an event-window proxy; report diagnosis type and blank/unknown status.
- `D_stable_baseline`: HCC diagnosis on at least two distinct encounter dates, with at least one on or before the index encounter. This is computed from pre-index/index information and is used for baseline stratification, not as a claim of confirmed disease.
- `D_single_or_unknown`: only one HCC-labeled encounter or insufficient ordering/type information.

Report `S_bundle∩D_event` and `S_explicit∩D_event` as ascertainment sensitivities, never as a causal or biological gold standard. The full-index primary analysis retains missingness indicators and does not drop patients who lack later diagnosis support. Paired-observed and full-index estimands remain as in the parent; observation weighting is predictive standardization, with training-only overlap, truncation and effective-sample-size diagnostics.

## Matched baseline and substantive learned/mechanistic alternative

All models use the same cohort, `P_broad` target, specificity overlays, splits, horizons, competing states and patient-clustered uncertainty.

- **B0 transparent history/pathway baseline:** regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazards from age, sex, year, department, pre-index procedure/diagnosis history, day-45 encounter intensity, procedure-source mix, missingness and local-observation variables; no post-index numeric trajectory.
- **Bobs observation comparator:** B0 plus assay availability, lab/examination intensity, diagnostic-contact opportunity, reassessment opportunity and local-observation features, but no AFP/hepatic numeric values. It estimates how much signal is explained by who is measured and retained.
- **B2 transparent trajectory model:** Bobs plus frozen pre-index and days 7–45 AFP/hepatic values, changes, timing, assay identity and measurement counts, with no unit pooling and no future measurements. Evaluate on `P_broad`, then identically on the specificity overlays and diagnosis strata.
- **M3 irregular-time learned/mechanistic alternative:** a sparse low-rank state-space model with separate latent AFP activity and hepatic-function states, assay-specific observation models, an encounter/diagnosis-contact intensity, and post-contact competing transition hazards. Fit only to history available before each landmark; evaluate on exactly the same locked outcomes. M3 can reveal asynchronous sampling, marker discordance and trajectory shape that B2’s paired summaries lose. It is not granted merit for complexity.

The baseline is selected for auditability and an interpretable capture decomposition. M3 is retained because the substantive uncertainty is whether irregular timing and discordance add information beyond availability, not merely whether a flexible predictor wins a small score. A generic transformer is deferred: the available data cannot supply procedure intent, adjudicated response, outside-care completeness or diagnosis timing, and additional sequence capacity would not resolve those missing labels. This is an evidence-bound deferral, not a ban on neural models or GPU use. Causal treatment models, image/text response models, mortality and external validation remain deferred for unavailable dependencies.

## Falsification and interpretation rules

Fix all margins and bootstrap units before reading the locked outcomes. Use patient-clustered bootstrap intervals, calibration uncertainty, threshold uncertainty, event counts, untimed/mixed fractions, overlap, positivity, effective sample size and local-observation censoring.

Supportive computation requires: (i) B2 versus Bobs reduces the full-index `P_broad` alert fraction at 90% sensitivity by >=10 percentage points with an interval excluding zero and acceptable calibration/overlap; (ii) the direction persists for `S_explicit` and `S_bundle` with at least 100 locked-test events or else is labelled sparse/inconclusive; (iii) `S_bundle∩D_event` does not reverse the direction when adequately supported; (iv) the effect is not similar for the non-liver capture control or pre-index placebo, is not confined to high-contact/continued-observation strata, and survives training-only observation weighting; (v) it is stable under 14/30-day opportunity descriptions and encounter-only versus patient-time episode sensitivity; and (vi) the dynamic diagnosis-contact result is directionally consistent without conditioning the primary on future diagnosis contact. If M3 is run, convergence and a predeclared improvement of >=5 alert-fraction points or >=0.02 mean log score on the same target with calibrated uncertainty are required for an M3-positive statement.

Adverse evidence includes Bobs explaining nearly all B2 gain; broad gain disappearing in `S_explicit`/`S_bundle` or `D_event`; gain confined to one source, high-contact patients, non-disappearing patients or the future-contact subset; similar gain for non-liver/placebo controls; reversal under encounter-constrained episodes; poor calibration/overlap; or M3 nonconvergence. These findings support capture, coding, episode identity or ascertainment explanations, not a disease-specific trajectory claim.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few primary/specificity events, sparse diagnosis-linked episodes, excessive untimed or mixed rows, unresolved diagnosis ordering, insufficient one-year local support, temporal-split failure, positivity failure, bootstrap intervals spanning margins, or unstable dynamic landmarks. Margins cannot be relaxed after seeing outcomes. A supportive computation establishes at most reproducible local recorded-care stratification with stronger procedural/diagnostic concordance. Clinical adjudication is still required for TACE indication, intent, completion, response, eligibility and whether the pathway reflects active tumour. A prospective or independently linked study is required for treatment benefit, survival, mortality, outside-care completeness, clinical utility, causal effects, recommendations or external validity. The verifier can check computation and interpretation-to-output linkage, but cannot establish those claims.
