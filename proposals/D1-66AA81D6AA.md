# Pressor-record–conditioned recovery shape and observed renal deterioration: an eICU successor

## Scientific question, evidence boundary and advance

The selected parent asks whether late-persistent versus early-recovered low-MAP trajectories carry renal-risk information beyond minimum MAP and total low-MAP burden. This successor asks a treatment-relevant refinement:

> Among otherwise comparable hypotension-recovery trajectories, does the association between late-persistent low MAP and a subsequent creatinine-defined renal deterioration signal differ according to whether a qualifying vasopressor infusion record is present before the landmark?

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is only that the frozen eICU snapshot contains ICU-relative MAP observations, labelled serial laboratory results, patient/stay and hospital identifiers, admission physiology, and time-stamped infusion-record rows. The parent, seed and public references do not establish that hypotension causes AKI, that a pressor was actually given for a particular MAP, that 65 mmHg is a treatment target, or that a creatinine change is adjudicated AKI.

The unresolved claim is an observational effect-modification claim: the renal-risk meaning of a recovery pattern may be different when a qualifying pressor record is documented. A clinically useful result would distinguish a resolving low-MAP episode from a persistent episode in a context where recorded vasoactive support is or is not present, potentially informing prospective review and monitoring. It would not identify the benefit or harm of giving a pressor, nor a treatment policy. The substantive advance over the parent is the prespecified interaction estimand and competing-outcome decomposition, rather than another scalar hypotension exposure.

The proposal is not a reproduction of any demonstration or expert seed. It is a bounded eICU adaptation of the parent’s recovery-shape question.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Retain adults in the first ICU stay per person: numeric `age >= 18`, mapping eICU’s `> 89` convention to 90 while recording nonnumeric ages; `unitvisitnumber=1`; nonmissing `hospitalid`; numeric `unitdischargeoffset`; and ICU stay length at least 36 hours. If more than one valid `unitvisitnumber=1` row exists for a `uniquepid`, retain the earliest valid admission row and report the conflict.

Candidate landmarks are t = 12, 18, ..., 72 hours after ICU admission. Retain a landmark only with a six-hour pre-window, at least 24 hours remaining before `unitdischargeoffset`, and no `carePlanEOL` save/discussion offset at or before t. Use all eligible landmarks in the primary patient-clustered analysis; select one fixed-seed eligible landmark per stay only for sensitivity. Every predictor timestamp, including `labresultrevisedoffset`, must be <= t. No future creatinine, future pressor record, or post-landmark support variable is a predictor.

## Exposure and treatment-context variable

Primary MAP construction follows the parent exactly. Keep numeric `vitalPeriodic.systemicmean) in [-50, 300] mmHg; sort within `patientunitstayid`; let each observation cover only `min(5, next_valid_offset-current_offset)` minutes, clipped to [t-360,t), with no carry-forward across gaps and no interval past t. Require at least 80% covered minutes, and report 70% and 90% sensitivities, density and gaps by hospital.

Calculate `minimum_map`, `low_map_minutes`, `low_map_depth_area`, `early_low_minutes` in [t-360,t-120), and `late_low_minutes` in [t-120,t). Define recovery shape only among landmarks with early low MAP: recovered = `early_low_minutes > 0` and `late_low_minutes = 0`; persistent = `early_low_minutes > 0` and `late_low_minutes > 0`. Retain minimum MAP and total low-MAP minutes/depth in all models. Primary support strata are minimum MAP 55-64, 45-54 and <45 mmHg, crossed with total low-MAP burden 0-60, >60-120 and >120 covered minutes, only where both recovery states are observed. The exposure remains a measured-monitoring summary, not continuous hypotension.

Define `pressor_record=1` if at least one `infusionDrug` row has `infusionoffset` in [t-360,t) and `drugname` matches a frozen, case-insensitive, whitespace/punctuation-normalized dictionary of norepinephrine/noradrenaline, epinephrine/adrenaline, vasopressin, phenylephrine or dopamine labels. The dictionary, matched raw labels, and unmatched candidate labels must be written before fitting. A row is evidence of a documented infusion record only; it does not establish start/stop, dose, actual administration, indication or treatment response. `pressor_record=0` means no qualifying row was found, not that no pressor was given. Do not use `drugrate`, `infusionrate`, `drugamount`, `volumeoffluid` or `patientweight` as dose without validated units. The primary pressor interaction is therefore a documentation-context interaction, not a treatment effect.

Repeat MAP construction with `vitalAperiodic.noninvasivemean), and with periodic-first/aperiodic-only-when-periodic-absent rules. Repeat MAP cutoffs 60 and 70 mmHg. A `medication` table row may be audited for concordance using its available `drugorderoffset`, `drugstartoffset`, `drugstopoffset`, `drugname`, `drugordercancelled`, `routeadmin` and `activeupondischarge`, but medication orders are not administration evidence and are not substituted into the primary exposure.

## Outcome, observation censoring and competing events

The primary outcome is a creatinine-defined renal deterioration signal, not adjudicated AKI. Require two positive numeric creatinine results with comparable `labname`, `labmeasurenamesystem` and `labmeasurenameinterface`, timestamps in [t-48h,t], both with `labresultrevisedoffset <= t`, and separation of 6-48 hours. Use the last as baseline and retain the pre-t two-result slope. The compiled audit must identify exact retained labels/system/interface values, numeric parsing and plausible range. Among landmarks with at least one future creatinine in (t,t+24h], call the first future result an event if it is >= baseline + 0.3 on the retained source scale or >=1.5 times baseline. If comparability or units cannot be defended, stop clinical interpretation and label feasibility inconclusive rather than calling this KDIGO AKI.

In the 24-hour cumulative-incidence analysis, ICU death in (t,t+24h] and alive ICU discharge/transfer before 24 hours are competing events; use `unitdischargestatus` and `unitdischargelocation` with `unitdischargeoffset` to distinguish these as far as the structured fields permit. A future creatinine observed before a competing event can define the renal event. Missing future creatinine with no competing event is observation censoring, never a negative. Censor at the last observable opportunity and report complete-case, unweighted and inverse-probability-of-observation weighted analyses. Fit the observation model only from pre-t covariates and landmark time, with no future laboratory or treatment information. Report ICU death and alive discharge/transfer separately as secondary competing outcomes.

The primary estimand is the standardized 24-hour renal cumulative-incidence risk difference persistent minus recovered, separately within `pressor_record=1` and `pressor_record=0`, within the prespecified minimum-MAP/total-burden support strata and then standardized to the eligible-landmark distribution. The treatment-relevance estimand is the interaction contrast:
`RD_pressor-record - RD_no-pressor-record`.
It is an association between recovery-shape marker and renal-signal risk conditional on an imperfectly observed infusion-record context. It is not an effect of a pressor, a mediation effect, a treatment recommendation, or a causal policy contrast. Report the corresponding cause-specific and competing-event CIF contrasts only when the support gate passes.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The actual scientific deliverable requires newly fitting both a transparent nested model and a question-specific order-aware model.

1. The transparent baseline is a regularized discrete-time cause-specific pooled-logistic model, with landmark time and hospital-stratified intercepts, using admission demographics, admission-only Apache variables/scores, pre-t baseline creatinine and slope, minimum/mean/SD/last MAP, covered fraction, low-MAP minutes, depth area, recovery shape, pre-window HR/respiration/SpO2 summaries and slopes, documented ventilation indicators, missingness indicators, and `pressor_record`. The primary extension includes only the prespecified recovery-shape-by-pressor-record interaction (with the declared MAP/burden terms); the nested comparator removes recovery shape and all shape interactions. Standardize predicted cause-specific hazards to produce the renal CIF and the interaction risk difference. Patient clustering is handled in inference; hospital is a stratification/transport unit, never a feature.

2. The substantive alternative is a small temporal-convolution model over the same six one-hour pre-t bins and the same target. Inputs are hourly summaries and masks for MAP, HR, respiration, SpO2, creatinine, `pressor_record`, documented ventilation, and gap/coverage channels, plus the same admission covariates. Use a matched duration/depth-only model and a full-order model. The full model can reveal partial recovery, late persistence, irregular observation and pressor-record timing patterns that one binary recovery indicator and scalar summaries lose. It produces observational standardized renal CIFs and the same two pressor-record-stratum recovery contrasts; it must not simulate giving or withholding pressors.

Use fixed five-fold hospital grouping by SHA-256(hospitalid) modulo 5 for transport evaluation, with patient-level separation of validation data within the training folds. No hospital identifier is input to either model. Keep each `uniquepid` in one split and all landmarks from a stay together. Use three fixed random seeds for the learned model. Estimate 95% intervals by patient bootstrap nested within hospital; for learned models report seed dispersion separately and aggregate bootstrap uncertainty only after the split is fixed. Held-out hospital reporting requires at least 200 eligible landmarks and both recovery/pressor and renal-outcome levels; interaction cells with fewer than 50 landmarks or fewer than 10 renal events are descriptive only and do not support the interaction claim. These are support gates, not asserted counts.

The baseline was selected for interpretability, transparent stratum-specific standardization and a directly inspectable interaction. The learned alternative is scientifically useful because temporal order and timing of recorded pressor context—not generic nonlinear prediction—are the unresolved objects. It is not retained because of complexity, neural networks or a possible small score gain. Both methods are CPU-feasible within the 7,200-second science budget: allocate two CPUs and <=8 GiB; reserve <45 minutes for streaming extraction/regularized fitting and approximately 45-90 minutes for the small temporal variants plus three seeds. GPU is deferred because no distinct scientific question requires it. If learned fitting fails, the baseline estimand remains the primary deliverable and the failure is reported; it is not replaced by an easier endpoint.

## Falsification and interpretation

Before fitting, emit a machine-readable audit of population, stay and landmark filtering; MAP coverage/density/gaps; recovery and burden support by hospital and pressor-record stratum; the frozen pressor-label map; creatinine labels/system/interfaces, scale audit, baseline/future support; observation censoring; death/discharge competing events; and split assignments. A clinical estimate is withheld if creatinine scale comparability, recovery/pressor support or outcome support fails.

Prespecified falsification and sensitivity checks are:

1. Permute the order of valid MAP observations within each pre-t window while preserving MAP values, coverage, observation times/gaps and pressor-record status. The recovery-shape contrast and its interaction should attenuate; persistence suggests a scalar severity or documentation artifact.
2. Apply the same creatinine-rise rule as a pre-landmark placebo in [t-24h,t), using only results revised by <=t. A similarly strong placebo indicates reverse-time severity or ascertainment bias.
3. Repeat MAP source, periodic/aperiodic priority, coverage (70/80/90%), MAP cutoffs (60/65/70), burden bands, the two-result versus one-result baseline rule, and one-landmark-per-stay analysis.
4. Exclude landmarks within 12 hours of discharge/transfer, with EOL proxies, or with abrupt loss of all source coverage. Compare the interaction after adding documented ventilation and the row-presence pressor covariate; neither is causal treatment adjustment.
5. Repeat using only labels with verified exact concordance across infusion and medication-order records as a documentation sensitivity, while explicitly treating orders as non-administration evidence.
6. Perform leave-one-hospital-group-out evaluation and report calibration intercept/slope, Brier score, standardized CIFs and interaction intervals. Do not rank hospitals.
7. Compare a model with observation-process channels against one without them. A large transport loss or interaction change when observation channels are removed would support the testing-practice concern from seeds 02/08, but would not establish that reduced testing caused deterioration.

Supportive results require: (a) both pressor-record strata contain supported persistent/recovered contrasts; (b) the interaction interval excludes zero; (c) the sign and magnitude are reasonably stable across MAP source/coverage/cutoff, renal-baseline and observation-weighting analyses; (d) the pre-landmark placebo is weak; (e) time permutation attenuates the recovery contrast; and (f) the interaction or an order-aware temporal pattern reproduces in held-out hospitals with acceptable calibration. This supports a transportable observational marker whose interpretation depends on documented support context and justifies prospective renal adjudication/monitoring research. It does not support a pressor effect, treatment threshold or bedside policy.

Adverse results include a precise null interaction, reversal under plausible source or censoring rules, a strong placebo, failure of time-permutation attenuation, or failure to reproduce across held-out hospitals. These would favor total burden/minimum MAP or documentation severity over pressor-conditioned recovery shape, and would argue against using this marker to prioritize monitoring.

Inconclusive results include no within-stratum support, sparse qualifying pressor labels, unstable creatinine label/unit mapping, too few two-result baselines/future labs, unresolved observation censoring, insufficient held-out support, or intervals too wide to distinguish the prespecified supportive and adverse patterns. No clinical recommendation follows.

Computationally checkable claims are snapshot and file hashes, source/header/schema bindings, exact filters and joins, ICU-relative timestamps, MAP arithmetic, pressor-label matching, exposure support, creatinine parsing/timing, competing-event coding, split integrity, model fitting, standardization, calibration/Brier scores, bootstrap/seed uncertainty and falsification outputs. Clinical adjudication or another study is required to decide whether MAP observations represent perfusion, whether infusion rows represent administration and clinical intent, whether creatinine changes are KDIGO AKI, how urine output and outpatient baseline would alter classification, whether unobserved treatments/confounding explain the interaction, and whether acting on the marker improves renal or patient outcomes.

## Demonstration dispositions

The three demonstrations were used as methodological context, not as a fixed topic list.

- Delphi: the accessible local materials support the general idea that ordered structured histories can be modeled; the original UK Biobank training and external setting are not reproduced here. The temporal-convolution alternative is an eICU question-specific adaptation, with a different outcome and explicit hospital-held-out evaluation.
- ALADYNOULLI: the accessible materials motivate longitudinal latent trajectories, but the original genetic inputs and full reproduction dependencies are unavailable. An EHR-only latent model would answer a different question and is deferred because the current alternative directly tests recovery/pressor ordering with more inspectable outputs.
- Oncoformer: the main article and complete STAR Methods remain unavailable in the local bundle, and the image modality is absent. A lab-only cancer adaptation is irrelevant to this hemodynamic estimand and is deferred.

## Expert-seed dispositions

The imported eICU seed candidates were assessed as repairable sketches before parent selection; expert origin gives no validity or ranking bonus. Seed 03 (duration of hypotension) is the parent lineage and is retained as the matched MAP-duration/depth foundation. Seed 01 (hospital vasopressor strategy heterogeneity) is relevant but deferred as a causal strategy study: `infusionDrug` has no stop time, row presence is not administration, hospital preference is not an instrument, and the required confounding/support audit is unresolved. This successor uses only a pressor-record context interaction and does not claim the seed’s treatment-effect question.

Seed 02 (testing-practice transportability) and seed 08 (reduced testing and deterioration) remain relevant comparators: future-creatinine censoring, coverage channels and hospital holdout directly test their ascertainment concern, but this proposal does not treat missing labs as deterioration. Seed 10 (hospital recovery speed) is deferred because a practice/quality estimand would require stronger organ-support and organizational evidence and must not become hospital ranking. Seeds 04 (hyperoxia), 05 (ventilatory burden), 06 (nighttime discharge), 07 (interhospital transfer risk) and 09 (hypoglycemia after insulin) remain deferred: their key treatment, timing, post-ICU, nutrition or clinical-intent dependencies are not sufficiently verified for this renal interaction. In particular, `infusionDrug` lacks end fields, eICU lacks adjudicated outcomes, raw waveforms and narrative notes, and nutrition cessation cannot be inferred from intake/output absence.
