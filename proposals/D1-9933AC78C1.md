# Incremental laboratory information for three recorded HCC procedure pathways after documented pre-landmark planning

## Scientific deliverable and falsifiable hypothesis

The new scientific deliverable is a locked, held-out competing-risk analysis that estimates whether early laboratory trajectories add reproducible information about the next recorded liver-directed modality after accounting for pathway-named orders already documented before the 48-hour landmark. Completion requires:

1. a frozen adult first-observed HCC-coded encounter cohort and exact procedure/order dictionaries;
2. an audit of joins, multiplicity, timestamps, missing procedure starts, procedure source, and pathway/order concordance;
3. three mutually exclusive modality outcomes plus mixed/ambiguous and discharge competing events;
4. B0, B1, and an irregular coupled latent-state alternative fit on identical patient-level splits;
5. held-out cause-specific predictions, cumulative-incidence estimates, calibration, paired bootstrap uncertainty, and all falsification analyses;
6. a claim-to-output table that separates computational claims from clinical-adjudication and future-study claims.

Primary hypothesis:

> Among adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient encounter who have no eligible liver-directed procedure in hours 0–48 and remain in the hospital at 48 hours, early hepatic/renal laboratory trajectories contain incremental, held-out, pathway-differential information about the first recorded liver-directed modality in hours 48–120 beyond admission/pre-admission information and pathway-named orders documented before the landmark; specifically, a prespecified worsening hepatic/renal reserve pattern has a larger standardized cause-specific risk contrast for embolization/interventional treatment (E) than for liver resection (S).

This is a prognostic hypothesis about a recorded action. “Order documented” is a care-plan proxy, not validated intent. “Larger” is tested as the contrast of standardized cause-specific risk changes, with a two-sided 95% interval and a relevance threshold fixed before test inspection. The direction may be absent or reversed. Ablation (A) is a co-primary modality for estimation and an exploratory contrast against E and S; it is not silently combined with resection.

The clinically consequential unresolved question is whether a laboratory trajectory adds information beyond the pathway a clinical team had already documented, and whether that information differs among embolization, local ablation, and resection. This is more decision-relevant than predicting a heterogeneous “any procedure” label, because these modalities have different procedural burden and physiologic suitability considerations. It remains deliberately narrower than treatment selection: the data lack intent, stage, tumor burden, resectability, and benefit.

The strongest available evidence is data feasibility, not a clinical conclusion. The incumbent’s provisional audit found approximately 2,653 E and 11,969 combined resection/ablation first-pathway encounters before final exclusions, plus 28 same-time mixed-family cases. Those numbers are not results for this child; the solver must regenerate counts after splitting A and S, freezing exact strings, applying the order proxy, and enforcing all time rules. A bounded source vocabulary audit found 338,040 procedure rows, 7,781 distinct procedure strings, 15,874 missing procedure starts, and both 病案手术 and 手麻系统 sources. It also found common diagnostic-only 肝动脉造影 strings, therapeutic composites, and repeated orders, so broad keyword matching cannot establish the endpoint.

## Population, time, outcome, and estimand

2. Select the earliest eligible encounter per 患者主索引, ordered by native 入院时间 and then 就诊号. Do not silently replace an invalid earliest candidate. Report duplicate encounter keys, diagnosis multiplicity/types, unmatched keys, invalid/ongoing times, overlap, and earlier HCC-coded encounters.

3. Missing/invalid procedure start is not proof of no procedure; report it and perform a prespecified missing-time sensitivity only if its scale is interpretable. 4. The landmark is 入院时间 + 48 hours. Link procedures and orders only on the exact pair (患者主索引, 就诊号). Procedure onset is native 开始时间, never 结束时间. Orders use native 开立时间 for whether a pathway token was documented before the landmark; native 开始时间 is retained for a scheduled-start sensitivity. The primary event interval is [48, 120) hours from admission, or [0, 72) from the landmark, represented in 6-hour bins. Require procedure 开始时间 <= native 出院时间.

5. Freeze exact procedure strings after a blinded dictionary review of all candidate strings and composites. Define:
   - E: therapeutic embolization/interventional strings, including explicit hepatic-artery embolization, TACE/chemoembolization, and therapeutic hepatic-artery catheter intervention. Exclude angiography/catheterization-only strings unless the same exact string contains a therapeutic action.
   - A: explicit liver-lesion local ablation, including radiofrequency, microwave, cryoablation, or percutaneous ethanol ablation. Do not include biopsy-only strings.
   - S: explicit liver resection, such as partial, segmental, lobar, hemi-liver, or tumor resection. Do not include cholecystectomy, unrelated surgery, or transplant in S.
   - X: a first liver-directed therapeutic row that is ambiguous, mixed between E/A/S, transplant/other liver-directed therapy, or otherwise cannot be assigned without clinical interpretation. Same-time rows from different families are X unless a deterministic composite rule is frozen before outcome modelling.
   - D: native discharge before 120 hours with no prior E/A/S/X event. Discharge is a competing loss of inpatient procedural opportunity.
   - Administrative censoring: no E/A/S/X/D by 120 hours. No death endpoint exists and death must not be imputed as a competing event.

Report every exact string included/excluded, row/patient/encounter counts, same-time conflicts, source-system contribution, invalid/missing time counts, and low-frequency dictionary members. If any family has fewer than 100 locked events or X is too frequent to make the labels coherent, the primary result is inconclusive.

6. Construct the documented-care-plan proxy from the orders table. After the order dictionary is independently frozen, define pre-E, pre-A, and pre-S indicators as at least one valid order string matching that pathway with valid native 开立时间 in [入院时间, landmark). Do not call these intent, recommendation, eligibility, or scheduling labels. Deduplicate by exact encounter, pathway, and order text/time; report repeated orders and invalid times. Orders after the landmark are excluded from all primary predictors and may appear only in a labelled leakage audit.

The primary estimand is the 72-hour landmark cumulative incidence for E, A, S, and X under the observed competing process, and the incremental standardized cause-specific risk contrast for the worsening hepatic/renal pattern after adding labs to B0. The principal directional contrast is ΔE-S = standardized lab-associated risk contrast for E minus that for S. Report ΔE-A and ΔS-A as secondary contrasts. Estimate cause-specific hazards for E/A/S/X/D with all other causes competing, then compute CIFs; never interpret a cause-specific hazard ratio as a treatment effect or marginal probability.

The primary order-adjusted hypothesis is supported only if B1 improves held-out calibration or prespecified pathway-risk stratification over B0, ΔE-S has the prespecified direction and uncertainty excludes the null/relevance boundary, and the effect is not removed by order/source/measurement falsifications. If a clinically defensible relevance threshold cannot be fixed before fitting, report the estimate and interval without directional support.

## Models, split, uncertainty, and evaluation

B0 is a transparent cause-specific pooled-logistic or discrete-time hazard model containing age, sex, admitting department, pre-admission/encounter timing descriptors, pre-admission lab summaries if supported by the frozen assay audit, and pre-E/pre-A/pre-S order indicators. B1 adds the same early labs as prespecified first/last/change or slope features, observation density, missingness, and timestamp-gap terms. B0 and B1 use the same patients, bins, competing causes, preprocessing rules, and split.

The substantive alternative is a low-dimensional continuous-time irregular state model with separate fitted hepatic-reserve and renal-stress states, assay-specific observation offsets/noise, irregular gaps, patient shrinkage, and an explicit observation-process term. At the landmark its E/A/S/X/D heads use latent levels, recent slopes, coupled contrast, posterior state uncertainty, and B0 covariates. States are fitted summaries, not validated organ-failure diagnoses. Select dimensions, regularization, and observation-process specification only in buckets 0–59 and 60–69. A GRU/temporal-attention model is deferred because it would add complexity without a currently demonstrated need; revisit only for a new child if locked residuals show reproducible nonlinear timing interactions.

Primary locked test is buckets 70–79, with patient-level splits by 患者主索引 so no rows or encounters cross partitions. Report cause-specific Brier and integrated Brier scores with competing-risk weighting, time-binned discrimination only as secondary summaries, 72-hour CIF calibration, calibration intercept/slope, observed-versus-predicted CIF by prespecified risk bins, and standardized E/A/S risk contrasts. Use at least 500 paired patient bootstrap resamples for B1-B0, alternative-B1, all modality contrasts, calibration, and relevance estimates. Preserve event prevalence and absolute as well as relative differences; no policy threshold is validated.

Supportive results require all of: a nonzero, directionally coherent ΔE-S with reported uncertainty; B1 improves over B0 without major calibration harm; the alternative adds prespecified clinically meaningful calibration or CIF information over B1, or the simpler B1 is retained because the alternative adds no information; the result persists in an order-adjusted and all-recorded analysis; no one source system, department, era, assay, exact string, or mixed category drives it; and procedure/order concordance does not show a signal confined to poorly documented records.

Adverse results include a null/opposite ΔE-S, no incremental B1 gain, no alternative gain, disappearance after pre-landmark order adjustment, strong dependence on measurement density or timestamp, persistence under timestamp permutation, equivalent pathway discrimination for a negative-control workflow endpoint, source-system discordance, or domination by a single procedure string. These results would show that the incumbent recorded-pathway association is not robust evidence of independent physiologic information.

Inconclusive results include fewer than 100 events in any primary family, excessive X or missing-time rows, failure to freeze a coherent dictionary, inadequate dated assay support, incomplete discharge ascertainment, nonconvergence, unstable CIF weighting, or intervals too wide to distinguish a meaningful incremental pathway signal from no signal.

## Falsification and validity analyses

1. Compare B0, B1, and the alternative with and without pre-landmark order indicators. A large loss after adding orders means the laboratory result was redundant with documented care planning, not independent physiologic evidence.
2. Require, as a labelled outcome-validity stress test, a pathway-concordant order opened in [admission, procedure start) for the same encounter and compare with all-recorded outcomes. This is not a gold standard and does not redefine missing orders as absence of intent. The pathway claim fails if support is confined to uncorroborated records or reverses under this stress test.
3. Stratify results by procedure source 手术来源 and report source-specific event rates and exact-string contributions. Material source discordance is adverse, not a reason to pool silently.
4. Permute laboratory timestamps within patient and exact assay while preserving values and assay identity. A timing-specific trajectory claim should attenuate; unchanged performance implicates value, density, or care intensity.
5. Add/remove measurement count, missingness, timestamp gaps, and observation-process terms. If the contrast disappears, label it surveillance/selection signal.
6. Use pre-admission labs as a negative timing control and verify no primary token precedes admission or exceeds the 48-hour landmark.
7. Repeat under frozen same-assay/same-timestamp aggregation and after duplicate removal; report values exactly at both lab-window boundaries.
8. Repeat 48–168 hours from admission with identical dictionary, order, discharge, and competing-event rules as a secondary sensitivity.
9. Repeat by admitting department, calendar era, measurement density, and source-system contribution; treat these as transportability diagnostics, not causal effect modification.
10. Retain X as a competing event and separately report an analysis excluding X. If conclusions depend on forcing mixed strings into E, A, or S, the claim fails.
11. Use central-line/venipuncture as a negative-control workflow endpoint. Comparable lab-pathway discrimination implicates general care intensity.
12. Run a deliberate naive analysis that ignores competing events and quantify its divergence from the CIF analysis; it is not primary.
13. Use post-landmark orders/procedures only in a labelled leakage demonstration, never primary predictors.
14. Preserve the parent’s E versus combined R bridge analysis, but mark it secondary: it cannot substitute for the separated A/S estimand.

## Evidence limits and required future work

Computationally checkable claims are source/schema verification, exact dictionary and order audits, cohort flow, join integrity, split isolation, event/discharge/censor counts, model convergence, held-out predictions, CIFs, calibration, bootstrap intervals, source concordance, and whether prespecified falsifications occurred.

The snapshot cannot establish that an order represented intent, that a procedure was indicated or elective/emergent, that a patient was eligible for another modality, or that any modality improved survival, recurrence, symptoms, toxicity, or quality of life. It cannot establish validated HCC diagnosis, pathology, stage, tumor burden, vascular invasion, resectability, treatment necessity, multidisciplinary recommendation, death, transplant candidacy, outside-hospital events, or causal treatment effects. It cannot distinguish the physiologic effect of cancer from clinician selection based on unrecorded stage.

Clinical adjudication is required to validate the exact procedure dictionary and determine whether composites represent therapeutic action. A stronger study requires timestamped radiology/pathology, stage and resectability, tumor burden, treatment intent and recommendation, laboratory units/reference ranges, complete mortality and multi-hospital linkage, and external validation. Only then could an analysis assess treatment selection, comparative effectiveness, or a clinical policy.
