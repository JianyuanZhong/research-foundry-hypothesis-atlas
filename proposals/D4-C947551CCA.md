## Audited conclusion and falsification meaning

Independent aggregate and private-integrity audit job `[research job]` found no computation failure and classified the frozen experiment as **inconclusive localization**. This is not an adverse verdict on cystatin C. The execution supports a real, strongly borderline-concentrated held-out log-loss pattern under the selected-cohort protocol, and that pattern passes the repaired empirical-null diagnostic. It does not support the stronger claim that the information yields clinically meaningful concentration at fixed global capacity: the borderline global contribution is negative, the simultaneous interaction interval narrowly misses the fixed `-0.0010` margin, and the inherited +5 global capacity gate remains unmet. Loss localization and capacity localization therefore do not jointly satisfy the frozen supportive rules.

Had H and its required joint uncertainty cleared the localization margin, the empirical-null diagnostic passed, the borderline global contribution met its positive simultaneous +2 requirement, and the inherited global gate passed, the result would have supported protocol-bounded localized prediction information. A narrow H interval inside `[-0.001,+0.001]`, favorable stratum increments, and passed global gates would have supported a diffuse pattern. A materially positive H beyond `+0.001` with the required uncertainty would have been adverse to the prespecified borderline hypothesis. Here the mixed estimands and failed capacity gates make the result inconclusive rather than localized supportive, diffuse, or adverse.

The empirical-null result is conditional on the frozen predictor distribution, strata, selected complete repeat attenders, and refitted fold mechanism. Stronger conclusions about CKD, measured renal function, baseline deployment, assay validity, testing benefit, mechanism, missingness, attendance selection, or transportability require clinical adjudication, external prospective data, and a separate baseline-deployable decision study. In particular, observed elapsed repeat time is future-known and the population is selected on complete first-repeat attendance, so this execution cannot establish deployable clinical utility.
