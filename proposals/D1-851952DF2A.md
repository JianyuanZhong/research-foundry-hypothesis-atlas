# HCC trajectory repair: observation-selection-robust day-42 increment

Status: substantive child proposal for parent [prior hypothesis]. Design only; no cohort count, fit, prevalence, endpoint adjudication result, or clinical conclusion is claimed.

## Remaining clinical interpretation threat

The inherited design asks whether pre-index-to-day-42 assay change/order improves prediction of a later locally recorded repeat-TACE event beyond baseline state and care workflow. Its strongest remaining threat is selection at the prediction landmark, not merely unmeasured severity. Requiring a usable record through day 42 and then using a day-42 assay conditions on a future observation process that can be affected by current severity, impending retreatment, clinician scheduling, transfer/outside care, and documentation intensity. A late assay can consequently be a marker of being observed and prepared for repeat TACE. Ordinary adjustment for P_obs/P_care inside the selected R42 cohort does not show that the trajectory would carry information in the index population or that it is not a consequence of the selection process.

The leading explanation predicts a trajectory increment that survives an explicitly modeled R42 observation-selection mechanism and remains when the final 7-day records are removed. The strongest rivals predict that the increment vanishes after selection weighting, is reproduced by a process-only channel, or exists only when values close to the day-42 boundary are available. This is a predictive/descriptive test of recorded information, not a causal or biologic test.

The exact unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among the inherited index-TACE population, after accounting for baseline severity, baseline assay state, measured care workflow, and the probability of remaining observable through day 42, does the pre-index-to-day-42 assay trajectory add held-out information beyond the latest day-42 assay snapshot for a later operational repeat-TACE record, with the increment surviving a pre-day-42 landmark falsifier?

A supportive result would justify a prospective study with scheduled assays and adjudicated intent/imaging. It would not establish treatment response, treatment benefit, intent, or mechanism.

## Falsification and decision gates

All controls are frozen before held-out scoring and run once.

1. **R42 selection falsifier.** Compare unweighted R42 results with primary stabilized R42 weights and the R28 arm. If the day-42 increment disappears after R42 weighting, or the weight has non-overlap/unstable tails, the observation-selection explanation is favored or the estimand is INCONCLUSIVE.
2. **Pre-day-42 falsifier.** If A_traj42 is positive but A_traj28-vs-snap28 is absent or adverse, the result is late-window-dependent and cannot support a durable pre-index-to-day-42 trajectory interpretation. This does not show that day-28 biology is uninformative.
3. **Late-documentation lead time.** Refit identically after excluding lab/process rows in (t0+35,t0+42] and using the fixed day-28 cutoff. A trajectory increment that is erased is adverse to the claimed durable trajectory and compatible with late review/scheduling documentation.
4. **Process-only rival.** Compare A0, a process-only P_obs/P_care block, A_snap42 and A_traj42. If process-only matches the trajectory within the predeclared paired uncertainty/resolution rule, retain only a workflow-associated prediction.
5. **Value/timestamp permutation.** Within patient and assay label, preserve times and availability but permute result content across admissible times in development-frozen strata; separately permute assay labels. A matched increment indicates timing or generic measurement-volume artifact.
6. **Horizon and corroborator controls.** Preserve the inherited [43,90]/[91,181), [t-42,t-1]/[t-28,t-1]/[t-7,t-1] corroborator restrictions, early semantic-procedure alias audit, and ordinary versus weighted analyses.
7. **Support.** Report paired-label availability, missing times, qualitative/inequality counts, near misses, R28/R42 counts, events, censoring, ESS and tails before interpretation. Inherited O1/O2 minimum-event and ESS gates remain; selection-weight failure or overlap failure is INCONCLUSIVE.

SUPPORTIVE requires overall O0 and O1 support, positive simultaneous lower bounds for D42 and D42-vs-snap, retention after R42 weighting, no adverse late-window/day-28 falsifier, no matching process-only or permutation control, and stable direction in the matched learned sensitivity. This supports recorded trajectory information only.

ADVERSE means no increment after A0, snapshot-only support, disappearance under R42 weighting, late-window-only signal, or a matched process/timestamp permutation. Retain a narrower snapshot/workflow result when supported; do not conclude assays lack prognostic value.

INCONCLUSIVE means inadequate paired support, positivity/ESS or event support failure, wide uncertainty, unstable representation results, or endpoint adjudication weakness. Freeze the direction and seek an independent or prospectively scheduled data source rather than changing windows or subgroups.

## Baseline and substantive learned alternative

The confirmatory baseline is a pooled logistic discrete-time hazard with elastic-net regularization for O0/O1/O2, using the four nested blocks, development-fold parser/scaler/imputer/weight fitting, the deterministic earliest-80%/latest-20% patient-disjoint split, five grouped development folds, and one untouched test set. Uncertainty is paired patient bootstrap with one max-|t| family across O0/O1/O2, snapshot contrasts, selection-weight contrasts and fixed falsifiers. This baseline is scientifically adequate because the question is a nested information increment and its blocks/weights must be auditable.

The alternative is a compact time-aware GRU over the identical allowed event stream from t0-365 through t0+42, with label identity, numeric/qualitative/inequality content, relative time, time since prior event, and channel tag; separate hazard heads and identical A0/snapshot/change/trajectory and R42-weighted ablations. Three development seeds use the same split, outcomes, metrics and held-out test. It can reveal nonlinear ordered patterns, irregular timing and recovery-then-worsening combinations that elastic-net summaries lose. It cannot add assay units, images, outside care or intent. If GRU improves but transparent contrasts do not, call evidence representation-sensitive, not trajectory-established; if both agree, the result is more reproducible but still recorded prediction.

Planning estimate: primary materialization, two selection models, nested fits, paired bootstrap and permutation controls approximately 1–4 hours on 4 CPU/16 GiB; GRU sensitivity approximately 2–6 hours on 4 CPU/16 GiB with one allocated A100 using `cuda:0`. These are planning estimates, not measured runtime. The discovery limit is 9,000 science seconds with two GPU slots; the inherited future solver envelope is larger and separate. No GPU is required for the primary. Revisit the GRU only if the primary contrast has adequate event/support overlap; do not switch methods for a small predictive gain.

Not chosen: a larger transformer, NLP of untimed documents, a latent causal/intent model, and a mechanistic liver-response model. They require timed expert intent/response labels, validated narrative temporality, assay units/reference ranges, images/reports or outside-care capture that are absent. Complexity is not a reason to adopt them.
