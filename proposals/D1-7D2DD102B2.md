# Fixed all-randomized endpoint validation with uncensored safety flags, component-specific ambiguity budgets, cluster-aware sensitivity, and an empirical accrual contract

## Fixed question and substantive repair

The scientific question is unchanged: in a new randomized norepinephrine-first (NE-first) versus vasopressin-first (VP-first) discontinuation program, can a locked EHR endpoint reproduce the assignment-indexed arm risk differences obtained from linked pump-native delivery plus blinded intent adjudication, within absolute RD-distortion 0.025 for every clinically distinct pathway component? All randomized first patients remain in their assigned arm. The fixed confirmation remains 7,602 patients (3,801/arm), the co-primary intersection-union claim still requires every component plus the legacy guardrail to pass, and no result estimates treatment benefit.

This child repairs four consequential specifications without changing that question. First, rescue no longer closes safety observation: death and first alive ICU exit are flags observed through six hours and can co-occur with rescue. Second, generic percentages of “indeterminate” are replaced by component- and direction-specific feasible-state ambiguity counts, exact partial-information budgets, and one integer-compatible worst completion shared by all endpoints. Third, patient-level Clopper-Pearson (CP) bounds are explicitly conditional measurement bounds, not exact marginal guarantees under shared configuration failures; a hospital-cluster wild-bootstrap sensitivity is a required, approximate robustness gate and is not called exact. Fourth, a newly executed MIMIC screen supplies an empirical medication-only upper ceiling, demonstrates why calendar/site-month extrapolation is impossible, and is paired with a prospective site-month readiness contract that can falsify accrual before confirmation starts.

## Evidence already supported and the unresolved claim

Frozen adverse MIMIC evidence remains unchanged. There were 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks but only 211 observed NE-first and 167 VP-first actions (3.49% and 2.76%, below the frozen 5% positivity gate); the 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort had 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate failures. Of 73 failures in 136 determinate episodes, 59 included a charted assigned-drug restart and 36 were restart-only. MCS status was unknown in all 137. Compact temporal models had negligible Brier improvement and adverse transport. In the complete transaction audit, five symmetric reconstructions invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices; the inherited shared-chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop and 13/59 restart indices, all at `Paused` boundaries. These establish sparse, restart-dominated, transaction-sensitive EHR labels, not interrupted pump delivery, rescue intent, strategy efficacy, or prospective eligibility.

A new source-row computation inspected the supplied MIMIC-IV 3.1 archive rather than extrapolating from inherited counts. Among 65,366 adult ICU patients and 94,458 adult ICU stays, there were 5,232 stays with valid positive NE and VP records, 5,031 with any temporal overlap, 3,419 with any overlap under both eligibility dose caps, 1,993 stays and 1,916 first patients with at least six continuous hours of overlapping charted NE at no more than 0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP at no more than 0.04 U/min. Thus 1,916/65,366 (2.93%) is a medication-only first-patient **upper ceiling** over the entire supplied single-center snapshot, before stability, other-pressor, MAP, treating-team permissibility, pump-linkage, MCS, consent, and configuration requirements. It is not exact eligibility and cannot be divided by calendar months: MIMIC timestamps are shifted within subject, cross-subject calendar alignment is invalid, the source is one center, and site/configuration identities needed by the prospective design are absent. The 7,602-patient claim therefore has no defensible MIMIC-derived site-month rate; it must stand or fall on the prospective contract below.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is only that the retrospective EHR endpoint and a medication-only eligibility ceiling are computable, while observed actions are sparse and restart labels are unstable. The unresolved claim is prospective paired measurement validity of every assignment-indexed component RD in the locked enrolled configuration mixture. Pump-native delivered rate, clinician intent, randomization/permissibility, an escrowed cross-system clock map, dependable MCS exclusion, and multicenter accrual are required and absent from MIMIC.

## Population, assignment, reference data, and clocks

The population and intervention are inherited. At least 20 and at most 24 hospitals must pass readiness before confirmation; this strengthens the operational configuration support but does not alter patient eligibility or the target enrolled mixture. There are at least two adult ICUs per hospital where available and at least two pump-EHR interface families. A configuration is `(site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major version, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version, clock-map method/version)`. Every change after first confirmation enrollment is a new configuration and cannot be silently pooled.

Each configuration completes the inherited 50-new-episode technical run-in: at least 95% unique patient-pump-channel-drug linkage and complete pump export from two hours before candidate assignment through endpoint closure, an escrowed clock map, and at least 90% contemporaneous material for blinded intent adjudication. One wholly new 50-episode repeat is permitted after repair; second failure excludes that configuration. Run-in patients enter no estimand.

Eligibility is fixed before randomization: age at least 18; alive in an adult ICU; linked pump-delivered NE and VP concurrently for at least six hours; at the beginning of the final 30 minutes NE greater than 0 and no more than 0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP greater than 0 and no more than 0.04 U/min, neither increased during that 30 minutes; no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours; at least three valid MAPs spanning three of four prior 30-minute bins including the final bin, median at least 65 and minimum at least 55 mmHg; no prospectively defined active MCS; and a prereandomization treating-team record that either strategy is permissible. The first eligible randomization per person is the sole primary unit.

Immutable randomization time is `tr`; `Z=0` assigns NE-first and `Z=1` VP-first in configuration-stratified permuted blocks. Actual first stop never replaces assignment. Assigned cessation should occur within 120 minutes. Reference qualifying cessation `t0_R` is the first linked pump-native delivered-rate time in `[tr,tr+120 min]` below 0.005 mcg/kg/min NE or 0.005 U/min VP continuously for five minutes while the comparator remains above its threshold. A programmed but unlinked rate is insufficient. Nonassigned cessation first is crossover; both within five minutes are simultaneous; later assigned cessation cannot erase either.

Each source uses a prospectively escrowed affine clock map with offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving rule, fitting rows, residuals, and version. The ambiguity band is the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual capped at 60 seconds. Uncertainty crossing an ordering, five-minute, 120-minute, or six-hour boundary is indeterminate, never rounded toward agreement.

## Pathway states and safety events that rescue cannot censor

Implementation is observed to `min(tr+120 min, death, first alive ICU exit)`. Every randomized patient has immutable reference and EHR implementation state `I`: `AQ`, `XO`, `SIM`, `NI_OVERRIDE`, `NI_NO_STOP`, `EXIT_PRE`, `DEATH_PRE`, or `IND_I`. `NI_OVERRIDE` requires a prospectively timestamped reason. For `AQ`, reference rescue is return of the assigned drug to threshold for at least five minutes before `t0_R+6h`, with two blinded adjudicators classifying deliberate treatment or prevention of worsening hypotension/impaired perfusion; otherwise unresolved intent is indeterminate.

Crucially, rescue is an event, not a censoring time. Pump export, vital status, and ICU location continue through `t0_R+6h` after `AQ`, regardless of rescue or an earlier alive exit. For no `AQ`, implementation safety is observed through `tr+120 min`. The source-specific event flags are:

* `R=1`: adjudicated rescue by `t0+6h` after `AQ`;
* `D_pre=1`: death before `AQ` and by `tr+120 min`;
* `D_6=1`: after `AQ`, death from any source by `t0+6h`, even if rescue or alive ICU exit occurred first;
* `X_pre=1`: first ICU exit alive before `AQ` and by `tr+120 min`;
* `X_6=1`: after `AQ`, first ICU exit alive by `t0+6h`, even if rescue occurred first;
* `S_6=1`: `AQ` and alive, still in ICU, and without rescue at `t0+6h`.

`R`, `D_6`, and `X_6` are not mutually exclusive. A patient rescued and then dying has `R=D_6=1`; rescue followed by alive exit has `R=X_6=1`; alive exit followed by death before six hours has `X_6=D_6=1`. `S_6` is incompatible with any of those three. The mutually exclusive descriptive pathway `C` is retained for audit, but no first-disposition partition is allowed to determine these safety flags.

## Seven co-primary all-randomized components

For every randomized patient and each source EHR/reference, derive:

1. `H_impl=1{I=AQ}`;
2. `H_rescue=R` (zero when no `AQ`, indeterminate only when rescue status is genuinely unresolved);
3. `H_death_pre=D_pre`;
4. `H_death_6=D_6`;
5. `H_exit_pre=X_pre`;
6. `H_exit_6=X_6`;
7. `H_stable=S_6`.

All use the full assigned-arm denominator. For arm `z`, `p_zk=P(H_Rk=1|Z=z)` and `Delta_R,k=p_1k-p_0k`; corresponding EHR quantities use `H_Ek`. Report all arm-specific rates/RDs, overlaps among rescue/death/exit flags, and implementation states. Conditional rescue among `AQ` is only a selected postrandomization diagnostic beside all-randomized implementation. There is no scalar treatment ranking.

The inherited legacy guardrail remains an eighth required gate after clinical panel ratification, but is not the clinical estimand and cannot rescue a component failure. Its inherited mapping is supplemented by the uncensored flags in displays; rescue followed by death is never hidden even if the legacy first-disposition label is rescue.

## Paired distortion, co-primary IUT, and conditional scope of the exact bounds

For each arm/component, among all randomized patients define `f_zk=P(H_Ek=1,H_Rk=0|Z=z)` and `g_zk=P(H_Ek=0,H_Rk=1|Z=z)`. The exact identity is

`b_k=(Delta_E,k-Delta_R,k)=f_1k-g_1k-f_0k+g_0k`.

For each component separately, construct four two-sided CP intervals, one for each error probability, at interval `alpha=0.0125`; their Bonferroni Cartesian product has at least 95% coverage under the binomial sampling model for the four patient-level indicators. Compute

`b_Lk=L(f_1k)-U(g_1k)-U(f_0k)+L(g_0k)`,

`b_Uk=U(f_1k)-L(g_1k)-L(f_0k)+U(g_0k)`, and

`B_Uk=max(|b_Lk|,|b_Uk|)`.

Reject `H0k: |b_k|>=0.025` only if `B_Uk<0.025`; equality fails. The global null is the union of the seven component nulls and the global claim requires all seven rejections plus the legacy gate. The intersection-union logic controls the false all-components-pass claim without selecting or trading components.

The CP statement is explicitly **conditional** on the enrolled configuration mixture and on patient discordance indicators behaving as independent Bernoulli observations (or, more narrowly, as a conservative working measurement model conditional on fixed configuration processes). Common pump software, interface, clock, or adjudication failures can correlate errors within a site/configuration, so these intervals are not advertised as exact marginal site-cluster-robust coverage. The fixed `n=3,801/arm` calculation remains a conditional planning calculation under `f=g=0.005`: the inherited exact 8-through-33 count envelope gives worst component `B_U=0.0249973642`, dependence-robust assurance 0.915035 over the 32 planned counts by a union bound, while `n=3,800` fails. “Dependence-robust” here means dependence among the 32 count-tail events under their binomial marginal planning models, not arbitrary within-configuration patient correlation.

## Prespecified configuration-aware sensitivity without a false exactness claim

Let `A_ik=H_Eik-H_Rik` in `{-1,0,1}` after each joint completion. Then `b_k` is the assigned-arm contrast in `A`. For every completed component and the legacy endpoint, fit the prespecified patient-level model `A_ik = configuration fixed effects + beta_k Z_i`, weighting configurations to their observed full randomized confirmation mixture and retaining zero-error configurations. Report `beta_k`, the mixture-standardized contrast, and a two-sided 95% CR2 small-sample sandwich interval clustered at hospital. In parallel, conduct 99,999 Rademacher wild-cluster bootstrap-t draws at the hospital level, imposing each equivalence boundary (`beta=-0.025` and `+0.025`) and respecting configuration-stratified assignment in the design matrix. The sensitivity passes only when both one-sided boundary p-values are below 0.025 and the CR2 interval lies strictly inside `(-0.025,0.025)` for every component and legacy endpoint under the same worst joint completion.

This is an approximate configuration-sharing sensitivity, not an exact confidence procedure. It requires at least 20 contributing hospitals, at least 16 with both arms and at least 50 patients total, no hospital over 15% of either arm, both interface families represented in at least six hospitals, CR2 residual degrees of freedom at least 10, full convergence, and no single hospital whose deletion changes the sign of `beta` or moves the point estimate outside the margin. Failure of a completed cluster sensitivity, material assignment-by-configuration interaction, or an adjusted configuration error range above 0.10 is adverse transport/robustness evidence; fewer effective clusters, singular fit, failed bootstrap, or inability to evaluate a completion is inconclusive. Neither outcome is relabeled as an exact clustered test. The primary claim remains restricted to the observed locked mixture; no superpopulation-of-sites claim is made.

Also report leave-one-hospital and leave-one-interface-family-out patient-level exact working bounds. Every leave-out mixture must pass all component and legacy gates, but these diagnostics do not create marginal cluster coverage.

## Component-specific feasible-state ambiguity budgets and one joint completion

There is no generic overall or arm-by-configuration indeterminacy percentage. Each unresolved datum is recorded by source and feasible endpoint states, never as an unrestricted “unknown”: `pump_link/clock` can alter implementation and downstream flags; `rescue_intent` can alter rescue and stable but cannot alter death or exit; `vital_status` can alter death and stable; `ICU_location` can alter exit and stable; a boundary crossing can alter only states adjacent to that boundary. Death documented independently remains known even when rescue intent is unresolved. Alive ICU exit documented independently remains known after rescue. Thus component-specific partial information preserves safety evidence rather than propagating rescue ambiguity into death/exit.

For each arm `z`, component `k`, and error direction, produce counts:

* `u_zk^+`: patients whose feasible joint states permit `(H_E,H_R)=(1,0)`;
* `u_zk^-`: patients whose feasible joint states permit `(0,1)`;
* `u_zk^both`: patients permitting both discordance directions across different states;
* `u_zk^0`: patients forced concordant despite another component being unresolved;
* reason-specific versions for `pump_link/clock`, `rescue_intent`, `vital_status`, `ICU_location`, and `other_adjudication`.

For each observed arm/component table, calculate four **component-specific exact ambiguity capacities**: the largest integer number of additional feasible discordances in each one of `f_1k,g_1k,f_0k,g_0k`, holding the other three observed counts fixed, for which the CP `B_Uk` remains strictly below 0.025. Capacity is recomputed from observed counts, not expressed as a percentage. A direction is over budget when its feasible count exceeds its capacity. Because capacities considered separately are not jointly additive, they are only diagnostics and can never establish a pass.

The decisive analysis jointly assigns every unresolved patient to one nonnegative integer-compatible source-state pair, constrained by that person's feasible states, immutable `I`, event timing, known death/exit, component derivations, arm/configuration marginals, and all cross-component logical identities. For every feasible **single joint completion**, compute all 28 directional component errors, four legacy errors, every CP bound, and every cluster sensitivity. Optimize the global failure score `max(B_Ulegacy, B_U1,...,B_U7)` and retain (a) its maximum, (b) each component-specific maximum, and (c) integer witness rows mapping ambiguity-pattern counts to completed source-state pairs. Supportive status requires every feasible completion to pass every exact bound and the cluster sensitivity; equivalently, no feasible integer completion can violate a gate. Metric-specific favorable imputations cannot be combined.

Pilot go/no-go uses the same feasible-state machinery at the prespecified pilot margin 0.060: proceed only if each component's and legacy endpoint's maximum joint-completion `B_U<0.060`, no death or exit uncertainty is caused by rescue censoring, and reason-specific ambiguity can be resolved prospectively without changing mappings. The confirmation margin remains 0.025. Timeout, absent witness, disagreement with exhaustive person-level enumeration for test cases with at most eight unresolved labels, noninteger/marginal incompatibility, failed state invariants, or disagreement between direct and state-derived components is inconclusive. Missing verification of EHR negatives or coding lost capture as no event invalidates the analysis.

## Empirically falsifiable accrual contract

The MIMIC screen is a ceiling only and supplies no calendar denominator. Accordingly, the confirmation cannot launch on a narrative projection. Before pilot release, each proposed hospital must submit six consecutive prospective shadow-screening months with one row for every dual-pressor ICU patient and fields `site_id, configuration_id, month_index, screened_dual, six_hour_dual_under_caps, map_stable, no_other_pressor, no_MCS, strategy_permissible, consentable, randomized_capacity, exclusion_reason`; no protected text leaves the site. Months and hospitals are real prospective operational units, unlike deidentified MIMIC calendar dates.

Before confirmation launch all of the following immutable go/no-go terms must pass:

1. 20-24 hospitals and every participating configuration have passed run-in; both interface families are represented in at least six hospitals.
2. Signed five-year contracts specify monthly first-patient randomization capacities `q_s`; `sum(q_s)>=145/month`, no hospital supplies over 15%, and capacity after removing any one hospital is at least 126.7/month.
3. Across the last six shadow/pilot months, the aggregate randomized-capacity rate is at least 145/month and its exact one-sided 95% Poisson lower confidence bound is at least 130/month. The same calculation after removing the largest hospital must remain at least 126.7/month. A hospital contributes only months after its final configuration passed run-in.
4. The final three months independently meet the same 145/month observed and 130/month lower-bound thresholds, demonstrating sustained rather than historical capacity. Screen failures, refusals, clinical overrides, downtime, and first-patient deduplication remain in denominators.
5. A locked month-by-site schedule shows capacity for at least 8,700 randomizations over 60 months (14.4% buffer over 7,602) and at least 7,602 after applying each site's observed eligibility-to-randomization yield; expansion or substitution is allowed only before the common confirmation start.

These thresholds intentionally exceed the arithmetic minimum 7,602/60=126.7/month. Failing any readiness term is a no-go/inconclusive feasibility result, not permission to reduce N, extend follow-up, pool pilot patients, or relax endpoints. Confirmation then enrolls exactly 7,602 new first randomized patients under one common start and database lock, with no outcome-, component-, arm-, error-, adherence-, or event-dependent stopping and no sample-size re-estimation. Failure to complete by 60 months is inconclusive. This contract is empirically falsifiable and does not pretend that 1,916 historical MIMIC ceiling patients imply multicenter accrual.

The independent pilot remains exactly 1,200 consecutive randomized first patients, 600/arm, excluded from confirmation. In addition to inherited linkage, adjudication, implementation, cancellation, and safety gates, pilot release and confirmation launch now require the component-specific ambiguity and accrual contracts above. Any alteration of N, alpha, components, eligibility, mappings, windows, cluster unit, or configuration mixture requires a new protocol, run-in, pilot, and fixed design.

## Gates and interpretation

**Supportive:** all 7,602 confirmation patients are retained; every feasible joint integer completion has legacy and all seven conditional CP `B_U<0.025`; every component-specific ambiguity budget and witness is reported; all seven and legacy approximate hospital-cluster sensitivities pass; uncensored death/exit, implementation, cancellation, adjudication, clock, configuration concentration, leave-out, and safety gates pass. This supports only measurement of the specified component RDs in the locked enrolled configuration mixture under the stated conditional and sensitivity scopes.

**Adverse:** completed confirmation has legacy or any component conditional `B_U>=0.025` under a feasible completion; any required completed cluster sensitivity fails; rescue-censored death/exit, cancellation, harmful configuration heterogeneity, or direction-specific ambiguity over capacity is adverse measurement/robustness evidence. Separately, poor assigned implementation, crossover/simultaneous stopping, more rescue, more death, earlier death, alive exit patterns, or safety-board concern is adverse clinical/strategy evidence, not measurement error unless EHR and reference disagree. Success in six components never rescues the seventh.

**Inconclusive:** governance or randomization is impermissible; run-in, pump linkage, clocks, intent, MCS ascertainment, feasible-state optimization, effective cluster count, or accrual contract fails; the pilot cannot meet its fixed go/no-go gates; 7,602 are not completed by 60 months; or definitions, population, configurations, windows, cluster unit, death/exit handling, or analysis change. A completed exact-bound failure is adverse, not merely underpowered.

Supportive results do not establish benefit, safety, efficacy, noninferiority, exchangeability of pathway states, or transport to a changed interface/site population. Those require clinical governance, safety review, additional data, and a separately powered randomized effectiveness study. The verifier can check row accounting, eligibility logic, event noncensoring, state/component derivation, integer-compatible completions, exact bound arithmetic, IUT logic, cluster code/output consistency, accrual-contract arithmetic, and whether conclusions match outputs. It cannot establish pump truth from EHR, clinician intent without adjudicators, MCS absence, site independence, safety/equipoise, or treatment choice.
