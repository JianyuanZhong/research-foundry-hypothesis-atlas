# Episode 5 candidate: a strictly time-safe multi-hospital test of pH-conditioned hidden hyperoxemia

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and substantive advance

At a monitor SpO2 of 96–97%, clinicians often infer that arterial oxygen tension is not markedly excessive. Acidemia shifts the oxygen–hemoglobin dissociation curve, but it is unresolved whether this creates a clinically large and reproducible failure of that inference across heterogeneous ICUs.

**Primary falsifiable hypothesis:** among adult EICU observations during probable invasive ventilation in the first 72 ICU hours, with a strictly nonfuture charted FiO2 and preceding released monitor SpO2 median of 96–97%, the acidemic-minus-normal-pH marginal standardized risk difference (RD) for observed PaO2 >120 mm Hg is at least +10 percentage points. Acidemia is pH <7.35 and normal pH is 7.35–7.45. Full support also requires a positive adjusted RD in at least five of the six prespecified exposure-adequate hospitals.

The clinical decision at stake is whether a fixed SpO2 interpretation has a sufficiently important acid-base blind spot to justify a prospective, synchronized study of selective blood-gas reassessment. The advance over the parent lineage is not a new endpoint or a more complex model. It is the first design in the lineage whose 72-hour cohort has been recomputed under the same strict rules that will be executed: FiO2 must both refer to and be entered no later than the blood-gas draw; SpO2 is the exact median over [draw−5, draw]; hospital eligibility does not use PaO2 outcomes; and latest-revision conflicts, discharge bounds, ventilation sentinels, repeated observations, and claim limits are frozen.

This is a contemporaneous measurement/transportability study. Same-panel pH cannot be a deployable warning because PaO2 is already available at that draw. The study does not test oxygen toxicity, tissue hyperoxia, benefit from lowering FiO2, or patient outcomes.

## Strongest supported evidence versus the claim tested

Delgado et al. (Antioxidants 2026, DOI 10.3390/antiox15020235) was acquired and its complete Europe PMC XML was inspected: source [source checksum], [source checksum]. It supports the strongest available empirical claim: in a 2017–2022 single tertiary center, 21,406 adults contributed 717,064 aligned measurements; ABGs were paired with median SpO2 and FiO2 in the preceding five minutes; SpO2 at least 98% often accompanied PaO2 hyperoxemia; 96–97% was generally physiologic; and pH and FiO2 modified the mapping. It does not support a +10-point EICU contrast, US multi-hospital reproducibility, or treatment benefit.

Diop and Mounier (Medical Gas Research 2026, DOI 10.4103/mgr.MEDGASRES-D-25-00028) was inspected from the acquired full HTML: source [source checksum], [source checksum]. It supports the limitation that PaO2 is not oxygen content, cellular oxygen exposure, oxidative injury, or clinical benefit.

Therefore, evidence already supports a single-center contemporaneous association and the physiologic distinction between hyperoxemia and tissue hyperoxia. This experiment tests the unresolved magnitude and limited cross-hospital consistency of that association in EICU. A usable prior-pH rule and the effect of changing oxygen are separate studies.

The research-ambition availability guide was inspected. Its demonstrations set the expected rigor but are not substantive evidence for this hypothesis. In particular, the cancer main article is unavailable and is not claimed as read.

## Variables, estimand, and baselines

Exposure is acidemia versus normal pH. Primary outcome is observed PaO2 >120 mm Hg. Secondary outcomes are PaO2 >150, continuous PaO2, and PaO2 <60 as a safety description. They are arterial gas states, not tissue oxygen injury.

The primary estimand is the observation-weighted marginal standardized RD in PaO2>120 for acidemia versus normal pH among the 618 primary-group observations with median SpO2 96–97. Standardize to each observation's measured SpO2, FiO2, PaCO2/missingness, and six-hour block; set acidemia and its FiO2 interaction jointly to 1 and 0. This is a descriptive conditional measurement contrast, not a causal effect of changing pH.

Use fixed models:

- B0: intercept + I(SpO2=97).
- B1: B0 + linear FiO2 per 10 percentage points + linear PaCO2 per 10 mm Hg (set missing to 40 with an explicit missing indicator) + categorical six-hour block.
- B2: B1 + acidemia + acidemia × linear FiO2.

Do not select terms, transformations, or interactions from outcome performance. A three-band FiO2 specification (21–39, 40–59, 60–100) is a sensitivity only. Age per decade, gender, broad unit type, and admission source are a separately labeled case-mix sensitivity with penalization; they do not replace the sparse primary model. APACHE fields have no event timestamps and are QC only.

## Analysis, repeated observations, and limited transportability

Tabulate exact denominators and outcomes by pH group, integer SpO2, FiO2 band, six-hour block, hospital, stay, and person. Fit B0–B2 as logistic marginal models with GEE exchangeable working correlation clustered by uniquepid. Report coefficients, robust intervals, absolute standardized risks, the B2 RD, and Brier/log-loss calibration summaries. Model comparison is secondary to the prespecified RD.

For the primary 95% interval, resample hospitals 1,000 times with replacement and refit B2. When a hospital is drawn more than once, copy its rows and assign new bootstrap hospital and person-cluster IDs so duplicate draws are independent clusters. Report failed fits and the percentile interval. Also report the ordinary person-clustered sandwich interval. A one-observation-per-person analysis tests sensitivity to repeated measurements.

For each of the six preselected hospitals, fit the reduced fixed model outcome ~ acidemia + I(SpO2=97) + linear FiO2 using Firth or another prespecified bias-reduced logistic estimator solely to handle sparse outcomes/separation, then standardize within that hospital. Every site remains in the denominator; sparse or zero-event sites are reported, never silently dropped. Pool site RDs by random-effects meta-analysis and report tau-squared, I-squared, and a 95% prediction interval. Five of six positive RDs implements the at-least-70% sign criterion. This can establish only limited consistency in six documented sites, not broad US transportability.

Prespecified sensitivities are: one observation per person; first 24 hours; last preceding SpO2 rather than median; 10-minute SpO2 window; FiO2 lookbacks 5, 10, and 60 minutes; exact alternate FiO2 labels 'FIO2 (%)' and 'Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)'; allowing late-entered FiO2 as a documentation-bias contrast; greatest-labid revision selection rather than same-revision conflict exclusion; prior nonzero ventilation intervals; strict closed current intervals as a feasibility boundary; three-band FiO2; case mix; PaO2>150; continuous PaO2; one-at-a-time deletion of each adequate hospital; and observation weights for strict FiO2+SpO2 linkage estimated only from pre-draw patient, hospital, block, and already charted respiratory variables, truncated at the 1st/99th percentiles. Weighting cannot prove missing at random because selection may depend on unobserved physiology.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive for the full hypothesis** requires all of: B2 standardized RD point estimate at least +10 points; two-sided hospital-bootstrap 95% interval excludes 0; at least five of six prespecified hospital RDs are positive; no deletion of one adequate hospital reverses the pooled direction; the random-effects prediction interval does not include an important opposite effect below −5 points; and no primary timing/ventilation sensitivity changes the RD by more than 5 points or reverses its sign. This supports a reproducible concurrent measurement warning and justifies a prospectively synchronized prior-pH/ABG study only.

**Adverse** includes an upper 95% bound below +10 points, which falsifies the prespecified clinically important magnitude even if a smaller positive association remains; an upper bound below 0, which supports an opposite association; or at least four of six hospitals with nonpositive RDs, which is adverse to limited consistency. A precise +6-point RD is adverse to the +10-point hypothesis, not evidence of no association. Failure of B2 to improve Brier score over B1 is adverse only to incremental measurement-model value, not automatically to the physiologic contrast.

**Inconclusive** includes an interval spanning both 0 and +10; more than 10% failed bootstrap fits; the site prediction interval spanning both at most −5 and at least +10 points; a more-than-5-point change or sign reversal under a primary timing/ventilation sensitivity; failure to reproduce the frozen cohort; severe FiO2 nonoverlap between pH groups; or inability to estimate/report all six site contrasts even with the prespecified bias-reduced model. If the pooled criterion is supportive but site criteria fail, conclude 'pooled association supported; transportability inconclusive,' not full support. Negative, adverse, and inconclusive results are scientifically valid outcomes.

## Evidence limits, adjudication, and verifier contract

EICU cannot establish that same-offset lab rows came from one arterial specimen; that the airway remained present; that zero-ended respiratory records mean continuous ventilation until status time; that charted FiO2 was delivered; that sao2 was artifact-free or calibrated comparably across devices/sites; or that selection into ABG and monitor documentation is ignorable. Clinical informatics/device adjudication and prospectively synchronized specimen, airway, delivered-FiO2, waveform-quality, and device metadata are essential for those claims.

A deployable pH-conditioned alert requires a separate study with pH known before a future decision. Recommending an oxygen target or claiming benefit/harm requires expert review and a prospective randomized protocol with hypoxemia, organ injury, and patient-centered outcomes.

The automatic verifier can check source hashes and headers, all joins/ranges/timing inequalities, latest-revision conflicts, discharge and six-hour bounds, nonfuture FiO2 entry, median SpO2, attrition, the six outcome-blind hospital gates, model formulas, interaction-aware standardization, copied cluster IDs in hospital bootstrap, sensitivity execution, intervals, and deterministic interpretation. It must reject correct numbers paired with claims of tissue hyperoxia, oxygen toxicity, treatment benefit, broad national transportability, or deployment. It must accept appropriately limited interpretations of supportive, adverse, partial-support, and inconclusive outputs. It cannot adjudicate specimen identity, ventilation truth, delivered FiO2, device calibration, clinical benefit, or transport beyond documented EICU sites.
