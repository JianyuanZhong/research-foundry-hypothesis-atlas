# Dynamic post-TACE liver-state information versus observation process in locally recorded HCC care

Status: episode-2 evolved design. No clinical or predictive result is claimed as executed. The bounded audits below are feasibility observations; the Harbor solver must fit the prespecified models and report held-out outputs.

## Scientific opening, hypothesis, and clinical importance

The HCC snapshot contains repeated procedures, laboratory measurements, examinations and encounters, but it does not contain adjudicated tumor response, treatment intent, outside-hospital care, death, or validated Child-Pugh/mRECIST labels. Existing evidence supports that AFP and other routine variables can be prognostic after TACE [K1], and that HCC response depends on viable enhancement/necrosis rather than size alone [K2]. Surveillance signals can also be asynchronous and heterogeneous, so serum or imaging records should not be treated as standalone treatment triggers [K3]. These observations do not establish whether an early laboratory trajectory in this local record represents patient state beyond baseline severity, or simply who was measured and brought back for another procedure.

Primary falsifiable hypothesis: among patients with a first locally recorded, exact-coded TACE, an assay-exact laboratory trajectory observed 7–42 days after index TACE is associated with a second exact-coded TACE during days 43–180 after index, after baseline severity and treatment history are accounted for; the association should persist after explicit adjustment for observation intensity. The leading rival is a care-process explanation: post-index testing and repeat intervention opportunities generate the apparent signal, so state-only information will not add held-out information beyond baseline/process variables, or process-preserving permutations will reproduce it.

This is a prognostic/descriptive test, not a causal claim that a laboratory change causes repeat TACE, indicates radiologic progression, or identifies a treatment effect. Repeat TACE is an operational endpoint that may encode planned retreatment, residual disease, clinician preference, access and follow-up opportunity. A positive result could justify an external, adjudicated study of risk-adapted review; a process-dominated result would caution against using this trajectory as a treatment trigger and would redirect effort toward measurement/workflow quality.

The substantive advance over the closest inspected TACE biomarker work is the direct state-versus-observation comparison, strict time ordering, process ablations and held-out confirmation. The learned alternative is not included for a small AUC contest: it tests whether event order, time gaps and transient trajectories carry information that summary features lose.

## Population, temporal boundaries and endpoint

Use only the HCC snapshot `[source checksum]). The primary index is the earliest row per patient in `procedures` whose normalized `手术` is exactly case-folded `tace` after trimming and removing only whitespace. This strict rule was audited in the source: 1,761 matching rows, 1,404 with parseable `开始时间`, and 1,169 patients; there was one same-patient/same-start-time normalized-label duplicate. Deduplicate only that exact duplicate deterministically and report the audit. Do not replace the primary rule with broad substring matching: broad TACE/化疗栓塞 matching produced 78,067 rows in the bounded audit and mixes composite and nonidentical procedures. Report broad matching only as an exploratory sensitivity.

Require nonmissing `患者主索引`, `就诊号` and `开始时间`, and inner-join the index row to `encounters` on the documented composite (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`). Exclude any patient with an earlier strict-TACE row in the available local history; call this “first locally recorded,” never first-ever TACE. Record ties and the available-history boundary.

Index time is `procedures.开始时间`. Baseline is -365 days through index. Dynamic response window is days 7–42 inclusive. The outcome window is days 43–180 inclusive. No observation after day 42 may enter a predictor. Primary outcome is a second strict-TACE procedure in the outcome window, linked by patient key and `procedures.开始时间`. A secondary operational endpoint is any later procedure in a pre-registered list of TACE, ablation and resection terms; it must not be called progression or recurrence.

Define follow-up status before model fitting. A patient with a repeat TACE is Y=1 at its first qualifying time. Y=0 is allowed only if the last parseable observation across the prespecified observation sources reaches index+180 days; otherwise the patient is censored/unknown, not a non-event. The observation sources are `encounters.就诊时间`, `encounters.入院时间`, `encounters.出院时间`, `procedures.开始时间`, `procedures.结束时间`, `examinations.开始时间` and `labs.检验时间`. Procedure end time is an availability signal, not an index or endpoint time. The encounter-only feasibility audit found 385/1,169 strict-index patients reaching 180 days; this is a lower bound, not the final all-source count. Primary reporting must include a complete-window analysis and an all-index time-to-event/censoring sensitivity, with the final eligible counts and event count as solver outputs.

## Methods and scientific deliverable

The actual deliverable is newly fitted patient-level out-of-fold and untouched-test predictions, model coefficients/attributions limited to prespecified features, calibration and uncertainty summaries, and permutation/negative-control outputs. A proposal alone is not completion.

Fit a transparent baseline on the same estimand. M0 is regularized logistic regression (and, if event count permits, discrete-time pooled logistic hazard) using only pre-index state/severity and history: age/sex, prior encounters/procedures, diagnosis-dictionary counts, department/admission duration, assay-exact baseline summaries and pre-index medication context. M1 adds only response-window observation/process summaries: encounters and observed days, assay-specific lab counts and time gaps, examination counts/types, orders, medications and missingness indicators. Scale and impute within training folds, use missingness indicators, and do not use post-index repeat-procedure rows as predictors.

Fit a compact time-aware GRU alternative on the same cohort and target. Each event token in -365 through +42 days includes event type/category, time since prior event and index, and numeric value only for assay-exact representations. Maintain state and process channels. Prespecified ablations are state-only, process-only, combined, and combined with within-patient time permutation or cross-patient process-mark permutation preserving counts. A time-shuffled baseline-feature control is required. The GRU is selected because the scientific alternative is event ordering/transient trajectory information, not generic complexity; its result must be interpreted only against M0/M1 and the ablations.

Use a fixed patient-level 80/20 split; select hyperparameters in the 80% training portion with grouped five-fold validation, never tune on the test portion. Use three fixed seeds for the learned fit. Report Brier score, held-out log loss, calibration intercept/slope and plots, AUROC/AUPRC with bootstrap 95% intervals, and paired patient-bootstrap differences. If a time-to-event analysis is used, report censoring assumptions and time-dependent calibration. Primary scientific comparison is incremental held-out log loss/calibration and whether state-only remains informative after M1, not a small AUC gain.

Supportive result requires enough assay-exact paired observations and events for precision; state-only/combined must improve held-out calibration or log loss over M0, the increment must remain similar after M1, and process-only/permuted models must not reproduce it across complete-window and observation-complete sensitivities. This supports incremental prognostic information in recorded state, not biology, radiologic response or causal treatment benefit.

Adverse result is M0 comparable to state models, the increment disappearing after M1, or process-only/permuted timing reproducing it. This supports baseline severity or observation/workflow explanation and argues against response-triggered management.

Inconclusive result is fewer than 50 repeat events, unstable alias mapping, insufficient paired observations, wide intervals, severe censoring, or nontransportable local coding. This does not refute a biological claim; it specifies the need for unit/reference-range metadata, adjudicated TACE intent and response, validated imaging labels, death/outside-care linkage, or another institution.

## Alternative-method decision and demonstration dispositions

The simple baseline is scientifically adequate for estimating whether a prespecified state increment survives explicit process adjustment and is transparent enough to audit. The GRU adds event order, time gaps and transient trajectories that M0/M1 compress; process ablations make informative observation testable. A multimodal image/text transformer was deferred because this snapshot has no image payload and documents lack a temporal field. A mechanistic joint tumor/liver state-space model was deferred because tumor burden, treatment intent, units/reference ranges and adjudicated response are unavailable; adding complexity would replace the unresolved question with untestable assumptions. Revisit those alternatives only if those dependencies are supplied.

The natural-history demonstration informed the bounded dated-sequence alternative, but this is an HCC adaptation, not reproduction; its external Danish validation is unavailable here. The cancer demonstration’s image modality and complete main-paper/STAR-Methods text are unavailable, so only the local lab/process adaptation is retained. The Bayesian demonstration’s genetic inputs and exact reproduction dependencies are not required for this EHR-only question; its latent mechanistic adaptation is deferred. These dispositions are scientific/dependency decisions, not blanket restrictions on learned models or GPUs.

## Compute, falsification, and clinical boundary

Discovery used schema inspection, a procedure-row audit, and a managed full lab label scan; no model was trained and no hypothesis result was computed. Future solver planning envelope is 16 CPUs, 8 GPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds. Materialize cohort/features with 8–16 CPUs and 64–128 GiB; fit M0/M1 and bootstrap on CPU; fit the compact GRU on one allocated A100 with explicit `cuda:0`, three seeds and checkpoints. These estimates are unverified on the mounted files. If the GRU exceeds the envelope, complete M0/M1 and negative controls, preserve cohort outputs, and label the learned comparison incomplete rather than changing the population or estimand.

No automatic verifier can establish recurrence, treatment intent, radiologic response, mechanism, causal treatment benefit, death, outside-care completeness, clinical units, or whether a repeat procedure was indicated. It can check source bindings, time leakage, cohort construction, model fitting, held-out metrics, uncertainty calculations and whether conclusions match supportive/adverse/inconclusive outputs. Expert adjudication or another study is required for the stronger clinical claims.
