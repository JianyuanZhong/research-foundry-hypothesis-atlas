## Scientific question and substantive advance

The parent established a useful direction-free representation of change between two biomarker panels, but its main comparison can still attribute repeat-selection information to movement: the baseline contains current values and repeat spacing, while selection is handled mainly as a sensitivity weighting. The successor makes the separation explicit in the estimand.

The unresolved question is:

> Among UK Biobank participants who have a valid repeat panel, does the magnitude and cross-field pattern of movement between the prior and current eight-field panels improve five-year prioritization for a subsequently recorded circulatory diagnosis, beyond current biomarker levels, prior levels, repeat spacing, calendar timing, and an observed repeat-panel selection model?

This matters because a repeat panel is actionable only if change identifies people for review beyond what is already known from the current panel and from who was selected for repeat testing. A positive result would support prospective evaluation of a movement-aware review policy; it would not show that retesting causes benefit, that a biomarker is biologically worsening, or that the policy improves patient outcomes.

The substantive change from the parent is the primary contrast. The parent’s direction-free B/H increment is retained as a representation, but the primary decision comparison is now movement-plus-prior versus prior-only under the same fixed review capacity and with selection-adjusted sensitivity. The prior-only model is a necessary counterfactual: without it, movement can simply re-encode prior baseline risk. The design also reports a second, clinically interpretable five-year absolute-risk increment rather than treating ranking gain alone as evidence of clinical value.

The experiment tests, rather than assumes:

1. whether movement adds held-out fixed-capacity capture and yield beyond current and prior levels;
2. whether that increment survives adjustment for repeat spacing, assessment calendar timing, and observed repeat-panel selection;
3. whether the signal is additive and stable or depends on nonlinear thresholds/interactions;
4. whether the result transports from an early fitting era to a no-refit 2013 application era.

No fitted estimate, event count, causal effect, prospective utility, or external-validation claim is made in this proposal.

## Movement representation and observation adjustment

Within every early training fold, fit an empirical percentile map F_j to eligible training-fold T0 values only. Do not use outcomes, held-out values, or late values. Apply the map unchanged to current and prior values in the held-out fold and to the late era. Clip outside-range values to training endpoints and report clipping.

For each field j, define direction-free movement:

u_ij = |F_j(x_i,T1) - F_j(x_i,T0)|.

Set B_i=mean_j(u_ij), the overall movement burden, and H_i=SD_j(u_ij), the discordance of movement magnitudes. These are unit-scaled representations of movement, not diagnoses. A secondary model may use all eight u_ij values, without assigning analyte names, biological directions, or mechanisms.

The selection probability p_i=P(S=1 | T0 panel, age, sex, T0 calendar year) is fitted in early training folds using a prespecified regularized logistic model and applied unchanged to held-out early participants and to late participants. Use stabilized weights w_i=P(S=1)/p_i, truncated only at the early 1st and 99th percentiles; report both unweighted and weighted results. This weighting addresses measured repeat availability, not unmeasured clinical need, healthcare utilization, treatment, specimen handling, or access. Do not include T1 values or movement in the selection model.

## Split and nested model comparisons

Create one deterministic participant-level five-fold hash split on eid in the early era. Every imputation, range rule, percentile map, propensity model, penalty, booster setting, calibration, and risk cutpoint is training-fold-only. Late data are an application set with no refitting.

The primary decision population is the current-risk ambiguous band A, frozen from early out-of-fold predictions of the current-only baseline. Define:

- M0: current eight-field panel at T1 + age + sex + T1 calendar year;
- M1s: M0 + L + T0 calendar year + the fold-local logit selection propensity (observation metadata);
- M1p: M1s + the eight prior T0 biomarker levels;
- M1m: M1p + B + H (primary movement model);
- M1u: M1p + all eight u_ij (secondary movement-pattern model).

M0 is the direct current-level comparator. M1s tests repeat spacing and observed observation-process metadata. M1p prevents movement from being credited for information carried by prior absolute levels. M1m is the primary transparent movement contrast. All M1 models are regularized multinomial T/D/N models with a training-only penalty grid.

Define A using early out-of-fold M1s target probabilities, with q20 and q80: A is q20 <= r < q80. Apply the early q20/q80 cutpoints unchanged in 2013. This ensures the decision band is defined without movement or late outcomes. Before inspecting outcomes, set K=floor(0.10*n_A,early). Require K>=40, n_A,late>=K, at least 100 T events overall, finite weights, and adequate weighted overlap/ESS. Use exactly K top held-out target-risk ranks within A, with deterministic eid-hash tie breaks, for every compared model and era.

For the substantive learned alternative use matched shallow gradient-boosted multinomial T/D/N models:

- M2s: the same booster family/grid and inputs as M1s;
- M2p: the same family/grid and inputs as M1p;
- M2m: the same family/grid and inputs as M1m;
- M2u: the same family/grid and inputs as M1u.

The only difference within each pair is model family versus transparent additive structure; the same participants, split, target, frozen A, K, weights, and evaluation are mandatory. M2m can reveal movement thresholds, saturation, and interactions among B/H and current/prior levels that M1m averages away. A nonlinear gain without a matched additive gain is evidence about functional form, not evidence of biological worsening or mechanism. GPU use is not assumed or rewarded; this is a small tabular fit and is CPU-suitable.

The parent’s direction-free fixed-K contrast remains a continuity analysis: M1m versus M1s. The new primary contrast is M1m versus M1p, with M2m versus M2p as the matched nonlinear alternative. This separation directly tests whether movement adds information beyond both current and prior levels and the observed repeat-selection process.

## Estimands, uncertainty, and decision criteria

For model M and era e, let B_Mi(K) indicate membership in the K highest held-out target-risk predictions within A. Report both unweighted and selection-weighted estimates:

C_A(M,e) = sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=T) B_Mi(K) / sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=T)

P_A(M,e) = sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=T) B_Mi(K) / sum_i w_i B_Mi(K).

The primary decision estimands are:

- DeltaC_m(e)=C_A(M1m,e)-C_A(M1p,e), the incremental capture of recorded target events at fixed capacity;
- DeltaP_m(e)=P_A(M1m,e)-P_A(M1p,e), the incremental target yield among reviewed people;
- tauC_m=DeltaC_m(late)-DeltaC_m(early), the temporal transport contrast.

The prespecified operational support criterion is a positive late weighted DeltaC_m > 0.05 with a two-sided 95% interval whose lower limit exceeds 0.05, no early-to-late sign reversal, and a late weighted DeltaP_m in the same direction. The five-percentage-point margin is a design margin, not a clinical-benefit threshold. Also report the parent-continuity M1m-M1s contrast and the movement-pattern M1u-M1p contrast.

As a secondary clinical-risk estimand, report the weighted standardized five-year target-risk increment:

RDelta = mean_w[p_M1m(T) - p_M1p(T)]

over the fixed A population, together with competing death risk, multiclass Brier score, log loss, calibration intercept/slope, reliability, resolution, and observed/predicted T/D/N frequencies. Do not convert RDelta into a treatment recommendation. The secondary learned contrast is M2m-M2p on the same risk and decision measures.

Use paired participant bootstrap intervals with 2,000 replicates, resampling within fixed era-by-A strata while retaining frozen split, A, K, predictions, weights, and selection lists. Report failed-support replicates and use a declared simultaneous interval or multiplicity adjustment for the primary and key secondary contrasts. A separately labelled full-pipeline bootstrap may refit fold processing; it cannot replace the primary held-out analysis.

## Falsification and integrity criteria

Retain source hashes, schema hashes, headers, row counts, unique-eid checks, join coverage/disagreements, all 259 endpoint-pair parsing results, death reconciliation, semantic/timing certificates, fold manifest, propensity overlap/ESS, percentile maps, clipping, q20/q80, A counts, K, predictions, list membership, bootstrap failures, and all null outputs.

Run these preregistered falsification checks:

- permute T/D/N labels within fold and era; systematic movement gains invalidate outcome handling;
- permute B/H within frozen A strata while preserving current levels, L, propensity, missingness, and K; a retained primary gain falsifies movement attribution;
- permute each prior panel across participants before constructing u, with current panel unchanged; a retained gain indicates leakage or a baseline confound;
- replace prior with current values so u=0; any residual movement increment is an implementation failure;
- permute the early/late era labels and repeat the transport calculation;
- compare against deterministic random-K lists and verify that they do not systematically outperform the declared model;
- deliberately break same-position code/date pairing and require the integrity gate to fail;
- compare complete-case and fold-local missingness policies, with the primary policy fixed before outcomes are viewed;
- verify that all transforms, selection models, risk-band cutpoints, and hyperparameters are fit only inside the permitted training data.

A movement gain that disappears under prior permutation, B/H permutation, or selection weighting is adverse to the movement-specific claim. A gain surviving these nulls, or a gain from endpoint/era permutations, invalidates the implementation.

## Interpretation

Supportive evidence requires valid joins and source hashes; certified field identity, units/ranges, missingness and comparable timing; same-position endpoint integrity; complete five-year ascertainment; feasible K; adequate overlap and ESS; calibrated held-out predictions; a positive late weighted M1m-M1p capture increment above the operational margin with the complete interval above it; concordant yield and risk direction; no early-to-late reversal; stable folds; and null permutations that remove the signal. This supports only a conditional association: in the supported repeat-observed UKB population and current-risk band, the recorded movement representation improved prioritization of the specified recorded target family beyond the declared current/prior/timing/selection information.

Adverse evidence includes no increment, a gain explained by prior levels, repeat spacing, or selection weighting, disappearance in movement nulls, poor calibration, fold instability, temporal reversal, random-policy superiority, or invalid endpoint/ascertainment controls. This weakens the case for movement-aware prioritization for this endpoint and population; it does not prove that no biomarker movement is useful elsewhere.

Inconclusive evidence includes missing field dictionaries, incompatible specimen timing, malformed or independently sorted arrays, death disagreement, incomplete five-year follow-up, sparse events, infeasible K, poor overlap or ESS, broad intervals, unstable clipping/percentiles, conflicting missingness or weighting sensitivities, or inability to distinguish movement from regression to the mean, analytical variation, treatment, assay drift, or unmeasured clinical testing selection.

Computationally checkable claims are source/schema integrity, joins, filtering flow, date arithmetic, endpoint construction and event ordering, movement construction, selection model, fits, held-out predictions, frozen A/K, capture/yield arithmetic, calibration, bootstrap uncertainty, and null behavior. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for specimen validity, actual disease onset, outpatient disease, symptom/severity context, treatment effects, action thresholds, prospective benefit, causal effects, and external validity. The arrays do not identify a biological turning point. The late era is internal temporal application, not external validation. An automatic verifier cannot establish the unavailable clinical claims.
