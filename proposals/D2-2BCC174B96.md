# MerTK-dependent macrophage control of the pathogenic FLS effector program (episode 3)

Domain: therapeutic_targets (D2). Rubric: life-science-stage-aware-v1.
Seed: D2-T2 (repaired, direction made falsifiable). Parents: [prior hypothesis], [prior hypothesis] (both repairable).

## Unresolved question
Does engagement of the efferocytic receptor MerTK on synovial macrophages (the MerTK/CD206+
subset) actively suppress a pathogenic fibroblast-like synoviocyte (FLS) effector program, such
that pharmacological MerTK inhibition *de-represses* matrix-degrading / inflammatory FLS genes?

The prior seed assumed MerTK+ macrophages are "protective" as a premise. The testable claim is
directional and falsifiable: if MerTK signalling is the brake, blocking it in a matched
macrophage-FLS coculture should increase FLS effector output; if instead the MerTK+ state is a
marker rather than a causal brake, blocking it will not increase effector output, or the effect
will vanish when FLS donor identity is accounted for.

## Clinical importance
An estimated third of RA patients fail to sustain remission on current DMARDs. If MerTK agonism
(rather than inhibition) is the direction that restores a failing resolution program, then
MerTK-inhibitor oncology candidates repurposed toward inflammatory disease would be
counter-productive, and resolution-restoring (efferocytosis-promoting) strategies would be
prioritised. This is a consequential target-direction decision.

## Estimand
Within-donor paired mean difference in a prespecified FLS effector score
(matrix-degrading + inflammatory genes measurable in the 445-feature panel), comparing
MerTK/CD206pos coculture vs MerTK/CD206neg coculture, and MerTK-inhibitor vs no-inhibitor within
matched macrophage condition. Directional a-priori prediction: effector score is LOWER with
MerTK/CD206pos macrophages; MerTK inhibition raises it.

## Baselines / alternatives (method exploration)
- Baseline 1: pseudobulk paired donor test on the effector score (donor-aware, no donor t-test
  pooling - E-MTAB-8873 requires contributing-donor dependence modelling).
- Baseline 2: per-gene moderated paired test on the measured panel.
- Substantive alternative: a donor-condition linear mixed model / regularised regression that
  jointly estimates macrophage condition, MerTK-inhibitor, and a donor random effect, so that an
  apparent MerTK effect from FLS-donor composition is separated from a within-donor perturbation
  effect. This is the key discriminator between "MerTK engagement is a causal brake" and a
  "donor/technical composition artefact" rival. It is a small tabular/linear fit, CPU-only.

## Falsification
- Supportive: pos<neg effector score with CI excluding zero AND inhibitor raises it within donor.
- Adverse (refutes causal-brake claim): no within-donor inhibitor effect, or inhibitor LOWERs
  effector score, or direction reverses after donor adjustment.
- Inconclusive: effect present but driven by one donor string / n=1 cells, or CI straddles zero.

## Honest limits
n=1 sample for neg+inhibitor; mixed donor strings require dependence modelling; targeted panel
does not measure all effector genes; no wet-lab causality. Cross-sectional E-MTAB-8322 cannot
establish transitions. These bound the claim to "directional, hypothesis-generating in-vitro
evidence from a matched coculture perturbation", not a clinical efficacy claim.
