# Proposal: observation-aware post-TACE trajectories with time-aligned examination sensitivity

## Successor objective and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s HCC dataset island, one index per patient, day-45 landmark, and prognostic (not causal) estimand, while repairing the central ambiguity: a repeat TACE-like procedure may reflect residual disease and treatment escalation, or it may preferentially occur among patients who are tested, reachable, eligible, or still receiving care at this institution.

The actual deliverable is a newly fitted, locked-test comparison of three transparent nested baselines and one observation-aware longitudinal model, plus a separately audited examination-proxy sensitivity:

1. (B_0): pre-index clinical snapshot and recorded treatment history.
2. (B_1): (B_0) plus the prespecified AFP/hepatic-function trajectory phenotype.
3. (B_2): (B_1) plus explicit post-index testing/access proxies, without report text.
4. (M_3): a joint latent-trajectory/observation-process/discrete-time hazard model fitted to the same target.
5. (E): an examination timing and report-language sensitivity, never treated as validated radiologic adjudication.

Completion requires fitted parameters and patient-level locked-test predictions for all eligible models, uncertainty intervals, cohort and missingness flow, observation-process diagnostics, the report-proxy audit, and a machine-readable manifest proving that no post-landmark or post-outcome information entered prediction.

## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the inspected evidence and local files is limited: AFP and hepatic-function tests are clinically available but heterogeneous; the HCC source contains dated examinations with free-text findings/diagnoses; and repeat TACE-like procedures are recorded clinical actions. The evidence does not establish that an AFP fall or hepatic recovery identifies true residual tumor response, nor that a repeat TACE means recurrence or progression. The methods-and-compute guide supports bounded adaptation of longitudinal learned/latent-state models, but does not provide a clinical validation of this HCC question. The inaccessible main article associated with the cancer demonstration is not used as evidence or claimed to have been read; only the local reference descriptions are used to motivate method comparison.

Primary hypothesis, frozen before fitting:

> Among adults with a structured HCC diagnosis and a first recorded TACE-like procedure, early AFP improvement with non-deteriorating hepatic markers is associated with lower odds of a subsequently recorded TACE-like procedure from days 46–365. This association will remain materially present after adjustment for measured post-index testing and examination intensity, and it will be directionally concordant with time-aligned examination evidence when that evidence is available.

The estimand remains an adjusted prognostic association with a recorded action. The study does not estimate treatment effects, recurrence, survival, radiologic response, or utility of an escalation policy.

The observation-process repair is itself falsifiable:

- If the phenotype association is stable after adding testing/access proxies and an observation-aware model, and a blinded examination-proxy sample is directionally concordant, the data support a local prognostic signal that is less compatible with a purely measured-observation artifact.
- If the association attenuates substantially after process adjustment, is confined to patients with early examinations/labs, or is not concordant with the examination proxy, the residual-disease interpretation is weakened and observation/eligibility selection becomes a more plausible explanation.
- If examination reports are too sparse, lexically ambiguous, or clinically discordant, the correct result is an unresolved proxy layer, not clinical validation.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Use one index per patient: the earliest recorded procedure episode satisfying the TACE-like rule, with the procedure start time as index time. Collapse same-patient procedure rows within 24 hours into one episode while retaining all raw names and row counts. Require:

- age >=18 and nonmissing sex at the index encounter;
- a diagnosis name containing the literal `hepatocellular carcinoma` on an encounter from 180 days before through 7 days after index;
- a usable procedure start time, joined to the encounter by (`patient master index`, `encounter number`);
- index date on or before 2025-01-01, so a nominal 365-day outcome window ends within the local 2026-01-01 snapshot.

The prespecified TACE-like name rule is a procedure name matching `TACE`, `transarterial chemoembolization`, or both `hepatic artery` and `embolization`, case-insensitive for the Latin token. A literal-name sensitivity uses only names containing `TACE` or `transarterial chemoembolization`. Retain the original name, procedure source, and episode construction.

The landmark is day 45 after index. No lab, encounter, examination, document, pathology, medication, order, or procedure record with time after day 45 may enter a prediction feature. The primary outcome is the first later TACE-like episode with 45 < days <=365. A secondary recorded-action outcome is the first later liver-directed procedure with a name containing `resection`, `ablation`, `radiofrequency`, `microwave`, or the TACE rule. This remains an action endpoint, not a disease endpoint. Report first-event timing and binary 46–365-day results; do not silently treat undocumented death or outside treatment as absence of disease.

The primary biomarker population requires at least one parseable numeric AFP in both the pre-window (days -30 to -1) and early post-window (days 7 to 45). Use the last pre-window and first early-post value, retaining qualitative inequality flags. The all-eligible population is a separate missingness analysis; absent testing is not coded as biologic response. The parent’s bounded profile is approximately 1,456 complete paired-AFP patients; the solver must recompute and report exact counts under this frozen successor filter.

## Features and observation-process controls

All features are generated relative to index time and are frozen at day 45.

### Transparent nested baselines

(B_0) contains age, sex, index year, index procedure-name family, prior 180-day counts/indicators for structured diagnoses containing `cirrhosis`, `portal vein`, `intrahepatic metastasis`, `lung metastasis`, `bone metastasis`, and `ascites`, prior liver-directed procedure counts, and the last pre-index-window value plus missingness for each of the five assays.

(B_1) adds the parent’s prespecified early-response phenotype: at least a 50% fall in log1p AFP from the last pre-window value to the first value in days 7–45, with no deterioration in at least two of albumin, total bilirubin, INR, and platelet count over the corresponding windows. Also retain continuous assay-specific changes and missingness for model adjustment; do not infer units.

(B_2) adds only measurable observation-process variables, all known by day 45: number of distinct encounter dates in days 7–45; count of lab rows and distinct lab dates; indicators and days-to-first measurement for AFP and each hepatic assay; count and days-to-first relevant examination; relevant-examination modality/name-family indicators; and whether the paired-AFP inclusion requirement was met. It does not use report text. A logistic model for being biomarker-complete, trained on the fitting split only, provides an inverse-probability-weighted sensitivity with overlap checks and 1st/99th percentile weight truncation. Observation variables are not described as access, adherence, or clinical appropriateness; they are local record-capture proxies.

Fit regularized logistic regression for the 46–365-day binary repeat-TACE outcome, with predeclared penalty selected on the 2023 tuning split only. Report adjusted phenotype odds ratios with robust or patient-bootstrap intervals, and AUROC, PR-AUC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, and mean log score on the locked temporal test split. Use the same feature family for the secondary liver-directed-action outcome.

### Substantive alternative: joint state and observation model

(M_3) is a prespecified joint longitudinal model, not a cosmetic classifier. It has two patient-level latent proxy states over days -30 to 45:

- a tumor-activity proxy informed by log1p AFP and its irregular timing/censoring flags;
- a hepatic-function proxy informed jointly by albumin, bilirubin, INR, and platelets, with assay-specific standardized values and no cross-assay unit assumptions.

Use random intercepts and slopes or a low-rank state-space representation for irregular measurements. Jointly fit an observation submodel for whether each assay and relevant examination is recorded on each observed encounter/day, conditional on prior measurements, baseline covariates, and latent state. Link the day-46 onward discrete-time hazard of a first recorded repeat TACE to the posterior day-45 state levels, slopes, uncertainty, missingness/observation intensity, baseline history, and index treatment family. Fit only on training patients; choose regularization/epochs/optimization settings on the 2023 tuning split. Do not use future observations to smooth the day-45 state.

The alternative could reveal whether timing and direction of asynchronous recovery, and the distinction between a latent biomarker pattern and its probability of being measured, carry information that a last-value/snapshot model loses. It also tests whether the apparent AFP signal changes when hepatic deterioration and examination intensity are modeled jointly. It cannot recover true tumor burden, true hepatic reserve, causal treatment response, or unmeasured access.

The raw examination report text is not part of the primary (M_3) model. In sensitivity (E), add only predeclared report-language flags from relevant examinations in days 7–45 after stripping HTML tags/entities deterministically:

- `concern_language`: any of `residual`, `recurrence`, `progression`, `new onset`, `enlargement`, `increase`, `activity`, `enhancement`, `metastasis`, `deterioration`, `no obvious reduction`;
- `response_or_stable_language`: any of `shrinkage`, `decrease`, `resolution`, `disappearance`, `stable`, `no obvious enhancement observed`, `no obvious enhancement`, `necrosis`, `lipiodol deposition`, `after treatment`;
- `explicit_negative_language`: any of `not seen`, `not found`, `not suggestive of`, `no obvious`, `none`, `denies`.

These are high-recall lexical flags, not diagnoses. The solver must report raw flag rates, co-occurrence, report-name mix, and duplicate counts. A flag is not called “residual disease” and no negation/context correction may be claimed without clinician review. A report occurring after the first repeat TACE is excluded from all prediction and proxy analyses.

For an optional clinician-adjudicated proxy, draw a fixed, stratified sample of relevant patient-level reports from the locked test set before revealing outcome or later procedures, stratified by the three lexical categories and report modality. Two clinicians independently label: residual/viable-disease concern, response/stable appearance, indeterminate/not assessable, or non-target examination. Resolve disagreements by a third reviewer; report agreement and the adjudicated association with repeat TACE. This is a validation of the proxy in a sample, not validation of the complete cohort. If clinicians or a time-valid review process are unavailable, omit this claim and retain only the computational lexical sensitivity.

## Leakage, splitting, and uncertainty

Use a temporal patient-level split with complete 365-day ascertainment: index years 2010–2022 for fitting, 2023 for tuning and threshold selection, and 2024 through 2025-01-01 for the locked test. The solver must confirm no patient crosses splits and publish split-membership hashes. A grouped random split is a stability sensitivity only.

All parsing rules, assay labels, thresholds, lexical rules, standardization medians/IQRs, imputation, feature selection, penalty, latent-model settings, and missingness models are learned or fixed without locked-test outcomes. No row after day 45 enters feature construction. For the binary outcome, patients without a recorded event are followed through 365 days in the complete-ascertainment cohort; report incomplete-follow-up exclusions explicitly. For the discrete hazard, use only risk intervals with known local ascertainment and do not impute death.

Use patient bootstrap intervals stratified by split and outcome where feasible; otherwise robust sandwich uncertainty is acceptable and must be declared. The key incremental comparison is (B_2) versus (M_3), not a contest for a small predictive gain: report paired locked-test log-score difference and calibration change with bootstrap intervals. A prespecified negligible margin is 0.02 mean log-score units; this is an operational model-comparison margin, not a clinical utility threshold.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence requires:

1. The phenotype’s adjusted OR is at most 0.80 with a 95% interval entirely below 1.0 in the primary paired-AFP analysis, remains directionally consistent under literal TACE naming and 90-/180-day outcome windows, and is not attenuated by 30% or more after adding (B_2) observation variables.
2. (M_3) improves locked-test mean log score over (B_2) by at least 0.02 with a bootstrap interval excluding zero, without worse calibration slope/intercept beyond prespecified uncertainty, and the improvement is not confined to a single report/testing stratum.
3. The examination timing/process association and the lexical sensitivity are directionally concordant with the phenotype; if an adjudicated sample is performed, lexical agreement and adjudicated direction must be reported rather than assumed.

Adverse evidence is a null or reversed phenotype association, >=30% attenuation after observation adjustment, a strong phenotype-by-testing interaction showing signal only among measured patients, instability across naming/window/missingness rules, deterioration in calibration, or an (M_3) gain that disappears when temporal leakage and measurement intensity are handled. A lexical flag that is mostly a report-template or modality marker, has poor clinician agreement, or fails to associate with the action endpoint is adverse evidence against using it as a residual-disease proxy.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few paired AFP cases/events in the locked test, insufficient overlap for inverse-probability weighting, wide intervals spanning the prespecified margin, sparse or non-dateable examinations, high censoring/unknown units, failed latent-model convergence, or lack of independent clinician review. Inconclusive means the question remains unresolved; it is not confirmation.

Even supportive results establish only a local association with a recorded treatment action and possibly a convergent, imperfect report proxy. They do not establish recurrence, residual viable tumor, treatment appropriateness, survival benefit, causal effect, or transportability. Radiologic response, Barcelona stage, treatment intent, death, complete outside-care capture, true hepatic decompensation, and clinical utility require adjudicated imaging/pathology, mortality/claims linkage, expert review, prospective follow-up, or external validation.

## Alternatives considered and deferral record

The baseline and (M_3) are scientifically comparable because both target the same day-46-to-365 recorded repeat-TACE question, use the same landmark and patient split, and are evaluated by the same uncertainty and calibration outputs. (B_0) is retained to distinguish clinical history from trajectory information; (B_1) tests the parent claim; (B_2) tests observation-process explanation; (M_3) tests whether latent asynchronous state plus observation intensity adds information.

A learned event-sequence transformer was considered. It could represent the ordering of labs, examinations, procedures, and encounters and might reveal sequence motifs that the state model compresses. It is deferred because the complete paired-AFP population is approximately 1,456, report text is unvalidated, the principal outcome is a clinician action, no external HCC cohort is available, and no independent radiology label is available. This is a data/validation-based deferral, not a neural-network or GPU ban. Revisit it only if a larger validated outcome set, reliable timestamps, or an external test cohort becomes available. A GPU is not scientifically required for the proposed tabular/state model; a future solver may use CPU resources, with any GPU use justified by measured runtime rather than rewarded.

The discovery scan over the 1.14-GB examination source took approximately 23 seconds in the local shell; this is measured preprocessing feasibility, not a model-training estimate. The future solver planning envelope is 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB memory, up to 8 allocated GPUs, and 28,800 seconds. The (B_0)–(B_2) fits and a low-rank (M_3) are expected to fit within that envelope, but convergence and exact runtime are unverified and must be recorded from the actual run. No proposer/solver weight training is authorized or needed.
