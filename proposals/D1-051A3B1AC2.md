# Episode 48 (MIMIC-IV island) — EVOLUTION of board leader `[prior hypothesis]`
Antimicrobial de-escalation after culture results (expert seed `[starting question]`).
Status: Lead design + bindings + reference receipts. NO cohort rows, models or estimands computed this episode.

## Question and clinical importance
When culture/susceptibility results return in a critically ill patient on empiric broad-spectrum
antimicrobials, is **early de-escalation** (narrowing coverage) safe *on average*, and is any apparent
safety an artifact that hides harm in patients who are **not yet improving**? This is a guideline-level ICU
decision: continuing broad coverage drives resistance/toxicity, but premature narrowing in a still-
deteriorating patient could delay adequate therapy.

## What this child adds over the frozen parent (the substantive advance)
The parent (ep-45) already contrasted the leading claim H1 with the confounding-by-indication rival H2.
Its own assessment left two objects unresolved: (a) **prevalence/power unobserved**, and (b) `storetime`
used as the proxy for "results became available". This child closes both *by design* and adds a third,
previously unaddressed rival:

- **H1 (leading):** early de-escalation is not harmful overall and remains non-harmful in non-improving patients.
- **H2 (confounding by indication / care-trajectory):** average "benefit" is selection of recovering
  patients into de-escalation; in non-improving patients de-escalation may be harmful.
- **H3 (NEW — timing/ascertainment artifact):** the apparent safety is produced by *when* the culture
  result was recorded, not by a clinical effect: `microbiologyevents.storetime` (lab receipt/storage) is
  used as the landmark while the clinical decision uses the result's availability; or the landmark
  itself induces immortal time.

H1, H2, H3 make **different, separable predictions**:
- H1 predicts RD ~0 (or <0) overall *and* Delta = RD(non-improving) - RD(improving) ~0, with a flat
  negative-control-exposure association.
- H2 predicts Delta > 0 (harm concentrated in non-improvers) even if RD <= 0 overall, **and** an
  apparently protective negative-control exposure (a non-antimicrobial chronic medication discontinued at
  the same landmark).
- H3 predicts the H1 pattern is *unstable* under an ascertainment change: replacing storetime with the
  specimen `charttime` (and/or adding a placebo landmark shift) materially changes/flips RD or Delta
  beyond the predeclared tolerance.

## Population (MIMIC-IV v3.1; first ICU stay per subject)
Adults >=18 (`patients.anchor_age` + `anchor_year` offset), first `icustays.stay_id` per `subject_id`,
non-null `hadm_id`, and either a microbiology result with an organism/susceptibility result, and empiric
broad-spectrum antibiotic coverage during the ICU stay. Defined ONLY from MIMIC-IV; no cross-dataset joins.

## Landmark, exposure, modifier, outcome (exact bindings)
- **Landmark t_R:** earliest qualifying `microbiologyevents` row for the `hadm_id` with `org_name` non-null
  OR `interpretation` in {S,I,R}, qualifying row time within `[icustays.intime, icustays.intime+72h]`.
  Primary landmark time = `storetime` (parent definition); **sensitivity landmark time = `charttime`**
  (H3 test). `micro_specimen_id` links susceptibility rows; `spec_type_desc` and `org_name` are covariates.
- **Exposure (de-escalation):** within `(t_R, t_R+48h]`, total antimicrobial **spectrum-breadth score**
  (explicit drug→breadth dictionary, 0–4 over gram-negative/pseudomonal, gram-positive/MRSA, anaerobe,
  atypical) decreases by >=1 with no new broad-spectrum agent added. Ongoing coverage reconstructed from
  `prescriptions.starttime`/`stoptime`/`drug` and cross-checked in `pharmacy.starttime`/`stoptime`/`medication`
  and `emar.charttime`/`medication`. Continuation = score unchanged or increased.
  **Negative-control exposure:** discontinuation, at the same landmark, of a non-antimicrobial chronic
  medication (no expected infection effect).
- **Effect modifier M:** pre-landmark organ trajectory, primary = prespecified partial-SOFA change between
  `(t_R-48h, t_R-24h]` and `(t_R-24h, t_R]`, dichotomized improving vs non-improving at a cutoff fixed
  before fitting; alternative (Generation branch) = trajectory classes / continuous slope (see method note).
- **Primary outcome:** 28-day all-cause mortality from `admissions.deathtime` (else `patients.dod`, using
  `patients.anchor_year` offset), measured from t_R. Secondary: 14-day mortality; in-hospital death
  (`admissions.hospital_expire_flag`); negative-control outcome = 28-day non-infection event.

## Analysis — baseline vs substantive alternative (same estimand)
- **B0 (interpretable baseline):** propensity-adjusted logistic/Cox over the M0→M1→M2 ladder reporting the
  adjusted 28-day risk difference `RD` with bootstrap 95% CI, overall and by M; interaction `Delta`.
- **A1 (substantive alternative):** target-trial-emulation with time-varying IPTW/AIPW (g-computation),
  cloning and censoring on post-landmark switching; reports marginal `RD`, `Delta`, and the same
  artifact-sensitivity. Chosen because the baseline cannot represent post-landmark time-varying
  confounding/attainment that H2 turns on (K3 precedent).
- **A0 (H3 test, required):** re-run the primary estimand with `charttime` landmark and a placebo
  landmark shift (±6h) and report the change in RD/Delta.

## Estimated / computed outputs the solver must produce
1. Eligible-stay counts overall and by modifier group; de-escalation prevalence overall and by group.
2. Adjusted RD (28-day mortality) and Delta with 95% CIs (B0 and A1).
3. Minimum detectable Delta at 80% power given observed prevalence and group sizes.
4. Negative-control exposure and outcome estimates.
5. Ascertainment sensitivity (storetime vs charttime; placebo shift).

## Falsification criteria (predeclared)
- **Supportive (H1):** RD <= +0.02 with upper CI <= +0.05, Delta <= +0.02 (upper CI <= +0.05), negative-
  control exposure RD <= +0.01, and ascertainment sensitivity |change in RD| and |change in Delta| <= 0.02.
- **Adverse (H2):** Delta >= +0.03 with lower CI > 0 (harm concentrated in non-improvers), OR overall
  RD >= +0.03 with lower CI > 0.
- **Artifact (H3):** |change in RD| or |change in Delta| >= 0.03 when storetime is replaced by charttime
  or under the placebo shift, even if the primary contrast looks protective.
- **Inconclusive:** CIs cross the supportive/adverse boundaries, or eligible counts / prevalence are too
  low for the minimum detectable Delta to exclude +0.03.
