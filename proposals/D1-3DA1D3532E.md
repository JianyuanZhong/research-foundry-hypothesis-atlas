## Three demonstration dispositions

- Delphi is adapted as a same-input irregular trajectory comparison, not reproduced: HCC has encounter-anchored diagnoses and dated labs but not UKB/Danish registries or ICD-10 lifetime histories. Its temporal masking, patient-level held-out evaluation, calibration and date-permutation ideas are useful.
- ALADYNOULLI is adapted only at the level of an EHR latent state. HCC has no verified genetic inputs, so no genetic or paper-reproduction claim is allowed. A latent hepatic-reserve/renal-stress representation is scientifically useful because it can separate current value from direction and timing under irregular measurement.
- Oncoformer’s full image/EHR model is unavailable and not reproducible: HCC contains no image files, only examination text/metadata, and the main article/complete STAR Methods were not available in the reference bundle. A lab-only learned model is a possible deferred sensitivity alternative, but no multimodal claim is made.

## Scientific question and substantive advance

Among adults with HCC and a first observed TACE-type procedure, does the post-procedure trajectory of AFP, hepatic reserve and renal function identify a higher risk of a subsequent inpatient encounter within 30 days after discharge that has no qualifying liver-directed procedure in its first 24 hours, beyond pre-procedure clinical history and baseline laboratory values?

Hypothesis: a coupled early deterioration phenotype—rising or nondeclining AFP together with worsening hepatic-reserve measurements and/or creatinine relative to each patient’s pre-procedure trajectory—will be associated with greater risk of this non-procedural subsequent inpatient encounter and will improve held-out calibration and risk stratification over a pre-TACE baseline. This is a prognostic association for observed care burden. It is not a causal effect of TACE, a diagnosis of post-embolization syndrome, hepatic failure, acute kidney injury, progression, recurrence, or an instruction to change care.

The advance over the parent is an explicitly non-procedural primary event and a mechanistic decomposition of trajectories: it asks whether the same early measurements can distinguish a return likely reflecting post-discharge illness burden from a planned/management-driven repeat liver-directed episode. It retains the broader subsequent inpatient encounter and repeat intervention as secondary outcomes, so a null non-procedural result is informative rather than being hidden by endpoint substitution.

## Exact cohort and time

Use catalog discovery buckets 0–79 only; buckets 80–99 are inaccessible and are not external validation. Index is each patient’s earliest observed qualifying TACE-type procedure with valid 手术.开始时间 on or after 2011-01-01, joined to encounters by 患者主索引 and 就诊号. Freeze before outcome inspection a dictionary including the exact labels 经导管肝动脉栓塞术, 动脉化疗栓塞, 经导管肝动脉造影化疗栓塞术（TACE）, and combined strings containing both embolization and chemoembolization components; exclude angiography-only 肝动脉造影 and any row without a valid start or matching encounter. Require a diagnosis name containing 肝细胞癌 or 肝癌 on an encounter at or before index; diagnoses have no native time, so the joined encounters.就诊时间 is the only temporal anchor and this is first observed, not first-ever.

Require index encounters.入院时间 and 出院时间, and discharge at or after procedure start. Require discharge no later than 2025-12-01 23:59:59 for a complete 30-day window under the local snapshot ending 2026-01-01. The prediction landmark is discharge. Post-procedure features are restricted to procedure start through index discharge; no post-discharge value, later encounter, later diagnosis, or later procedure may enter features.

Primary outcome: first subsequent encounter for the same 患者主索引, different 就诊号, with subsequent 入院时间 strictly after index 出院时间, within 30 days, nonmissing 出院时间, length of stay at least 24 hours, and no qualifying liver-directed procedure whose 手术.开始时间 falls in the first 24 hours of that subsequent admission. Call this a “subsequent inpatient encounter without an early liver-directed procedure,” not an unplanned readmission: the source has no validated planned/unplanned field, outside-hospital capture, or admission reason. If this exclusion produces fewer than 100 events, primary analysis becomes inconclusive and the broader >=24-hour subsequent inpatient encounter remains a descriptive sensitivity endpoint rather than silently relaxing the hypothesis.

Secondary outcomes: any >=24-hour subsequent inpatient encounter within 30 days; any subsequent encounter; first 30-day return with a liver-directed procedure in its first 24 hours; repeat TACE; count of subsequent inpatient encounters; and repeat liver-directed intervention during days 31–180. None is labelled recurrence, progression, response, survival, or treatment failure.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Baseline: training-only standardized elastic-net logistic regression for the primary binary event. Covariates are age, sex, department, calendar era, procedure subtype, pre-index HCC/liver disease/portal-hypertension/ascites/metastasis flags, prior inpatient count and liver-directed procedure count in 180 days, nearest pre-index AFP/reserve/creatinine values, pre-index slopes where estimable, days since each assay, and missingness/density. No post-procedure labs, discharge duration, later encounters, or future information.

Substantive alternative: fit an interpretable irregular multichannel state-space/latent trajectory model. A latent hepatic-reserve state and a distinct renal-stress state evolve from day -90 through discharge using elapsed-time random effects or Gaussian-process basis with shrinkage; AFP is a separate marker channel. Observation models use assay-specific noise and missingness indicators. At discharge, link latent levels, slopes and uncertainty to the primary binary outcome with a regularized logistic model. Fit on dated numeric lab observations plus the 30-day event target. Report latent-state contrasts and uncertainty, not only a score. This reveals whether direction/timing and coupled organ patterns carry information lost by nearest-value summaries.

Deferred alternative: a small GRU or temporal-attention model using the same dated assay tokens, values, labels, time gaps, missingness, diagnosis/procedure tokens and pre-index history, trained on the same target and split. Revisit only if the latent model fails convergence or pre-specified residual checks show nonlinear timing interactions; no tiny predictive gain alone justifies replacing an interpretable state model. A full multimodal transformer is deferred because images are unavailable and the Oncoformer main methods are unavailable.

Split and evaluation: deterministic participant hash buckets 0–59 fit, 60–69 select, 70–79 locked test; no participant crosses partitions. A chronological later-era stress test is secondary and not external validation. Fit imputation/scaling and tune regularization on fit/selection only. Report AUROC, PR-AUC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope/plots, paired patient bootstrap intervals for model differences, exploratory decision curves across broad thresholds, and prespecified subgroup results by era, department, assay availability and measurement density. Require at least 100 primary events; otherwise report inconclusive.

Falsification: (1) permute lab dates within patient while retaining values/labels; trajectory-specific gain should attenuate; (2) remove measurement density/missingness to quantify surveillance confounding; (3) exclude index length of stay and compare with an explicitly labelled discharge-severity sensitivity; (4) compare exact AFP with a pre-specified related-label sensitivity without pooling; (5) test calendar-era/department heterogeneity; (6) audit overlapping encounters and all feature timestamps; (7) compare primary non-procedural events with broader and liver-directed returns. A null or adverse held-out association, no meaningful calibration/risk improvement over baseline, or instability after these checks weakens/falsifies the hypothesis. “Meaningful” must be set before test unblinding as an absolute risk metric or calibration/risk-stratification improvement, not an arbitrary small AUROC change.

## Interpretation and deliverable

Support requires coherent prespecified organ/AFP directions, held-out improvement over baseline with uncertainty excluding triviality, calibration, stability after surveillance and date-leakage checks, and enough events. Adverse results argue against using these early trajectories to prioritize post-discharge surveillance. Inconclusive results include <100 events, assay ambiguity, nonconvergence, incomplete follow-up, wide uncertainty, or coding/measurement domination.

The solver must newly fit both models and output: frozen dictionaries and source hashes; cohort flow, joins, duplicate checks and exclusions; partition assignments; model parameters and latent summaries; locked predictions; discrimination, calibration and decision tables; date-permutation, measurement-intensity, era, procedure-dictionary and LOS sensitivity outputs; and a conclusion mapping each claim to a computed output.

Even supportive results cannot establish recurrence, radiographic response, hepatic failure, AKI, mortality, transplant eligibility, outside-hospital completeness, or a causal care recommendation. Those require clinical/radiology adjudication, units/reference ranges, validated staging and treatment intent, death/transplant linkage, complete care capture, expert review, external validation, or a prospective causal/implementation study.
