## Identified estimand and model

Let `G=1` denote systemic-record and `G=0` TACE2-only, `D_a=P_a−B_a` denote pre-repeat evolution, and `Y_a` post-repeat level. The primary estimand for assay `a` is the arm-specific conditional mean slope of post-repeat level with respect to evolution at a fixed baseline level, and its difference:

`Delta_a(b) = d/d d E[Y_a | B_a=b, D_a=d, G=g, Z]` for `g=1` minus the same derivative for `g=0`, evaluated over prespecified observed-overlap values of `b` and `Z`.

Fit the nonredundant raw-scale model

`Y_a = alpha_a + kappa_a B_a + lambda_a D_a + delta_a G + eta_a (D_a × G) + gamma_a^T Z + error`.

Here `lambda_a` is the TACE2-only evolution slope and `eta_a` is the explicit trajectory-by-arm contrast; systemic slope is `lambda_a+eta_a`. Since `P_a=B_a+D_a`, this is exactly equivalent to `Y_a ~ B_a + P_a + G + P_a×G + Z` under a one-to-one coefficient transformation, but it is not equivalent to a model that includes `B_a`, `P_a`, and `D_a` together. Do not report a baseline-by-arm coefficient as if it were the trajectory interaction. Do not interpret `D_a` as an independent biological variable conditional on both of its algebraic components.

The phrase “beyond baseline/current state” is constrained explicitly: evolution beyond baseline is identified by `B_a+D_a` (equivalently baseline plus current pre-repeat level); evolution beyond both baseline and current is not separately identifiable because it is a deterministic difference. A complementary current-state parameterization may report the same fitted predictions and transformed slopes, solely as an audit of invariance. The raw change-on-change model `(Y_a−P_a) ~ (P_a−B_a)×G` is coupling-sensitive and secondary only.

Use minimal prespecified `Z`: arm/event day (linear or prespecified restricted representation), entry year, age, sex if support permits; no post-event or panel-derived variables. Primary outcome is raw assay scale with heteroskedasticity-robust 95% intervals and patient-level influence diagnostics. Rank and 20% winsorized outcomes, duplicate/inequality exclusions, same-timestamp baseline, pre windows 24/48/168 hours, first-versus-latest pre selector, post +48-hour and first-post selectors, and calendar days +1–3 are locked sensitivities. Use baseline overlap weights learned from all 1,810 patients and cross-fitted event/observation selection models exactly as in the parent, with no claim that weighting removes collider bias.

Because the assay-specific audit yielded only 40 systemic albumin triplets and 41 systemic bilirubin triplets (118/119 TACE2-only), interaction inference is exploratory and should be downgraded to descriptive prediction contrasts unless systemic effective sample size and positivity gates pass. If ESS <25 or weighted balance/positivity fails, do not present an adjusted arm interaction. If fewer than 50 systemic triplets, never call it confirmatory.
