# Residual instability before ICU-to-ward transfer: audited coupling-control support

## What is known, what remains unresolved, and why it matters

The strongest evidence supports three bounded premises. Longitudinal ICU-discharge models can outperform a snapshot score, but operational factors, treatment limitation, and composite components remain unresolved [K1]. Across ICU-readmission studies, longitudinal predictors can improve performance, but populations, windows, outcomes, and risk of bias vary [K2]. Observation timing and frequency can be more predictive than measured values because EHR trajectories encode healthcare processes [K3]. The parent further established an exact 49,157-transfer MIMIC-IV cohort, 2,607 raw 48-hour ICU-return/death events, and a mathematical-coupling rival: endpoint severity and recent direction share late measurements.

This child establishes only computability. It exactly reproduces the frozen cohort and shows adequate pre-outcome support for temporally disjoint `G_pre` and endpoint-fixed order permutations in the full cohort and every reconstructible approximate-era role. It does not establish that direction predicts an outcome.

The unresolved hypothesis is unchanged: among ICU-to-general-ward transfers with the same measured endpoint state at `t0`, unusually worsening rather than improving recent measured-value history changes calibrated 48-hour ICU-return/death risk and fixed-capacity ranking, particularly when endpoint state and direction disagree. The leading bounded explanation is residual temporally ordered measured-value information. The strongest rival remains mathematical coupling/regression to the mean plus treatment- and observation-process dynamics. Resolution could determine whether ward/rapid-response review priority should depend on recent direction in addition to current state; it cannot show that review, delayed transfer, or ICU retention improves outcomes.

## Corrected meaning of the movable-bin gate

The measured positional gate passes for every raw-G-eligible row, but this is logically entailed by the frozen definitions: a raw-G-eligible domain has at least two observed bins in each three-bin half, hence at least four observed bins. Freezing its final observed bin necessarily leaves at least three movable nonfinal bins. Four raw-G domains therefore imply four domains meeting the movable-position rule.

This resolves attrition/computability, not attribution power. A row can have movable positions but identical nonfinal summaries, yielding little effective variation. Without changing eligibility or the hypothesis, the full extraction must report:

1. the fraction of raw-G rows with at least four domains having at least two distinct nonfinal abnormality-margin summaries;
2. per-row and per-era variance of recomputed `G_perm` across the 200 frozen seeds;
3. fraction unchanged under every permutation and under reversal;
4. observed-minus-null interval width for interaction, Brier gain, and quota capture.

These are null-informativeness diagnostics, not post-label selection gates. If permutations are degenerate or observed-minus-null intervals include material benefit and harm, order attribution is inconclusive. L2-D cannot rescue it.

## Baseline and learned alternative remain matched

The transparent baseline remains `D1-perp` alongside E0 and raw D1. It estimates the sign and size of unusual recent direction at matched endpoint state and exposes the exact `C×direction` contrast. Ridge versus depth-3 boosted nuisance regression is chosen only by subject-grouped development RMSE and residual-balance gates, never by R+D association.

The scientifically substantive alternative remains `L2-D`, the two-stream causal TCN over the same time grid and static E0 context. It can reveal nonlinear late relapse, cross-domain lag, and opposing-domain patterns that scalar G loses. It is selected only if calibrated and seed-stable, better than D1-perp in both locked late eras, and its value/full gain exceeds both process-only and endpoint-fixed order-null heads. Otherwise prefer the transparent model. A latent-state/domain-adversarial model remains deferred until independent physiologic, policy, transfer-intent, or external-site evidence can identify what it removes.
