# Graded, MerTK-dependent brake vs composition/state artifact: a cross-setting perturbation test on the MerTK/CD206pos macrophage -> FLS effector axis (E-MTAB-8873 + E-MTAB-8316)

Domain: therapeutic_targets (D2). Seed: D2-T2. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis] (island leader, valid, mu 32.41, gen 5). Key works [K1],[K2],[K3]. Screening status: **planned, not executed** (read-only inspection of prepared files only; receipt work/lead/support/DATA_PROBE.txt).

## 1. Unresolved question (opening)
In RA synovium, does the MerTK/CD206pos (MRC1+) macrophage population actively exert a **graded, dose-like, MerTK-dependent brake** on the FLS matrix-degrading/effector program, or is the pos-vs-neg FLS-effector difference a **fixed composition/state artifact** (myeloid load, an SPP1+ vs FOLR2/TREM2 state, or the shared cross-tissue fibroblast program [K2])? [K3] shows a MerTK+ synovial macrophage subset tracks remission, but is observational: it does not show the brake is graded, per-cell, or MerTK-mediated.

## 2. Clinical importance
A substantial fraction of RA patients fail to sustain drug-free remission and become "difficult-to-treat," increasingly framed as a failed inflammation-resolution program [K3]. If the MerTK+ subset **dose-dependently** lowers the FLS effector program, then restoring that subset/signalling is a distinct target-prioritisation decision; if the FLS difference is fully explained by composition/state [K1], MerTK positivity is a state marker and a poor standalone target. This changes whether MerTK-directed resolution therapy is worth prioritising.

## 3. Hypothesis, rival, and what separates them
- **H_G (graded MerTK brake).** Raising the MerTK/CD206pos macrophage fraction within the *same* FLS donor lowers the FLS effector program *per cell*: the effector-high FLS fraction falls monotonically with pos_fraction beyond load/state adjustment, **and** pharmacological MerTK inhibition releases the brake (raises the effector program) concordantly across settings.
- **H_C (fixed composition/state).** The pos-vs-neg difference is a step change from composition/state/donor identity; the per-cell effector fraction is flat once load/state/donor are accounted for, with no monotone gradient and no MerTK-inhibitor release.

**Advance over the parent (three additions).**
1. **Calibrated composition/load null.** A within-FLS-donor cell-label permutation gives the effector-high-fraction statistic a null distribution, so the parent's "well-name load proxy" rival becomes a tested null rather than an adjusted covariate.
2. **Two-setting pharmacological-perturbation concordance.** MerTK-pathway inhibition is present in *two independent datasets*: E-MTAB-8873 (macrophages pre-treated with MerTK inhibitor) and E-MTAB-8316 (donors treated with the MerTK inhibitor UNC106238 at dose 250, LPS-stimulated). H_G requires concordant *release* (effector program up); discordance is adverse evidence the parent cannot produce.
3. **Pre-registered negative branch** (inconclusive/adverse allowed; no forced positive).

**Discriminating predictions (verified names, E-MTAB-8873).** The one design point holding FLS donor and macrophage donors fixed while varying composition: `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_1Mpos_SA266A_3Mneg` (pos_fraction 0.25) vs `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_3Mpos_SA266A_1Mneg` (0.75), plus the SA258R ladder {0.0 FLS_only, 0.25, 0.75, 1.0 Mpos} and donor-matched inhibitor arm `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos_Inhibitor`.
- H_G predicts: effector-high fraction at 0.25 > 0.75; monotone non-increasing across the ladder; donor-clustered 95% CI excluding 0; inhibitor arm de-represses (effector up); E-MTAB-8316 UNC106238 vs none shifts the monocyte effector/activation program in the same release direction.
- H_C predicts: flat/load-explained/state-absorbed fraction, no monotone gradient, no inhibitor release, or no cross-setting concordance.

**Estimand.** Per-FLS-cell FLS effector score over a pre-registered 9-gene set MMP1/MMP3/MMP9/MMP13, IL6, CXCL8, CXCL10, CCL2, CCL5 (all confirmed present as `Reference_end` rows). Primary statistic: difference in **effector-high fraction** (top-tercile cutoff fixed on the FLS-only baseline) between 0.25 and 0.75, with FLS-donor-clustered 95% CI and permutation null. Secondary: distribution shift (mean/median), ladder monotonicity, and two-setting inhibitor-release concordance.

## 4. Method comparison (required reasoning)
- **Baseline (retained):** pseudobulk sample-mean effector score with well-name pos_fraction/load covariates, as in the parent. Cheap; already carries the composition adjustment.
- **Substantive alternative:** per-cell analysis of the 445-feature FLS matrices quantifying the *distribution* (effector-high fraction, quantiles) with a permutation null, plus a cross-dataset perturbation-concordance test. What the alternative reveals that the baseline loses: pseudobulk means are identical whether a subset of FLS cells responds strongly or all cells respond weakly — the exact distinction the brake hypothesis makes; and the cross-setting perturbation tests direction of change, which a single-sample mean cannot.
- **Why not a neural/compositional model:** the uncertainty is about the *shape* and *direction* of a per-cell distribution, not a predictive gain; a heavier model adds no scientific information for this estimand and would obscure the pre-registered null. Recorded as an explicit non-choice; revisit only if distribution-shape analyses prove uninformative.
- **Bound resource estimate (solver envelope, not measured here):** 33 samples x <=~1400 cells x 445 features ~= 20M values; fits in CPU memory (<2 GB) after row selection. CPU-only; ~minutes. No GPU needed. (Discovery-time verification was read-only/unallocated.)

## 6. Essential limits (what this cannot establish)
- Cell-level members in E-MTAB-8873 are **FLS-only**: per-macrophage MerTK dosage/signalling is not measurable; the pos-vs-neg contrast is via composition of a mixed well, not per-macrophage readout.
- Panel limit: **MERTK present, but GAS6/PROS1/TYRO3 absent** from the 445 features -> ligand/co-receptor-level MerTK mechanism is untestable here.
- E-MTAB-8316 is **LPS-stimulated monocytes, not FLS**; it tests pathway-direction concordance, not the FLS brake directly.
- Nested/reused donors require explicit dependence modelling; no twelve-independent-donor claim.
- Cross-sectional designs cannot establish transition/clinical efficacy; causality and clinical benefit require protein/functional perturbation, expert review, or another study.

## 7. Falsification
- **Supportive:** effector-high fraction 0.25 > 0.75 with FLS-donor-clustered 95% CI excluding 0; monotone ladder; both inhibitor settings release the brake in the predicted direction.
- **Adverse:** no monotone gradient; no inhibitor release or opposite direction; discordant datasets.
- **Inconclusive:** wide CI overlapping 0, underpowered nested donors, or discordant-only evidence; does not refute H_G.
