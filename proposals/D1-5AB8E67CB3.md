# IV loop start-or-restart at ICU hour 48 after vasopressor-treated shock

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and advance

The bedside decision is unchanged: should an invasively ventilated adult with substantial documented positive balance, after shock has stabilized, receive or restart an IV loop diuretic now or continue without a loop?

The strongest claim supported by available evidence is narrower than clinical benefit. The RADAR-2 indexed abstract (180 randomized patients; DOI `10.1007/s00134-021-06596-8`; frozen Europe PMC search source `[source checksum]`) reports lower day-3 fluid balance but was underpowered for clinical outcomes; the mortality point estimate did not favor intervention. A 2026 systematic-review publisher page (13 randomized studies, 2,495 patients; DOI `10.5826/tuj.2026.18750`; frozen HTML `[source checksum]`) reports fluid-balance separation in 7 studies and no positive hard-outcome trial. Only its abstract and reference list were inspected, not a full methods/article file. The full Orieux et al. review XML (DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083`; frozen source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) explicitly treats ICU renal congestion, its criteria, and therapeutic targets as unvalidated.

Thus existing evidence supports that active deresuscitation can reduce recorded balance. It does **not** establish that loop initiation improves ventilator-free survival or is safe. This study tests the unresolved causal claim and separates it from the process response.

**Hypothesis.** Among the fixed population below, a dynamic policy of actual IV loop initiation/re-initiation within 12 hours of `t0`, compared with no loop by any route through 24 hours, increases mean 28-day days alive and free of invasive ventilation (VFD) by at least 1 day, while excluding more than 3 percentage points excess 28-day death and more than 5 points excess MIMIC-recorded new RRT by day 7. A lower 24/48-hour documented balance is a required treatment-contrast/process check, not proof of patient benefit. Pulmonary edema on a result-available chest report remains an exploratory modifier, not “renal congestion.”

## Fixed target population and time zero

Use SHA-256 discovery buckets 0–79 and every available record for those subjects; buckets 80–99 are inaccessible, not a validation set. Compute `sha256(str(subject_id)) mod 100` using a frozen canonical decimal string. Select the first chronologically eligible ICU stay per `subject_id`.

`t0 = icu/icustays.intime + 48 hours`. Age is `patients.anchor_age + year(t0) - patients.anchor_year` (recognizing age >89 is top-coded). Require:

- age >=18; `icustays.intime <= t0 < outtime`; no `admissions.deathtime <= t0` and no `patients.dod` before the date of `t0`;
- invasive ventilation item `225792` with `starttime <= t0 < endtime` and observed status in {FinishedRunning, Stopped, Paused};
- actual vasopressor delivery between ICU admission and `t0-6h`, and none overlapping `(t0-6h,t0]`, using positive-amount `inputevents` segments for epinephrine `221289,229617`, dopamine `221662`, phenylephrine `221749,229630,229631,229632`, norepinephrine `221906`, and vasopressin `222315`;
- median valid MAP >=65 mmHg in `(t0-3h,t0]` from `chartevents.itemid` `220052,220181,225312`, `valuenum` 20–200 mmHg, `warning=0`, with `storetime<=t0`;
- documented net ICU balance >=50 mL/kg from `intime` through `t0`, weight 30–300 kg, urine output >0.1 mL/kg/h in `(t0-6h,t0]`, and at least one valid input and one urine-output event available in each of four 12-hour blocks;
- latest blood potassium available in `(t0-12h,t0]` is >=3.0 mmol/L, using `labevents` `50971,52610`, `storetime<=t0`;
- no actual loop by any route in `(t0-12h,t0]`;
- no RRT before `t0` in any ICU stay of the hospitalization, from procedure items `225441,225802,225803,225805,225809,225955`; and no ECMO, defined as procedure line `229529` or `229530` overlapping `t0` or a positive ECMO flow `chartevents` value for `224660` or `229270` available in `(t0-1h,t0]`.

Baseline AKI is not excluded; KDIGO stage is frozen as an effect modifier. Prior loop use from ICU admission through `t0-12h` is allowed, preserving the clinically common start-or-restart question; a never-treated new-user analysis is required.

There is no audited structured comfort-measures-only field in the configured files. Therefore no note-derived or provider-ID proxy exclusion is permitted. This resolves the parent's noncomputable criterion without changing its observable population; goals of care and treatment indication remain unmeasured confounders requiring clinical adjudication.

## Event-level documented balance

This is a documented event ledger, not total-body fluid accumulation or validated congestion.

For `icu/inputevents`, include positive `amount` with exact `amountuom` in {`mL`, `L`} and observed `statusdescription` in {FinishedRunning, ChangeDose/Rate, Stopped, Paused, Bolus}; convert L x1000. “Stopped,” “Paused,” and “ChangeDose/Rate” are valid delivered segments, not cancelled orders. Exclude the rare `Reported` status, nonpositive/missing amounts, and other units, reporting each exclusion. A zero-duration bolus is assigned fully at `starttime`; otherwise allocate `amount * overlap_duration/(endtime-starttime)` to the interval. Do not collapse sequential `orderid/linkorderid` segments; remove only exact duplicate rows.

For `icu/outputevents`, all rows are mL in the audited source. Include nonnegative numeric `value`, except `227488` (GU irrigant volume in), which is added to input. Primary ICU balance excludes semantically pre-ICU/PACU/OR items `226626–226633`; repeat including them. Total output includes urine, gastrointestinal, drain, blood-loss, and chest-tube output. Urine output is restricted to `226557,226558,226559,226560,226561,226563,226564,226565,226566,226567,226584,227489`; report a sensitivity excluding mixed urine/irrigant items `226566,227489`.

Eligibility uses only events with event time by `t0` and `storetime<=t0`. An input interval crossing `t0` is truncated there. Report, by 6-hour block, valid counts and volumes; missing/nonpositive/unit/status exclusions; chart-to-store lag; duplicate rate; and the proportions with no input, no total output, and no urine event. Primary completeness is one input and one urine event per 12-hour block; repeat with 6-hour completeness, inclusion of delayed (`storetime>t0`) entries, and continuous balance. Failure of source accounting is not interpreted as physiologic zero.

Weight hierarchy is latest valid pre-`t0` admission weight `226512`, then latest daily weight `224639`, then median valid `inputevents.patientweight`; all require availability by `t0`. Freeze item/unit/status maps before outcome estimation.

## Actual loop exposure and dynamic grace periods

Normalize exact drug strings to furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide, ethacrynic acid/ethacrynate sodium; exclude desensitization, inhaled formulations, orders without administration, and ambiguous strings.

An actual IV loop event is either:

1. a positive-amount ICU `inputevents` segment for furosemide `221794,228340` or bumetanide `229639` with one of the five valid administered statuses above; or
2. an `hosp/emar` event joined to all `emar_detail` siblings on `subject_id,emar_id,emar_seq`. For a bolus, require `event_txt` in {Administered, Delayed Administered, Partial Administered, Administered in Other Location}, a positive numeric `dose_given`, and `complete_dose_not_given` not Yes/Y. For infusion initiation, require administration type `IV Infusion` and `event_txt` in {Started, Delayed Started, Restarted, Started in Other Location}, or a new-bag indicator Y with a positive infusion rate. Stopped, Not Started, Not Given, Hold Dose, Not Confirmed, blank, and order status alone never initiate exposure.

Route is coalesced across the eMAR-detail event group, then from `hosp/pharmacy` joined on `subject_id,hadm_id,pharmacy_id`; IV requires exact route `IV` or `IV DRIP`. Conflicting route sources are discordant and excluded from primary eMAR exposure. Pharmacy/prescription rows alone never establish administration. This captures all four loop agents; ICU item IDs alone do not.

For binary exposure, deduplicate same normalized drug within 15 minutes across sources. For dose, prefer ICU amount for matched events and otherwise eMAR dose; report source-specific events, matched pairs, unmatched events, route conflicts, and drug counts. Furosemide-equivalent dose is secondary and freezes 40 mg IV furosemide = 1 mg IV bumetanide = 20 mg IV torsemide = 50 mg IV ethacrynate; repeat without conversion. eMAR `charttime` and ICU `starttime` are event times. Later `storetime` may ascertain treatment retrospectively but cannot enter a covariate history before it became available; repeat exposure requiring `storetime` by the hourly boundary.

Actual loop use by any route for washout/control uses the same eMAR administration rules and route set {IV, IV DRIP, PO, PO/NG, NG, ORAL}, plus ICU input events.

The strategies are explicitly dynamic to handle terminal events during grace:

- **A:** while alive and still in the indexed hospital, receive no oral/non-IV loop before the first qualifying IV loop and initiate IV loop by `t0+12h`; after initiation, management is unrestricted.
- **B:** while alive and still in the indexed hospital, receive no loop by any route through `t0+24h`; after hour 24, management is unrestricted.

Clone each patient to both at `t0`. Censor A at the first non-IV loop before IV initiation or at hour 12 if alive, hospitalized, and untreated; censor B at first loop through hour 24. A first loop in hours 12–24 follows neither policy. **Death before a deadline is an outcome, never artificial censoring:** an untreated early death remains adherent to both “while alive” policies and receives VFD=0. Discharge alive before a deadline also ends the opportunity and is adherent to the dynamic policy; repeat treating untreated discharge before hour 12 as A nonadherence. ICU transfer does not end hospital eligibility. This refinement removes immortal-survivor selection while preserving the 48-hour decision and per-protocol policy contrast.

## Outcomes and observation

The primary estimand is the per-protocol marginal mean difference A minus B in 28-day VFD from `t0`. Merge all valid item-`225792` procedure intervals across every ICU stay and MIMIC hospitalization for the subject during 28 days, union overlaps, and compute unventilated hours/24. If `admissions.deathtime` or `patients.dod` indicates death by day 28, VFD=0. Otherwise, discharge alive is assumed alive and unventilated until day 28 unless a later MIMIC ventilation interval or death contradicts it. Report a hospital-observed version that makes no post-discharge ventilation assumption. MIMIC cannot observe out-of-network ventilation/readmission.

Secondary outcomes are 48 continuous hours alive without invasive ventilation by day 7; 28-day death; MIMIC-recorded new RRT by day 7 using the six procedure IDs across all ICU stays; ICU-, hospital-, and ventilator-free days; documented net balance and urine output at 24/48 hours; and KDIGO worsening by day 7. Creatinine uses blood items `50912,52546`; potassium uses `50971,52610`; freeze units and use result time for outcomes but availability time for predictors.

Acute safety outcomes are (i) vasopressor restart lasting >=1 hour or two valid MAP values <55 mmHg 15–120 minutes apart within 48 hours, and (ii) any measured potassium <3.0 mmol/L within 48 hours; also report through day 7. A missing potassium is not “normal”: report measurement frequency and timing by arm and require >=90% 48-hour ascertainment in each weighted arm for a supportive electrolyte claim.
