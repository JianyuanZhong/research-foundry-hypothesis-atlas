# Raw-reconstructed denominator and bounded downstream replication of eICU semantic undercoverage

## Question, supported evidence, and substantive advance

The assessed parent `[prior hypothesis]` established a raw-source reconstruction of the target-independent denominator and an outcome-blind exhaustive audit of the eICU `carePlanGeneral` Ventilation vocabulary, but was correctly assessed **repairable** because its full downstream chart-proxy outcome and hospital-held-out model pipeline had not been independently rerun. The assessed-valid parent `[prior hypothesis]` independently established that post-classification observation is informative, separated ICU death and live ICU discharge from computable chart outcomes, and found no measured-selection rescue, while conservatively retaining an overall selection-sensitive/inconclusive interpretation.

This child makes the remaining claim narrower and executable. It tests whether an outcome-blind broader literal `Ventilated -` vocabulary materially rescues the exact care-plan encoding for a frozen, structured-documentation prediction task. The newly executed evidence combines (i) the complete raw reconstruction of the denominator, airway definitions, care-plan vocabulary, and exact-receipt positive control with (ii) a bounded leave-one-hospital-out point-estimate replication using the raw-derived enriched row artifact. The primary claim is the fully raw-supported denominator/vocabulary/receipt result; the downstream model result is explicitly a point-estimate replication without a new bootstrap. Uncertainty from the previously assessed-valid nested refits is inherited as prior evidence and is not represented as newly rerun here.

The clinical decision relevance is measurement validity: if additional exported strings materially expanded multisite receipt and improved out-of-hospital prediction after documentation-opportunity controls, further semantic adjudication would be justified. If not, outcome-guided synonym expansion of this field should not be presented as a generic multicenter rescue. Neither result establishes bedside ventilation, extubation evaluation, a spontaneous breathing trial (SBT), readiness, extubation, care quality, benefit, harm, or causality.

## Strongest supported claim and exact unresolved claim

**Strongest claim supported before this child:** the two exact Ventilation-group labels are sparse and hospital-concentrated, and the assessed parent analyses found no transportable predictive rescue after generic care-plan documentation controls; however, omitted or site-local Ventilation strings remained a plausible explanation for the exact-label failure.

**Falsifiable claim tested by this child:** in the frozen eICU snapshot, an outcome-blind broader vocabulary consisting of every normalized Ventilation value beginning with the literal prefix `ventilated -` will materially expand receipt across hospitals and improve hospital-held-out prediction of the later structured respiratory-setting reduction beyond generic documentation-process controls and the exact encoding.

The raw reconstruction directly tests the vocabulary and coverage portion without accessing outcomes during vocabulary discovery. The bounded LOHO execution tests the predictive portion as a point estimate. Post-classification competing observation states and measured-selection sensitivity are inherited from the assessed-valid ascertainment parent, not claimed as a new rerun in this child.

## Frozen population, temporal design, and outcome

The target-independent population is the first qualifying ICU stay per `uniquepid`, ordered by `unitvisitnumber` and then `patientunitstayid`, with no care-plan target used in denominator selection. It preserves adult eligibility, ICU continuation through landmarks `L = 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, 7,200` minutes, strict pre-landmark physiologic screening, recent FiO2 and PEEP requirements, the prespecified SpO2/respiratory-rate/MAP conditions, pre-landmark end-of-life exclusion, and exclusion for recent `Spontaneous - adequate`. Repeated landmark rows remain clustered by `sid = patientunitstayid`.

The four primary airway definitions are unchanged:

* `exact_unbounded`
* `exact_airway_24h`
* `exact_airway_12h`
* `exact_airway_6h`

Exact invasive-airway evidence is as defined by the frozen respiratory-care logic; interval zero values are treated as unavailable/open sentinels rather than genuine starts. Classification is outcome-blind and occurs only in the inclusive `[L, L+360]` window. The exact receipt is presence of either normalized literal target:

* `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation`
* `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`

The later outcome is the inherited non-peak structured respiratory-setting reduction in `(L+360, L+1080]`, requiring ICU discharge after `L+1080`. A robust reduction is documented if at least one computable component decreases by the prespecified threshold: FiO2 by 0.05, PEEP by 2 cm H2O, pressure support by 2 cm H2O, or set ventilator rate by 2 breaths/min. Peak-pressure fields are excluded, paired missing components are not imputed as non-events, and the outcome is a chart proxy rather than a clinical endpoint.

For the bounded LOHO point estimates, each definition retains only rows with `complete_outcome_window == True` and nonmissing `robust_reduction`; no receipt or outcome is used to construct the denominator or discover vocabulary.

## Raw reconstruction and outcome-blind vocabulary audit

The raw executable reconstructs the denominator before attaching care-plan receipt, then scans the complete `carePlanGeneral` source for vocabulary. Normalization trims strings, collapses internal whitespace, case-folds, and maps missing/blank values to explicit sentinel values. Every normalized value whose normalized group is `ventilation` is enumerated before any outcome-linked restriction. The primary broader vocabulary is all values beginning with the literal prefix `ventilated -`; no value is selected by outcome, model fit, hospital performance, or frequency threshold.

The complete `carePlanGeneral` scan contained 3,115,018 rows and exactly eight normalized Ventilation values:

* missing: 7,581
* `Non-invasive ventilation`: 26,836
* `Spontaneous - adequate`: 190,809
* `Spontaneous - tenuous`: 32,587
* `Ventilated - chronic dependency`: 3,105
* `Ventilated - rapid wean/extubation`: 5,705
* `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation`: 51,862
* `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`: 14,907

Thus the broader primary vocabulary has exactly four indicators: the two exact targets plus chronic dependency and rapid wean/extubation. The all-eight vocabulary is a secondary exhaustive sensitivity and is not treated as a homogeneous ventilation phenotype.

The raw reconstruction produced 3,722 rows for `exact_unbounded`, 2,612 for 24 hours, 1,774 for 12 hours, and 1,178 for 6 hours before complete-outcome restriction. The exact-receipt positive control reproduced 540 rows across 443 stays and 74 hospitals with zero missing or extra `(sid, landmark)` keys. In the exact-unbounded raw denominator, 108/3,722 rows had an exact target receipt (2.902%), only 18 of 77 hospitals contributed receipt, 59 hospitals were target-free, and target receipt HHI was approximately 0.4674. The bounded definitions were similarly sparse and concentrated. These results falsify a generic missing-exported-synonym explanation at the raw vocabulary/coverage level for this snapshot.

## Bounded downstream LOHO replication

The downstream replication uses the raw-derived enriched row artifact and refits a separate model for each held-out hospital. Eligible held-out hospitals have at least 20 complete outcome rows and both outcome classes. Training uses all other eligible observed hospitals; the held-out hospital is never used for fitting. The models use one-hot landmark, median imputation with missingness indicators, standardization, and logistic regression (`C=1`, `max_iter=2000`).

The comparisons are:

1. pre-landmark respiratory baseline;
2. baseline plus generic post-freeze care-plan opportunity controls (`cp_any`, log care-plan row count, distinct care-plan groups, any Ventilation row, log Ventilation-row count, and distinct Ventilation values);
3. process controls plus exact receipt/label encoding;
4. process controls plus the four outcome-blind broader `Ventilated -` indicators.

The primary contrasts are broader-minus-process LOHO log loss, reported both micro row-weighted and macro equal-hospital. Positive values mean the broader model has worse loss. Coverage and alternative-only receipt are computed on the same complete-outcome rows used for the point estimate.

The executed point estimates were:

| airway definition | broader-minus-process micro log-loss | broader-minus-process macro log-loss | coverage gain | alternative-only hospitals |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `exact_unbounded` | +0.000988 | +0.000886 | +0.000910 (0.091 percentage points) | 1 |
| `exact_airway_24h` | +0.008246 | +0.001920 | +0.001320 (0.132 percentage points) | 1 |
| `exact_airway_12h` | +0.009363 | +0.002772 | +0.001286 (0.129 percentage points) | 1 |
| `exact_airway_6h` | +0.015376 | +0.007488 | +0.000982 (0.098 percentage points) | 1 |

The bounded semantic-undercoverage gate requires at least a 0.02 absolute coverage gain, a multisite alternative expansion, and a favorable predictive contrast. No bounded definition meets any part of the supportive gate: gains are below 0.02, every alternative-only set is confined to one hospital, and broader-minus-process micro and macro loss is positive in every definition. The local point-estimate classification is therefore `adverse_semantic_undercoverage_explanation`.

These are point estimates only. No new nested bootstrap completed in this child. The previously assessed-valid nested-refit uncertainty is inherited as prior evidence, not relabeled as a new execution. That inherited evidence is consistent with the bounded adverse direction but remains conditional on the observed hospital set and the disclosed resampling design.

ICU death and live ICU discharge are not assigned a chart-outcome value and live discharge is not called success. Sparse respiratory documentation is not interpreted as no clinical care. ## Interpretation and falsification

**Supportive result:** a reproducible multisite expansion of receipt with material coverage gain and favorable hospital-held-out predictive improvement after process controls. This would justify local interface review and prospective semantic/clinical adjudication, not bedside interpretation. It did not occur.

**Observed adverse result:** exhaustive enumeration found no additional exported site-local lexical string; the two added literal-prefix values contributed only 1–3 alternative rows per definition in the outcome-linked sets, all from one hospital; and broader encoding worsened every bounded LOHO point contrast. The evidence supports stopping generic outcome-guided synonym expansion for this exported field and this structured chart proxy in the observed eICU snapshot.

**Inconclusive boundary:** the result does not establish that no hospital has an unexported upstream synonym, that local interfaces map concepts identically, that missing documentation is random, or that a local implementation cannot be useful. It also does not establish that the care-plan values represent actual invasive ventilation, daily evaluation, SBT, extubation, readiness, quality, benefit, harm, or causality. Stronger claims require local data dictionaries/interface metadata, direct time-resolved airway/device/SBT/extubation events, blinded clinical adjudication, and external or prospective multisite validation.

An automatic verifier can check source hashes, schemas, filter flows, first-stay and landmark construction, normalization, all-row vocabulary enumeration, exact-receipt reconstruction, positive-control identity, coverage/concentration, row joins, model separation, LOHO fitting, point metrics, and gate logic. It cannot establish bedside semantics, workflow equivalence, clinical benefit/harm, causal effects, or transport to unobserved hospitals.
