# Provisional donor-aware hypothesis: MerTK-positive macrophage context induces a reproducible FLS transcriptional response in E-MTAB-8873

## Proposed relationship

In the frozen E-MTAB-8873 targeted expression experiment, exposure of rheumatoid-arthritis synovial fibroblast-like cells (FLS) to MerTK/CD206-positive macrophages may induce a reproducible, condition-specific transcriptional response that is distinguishable from FLS-only and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage co-culture after accounting for contributing donors and repeated context measurements. This is a hypothesis, not a computed result.

The strongest available evidence supports only that macrophage-containing synovial interactions are biologically plausible and that this dataset contains paired FLS/co-culture conditions. The inherited references motivate the question but do not establish a response in this assay, direct ligand-receptor action, or causality. The unresolved claim is therefore the assay-specific, donor-aware condition response; the experiment will test it rather than assert it.

## Falsifiable test

Population: all 33 E-MTAB-8873 FLS/co-culture records with verified sample-to-design joins and defensible contributing-donor representation; do not silently drop records or infer missing donor identity.

Primary contrasts:

1. MerTK/CD206-positive macrophage co-culture versus FLS-only, within the same FLS donor context where available.
2. MerTK/CD206-positive macrophage co-culture versus MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage co-culture, preserving the same donor/condition structure.
3. Mixed macrophage conditions as a secondary composition check, not independent validation.

For each feature, estimate the condition contrast in log-expression space after a small count offset. Use a donor-aware paired/stratified contrast or contributing-donor random-effects model. The uncertainty unit is the contributing donor/context, not the cell. Use a condition-preserving donor/condition permutation null. LPS and MerTK-inhibitor conditions are mechanistic context, not independent causal replicates.

Before inspecting results, prespecify direction, an effect-size threshold (for example log2 fold change 0.25), and a donor-level false-discovery or permutation threshold (for example 0.05), subject to verification that the design supports the estimand. Report feature effects, uncertainty intervals, permutation results, and the number of donors contributing to each contrast.

Supportive: a reproducible condition-specific signature with donor-aware uncertainty, separation from both controls, and no comparable signal in the negative-control contrast. Adverse: no separation, reversal, or failure to survive donor/condition permutations. Inconclusive: unresolved donor mapping, insufficient independent donor contexts, low detectability, or uncertainty too large to distinguish communication from composition or technical artifacts.

## Competing explanations and limits

The strongest rival explanations are FLS donor identity or age/sex differences, macrophage composition or ratio, batch/technical effects, LPS or MerTK-inhibitor treatment rather than macrophage exposure, and targeted-panel measurement or cell-count differences. Paired donor contexts, FLS-only and macrophage-negative controls, condition-preserving permutations, and explicit treatment/context labels address but do not eliminate these explanations.

Expression association cannot establish direct target action, ligand-receptor causality, clinical transition, or treatment benefit. Clinical phenotype, treatment history, age completeness, and independent external validation are not assumed available. Causal interpretation requires perturbation or independent experimental evidence.
