## Three recording-time objects

All recording-time work is frozen before reading post-`t0` loop strategy or outcomes.

### T: audit-only maximal timestamped-gate as-of reconstruction

Across every frozen opportunity, reconstruct each timestamped gate using the identical clinical-time, item, unit, status, tie, and ambiguity rule but permit a row to enter only when `storetime` is nonmissing and `storetime<=t0`. This applies to ventilation/ECMO/RRT procedureevents; pressor, fluid-input, washout, and RRT inputevents; MAP/weight/ECMO/RRT chartevents; urine and all balance outputevents; and potassium labevents. Clinical time remains `starttime/endtime` or `charttime`; `storetime` never replaces it. Recompute derived sums, medians, selected values, interval unions, and all eight balance bins from the retained rows.

Retain the final-table structural opportunity, age/admission/ICU topology, transfers, and date-bracketed RRT ICD rule because those members lack recording clocks. Set `structural_availability_clock_missing=true` and `rrt_icd_availability_clock_missing=true`. Consequently T is “maximal timestamped-gate as-of final-table proxy,” never “all-information,” “decision-observable,” “clinician-known,” “live,” or “bedside.”

Let `E_T` be T eligibility across all frozen opportunities. `E_T` is audit-only because omission of late contraindicating rows can make `E_T\E_R` nonempty. Never clone, fit, weight, or estimate an outcome in `E_T` alone.

### M: strict non-adding maximal timestamped-gate cohort

For every row with clinical time no later than `t0` but missing `storetime` or `storetime>t0`, compute a treatment/outcome-blind late-conflict flag. A conflict is present if adding that row to T changes: any gate pass/fail/ambiguous state; selected ventilation, MAP, weight, potassium, ECMO, or RRT state; recent-course union/contiguity category; six-hour stability; loop washout; any +30/+50/+100 balance category or eight-bin coverage; urine threshold; or the accepted/ambiguous status of a row used by a gate. Save stream, item, clinical time, storetime class, gate, and conflict reason without identifiers in aggregate output.

Define
`E_M = E_R ∩ E_T ∩ {late_conflict=false}`.

M can only remove retrospectively eligible subjects. A subject in `E_T\E_R` is reported as an as-of-only addition and is never admitted to M. Rebuild all M baseline covariates only from rows satisfying the T rule; do not reuse retrospective X0, folds, models, weights, endpoints, or imputations.

Always report `|E_R|`, `|E_T|`, `|E_R∩E_T|`, `|E_T\E_R|`, `|E_R\E_T|`, and `|E_M|`; the Jaccard `J=|E_R∩E_T|/|E_R∪E_T|`; retained fraction `R_M=|E_M|/|E_R|`; gatewise 2x2 tables; ordered funnels after opportunity, ventilation-recorded, recent pressor, stable, MAP, weight, urine, potassium, balance, ECMO, RRT, washout, and late-conflict exclusion; disagreement reasons; and per-stream missing, late, negative-lag, and conflict counts. `J<0.90` or `R_M<0.90` falsifies temporal population concordance and yields temporal-validity-inconclusive, regardless of treatment direction.

### G: nested all-row database-recorded-time cohort

G preserves the all-gate concern from candidate 531 without overstating clock coverage. Starting from M, require every accepted or gate-relevant ambiguous row evaluated as contributing to a timestamped pre-`t0` gate to have nonmissing `storetime<=t0`. For interval/snapshot gates this includes every row used to establish the accepted interval, selected point, absence rule, tie, or ambiguity state. For urine it includes every accepted selected-urine row in `(t0-6h,t0]`. For balance it includes every accepted input/output row allocated to `[intime,t0]` and every gate-relevant ambiguous row in the eight bins. It never drops a late row and silently recomputes a favorable value. Clockless structural and ICD residuals remain unchanged and flagged, so the exact label is “all-storetime-bearing-gate-row database-recorded-time cohort,” not literally all-gate observable.

Thus `E_G⊆E_M⊆E_R`. Always report `R_G=|E_G|/|E_R|`, attrition by stream/gate, and whether G differs from M because of late rows that did not cross a gate threshold. G is the strict completeness sensitivity; M is the principal recording-time inferential robustness population.

For point measurements, a separate non-gating coherent-lag diagnostic requires `charttime<=storetime<=t0`. For interval rows it requires `starttime<=storetime<=t0`; the conservative cessation-confirmation diagnostic requires `endtime<=storetime<=t0`. Negative lags are reported, never automatically coerced or interpreted. Sparse or discordant coherent-lag results are measurement-limit findings, not treatment evidence.

## Falsification and result branches

A feasible branch is materially reversed if its categorical result branch differs from E_R, either airway bound moves by at least 0.05, or midpoint signs reverse with both absolute midpoint magnitudes at least 0.02.

- **Supportive-recorded** requires E_R feasibility and validity; no Holm-rejected adverse family; airway lower limit >+0.05; chart and continuous-ICU airway lower limits >0; RCR48/RCRD48 upper limits <+0.03; RRT7_M/RRTD7 upper limits <+0.05; terminal/true-mortality upper limits <+0.03; nonadverse disposition; J and R_M each >=0.90; and no material reversal in every feasible mandatory branch, including M and G when each meets minima. It supports external validation or randomization only.
- **Adverse-FWER** requires a prespecified adverse-oriented E_R statistic rejected after Holm with validity passing. It is a record-defined observational concern that may justify critical-care/nephrology review or de-prioritization, not proof of toxicity or harm.
- **Margin-falsified** requires no adjusted adverse family and the E_R airway upper limit <=+0.05. It rejects only the prespecified five-point recorded-proxy improvement, not every physiologic benefit.
- **Temporal-validity-inconclusive** applies when J<0.90, R_M<0.90, a feasible M pipeline materially reverses, or required late-conflict/concordance computation fails. Estimates remain descriptive.
- **Recording-time-inferential-infeasible** applies when M cannot meet its frozen inferential minima. Its funnel and concordance remain mandatory; no treatment direction follows, and this alone does not invalidate the explicitly retrospective E_R estimate.
- **All-row measurement-limit** applies when G is sparse/infeasible, its retained fraction is low, or coherent-lag diagnostics are discordant while M remains valid. This describes database timestamp completeness only.
- **Validity-inconclusive** applies to any other required source, ambiguity, positivity, calibration, balance, observation, renal ascertainment, timing, process, bootstrap/Holm, or material-sensitivity failure.
- **Statistically inconclusive** is every other feasible, validity-passing result. It does not mean no effect, equivalence, or safety.
