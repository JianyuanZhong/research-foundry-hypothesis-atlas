## 1. Unresolved question, importance, and advance over the closest inspected works
The parent closed a circularity gap and added a monocyte-only, in-vitro MerTK perturbation. Its own
assessment records the untested gap: the perturbation is **in-vitro monocytes**, not synovial tissue,
and it treats E-MTAB-8873 only as a pseudobulk/targeted-panel composition confound.

Question: In RA, does macrophage **presence** shift the **within-cell FLS (synovial-fibroblast) state**,
or is the observed co-culture difference explained by **macrophage abundance/composition** alone?

- Leading claim **H1 (directional communication, D3-M2)**: adding macrophages changes the FLS cells' own
  expression program (a within-FLS, communication-induced state), independently of macrophage count.
- Strongest rival **H2 (composition/abundance)**: the co-culture difference reflects the fraction of
  macrophage vs FLS cells (a cell-mixture shift) plus ambient/technical effects, with ~0 residual
  within-FLS program after a *disjoint* composition adjustment. [K2]
- Rival **H3 (donor-state / technical artifact)**: the contrast tracks FLS donor identity, sequencing
  depth, or the fixed 445-feature targeted-panel scope rather than macrophage presence.

Clinical importance: RA drug-free remission vs treatment resistance is a consequential fork. If
macrophage presence causally reprograms FLS to a persistent (non-resolving) state, then the
**macrophage-FLS circuit** is a target; if the signal is composition/donor-state, blocking a single
macrophage mediator will lower inflammation without restoring resolution -- a different next decision. [K3]

Advance over closest inspected works:
- [K1] Alivernini 2020 associates remission with a MerTK+/CD206+ synovial macrophage subset, but
  **cross-sectionally**; it cannot separate subset abundance from an induced within-FLS program.
- [K2] Zhang 2023 (CTAPs) shows synovial **cell-abundance** classes predict response -- the strongest
  rival that a within-cell claim must exclude, but it is cross-sectional and provides no within-donor
  macrophage-presence perturbation.
- [K3] Wei & Xu 2026 propose a reciprocal stromal-myeloid "failed-resolution switch" but as a **review
  hypothesis**; it supplies no within-donor directionality.
This proposal adds what none provide: a **within-donor, tissue-relevant perturbation** (same FLS donor,
FLS-only vs FLS+macrophage) at **single-cell resolution**, with a data-driven composition axis that is
gene-disjoint from any curated module.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries, variables, outcomes
- Population: the >=5 RA FLS donors above with a within-donor FLS-only vs FLS+macrophage pair; cells =
  all cells in each record. Consumed as recorded sample-level replicates (no independent-donor claim).
- Temporal boundary: cross-sectional assay (fixed co-culture endpoint); no longitudinal-transition claim.
- Variables: exposure = macrophage presence (FLS-only vs FLS+macrophage) within FLS donor; cell-level

- Outcomes: (i) within-FLS program score (cell-level, FLS cells only); (ii) composition fraction
  (macrophage vs FLS); (iii) adjusted between-arm effect.

## 4. Method: simple baseline vs substantive learned/mechanistic alternative
- **Baseline:** pseudobulk group mean of FLS-only vs FLS+Mpos with a curated resolution module score
  (mean log2CPM), donor-paired; a straightforward, interpretable comparison.
- **Substantive alternative (what the baseline loses):** a **donor-aware cell-level decomposition** --
  `expression ~ within-FLS-program term + data-driven composition term + donor random effect + log(n_umi)`,
  fit with leave-one-donor-out. The **composition axis is built data-driven and gene-DISJOINT** from the
  curated module (HVG/embedding clustering with the curated genes removed; explicit gene-set-overlap
  diagnostic) so the two terms are not the same construct. The alternative separates an *induced
  within-FLS state* from *abundance*, which the pseudobulk mean cannot: pseudobulk conflates FLS-cell
  state with the macrophage/FLS mixing ratio.
- **Deferred branch:** a nonlinear conditional-VAE / graph communication model -- deferred because the
  fixed ~445-feature panel and small donor count make over-parameterization the dominant risk; revisit
  only if the linear decomposition shows a plausible within-FLS term with residual donor structure.
- Budget: CPU-only, small (per-cell matrices are small; the panel is 445 features); approximate 10-20 CPU-min
  for the full leave-one-donor-out decomposition. No GPU needed.

## 5. Supportive / adverse / inconclusive outcomes and falsification
- **Supportive:** a reproducible within-FLS program term that survives disjoint composition adjustment and
  donor/hold-out (consistent sign across >=4/5 FLS donors) -> macrophage presence is associated with an
  FLS-intrinsic state.
- **Adverse:** the within-FLS term collapses after disjoint composition adjustment, or the "signal" is
  explained by donor identity or depth -> composition/donor-state, supporting H2/H3.
- **Inconclusive:** wide CIs / donor-inconsistent sign / panel lacks key genes -> cannot separate H1/H2.
- **Falsifier:** if a gene-disjoint composition term absorbs the effect (residual ~0) OR the effect sign is
  donor-inconsistent, H1 is not supported by these data.

## 7. Honest limits and required clinical/expert adjudication
- Fixed ~445-feature panel; **unmeasured genes are unavailable** (a targeted panel cannot test absent
  ligands/receptors). Cell-type assignment from per-cell matrices needs protocol reconciliation.
- Co-culture is not whole synovium; **co-expression is not causation** (seed D3-M2 caution). The
  inhibitor arms add a pharmacological perturbation but MerTK-inhibitor selectivity/target engagement
  is **untested here** and needs expert review / wet-lab.
- Small donor count (>=5 FLS donors, duplicated macrophage donors) -> dependence must be modeled; an
  imprecise null would not refute a real effect.
- These data are cross-sectional; a transition/dynamics claim needs a longitudinal study.
