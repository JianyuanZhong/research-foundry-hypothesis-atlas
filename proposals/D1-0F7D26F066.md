# Outcome-ascertainment audit: discordant-AKI recovery

Status: proposed successor design; no cohort scan, model fit, or scientific result was run.

Parent audited: [prior hypothesis].  
Purpose: resolve the remaining uncertainty that the 48-hour discordant-versus-concordant contrast may be created by conditioning on survival/continued ICU observation, residual pre-landmark severity, or unequal recording opportunity.

## 1. Scientific opening and substantive change

Status: candidate-ready proposed Harbor experiment; no cohort scan, model fit, or scientific result was run.

Parent audited: [prior hypothesis]. It already addresses survivor-landmark selection, residual severity, sparse urine recording, and dynamic persistence. The remaining consequential ambiguity is outcome ascertainment: its primary seven-day union combines hospital death, observable through admissions, with ICU-recorded RRT, observable only while the index ICU stay continues. A positive union could therefore reflect ICU destination or surveillance rather than a renal-support signal.

The strongest available evidence supports only bounded claims. [K1] reports creatinine eGFR overestimation during prolonged critical illness and muscle loss, but not this MIMIC phenotype. [K2] shows that urine-output windows and thresholds change AKI mortality prediction, but not recovery discordance or treatment effect. [K3] associates positive recorded balance with ICU AKI/mortality while noting omitted insensible losses and clinician-dependent KRT, but does not establish true volume status or this comparison.

The unresolved claim remains prognostic, not causal:

> Among adults reaching a specified post-AKI ICU landmark with creatinine recovery and a classifiable urine/recorded-volume state, does discordance predict excess short-term hospital death, ICU-recorded RRT, or both over concordant recovery after measured severity and observation opportunity are accounted for?

This child makes hospital death and ICU-recorded RRT separate component estimands with different competing events and observation boundaries; the old union is exploratory only. It can distinguish a broad fatal-risk association from an ICU-surveillance-limited renal-support association. It cannot identify true GFR, complete ward RRT, total-body volume, treatment intent, mechanism, or a causal effect.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries, and estimands

Use adults with anchor_age >=18, the first eligible ICU stay per hospital admission, and valid icustays.intime < outtime, joined on (subject_id, hadm_id). Define t0 as the earliest valid creatinine observation within 72 hours of ICU intime meeting the parent KDIGO-like increase: at least 0.3 mg/dL over a value in the prior 48 hours or at least 1.5 times the valid minimum in the prior 7 days. Keep an admission-baseline sensitivity when no prior value supports onset; never invent baseline. Exclude established RRT before t0. Use patient-relative clinical times.

For L in {24,48,72} hours after t0, R_L contains patients alive, in the index ICU, and without incident outcome-defining RRT at L. Report deaths, ICU exits, and RRT before L. The 48-hour result is a survivor/ICU-landmark prognostic estimand, not an onset-cohort or causal estimand.

Exposure uses only [L-24h,L]. Use the most recent creatinine in [L-12h,L] and peak creatinine in [t0,L]; recovery is peak minus landmark >=0.3 mg/dL, with peak/landmark ratio <=0.8 sensitivity. Use the parent mutually exclusive concordant, low-urine-only, positive-balance-only, both, unknown/insufficient, and no-recovery states. The primary exposure remains discordant = low urine OR positive recorded balance; unknown is never imputed.

All outcome windows are half-open [L,L+7 days):

- Hospital death D_L,7 is admissions.deathtime in the window before an alive admissions.dischtime and before day 7. Alive discharge is a competing event; alive and hospitalized at day 7 is administrative censoring. hospital_expire_flag checks consistency and does not supply an event time when deathtime is absent. Missing-time or inconsistent records are exceptions, not silently assigned events.
- ICU-recorded RRT R_L,7 is the first audited procedureevents RRT item with starttime in the window and before index icustays.outtime, while alive and in the index ICU. ICU discharge and hospital death before RRT are competing events. Ward-only RRT and post-outtime RRT are unavailable for this endpoint.
- The parent death-or-ICU-RRT union U_L,7 is exploratory and must show both component counts and its mixed ascertainment.

For D, the estimand is death under the observed discharge process. For R, it is ICU-recorded RRT before index-ICU exit/death, not all hospital RRT. Use Aalen-Johansen cumulative incidence and/or cause-specific six-hour discrete-time hazards with the stated competing events; do not treat discharge as ordinary noninformative censoring for R. Standardize contrasts over observed R_L.

## 4. Landmark-selection and post-landmark ascertainment audit

Report parent retention flows at t0, 24, 48, and 72 hours: alive/in-index-ICU, death, ICU discharge, incident RRT, missing creatinine, urine opportunity, balance opportunity, and classifiable states. Plot retention, outcome-before-landmark, unknown state, and each component contrast across L. A contrast appearing only after sharp loss of high-risk patients or after dropping unknowns is not stable evidence.

For every L and exposure state, add separate post-landmark tables for hospital death, alive discharge, ICU-recorded RRT, ICU discharge before RRT, death before RRT, administrative day-7 completion, and death-time exceptions. ICU discharge is a competing event for R_L,7 but does not erase an observable later hospital death for D_L,7. Do not pool these clocks or imply uniform ascertainment of U_L,7.

The 24-to-48 trajectory uses only pre-24/pre-48 windows and outcomes after 48. Patients with an earlier outcome remain in the flow and are not assigned a transition. Use cluster bootstrap or influence-function inference because a patient can contribute to multiple landmarks.

## 5. Residual severity and observation-intensity rivals

### Residual severity

The leading rival is that the discordant group is simply more severely ill at or before the landmark. For each L, fit and report:

- an unadjusted standardized state-specific risk;
- a prespecified severity-adjusted model using onset AKI stage, age, sex, admission type, pre-exposure baseline creatinine, creatinine immediately before the [L-24,L] window, peak creatinine through L, pre-window urine/output and recorded-volume summaries, pre-window RRT/support indicators, and ICU timing;
- stratum-specific estimates across bounded categories of pre-window creatinine, AKI stage, and support intensity, with no estimate reported for empty or sparse cells;
- a sensitivity excluding patients with RRT in (t0,L) and a sensitivity that treats pre-L RRT/death as selection events rather than silently including them in a post-L risk contrast.

All such adjustment is prognostic standardization, not causal control. Variables measured in the exposure window are not claimed to be pre-exposure confounders; their use is a residual-severity sensitivity, and the exact timing must be reported. Do not adjust for post-L data, future outcome proxies, or the learned model’s predictions.

A contrast that vanishes in every well-supported severity stratum supports the residual-severity explanation. A contrast that survives some strata is still only an association; it does not prove intrinsic kidney injury, dilution, congestion, or muscle loss.

### Observation and measurement process

Use exact numeric observations, not imputed zeros:

- For primary urine, numeric mL values in `icu/outputevents` from itemids 226559 Foley, 226560 Void, 226561 Condom Cath, 226563 Suprapubic, and 226567 Straight Cath.
- Count zero-valued numeric output as an observed zero. A missing six-hour bin is unknown, not zero.
- Define urine observation opportunity as the number of distinct six-hour bins in [L-24,L] containing at least one valid primary-set output record. The primary classifiable urine definition requires at least 3 of 4 bins; sensitivities require at least 2 and all 4. Report the exact chosen rule and the other two sensitivities.
- Convert urine to mL/kg/h using the nearest valid weight at or before L, within 48 hours. Weights are `chartevents` itemids 224639, 226512, and 226531 with unit conversion verified from `icu/d_items`.
- Exclude nephrostomy 226564/226565, OR/PACU 226627/226631, mixed GU-irrigant 226566/227489, text estimate 226713, and Apache-derived 227519 from the primary phenotype; report them only in sensitivity/measurement-error outputs.
- For recorded balance in [L-24,L], use one mutually exclusive administered-volume rule from `inputevents`: never sum `amount` and `totalamount` from one row. Retain item-level composition, unresolved/non-mL fractions, and missingness. Recorded balance is input proxy minus direct urine output, not true fluid balance.
- Count output, creatinine, input, weight/CVP, and non-renal chart/lab observations in four six-hour bins. Use `charttime`/clinical time for the primary process counts; `storetime` ordering is a labeled sensitivity because documentation time is not an availability truth.

The process-only falsification check is prespecified: replace kidney discordance by quartiles of pre-landmark non-renal documentation density (non-creatinine `labevents` count plus non-weight/CVP `chartevents` count, with input/output event opportunity counts reported separately), and estimate its association with the same Y outcome. This is a diagnostic for acuity/recording intensity, not a valid negative-control outcome and not evidence that documentation causes death. If the process-only gradient is as large as the kidney contrast and the kidney contrast attenuates with process strata, the clinical interpretation is weakened. A post-L non-renal documentation count may be reported only as a descriptive downstream process endpoint among subjects event-free at L; it cannot be used as a primary adjustment variable.

The explicit unknown state is essential. Compare its size and outcome rate with classifiable states. If unknown patients are high risk, complete-case discordant-versus-concordant results are selection-sensitive. Do not impute unknown urine as low or normal in the primary analysis.

## 6. Dynamic trajectory estimand

Among subjects classifiable at both 24 and 48 hours, estimate hospital-death and ICU-RRT cumulative incidence separately for concordant-to-concordant, discordant-to-discordant, discordant-to-concordant, concordant-to-discordant, component transitions when supported, and transitions involving unknown. The parent 48-hour binary contrast is the baseline, now reported as two component estimates plus exploratory U_L,7.

## 9. Falsification and interpretation

Support requires a clinically nontrivial, directionally consistent component contrast across 24/48/72 hours, survival of supported severity and recording strata, adequate unknown-state behavior, and greater risk for persistent than resolving discordance. The supported claim is only a prognostic marker for the component(s) that replicate: death does not prove persistent kidney dysfunction, and ICU-RRT does not estimate ward or total-hospital RRT. It would motivate prospective validation with measured GFR/cystatin C, biomarkers, complete urine/fluid measures, treatment intent, and ward-RRT capture, while remaining noncausal.

Adverse evidence includes disappearance across landmarks/strata, reversal, unknown-driven results, a documentation-density gradient comparable to the kidney contrast, a union positive only because ICU surveillance differs, death without ICU-RRT (weakening renal-support interpretation), or signal only in contaminated definitions. This weakens the recovery-discordance claim but does not prove creatinine validity, harmless fluid management, or no treatment effect.

Inconclusive evidence includes sparse RRT/transition cells, wide clinically meaningful intervals, unstable windows, large unknown states, death-time exceptions, incomplete ascertainment, discordant components without power to distinguish them, or a small learned-model gain without a reproducible component distinction. Computationally checkable outputs are cohort construction, joins, timing, filters, event/competing-event counts, estimates, uncertainty, and held-out metrics. True kidney function, complete fluid collection, treatment intent, ward RRT, mechanism, causality, and transportability require adjudication or another study; automatic verification cannot establish them.

## 12. Alternatives not chosen and revision decision

- Fixed 48-hour death-or-ICU-RRT union as primary: rejected because its components have different post-landmark observation boundaries; it remains exploratory.
- Death alone: rejected because it would discard a clinically relevant but bounded ICU-support signal.
- Ordinary censoring of ICU discharge for RRT: rejected because discharge is informative and is an observation process; it is a competing event.
- Inverse-probability correction for ward RRT/discharge: deferred because ward RRT capture, treatment intent, and defensible informative-discharge models are unavailable.
- A single complex sequence model as primary: deferred because prediction alone cannot resolve measurement or ascertainment; it remains secondary.
- Causal treatment analysis: not selected because treatment intent, complete fluid exposure, ward RRT, and informative discharge/death are unavailable.
- All AKI-onset patients with imputed later state: rejected because pre-landmark death/discharge makes later state undefined.

Revisit deferred branches with an adjudicated cohort containing complete urine/fluid collection, cystatin C or measured GFR, treatment intent, ward-RRT capture, and prospective discharge status, or with external prospective landmark follow-up. Abandon the direction after a well-powered observation-complete validation shows no reproducible component or trajectory contrast.
