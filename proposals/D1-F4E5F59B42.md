## Strongest supported evidence, unresolved claim, and hypothesis

Available MIMIC evidence supports only that a reproducible first-eligible admission frame can be constructed from exact admission-linked radiology text and that discharge, service, ICU, and storage-time proxies can be joined. It does not establish that a stored discharge note was visible at physical departure, that a service code identifies the responsible team, that an expert label is clinically correct, or that a route improves care.

The unresolved claim is whether a support-qualified atom that passes the parent's split-view audit also passes an outcome-blind, whole-admission challenge designed to expose shared codebook and shared-text errors. The falsifiable hypothesis is: every atom placed on an `AUTO` or `RECON` allow-list will pass all inherited minimax gates **and** the independent holistic challenge gates, including adverse-compatible disagreement bounds, while predefined adversarial pairs show no route-defining invariance violation and no route change under route-irrelevant perturbation. Any supported atom failing either challenge is excluded even if the parent's component panels and pooled estimates pass. Unsupported/rare/unknown atoms remain unreleasable `DEFER`, not negative evidence.

## Blinded holistic panels and distinct codebooks

For each challenge admission, create two whole-admission packets independently from the sealed raw extract. Each packet contains reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain, permitted admission/death/disposition fields, canonical DS and its permitted detail artifact, and explicit missingness/time metadata, but never parent component labels, routes, strata, atom identity, weights, post-`D` text, POE, or outcomes. The two panels have disjoint rosters, independent packet ordering, independent pseudonyms, and no communication. Panel H1 uses codebook `CB-H1`; H2 uses a separately written `CB-H2` with independently worded definitions, independently ordered labels, distinct examples, and no shared training cases. Both must record atomic target/action/timing/recipient/responsibility/polarity/replacement/conflict, unit inventory, departure artifact status, and whole-admission route recommendation plus confidence/unknown. Codebooks are frozen and hashed before packet generation; no codebook is tuned after seeing disagreement or route results. No panel sees any parent label or the other panel's output.

A senior holistic challenge panel H3, also roster-disjoint and blinded, receives the same packet only after H1/H2 labels are append-only sealed. H3 sees no routes or component labels and adjudicates only whether each observed textual route-defining assertion is supported, contradicted, or indeterminate by the supplied artifact. H3 is not a truth oracle: its labels are an additional measurement system. H3 cannot resolve disagreement by consensus deletion; all three outputs remain available for compatible-set analysis.

## Adversarial counterfactual and negative-pair controls

Generate controls before labels using a locked transformation manifest and store transformed text separately from source text. Controls never alter the primary MIMIC labels or estimands. For each feasible challenge case, include:

1. a **route-irrelevant negative pair** that preserves target/action/timing/polarity and all route-relevant spans while permuting unrelated section order or replacing a non-target, non-temporal sentence with a provenance-matched sentence from another admission; expected route and unit inventory must remain unchanged;
2. a **route-decisive negative pair** that changes exactly one locked atomic field (for example explicit replacement versus no replacement, or ample versus under-24-hour opportunity) while preserving all other text; expected route must change only as specified by the locked route table;
3. a **counterfactual omission/addition pair** in which a recommendation or explicit discharge-plan statement is masked or inserted at a prespecified location, with the expected compatibility-set transition frozen before review; and
4. a **nuisance-feature pair** changing author/signature-like or formatting tokens while preserving semantics, where no route change is allowed.

Transformations are applied only when the requisite spans and times are unambiguous under a pre-label parser; otherwise the case is marked control-infeasible, never hand-edited. H1/H2 review randomized pair order and are blinded to the transformation type and expected answer. A route-invariant pair is a **negative control**; a route-decisive pair is a **positive control** for sensitivity, not evidence of clinical correctness. Any observed change in a route-invariant pair, or any change outside the locked route table for a decisive pair, is a falsification signal. Controls cannot rescue an atom with poor real-case purity.

Do not fit a latent-class model as a primary analysis: without an external gold standard, conditional independence is not identifiable and shared expert error cannot be estimated as a point prevalence. Instead report sharp compatible bounds. For each atom and route, define observed panel outputs `(H1,H2,H3)` and the parent's sealed component outputs. The lower endpoint assumes only jointly observed support is valid; the upper endpoint assigns every unresolved or disagreement pattern to the adverse route-compatible state. Report interval bounds for holistic agreement, parent-versus-holistic discordance, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, `leak_AUTO`, route capture, and workload. A sensitivity family may vary a prespecified dependence parameter only as a transparent assumption analysis; it cannot alter certification. If a separately blinded expert reference subset is obtained, use it only as an external measurement subset and state its ascertainment; do not call it a gold standard.

A useful sensitivity is a three-rater latent-class model only if an independently justified conditional-independence or validation constraint is documented before labels and the model has finite-sample identifiability. Otherwise the model is diagnostic, and its estimates cannot narrow adverse bounds or certify an atom. The primary report must include a Fréchet-style worst-case shared-error envelope, exact finite-population inversion for binary challenge outcomes, and 20,000 Rao-Wu replicates retaining all admission-level labels together. Use one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over inherited gates, atom gates, holistic disagreement, control invariance, and minimax endpoints; missing challenge labels, packet corruption, control infeasibility, or unknowns are adverse-compatible, not complete-case deletions.

## Interpretation and evidence ceiling

Supportive results require all inherited gates, at least one fully passing support-qualified atom, and the holistic/control gates for every atom in the stated allow-list. They support only a prospective within-site silent validation of those exact observable MIMIC signatures, with all other signatures forced to `DEFER`.

Adverse results include a holistic disagreement or shared-error envelope crossing a gate, a route-invariant control flip, decisive-control failure, domain-specific workload failure, or a supported atom whose adverse compatible labels can make `AUTO` unsafe. That atom is excluded despite favorable component agreement. Inconclusive results include fewer than required challenge cases, sparse route events, infeasible controls, nonresponse, broken seals, wide partial-identification bounds, or absent identifiability conditions. Inconclusive is not evidence of safety or transport.

The MIMIC records cannot establish actual viewing, finalization/signature semantics, physical departure visibility, communication, order/referral execution, responsibility, patient preferences, outside care, malignancy, appropriateness, follow-up completion, harm, utility, causal effects, or external/forward-calendar transport. Stored text reproducibility is not clinical truth. A successful retrospective challenge must be followed by a separately approved prospective silent bridge using actual dates and observed viewing/edit/sign/transmission, responsibility, orders/referrals, scheduling, outside-plan, and outcome evidence; novel workflows remain `DEFER`. Only further expert-governed prospective work could evaluate live safety, utility, or benefit.
