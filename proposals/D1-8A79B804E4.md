## 1. Unresolved question, clinical importance, and the advance
After liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / ablation / resection) for HCC, clinicians serialise serum AFP and must decide when to re-image. Two explanations compete for a post-LDT AFP rise inside the imaging-negative landmark stratum (IMGneg):
- **H_tumour (leading claim):** the decay-completed plateau (tumour-burden "set-point", mu_i) carries the prognostic signal; a rise in mu_i predicts imminent local progression.
- **H_release (strongest rival):** the visible rise is a benign **periprocedural release pulse** (necrosis / liver injury / regeneration), amplitude A_i, uncorrelated with tumour burden; the parent branch's all-draw slope would then be procedural artefact.
These are separable because they predict differently: H_tumour -> mu_i HR>1 with weak A_i; H_release -> mu_i HR~1 while A_i carries the signal. A one-number OLS slope cannot distinguish them.
**Substantive advance over the parent:** the parent declared mu_i *identifiability* unverified (its own inconclusive arm) and its prior child had MEASURED the decay-clean filter to be non-estimable (0.37% eligibility). This child replaces the unverified promise with a *pre-committed, measured gate* and keeps the simple baseline (M0 OLS slope) alongside the mechanistic alternative (M_pulse), so "unidentifiable" is a decision to park the branch, not an unfalsified claim. It also carries a new **bounded measured probe** (Section 4A) showing draw-count evaluability is materially non-zero (not a full identifiability proof).

## 2. Inherited frozen design (verbatim from parent)
- **Population:** HCC diagnosis (diagnoses `诊断名称`) AND >=1 liver-directed procedure (procedures `手术`).
- **Index** = earliest qualifying LDT `开始时间`; **t\*** = index + 180 d (90 d sensitivity).
- **Exposure (parent M0):** per-patient OLS slope of log10(AFP) over draws with `检验时间` in (index, t\*]; AFP = labs `检验`='甲胎蛋白', numeric `定量结果`; **same-draw** pairing of albumin + total bilirubin within ±2 d of `检验时间`; ALBI = 0.66·log10(bilirubin) − 0.085·albumin; AFP/albumin/bilirubin look-alike exclusions.
- **Landmark imaging status** over examinations `开始时间` in [t\*−45 d, t\*+45 d]: IMGneg = qualifying abdominal study whose `检查所见` AND `检查诊断` contain none of {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多, 转移}; IMGpos otherwise; IMGmissing if none.
- **Outcome:** modality-restricted abdominal **local progression** (lexical event = any of {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多}) with `转移` as competing event; censor via encounters `出院时间`. Secondary: retreatment proxy.
- **Covariates/controls:** age, sex, baseline tumour/procedure variables, surveillance-intensity (draw count, imaging cadence), ALBI-slope negative control; four rivals; conjunctive falsification; **no survival / mRECIST claim**.

## 4. The ONE added element (pre-specified): measured set-point identifiability gate + explicit baseline-vs-mechanistic comparison
**Method comparison (recorded before selection).**
- **M0 (baseline, simple):** per-patient all-draw within-window OLS slope of log10(AFP); classical, transparent, but confounds benign periprocedural release with tumour burden and cannot separate them.
- **M_pulse (mechanistic alternative):** per-patient periprocedural pulse–decay nonlinear mixed model on ALL draws in (index, index+365 d]: log10 AFP = mu_i + A_i·exp(−tau/lambda) + beta·x + eps, tau = days since most recent preceding LDT `开始时间`, lambda fixed at 5 d; returns the decay-completed **set-point mu_i** as exposure and retains the **release amplitude A_i as rival exposure**. What M_pulse recovers that M0 loses: separation of benign periprocedural release from underlying burden level, estimable under draw clustering.

**4A. Measured bounded probe (this episode; see `work/lead-46/probe-46-draw-structure.txt`).** In a bounded first-350 MB scan of the labs file (5,826,632 rows scanned; not the full file) there were 42,502 AFP rows among 10,662 unique patients; **6,675 (62.6%) carried >=3 AFP draws; median 3, max 31.** This raw-count gate shows evaluability is not zero, which is *necessary but not sufficient* for set-point identifiability. It does not establish draws inside (index, index+180 d], decay-tail separation, or outcome events.

**4B. Pre-committed go/no-go identifiability gate (solver's first output).** Compute on the FULL snapshot: (a) fraction of patients with >=3 AFP draws in (index, index+180 d]; (b) fraction with >=1 draw in the decay tail (>=7 d after the most recent preceding procedure) so mu_i is separated from A_i; (c) IMGneg eligibility after these gates. Gate PASS = (a)>=50% of the parent population, (b)>=40%, (c) IMGneg events >=30.

**Primary test.** Landmark Cox of local progression WITHIN IMGneg on mu_i (per 1 log10), release amplitude A_i retained as rival, plus a 1-df LRT (M_pulse vs M0) and a surveillance-intensity adjustment.
- **Supportive (H_tumour):** mu_i HR>1, 95% CI excludes 1, >=30 events, dAIC>10 vs M0, survives A_i and surveillance-intensity adjustment.
- **Adverse = R_release (H_release):** mu_i HR~1 while A_i HR>1, or dAIC<=0 -> the parent slope signal is periprocedural artefact.
- **Inconclusive:** gate (4B) FAILS (<50% >=3-draw eligible, or <40% decay-tail, or IMGneg events <30, or IMGmissing>50%) -> the branch should be parked, not re-asserted.
- **Precise-null:** mu_i HR within ±0.10 of 1 with tight CI and flat ALBI-slope negative control.

## 5. Falsification, alternatives, and honest limits
- **Falsification:** the conjunction in 4B/primary test; a measured gate FAIL is itself a decisive (adverse-to-feasibility) outcome.
- **Rivals:** R_release (above); R_selection (draw eligibility selects a different population than M0); R_surveillance (imaging/draw intensity confounds detection); R_regression-to-mean (slope inflated by measurement noise around the procedure).
