# Later same-encounter laboratory persistence after the frozen early post-repeat-TACE panel

## Clinical question and evidence boundary

The assessed parent established that the clinically consequential 30-day hepatic-decompensation endpoint is not supported in this HCC snapshot: the conservative recorded endpoint has zero qualifying events, and routine later encounters cannot be treated as escalation. The remaining falsifiable laboratory question is narrower:

> In the frozen 131-episode cohort with an exact common albumin/total-bilirubin panel at the fixed 12–36-hour landmark after the independently selected repeat TACE, is the recorded albumin-down/total-bilirubin-up direction still present at a later common panel in the same repeat-TACE encounter, or is it a short-lived peri-procedural observation?

The strongest supported claim before this child is only that 86/131 observed early panels have albumin strictly below and total bilirubin strictly above the contemporaneous pre-repeat panel. That is a laboratory direction, not hepatic failure, decompensation, toxicity, recovery, safety, treatment response, or a clinical outcome. The unresolved claim is whether the direction is recorded again at a later, comparable panel after the early landmark. This child does not infer biology when a later measurement is absent and does not claim that a recorded late direction represents clinical harm.

## Executable primary experiment

The estimand is a descriptive/prognostic laboratory persistence contrast among the 41 episodes selected by the locked late-panel availability rule, not a causal treatment effect:

- For each assay separately, outcome is the absolute numeric value at the selected later common timestamp (`late_albumin` or `late_bilirubin`). No late-minus-early change score is the primary outcome.
- The primary exposure is `early_signal`, with `arm` retained as a prespecified covariate, not interpreted as treatment. Include the corresponding early landmark level (`y_albumin` or `y_bilirubin`) and corresponding pre-repeat level (`p_albumin` or `p_bilirubin`) as adjustment variables, plus age and sex only if available and nonmissing. Do not include a signal-by-arm interaction as a confirmatory model because only 14 systemic late-panel episodes exist.
- Fit two pre-specified parsimonious assay-specific linear models, using complete covariates and deterministic outcome-blind preprocessing. Report the adjusted early-signal coefficient, arm coefficient, sample size, residual diagnostics, and uncertainty from a patient bootstrap that resamples episodes within arm. If normality is untenable, report a robust-regression sensitivity but do not change the primary estimand. Do not standardize across assays or interpret raw values clinically across assays.
- The primary persistence question is supported only if both assay-specific early-signal coefficients have the same direction, their bootstrap intervals exclude zero, the direction survives the locked timing sensitivity `>36–120h`, and the result is not driven by one episode under leave-one-out influence checks. This is a stringent exploratory rule, not a clinical threshold.
- Directional persistence is secondary and descriptive: report the proportion with late albumin below early albumin and late bilirubin above early bilirubin, and the proportion with the same joint direction as early signal, by arm and pooled with exact binomial intervals. Do not call a late direction recovery, toxicity, decompensation, or benefit.

If only one assay has enough complete covariate support, that assay can be reported as a one-assay exploratory result; it cannot be promoted to a corroborated two-assay conclusion. If model support is too sparse or separation/collinearity prevents stable estimation, report negative feasibility rather than widening the window or altering the cohort.

## Baselines, selection, and uncertainty

The contemporaneous early value is the key assay-specific baseline; `P` is retained to anchor the pre-event state and expose the information already used to define the early direction. Age, sex, arm, event timing, and exact late-panel timing are descriptive covariates. No weighting is allowed to turn the 41 observed panels into all 131 episodes. Availability is itself selected by same-encounter laboratory measurement, likely related to length of stay and care process; the 41-episode estimand is explicitly conditional on a recorded late panel.

Use cluster-free episode bootstrap because the frozen design selects one episode per patient; if patient identity can repeat in a source row, deduplicate by the frozen episode map before fitting. Report arm-specific denominators, missing covariate counts, panel timing, specimen-type distributions, and discharge opportunity. Assay units/reference ranges, infusion/transfusion/fluid context, TACE extent, tumor burden, Child-Pugh/MELD components, clinician interpretation, outside-hospital events, mortality, and verified procedure/medication administration remain unavailable.

## Falsification and negative controls

1. **Reconstruction falsification:** any mismatch in frozen counts, source hashes, exact parser, duplicate resolution, event encounter, or early timestamp selector invalidates the experiment.
2. **Timing falsification:** compare the primary `>36–96h` result with the locked `>36–120h` sensitivity; a materially different direction or loss of support is timing-sensitive, not supportive.
3. **Selection falsification:** report the full 131 denominator, 41/131 late-panel availability, arm and early-signal availability, and discharge-by-panel opportunity. A conclusion about all 131 episodes is forbidden. If late observation is strongly arm- or signal-dependent, state selection limitation and downgrade.
4. **Panel integrity falsification:** verify exactly two assays at one timestamp, same event encounter, no inequalities, no unresolved discordant duplicates, and one selected panel per episode. A single-assay or mixed-encounter panel cannot enter the primary analysis.
5. **Placebo-clock negative control:** repeat the same common-panel extraction in the same event encounter for a pre-event pseudo-window 36–96 hours before `event_time`, using the identical assay parser and deterministic nearest-to-80-hour rule relative to the pseudo-origin. It is a measurement-process check only; an apparent early-signal contrast in this pre-event window indicates residual selection/timing structure and blocks a strong persistence interpretation. This control is not allowed to change the primary late window.
6. **Influence falsification:** leave-one-out estimates and arm-stratified bootstrap; one or two observations cannot determine the conclusion.
7. **Unit/clinical falsification:** if source-specific units or reference ranges are discovered during compilation, preserve them as metadata and do not silently pool or reinterpret prior numeric values; clinical threshold claims remain blocked.

## Interpretation branches

- **Supportive:** both assay-specific adjusted late-level contrasts point in the same direction, both uncertainty intervals exclude zero, timing sensitivity agrees, the placebo clock is negative, and influence/selection diagnostics pass. Conclude only that the early recorded direction is associated with a persistent difference in later recorded assay levels among the 41 observed same-encounter panels. This motivates external replication with adjudicated clinical context; it does not establish persistence in unobserved episodes or clinical harm.
- **Adverse/transient:** later levels do not retain the early-direction association, or both assays move consistently opposite the early direction with adequate uncertainty. Conclude that the proposed recorded persistence is not supported in this selected observed subset; do not claim recovery or safety.
- **Precise null:** both assay-specific intervals lie inside a locked, unit-specific standardized null region chosen before value inspection, or a pre-registered standardized effect margin after assay-wise training-only scaling is available. This rules out only the specified recorded-level contrast in the observed subset, not biological change or clinical outcomes. Because units are absent, a clinical null margin is not available here; use a statistical standardized margin only if the compiler pre-registers it before fitting.
- **Inconclusive:** intervals span meaningful positive, adverse, and null directions; only one assay supports; covariate support is too sparse; timing or selection sensitivity is material; placebo control is positive; or influence is excessive. Report estimates and feasibility without choosing a persistence conclusion.
- **Falsified:** reconstruction, exact common timestamp, same-encounter requirement, numeric parser, duplicate rule, or locked-window implementation fails. No scientific interpretation follows.

## Substantive advance and conclusion

Relative to the negative-feasibility parent, this child does not broaden the unavailable endpoint or manufacture a care-escalation proxy. It uses the only nearby outcome that is concretely supported: a later, exact, common albumin/total-bilirubin panel in the same repeat-TACE encounter. It locks timing from a value-blinded full-source audit, quantifies sparse support and discharge opportunity, and makes absolute later assay levels—not mathematically coupled change scores—the primary estimands. The result can falsify or support only a recorded laboratory persistence association conditional on later observation. If execution confirms inadequate support for stable two-assay inference, the honest result is negative feasibility and the parent remains preferable; no clinical safety, toxicity, recovery, decompensation, or monitoring-benefit conclusion is licensed.
