# Provisional hypothesis: macrophage context leaves a reproducible FLS transcript signature

## Proposed relationship

In the frozen E-MTAB-8873 targeted human FLS/coculture assay, the measured FLS transcript state differs reproducibly between MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage coculture contexts, beyond the recorded cell count and the complete set of contributing donors. This is a context-associated hypothesis, not a causal claim that macrophages directly alter a specific pathway.

No expression contrast or fitted model has been executed in this episode. Therefore this draft makes no biological association claim beyond the assay's documented design.

## Falsifiable test

Use the none-treatment Mpos versus Mneg samples as the primary contrast, retaining the complete contributing-donor sets as the analysis unit and excluding or separately handling any malformed donor record rather than treating samples as independent. Compare a donor-aware baseline that adjusts for recorded cell count and donor-set composition against a structured alternative that permits a reproducible context effect across features. Report feature-level uncertainty, dependence-aware uncertainty, and the fraction of features whose direction is consistent across donors.

Supportive evidence would be a reproducible, uncertainty-bounded context contrast that persists after donor-set and cell-count adjustment. Adverse evidence would be no reproducible contrast, or a contrast confined to one donor/technical replicate and explained by the recorded covariates. Inconclusive evidence would be an imprecise estimate, unresolved feature-map ambiguity, or insufficient independent donor information; this would not refute the hypothesis.

## Uncertainties and missing evidence

The assay is targeted, so it cannot establish whole-tissue composition or the status of unmeasured genes. The design does not by itself establish a direct macrophage-to-FLS mechanism, and the available metadata may not resolve all protocol or feature-instance ambiguities. The three inspected key works with frozen receipts, a complete life-science experiment card, and an executed contrast remain to be completed. Clinical adjudication and wet-lab validation would be required for any stronger mechanistic or treatment claim.
