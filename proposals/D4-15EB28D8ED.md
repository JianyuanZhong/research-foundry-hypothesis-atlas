# Assay-certified, selection-aware baseline prioritization for repeat kidney-function assessment

## Lineage and substantive change

This proposal is a substantive child of assessed `[prior hypothesis]` (repairable) and `[prior hypothesis]` (valid). It preserves the frozen UK Biobank snapshot, one-to-one `eid` joins, baseline-only deployment boundary, schedule-marginal first-repeat outcome, fixed-capacity and assumption-indexed estimands, cross-fitting, paired simultaneous bootstrap, and fold-reconstructed empirical null procedure. It repairs four unresolved issues rather than silently changing them during compilation:

1. laboratory field/assay certification and field-specific missingness handling;
2. interpretation of the observed first-repeat interval as a schedule-marginal estimand rather than a fixed-horizon claim;
3. selection into complete first-repeat attendance, with an explicit descriptive/IPCW sensitivity that cannot be mistaken for identification of clinical utility; and
4. the secondary cystatin-C outcome and the limits of decision-curve thresholds.

The previously proposed ad hoc `Rule-Discordant` Boolean policy is removed. No hand-built rule is used as a comparator.

## Clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

Among UK Biobank participants with complete baseline kidney biomarkers and a complete first repeat assessment occurring 2--8 years later, does baseline cystatin-C/creatinine discordance add reproducible information for ranking a future repeat creatinine-equation eGFR threshold crossing, beyond sex, baseline age, and baseline creatinine-based eGFR?

The falsifiable predictive hypothesis is that the discordance-augmented baseline model (`M1`) has a positive incremental event-capture curve over a prespecified contiguous capacity region, with uncertainty excluding zero, stable across repeat folds and the prespecified eGFR strata, and not reproduced by the fold-aware label or predictor nulls. The corresponding clinical-utility hypothesis is **not identifiable here** and is not tested as a conclusion: UK Biobank does not observe the testing decision, costs, harms, treatment changes, patient preferences, measured GFR, or adjudicated CKD.

Existing evidence supports confirmation of an abnormal kidney marker and use of combined creatinine/cystatin-C estimates when creatinine-based GFR may be inaccurate and GFR affects a decision. That evidence does not establish a universal probability threshold, a UK Biobank-specific harm-to-benefit exchange rate, or that this repeat threshold crossing is CKD. This experiment therefore advances the narrower, computable question of baseline risk prioritization while explicitly separating it from benefit of ordering a test.

## Equations and outcomes

Use the race-free 2021 CKD-EPI creatinine equation. For serum creatinine `Scr` in mg/dL, age in years, `k=0.7, a=-0.241` for females and `k=0.9, a=-0.302` for males:

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^a * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^Age * 1.012^(female)`.

Use the prespecified cystatin-C-only equation, explicitly fixed before execution. For cystatin C `Scys` in the certified equation unit and age in years:

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.996^Age * 0.932^(female)`.

Define `Z = log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)`. The primary outcome is `Ycr=I(eGFRcr_1 < 60)`. The secondary assay-dependent outcome is `Ycys=I(eGFRcys_1 < 60)`, calculated only when certified repeat cystatin C and repeat age are valid; otherwise it is missing and is not imputed. The primary outcome uses repeat creatinine; repeat cystatin C is required for the inherited complete-repeat evidence boundary but does not enter baseline predictors or `Ycr`.

Neither outcome is measured GFR, adjudicated CKD, persistent CKD, kidney failure, treatment response, or a patient-important outcome. A threshold crossing after a variable interval may reflect biological change, measurement variation, or both.

## Baseline-only models and estimands

Fit, within each training fold, fixed L2 logistic models with no tuning, class weighting, or future variables:

- `M0`: sex, baseline age, and baseline eGFRcr, using the inherited fixed four-column natural-cubic-spline basis for age/eGFRcr plus sex.
- `M1`: `M0` plus one training-fold-standardized linear column `Z`.

All spline knots/boundaries, standardization, imputation (only in the sensitivity analysis), and fitting are training-only. Use ten deterministic outcome-stratified round-robin five-fold repeats. Produce paired out-of-fold risks for every `S_A` participant. Ties in ranking use a participant-independent stable ordering fixed before outcome inspection.

For `q=0.01,0.02,...,0.20` and the inherited `q=0.10` point, select the top `ceil(q*n)` risks. Report event capture, fraction of all events captured, PPV, and `DeltaC(q)=C_M1(q)-C_M0(q)`. The primary contrast is the full curve, not a post hoc capacity.

For the fixed probability grid `{0.001,0.0025,0.005,0.0075,0.010,0.015,0.020,0.030,0.040,0.050,0.075,0.100,0.150,0.200}`, report standard mathematical decision-curve quantities
`NB_m=TP_m/n - FP_m/n * p_t/(1-p_t)` and `DeltaNB=NB_M1-NB_M0`, plus test-all and test-none references. Repeat by the two baseline eGFR strata. The ratio `p_t/(1-p_t)` is only an externally assumed exchange rate; it is neither estimated nor validated here. No threshold is selected, recommended, or called net clinical benefit. Thresholds above prevalence remain visible and are not interpreted as clinically dominated without external harm/benefit information.

## Decision gates and interpretation

Supportive for baseline predictive prioritization requires: certified fields; exact anchor reproduction; complete null/bootstrap arithmetic; M1 calibration not materially worse; a contiguous prespecified capacity region with the simultaneous 98% lower band for `DeltaC` above zero; stability across repeats and supported strata; and observed results more favorable than the corresponding fold-aware empirical label null. The predictor-null and reversed-outcome checks must not show comparable gains. The IPCW sensitivity is reported separately and need not agree to retain the conditional primary finding, but large selection sensitivity or poor positivity downgrades transport interpretation.

Adverse evidence is a reproducibly negative incremental capacity curve, calibration deterioration, null-compatible observed gain, predictor-null gain, leakage, assay failure, anchor mismatch, or non-finite/incomplete computations. These block a positive interpretation; a negative result remains informative against this prioritization hypothesis in the selected evidence boundary.

Inconclusive evidence includes bands crossing zero, isolated grid-point gains, instability by repeat/stratum/schedule bin, unsupported strata, wide or extreme IPCW weights, assay uncertification, or failure to complete the prescribed null. Inconclusive results do not justify a testing threshold.

Even a fully supportive result establishes only conditional predictive ranking for complete UK Biobank repeat attenders and the specified eGFR-equation threshold outcome. It does not establish that repeat testing improves outcomes, that testing is cost-effective or harmless, that a threshold crossing is CKD, that either assay estimates measured GFR accurately or interchangeably, or that the result transports to routine-care patients. Those claims require certified external assay metadata, clinical adjudication, measured GFR and persistent-CKD follow-up, observed testing decisions and downstream treatment, patient preference/cost/harm data, and prospective validation.
