## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
ICU teams titrate fluids/vasopressors to a MAP target, and more (0,24]h time below MAP 65 mmHg is widely reported to track higher creatinine-based AKI. Three rivals make different predictions and carry different clinical consequences:
- **H-dose (physiologic):** the burden is an ischemic dose that independently raises (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI risk → "time below target" is a treatment target.
- **H-observation (detection artifact):** hypotension triggers more creatinine testing, so AKI is *detected* more without proportionate injury → the apparent target partly reflects who was watched.
- **H-treatment:** the association is carried by vasoactive/fluid intensity (a confounder/mediator of the exposure).
