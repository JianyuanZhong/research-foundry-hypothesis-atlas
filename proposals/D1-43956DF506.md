## Alternatives and decision record

The simple transparent sequence E0 → P1/D1-perp → J1 is retained because it distinguishes endpoint, recorded-process, residual-direction, and joint prediction with inspectable contrasts. The independently restricted, final-12-hour TCN remains the scientifically substantive alternative because it may retain nonlinear lag and cross-domain cancellation that scalar G-perp loses on the same information. A 24-hour TCN was rejected for the primary comparison because extra history confounds representation with information access; it may be revisited only as a separately labeled window-extension study. Shared heads were rejected because excluded streams can leak through a common encoder. Reusing the observed nuisance coefficients for null G was rejected as the primary null because it does not preserve the residualization operation; it remains a diagnostic. Reselecting nuisance family/hyperparameters per seed was rejected because it gives each null a different adaptive procedure. Omitting a residualized null and relying on raw G was rejected because D1-perp/J1 are central attribution estimands. Latent/domain-adversarial modeling remains deferred because no independent physiology, policy, staffing, or intent signal identifies what such a representation removes. Revisit it only with such external information.
