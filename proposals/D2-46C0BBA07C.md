# Provisional hypothesis: context-specific MerTK-inhibitor response in E-MTAB-8873

## Status

Provisional, planned research task for episode 15. No E-MTAB-8873 effect estimate has been computed in this episode. This is a hypothesis, not an observed result and not a claim of direct MerTK action, causality, protection, clinical efficacy, or patient benefit.

## Proposed relationship

In the available E-MTAB-8873 RA macrophage–fibroblast coculture assay, the MerTK-inhibitor-associated transcriptional response differs between MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage contexts. The primary estimand is a donor-aware, condition-verified context-by-inhibitor interaction, not a generic mean-expression change. A secondary descriptive question is whether the interaction separates a macrophage-linked resolving/repair-associated program from a fibroblast-linked inflammatory or harmful program.

This matters because a context-specific response would justify targeted mechanistic follow-up, whereas a generic response would redirect attention to treatment, donor, composition, or assay artifacts. The inherited board evidence supports biological plausibility: MerTK-positive synovial macrophage subsets are associated with remission and repair-related fibroblast responses; macrophage reprogramming can alter inflammatory homeostasis; and macrophage–stromal repair circuits are context-dependent and can become maladaptive. These observations do not establish that UNC106238 acts directly on MerTK or that any assay pattern transfers to patients.

## Evidence and unresolved claim

The strongest available evidence supports only biological plausibility and the measurability of targeted expression in the configured assay. It does not establish the proposed inhibitor interaction, direct target engagement, causal mechanism, or clinical benefit. The unresolved claim is whether the interaction survives donor-aware adjustment and negative controls.

Fit a donor-aware additive baseline for condition, treatment, age, sex, tissue, cell type, cell count, and contributing-donor structure. Compare it with a prespecified context-by-inhibitor interaction model, using donor-clustered or permutation uncertainty that respects repeated/mixed donors. Add composition adjustment/stratification and a within-context treatment negative control. Define programs only from the 445 measured features with a prespecified feature audit and sensitivity analysis.

Supportive results require a reproducible interaction that remains directionally consistent after composition and treatment controls with adequate uncertainty. Adverse results include a null/reversed interaction, disappearance after composition adjustment, or a generic treatment effect across contexts. Inconclusive results include unstable contrasts, missing controls, insufficient independent-donor information, ambiguous feature definitions, or imprecise estimates. Supportive results justify mechanistic follow-up only.

The simple donor-aware additive model is the baseline. The substantive alternative adds the context-by-inhibitor interaction and negative-control comparisons; it can reveal a context-specific response averaged away by the baseline while exposing donor, composition, and generic-treatment explanations. Both use the same inputs, target, split, and uncertainty evaluation. A mechanistic program model is optional only if prespecified and evaluated on the same features; predictive gain alone is not sufficient.

## Missing evidence and limitations

The assay lacks whole-tissue or cell-state-resolved measurements, an independent clinical cohort, temporal labels, and patient outcomes. Drug-associated expression cannot identify direct target action. Direct-target, mechanistic, causal, and therapeutic conclusions require perturbation/mechanistic evidence, expert review, and independent validation.

## Alternatives not chosen

A generic predictive model is deferred because it would not distinguish context-specific biology from donor or treatment composition. A neural sequence model is deferred because no verified longitudinal patient trajectory or temporal outcome is available; this is a dependency decision, not a blanket rejection of learned methods.
