## Unresolved clinical question and advance

At ICU hour 48, among adults with a uniquely recorded ongoing invasive-ventilation interval, recent but now absent vasopressor support, marked positive recorded fluid balance, MAP at least 65, urine output above 0.1 mL/kg/h, potassium at least 3.0, and no active RRT/ECMO or recent loop course, is starting/restarting an ICU-input-system furosemide or bumetanide delivery during the next 12 hours before the baseline recorded ventilation interval closes associated with at least a five-percentage-point improvement in alive recorded durable airway liberation at day 7, without crossing prespecified renal, RRT, death or disposition harm margins?

This decision matters because fluid removal might aid airway liberation but could worsen kidney injury, dialysis risk or survival. Current evidence does not resolve it. Full XML was inspected for Bircher et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13154636`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100075`, frozen source `[source checksum]`), which states that optimal timing, volume and duration of active fluid removal remain uncertain; Orieux et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13218125`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083`, source `[source checksum]`), which calls renal congestion hypothesis-generating without established therapeutic implications; and Kuriyama et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13476641`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120`, source `[source checksum]`), which reports 26 heterogeneous trials/1,652 participants and mostly low or very-low-certainty evidence. These sources establish clinical uncertainty, not benefit.

The strongest data-supported claim is that MIMIC-IV 3.1 can define a retrospective recorded-policy association using imperfect structured ICU records. Two treatment- and outcome-blind source audits demonstrate nonredundant defects in the previous protocol:

1. Among 8,709 broad stays with exactly one accepted item-225792 interval crossing hour 48, only 23 (0.264%) had `storetime<=t0`; median start-to-store was 112.23 hours but end-to-store was 0.05 hours. V0 `storetime` therefore cannot validly act as row-entry or clinician-awareness time.

Among 1,826 broad unique-V0 records satisfying the inherited procedure/death-only X+48 candidate rule, 102 (5.6%) had an exact contradiction before `t0+7d`: 61 later airway events and 45 later exact deaths, with overlap. The 48-hour endpoint can mature before clinically consequential day-7 information.
