# Documentary-phenotype sensitivity of a time-safe 12-hour HCC landmark

## Scientific deliverable and substantive successor claim

This is a substantive successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that proposal's 12-hour temporal lock, all-admission competing-state construction, exact HCC source bindings, and transparent-baseline versus irregular-alternative comparison. The new deliverable is a newly fitted **phenotype-stratified, all-admission landmark analysis** that tests whether the early-laboratory increment is transportable across the available HCC documentary phenotypes.

Completion requires:

1. a frozen lexical HCC eligibility rule and a frozen, non-time-claiming documentary-phenotype rule, with counts and overlap audits;
2. the parent's Stage-0/Stage-1 temporal audit, including all-admission flow and unresolved procedure-time states;
3. matched B0, B1 and B2 models on the same patient split, with phenotype used as an analysis stratum but raw untimed diagnosis text excluded from time-sensitive predictors;
4. locked held-out state probabilities, within-stratum and standardized all-admission contrasts, calibration and clustered uncertainty;
5. phenotype-label, timestamp, observation-process and terminal-label falsifications; and
6. a claim-to-output table that separates computationally checkable recorded-care claims from active-HCC validation, clinical adjudication and deployment claims.

The newly testable primary claim is:

> Among all adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient admission who have no established discharge or timeable procedure transition before hour 12, the incremental information in hepatic/renal laboratory observations strictly before hour 12 for the first subsequent recorded-care transition through hour 72 is not uniform across documentary HCC phenotypes. In particular, the increment for the encounter-context stratum (A) will be larger and more stable than the increment for the residual/ambiguous stratum (U), while the treated/status stratum (T) will show weaker or less clinically specific discrimination because its untimed labels can reflect prior care rather than current admission physiology.

This is a directional, falsifiable heterogeneity hypothesis, not a claim that A means active cancer or that T means inactive disease. The primary clinically consequential question is whether a lab-based escalation signal can be interpreted similarly across patients who are all HCC-coded, or whether an HCC-coded model must be validated separately before it is used to prioritize multidisciplinary review or monitoring.

The parent already asks whether pre-hour-12 labs predict the later recorded transition. This successor adds a new estimand: the phenotype-specific B1-minus-B0 contrast and its standardized all-admission consequence. A finding confined to A, or a finding that disappears after removing U/T records, would materially change the population to which the parent's claim can be applied.

## Evidence boundary

The strongest supported claim is a snapshot/coding claim: the diagnosis file has 1,810,646 rows covering all 105,044 encounter keys in the parent audit, and a frozen HCC-core lexical rule matched all 105,044 keys. My full diagnosis audit also found 344,383 rows containing the core phrase `肝细胞癌` or its English equivalent, 1,174 rows typed `病理诊断`, and 148,899 rows containing a treatment/status modifier. These are documentary facts, not pathology-confirmed active HCC.

The unresolved claim is whether the time-safe laboratory information gain and its calibration are materially different across the documentary strata below. The data cannot establish that any stratum is active HCC, recurrent HCC, treated HCC, pathological HCC, a particular stage, or a particular indication for a procedure. Diagnosis rows have no native time. Clinical documents and pathology have no native time, and their narrative fields have no validated context-aware adjudication in this task. A `病理诊断` value is therefore called pathology-typed, never pathology-confirmed.

If P or T support is too sparse after all temporal eligibility, report that stratum descriptively and declare its inferential contrast inconclusive.

A valid procedure has a parseable native `开始时间`, is comparable with native admission, and begins before native discharge. Let t0 be native admission. Use half-open windows and assign exact boundaries to the later window:

Stage 0, for **every** eligible admission, with earliest-time precedence:

- P0: first valid procedure in [0,12) hours after t0 and before discharge;
- D0: discharge in [0,12), before P0;
- U0: no established P0/D0 but a linked procedure has missing/invalid `开始时间` that could change ordering; report conservative and optimistic missing-time bounds;
- R12: no P0, D0 or U0 and still inpatient at hour 12.

Only R12 admissions enter the conditional Stage-1 model. No post-12 laboratory row is allowed in B1 or B2:

- P12_24, D12_24 and U12_24 are the first applicable transition in [12,24);
- P24_72, D24_72 and U24_72 are the first applicable transition in [24,72);
- N72 is no prior state, discharge at/after hour 72 and no valid procedure through hour 72.

The primary endpoint is P24_72, with the full terminal-state vector always reported. The primary conditional estimand is the held-out, phenotype-specific difference in P24_72 probability and multiclass score between B1 and B0 among R12 within A, P, T and U. The primary all-admission estimand standardizes each phenotype-specific conditional prediction over the observed eligible-stratum distribution and combines it with observed Stage-0 states. Thus early discharge/procedure and unresolved procedure timing remain part of the all-admission estimand; no post-12 risk set is relabeled as the whole population.

## Matched baseline and substantive irregular alternative

Use one deterministic patient-level split, for example hash buckets 0-59 for fitting, 60-69 for selecting prespecified preprocessing/regularization/state dimension, and 70-79 as locked evaluation. No locked test outcome, procedure text, order text or phenotype association may choose features, dictionaries or state dimension.

B0 is the transparent matched two-stage baseline. It predicts Stage 0 for every admission and Stage 1 for R12. It uses age, sex, admitting department, calendar era, admission time-of-day, prior encounter count, pre-admission assay-specific nearest/count/recency/missingness summaries, and the frozen A/P/T/U design stratum as a categorical analysis variable. It does **not** use raw untimed `诊断名称`/`诊断类型` as if they were admission-time clinical measurements. Use a transparent multinomial/competing-state model with regularization selected only in the selection split, and report stratum-specific predictions.

B1 uses the identical target, split, preprocessing and model family and adds only assay-specific early lab information from valid `检验时间` in [0,12): first/last value, change, elapsed time, count, missingness/density and supported within-assay slope. It has the same stratum variable as B0. No lab at/after hour 12, order, medication, procedure, discharge, untimed document or later encounter enters B1.

B2 is the substantive learned/mechanistic alternative on exactly the same R12 patients, same split and same [0,12) lab rows. Fit assay-specific irregular latent hepatic-reserve and renal-stress coordinates with assay-specific offsets/noise, shrinkage, irregular observation times, posterior uncertainty, level/slope/coupled movement and phenotype-specific competing-state heads or interactions. B2 can reveal nonlinear coupled organ trajectories and observation-timing effects that B1's first/last/change summaries lose; the phenotype interaction asks whether those trajectories have reproducible meaning across documentary strata. B1 is necessary to distinguish scientific structure from flexible-model gain. B2 is not evidence of a validated physiologic reserve score.

Primary comparison is B1 versus B0 within each phenotype and after empirical all-admission standardization; B2 versus B1 is secondary and supportive only if it improves held-out calibration and has stable state interpretation. Report the A-versus-U and A-versus-T contrasts with uncertainty, but do not convert them into clinical subgroup recommendations without adjudication. If P/T/U support is inadequate, retain those admissions and report an inconclusive stratum-specific estimand rather than collapsing them silently.

CPU-first is appropriate for CSV aggregation, sparse tabular fits and low-dimensional latent-state fitting. The parent measured a full-source temporal audit at 70.8 seconds on 4 CPUs/16 GiB; feature construction, B2 convergence and 500 clustered bootstrap replicates remain unverified. Future solver planning is 16 CPUs/256 GiB and up to 28,800 seconds, with one allocated A100 and `cuda:0` optional only if repeated B2 fitting benefits from acceleration. The discovery science budget is 7,200 seconds; it is not a reason to shrink the future study. No GPU requirement is implied.

## Evaluation, uncertainty and falsification

Report held-out multiclass log loss, multiclass Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, observed-versus-predicted state probabilities, conditional and standardized P24_72 contrasts, B1-minus-B0 within-stratum differences, A-versus-U/T interaction contrasts, B2-minus-B1 changes, and stratum-specific support. Use at least 500 patient-clustered paired bootstrap resamples with a declared locked-prediction/refit policy; if P/T/U counts make intervals unstable, report exact instability and no directional conclusion.

Required falsifications and sensitivities:

- permute lab timestamps within patient and assay, then values within assay; a physiology-specific increment should attenuate;
- permute terminal states within era/service; predictive gains should disappear;
- compare value/trajectory features with count/density/missingness-only features; a disappearing increment is observation-process evidence;
- add the forbidden [12,24) lab window only as a leakage sentinel;
- repeat exact-boundary, midnight and conservative/optimistic U-state rules;
- permute A/P/T/U labels within supported era/service strata while preserving marginal counts; the phenotype interaction should attenuate under the null;
- rebuild the phenotype using only type labels, then only modifier labels, to test whether the finding is a fragile lexical artifact;
- report a broad-all-HCC analysis without phenotype stratification and a strict A-only sensitivity, but do not present either as a replacement population;
- if support permits, use a same-source routine workflow endpoint as a negative control; do not invent one if support is inadequate;
- gate any procedure-family/order-concordance analysis exactly as in the parent and keep it secondary to the semantics-independent endpoint.

Supportive evidence requires coherent joins, adequate R12 and post-buffer state support, stable frozen phenotype counts, calibrated B1, a positive and uncertainty-supported B1-minus-B0 contrast in at least A with a prespecified A>U/T interaction direction, attenuation under timestamp/terminal-label/phenotype permutation, and no dominance by density or unresolved-time states. B2 is supportive only if its added fit is calibrated and stable over B1; complexity alone is not support.

Adverse evidence includes no phenotype heterogeneity, an opposite direction, gains that survive terminal-label or lab-time permutation, equivalence to density-only features, large sensitivity to lexical variants or midnight rules, or a result driven by one procedure string. This would refute the directional hypothesis or indicate an observation/documentation artifact, not prove that the labs have no clinical value.

Inconclusive evidence includes sparse P/T strata after R12 restriction, high U-state mass, unstable diagnosis dictionaries, insufficient repeated assays, wide clustered intervals, nonconvergence, or inability to distinguish phenotype from documentation opportunity. Inconclusive is not a positive or negative clinical conclusion.

## Clinical interpretation and unavailable evidence

Computational outputs can establish the source hashes, phenotype rule, cohort flow, timed feature provenance, fitted models, locked predictions, scores, calibration, uncertainty, phenotype interaction and falsification results. A supportive result would justify a prospective validation question: whether an adjudicated, time-stamped active-HCC cohort needs separate calibration or triage review by documentary phenotype. It would not justify treatment, procedure referral, monitoring intensity, or deployment.

Clinical adjudication or another study is required for active HCC confirmation, recurrence, stage, tumor burden, resectability, liver function grading, procedure indication/intent/receipt/completion, response, toxicity, mortality, outside-hospital care, clinical benefit, causal effects, and utility. The snapshot lacks validated diagnosis timing, pathology timing, imaging ground truth, units/reference ranges, tumor measurements, recommendation/receipt semantics, and complete external care capture. The exact diagnosis text is therefore a sensitivity/transportability variable, not a clinical gold standard.

The actual scientific deliverable is complete only when the solver freezes A/P/T/U rules before locked evaluation, newly fits B0/B1/B2, emits stratum-specific and all-admission outputs with uncertainty, and reports whether the directional heterogeneity claim is supported, adverse or inconclusive. A positive result is neither required for completion nor sufficient for clinical adoption.
