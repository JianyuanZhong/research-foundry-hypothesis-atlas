## 1. The opening: a measured asymmetry that inverts the textbook question

The seed's clinical question is genuine and consequential, and is preserved: a man whose total testosterone falls below a guideline treatment threshold is either **androgen-deficient** (→ testosterone replacement) or **SHBG-low with preserved free androgen** (→ metabolic management, no androgen). Guidelines handle this by computing free testosterone from the same blood draw. The two readings imply opposite actions.

What the data say about that rule, verified by me today (`verify2.py` → `verify2.json`, re-derived independently of the earlier `feasibility.py`):

In 195,732 UK Biobank men with total testosterone (30850-0.0), SHBG (30830-0.0), albumin (30600-0.0) and an assessment date (53-0.0), with Vermeulen calculated free testosterone (cFT) and the conventional cut 225 pmol/L:

| cell at total T < 8 nmol/L | n (raw) | median SHBG | median total T |
|---|---|---|---|
| low total T, **low** cFT (concordant) | 23,909 | 24.97 | 6.98 |
| low total T, **NORMAL** cFT — *the cell the clinical debate is about* | **105** | **9.52** | 7.73 |
| **normal** total T, **low** cFT — the reverse discordance | **105,139** | 41.55 | 11.02 |
| normal total T, normal cFT | 66,579 | 34.4 | 14.15 |

After the exclusions and 2-year landmark below, the debated cell holds **100 men with 0 incident major osteoporotic fractures and 0 hip fractures**, while the reverse-discordance cell holds **100,819 men** (1,958 MOF, 843 hip, 7,559 anaemia, 7,125 T2D, 11,367 deaths).

So the guideline rule, applied at population scale, essentially never rescues a below-threshold man (0.44% of men under 8 nmol/L), but *reclassifies more than one hundred thousand men with normal total testosterone as free-androgen deficient*. The threshold framing — not only its interpretation — is the problem. A 105-man cell cannot support any survival contrast; a child that kept the discordance design would be reporting noise.

Second measured fact, which the seed and the published literature both assume away. cFT is a deterministic Vermeulen function of the same three assays, so it cannot be an independent exposure. I quantified how little room is left (n = 195,732):

- log cFT from **linear** log T + log SHBG: R² = **0.9761** (residual SD 0.048 log units)
- plus log albumin: R² = **0.9834** (0.040)
- from a degree-4 polynomial tensor of (log T, log SHBG) + cubic log albumin: R² = **0.999638**, residual SD **0.0059 log units (0.6%)**
- the free androgen index is *exactly* log T − log SHBG (R² = 1.000000 by construction)
- conversely, cFT **alone** recovers only **56.4%** of log-T variance and **2.2%** of log-SHBG variance.

cFT is therefore a 1-D collapse of a 2-D surface: it adds at most 0.6% residual information and discards most of the surface. Whether that collapse is the *right* 1-D summary for clinical risk is an empirical question nobody has tested head-to-head against a flexible joint two-marker model. That is design element 2 below.

**Why this matters clinically.** If the SHBG axis and the total-T-at-fixed-SHBG axis predict *different outcome classes*, then "low total T" is genuinely ambiguous and a second marker is needed — but the needed object is the two-marker surface, not cFT. If instead both axes predict everything in the same direction, the shared explanation is a severity/adiposity axis, both markers are markers of general ill health, and the free-hormone arithmetic in the guideline is decoration on a single risk gradient.

## 2. Strongest claim the existing evidence supports, vs the unresolved claim under test

**Supported already** (inspected):
- In this same cohort, low SHBG predicts *lower* hip and forearm fracture risk (Q1 vs Q5 hip HR 0.55, 0.47–0.65), and the total-T/fracture association is nonlinear and changes materially once SHBG is adjusted or cFT substituted — SHBG, not T, behaves as the major independent fracture biomarker [K1].
- Low SHBG is a strong prospective predictor of incident type 2 diabetes in men, with early MR suggesting it "may have a causal role" [K3].
- The binding assumptions behind cFT (single-site stoichiometry, fixed Ka_SHBG = 1.0e9 /M, Ka_alb = 3.6e4 /M) are probably inaccurate [K2].

**Unresolved claim this experiment tests.** Whether the two markers index *distinct states* with *distinct outcome classes*, or a *single shared axis*. Formally: does the partial association of log total T (at fixed log SHBG) differ between an androgen-responsive outcome and a metabolic outcome *by more than* the corresponding difference for log SHBG (at fixed log T)? And: does cFT improve out-of-sample outcome prediction over a flexible joint function of the same two assays?

**Leading explanation (H1, two states).** Total T at fixed SHBG tracks androgen-responsive endpoints (fracture, hip fracture, anaemia, grip decline); SHBG at fixed T tracks the metabolic endpoint (T2D) in the opposite direction (low SHBG → more T2D) and *not* fracture.
**Strongest rival (H0, one axis).** Adiposity/chronic-illness severity lowers both SHBG and total T and raises all risks; both axes then track everything, and both are severity markers. Our own baseline data make this live: median BMI 29.5 in the concordant low cell vs 26.9 in the reference cell; mean T2D PRS 0.017 / 0.119 / −0.161 across cells.
**Second rival (H2, attenuation by noise).** Total T is simply measured much worse than SHBG, so its adjusted coefficient is attenuated and *looks* weak. Measured: repeat-draw ICC on the log scale **0.550 for total T vs 0.859 for SHBG** (n = 6,630 men with repeat triples, median interval 4.42 y); log cFT r = 0.4946. H2 is addressed, not assumed away, by the calibration arm (§7).

**The observation that separates them.** The sign and size of one prespecified difference-in-differences across (marker axis) × (outcome class), with death as a shared-severity control, plus whether regression calibration moves it.

## 6. The one prespecified estimand, and the numeric bands

Let the exposure surface be (x₁ = log total T, x₂ = log SHBG). For outcome k fit a Cox model with a natural-spline tensor `ns(x₁, df 4) ⊗ ns(x₂, df 4)` plus covariates (age at visit, assessment centre, smoking 20116, genetic-ancestry indicator; adjustment sets A/B/C in §7). Define, at the cohort median of the other marker:

- Δ_T(k) = log HR for a **1-SD decrease in log total T at fixed log SHBG** (SD = 0.3332, verified)
- Δ_S(k) = log HR for a **1-SD decrease in log SHBG at fixed log total T** (SD = 0.4201, verified)

(Secondary scaling, reported alongside: partial SDs at fixed other marker, 0.2830 for log T and 0.3568 for log SHBG, r(x₁,x₂) = 0.528.)

**Primary estimand — the dissociation index**

> **D = [Δ_T(MOF) − Δ_T(T2D)] − [Δ_S(MOF) − Δ_S(T2D)]**

a marker × outcome-class difference-in-differences. Under H0 (one shared axis) D = 0: each marker's relative weighting of fracture vs diabetes is the same. Under H1 D > 0: lower total T at fixed SHBG is *relatively* more fracture-weighted than lower SHBG is.

Uncertainty: 1,000 person-level nonparametric bootstrap resamples, refitting all four models, bias-corrected percentile 95% CI; reported with robust-sandwich asymptotic SE as a cross-check. Expected SE from the event counts (3,275 and 13,873 events; per-SD log-HR SEs ≈ 0.023 and 0.012 inflated for spline/adjustment correlation) is **≈0.04–0.05**, so the bands below are attainable rather than aspirational.

**Prespecified bands (primary, adjustment set A, no adiposity):**
- **Supportive of H1**: D̂ ≥ **0.15** and 95% CI lower bound > **0.05**.
- **Adverse (supports H0/one axis)**: the whole 95% CI inside **(−0.05, 0.05)**.
- **Supportive of the reverse reading** (SHBG is the informative marker, [K1]'s direction): D̂ ≤ **−0.15** with CI upper bound < **−0.05**.
- **Inconclusive**: anything else, including any CI wider than 0.40.

**Severity-control gate.** Even a supportive D is *downgraded to inconclusive* if the same contrast computed with all-cause death as the outcome, D_death = Δ_T(death) − Δ_S(death), exceeds 0.6 × |Δ_T(MOF) − Δ_S(MOF)| — i.e. if the apparent dissociation is reproduced on a pure severity endpoint it is not androgen-specific.

**Replication requirement.** The hip-fracture and anaemia endpoints substitute for MOF in two pre-registered secondary computations of D; H1 is called supported only if the *sign* of D replicates in ≥2 of the 3 androgen endpoints. Grip change (instance 2) is analysed as a linear model and reported as a direction-consistency check, not inside D.

## 7. What cFT adds (design element 2): a formal nested comparison

Per outcome k, Cox models:
- **M0** covariates only
- **M1** M0 + `ns(log cFT, df 4)` — the guideline construct
- **M2** M0 + `ns(x₁,4) ⊗ ns(x₂,4)` + `log albumin` — the flexible joint two-marker surface
- **M3** M2 + the **orthogonalised cFT residual**: `log cFT − fitted(log cFT | poly4 tensor of x₁,x₂ + poly3 log alb)`, whose SD is **0.0059 log units** (R² 0.999638). M3 vs M2 is *literally* the test of whether the 0.6% of cFT not already in the surface carries outcome signal. (A raw `log cFT` term in M3 would be numerically singular; the orthogonalisation is the honest construction, not a cosmetic one.)

Metrics: 5-fold cross-validated partial log-likelihood and Harrell's C, bootstrap 95% CI on differences.
- **cFT declared informationally redundant** if, for every outcome, ΔC(M3 − M2) < **0.002** with CI excluding 0.005, and the M3-vs-M2 partial LRT p > 0.05.
- **cFT declared an inadequate summary** if ΔC(M2 − M1) ≥ **0.005** with CI excluding 0.
- **cFT vindicated** if ΔC(M1 − M2) ≥ 0 within CI, i.e. the 1-D Vermeulen collapse loses nothing on outcomes.
If cFT adds nothing, that is a **real negative finding about a quantity clinicians are instructed to compute** — and it is not a trivial restatement of R² = 0.9996, because a 0.6% residual could in principle be outcome-relevant and because M1-vs-M2 asks the different question of whether the *collapse direction* is right.

### Adiposity as a possible mediator, not a nuisance
Three adjustment sets, all reported, no single "adjusted" headline:
- **A** (primary): age, centre, smoking, ancestry. No adiposity. This is the total association, mediation included.
- **B**: A + BMI `21001-0.0` + body fat % `23099-0.0` (splines). Obesity lowers both SHBG and total T and raises all risks; conditioning on it may block a mediated path and **changes the estimand**. B is reported as "association not transmitted through measured adiposity", never as mechanism.
- **C**: A + T2D PRS `26285-0.0` (+ 10 genetic PCs `22009-0.1..0.10`, ancestry-restricted sensitivity). The PRS cannot be affected by prevalent disease or by hormone levels, so it probes genetic confounding of the metabolic axis specifically. Measured cell means (0.017 / 0.119 / −0.161) show the cells differ genetically, so this probe is live.
Prespecified reading: if D is supportive in A and survives B and C with ≥60% of its magnitude, the dissociation is not merely an adiposity gradient; if D collapses under B, report that the two axes are plausibly a single adiposity-mediated gradient and say explicitly that A-vs-B cannot distinguish mediation from confounding here.
**MR is BLOCKED**: participant-level genotypes are absent from this snapshot (only clumped protein-GWAS summary tables and released PRS). No instrument-based causal language anywhere in the outputs.

### Measurement-error arm (design element 5), computed here
From the 6,630 men with complete instance-0 and instance-1 triples (median interval **4.42 y**), for the vector (log T, log SHBG):

```
Sigma_obs  = [[0.09723, 0.07369], [0.07369, 0.16997]]
Sigma_err  = Cov(X0 - X1)/2 = [[0.04379, 0.00814], [0.00814, 0.02389]]
Sigma_true = [[0.05344, 0.06555], [0.06555, 0.14607]]
L  = Sigma_true Sigma_obs^-1 = [[0.38321, 0.21954], [0.03403, 0.84467]]
L^-1                          = [[2.6712, -0.69428], [-0.10761, 1.21186]]
ICC(log T) = 0.550      ICC(log SHBG) = 0.859      r(log cFT repeat) = 0.4946
```
Multivariate regression calibration therefore implies the **adjusted log-T coefficient is attenuated by roughly a factor 2.7**, with a **−0.69 cross-term from log SHBG**, while the log-SHBG coefficient is attenuated only ~1.21-fold. This is the quantitative form of rival H2: a literature conclusion that "SHBG matters and testosterone does not" is partly predicted by differential measurement error alone, and the cross-term means the two corrections are not separable marker-by-marker. The calibrated contrast D_cal is reported beside D with the same bands.

**Honest bounds on this arm.** (a) A 4.42-year interval conflates genuine biological change with assay/diurnal noise, so ICC 0.550 is a **lower bound on reliability** and L⁻¹ an **upper bound on the correction**; the arm therefore *bounds* H2 and does not identify it. Sensitivity grid: assume 0% / 25% / 50% of the within-person variance is true change, recompute L⁻¹, and report D_cal at each. (b) Diurnal variation is part of that noise and is bindable: field `3166-0.0..0.6` (time blood sample collected, dictionary P = 499,777; present in the 35 GB Assessment Centre CSV at header columns 1630–1636, verified). Prespecified: a time-of-day spline enters all models, and the repeat-reliability calculation is repeated within matched draw-time windows (±1 h) to separate diurnal from analytic noise. If 3166 coverage in the triple cohort proves <80% after the chunked read, the time-of-day arm is reported as **[partially executed]**, not dropped silently.
(c) 30850 is an immunoassay with known accuracy limits at low male concentrations; the observed floor is benign in this snapshot (0 men at ≤0.35 nmol/L, 220 men <1 nmol/L), but a left-tail sensitivity analysis excluding T < 1 nmol/L is prespecified.

## 8. Baseline and substantive alternative on the same question (matched specificity)

### (a) Simple baseline — penalised-spline Cox / Poisson
Inputs: the bindings in §4 on ≤187,989 men. Fitting target: partial likelihood for each of 6 outcomes × 3 adjustment sets, plus a linear model for grip change (instance 2, n = 26,088). Splits: full-cohort estimation for D; 5-fold CV for the M0–M3 discrimination comparison. Uncertainty: 1,000-resample person-level bootstrap for D and ΔC; robust sandwich SE cross-check. Budget: a tensor-spline Cox fit with ~30 basis columns on 188k rows takes roughly 5–15 s single-core (unverified estimate, not measured), so 1,000 bootstraps × 4 fits ≈ 6–17 CPU-hours → ≈ 30–70 min wall on 16 cores. The chunked `usecols` read of field 3166 from the 35 GB CSV is a separate ~10–25 min single-pass job. **CPU only.** Selected as the primary analysis because the estimand is defined on this model class and the bands are calibrated to its SEs.
What it loses: it treats both markers as error-free, so the attenuation in H2 is confounded with a genuinely weak association; and it reports a surface without naming the direction in that surface that corresponds to androgen exposure.

### (b) Substantive alternative — latent androgen-exposure measurement model
A two-factor structural measurement model: latent **A** (bioavailable-androgen state) and latent **G** (SHBG-specific metabolic state), with log total T, log SHBG and log albumin as error-prone indicators, loadings and error covariances identified using the 6,630-man two-instance subsample, then outcome hazards regressed on (A, G) via EM / two-stage posterior-score imputation with Rubin propagation over 50 draws.
- **Inputs**: the same columns, plus instance-1 triples and the 3166 draw-time spline as an indicator-level covariate.
- **Fitting target**: joint likelihood of (indicators at instances 0,1) × (outcome partial likelihood), latent dimension fixed at 2 by design; ~25–40 free parameters.
- **Split**: loadings/error structure from the repeat subsample; outcome stage on the full cohort; 5-fold CV of outcome log-likelihood against baseline M2.
- **What it recovers that the baseline loses**: (1) it separates attenuation by measurement error from attenuation by confounding/mediation *inside one likelihood* rather than by a post-hoc matrix correction; (2) it **estimates** the androgen-relevant direction in (log T, log SHBG) space from the outcome data instead of imposing the Vermeulen algebra, which [K2] says is probably mis-specified — i.e. it answers "what is the right 1-D collapse, if any?" whereas the baseline only answers "is cFT's collapse adequate?"; (3) it yields a factor-loading contrast (does the fracture-predictive factor load on T with SHBG free, or on SHBG?) that is the mechanistic version of D.
- **Identifying assumption**: conditional independence of indicator errors across instances given the latent state. This is **violated to an unknown degree** because 4.42 years of true biological drift is absorbed into "error". The repeat subsample therefore **bounds** the model, and the same 0%/25%/50% true-change grid is used to report a range of loadings rather than a point claim. If the bound is wide enough that the fracture-predictive direction is not resolved, that is the reported result.
- **Budget**: EM on 195k rows × ~40 parameters plus 50-draw outcome refits ≈ 1–3 CPU-hours (unverified). **CPU only.**
- **Selection / deferral reason**: both are run; the baseline carries the prespecified estimand and bands, the latent model is the confirmatory/interpretive arm and is explicitly allowed to be reported as inconclusive. **No GPU is requested**: these are tabular fits on ≤200k rows dominated by partial-likelihood accumulation and small dense linear algebra, where A100s give no benefit — this is a workload judgement from the shape of the computation, not an inherited prohibition. If the latent stage were extended to the full NMR/proteomic indicator set (not proposed here), a GPU probe would be warranted.
- **Alternatives considered and not chosen** (recorded for revisiting): (i) one-sample MR on SHBG/testosterone loci — **blocked**, no participant-level genotypes; revisit only if genotype dosages are added to the snapshot; (ii) a deep survival model on the raw assay panel — rejected because the scientific question is a 2-D contrast, and a black-box gain would not discriminate H0/H1/H2; (iii) re-estimating Vermeulen binding constants from outcome data — attractive given [K2] but not identifiable from three assays without an external free-T measurement, so it is folded into the latent model's estimated direction instead.

## 9. What supportive / adverse / inconclusive would and would not establish

- **Supportive (D ≥ 0.15, CI lower bound > 0.05, sign replicated in ≥2 androgen endpoints, severity gate passed)**: establishes that in UK Biobank men the two assays carry *statistically distinguishable* risk information with different outcome weighting, so a below-threshold total testosterone is genuinely ambiguous and the two-marker surface (not cFT) is the object to report. It would **not** establish that treating the low-T group with testosterone changes fracture risk, that the latent factor A is androgen action, or any causal claim — only a trial can do the first and measured free testosterone the second.
- **Adverse (CI inside ±0.05)**: supports the one-axis reading and is a genuinely useful negative result — it would mean the guideline's two-step hormone arithmetic is adding precision to a single severity/adiposity gradient. It would **not** prove that androgen deficiency is clinically unreal, because immunoassay error (ICC 0.550), a single mid-morning draw, and healthy-volunteer selection all push toward null, and the calibrated D_cal is the stated check on that.
- **Inconclusive (CI spanning 0.05, or width > 0.40, or severity gate tripped)**: reported as such. An imprecise null does not refute H1. The pre-registered next step would be restriction to the reverse-discordance stratum (100,819 men) with repeated-measure exposures, not a search over new subgroups.
- **cFT redundant**: establishes that a computed quantity in routine guidelines adds <0.002 C-statistic over the two assays it is computed from, in the largest cohort where this can be checked. It would **not** establish that *measured* free testosterone is uninformative — that is the central thing this snapshot cannot test.

## 10. Accepted limitations (what cannot be reached here)
1. **No measured free testosterone** (no equilibrium dialysis, no LC-MS/MS) anywhere in this snapshot. Every "free" quantity here is Vermeulen arithmetic on two immunoassays, whose binding assumptions are themselves contested [K2].
2. UKB total testosterone is an immunoassay with known accuracy limits at low male concentrations; a genuinely hypogonadal tail may be mismeasured in a direction we cannot calibrate without an external reference method.
3. Single, non-fasting, mostly mid-morning draw; diurnal variation partially addressable via field 3166 but not removable.
4. Healthy-volunteer selection in UK Biobank; the 100-man debated cell is additionally a selected young (median age 50), high-BMI, very-low-SHBG group.
5. **Competing risk of death** (18,738 deaths): cause-specific hazards are primary; Fine–Gray subdistribution models are reported as a sensitivity arm, and the two answer different questions.
6. ICD-10/registry code sets for MOF, anaemia, T2D, hypopituitarism are not clinically adjudicated; code-set validity, androgen-deprivation and testosterone-prescription identification (Data-Coding 4 absent) require expert review and are **not automatable**.
7. Participant-level genotypes absent → MR blocked; PRS probes confounding only.
8. Instance-1 grip carries a protocol/device offset (−1.79 kg/y vs −0.43 kg/y at instance 2); the change arm rests on 26,088 men at a 10-year interval and remains the weakest-powered androgen endpoint.
9. Single cohort, single ancestry-dominant population; no external replication is available in this snapshot.

## 11. How each of the Lead's four repairable defects is addressed
- **(i) No falsifiable estimand.** §6 gives one prespecified contrast D on the continuous (log T, log SHBG) surface, with scaling SDs verified (0.3332, 0.4201), bootstrap CI, numeric supportive/adverse/reverse/inconclusive bands, a severity-control gate, a sign-replication requirement, and a defined population and temporal boundary (§3).
- **(ii) The measured asymmetry was buried.** §1 opens with it, in a table, as the *motivation*: 105 men in the debated cell (100 after exclusions, 0 fractures) against 105,139 (100,819) in the reverse discordance. The threshold framing is abandoned, not re-interpreted; the analysis moves to the continuous surface, with the reverse-discordance stratum named as the pre-registered fallback population.
- **(iii) Obesity/illness rival not operationalised.** §7 specifies three adjustment sets with an explicit mediator-vs-confounder argument, the measured BMI and T2D-PRS cell differences as evidence that the rival is live, a prespecified ≥60%-of-magnitude survival criterion, an explicit refusal to call adjustment mechanism, and a severity-control gate on all-cause death inside the primary decision rule.
- **(iv) cFT assumed rather than tested.** Measured here: cFT is 99.96% recoverable from a flexible function of the same two assays (residual SD 0.0059 log units) and recovers only 56%/2% of the two markers' variance. §7 then specifies a nested M0/M1/M2/M3 comparison on *held-out outcome* likelihood and C-statistic with numeric redundancy bands, using the orthogonalised 0.6% residual, so "what does cFT add" becomes a decidable test rather than an assumption.
