# Capacity-complete patient documentary coverage for 24-hour HCC documentary-M2 review

## Focused successor and the one repair

This successor combines the valid temporal/source audit of `[prior hypothesis]` with the identity-robust coupled-world guarantee of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the adult first eligible resection population, 24-hour confirmatory estimand, acquisition-before-exposure five-level monotone whole-accession onset state, coupled operation/source/accession-identity/reader/pathology worlds, locked B/R/G/Gmask models, exact fixed K, comparator/random/deletion gates, and documentary/nonclinical interpretation.

It changes one decision-validity feature only. The parent makes the primary availability contract a lower band on the fraction of a patient's eligible accessions exposed. That measure is useful as a depth sensitivity, but it is not aligned to the capacity unit: one offered position is one patient. It can be altered by repeat imaging and, by itself, does not require a semantic top-K position to contain any report-bearing input. The successor therefore makes the primary coverage unit a whole patient and adds an exact capacity-completeness gate: every nonempty G position among the frozen K positions must have at least one clean, acquisition-eligible, report-bearing whole accession exposed at 24 hours. The inherited fractional-accession curve remains descriptive only.

“Documentary-input-supported” is intentionally narrower than “available to a clinician.” It means only that the frozen source/version world supplies at least one whole accession with stored report-surface text to the ranking input. It does not mean authored, finalized, released, ingested, retrievable, readable in a user interface, viewed, acted upon, sufficient for a clinical decision, or feasible within an actual service. HCC has no fields for those claims.

## Evidence, unresolved question, and hypothesis

The strongest claim already supported by inspected data is that the frozen HCC snapshot contains patient/encounter-linked procedures, acquisition-like examination times, whole-accession identifiers, examination report-surface text, and same-encounter pathology narratives. A new no-sampling scan of the live examinations source read 419,996 logical rows, including 72 rows with blank `examination number`, 392,854 distinct nonblank whole-accession keys, and 16,534 multirow keys. Of the nonblank keys, 392,726 have at least one non-whitespace value across `examination findings` or `examination diagnosis`; 128 have neither. Among report-bearing keys, 332,653 have both fields, 20,611 findings only, and 39,462 diagnosis only. These are whole-source storage counts, not CT/MRI eligibility, study-cohort prevalence, time-of-availability, or clinical adequacy.

Current public evidence motivates the clinical importance but does not answer this question. The inspected full XML for Li et al. (2025), PMCID PMC12132667, DOI 10.2147/JHC.S519578, describes a retrospective 131-patient resection cohort with confirmed MVI and a DCE-MRI clinical-radiological-radiomics model; its abstract reports test AUC 0.917. The inspected full XML for Li et al. (2025), PMCID PMC12309289, DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746, reviews MVI classification, preoperative prediction, and management and emphasizes that pathological diagnosis is postoperative and management evidence remains controversial. Neither source evaluates this HCC report-text ranking, documentary M2, report timing, fixed-capacity allocation, workflow, or patient benefit.

The unresolved claim is:

> At the locked 10% capacity and 24-hour preoperative cutoff, whenever at least K distinct eligible patients have documentary input—the minimum supply capable of filling K distinct patient positions—the locked G queue places at least one exposed report-bearing whole accession behind every surviving original top-K position and captures strictly more documentary-M2 patients than B, R, Gmask, and capacity-matched random review by more than 5 cases per 100 original offered positions, under every compatible identity, monotone-onset, coupled source/operation, global reader/pathology, and anchored whole-patient deletion world, separately in 2020 and 2021.

This hypothesis is falsifiable. It is about concentration of a historical encounter-documentary endpoint under a fixed ranking and a minimally source-supported queue. It is not a hypothesis about biological MVI, treatment choice, recurrence, survival, clinical report access, clinician behavior, workflow performance, safety, or benefit.

The substantive advance is that a passing availability contract now has a direct, auditable meaning at the decision unit: no surviving position in the proposed semantic queue is supported only by acquisition/demographic or unavailable-block features. Repeat scans can still add semantic depth, but they cannot make a patient count more than once toward primary reach or capacity fill.

## Preserved monotone whole-accession worlds

- O=1: first eligible readability in (t72,t48];

## The repaired patient/capacity coverage contract

Define an HCC-computable stored-content bit for each whole accession:

`B(i,ω,u)=1` iff the accession is source-clean and, across all of its retained rows, at least one raw value in `Examination findings` or `Examination diagnosis` is non-null and non-whitespace. No lexical content, outcome, or reader judgment enters B. B says “report-surface bytes exist,” not “clinically complete.”

At 24 hours define the patient documentary-input bit

`C(i,ω,O)=1{ there exists u with E(i,ω,24,u)=1, B(i,ω,u)=1, and O(i,ω,u)<=2 }`.

Each patient contributes at most one. Patients with no acquisition-eligible report-bearing accession remain in N with C=0. For each complete 24-hour source/onset world define patient reach `R24=sum_i C_i/N`. The confirmatory minimum-supply contract is the exact integer predicate `sum_i C_i>=K`: at least K distinct patients have documentary input, the arithmetic minimum capable of filling K distinct patient positions. This threshold is derived from the already frozen capacity and introduces no workflow rate, utility, or preference. Thresholds `sum_i C_i>=k` for k=K+1,...,N are ordered sensitivity cells only.

Let `S_G(z)` be the exact original top-K patient set for G in complete cell z, before deletion. For anchored deletion δ, ranks are not recomputed or backfilled; let `K_δ=K-1{δ in S_G(z)}` and
`F_G(z,δ)=sum_{i in S_G(z), i != δ} C_i / K_δ`.
The new capacity-completeness gate is the exact integer equality `sum C_i=K_δ`, equivalently `F_G=1`, for every deletion including none. The deliberately empty deleted slot is not called uncovered; every surviving offered patient must be supported. This does not assert that a human saw a report or could finish a review.

Let `Z24(K)` contain every coupled source/operation and accession-identity/onset world satisfying only `sum_i C_i>=K`. The minimum-supply contract is nonvacuous only if Z24(K) is nonempty in both test years. It passes only if, for every complete state in Z24(K), every global reader/pathology pair, and every deletion, the capacity-completeness equality and all inherited performance gates pass. Crucially, F=1 is tested over all minimum-supply states; it is not used to discard unfavorable states. A failure under Z24(K) is primary adverse even if all worlds with k>K pass. An aggregate audit cannot place coverage on favorable patients: all compatible identity assignments remain in Z24(K).

Compute the confirmatory extrema over Z24(K), then the nondominated sensitivity boundary over k=K+1,...,N, capacity completeness, captures, and false-positive burden with the inherited branch-and-bound or multi-valued decision-diagram oracle. Cache/quotient only complete identical vectors of source eligibility, onset bits, C bits, score bits, top-K identities, outcomes, and deletion outputs with reversible raw witnesses. Emit an attained worst-state witness for the primary and every sensitivity boundary cell. The inherited fractional curve `g_i=sum_u M/|A_i|` is retained as a secondary “accession depth” diagnostic; neither higher k cells nor fractional depth can veto or rescue the minimum-supply primary result.

An external archive census can identify C directly only after exact patient/encounter/accession/version crosswalk and onset mapping. An aggregate claim that at least K patients are covered remains identity-uncertain and must pass the all-identity Z24(K) oracle. A probability sample cannot establish F=1 unless every patient in the union of possible G top-K sets is audited or every unaudited possible selected patient is conservatively assigned C=0. No report release, workflow, viewing, or capacity observation is imputed.

No standard error is allowed to average away a failed world or a failed year. No superpopulation, transport, or causal confidence claim follows from a finite-corpus pass; those require another study.

## Disjoint falsification and interpretation

Apply this one-hot precedence to the 24-hour, rho=.10 result; 12/48/72-hour and other-capacity results are separately labelled sensitivities and cannot veto or rescue it.

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: any source/state/rank/reach/fill/outcome/witness/replay proof is incomplete or invalid.
2. `feasibility_inconclusive`: computation is complete but any primary N, K, event, or nonempty-state gate fails.
3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse`: the all-clean/all-exposed content world fails any inherited comparator, random, or deletion gate in either year. Reduced availability cannot rescue the locked semantic claim.
4. `minimum_supply_capacity_adverse`: maximum content passes, but under the nonempty minimum-supply set Z24(K), at least one compatible identity/world, reader/pathology pair, deletion, or year has an unsupported surviving G position or fails a performance gate. Passing only at k>K cannot rescue it.
5. `finite_corpus_capacity_complete_bridge_pending`: every state in the nonempty minimum-supply set Z24(K) passes capacity completeness and performance, but HCC lacks a qualifying external version/onset audit. This supports only computational feasibility under stated worlds.
6. `aggregate_identity_inconclusive`: an external aggregate or sampled reach bound exists but, after all compatible identities/unaudited selected patients are retained, it does not establish capacity completeness or a unique adverse conclusion.
7. `audited_documentary_adverse`: a valid exact-version audit yields a complete compatible state set, but fill or any performance gate fails in either year.
8. `externally_bridged_documentary_supportive`: a valid exact-version/onset audit identifies a nonempty compatible state set and every compatible state has F=1 and passes every performance gate separately in both years.

These labels are disjoint by precedence. Supportive means the frozen historical semantic queue is documentary-input-supported at every surviving position and robustly concentrates the encounter-documentary endpoint relative to the exact baselines. Adverse falsifies either signal or capacity-aligned coverage under the locked design; it is valuable even if another cutoff is favorable. Inconclusive means computation, feasibility, or identity/timing evidence cannot decide the claim—not that the policy works or fails. Preference remains “unmeasured,” “adverse,” “heterogeneous,” or “favorable” only as a separate annotation when external p evidence exists; it never changes the one-hot documentary label.
