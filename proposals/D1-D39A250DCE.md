# Joint patient-coverage and preference frontier for preoperative HCC documentary-M2 review

## Question and advance

The consequential decision is whether a scarce multidisciplinary review service should prioritize adults approaching a first liver resection when routine preoperative CT/MRI report text may identify patients whose eventual resection documentation records high-grade microvascular invasion (documentary M2). The action studied is allocation of a fixed number of review positions only; it is not a recommendation for resection extent, transplantation, systemic or locoregional therapy, surveillance, or any treatment.

The strongest supported claim is narrow. The HCC snapshot contains patient/encounter-linked procedures with start/end fields, examination rows with acquisition-like start times, CT/MRI labels and eventual report narratives, untimed same-encounter pathology narratives, and dated medication/order records. It can support a historical locked ranking experiment over a documentary endpoint. It cannot show report authoring, finalization, release, retrievability or viewing before the decision; it has no immutable report version/addendum chain, pathology time/specimen/slide/block key or sampling protocol, images, MDT capacity, clinician response, treatment, recurrence, survival, harms, cost or patient benefit.

The unresolved hypothesis is: after retaining all admissible source, clock, report, pathology-reader and whole-patient composition uncertainty, a locked semantic ranking has a nonempty, exactly computable frontier of patient-balanced report availability and outcome-blind stakeholder preference for which it captures more documentary-M2 cases than both an independently fitted acquisition-only comparator and capacity-matched random review in each held-out year and every retained uncertainty state.

The substantive advance over the selected parent is to replace its investigator-chosen 80% per-patient visibility contract with a complete finite Pareto frontier of patient coverage versus the lowest review-value preference threshold. The earlier fixed preference is not confirmatory. Harbor maps what the data establish; an external outcome-blind preference elicitation and report-archive audit determine whether any cell is actionable.

The research-ambition demonstrations were inspected at their available level. The natural-history article supports temporal ordering and external/temporal validation discipline; the Bayesian article supports explicit longitudinal uncertainty and selection-bias accounting; the cancer supplement supports cohort partitioning, washout, calibration, missingness analysis and prospective validation. The cancer main article and full STAR Methods were unavailable and are not claimed inspected.

The primary cutoff is 24 hours; the other cutoffs are locked sensitivities.

Use possibility-based chronology quarantine across all source and reader states: possible 2020/2021 cases are removed from 2019 and development; possible 2019 cases are removed from development; 2015-2018 develops, 2019 locks, and 2020 and 2021 are separate tests. 2022 onward is audit-only. No test outcome, year, top-K status or visibility status enters preprocessing, fitting, tuning or K selection.

The endpoint is encounter-documentary M2, never biological or specimen-linked MVI. Two independent Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists and a complete adjudicator encode one indivisible same-encounter pathology form with HCC-frame membership and M2 status. OUT, IN0-A, IN1-A, IN0-U and IN1-U are retained terminals; unresolved forms stay in the outer state set. No cross-reader mixing or complete-case deletion.

Two independent Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists plus an adjudicator provide six immutable reader books, giving nine radiology-by-pathology pairs. One pair applies to every patient, year, cutoff, model, visibility mask and deletion.

## Joint frontier

For patient i, let A_i be all source-clean, nonblank, whole CT/MRI accessions attached to i and inside the imaging window. Patients with A_i empty remain in the denominator with zero report coverage. For a full-roster mask e, e=1 exposes the complete eventual unit and e=0 replaces every report-derived block by the locked unavailable block in R and G, applies the same surface mask to G-mask, and leaves B unchanged. Source-unclean units are forced zero. One e pattern is shared across arms, years, cutoffs, reader pairs and deletion projections.

Define g_i(e)=sum(e_u)/|A_i| when |A_i|>0 and 0 otherwise. Do not choose 80% as primary. Instead record the full ordered patient-coverage curve Q_e(r), the r-th order statistic of g_i over every eligible patient. This reveals concentration and zero-coverage patients. Enumerate the nondominated finite Pareto set of coverage curves and worst-state scientific results; retain the parent global-accession and 80% patient-frontier values only as regression landmarks.

Let p be an externally elicited, outcome-blind exchange rate: documentary-M2 captures per review position required to justify review versus no review. Harbor does not assign p. For each mask, compute h_G, the minimum integer G documentary-M2 captures over both test years, all admissible states and whole-patient deletions, and p_safe=h_G/K. Review versus no review is favorable only under the exact rational predicate h_G>pK; equality is not favorable. Also require every comparator and random-K contrast and every singleton coordinate to exceed the strict >5 capture margin. Compute an equivalent worst-state false-positive burden and return attained year/state/mask/deletion witnesses. The primary object is the exact finite set of (patient coverage curve, p_safe, burden, witnesses), not a calibrated risk or interpolated probability.

The Harbor result is supportive only if maximum-content G is not adverse, all exact comparator/random and singleton gates pass, and the frontier is computed with a nonempty nondominated cell. Action-compatible status additionally requires an external archive audit producing an observed patient-coverage lower curve and an outcome-blind stakeholder interval [p_L,p_U], with every audited cell satisfying p_U<p_safe. A range crossing the frontier is preference-heterogeneous, not support.

## Computation and falsification

Create a no-sampling source/filter manifest with source hashes, headers, schemas, filters, dictionaries, row counts, retained/excluded reasons and actual sampling declaration. Create protected patient, operation-state, accession, pathology, reader, model, K/tie and visibility ledgers.

Run every finite state, reader pair, mask contract and whole-patient deletion with sealed outcomes. Exact MIP/branch-and-bound or exhaustive finite enumeration is permitted. Any state cap, sampled mask family, missing branch, unresolved incumbent, timeout, rank inversion, missing witness, floating-point-only breakpoint, quotient replay failure or fixture mismatch makes the computation gate false. Require transformed-loss residual <=1e-8, complete tie accounting, raw/quotient/hash replay, synthetic brute force through 12 patients and real shards through 20. Reproduce the parent’s accession and 80% patient-frontier outputs exactly.

Emit one disjoint label: computationally_inconclusive; maximum_content_signal_adverse; availability_frontier_adverse (maximum content passes but no attainable patient-coverage cell passes); frontier_estimable_documentary_supportive; or frontier_preference_heterogeneous. Support means only universal finite-corpus superiority of the locked ranking under the declared documentary estimand. Adverse maximum-content results falsify that locked ranking claim; frontier-only adverse results show dependence on the declared coverage contract. Inconclusive never supports.

A separate operational status remains bridge_pending or not_estimable_from_HCC until a protected accession/version audit provides version and predecessor IDs/hashes, authored/final/release/amendment/retraction/ingestion/readability times, timezone provenance, exact whole-pipeline hash crosswalk, validated operation clock and denominator. Experts must adjudicate procedure dictionaries, Mandarin semantics, reader qualifications, pathology sampling/specimen linkage and biological MVI. Stakeholders must elicit preferences and burden/cost/harm. A prospective silent-mode study is required for clinician action, transportability, treatment effects, safety and patient benefit.
