# Locked episode-21 execution: persistent lactate after final-hour recorded MAP recovery

## Question, hypothesis, and result

The frozen question was whether lactate nonclearance remains a clinically material prognostic marker when recorded mean arterial pressure (MAP) appears recovered immediately before an eight-hour reassessment. The locked hypothesis was that, in the exact EICU target below, nonclearance below 20% is associated with at least a **+10 percentage-point** higher measurement-process-overlap-standardized probability of hospital death than clearance of at least 20%.

Complete-source execution reconciled the frozen population and produced a C=10 overlap-weighted risk difference (RD) of **+24.389 points**: estimated mortality was 59.655% with nonclearance and 35.266% with clearance (risk ratio 1.692). The prespecified 5,000-replicate copied-hospital/person bootstrap gave a 95% RD interval of **+6.121 to +42.882 points** and a risk-ratio interval of 1.133 to 2.768. All computational and population gates passed, and all ten leave-one-hospital-out RDs were finite and positive.

The deterministic classification is nevertheless **inconclusive**, because the RD interval contains the +10-point materiality boundary. It is not “supportive” under the frozen rule, despite the large point estimate, all-positive site deletions, and an interval excluding zero. It is not adverse to materiality because the upper bound does not exclude +10, and it is not opposite because the upper bound is not below zero.

The strongest claim these results support is a positive, noncausal prognostic association between lactate nonclearance and hospital mortality in this narrowly selected, measured-overlap EICU phenotype under the implemented uncertainty procedure. The unresolved claim remains whether the true standardized contrast is at least +10 points. A larger independent cohort with synchronized measurement is required to resolve that magnitude. No result here establishes tissue hypoperfusion, treatment benefit, a lactate target, or a causal effect.

## Clinical importance and substantive advance

A normal-range recorded MAP near a reassessment can look reassuring while metabolic abnormalities persist. A reliably large mortality gradient would justify prospectively testing a mandatory multimodal reassessment trigger; exclusion of a +10-point gradient would argue against using this EICU-defined trajectory as a major escalation signal. The present execution advances the evidence from outcome-blind feasibility and a C=1 pilot checksum to a fully reconciled C=10 estimate with the locked hierarchical uncertainty, overlap gates, site-deletion analysis, comparators, and sensitivity hierarchy.

The result does not resolve what clinicians should do. Diab et al. (Frontiers in Medicine 2025; DOI 10.3389/fmed.2025.1679297), as inspected and frozen by the parent, reported a borderline adjusted association between >10% clearance and in-hospital death in 574 single-center emergency-department patients with sepsis or septic shock (OR 0.66, 95% CI 0.42–1.04). Tóth et al. (Annals of Intensive Care 2026; DOI 10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100106), also inspected and frozen by the parent, synthesized eight trials with 2,394 shock patients and did not find significant 30- or 90-day mortality benefit from tissue-perfusion-guided therapy. Those sources motivate separating prognostic information from intervention effects; neither tests this fixed EICU landmark phenotype.

## Locked population and temporal boundaries

All offsets are integer minutes relative to ICU admission. No source rows were sampled.

1. Adults have numeric age at least 18; released age `> 89` maps to 90.
2. Within each ICU stay, T0 is the minimum `infusionDrug.infusionoffset` from 0 through 1440 whose case-folded `drugname` contains `norepinephrine` or `levophed`. Dose and rate do not determine eligibility.
3. For each `uniquepid`, the retained stay has the smallest T0, with ties broken by `patientunitstayid`.
4. The landmark is L=T0+480. Eligibility requires `unitdischargeoffset>=L`, `hospitaldischargeoffset>=L`, and exact `hospitaldischargestatus` Alive or Expired. Hospital death is exact status Expired.
5. Baseline lactate B is the latest valid collection in [T0−360,T0], numeric 0.2–30 mmol/L, revised by T0+60, and B must be at least 2. At that collection, only the greatest eligible revision is retained; multiple distinct valid values invalidate the patient, while identical duplicates collapse to the smallest `labid`.
6. Follow-up lactate F is selected from [T0+120,T0+480], revised by L. Each collection offset is resolved to its greatest eligible revision and conflicting values invalidate that offset. The selected clean offset is nearest T0+360, then earlier, then smallest `labid`. Elapsed time E=follow-up offset−T0 enters the primary model.
7. Duplicate `vitalPeriodic.observationoffset` rows collapse separately to median valid MAP (20–180) and heart rate (20–250). Final-hour recovery requires at least six unique valid-MAP offsets in [T0+420,T0+480] and at least 80% of their medians at least 65. Final-hour median MAP and fraction at least 65 are model terms.
8. Baseline context requires at least three unique offsets in [T0−60,T0) with both valid MAP and heart rate; their medians enter the model.
9. Before outcomes, hospitals require at least five clearance and five nonclearance patients. The experiment must be inconclusive if fewer than eight hospitals, 50 people, or 20 hospital deaths per group remain.

Complete scanning found 13,973 adult earliest-person qualifying landmark stays, 3,544 with valid baseline lactate, 3,974 with valid follow-up lactate, and 612 with serial lactate plus baseline vitals. Final-hour recovery retained 403 people in 69 hospitals. The outcome-blind site gate retained **216 people in ten hospitals**: 106 clearance with 39 deaths and 110 nonclearance with 62 deaths. Crude risks were 36.792% and 56.364%, respectively (RD +19.571 points; RR 1.532). The crude contrast is a comparator, not the primary inference.

The target is an eight-hour survivor population with an early norepinephrine record, clinician-selected serial lactates, baseline monitor context, final-hour recorded pressure recovery, and hospital-level trajectory overlap. It is not all shock, sepsis, vasopressor use, pressure recovery, or tissue-perfusion recovery.

## Exposure, estimand, and locked model

Relative clearance is (B−F)/B. Exposure X=1 is nonclearance below 20%; X=0 is clearance at least 20%. A rise is nonclearance. Outcome D is exact hospital discharge status Expired.

The estimand is weighted P(D=1|X=1) minus weighted P(D=1|X=0) in the measured-overlap target induced by the fixed exposure model. It is a measurement-process- and physiology-standardized prognostic association, not a causal propensity estimand.

The exposure model is scikit-learn `LogisticRegression(C=10, penalty='l2', solver='lbfgs', max_iter=10000)` with age; sex indicators including missing; B; baseline median MAP and heart rate; T0; E; final-hour median MAP and MAP-at-least-65 fraction; and retained-hospital indicators. Continuous terms are pooled 1st/99th-percentile winsorized and standardized using pooled means and population SDs. Nonclearance receives weight 1−e and clearance receives e, normalized to mean one within group. No mortality enters population selection, preprocessing, hospital gating, or exposure-model fitting.

Required comparator estimates were:

- crude RD +19.571 points;
- hospital-only C=10 overlap RD +20.843 points;
- parent-term C=10 overlap RD +23.764 points;
- legacy parent-term C=1 checksum RD +23.922 points;
- continuous-clearance C=10 mortality model OR 0.867 per 10-percentage-point higher clearance, descriptive and noncausal.

## Reconciliation, overlap, uncertainty, and falsification

All frozen source hashes matched. The independently rerun parent audit reproduced every stage/population value; only two C=1,000,000 grid ESS values drifted by less than 10^-11. The locked script’s C=1 pilot reproduced the frozen pilot to at most 2.3×10^-16. All 204,533 released numeric lactate rows in the valid 0.2–30 range had system unit `mmol/L`; exact unit enforcement did not alter the frozen counts.

Primary C=10 diagnostics were:

- propensity range 0.1519–0.8897;
- ESS 96.93 clearance and 97.95 nonclearance;
- maximum group-normalized person-weight fraction 1.693%;
- maximum hospital-weight fraction 18.767%;
- maximum absolute weighted SMD 0.0728, arising from the `sex_Unknown` indicator;
- every model-column SMD is preserved in the aggregate result file.

The bootstrap used seed 20260921 and 5,000 replicates. In each replicate, ten hospitals were sampled with replacement and assigned unique hospital-copy IDs. Within each copied hospital and exposure group, `uniquepid` clusters were sampled with replacement to the original hospital-group size and assigned unique person-copy IDs. Winsorization, standardization, copied-site encoding, and the fixed C=10 model were recomputed. Repeated copies were never collapsed. All 5,000 replicates succeeded; failure fraction was 0%.

Bootstrap intervals were:

- clearance risk 21.860%–48.769%;
- nonclearance risk 45.472%–73.984%;
- RD +6.121–+42.882 points;
- risk ratio 1.133–2.768.

All ten leave-one-hospital-out fits were finite and positive, from +20.277 to +28.403 points (10/10 positive). Exact site-deletion estimates are in the supporting CSV.

Every frozen gate passed: both ESS at least 40; propensity within 0.02–0.98; person weight at most 5%; absolute SMD at most 0.10; hospital weight at most 35%; bootstrap failure at most 5%; at least eight of ten finite site deletions; ten hospitals, 216 people, and at least 20 deaths per exposure group. Gate success establishes computational adequacy, not the +10-point claim.

The locked outcome classes retain their original meaning:

- supportive requires every gate, lower RD bound at least +10 points, and at least 80% positive finite site deletions;
- adverse to materiality requires every gate and upper RD bound below +10;
- opposite requires every gate and upper RD bound below zero, without calling nonclearance protective;
- inconclusive applies when the interval contains +10, any gate fails, or computation is undefined.

This result is inconclusive solely because +10 lies inside +6.121 to +42.882.

The consistent positive point estimates and all-positive site deletions make a sign reversal less plausible within this snapshot, but they do not tighten the primary hierarchical interval or establish the +10 margin.

## Evidence boundaries and next study

EICU cannot establish that the first matching infusion row is drug initiation or administration; distinguish transfer on ongoing norepinephrine; harmonize dose/rate reliably; identify treatment intent, fluid administration, specimen type, ordering rationale, or exact collection time; validate the monitor; measure tissue perfusion, capillary refill, mottling, mental status, cardiac output, liver clearance, seizures, beta-agonist exposure, or complete organ support; or ascertain exact death time/cause. Serial-lactate testing, landmark survival, MAP documentation, and hospital gating select the target. Recorded MAP is not organ-perfusion truth.

The computed positive association does not justify fluids, vasopressor escalation, inotropy, lactate normalization, or deployment of a trigger. Resolving the +10-point magnitude requires independent, larger data with synchronized lactates and MAP, administered vasopressor doses, fluids, urine output, capillary refill, mental status, liver function, beta-agonist exposure, treatment limitations, and organ support. Treatment effects require randomization or a credible longitudinal target trial with expert protocol review; clinical utility requires prospective impact evaluation; sepsis generalization requires infection and organ-dysfunction adjudication.

An automatic verifier can check source hashes/headers, complete scans, read-only inputs, drug matching, T0 and person ordering, landmark inequalities, unit and revision/conflict logic, follow-up tie rules, MAP/vital windows, outcome-blind site gate, reconciliation, model matrix and fixed C, weights, copied IDs/seed/replicate count, diagnostics, intervals, site deletions, sensitivities, gates, and classification. It cannot establish drug administration, specimen identity, monitor validity, perfusion, causal exchangeability, clinical rationale, sepsis, treatment benefit, transport, or utility.
