# Episode 74 successor: are repeat lactates worth fixed review capacity beyond matched surveillance?

## Proposed advance and unresolved question

The unresolved decision is not whether a late lactate is statistically associated with death. It is whether, at a fixed workload, allowing the optional repeat-lactate process and result into a mortality-review queue identifies enough additional eventual deaths to justify spending scarce review capacity on those patients rather than on the queue generated by a surveillance-matched model.

Episode 73 (parent `[prior hypothesis]`) established the stronger comparator: late non-lactate laboratory surveillance is summarized explicitly, repeat-lactate availability is retained rather than complete-casing, and all models are evaluated in hospitals excluded from their own fits. This successor preserves those source bindings, windows, models, hospital-held-out procedure, and noncausal limits. It repairs the parent’s most important decision-validity weakness: the parent’s primary contrast was a fraction of all deaths captured, which does not directly say what a reviewer gains from each occupied queue slot.

The repaired primary estimand is precision of a fixed within-hospital review queue: eventual discharge deaths per 100 eligible patients selected for review. It is operationally interpretable while remaining a prognostic, observational estimand. A hospital-macro-average stress test makes the portability question visible instead of allowing large hospitals to dominate the pooled workload result.

The falsifiable primary claim is:

> Among adults meeting the locked EICU eligibility rules below, at a 24-hour landmark after the first qualifying recorded norepinephrine/Levophed row, the hospital-held-out R_full queue (matched surveillance plus optional repeat-lactate process and value) captures at least 3 additional eventual hospital-discharge deaths per 100 fixed review slots versus R_attention (matched surveillance without repeat-lactate process/value), in the pooled 10%-per-hospital queue; the 95% lower bound is nonnegative in both prespecified repeat-availability strata and in the hospital-macro portability stress test.

The 3-per-100 threshold is a prespecified decision-relevance anchor for a modeled review workload, not a validated stakeholder utility threshold and not a claim that reviewing a flagged patient changes outcome. The primary claim is a ranking/queue claim, not an ordering, treatment, survival, or causal claim.

## What evidence supports and what remains untested

The strongest evidence available to motivate this question is narrower than the hypothesis. The inherited evidence record reports that Baysan et al. (2022, DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750; PMCID PMC9444407; frozen source [source checksum]) reported in 4,440 MIMIC patients with sepsis a recalibrated C-statistic of 0.62 for APACHE IV and 0.64 after adding 24-hour lactate. That supports possible modest prognostic information from lactate in a measured cohort. It does not support optional-result queue value, lactate-specificity after matching surveillance intensity, multicenter robustness, ordering benefit, or patient benefit.

The inherited evidence record also reports that Sisk et al. (2021, DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242; PMCID PMC7810439; frozen source [source checksum]) defines informative presence/observation as health information carried by presence, absence, or timing of routinely collected data and that many reviewed studies used missing indicators or summaries. That supports representing the measurement process. It does not establish that these particular EICU summaries remove confounding or transport across hospitals.

Thus the evidence-supported claim is: measured late lactate and observation patterns can carry prognostic information. The untested claim is the fixed-workload, surveillance-matched, hospital-held-out increment stated above. A favorable result would still be only internal EICU evidence for a prospective validation target.

The three expert-selected demonstrations in `references/research-ambition/README.md` are ambitions and availability warnings, not a topic or method requirement. In particular, no unavailable article body or supplementary method is treated as inspected; the cancer demonstration’s main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable according to that README.

## Locked predictors and four models

All models are unweighted L2 logistic regressions predicting D. No hospital ID, diagnosis, treatment, clearance, F/B value, post-L24 data, outcome-derived information, post hoc interaction, or selected feature is permitted.

### R_state: inherited clinical-state baseline

Include:

- age, age squared, Female and Male indicators, with Other/Unknown as reference;
- t0/1440 and its square;
- baseline collection lag (t0-B_offset)/360, ln(B), and ln(B)^2;
- for each fixed non-lactate laboratory stream below, the latest valid value in [t0,L24], its age (L24-offset)/1440, and a missing indicator;
- for heart rate, respiration, and SpO2, the last, median, minimum, maximum, resolved-timestamp count, and no-valid-observation indicator in [L24-360,L24].

The fixed laboratory streams, matching names/units after trim and case-fold, and hard ranges are:

- potassium | mmol/L | [1,10]
- sodium | mmol/L | [100,180]
- chloride | mmol/L | [50,150]
- BUN | mg/dL | [1,300]
- glucose | mg/dL | [20,1000]
- creatinine | mg/dL | [0.1,20]
- bicarbonate | mmol/L | [2,60]
- calcium | mg/dL | [2,20]
- Hgb | g/dL | [2,25]
- platelets x 1000 | K/mcL | [1,2000]
- WBC x 1000 | K/mcL | [0.1,500]
- albumin | g/dL | [0.5,8]
- total bilirubin | mg/dL | [0.1,50]
- paO2 | mm Hg | [20,700]
- paCO2 | mm Hg | [10,200]
- pH | blank/missing unit | [6.5,8.0].

For every laboratory stream and collection offset, require finite values, `labresultrevisedoffset <= L24`, retain the greatest eligible revision, invalidate differing final-revision ties, and collapse identical ties to smallest `labid`. Choose the latest resolved timestamp for the state value.

For `vitalPeriodic`, use `observationoffset`. Accept heart rate 20-250/min, respiration 2-80/min, and `sao2` 40-100%. Resolve each stream independently at each person/timestamp: differing valid values invalidate that stream/timestamp, and identical ties collapse to smallest `vitalperiodicid`. Counts are resolved timestamps, not export rows. These are five-minute summaries without entry, validation, or display timestamps.

### R_attention: matched non-lactate surveillance baseline

R_attention contains all R_state terms plus six summaries computed only from valid resolved non-lactate rows in [t0+720,L24], with revision deadline L24:

- ln(1 + number of resolved routine marker-time pairs), where routine means the first 13 streams through total bilirubin;
- ln(1 + number of resolved blood-gas marker-time pairs), where blood gas means paO2, paCO2, and pH;
- ln(1 + number of distinct `labresultoffset` values across all 16 streams);
- number of distinct stream keys observed divided by 16;
- recency (L24 minus the latest qualifying non-lactate `labresultoffset`)/720 when any exists;
- no-qualifying-non-lactate-row indicator; recency is missing and training-imputed when this indicator is 1.

A marker-time pair is counted after exactly the R_state range, unit, revision, conflict, and duplicate rules. Lactate is excluded. These summaries are proxies for usable recorded surveillance, not verified orders, clinician effort, concern, workload, display, or bedside viewing.

### R_process and R_full: optional-repeat additions

R_process adds to R_attention:

- `F_available`;
- F measurement age (L24-F_offset)/720 when available;
- missing-age indicator.

R_full adds ln(F) and ln(F)^2. When F is absent, these continuous terms are missing and imputed using training-fold preprocessing; `F_available` remains explicit.

The parent’s primary continuity contrast is R_full versus R_attention. R_state, R_process, the measured-only decomposition, and the inherited capture fraction remain required secondary analyses.

## Fixed-workload estimands and locked baselines

For each retained hospital h with n_h people, set k_h=ceil(0.10 n_h). For every model m, rank outer-hospital-held-out raw linear predictors within h; Q_m,h contains the k_h highest scores, with ascending `patientunitstayid` breaking exact ties. Every model therefore consumes exactly the same locked number of review slots per hospital.

Let:

- S_m,h = sum of D_i over i in Q_m,h;
- P_m,h = S_m,h / k_h, the eventual discharge deaths per review slot;
- P_m,slot(S) = (sum over h in S of S_m,h) / (sum over h in S of k_h);
- Delta_slot(S) = P_full,slot(S) - P_attention,slot(S);
- Delta_macro = mean over retained hospitals h of (P_full,h - P_attention,h).

The primary estimand is Delta_slot(all retained hospitals), interpreted as the additional number of eventual discharge deaths captured per 100 fixed review slots. Its locked materiality margin is +0.03. Because the queues have equal quotas within every hospital, the contrast cannot be manufactured by giving R_full more review slots. The hospital-macro Delta_macro is a prespecified portability stress test: it weights each hospital equally and is not the primary pooled workload estimand.

Pre-specified secondary estimands are:

- Delta_slot in low- and high-availability strata, with the same hospital quotas;
- the Episode 73 continuity metric Delta10_capture(S) = C_full(S)-C_attention(S), where C_m(S) = (sum over h in S S_m,h)/(sum over h in S sum_i D_i), the fraction of all eventual discharge deaths captured;
- Delta10_naive = R_full-R_state on the slot-precision scale and on the capture scale;
- Delta_slot_process = R_process-R_attention;
- Delta_slot_numeric_measured = R_full-R_process after refitting both models only in the inherited F-measured population, using within-hospital 10% quotas there. This conditional decomposition cannot replace the all-population primary;
- paired queue overlap, additions, removals, true-positive changes, precision, sensitivity, false positives, and unflagged deaths by model and hospital;
- a 20% queue using k_h=ceil(0.20 n_h), secondary only and unable to rescue a failed 10% primary.

No numerical threshold is claimed to represent clinical utility. If a real review policy needs costs, review burden, false reassurance, or patient benefit, those quantities require expert elicitation and a prospective impact design.

## Hospital-held-out computation

Use leave-one-hospital-out prediction on the frozen original hospital set. For each outer hospital h and model m:

1. Exclude h completely from training.
2. On remaining hospitals, winsorize continuous predictors at the training 1st/99th percentiles, standardize using training means and population standard deviations, and mean-impute missing continuous values to zero after scaling. Counts enter after their stated transformations. Do not scale binary indicators.
3. Fit unweighted logistic regression with intercept, L2 penalty, C=1, `lbfgs`, `max_iter=10000`, `tol=1e-8`, no class weighting, and seed 20260973.
4. Score h using only training-fold constants. No feature may be dropped. A feature with no finite training values, zero post-winsorization variance, nonconvergence, or nonfinite prediction makes that required model not computable.
5. Rank with the raw linear predictor. Save raw logistic probability only for descriptive AUROC, Brier score, log loss, and calibration plots. Do not call it clinically calibrated or use calibration/threshold decision curves. A positive-slope monotone recalibration cannot alter queue membership.

Save one held-out score per person and model, queue membership and quota cells, fold constants, feature-order manifest, model convergence, and a deterministic state manifest.

## Ordered falsification and interpretation

Apply exactly this order.

1. **Not computable.** This label applies to a source/header/hash mismatch; failure to reproduce the frozen flow without a documented mechanical explanation; missing required key or clock; wrong one-person, revision, conflict, unit, range, or window logic; failure of any population gate; outer-hospital leakage; altered feature list; incomplete predictions or quotas; nonfinite/nonconverged required fits; or fewer than 1,900 finite bootstrap replicates for any required primary contrast. Do not substitute random person splits, complete-case primary analysis, calibration-based ranks, changed windows, post hoc features, or a different outcome.

2. **Supportive, actionable within-snapshot and portability-stressed.** All gates pass; the pooled 10% Delta_slot 95% lower bound is >=+0.03; low- and high-availability Delta_slot lower bounds are >=0; Delta_macro lower bound is >=0; at least 80% of pooled, 70% of each stratum, and 70% of macro deletion estimates are positive; and the 20% pooled slot contrast is not opposite. This supports only additional modeled eventual-death capture per fixed review slot beyond the specified surveillance comparator in this EICU snapshot, with the stated internal site stress test.

3. **Pooled actionable but site-process-dependent.** The pooled Delta_slot lower bound is >=+0.03, but the macro lower bound or an availability-stratum lower bound is <0, or its deletion criterion fails. This opposes the portability part of the hypothesis and bars an internally site-robust interpretation.

4. **Surveillance-explained adverse result.** Delta_slot_naive reaches the +0.03 margin, but Delta_slot_attention has an upper bound <+0.03. The apparent increment does not survive the locked non-lactate surveillance comparator. This does not prove that clinician attention causes the naive signal.

5. **Opposite direction.** The pooled primary Delta_slot upper bound is <0, or both availability-stratum upper bounds are <0. The proposed benefit direction is falsified for this population, comparator, quota, and outcome.

6. **Adverse to materiality.** The pooled primary interval is wholly nonnegative but its upper bound is <+0.03, with no opposite stratum. This rejects the locked material increment while allowing a smaller nonnegative association.

7. **Inconclusive.** Every other computable result, including an interval spanning zero or +0.03, discordant but non-opposite strata, unstable deletions, a favorable point estimate without required bounds, or a macro stress test that cannot satisfy the supportive rule.

Attach one descriptive mechanism label without changing the primary state:

- **numeric component supported** only if Delta_slot_numeric_measured has lower bound >0;
- **process-dependent** if the primary is supportive but the numeric component upper bound <=0 or the point estimate of Delta_slot_process is at least as large as the point estimate of the full primary;
- **numeric component unresolved** otherwise.

A supportive result justifies an independently designed prospective validation of a fixed-capacity optional-result review queue. It does not justify ordering repeat lactates, withholding lactates, displaying a score at bedside, changing treatment, or claiming survival benefit. Surveillance-explained, adverse, or opposite results argue against treating a retrospective increment as lactate-specific. Inconclusive means this EICU snapshot cannot resolve the locked margin.
