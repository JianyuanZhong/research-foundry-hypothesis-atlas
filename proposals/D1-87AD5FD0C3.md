## Scientific question and present evidence

The frozen unresolved claim is: among transfers with the same measured endpoint state at `t0`, does unusually worsening rather than improving recent measured-value history change calibrated 48-hour ICU-return/death risk and fixed-capacity ranking, particularly when state and direction disagree?

The strongest existing evidence supports only bounded premises. A longitudinal ICU-discharge model outperformed a snapshot score while leaving operational factors, DNR status and composite components unresolved [K1]. A systematic review found longitudinal predictors can help, but windows, populations, outcomes and bias vary [K2]. Observation timing/frequency can outperform measured values because EHR records encode healthcare processes [K3]. Inherited audits establish 49,157 transfers/2,607 raw events, approximate-era event support and broad repeated-measure feasibility. Neither the literature, inherited audits nor this outcome-blind audit establishes a direction effect.

The strongest rival remains mathematical coupling/regression to the mean: raw G compares early and late values while C contains final values from the late period. Treatment and clinician-triggered measurement can mimic the same pattern. A supportive experiment would advance understanding by showing that ordered measured-value history—not merely an endpoint reused in a difference—changes a consequential worklist. It would still not identify physiology, treatment response or intervention benefit.

## Completed outcome-blind support audit

The executable `work/audit_coupling_support_direct.py` reads only pre-outcome fields from the exact MIMIC-IV 3.1 archive; the aggregate contract is `work/coupling-support-audit.json`. No later return, post-t0 death, live-discharge class, 48-hour label or note is read.

A logical error in the parent gate is corrected. Raw-G eligibility requires, per eligible domain, >=2 observed bins in each six-hour half: at least four observed bins. Freezing the final observed bin therefore necessarily leaves >=3 nonfinal positions. Thus every raw-G eligible row passes the *positional* movable-bin rule in >=4 domains. Reporting a separate measured percentage as if this remained an independent feasibility gate is redundant.

The audit instead prespecifies an effective-permutation power diagnostic, measured pooled and in every strict approximate-era role: >=4 raw-G domains each have (a) >=3 nonfinal positions, (b) >=2 distinct exact nonfinal bin summaries and (c) nonfinal support on both sides of the raw-G half boundary. Failure does not invalidate permutation logic, but it means permutations cannot often change G and order attribution lacks power.

G-pre support is measured exactly without outcomes: bins `[t0-12,-10)`, `[-10,-8)`, `[-8,-6)`, `[-6,-4)` must all be observed in >=4 domains. E0-4 uses only `[t0-4,0)`; half-open intervals make chart-row overlap impossible. Gate: >=70% G-pre coverage in pooled test and each locked late role.

## Coupling controls and matched methods

### G-perp and transparent D1-perp

Construct Z from the six endpoint margins/freshness/missingness and C; destination, latest service, ICU source, clock and LOS; per-half bin/hour counts, store lag and caregiver count; and ventilation, vasopressor and oxygen transitions in `[t0-12h,t0)`. Fit additive ridge and depth-3 histogram boosting for `m(Z)=E(G|Z)` without R+D labels. Select only by subject-grouped development RMSE. Use out-of-fold development residuals and one frozen development fit thereafter:

`G_perp=(G-m_hat(Z))/SD_development(G-m_hat(Z))`.

Retain the parent's balance gates: absolute standardized residual-on-nuisance slope <0.05, |Spearman(G-perp,C)|<0.05, and no Z block explains >1% additional residual variance in validation and each evaluation role. Fit `D1-perp=E0+spline(G_perp)+C×G_perp` with the frozen multinomial ridge, calibration, interaction grid, quotas and uncertainty.

D1-perp reveals direction and material re-ranking that are unusual at a matched endpoint and recorded process context. Its scalar summary is transparent but loses within-window order, cross-domain lag, cancellation and nonlinear relapse. Balance does not prove error-free measurement or physiology.

### Temporally disjoint G-pre

For each domain, `G_pre,j=median([t0-8,-4))-median([t0-12,-8))`; require both two-hour bins in each half and >=4 domains. Fit the same E0 specification using final-four-hour endpoint values only and then `D1-pre=E0-4+spline(G_pre)+C-4×G_pre`. This is shared-row-disjoint corroboration. A null G-pre result alone is inconclusive because a true effect may be confined to the final four hours.

### Endpoint-fixed within-patient null

Freeze each patient/domain's final observed bin/value, missing positions, chart/store times, caregiver identities, C, outcome, split, era, and process/treatment stream. Permute only that patient's nonfinal observed bin summaries; never borrow across patients/domains. Recompute G and refit D1 under the same protocol for 200 predeclared seeds plus exact nonfinal reversal. Compare observed-minus-median-null interaction, Brier and fixed-quota capture with paired clustered intervals.

The effective-permutation audit reports null power. If >=70% of G-eligible rows in an evaluation role have >=2 unique nonfinal assignments in >=4 domains, run Monte Carlo as planned. Otherwise enumerate all unique assignments per domain when <=120 and sample otherwise, record each row's assignment count, and report power-weighted and all-row contrasts. The all-row contrast remains primary. If fewer than 70% have >=2 unique assignments in four domains, order attribution is inconclusive even if G-pre predicts.

Cross-patient donor histories, sign flips, residual bootstrap and time jitter are rejected: they do not preserve recipient severity, endpoint coupling or the observed value multiset and cannot distinguish the specified rival.

### Learned alternative: two-stream causal TCN

Retain L2-D on the same twelve two-hour final-24-hour bins and E0 static context. Value stream: standardized observed/forward-filled values without masks/gaps. Process stream: masks, deltas, counts, store lag and caregivers without measured values. Static stream: E0. Retain two causal convolution layers, kernel 3, dilations 1/2, 32/64 channels, dropout 0/0.2, Adam 1e-3/3e-4, five seeds, validation early stopping and temperature scaling; target/splits/eras/quotas/uncertainty match D1-perp. Mandatory heads are full, value+static, process+static and static-only; apply the exact endpoint-fixed value permutation while process remains fixed.

L2-D can reveal nonlinear late relapse, cross-domain lag and opposing-organ patterns that G-perp averages away. It cannot identify latent physiology merely because it is learned. Select it only if calibrated/seed-stable, superior to D1-perp in both late eras, and value/full gains and discordance shifts exceed process-only and endpoint-fixed null heads. It cannot rescue a coupling-adverse raw result.

## Scientifically valid fallback tree

**If G-pre fails but raw-G and order-null support pass:** keep the same population/estimand and replace disjoint-window corroboration with a conjunctive requirement that raw and G-perp signals persist in both no-recorded-support-change and observation-stable sensitivities. G-pre remains reported as unavailable/inconclusive; do not relax domains or windows.

**Outcome-blind reliability/repeated-measures fallback:** optionally fit a development-only hierarchical domain measurement model with patient-domain latent level/time trend and residual variance by density/store-lag strata. Generate posterior direction mean/SD and fit errors-in-variables D1-R under the same C×direction estimand. Require posterior predictive calibration and reliability >=0.20 in >=4 domains for >=70% of G-eligible rows in each evaluation role. This can stabilize attenuation and expose uncertainty; it cannot distinguish order from endpoint coupling and is valid only beside the endpoint-fixed null and quiet/stable sensitivities. Partial pooling may not borrow histories across patients for the null or change the exposure to a new latent-state estimand.

**If effective order-null support fails but G-pre passes:** D1-pre may support a row-disjoint prognostic result, but the stronger claim that observed fine-grained order exceeds coupling remains inconclusive. Reliability/shrinkage does not repair this attribution failure.

**If raw G coverage <70% in any evaluation role, or G-pre and effective order-null plus quiet/stable supports all fail:** defer the coupling-resolved hypothesis. Retain E0/S0 and parent trajectory analyses. Do not impute G, reduce the four-domain rule, widen windows, pool locked roles, substitute donor histories or ask L2-D to rescue support. Needed evidence is denser independent monitoring or another cohort. Coverage failure is not evidence against the hypothesis.

## Outputs and decision rules

Add `coupling-support-audit.json`, audit script/hash/job receipt, G-pre coverage, unique-assignment distribution, exact nonoverlap assertion, fallback choice manifest, reliability diagnostics if invoked, and inherited orthogonalization/permutation/coupling outputs. Freeze choices before outcomes.

Supportive evidence requires supportive raw D1; balanced transported D1-perp with same-sign interval excluding zero in pooled test and both late roles; positive observed-minus-null interaction/Brier intervals; >=2 additional R+D captures per 1,000 beyond null at a supported quota without worse overall capture; endpoint/adjudicated concordance; and either supported G-pre or *both* quiet and observation-stable corroboration. Learned claims must additionally exceed process-only and permuted-value heads. This supports ordered measured-value information, not mechanism or benefit.

Coupling-adverse: null reproduces raw D1, time reversal does not attenuate/reverse, quota advantage upper bounds are <2/1,000, and balanced D1-perp excludes material benefit. Treatment/process-adverse: quiet/stable effects disappear or reverse while process-only/active-treatment patterns reproduce ranking. Endpoint sufficiency retains upper bounds <0.002 Brier and <2/1,000 at every quota.

Inconclusive includes failed support/balance, low unique-assignment power, material imprecision, role/calibration or endpoint disagreement, and unstable learned seeds. G-pre null alone is not refutation. No subgroup/window/null is changed after outcomes.
