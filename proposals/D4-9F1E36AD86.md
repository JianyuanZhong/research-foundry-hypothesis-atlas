# UKB 38: Cancer and vascular risk trade-off associated with long telomeres

Admission: feasibility_required. Verify participant overlap, measurement dates, missingness and event support before committing this design.

## Hypotheses to Be Tested

At the same age and level of inflammation, longer leukocyte telomeres may correspond to a lower risk of arterial events, but not to a lower risk of some cancers.

## Study population and key data

Individuals with telomere, biochemical, and follow-up data; lung cancer, colorectal cancer, and coronary heart disease prespecified.

## Basic Approach

Compare nonlinear associations of telomeres with different outcomes, adjust for cell composition and check for lag; supplement with genetic analyses when suitable instruments are available.

## Significance of the Research

Test for potential trade-offs between cellular replicative capacity and different diseases.

## Main Challenges

Existing literature addresses this direction; the incremental contribution needs to lie in cancer type and cell composition; genetic analyses are limited by pleiotropy.

## References

## Life Sciences

Tumor development, host reserve, and hematopoiesis

## Competing Explanations

Smoking, cell composition, and potential tumor explanations differ in direction.

## Minimal validation and falsification criteria

First compare results before and after cell-composition adjustment and after excluding recent events; if the direction is unstable, this does not support a trade-off interpretation.
