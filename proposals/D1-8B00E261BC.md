# Calibrated departure availability versus content fidelity in incidental pulmonary-nodule handoff

## Decision question and substantive advance

When an incidental pulmonary-nodule CT recommendation is known before hospital discharge, should a health system first invest in a discharge-artifact finalization/availability check or in structured radiology-to-discharge content reconciliation? The parent design measured two useful but different MIMIC states: a discharge note whose `storetime` is after `dischtime`, and a note stored by `dischtime` whose text is not faithful to the recommendation. The unresolved clinical construct is whether the former was actually unavailable at the patient's physical departure. A note-store timestamp is not a visibility, finalization, transmission, or departure timestamp.

This child makes a synchronized prospective timestamp/visibility crosswalk part of the primary decision estimand. MIMIC remains a complete, frozen retrospective discovery and triage stage; it is not allowed by itself to select an availability intervention. A prospective validation stage estimates the mapping from the MIMIC proxy `S=I(storetime <= dischtime)` to true departure-time availability `V`, and then links that calibrated state to the same all-opportunity content states. The intervention decision is based on the calibrated/partially identified `Delta_true`, not on `Delta_store` alone.

The substantive advance is therefore not a new clinical cohort or a new surrogate outcome. It is a construct-valid two-stage experiment: (1) preserve the MIMIC all-opportunity decomposition and future-information protections, (2) obtain a prospective event-log gold standard at physical departure, (3) quantify misclassification of `storetime`, and (4) propagate that uncertainty into the availability-versus-content decision. If the crosswalk cannot establish the mapping, the result is explicitly a storage-timestamp finding and the intervention choice remains inconclusive.

## Evidence boundary and falsifiable claims

Published evidence supports a documentation/communication problem but not a causal benefit claim. Kwan et al. reported absent interval imaging in 35/65 patients with explicit follow-up suggestions and an association between discharge-summary mention and timely imaging (PMID 30794133; PMCID PMC6625441). A 2023 tracking-program report observed 34/67 follow-up scans after implementation versus 16/52 before implementation (PMCID PMC10287591), while a 2025 systematic review found promising but heterogeneous tracking interventions with serious or critical risk of bias in most nonrandomized studies (PMID 40081655; PMCID PMC12264345). These findings motivate the process question only. They do not establish recommendation appropriateness, communication, patient receipt, adherence, outside care, causality, or patient benefit.

The frozen MIMIC audit found 2,321,355 radiology rows and 1,194 first-subject lexical candidates; these are screening proxies, not clinical labels. The complete discharge audit found 331,794 `DS` rows: 86,609 stored by discharge, 147,800 after discharge through 24 hours, 76,977 from 24 hours through 7 days, 20,329 after 7 days, and 17 with missing `storetime`; every classifiable delayed row had `charttime <= dischtime`. It also found 25,735 reciprocal report/addendum edges, 25,600 fully resolved chronological same-admission `RR -> AR` pairs, and 135 edges involving absent nodes. The discharge-detail audit contains only masked `author` rows and no usable sections. These audits support feasibility and a potentially important timestamp mismatch; they do not validate `storetime` as availability.

The untested retrospective signal is `Delta_store = P(S=0) - P(S=1 and content failure)` over the same eligible-opportunity denominator. The clinically decision-valid target is:

`Delta_true = P(V=0) - P(V=1 and C)`,

where `V=1` means the recommendation-bearing discharge artifact was finalized and retrievable in the intended clinician-facing discharge workflow at the patient's actual physical departure, and `C` means a content omission or incomplete/discordant plan among truly available artifacts. `R` (explicit alternate resolution) is not C. Both terms are risks over the same all-opportunity population; this is not a comparison of two selected cohorts. The primary hypothesis is `Delta_true > 0.05`, with sensitivity margins 0 and 0.10. The 0.05 margin is an operational prioritization margin, not a clinical quality threshold.

The prospective crosswalk has its own falsifiable hypothesis: conditional on observable strata, `S` is an imperfect but useful classifier of `V`; specifically, the crosswalk must estimate `P(V=0|S=0)` and `P(V=0|S=1)` with two-sided 95% interval widths no greater than 0.10 and demonstrate that neither is compatible with an arbitrary mapping that reverses the intervention decision. This is a validation requirement, not an assumed result.

A DS predating the qualifying recommendation is retained and tagged as stale-artifact chronology; it is classified by text and not interpreted as writer awareness. An artifact stored after discharge remains `A_store` even if later text is faithful. Construct +24-hour and +7-day transitions without retroactively changing strict states. Use exact calendar arithmetic (`relativedelta`) from base `charttime` for the due date.

Two radiology-trained reviewers independently adjudicate lexical candidates blinded to discharge text and later outcomes; two transition reviewers independently abstract DS text blinded to radiology text, exact recommendation, chronology, and later care. Confirmatory gates are complete consensus, raw agreement >=0.80 and AC1 >=0.70 in each domain; otherwise revise once and use reviewer-specific bounds if persistent. Use 9,999 fixed-seed subject-level bootstrap replicates, recomputing p_Astore, p_Cstore, p_Fstore, the conditional content vector and Delta_store. ## Prospective timestamp/visibility crosswalk

### Target setting and sampling frame

Before viewing prospective outcomes, register a consecutive local-EHR implementation period and the exact eligible-opportunity algorithm ported from the frozen MIMIC protocol. The prospective population is every adult inpatient admission during that period meeting the same clinical opportunity definition: an incidental, adjudicated pulmonary-nodule CT recommendation for 3, 6, or 12 calendar months known before physical departure. The target departure is the ADT-recorded physical exit time `D_phys`, not an intended discharge order time. Do not use later imaging, follow-up, or intervention exposure to define eligibility.

Use a two-phase sample. Phase 1 captures all eligible opportunities during the registered period and all associated report, addendum, discharge-artifact, and event-log timestamps. Phase 2 obtains gold-standard observation on a prespecified stratified sample, with mandatory inclusion of every case in rare or safety-critical strata and random sampling within common strata. Strata are `S=0` versus `S=1` based on the locally computed storetime rule, missing/ambiguous storetime, artifact-before-recommendation chronology, service/ICU exposure, discharge destination, and artifact version complexity. Oversampling `S=0` is allowed, but sampling probabilities and all exclusions are recorded before gold-standard review; inverse-probability weights transport estimates to all Phase-1 opportunities. A minimum target is 250 gold-standard cases per S stratum when available, including at least 100 S=0 cases; if fewer occur, report precision and do not claim a decision. The sample is not a convenience sample of delayed notes or patients who remain in the system.

### Exact prospective data contract

For each eligible opportunity, require a row-level, immutable identifier linking `(local_patient_id, local_encounter_id, recommendation_id, discharge_artifact_id, artifact_version_id)` and a manifest of source-system clocks. Store event times in one timezone with UTC offset and retain raw values, event source, clock resolution, and clock synchronization status. The contract must contain:

1. **Clinical and boundary events:** encounter/admission identifier; admission time; actual physical departure `D_phys` from ADT/bed-exit or transport-confirmed log; discharge order time; intended discharge time; death/cancellation status; and departure destination. Intended/order times may describe discordance but cannot substitute for `D_phys`.
2. **Recommendation lineage:** report identifier and version; report creation/chart time; addendum parent/child identifiers; addendum creation and finalization times; recommendation availability event; and exact report-state text or an expert-adjudicated equivalent. The lineage service must expose both directions of every report/addendum relation, not infer relation from row order.
3. **Canonical discharge artifact:** artifact identifier and every version; draft creation time; last content-edit time; save/store time; signature time; finalization/lock time; cancellation/reopening time; publication time; first retrievable-in-UI time; first retrievable-in-discharge-workflow time; and version supersession relation. The prospective primary artifact is the version that was designated canonical at `D_phys`, not simply the latest version after departure.
4. **Visibility and transmission:** append-only UI audit events showing the artifact was retrievable by the responsible inpatient team at `D_phys`; user/team role and access context; patient-portal release time; electronic transmission time and delivery acknowledgement when applicable; print/export or receiving-facility handoff time when used; and a separate indicator for failed/queued transmission. Content of audit logs is not silently interpreted as a human reading the artifact.
5. **Physical-departure observation:** an observer or system event, recorded independently of the discharge author, confirms whether the canonical artifact was final and retrievable immediately before or at `D_phys`. If physical observation is impossible, the record is not gold standard; it enters an unresolved category and sensitivity bounds. A retrospective screen capture or audit entry after departure cannot back-impute availability.
6. **Content adjudication:** the full canonical artifact text as rendered at departure, the recommendation-bearing report state available by `D_phys`, and blinded expert labels for faithful, omission, incomplete/discordant, explicit alternate resolution, unreadable, and recommendation not yet available. Experts do not infer patient receipt, adherence, appropriateness, or benefit.
7. **Quality metadata:** missingness, duplicate event IDs, clock drift, event ordering contradictions, sampling stratum, sampling probability, reviewer IDs, and adjudication version. No event may be overwritten; corrections are append-only.

The minimum audit trail must permit independent reconstruction of whether, at `D_phys`, the canonical artifact was (a) final/locked, (b) retrievable in the intended clinician-facing workflow, and (c), as a separate secondary state, released/transmitted to the patient or receiving service. The primary `V` is (a)+(b). `V_recipient` is reported separately and must not be conflated with clinician availability. If the clinical workflow defines a different intended recipient, register that definition before sampling.

### Prospective gold-standard states

For each Phase-2 case define `V=1` only when the canonical artifact was final/locked and retrievable in the intended discharge workflow at `D_phys`, with all required event fields observed and no contradiction. Define `V=0` when the artifact was not final or not retrievable at `D_phys`, with the reason (not created, draft only, locked later, UI inaccessible, publication queue, or other audited category). Define `V_U` for missing, contradictory, unsynchronized, or unverifiable clocks; do not force V_U to V=0. Separately define `V_recipient=1` only for documented release/transmission by departure, and retain it as a secondary process state. Neither V definition means a clinician or patient actually read or understood the text.

For the same N-like all-opportunity denominator, classify mutually exclusive prospective states as `G` (V=0), `F_V` (V=1 and faithful), `C_VO` (V=1 and omission), `C_VI` (V=1 and incomplete/discordant), `R_V` (V=1 and explicit alternate resolution), and `U_V` (unresolved). If the recommendation was not available by `D_phys`, it is not an eligible opportunity; if an addendum first becomes available after `D_phys`, it is future information and cannot repair eligibility. A later artifact may be described in transition matrices but never redefine the departure state. If the artifact was created before the recommendation and is stale, classify it as a content state when V=1 and tag stale chronology; if V=0, classify G and retain the content observed later only as a post-departure transition.

### Crosswalk estimands and calibration

The prospective crosswalk estimates, in each registered stratum and after inverse-probability weighting to all Phase-1 opportunities,

- sensitivity `Se=P(S=0 | V=0)` and specificity `Sp=P(S=1 | V=1)` for the storage proxy as a classifier of true departure availability;
- `q0=P(V=0 | S=0)` and `q1=P(V=0 | S=1)`, with exact/weighted 95% intervals;
- the prevalence `g=P(V=0)` and true content risks `c=P(V=1,C_VO or C_VI)` and `f=P(V=1,F_V)`; and
- the decision contrast `Delta_true=g-c` over all eligible opportunities.

Do not algebraically correct for misclassification unless the gold-standard verification sample is representative after known sampling weights and clock-quality exclusions are accounted for. The primary estimator is a survey-weighted multinomial estimator or augmented estimator with stratum-specific crosswalk probabilities, clustered by patient if a patient contributes multiple opportunities. Use 9,999 fixed-seed patient-level bootstrap replicates that repeat Phase-2 sampling weights, calibration, and the final contrast. Report a matrix of observed S by gold V and full state transitions, not only sensitivity/specificity.

If gold-standard data are incomplete, use partial identification rather than an unverifiable missing-at-random correction. For each S stratum, permit `q_s` to range over the exact Clopper-Pearson or weighted score interval supported by observed gold cases, widened for `V_U`; if an entire prospective stratum lacks validation, set `q_s` over [0,1] unless an externally registered bound is justified. Propagate these ranges to bounds for `g`, `c`, and `Delta_true` using linear programming over the S-by-V-by-content transition table while preserving mutually exclusive totals. Report a calibration-corrected point estimate only as secondary; the primary decision uses the full 95% uncertainty/bounds. A result that depends on an untestable missing-at-random assumption is labeled sensitivity analysis, never definitive.

Calibration acceptance requires: at least 100 gold cases in each observed S stratum when available; raw agreement >=0.95 for event-log reconstruction of final/retrievable V against independent physical-departure review; no unresolved/contradictory clock state >5% of validated cases; and each of `q0` and `q1` estimated with interval width <=0.10 or a decision that remains unchanged across its wider bound. These are feasibility/construct gates, not claims of clinical importance. If physical departure cannot be independently anchored, the crosswalk fails and MIMIC remains a storage-timestamp study.

### Primary intervention-selection estimand and thresholds

The primary decision estimand is the weighted prospective `Delta_true = g-c` in the target eligible population, with MIMIC used to define the opportunity algorithm, estimate which strata merit validation, and provide a retrospective transport check. A positive availability-first recommendation requires all of the following:

1. the two-sided family-wise 95% lower bound for `Delta_true` exceeds +0.05;
2. the full partial-identification interval, including V_U and sampling uncertainty, remains above +0.05;
3. the crosswalk acceptance gates pass, and `q0/q1` do not show that late `storetime` is usually available at departure;
4. at least 200 prospective eligible opportunities, at least 20 true G and 20 true content-failure events, and a 95% interval width <=0.10; and
5. no prespecified stratum with adequate data reverses the direction in a clinically consequential way.

A content-first recommendation requires the two-sided family-wise 95% upper bound for `Delta_true` below -0.05, the same gates, and evidence that nonfaithful states are not predominantly explicit alternate resolutions. If the point estimate crosses a threshold but bounds do not, the result is inconclusive and supports a bundled or 2-by-2 factorial workflow experiment. If `Delta_store` supports availability but the crosswalk shows high `q1` (most S=1 artifacts were truly unavailable) or low `q0` (most S=0 artifacts were already visible/final), the storage signal is falsified as an intervention-selection proxy; prioritize timestamp/pipeline redesign or obtain better data rather than calling it a discharge availability failure.

The prospective study is not randomized and cannot estimate intervention effect. Once a mechanism is selected, a separately registered stepped-wedge, cluster-randomized, or factorial trial with complete outpatient and outside-system capture is required to estimate effects on communication, follow-up, cancer outcomes, harm, or patient benefit. The crosswalk estimates construct validity and process burden only.

## Statistical analysis and falsification

For MIMIC, retain the parent 9,999 subject bootstrap, all-opportunity denominator, exact calendar-month arithmetic, reciprocal-chain reconstruction, strict cutoff, delayed transitions, U_R/U_D reporting, and observation-aware surveillance appendix. Never use a future addendum, later DS version, downstream imaging, or continued trace to alter eligibility or departure exposure.

For the prospective stage, describe the full Phase-1 cascade and all sampling/filtering: number of admissions screened, lexical/metadata candidates, expert exclusions, N, S states, sampled strata, sampling probabilities, V_U reasons, and every content state. Use survey-weighted multinomial risks and patient-cluster bootstrap. Prespecify no covariate adjustment for `Delta_true`; service, ICU, discharge destination, and artifact complexity are descriptive transport strata. Use exact or score intervals for sparse cells and simultaneous max-|t| intervals for `(g,c,Delta_true)` and the six-state vector. A Bayesian model may be a secondary presentation only if prior sensitivity and its partial-identification counterpart are shown.

Falsification checks are deliberately adversarial:

- **Clock-order placebo:** perturb or permute `storetime`, finalization, UI visibility, and departure labels within audit-prespecified windows. A claimed crosswalk that remains unchanged under permutations is not measuring the intended construct.
- **Negative timestamp controls:** compare event-log availability for artifacts unrelated to pulmonary-nodule recommendations and for documents known to be auto-published. A large unexplained association specific only to note type may indicate workflow logging artifacts rather than availability.
- **Departure-anchor check:** compare ADT physical exit with transport/bed-exit confirmation. Material disagreement or retrospective rounding invalidates the strict V state and widens bounds.
- **Version/addendum leakage check:** independently regenerate report state using only events with true availability <= `D_phys`; inject post-departure addenda and confirm that eligibility and V do not change.
- **Visibility reproducibility:** two independent auditors replay the intended UI or use immutable access logs. Disagreement or an audit entry without retrievability is unresolved, not V=1.
- **Sampling/transport check:** reweighting diagnostics, leave-one-stratum-out calibration, and bounds under worst-case unvalidated strata. A decision that changes under plausible sampling weights is inconclusive.
- **Content blinding check:** content reviewers do not see V, S, later imaging, or intervention assignment until labels are locked. Stale artifacts and explicit alternate resolutions are separately adjudicated.
- **Surveillance placebo:** retain the parent pre-index and generic non-chest imaging controls. A same-direction placebo as large as the nodule-focused signal, acute imaging driving the association, or differential observation states makes surveillance interpretation inconclusive; it cannot rescue intervention selection.

## Interpretation matrix

- **Supportive for availability-first:** calibrated `Delta_true` lower bound and all partial-identification bounds exceed +0.05; true departure unavailability is common among MIMIC `S=0` and uncommon among `S=1`; gates and falsification checks pass. This supports testing a departure-time finalization/retrievability check, not a claim that anyone failed to communicate.
- **Storage proxy adverse:** MIMIC `Delta_store` is positive but prospective `S=0` artifacts are usually final/retrievable at departure, or `S=1` artifacts are often not. The MIMIC signal is a storage/export timestamp phenomenon; availability intervention selection is falsified. Seek pipeline semantics or a bundled intervention.
- **Content-first supportive:** calibrated upper bound for `Delta_true` is below -0.05, true availability is established, and omissions/discordance—not alternate resolutions—dominate. Test structured reconciliation/insertion, including stale-artifact updating.
- **Coexisting vulnerabilities:** both true G and true C are substantial or the contrast crosses the margin. Do not double-count an opportunity; retain one mutually exclusive state and test a factorial/bundled strategy.
- **Adverse process result:** both risks are low and precise, or most apparent content failures are explicit alternate resolutions. Neither artifact mechanism is the main observable bottleneck; tracking, scheduling, communication, or cross-system exchange needs a new study.
- **Inconclusive:** crosswalk fails, V_U is substantial, bounds cross the threshold, event/agreement/precision gates fail, departure cannot be independently anchored, or results reverse across registered strata. Report the storage signal and obtain better synchronized data; do not choose an intervention from a point estimate.
- **Secondary surveillance:** only an all-N, common-clock, observation-aware association with successful annotation, specificity, and missingness checks can be described; it remains same-system observation, not adherence, causality, or benefit.

## Limits and verifier requirements

The automatic verifier can check the MIMIC snapshot and source hashes, exact source paths and archive member, catalog schema fields, all-row scan, reciprocal graph, sole-DS rule, first-subject selection, strict `storetime <= dischtime` state, future-addendum exclusion, stale chronology, exact calendar arithmetic, mutually exclusive denominators, bootstrap and partial-identification calculations, prospective sampling probabilities, event-time ordering, V-state definitions, weighted crosswalk matrix, threshold gates, and whether reported conclusions follow from computed intervals and bounds. It can also test that conclusions unsupported by the computed crosswalk are rejected.

Clinical/radiology experts and local workflow auditors are required to adjudicate incidental status, CT coverage, unconditionality, exact interval, addendum meaning, faithful content, alternate resolution, physical departure, finalization, intended UI retrievability, and transmission semantics. MIMIC cannot establish what clinicians or patients saw, signature or UI visibility, actual physical departure, discussion, orders, referrals, appointments, outside imaging, adherence, recommendation appropriateness, cancer outcome, radiation harm, survival, or patient benefit. Even prospective event logs cannot establish that a person read, understood, or acted on an artifact. A randomized prospective study with complete cross-system outcome capture and clinical adjudication is required for causal or patient-benefit conclusions.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

This child preserves the parent candidate's exact MIMIC bindings, full radiology scan, reciprocal report/addendum reconstruction, strict `dischtime` boundary, one-final-DS rule, all-opportunity denominator, mutually exclusive `A/F/C_O/C_I/R/U_D` states, blinded content adjudication, exact interval concordance, future-addendum protection, common follow-up clock, observation-aware limits, partial-identification bounds, and explicit evidence limits.

The substantive repair is that prospective timestamp/visibility validation is now part of the primary decision rather than an optional caveat. It specifies a physical-departure anchor, finalization and retrievability event contract, recipient transmission as a distinct secondary state, stratified sampling and weights, gold-standard and unresolved states, calibrated estimands, uncertainty propagation, threshold gates, adversarial falsification, and decision interpretations. `Delta_store` remains a useful retrospective triage signal; only `Delta_true` can nominate an availability versus content intervention.

Uncertainty remains about local event-log completeness, clock synchronization, the intended recipient and UI, undocumented alternate plans, the transportability of a local crosswalk to MIMIC, sparse eligible opportunities, outside care, recommendation appropriateness, and clinical benefit. If any of these prevent a defensible V state, the correct scientific result is bounded or inconclusive—not an inferred communication failure or intervention effect.
