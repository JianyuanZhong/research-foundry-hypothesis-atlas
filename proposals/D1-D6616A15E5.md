# Is the postoperative NUC recurrence benefit PHASE-SELECTIVE? An early (<=24-mo, occult-residual) vs late (>24-mo, de-novo) recurrence-hazard decomposition with a signed NUC x HBV-DNA stratum contrast

**No HR, IPTW weight, CATE or Cox fit is computed in discovery.**

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
After curative resection of HBV-related HCC the recurring decision is whether to start/continue
a nucleos(t)ide analogue (NUC). Antivirals cut new HCC incidence, yet HCC after hepatectomy
recurs through two mechanistically distinct routes - early **occult residual disease** vs late
**de novo carcinogenesis** ([K3]). The closest prior work finds the NUC *regimen* was not
associated with RFS after resection ([K1]), and a 410-patient cohort found a naive
antiviral-RFS benefit that **disappeared after multivariable adjustment** ([K2]). The unresolved
question is not "do antivirals help at all" but: **is any protective NUC effect on recurrence
phase-selective - concentrated in the early, replication-driven window and larger where baseline
viral replication is active - or is it a phase-uniform, stratum-flat artifact of
confounding-by-indication/surveillance?** Resolving it decides whether NUC status should be
treated as an early-phase recurrence-modifying exposure and whether HBV-DNA stratifies it.

## 2. Inherited claim vs rivals (parent, unchanged)
- **H_effect (leading):** NUC started in the early postoperative window lowers early (<=24-mo)
  recurrence, with a benefit **larger where replication is active** (higher baseline HBV-DNA).
- **R_indication (strongest rival):** the crude association is confounding by indication -
  sicker, higher-viral-load, better-monitored patients are more likely put on NUC, so any
  "benefit" tracks prognostic factors/surveillance and vanishes once balanced ([K2]).
- **R_null:** no causal effect; null after overlap weighting.
- **R_detect (rival):** apparent benefit is a follow-up-intensity/detection artifact.

## 3. ADDED ELEMENT (the one change): phase-decomposed hazard + signed stratum contrast
Inside the parent's target-trial-emulation Cox on time since landmark t0, replace the single NUC
term by a **piecewise/time-varying hazard** with an early-phase indicator `E(t)=1{t<=24 mo}`:
`lambda(t|.) = lambda0(t) * exp( beta_early * NUC*E(t) + beta_late * NUC*(1-E(t)) + gamma*Z(t) )`,
plus a pre-specified **three-way signed stratum contrast** `NUC x HBV-DNA(high/low) x phase(early/late)`.
Time axis = months since t0 (t0 = index resection `开始时间` + 90 d). No other design element changes.

Predictions on a **signed** quantity (the phase-stratum log-HR pair, beta_early, beta_late):
- **H_effect:** beta_early < 0 (protective) AND beta_late ~ 0 (phase-selective), AND the early-phase
  NUC log-HR is more negative in the **high** baseline-HBV-DNA stratum than the low stratum
  (signed NUC x HBV-DNA contrast in the early phase). Mechanism ([K3]): suppression acts on
  active viral replication/occult residual disease, not on later cirrhosis-driven de-novo carcinogenesis.
- **R_indication [K2]:** beta_early ~ beta_late ~ 0 (near-null) AND stratum-FLAT after overlap
  weighting (naive crude association attenuates to the null).
- **R_detect:** a protective early-phase HR that collapses (>=50% toward null) once imaging-cadence /
  contact-intensity covariates are added, with a flat AFP-independent negative-control model.

**What the phase split reveals that M0/M1 lose.** A pooled recurrence endpoint mixes occult
residual disease (early) with de-novo carcinogenesis (late), whose only common cause is the
exposure; a single NUC HR can look null (mechanisms cancel) or protective (only one phase drives
it) while saying nothing about which process NUC modifies. M0 (conventional adjustment) and M1
(overlap-weighted Cox + NUC x HBV-DNA interaction) both estimate ONE recurrence hazard; they
cannot tell whether NUC shifts the early-phase (residual/replication) hazard or the late-phase
(de-novo) hazard - the exact comparison on which H_effect, R_indication and R_detect diverge.

## 5. Method comparison (baseline vs parent vs phase model)
- **M0 - fitting target:** single-cohort landmark Cox, one NUC HR, conventional adjustment; loses the
  stratum-specific HTE entirely.
- **M1 - fitting target:** the same cohort/time axis; overlap-weighted Cox + NUC x HBV-DNA interaction.
  Yields a stratum-specific but **time-collapsed** NUC HR.
- **M2 - fitting target:** the same cohort/time axis; overlap-weighted **piecewise** Cox with `beta_early`,
  `beta_late` and the NUC x HBV-DNA x phase contrast. M2 recovers the phase-resolution M0/M1 average away.
- **Split / held-out:** cohort split by calendar era (e.g., pre-2017 vs 2017+) as a held-out replication;
  any CATE uses cross-fitting with patient-level folds (no row leakage). Primary contrast reported with
  95% CI and a bootstrap; **pre-specified** (not data-chosen) at 24 months.
- **Approx budget (discovery estimate):** CPU-only, 1e3-1e4 weighted fits / bootstrap draws of the
  tabular piecewise Cox on ~2x10^4 resection patients - minutes-scale, well inside solver_planning_limits;
  no GPU or deep model needed (recorded as measured design intent, not executed here).
- **Alternative not chosen:** a shared-parameter joint observation-event model; revisitable only if
  detection-bias adjustment (Section 3, R_detect) does not suffice.

## 6. Falsification (Supportive / Adverse / Inconclusive / precise-null)
Conjunctive rule; primary contrast = (beta_early, beta_late) and the early-phase NUC x HBV-DNA sign.
- **Supportive (H_effect):** overlap-weighted beta_early<0 with 95% CI excluding 1 AND beta_late ~0
  (phase-selective), AND early-phase NUC log-HR more negative in the high-HBV-DNA stratum, robust to
  era/surveillance/AFP adjustment, with a flat negative-control output.
- **Adverse (R_indication) [K2]:** beta_early and beta_late both cross 1 (near-null) after overlap
  weighting, or a protective beta that is stratum-FLAT -> generic, not replication-specific.
- **Adverse (detection-bias):** a protective beta_early attenuates >=50% toward the null once
  imaging-cadence / contact-intensity covariates are added.
- **Inconclusive:** HBV-DNA modifier too sparse, OR **<30 events in a phase/stratum** (see measured
  probe: the late phase is far under this), OR positivity/near-overlap violation -> narrow to the
  cirrhosis fallback and to the early phase only.
- **Precise-null:** tight CI centered on 1 for both phases/strata -> NUC status (as defined) does not
  modify early recurrence.

## 8. Rivals and honest limits
Rivals: confounding by indication ([K2]); surveillance/detection intensity; healthy-initiation; era/treatment
drift; immortal time (removed by windowed exposure). Limits: the recurrence endpoint is lexical and
requires radiologist mRECIST adjudication (keyword over-capture, incl. pre-index events); NUC vs never-NUC
and NUC-start classification require pharmacy/chart adjudication; baseline HBV-DNA density per patient is
unmeasured here (label present, per-patient coverage deferred); the LATE phase is under-observed (~159 events)
so de-novo conclusions need longer follow-up or another study; single institution; **no outcome model/HR
computed in discovery**. Claims computationally checkable: weighted beta_early/beta_late, the NUC x HBV-DNA
x phase contrast, attenuation delta and negative control. Claims needing adjudication/other data: true
recurrence timing (mRECIST), true adherence, HBV-DNA completeness, late-phase power.
