## Question, evidence, and advance

When a person appears to have preserved kidney function by creatinine, does adding cystatin C and grip strength identify a subgroup at higher subsequent risk of a recorded chronic-kidney-disease diagnosis than creatinine assessment alone?

This could inform whether a low-cost cystatin-C measurement is worth retaining in a kidney-risk triage pathway and whether strength adds useful context. It estimates prognostic information in observed UKB data, not the causal effect of ordering cystatin C, improving grip, changing medication, or preventing disease.

The seed's “severe AKI” claim is retired: the export has no KDIGO stage, urine output, serial inpatient creatinine, ICU status, measured GFR, adjudicated AKI onset, or severity. The primary outcome is later recorded inpatient N18; N17 and N17-or-N18 are secondary. N18 is still a recorded diagnosis, not adjudicated CKD onset or complete ascertainment.

The strongest inspected evidence is Haines et al., DOI 10.2215/CJN.0000000000000203, read from references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt. That 38-person prolonged-ICU study had serial creatinine, cystatin C, measured GFR in a subgroup and rectus-femoris ultrasound. It supports possible creatinine overestimation during prolonged critical illness, not this UKB population association, grip interaction, five-year N18 rule, or testing policy. The substantive advance is a direct, falsifiable test-value comparison in a dated UKB baseline cohort, with the acute biological interpretation explicitly deferred.

## Hypothesis and estimands

Among participants with valid baseline age, sex, assessment date, creatinine, cystatin C and grip, and no prior valid recorded N18 on or before baseline:

> eGFRcreat >=60 and eGFRcys <60 mL/min/1.73 m2 predicts higher five-year risk of a first later recorded inpatient N18 diagnosis than eGFRcreat >=60 and eGFRcys >=60, especially among participants in the sex-specific lowest grip quintile.

Primary effect: absolute five-year recorded-risk difference and risk ratio for discordant/low-grip versus concordant-preserved/high-grip. Secondary effect: discordant/low-grip versus discordant/high-grip.

For i, T0 is 53-0.0 and H_i = T0_i + 5*365.25 days. The mutually exclusive endpoint is T (first position-matched 41270 code with prefix N18 and same-position nonmissing 41280 date strictly after T0 and no later than H), D (death before T or tied with T, with precedence fixed before fitting), or N (neither qualifying N18 nor death record in the available source through H). Prior N18 excludes only with a valid same-position pair on/before T0. Separate N17 and N17-or-N18 analyses have their own risk sets.

The prediction estimand is held-out change in multiclass Brier score, log loss, calibration, and fixed risk movement when cystatin C and grip are added to a creatinine-only model. The practical Brier margin is delta=0.002, fixed before scoring, not a clinical utility threshold.

If ascertainment through H cannot be certified, no incidence, disease-free, or complete-follow-up claim is permitted. A bounded recorded-category analysis may be reported only after landmark and code/date gates pass, and its negative label means no qualifying source record, not absence of disease. Death fields are used only after date semantics pass.

## Transparent and learned methods

Fit on identical participants, endpoint and folds:

- B0: age, sex, baseline assessment calendar year and eGFRcreat (raw creatinine only in a relabelled non-eGFR audit if the eGFR gate fails);
- B1: B0 plus eGFRcys, left/right grip and discordance/grip indicators;
- B1a: B0 plus eGFRcys;
- B1b: B0 plus grip.

Use regularized binary/multinomial probability models for T/D/N. B1a and B1b separate cystatin and grip contributions. No post-T0 data enter.

The substantive learned alternative L1 is a small-grid gradient-boosted classifier on exactly B1 raw inputs: age, sex, calendar year, creatinine, cystatin C, left grip and right grip, with matched boosted B0. Fit fold-locally with training-only missingness handling, equation construction, grip percentile, tuning, inner-fold early stopping and calibration. Fix shallow depth 2–3, a small learning-rate/regularization grid and tree limit before fitting. L1 may reveal thresholds and cystatin-by-grip interaction hidden by additivity, but cannot redefine phenotype, endpoint, horizon or primary contrast. A small score gain without stable shape, calibration and held-out replication is not biological reserve or clinical utility.

A mechanistic model is deferred: muscle mass, inflammation, specimen timing, measured GFR and organ-specific assay validity are not verified. Revisit requires field dictionaries, collection dates, validated body-composition/inflammatory measures and an adjudicated kidney endpoint.

## Split, uncertainty and falsification

Use one deterministic participant-level five-fold hash split by eid within discovery data. All imputation, transformations, eGFR construction, grip percentile, class weights, regularization, tuning and calibration are training-fold-only. Produce one held-out prediction record per eligible eid for B0, B1, B1a, B1b, L1 and matched boosted B0.

Report overall and phenotype-stratified multiclass Brier, log loss, one-vs-rest calibration intercept/slope for T/D/N, reliability/resolution, observed-versus-predicted N18/death risk, absolute risk difference, risk ratio and descriptive risk movement at 1%, 2.5%, 5%, 10%, 15%. These thresholds are not validated treatment utilities. Use paired participant bootstrap intervals within phenotype strata, retaining fold, outcome, predictions and weights; report 95% intervals, support failures and whether model-training uncertainty is included. A full-pipeline bootstrap is a labelled sensitivity.

Primary N18 support gates: >=100 total N18 events, >=30 events and >=200 participants in each primary comparison group, and >=200 participants in each sex-specific grip stratum used for interaction. Secondary N17/composite outcomes have independent gates. If selection is substantial, fit a training-only stabilized selection model among T0-complete participants from age, sex, calendar year, baseline biomarkers and grip availability; apply unchanged to held-out data, truncate at training 1st/99th percentiles, report overlap/maximum weight/standardized differences/ESS. This is a missing-at-random sensitivity, not causal correction.

Run endpoint permutation within fold and baseline-year strata; cystatin permutation within sex, year and creatinine quantile strata; complete-case versus one fold-local missingness strategy; N17 and N17-or-N18 secondary outcomes; deliberate 41270/41280 position scrambling that must be detected; strict date/pre-baseline/duplicate-eid/coverage/range/leakage audits; no use of instance index as elapsed time; fold stability and checks for age, calendar year, assessment-centre or missingness drivers. Frozen phenotype labels are not recomputed after permutation.

## Interpretation

Support requires all semantic, timing, equation, code/date, prior-record and observation gates; support/overlap; no leakage/pairing failure; B1 improvement over B0 greater than delta with 95% lower bound above delta; discordant/low-grip risk interval excluding zero in the higher-risk direction; stability under missingness/selection; endpoint permutation destroying the pattern; acceptable calibration; and no fold-unstable interaction. L1 strengthens only with stable held-out shape.

Adverse evidence is negligible B1 increment, no higher discordant/low-grip risk, reversal under sensitivity, poor calibration, fold instability or persistence after permutation. This weakens adding cystatin/grip for this recorded-risk task but does not refute ICU cystatin-C value.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed field/equation semantics, absent specimen timing needed for prospective interpretation, malformed pairs, unverified ascertainment through H, sparse cells, nonoverlap, unstable weights, wide intervals or conflicting essential analyses. It is not evidence against the biological seed.

Computationally checkable: integrity, literal columns, joins, flow, exposure construction after semantic certification, dates, endpoint arithmetic, fits, predictions, scores, uncertainty, calibration, support, overlap and permutation. Clinical adjudication/another study is required for true GFR, AKI severity/onset/reversibility, CKD onset, complete ascertainment, outpatient disease, urine output, inflammation/body-composition confounding, medication consequences, test-ordering benefit, action thresholds, causal effects and external validation.
