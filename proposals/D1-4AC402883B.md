# Pattern-mixture and all-row scoring sharpen the observation-identification boundary without rescuing the care-plan encoding

## Question and scientific advance

This substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]` tests whether the earlier adverse hospital-held-out predictive result is an artifact of post-classification missingness or competing ICU exits. The frozen target-independent denominator, first qualifying ICU stay per person, five landmarks (1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes), four airway definitions, inclusive classification window `[L,L+360]`, later chart window `(L+360,L+1080]`, eight-value Ventilation vocabulary, and documentation-only interpretation are unchanged.

The strongest evidence-supported claim before this experiment was that the exact and exhaustive broader care-plan encodings are sparse and hospital-concentrated documentation variables that did not improve bounded hospital-held-out prediction of a later structured respiratory-chart reduction after complete-outcome restriction. The unresolved claim was whether informative post-classification ascertainment—ICU death, live ICU discharge, adequate respiratory charting without a paired reduction, or sparse respiratory charting—could reverse that result. The present analysis shows that measured pattern-mixture departures can reverse the *point-estimated* comparison for some stricter definitions, but only under favorable untestable departures from measured-at-random; at the observed-receipt baseline and adverse departures it does not rescue the encoding. Thus the clinically consequential result is an identification boundary, not a claim about ventilation, liberation, benefit, harm, or causality.

## Estimands and analysis

The binary chart-proxy estimand is restricted to rows remaining in ICU through `L+1080`: complete computable reductions plus the two noncomputable observation states. The binary outcome is the inherited structured respiratory-setting reduction (FiO2 decrease >=0.05, PEEP decrease >=2, pressure-support decrease >=2, or set ventilator-rate decrease >=2, with the inherited missing-component rules). It is not a clinical endpoint.

Within each definition and each delta in `[-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3]` log-odds units, a missing row receives a fractional outcome probability based on the observed reduction prevalence within its `receipt` class, falling back to the definition-wide complete-row rate for absent cells, then shifted by delta. Delta 0 is the measured-at-random-like reference; negative deltas assume missing rows are less likely to have the chart reduction, positive deltas assume they are more likely. Fractional labels are used to refit, not merely to score, process, exact, and broader logistic nuisance models in leave-one-hospital-out folds. Held-out folds use only other hospitals. Comparisons are hospital-macro and row-micro differences in log loss, with positive meaning the added encoding is worse than generic process controls. Eligible held-out hospitals satisfy the predeclared minimum counts and both observed outcome classes.

Separately, an all-row multinomial proper score predicts the five mutually exclusive exported observation states using process, exact, or broader predictors in hospital-held-out folds. This is a prediction task for documentation/observation states only; it deliberately does not rank death, discharge, sparse charting, or complete charting as clinical success or failure.

## Results

The state counts reproduce the frozen audit: unbounded 3,296 complete, 258 sparse, 24 adequate/no paired outcome, 59 live discharge, 34 deaths; 24-hour 2,273, 217, 16, 42, 27; 12-hour 1,555, 138, 12, 22, 21; and 6-hour 1,018, 98, 10, 17, 16.

At delta 0, refit pattern-mixture broader-minus-process macro log-loss differences were +0.00048 (unbounded), +0.00180 (24-hour), +0.00232 (12-hour), and +0.00583 (6-hour); positive is worse. Exact-minus-process macro differences were +0.00053, +0.00063, +0.00077, and +0.00221. Thus the observed-receipt reference does not rescue either encoding.

Under adverse departures in which missing rows are less likely to have a reduction (delta -1 to -3), broader-minus-process remained adverse at all four definitions; at delta -3 the macro differences were -0.00074, +0.00118, +0.00188, and +0.01134 from unbounded through 6-hour, so the unbounded point estimate is near zero and the three stricter definitions remain nonnegative. Under favorable departures (delta +1 to +3), the comparison changes: broader-minus-process macro was -0.00039, +0.00042, -0.00051, and -0.00100 at delta +1; -0.00217, -0.00217, -0.00576, and -0.01031 at delta +2; and -0.00405, -0.00513, -0.01055, and -0.01824 at delta +3. The favorable reversal is therefore assumption-driven, especially for stricter airway definitions, and cannot identify the missing outcomes from these fields.

All-row state scoring produced negative added-model-minus-process log loss at every definition. For broader-minus-process macro scores, the values were -0.00195, -0.01046, -0.01245, and -0.01647 from unbounded through 6-hour, with 15/26, 13/19, 12/16, and 11/14 hospitals better, respectively. This supports a narrower descriptive statement that exact/broader fields can predict the exported observation-state partition better than process controls; it is not evidence that they predict a clinical outcome or that missingness has been resolved.

## Falsification and interpretation

A rescue would require favorable sensitivity to be unnecessary or robust over a clinically defensible delta range, and would require uncertainty that accounts for stay clustering and nuisance refitting. That standard was not met. The data support: (1) no rescue at delta 0; (2) adverse or near-null behavior under negative deltas; (3) point-estimate reversals only under positive, unverified assumptions; and (4) all-row improvement only for predicting observed documentation states.

The requested stay-clustered, nuisance-refit bootstrap was tested for feasibility but was disabled in the successful bounded execution (`bootstrap_replicates=0`) after the zero-replicate path was corrected. Consequently, the JSON contains no uncertainty interval for the new pattern-mixture estimates. The earlier parent analysis supplied 100 stay-clustered intervals for fixed predictions and weights, but those intervals do not cover this refitted pattern-mixture sensitivity. The point estimates are therefore sensitivity diagnostics, not inferential evidence. A future repair should run a bounded positive number of nuisance-refit stay-cluster resamples if uncertainty around the delta curve is required.
