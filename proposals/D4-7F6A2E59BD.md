## Frozen population, temporal boundaries, and event construction

Calculate completed age at `L` using the 15th of recorded birth month. Require age 40–75 inclusive, `365 <= L-B <= 2922` calendar days, and complete finite haemoglobin, MCV, and platelet-30080 values at both instances. Define entry `E=L+366 days` and horizon `H=L+1825 days`. Washout is `[L,E)` and follow-up is `[E,H]`, so an event exactly on `E` belongs to follow-up.

Construct cancer long form only by pairing each `40005-i.0` date with `40006-i.0` for the identical suffix `i`; retain suffix provenance. Trim/uppercase codes and remove dots for classification. Malignancy is ICD-10 C00–C97, and CRC is C18–C20 including descendants. Collapse exact `eid/date/code` duplicates only after suffix provenance has been retained. Exclude cancer strictly after recorded death. Under the primary death-first rule, cancer on the death date does not qualify as before death. If CRC and non-CRC codes share the first malignant date, classify as CRC primarily and report the count.

Exclude participants with a first recorded malignancy or death before `L`, then exclude first recorded malignancy or death in `[L,E)`. The recorded-case fallback includes only participants whose first recorded malignant date is in `[E,H]` and strictly before recorded death. Participants without a recorded malignancy are excluded and are never controls. Outcome `Y=1` is CRC on the first malignant date; `Y=0` is non-CRC malignancy only on that date.

The conditional estimand is the paired out-of-fold change in discrimination and log loss for recorded CRC versus recorded non-CRC case type among this selected future-case population. It is not `P(CRC)` in the eligible population.

For marker `m`, define `slope_m=(m_1-m_0)/((L-B)/365.2425)`. Orient larger values as prespecified adverse change: `-slope_Hb`, `-slope_MCV`, and `+slope_platelet30080`. The comparator includes age at `L`, sex, instance-1 haemoglobin, instance-1 MCV, instance-1 platelet count, and the fixed diagnosis-lag indicator. The trajectory model adds exactly these three slopes.

Diagnosis lag is `first_malignant_date-E`. Freeze bins as early 0–365 days and late 366–1459 days. Include the late indicator as an unpenalized nuisance term in both models. Jointly stratify folds by label and lag whenever support permits. Diagnosis lag is observed only after entry and is unavailable to a prospective model; adjustment reduces diagnosis-time composition differences but does not make the contrast prospective.
