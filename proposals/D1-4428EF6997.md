# Coverage-gated lactate-by-perfusion heterogeneity at the first observed live ICU exit

## Scientific deliverable

The future Harbor solver must newly produce a leakage-controlled competing-risk study of whether the prognostic meaning of persistent hyperlactatemia depends on the direction of measured systemic perfusion immediately before an observed live ICU exit.

Completion requires an all-eligible flow, a separately reported phenotype-complete cohort, and a frozen gate manifest; the first qualifying live exit per admission with a one-hour pre-exit landmark; the primary persistent-lactate × perfusion interaction and 48-hour risk contrasts; matched held-out B0/B1/Bmask/Bdisc predictions plus a training-only learned M2 sensitivity; source, timing, unit, coverage, ascertainment and split provenance; endpoint, process and selection sensitivities; outcome and timing permutation falsifications; and a claim ledger linking each conclusion to computed outputs.

A gate failure produces a complete flow, failure counts and descriptive quantities but makes the primary interaction inconclusive. It does not permit loosening windows, coding missing pressor data as zero, merging cells, choosing thresholds after outcomes, or selecting a better-looking subset. No clinical result is claimed here.

## Unresolved question and importance

The strongest available evidence supports only that the configured MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot contains repeated lactate, MAP, vasoactive-interval, urine-output and organ-function records from which candidate trajectories can be constructed. The expert seed is explicitly untested. MIMIC provenance and within-person timing are documented, but neither the biological validity of these proxies nor their prevalence and event support is established. The demonstration references motivate fair simple-versus-longitudinal learning comparisons; they do not establish this phenotype. The main Oncoformer article and complete STAR Methods are unavailable locally; only its supplement is available, so no reproduction claim is made.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among first eligible live ICU exits with repeated lactate and paired, adequately ascertained MAP/vasoactive measurements, is the excess 48-hour risk associated with persistent hyperlactatemia smaller when measured perfusion is improving than when it is worsening?

The primary estimand is:
`[CIF(R/D | persistent, improving) - CIF(R/D | nonpersistent, improving)] - [CIF(R/D | persistent, worsening) - CIF(R/D | nonpersistent, worsening)]`,
where R/D is readmission to ICU or death and alive discharge/observation boundary S competes. The directional hypothesis is negative. The primary clinically meaningful null region is -2 to +2 percentage points; a negative estimate inside that region is not primary support.

This matters because elevated lactate can reflect unresolved illness, impaired clearance, adrenergic metabolism, or observation/selection processes. A robust interaction would support prospective evaluation of context-aware risk communication; a null result would argue against adding this retrospective distinction to risk stratification. Neither result establishes microcirculatory dysfunction, fluid responsiveness, treatment benefit, or discharge policy.

## Population, landmark and outcomes

Use one index per `hadm_id`: the first `icu/icustays` row with valid `intime < outtime`, a live exit at `t0=outtime`, and `intime <= tL-12 hours`, where `tL=t0-1 hour`. Exclude reversed/missing intervals and `deathtime <= t0`. Admissions lacking an admission row are linkage failures in the flow, not silent exclusions. A `hospital_expire_flag=1` with missing `deathtime` is unresolved U, not N.

The primary feature window is `[tL-12h,tL]`, split into early `[tL-12h,tL-6h]` and late `(tL-6h,tL]`. Charted rows require `charttime <= tL` and `storetime <= tL`. Input intervals require finite `starttime < endtime`, `starttime <= tL`, `storetime <= tL`, and are clipped at `tL`. No feature uses `outtime`, `los`, `last_careunit`, future transfers, endpoint fields, notes or post-landmark rows. A chart-time-only matrix is a fixed sensitivity.

Define first transitions in `(t0,t0+48h]`: R is a different `stay_id` in the same admission with `intime > t0`; D is `deathtime); S is alive `dischtime` before later R/D, an observation boundary rather than a safe outcome; N is no R/D/S while ascertainable; U is unresolved timed-death ascertainment. D precedes R at equal times. Report R, D, S, R/D and N with cause-specific hazards and 48-hour CIFs. Require fixed 7-day, complete-ascertainment and adverse/non-adverse U-bound sensitivities. R and D interactions are secondary.

## Endpoint-independent coverage and measurement gate

The coverage gate uses no endpoint labels or outcome results. It is written before outcome modeling, with one row of flags/reason codes per eligible exit. Missingness is not imputed to normal physiology or zero support.

For each half h, record:

- `lactate_valid_n_h`: distinct `labevent_id` rows with numeric `valuenum`, accepted canonical `valueuom=mmol/L` after case/whitespace normalization, and both `charttime` and `storetime` at or before `tL`;
- `lactate_distinct_time_n_h`;
- `map_valid_n_h`: distinct clinical timestamps from numeric `chartevents` items 220052, 220181, 225312, `valueuom=mmHg`, both times by `tL), with same-time duplicate items collapsed by median;
- `map_item_present_h`, a bit vector for those three channels;
- `pressor_valid_interval_n_h`: finite target-item `inputevents` intervals overlapping h with `storetime <= tL), after collapsing exact repeated (`stay_id,itemid,starttime,endtime,orderid,linkorderid`) rows and unioning overlaps;
- `pressor_target_item_present_h`, a bit vector for the eleven target IDs;
- `input_any_valid_n_h`: all valid stored-by-landmark input intervals, a process-intensity audit, never evidence of no pressor;
- `foley_valid_n_h`: numeric item 226559 output rows satisfying both times by `tL`, for secondary corroboration; and
- per-organ-analyte value/test indicators and `organ_test_n_h` for ALT 50861, AST 50878, bilirubin 50885, creatinine 50912, bicarbonate 50882, hemoglobin 51222, platelets 51265 and WBC 51300.

For every domain also record counts before and after timing/unit/value filters, distinct-time counts, median chart-to-store lag, fraction available by `tL`, rejected-unit counts and invalid-value counts. Unsupported units, nonnumeric values and unsupported conversions are rejected and counted. Valid join keys and no future clinical/recording times are required.

The strict primary gate requires in both halves: (1) at least one valid lactate; (2) at least two valid MAP observations at two distinct clinical timestamps; and (3) at least one valid target-agent interval. The third rule is intentionally conservative: a missing target-agent row is missing ascertainment, not zero vasoactive support. Patients failing it remain in all-eligible flow and fixed MAP-only/coverage sensitivities but cannot enter the strict concordant interaction. This narrows generalizability and must be reported.

## Phenotype

Persistent hyperlactatemia means early and late lactate medians both `>=2.0 mmol/L`; 4.0 is a fixed sensitivity. The primary perfusion trajectory uses MAP medians and unioned minutes of target vasoactive intervals for items 221906, 222315, 221289, 229617, 221749, 229630, 229631, 229632, 221662, 221653 and 221986. Rates and amounts are not pooled.

Improving requires MAP late-minus-early `>=5 mmHg` and late pressor minutes no greater than early. Worsening requires MAP change `<=-5 mmHg` and late pressor minutes no less than early. All other cases are indeterminate and retained descriptively, never forced into the primary contrast. Foley presence/normalized or log volume is a secondary corroboration and cannot rescue missing primary MAP/pressor coverage.

## Frozen event-support and overlap gate

Event support necessarily counts frozen endpoint labels; it is therefore endpoint-count-based but outcome-result-independent: the rules below are fixed before any model fit, effect estimate, calibration result or held-out prediction is inspected, and do not use association direction or magnitude.

For each of four primary cells (lactate persistent/nonpersistent × improving/worsening), require:

- at least 40 subjects;
- at least 10 R/D events in the full gated cohort;
- at least 8 subjects and at least 3 R/D events in the deterministic held-out test cell; and
- U <=10% overall and <=20% in any cell.

The primary gate concerns composite R/D. A cell with fewer than 5 R or fewer than 5 D events cannot support a component-specific claim. No cell may be dropped, merged with indeterminate, or redefined for support. If integer allocation prevents the stated test support, the gate fails.

After eligibility and gate variables are frozen, use a subject-level 70/15/15 split, stratified only by primary cell, endpoint label and anchor-year group; no subject crosses partitions. Fit preprocessing, clipping, scaling, imputation, calibration and learned assignments on training/validation only.

For the outcome-free exit-selection audit, fit q on pooled ICU-stay hours (live-exit hours versus eligible non-exit ICU hours), using one-hour bins and only information available by each hour. In each primary cell, common support requires at least 90% of index exits with q in [0.05,0.95], at least 20 non-exit hours in each occupied q decile of the index-exit distribution, and inverse-odds ESS `(sum w)^2/sum(w^2) >=50`. Report unweighted and stabilized weights. q failure does not repair the cohort: the unweighted interaction remains descriptive and the selection-robust primary interpretation is inconclusive. q is a selection stress test, not a discharge-policy counterfactual.

## Fair baseline, substantive alternative and learned sensitivity

All models use the same strict gated population, landmark, labels, split, held-out set and subject bootstrap. B0 is a penalized discrete-time cause-specific complementary-log-log model for R, D and S using admission/demographic/current-ICU context, terminal physiology, terminal missingness and process variables. It is the transparent latest-state baseline and loses explicit trajectory direction and interaction.

B1 adds order-blind summaries in twelve one-hour bins over the same physiology, labs, output, pressor and process fields. It tests generic history/measurement intensity. Bmask retains context, masks, counts, testing, Foley-charting and pressor-presence history but removes physiologic values; if Bdisc does not exceed Bmask, physiology-specific interpretation fails.

Bdisc is the selected substantive alternative: B1 plus persistent lactate, fixed improving/indeterminate/worsening direction, early/late values, MAP/pressor changes and interaction, organ values and separate availability indicators. It can reveal interpretable cross-domain effect modification lost by B0/B1 while remaining auditable. M2 is a training-only diagonal-covariance two-component Gaussian mixture on standardized early-to-late lactate/MAP/pressor change vectors, with posterior probability entered in the same hazard model. A component gets improving/worsening language only when its training means satisfy the fixed direction rule. M2 may expose nonlinear coupled trajectories but is deferred from primary status because strict pressor ascertainment may make labels unstable and it cannot validate a proxy alone.

A GRU/transformer is deferred because the parent already tested chronology versus order-blind history and this successor addresses coverage and interaction support. A fluid-response model is deferred because available inputevents lack fluid responsiveness, intent, counterfactual outcomes and adequate time-varying confounding control. These are evidence-bound scientific deferrals, not CPU/GPU prohibitions.

## Analysis, falsification and interpretation

Fit competing-risk cause-specific models and report 48-hour CIFs, transition-aware Brier scores, calibration intercept/slope, AUROC/AUPRC for components, paired Bdisc-B1 and B1-B0 differences, the interaction, standardized CIF contrasts and subject-bootstrap 95% intervals. Fixed 5/10/20% test-set review tables are descriptive, not utility or staffing evidence.

Falsifications/audits are: source labels/link/units/ranges; timestamps and clipping; joins/duplicates/first-exit/prohibited fields; Bdisc versus Bmask and phenotype-only process model; within-subject early/late permutation preserving counts/availability; outcome permutation within partitions; available-by-landmark versus chart-time-only; fixed lactate 4.0, arterial-MAP-only, MAP-only, pressor-only, Foley, continuous-change, 4/6/8-bin, 7-day and U-bound sensitivities; separate R/D/S reporting; the outcome-free six-hour live-exit negative-control model; q/care-unit/anchor-year/measurement-intensity strata; and a stricter fixed gate audit that cannot rescue the primary.

Support requires every gate, a negative interaction below -2 points with its interval entirely below the null region, lower persistent-lactate R/D CIF in improving perfusion, calibrated held-out Bdisc improvement over B1, Bdisc exceeding Bmask, and stability across fixed timing/component/coverage/q/endpoint sensitivities. Adverse evidence includes null/reversed interaction, Bdisc matching B1/Bmask, process-only or chart-only explanation, instability, gains confined to S/N, or outcome-permutation resistance. Inconclusive includes any gate failure, sparse cells, high U/indeterminate fraction, incompatible units, q failure, unstable calibration, or interaction interval wider than 6 points. These labels are assigned from frozen rules, not hypothesis confirmation.

Computationally checkable claims are provenance, flow, timing, units, item support, joins, gate flags, prevalence, split integrity, fits, predictions, CIF/Brier/calibration, contrasts, uncertainty, q and falsifications. Clinical adjudication/another study is required for microcirculatory dysfunction, lactate etiology, clinician intent, fluid response, discharge appropriateness/preventability, causal treatment/discharge effects, monitoring benefit, utility, ward surveillance, transportability and bedside recommendations.
